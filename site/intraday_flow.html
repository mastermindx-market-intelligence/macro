How to read this board如何看懂本看板 field guide实战指南

1 · The one job of this board1 · 本看板的唯一任务 ▸

This board watches a fixed list of institutional leaders — the Mag 7, AI infrastructure, AI software and semiconductors, semicap equipment, reshoring, defense and power-grid names. It looks for one specific thing: a leader that sold off, absorbed the selling, and is now being bought back on real volume. For every name it gives you a plain call — buy now, almost ready, in favour, take profits, watch, or stand aside — and the reason behind it. It is not a stock screener for cheap stocks; it is a timing board for continuation entries in names that already have sponsorship.

本看板跟踪一份固定的机构领涨股名单——Mag 7、AI基础设施、AI软件与半导体、半导体设备、回流制造、国防与电网标的。它只找一件事：某只领涨股经历下跌、消化卖压、如今正被放量重新买入。对每只标的给出一个通俗结论——现在买入、即将就绪、走势占优、获利了结、观望或暂时观望——并附上理由。这不是找廉价股的筛选器，而是为已有机构参与的标的把握延续入场时机的择时看板。

2 · What each call means2 · 每个结论的含义 ▸
  • 🟢 Buy now — sold off recently, reclaimed above the day’s average price on durable (not one-spike) volume, and it is a clean leader. The continuation setup is live; stop goes below the washout base.
  • 🔵 Almost ready — the base is built but price hasn’t reclaimed yet. Waiting for a push back above the average price on volume.
  • 🏃 In favour — already trending and holding above the average price, but with no fresh washout. Ride an existing position; not a fresh entry.
  • 🟠 Take profits — stretched well above the average price, or jammed into a dealer ceiling / expiry pin. Expect a pullback; don’t chase.
  • 👀 Watch — don’t chase — moving, but without a washout base, or a name with a history of faking breakouts. Wait for structure.
  • ⚪ Stand aside — quiet tape, no setup right now.
  • 🟢 现在买入 —— 近期下跌后，在持续（非单次）放量中收复当日均价，且为干净的领涨股。延续布局已成立；止损设在洗盘底部下方。
  • 🔵 即将就绪 —— 底部已成，但价格尚未收复。等待放量重回均价上方。
  • 🏃 走势占优 —— 已在趋势中并守住均价上方，但无新的洗盘。适合持有已有仓位，非新入场点。
  • 🟠 获利了结 —— 大幅高于均价，或顶到做市商天花板/到期钉价。预期回落，勿追。
  • 👀 观望——勿追 —— 有异动，但无洗盘底部，或该标的有假突破前科。等待结构。
  • ⚪ 暂时观望 —— 盘口平静，当前无布局。
3 · The options tape (approximate direction)3 · 期权资金带（方向为近似） ▸

The options column shows tape activity and how persistently it leans call-heavy or put-heavy. The direction is approximate — it is signed by a tape rule, not by matching each trade to the bid/offer, so read it as a lean, never a confirmation (that is what the ~soft mark means). Unusual-size flags (big blocks, fresh positions, size-vs-open-interest) are exact counts. It is a supporting input, not a trigger on its own.

期权栏展示资金带的活跃度，以及其偏看涨或看跌的持续程度。方向为近似——它由盘口规则定向，而非将每笔交易与买卖报价匹配，故仅作倾向、绝非确认（这正是 ~软性 标记的含义）。异常规模标记（大单、新建仓、规模对比未平仓量）为精确计数。它是辅助输入，不单独作为触发。

4 · The dealer map (levels & stops, not direction)4 · 做市商地图（价位与止损，非方向） ▸

Built from daily options chains, the dealer map shows the nearby levels market-makers tend to defend — a ceiling above and a floor below — the day’s expected move, and whether price is being pinned near a big strike into expiry. Use it for timing and stop placement: a hard ceiling just overhead argues against chasing; a strong floor below is a logical stop reference. It is never a direction call, and for a single stock the long/short-gamma label is a near-constant trait of the product, not a live signal — so it is shown as context only.

做市商地图由每日期权链构建，展示做市商倾向防守的邻近价位——上方天花板与下方地板——当日预期波动幅度，以及价格是否在到期临近时被钉在某大行权价附近。用于择时与设置止损：头顶紧邻硬天花板则不宜追高；下方有强地板可作止损参考。它绝非方向判断；对单只股票而言，多头/空头gamma标签是产品近乎恒定的属性，而非实时信号——故仅供参考展示。

Options-tape direction is approximate (~soft — a tape rule, not bid/offer matching). Every call is a deterministic rule with no score and no black box. Reference levels and the dealer map are context for timing and stops, not trade advice.期权带方向为近似（~软性——盘口规则，非买卖报价匹配）。每个结论均为确定性规则，无评分、无黑箱。参考价位与做市商地图仅为择时与止损背景，不构成交易建议。
