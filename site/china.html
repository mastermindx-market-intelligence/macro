What confirms each phase?各阶段如何确认？

A pullback starts after two consecutive settled closes at least 2% below the same recent reference high, or one close at least 5% below it. Its reference then stays fixed.连续两次收盘较同一近期参考高点下跌至少2%，或单次收盘下跌至少5%，确认回撤；之后参考高点保持固定。

Stabilizing needs three observed sessions without a new low and a close above the five-close average. Recovering needs a rising 20-close average, five closes above it and ten sessions without a new low. Missing sessions reset confirmation counters.止跌观察需要三个已观测交易日未创新低，且收盘高于五次收盘均线。修复中需要20次收盘均线向上、连续五次收于其上，以及十个交易日未创新低。缺失交易日会重置确认计数。

Resolution requires two closes reclaiming the high, or sustained trend repair: twenty closes above the 20-close average, a new 20-close high and 21 sessions without a new low.
