How do I read a signal on this site?我该怎么读这里的信号？
Every signal shows a plain-word stance first; the method behind it is on the Methodology page.每个信号先给出一句大白话结论；背后的方法在“方法论”页面。 Methodology方法论
What does calibration mean here?这里说的“校准”是什么意思？
It is our own scorecard: we publish how our past calls turned out, wins and misses together.那是我们自己的成绩单：我们把过去判断的结果公布出来，对的和错的都在。 Calibration Lab校准实验室
How do you know a signal works?你们怎么知道一个信号有效？
We write the test down before it runs and publish the result either way, including when there is none.我们在测试开始前先把标准写下来，无论结果如何都公布，包括没有效果的情况。 Calibration Lab校准实验室

How often do the pages update?页面多久更新一次？
Pages rebuild once a day, overnight. Each page carries the time it was last built.页面每天夜间重建一次。每个页面都标有最近一次生成的时间。

How do I sign in?我怎么登录？
Use the sign-in link at the top right of any page, with the email address on your account.在任意页面右上角，用你账户的邮箱地址登录。 Sign in登录
How do I get help from a person?我怎么联系真人客服？
Write to us from the support page. You get a reference number straight away and a reply by email.在“支持”页面写信给我们。你会立即拿到一个工单编号，回复通过邮件发送。 Support支持
What happens after I send a support message?提交客服信息之后会怎样？
We store it, show you a reference number, and email you a copy of what you wrote.我们会保存你的信息、给你一个工单编号，并把你写的内容抄送到你的邮箱。
Can I report something that looks wrong?我可以反馈看起来不对的地方吗？
Yes. Pick "Something is broken" on the support form and tell us the page and what you saw.可以。在支持表单里选“有功能坏了”，告诉我们是哪个页面、你看到了什么。 Support支持
Will you send me marketing email?你们会用我的邮箱发营销邮件吗？
Only while you leave marketing email switched on. Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link.只有在你保留营销邮件开关时才会。每封营销邮件都带有退订链接。
Why does a page show a dash instead of a number?为什么有的地方显示一条横线而不是数字？
A dash means the number is not available yet. A zero always means a real zero.横线表示这个数字目前还没有。零就是真的零。

The "What changed" list on this page is dated, newest first, and covers changes you can see.本页的“更新记录”按日期倒序排列，只列出你能看到的变化。
