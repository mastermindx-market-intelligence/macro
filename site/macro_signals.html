Stress selloff filter压力抛售过滤器 ?A risk FILTER that sits in FRONT of the capitulation gauge: when a stress dislocation hits (VIX panic, a >10% drawdown, a variance-premium extreme or VIX backwardation), it asks ONE question — is the Fed put present? PUT-ABSENT = a recession is underway (real-time Sahm >= 0.50) OR inflation locks the Fed (sustained 10y breakeven >= 2.5%). What the replay actually shows: it REFUSED the 2008 crash (put-absent from 2008-04), but it printed buyable early in the 2000-02 bear and twice in 2022 with the breakeven within 0.02pp of the cutoff.
