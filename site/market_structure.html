? How shock absorbers work
When dealer net GEX (gamma exposure — the options-book hedge that absorbs or amplifies moves) is positive (long gamma), they sold options and hedge by buying dips / selling rips — damping moves. When negative (short gamma), hedging requires buying rallies and selling dips — amplifying moves.

Net GEX in this snapshot: -86.3B (1st pctile since 2026-06-15, 78 sessions). This is a short series — that percentile is a rank among 78 readings, not years of history.

Model estimate only — actual dealer books are unobservable. Reconstructed from the SPX options chain. 缓冲机制原理
当做市商净GEX（伽马敞口——吸收或放大波动的期权账簿对冲）为正（多伽马）时，他们卖出期权，通过逢低买/逢高卖对冲——抑制波动。当GEX为负（空伽马）时，对冲需要追涨杀跌——放大波动。

当前净GEX：-86.3B（自2026-06-15以来1百分位，共 78 个交易日）。 本序列较短——该百分位是 78 次读数中的排名，并非多年历史。

仅为模型估算——做市商实际账簿不可观测，由标普500指数期权链重建。
