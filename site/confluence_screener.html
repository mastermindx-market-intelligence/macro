  • Small recent samples. A recent-era rate can rest on only a couple dozen monthly readings — samples that thin will wobble. Months and fires are shown beside each rate; open “How these readings are calculated” for definitions. 近期样本偏小。近期胜率可能只基于二十来个月度读数——样本这么小，数字会有波动。月份与触发次数列在各读数旁；打开「这些读数如何计算」可查看定义。
  • Win rates are historical, month-by-month reads. First average wins within each month, then average those months equally. This does not remove dependence between observations or selection bias from searching many recipes. 胜率是逐月的历史读数。先计算每个月内的胜率，再对各月等权平均。这不能消除观察值之间的相关性，也不能消除搜索大量组合带来的选择偏差。
  • The honest stance is watch, not buy. A signal lining up tells you where to look. Your own entry, sizing and stop are still your call. 客观立场是观察，而非买入。信号共振告诉你该看哪里。具体入场、仓位与止损仍由你决定。
