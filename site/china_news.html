Wang Xin, Director of the Research Bureau of the People's Bank of China, pointed out that in recent years, the scale of direct water consumption by global computing power centers has risen rapidly. In China specifically, the proportion of water used by computing power centers in total national water consumption is not yet high, but structural problems have gradually become prominent. In response to the water resource issues in AI and computing power development, corresponding measures can be taken from the aspects of computing power development itself as well as green finance and sustainable finance: first, strengthen water resource measurement, monitoring, data verification and information disclosure; second, promote water-saving technologies and improve the resource efficiency evaluation mechanism for green data centers; third, better leverage the role of water pricing in resource allocation; fourth, leverage the role of green finance to support the coordinated development of AI and water resources.
