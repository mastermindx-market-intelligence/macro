London lists steel contract based on "Shanghai price", marking the first export of China's metal pricing influence overseas伦敦挂牌“上海价格”钢材合约，中国金属定价影响力首次输出境外

The London Metal Exchange (LME) will officially list the LME Shanghai Hot-Rolled Coil contract on October 27, based on the settlement price of the hot-rolled coil futures of the Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE). This is the first time a metals "Shanghai price" has been exported to a mature overseas market. At a pre-listing seminar during LME Week, the reporter noted that international traders and fund managers present said that in past years they had "only one leg" compared with Chinese exporters.
