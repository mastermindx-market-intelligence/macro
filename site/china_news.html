The GREATPORT multimodal railway terminal in the China-Belarus Great Stone Industrial Park on the outskirts of Minsk, Belarus, officially opened and began operations on the 6th, with the first China-Europe container trains, the "Wheat Ear" and the "Cornflower," departing from the terminal for China.
