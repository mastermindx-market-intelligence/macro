Chile copper exports drop to one-year low despite price rebound智利铜出口量跌至一年低点，尽管铜价出现反弹

Despite a sharp rise in copper prices, Chile, the world's largest copper producer, saw August copper exports fall to their lowest level in over a year due to severe winter storms and disrupted mining operations. Central bank data released Monday showed copper export revenue of $4.62 billion in August, down 14% from July and 3.2% year-on-year, the lowest monthly figure since July 2025. The decline occurred against a backdrop of strong copper prices, with August average prices more than 40% higher than the same period last year. Chile's mining industry has faced persistent production disruptions this year; from July to August, storms bringing heavy rain, snow and strong winds hampered mining operations, while rough seas intermittently restricted port activity. Production difficulties in this country, which accounts for about a quarter of global copper mining output, have underpinned copper prices, further tightening an already strained global supply situation caused by disruptions elsewhere.尽管铜价大幅上涨，但受强冬季风暴与矿山生产受阻影响，全球最大产铜国智利8月铜出口量跌至一年多以来最低水平。智利央行周一公布的数据显示，上月铜出口额为46.2亿美元，较7月下降14%，同比下降3.2%，为2025年7月以来单月最低值。此次出口下滑发生在铜价走强的背景之下，8月铜均价较去年同期高出40%以上。今年智利采矿业持续遭遇生产故障；7‑8月，暴雨、降雪、强风形成的风暴干扰矿山作业，同时海面风浪过大也间歇性限制港口运转。这个占全球铜矿开采产量四分之一的国家出现生产困境，为铜价提供支撑，本就因其他地区供应中断而紧张的全球供应局面进一步收紧。
