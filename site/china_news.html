The People's Bank of China recently announced that it will issue a set of 2027 Panda precious metal commemorative coins on October 30, 2026. Industrial Internet Operating System Industry Alliance established in Shenzhen工业互联网操作系统产业联盟在深成立

The CEIC 2026 Consumer Electronics Innovation Conference was held in Shenzhen from the 22nd to the 24th.
