U.S. budget deficit reaches $1.96 trillion cumulatively in the first 11 months of fiscal 2026美国2026财年前11个月预算赤字累计达到1.96万亿美元

The US federal government's budget deficit reached $1.96 trillion in the first 11 months of the fiscal year, one of the highest levels on record. Treasury Department data released Friday showed the deficit for the 11 months through August edged down from $1.97 trillion in the same period of 2025. For August alone, the fiscal gap was $166.8 billion. The final month of the fiscal year (September) typically posts a surplus due to corporate tax deadlines. Part of the reason for the widening deficit is rising debt interest expenses, and higher US Treasury yields are one reason for the higher interest costs. Treasury data showed that as of the end of August, the average rate on marketable US Treasuries rose to 3.48%, more than 2 percentage points higher than five years ago.
