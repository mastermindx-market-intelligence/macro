In January 2026, in light of the market conditions at the time, the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing stock exchanges raised the margin requirement ratio for financing from 80% to 100%, which played a positive role in mitigating the procyclical accumulation of leverage risk, promoting rational trading and preemptively preventing risks. Recently, financing balances and trading activity have declined noticeably, and the overall leverage level has trended toward stability.
