Liu Jipeng: State-driven stock market must let people make money; without a thriving market, consumption cannot rise刘纪鹏：国家拉动的股市得让人民挣到钱 股市不兴 消费不起

Liu Jipeng, professor at China University of Political Science and Law, notes that most investors losing money directly impacts confidence and consumption. The Shanghai Composite Index hit 4,000 in October last year after rising 960 points from 3,040 on April 7, but has retreated from 4,000 over the past nine months. This pattern has repeated several times in China's history—bull markets rarely last more than a year. Whether the third breakout above 4,000 can be sustained and overcome the "short bull, long bear" dynamic depends on institutional changes. Liu points out this is a state-driven market rally.
