El Niño disrupts supply, agricultural futures prices heat up厄尔尼诺扰动供给，农产品期货价格升温

Affected by El Niño, the risk of extreme weather has risen in some global production areas, disrupting supply expectations for some agricultural products. Recently, from planting and seed breeding to fertilizers and pesticides, the A-share agricultural industry chain has continued to heat up, and prices of various agricultural futures have strengthened. The World Meteorological Organization recently forecast that this round of El Niño will further intensify in the coming months.
