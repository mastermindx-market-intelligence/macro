A spokesperson for the Chinese Embassy in the UK, responding to reporters' questions on some media hyping the so-called "rise of Chinese manufacturing posing a shock to Western high-end industries," stated that the root cause of Europe's industrial difficulties lies in its own structural problems—industrial hollowing-out, excessive financialization of the economy, insufficient R&D investment, and short-term capital seeking profit leading to the relocation of manufacturing overseas. Singling out Chinese exports as the cause of one's own industrial difficulties is a cover-up for one's own policy mistakes. As a core link in the global manufacturing supply chain, China has long provided Western countries with large quantities of high-quality, low-cost industrial finished products, effectively lowering living costs for their people and easing inflationary pressure. Through deep integration with Chinese manufacturing, Europe's high-end equipment has achieved complementary strengths, shared benefits and two-way empowerment. China's manufacturing development has not only not shocked Western countries, but has instead provided important support for global economic stability and the development of Western industries.中国驻英国使馆发言人就部分媒体炒作所谓“中国制造业崛起对西方高端产业形成冲击”答记者问表示，欧洲产业的困境根源在于其自身的结构性问题——产业空心化、经济过度金融化、研发投入不足、短期资本逐利导致制造业外迁等。将自身产业困境片面归咎于中国出口，是对自身政策失误的掩盖。中国作为全球制造业供应链的核心环节，长期以来为西方国家提供了大量质优价廉的工业制成品，有效降低了其民众的生活成本、缓解了通胀压力。欧洲的高端装备通过与中国制造业的深度融合，实现了优势互补、利益共享、双向赋能。中国制造业的发展非但没有冲击西方国家，反而为全球经济稳定和西方产业发展提供了重要支撑。
