How to use this comparison pattern如何使用此比较模式

One horizontal scale (Risk-off ←→ Risk-on) reused identically by five market rows. The marker takes its stance ink, never a category hue; travel uses muted measurement ink plus an explicit arrow — never --up/--down, which dodges the 红涨绿跌 flip (a travel figure means the same thing in both languages). Zone ticks at 25/50/75 are notches, not labels; the axis is anchored in words (Risk-off · Neutral · Risk-on), never in a bare 0–100. Subject market is the only marked subject — one per spine. Null row uses a dashed rail and a "No score" chip.

同一水平标尺（避险←→趋险）由五个市场行共用。标记取其立场墨色，绝不取分类色相； 移动值使用中性测量墨色并显式带箭头——绝不取 --up/--down，从而回避红涨绿跌翻转（移动值在两种语言下含义一致）。 25/50/75 处的分区为刻度而非标签；轴线锚定于文字（避险·中性·趋险），而非裸 0–100。 主体市场是唯一被标出的主体——每个脊柱仅一个。空值行使用虚线轨道与「暂无分值」徽章。
