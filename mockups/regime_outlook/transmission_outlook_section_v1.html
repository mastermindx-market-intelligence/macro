Mockup notes — not part of the page

  • Real: every reading on the nine cards and in "Now" is the 2 October 2026 read of the frozen mapping (contract revision 3.1, Appendix A.6). Card order is the contract's fixed order.
  • Sample: the "What changed" chips, the release dates under "Watch", and the "Previous read" date. The live section reads these from the projection.
  • Proposed position: after "How a shock travels", before "If this holds for three months". One include line on the page.
  • Light is the research workspace: white cards, hairlines, soft shadow, ledger on white. Dark is the command centre: no shadows, the ledger is a recessed well, filled marks carry a faint halo, the centre line fades at both ends.
  • No green or red anywhere: "fits" is not "good". Readings differ by mark shape, so the section also reads correctly in the Chinese colour convention and for colour-blind readers.
  • Open "Evidence and dates" on the first card to see how each row traces to its source and date. Only that card is filled in; the other eight use the same rows.
  • Cut on purpose: a row of summary marks in each card's corner. It made the nine cards easy to compare at a glance, which is the one thing this section must not invite.
