**Dark:** instrument-like, quiet canvas; secondary panels separated by hairlines and an inset top edge, not lifted white-card shadows. **Light:** cool research canvas with white material, disciplined borders and existing panel shadows. The summary has visible separation without relying on dark luminance effects. Type, hierarchy, ordering, accessible controls and the causal meaning are identical to dark.
