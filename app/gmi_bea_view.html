About this dataset

BEA Global Value Chains accounting allocates industry imports to seven source regions. These are estimated industry allocations, not observed company-to-company supplier links. The 2017–2024 history belongs to the May 2026 publication and does not establish what was known in earlier years. This view does not provide an investment ranking, entry signal or trade recommendation.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis · Publication precision: day · No historical point-in-time claim ·
