# Tiingo completed research corpus registration

Exactly four existing EOD/fundamentals L0/L1 contracts have authenticated original
receipts, actual intended-store artifacts and verified retrospective consumption.
Their `PRODUCED` status records this existence. The other twenty Tiingo contracts
retain their existing `PROPOSED` states and separate acceptance gates.

`accepted-corpus.json` binds this bounded snapshot to the original immutable
qualification and installed-reader evidence with document SHA256 values. Those
document hashes identify proof files; they are not raw vendor payload digests.
Raw lineage continues to join `source_sha256` to the original receipt's
`raw_sha256`, and `source_receipt_id` to its validated receipt identity. No price
or fundamental metric values, credential, private blocked handoff or raw refusal
body is included here.

Data & Lab contract discovery can now identify the produced research datasets and
inspect their declarations and lineage. Existing file reads were already available;
this registration does not confer a new access grant. The adapter retains read-only
access, `NOT_ESTABLISHED_BY_THIS_ADAPTER` production qualification and
`NOT_ESTABLISHED_BY_DECLARATION` availability. Its existing immutable installed
release remains a separate fact until a newly landed release is selected and tested.

The EOD acquisition campaign remains incomplete. The two fundamentals cohorts
serve three years despite a 1990 request start; they do not establish all-symbol
or deep-history account scope. Dated identity, historical upstream availability,
PIT/backtests, canonical market-price authority, continuous service, rights mapping,
BOATS publication and Terminal release remain unproven or unadmitted. Held failed
requests stay excluded without replay.

The independent architecture ruling accepted the descriptive-existence change.
It rejected an earlier review's invented full-history/PIT prerequisites for L0/L1
and its raw/qualification-digest equality claim. The actual provider and platform
refusals remain unchanged on their original carriers.
