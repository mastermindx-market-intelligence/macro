## 4. Detailed review findings and task amendments

### IR-01 — Unqualified comparisons must not become investment badges

The new shared warning can coexist with a `comparable` classification and an above/within/below-range position. M1 must retain the reported values, preserve the warning and avoid describing the outcome as a validated beat, miss, or improvement when the necessary definition is unqualified. This is a required consumer presentation rule, not permission to rewrite the shared helper.

Add a T03/T04 test where both definitions are null, the actual numerically exceeds the prior range, and the helper emits `definition_unqualified:definition`. The investor may inspect both values. No favorable badge, supporting headline or model-readable confirmed-surprise claim may be produced from that comparison. Also test fully qualified compatible inputs so the consumer does not suppress a genuine positive comparison. This strengthens MGD-16 and MGD-22 without replacing their test IDs.

### IR-02 — The reusable comparison unit is an economic observation, not a convenient number

For W-C, keep management's earlier point estimate a point estimate. No invented lower/upper range, no automatic Street-surprise label, and no isolated-productivity conclusion from a cost measure whose by-product assumptions changed. For W-R, source row, quarter versus half-year, elimination sign and GAAP/non-GAAP basis travel together. A correct numeric span without its label/header cannot prove the selected measure.

T02 must test a valid literal appearing in both Q2 and H1 columns, a negative parenthesized number and a positive style attribute. The selected cell and the period/definition context must be replayable or the fact is held. T03 must test that derived arithmetic binds all consumed revisions, not merely the issuer or event ID. These are obligations for the existing receipt/financial owners, not a new Mining parser framework.

### IR-03 — Prevent whole-corpus context loading from becoming the default worker task

Each bounded implementation packet carries the plan plus addendum, exact permitted paths, the relevant research sections and a deterministic gate. A worker implementing a receipt test does not need eight full sector reports. Fable reads the index and selected cases deeply, resolves cross-owner decisions and requests additional source sections only for a material uncertainty. Do not trade reduced worker context for omission of the source footnote that determines correctness.

### IR-04 — Preserve shared version identity and meaningful regression

Freeze legacy input bytes, revisions and accepted outputs under their actual version. A v1.1 source statement must not be renamed v1 for compatibility. Unknown versions refuse. For a deliberately changed interpretation, current answers may change with named evidence while earlier cutoff answers remain reproducible. Tests that regenerate expected hashes using the candidate alone are not independent legacy-parity evidence.

Extend T01/T07 coverage with unknown version, unchanged v1 replay, a changed source with stale interpretation, and a later mapping that must not appear before its system-recorded cutoff. The forty MGD obligations are the floor; the review examples explain how to test them, not forty more invented release gates.

### IR-05 — Public access and permission to republish must remain separate

The shared `sec_edgar` registry disposition cites a public-domain rationale. The Industrials consumer raised a narrow qualification in comment `5810041817`: federal hosting alone does not establish federal-employee authorship. Official USAGov guidance explicitly warns that federal websites can contain protected third-party material; the Copyright Office's definition concerns official employee authorship. [E1–E2]

Mining adopts this as a source-use qualification, not as a ruling that every filing fact is restricted or that all EDGAR analysis must stop. The existing rights owner must distinguish factual synthesis, permitted excerpts, links, original-body retention, verbatim body display and bulk redistribution. Do not rely on a blanket government-work assumption for new full issuer/exhibit-body publication. A logged-in reader and house-authored paraphrase do not automatically settle upstream reuse rights. No registry edit, legal approval or license acquisition was performed in this review.

### IR-06 — A deferred shared task must have a useful next action, not an idle principal

Before the shared profile is delivered, permitted independent work is Mining content/field mapping, native-source qualification through its owner, domain conformance cases against the frozen candidate, and a rights-safe demonstration specification. None requires taking over the base. Once those bounded units are exhausted, return a compact exact dependency state through the existing owner; no principal or new watcher should poll unchanged PRs.

At integration time, Fable consumes current accepted output, not a historical promise. It may proceed directly with disjoint, lawful tasks when no active/pending modifier is displaced. It must preserve any unknown or STARTed effect on its original carrier and may not create a replacement execution merely to escape a blocker.

### IR-07 — Handing over M1 must not silently discard the sector mission

The first two positive cases are a release slice. Keep the prior roadmap for asset/project depth, contractual rights, commodity balances, power and processing economics, supplier exposure, expectation history and long-tail commodities. Record M1 acceptance separately from full Mining completion.

Prioritize the next expansion by the investor question it unlocks and the false-positive it prevents. A missing contract balance holds that calculation. A materially different business model needs principal research before its claims are admitted. Do not ask routine subagents to rediscover the sector or silently promote speculative research into trading policy.
