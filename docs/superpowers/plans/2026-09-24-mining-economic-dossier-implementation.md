- Consume the one shared GMI foundation; no Mining copy of assertion, identity, graph, financial/expectation, correction, rights, publication, queue, scheduler, watcher or auth owners.

- A same-year guidance revision is not a next-period outlook. Management estimates, consensus and house forecasts remain distinct.

- Preserve #7870, #7462, #7669 and #7331 source custody. An absent reply, stale checkpoint or green CI does not release a writer.
- Both W-C and W-R require populated real economics, source evidence, correct company navigation and counter-thesis. Optional derived numerics/consensus may degrade; a wholly empty economic result fails.
- Broader Mining coverage remains in scope for later increments. M1 is neither a global company census nor completion of the full mission.
