The investor must understand an industrial business's economic change, connect it to profit, cash and reinvestment, inspect the evidence, identify a credible counterargument and return to the existing theme/company workflow. The machine must retain source scope, dates, identities, definitions and revision dependencies rather than provide a persuasive but uncheckable narrative.

Better operating evidence does not automatically produce a higher multiple, a price target, a confidence probability or trade authority.

## 5. The semantic cell is larger than its number

A usable numerical input needs an exact value together with governing row/column context, source unit/scale, period, business dimensions, denominator and statement mode. A byte receipt over the characters `68` does not establish any of those meanings. W11-R03 distinguishes address-only table positions from replayable spans; do not promote one into the other. Use the existing financial/source owner to preserve a complete addressable cell and its governing headers, or a validated structured receipt that supplies them. No extra generic parser is prescribed here. When the source cannot support a complete interpretation, keep the exact source label and qualification rather than standardizing by guesswork.

## 6. Freeze comparisons by purpose, not a misleading equality rule

A year-over-year comparison necessarily uses different literal dates. Requiring all dates to be identical would reject the intended task; requiring only equal period ends would admit quarter-versus-half-year mistakes. The later native implementation must select an explicit comparison purpose and verify its dimensions.

For year-over-year flow growth, require the same reporting entity and measure definition, corresponding fiscal durations, compatible consolidation/recast basis, currency treatment and disclosed units. Preserve actual endpoints and material differences in reporting weeks. For a margin, numerator and denominator need the same economic scope and period. For a segment bridge, include corporate/eliminations and disclose non-additive overlaps. For sequential final-versus-preview arithmetic, hold the target fiscal outcome fixed but keep both publication stages and source dates.

Unit conversion is an explicit lossless transform where supported, not a silent magnitude correction. Per-share, consolidated, segment and customer measures are not substitute denominators. Zero or negative bases need a defined display convention; do not emit an ordinary growth percentage that reverses the economic interpretation. Show absolute changes or a named alternate calculation instead.

The paired Exponent adjustment keeps both expense and income legs. Removing equal legs preserves pretax profit; it does not identify cash earnings or all recurring earnings. Pentair's reclassified Flow/Water Solutions comparisons are distinct from Pool, which the source says is unchanged. Current-basis analysis may use supplied recasts, but an earlier historical view cannot use them before their qualified availability. [W11-S01; W11-S02]

## 8. Information time and current disclosure policy are different axes

Current explanation uses the latest selected accepted sources and states their periods. Source chronology reconstructs documented publication sequences without claiming system retention at those dates. Native replay requires the owner's retained generations, code/inputs and qualified availability clocks. An unknown publication instant never becomes midnight because a helper accepts a date.

A later filing can improve today's interpretation without updating what the system says was knowable on release day. Any synthesis requiring that filing has no earlier evidence eligibility than its latest necessary input. A published fiscal endpoint, signature, scheduled call and file-refresh time retain their actual roles.

Permission is evaluated at the time of disclosure, including for an old historical view. Keep historical lineage but deny disallowed content now. Rights expiry is not a reason to rewrite source history, and historical access does not override a current revocation.

W11-R05's path-keyed cache means a same-path file update is not inherently a new policy observation. Before shipping this role, the existing rights/access owner must prove its policy-version or reload contract in the actual serving process, including multi-process behavior where relevant. An isolated research cache clear is not that implementation or proof. No current leak is alleged and no new policy service is proposed.

## 9. Failure handling and publication coherence

Authenticate and authorize before opening private content or testing private record existence. Source-use permission is a separate required check; a public-emission function is not automatically a private-member entitlement policy. Unknown policy or unproven required permission fails closed for the affected disclosure.

After those gates, distinguish missing record from unavailable store, invalid digest/schema, unresolved identity, incompatible calculation and unavailable optional evidence. Do not let a corrupt generation masquerade as an empty dataset. Do not keep a dependent summary when a required fact is withheld. A pre-admitted separately publishable section may remain useful, but the first implementation may conservatively refuse a whole affected record until the owner supports safe section-level omission.

Publish complete immutable dependencies and verify readback before the existing pointer promotion. On a failed new generation, do not advertise its values alongside an older generation's interpretation. Any fallback to a previously admitted generation requires current rights, valid receipts and visible age; it is not an automatic escape from an integrity failure. Browser responses never expose internal storage errors, private locators or credentials.

Logout, entitlement revocation and rights withdrawal must be included in the actual release tests. Browser no-store headers alone do not demonstrate every cache and retained DOM is cleared; verify supported client behavior without inventing another auth framework.
