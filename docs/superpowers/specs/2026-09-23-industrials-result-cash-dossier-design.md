## 1. Outcome and the first complete user job

A signed-in investor investigating an industrial business should be able to explain what changed, distinguish demand from accounting or reporting effects, trace earnings into cash and investment, inspect the evidence, and understand what would overturn the interpretation. The investor should return to the incumbent theme/company workflow without reconstructing the company from a stack of reports.

The machine job is to preserve scope, source mode, measurement definitions, dates, ownership, revisions, uncertainty and the exact supporting cells. It should calculate only qualified comparisons and expose failed prerequisites without filling them with plausible numbers. It must not originate, rank, gate, size or execute a trade.

The first reference pair is Exponent and Pentair. They test professional-service economics and a diversified equipment business, respectively. Together they require reimbursement denominators, paired expense/income effects, cash conversion, segment reconciliation, preliminary versus final information, and missing consensus. They are research examples, not investment selections or newly admitted basket constituents.

Completion of the first product slice means both real dossiers work on the authenticated production path, source corrections propagate, an authorized reader can complete the task, and unauthorized or alternate-public access cannot retrieve the private-current payload. Green tests, a schema, a static mockup or a page filled with illustrative data does not meet that outcome.

## 2. Design decision and alternatives

**Rejected: encode everything in the current transcript context or public Company Intelligence highlights.** W10-R06/R07 are closed contracts with specific semantics. Making a release look like a transcript would be false even if it passed an API parser. Fronting an existing public object with a paid API would not make the object private.

The selected approach still requires explicit native work. The absence of shared support is a dependency, not permission to fork it. Implementation planning must reconcile the then-current Robotics/shared-template outcome before choosing changed paths. Existing product owners, not this research document, admit the native changes.

## 5. Source and measurement semantics

A successful evidence drawer must let the reader answer who said it, what it describes, which period and business it covers, where it is found, when it was published/observed, how it was transformed, and what it cannot establish.

Required meanings, supplied through the appropriate existing owners:

| Dimension | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Statement mode | Reported final, preliminary result, management forecast, management attribution, external estimate, derived arithmetic, house hypothesis or hypothetical sensitivity |
| Fiscal scope | Exact start/end where known, issuer fiscal convention, quarter versus year-to-date, comparative perimeter and restatement/reclassification basis |
| Units | Currency, scale, per-share basis, stock versus flow, percentage versus percentage points, reported precision |
| Business scope | Issuer, segment, product/customer scope when actually disclosed; segment performance is not narrow theme revenue |
| Source lineage | Native source/revision, exact table or span, upstream publisher, retention/rights status and correction predecessor |
| Calculation lineage | Exact operand references, formula/rounding version, matching-basis judgment and paired adjustment links |
| Identity | Source-native label separately from canonical company/security/listing and the effective-date proof |
| Limitations | Missing basis, publication grain, source conflict, insufficient denominator, expired review, unsupported identity or blocked use |

No amount can be silently zero-filled. A zero is an asserted measured value and needs evidence. A missing number has no sign, does not contribute to averages, and does not become a neutral confidence score.

Reported, company-adjusted and researcher-adjusted measures remain separate. The UI must show the original reported bridge even when an analytical comparison is useful. A paired remeasurement has linked expense and income legs; it is not automatically a cash adjustment. A measure called free cash flow by one issuer does not acquire another issuer's formula. A contribution to a period's profit change is not a stock beta or valuation weight.

## 6. Exponent dossier: two information editions, one retained lineage

### 6.1 Release-level view

W10-S01 can support a current reading of the quarterly operating result and disclosed guidance. The opening explanation should distinguish underlying professional-service revenue from reimbursements and avoid describing the entire top-line increase as improved service economics. The evidence table displays the issuer's net-revenue denominator alongside total revenue, with the quarter and comparative fiscal endpoints.

There is no full cash statement in the selected release. The release-only edition therefore displays a specific cash-detail absence. It must not fill that section with a later filing while retaining a July 30 knowledge label. The stale historical cache is a negative qualification example, not a substitute consensus source.

### 6.2 Filing-enriched view

W10-S02 supports an additional paired-adjustment explanation and a six-month cash reconciliation. Its signature is August 7; exact acceptance timing remains unqualified in this research. The filing-enriched edition must carry the actual owner receipt when acquired. Until then, it is a paper reference case, not a historically replayable native generation.

The paired sensitivity increases reported operating income by the corresponding deferred-compensation expense and decreases other income by the equal gain. It leaves pretax income unchanged. It is explicitly a researcher calculation isolating one matched effect, not an issuer-defined adjusted EBIT measure, comprehensive normalization or estimate of recurring profit. This protects the reader from both over-crediting operating leverage and unfairly removing income without its related expense.

The cash section uses the six-month statement, not the quarter's income denominator. It can explain working-capital contribution and the complete change in cash while separating financing uses from operating conversion. Cash after the statement's capital payments is a named researcher proxy, not automatically the issuer's free cash flow or cash distributable to shareholders. Segment capital additions cannot replace cash payments simply because both contain the words capital expenditure.

The opening synthesis should answer four questions: What operating activity improved? How much of the reported change needs accounting qualification? What happened to cash conversion and investment? What evidence would distinguish temporary growth funding from persistent collection deterioration? Customer retention and exact end-market profit remain unknown when undisclosed.

## 7. Pentair dossier: diversified operating result and an intervening warning

The timeline preserves W10-S03 as a preliminary disclosure and W10-S04 as the final report. A finalized value above the preview is not an external-consensus beat. A pre-warning estimate cannot be called latest without a retained revision chain and matching basis. Do not substitute management's preview for analyst expectations.

The business panel decomposes the quarter's segment-income change, retains corporate items and shows the prior-period reclassification basis. The point is to distinguish where the current earnings change resides; it is not to attribute each segment to a newly invented theme or customer. Corporate effects and tariff refunds remain visible. Without an evidenced allocation, no segment's margin is declared to exclude the refund.

The cash panel uses comparable windows. A large positive quarter can coexist with a weaker half-year result. The issuer's own free-cash definition includes the reported treatment of capital payments and property proceeds; the calculation retains that formula. A quarter and a half year may appear side by side for context, but not as a misleading growth pair.

The explanatory alternatives are customer demand versus channel behavior, mix versus underlying pricing/productivity, and timing versus enduring cash improvement. Management's destocking estimate is an attribution to investigate, not a measured final-demand series. Announced same-day acquisition news is shown as another event; it neither changes historical ownership nor disappears from any later price-attribution study.

## 8. Calculation and interpretation policy

The accompanying research verifier demonstrates selected arithmetic, not the production implementation. The later native derivation must refuse incompatible entity, period, currency/scale, fiscal perimeter or measure basis. Any explicit normalization preserves its original operands and definitions. Do not make an incompatible comparison legal by relabeling its output.

Examples that the accepted native calculator must reproduce from its own qualified cells include: reported versus paired operating-profit changes; the invariant pretax result after removing matched expense/gain legs; operating cash less stated cash capital payments; cash opening-to-closing reconciliation; segment plus corporate change; first-quarter plus second-quarter reconciliation to half-year cash; and final-versus-preview arithmetic labeled as such.

No direct fair value or return forecast is produced in the first slice. The valuation section may explain the mechanism and enumerate the cash, capital-return, duration, risk and expectation assumptions required for a later scenario. It must not assign a theme multiple, price target or probability from narrative confidence. Missing prices, forecast vintages or canonical security bindings keep the numerical valuation/reaction section unavailable.

A full later valuation must reconcile enterprise and equity claims, per-share effects, capital requirements and timing. A stronger mechanism is not proof that it is unpriced. Existing evaluation and trade authorities retain all decisions and validations.

## 9. Information time, corrections and replay

The visible timeline distinguishes fiscal period, source-publication day/time, first observation, owner retention, house-curation publication and display generation. These are not interchangeable freshness labels. Dates remain dates when time or timezone is unknown. A conference-call schedule, filing signature, cache refresh or commit date cannot invent a publication instant. The date-to-midnight conversion supported by a helper in W10-R08 is not evidence that a source was published at midnight. The accepted producer must preserve the upstream grain or an explicit unknown rather than send invented precision through a formally valid timestamp field.

Separate **source chronology**, reconstructed from documented publications, from **system historical replay**, which requires retained native generations and qualified clocks. Retrospectively reading a dated webpage does not prove the system held it at that date. The two public information sequences can be explained without advertising them as a completed backtest.

The compiler consumes the native owner's actual availability rules. Unknown clocks cannot pass a historical cutoff merely because their fiscal period ended earlier. Same-day date-versus-timestamp ambiguity must remain held where the existing K1 contract refuses it. A later filing or estimate revision cannot update an earlier dossier in place.

Corrections append the native successor, preserve the predecessor, invalidate affected calculations, and generate a new projection receipt through the existing owner. Missing one required operand holds that calculation, not an unrelated valid panel. Source withdrawal or rights revocation must suppress access to affected content under the current rights owner; historical existence does not override current disclosure restrictions.

## 10. User experience and degraded states

The shared theme-detail page gains an economically organized research entry, not another timing board. Existing holdings, rankings, entry labels and charts remain unchanged. A reader opens a business result and sees: a concise conclusion with caveat; the business/period and source editions; demand/profit/cash evidence; comparisons that passed their prerequisites; the strongest alternative explanation; and a source/limitations drawer. No arbitrary basket slug or new membership is required to present a research example.

On smaller screens, evidence becomes stacked labeled rows without losing units or periods. Tables remain keyboard accessible; meaning never depends only on red/green color. Numeric summaries and prose are produced from the same validated operands, not separate browser calculations. Translations must preserve signs, units, statement mode and missingness.

Degradation is panel-specific. Unsupported consensus should read, in substance, 'No matching dated external estimate is available; the operating result can still be inspected.' Unqualified cash should identify the missing period/source rather than display zero. A storage or digest failure is different from a genuinely absent record. An unresolved listing permits a source-label view where rights allow, but disables price joins and canonical company links. Authentication failure returns no private body.

The first pair must each contain useful synthesis and a discriminating next observation. Sources without interpretation fail the user job; interpretation unsupported by sources fails the intelligence job. A source-only degraded example is valuable negative proof, but does not substitute for the two fully identity-bound operating dossiers required for first-slice acceptance.
