## 1. Decision and intended outcome

Select **owner-composed Economic Change Dossiers within the existing sector/theme/company journey**. The product has two connected jobs: discover which granular Technology mechanisms are changing, and understand what those changes mean for a particular business's paid activity, financial expectations and retained economics. A company earnings-comparison panel alone does not satisfy the full mission. A larger collection of theme labels or source citations does not satisfy it either.

The investor should be able to move from a theme or a company to a clear answer: what the business sells, who pays, which economic mechanism changed, how strongly the evidence applies, what was already expected, what could invalidate the interpretation, and where the incumbent stock/entry workflow stands. The machine must preserve those meanings over time and across corrections, source definitions and regional reporting conventions.

The first bounded delivery proves the entire journey on **software management-outlook conversion**, using the Datadog source pair developed in R2, plus an incompatible-basis case that refuses the calculation while keeping the underlying evidence readable. Physical demand, reporting containment, geographic materiality and conditional valuation are successive capabilities using the same native ownership and shared presentation. They are not collapsed into the first PR.

Long-term coverage remains all fifteen Technology ex-Semiconductors families from R1: horizontal applications; vertical/transaction applications; data platforms; infrastructure/developer software; security; cloud/internet infrastructure; engineering/creative/scientific software; IT/engineering services; devices; enterprise compute/storage systems; networking; non-semiconductor components; electronics manufacturing/design; distribution; and electronic measurement. Coverage is explicit and staged, not assumed complete because a representative issuer exists in each family.

### 1.1 Alternatives and trade-offs

**A — Editorial evidence panels only.** This is useful for admission and basic company understanding, but does not remove the investor's burden of reconciling measures and expectations. Keep it as a truthful partial state, not the final product.

**B — Native-owner composition. Selected.** Existing source, financial, event, identity, theme, evaluation and publication owners retain truth. This design adds scoped native extensions where needed and a bounded derived read model. It can ship a useful question without first resolving every company or acquiring institutional consensus. Its cost is careful compatibility and source admission rather than a quick stand-alone database.

**C — Universal first-class product/economic graph immediately. Not selected.** It would offer richer queries but would force new identity, storage and rights commitments before a useful vertical. Preserve source-scoped objects, explicit predicates and versioned mappings so existing owners can later promote identities after evidence and migration review. Do not build that future graph as a hidden prerequisite.

Written-spec approval permits the executable implementation-plan stage. ## 4. Thematic discovery without a second signal engine

The sector view exposes the existing structural hierarchy alongside approved granular research facets. Its purpose is to answer where relevant economic change is being observed and what remains unmeasured. It reads accepted ThemeState, leadership, regime and timing from their existing owners; it does not compute substitutes.

A facet-level research brief contains the question, economic buyer, paid workload, enabling/dependent products, relevant company roles, source-backed changes, contrary evidence, required future observations, and coverage. Adopted causal relationships remain with existing relationship/propagation owners; shared theme membership alone cannot produce a causal arrow.

Use distinct views for physical containment, commercial relationships, budget flow, observed financial scope and conditional transmission hypotheses. The user can select a view; each edge says what relationship is asserted and by whom. Unknown weights yield an unweighted graph and equivalent accessible table. No edge width is invented to make the diagram visually complete.

### 4.2 Lifecycle and horizons

Narrative emergence, pilots, accepted deployments, paid use, reported financial results and competitive response are separately evidenced observations. A universal sequence is not imposed on all industries. An existing owner may provide a lifecycle classification; absent such a classification, show the observations rather than invent another state machine.

Structural, business-cycle, tactical and entry views stay separate. A strong structural mechanism can coexist with weak recent demand or extended entry timing. The shared federation's scope/timeframe/freshness/coverage/authority distinctions explain the difference. No new red/green verdict supersedes the native entry state.

### 4.3 Surfacing and proactive delivery

The first view sorts new accepted material changes by their disclosed change/publication semantics, with stable deterministic tie-breaking and user filters. It is not a return forecast or a stock rank. New capture of an old source must say it is newly incorporated evidence, not a newly occurring business event.

An existing watchlist, alert or briefing owner may consume the bounded change projection after its contract accepts it. No new Technology watcher, scheduler, retry queue or notification store is introduced. No alert should imply a trade. A corrected input can invalidate a prior explanation; the notification must identify correction rather than present it as independent confirming news.

## 8. Management-outlook comparison: exact question and arithmetic

### 8.1 Required sets

Let `A` denote the earlier information cutoff and `B` the later one. Let `C` be periods already completed and reported at A, `N` periods newly completed between A and B, and `R` the remaining periods of the same fiscal year. The source-defined annual measure must be additive over these periods.

`F_A` and `F_B` are the comparable annual guidance midpoints at A and B. `P_A` and `P_B` are sums of actuals for C known at A and B. `G_A_N` is the sum of the earlier management guidance midpoints for N. `X_B_N` is the sum of actuals for N known at B.

The earlier implied remaining amount is:

`R_A = F_A - P_A - G_A_N`

The later implied remaining amount is:

`R_B = F_B - P_B - X_B_N`

Therefore:

`R_B - R_A = (F_B - F_A) - (X_B_N - G_A_N) - (P_B - P_A)`

This is an algebraic decomposition of guidance and reported results. It is not a forecast of realized future revenue or Street-consensus surprise. The midpoint is a conventional point derived from the supplied range, not an expected value with a probability distribution.

### 8.2 Eligibility before arithmetic

Require the same issuer/business perimeter or an explicit comparable recast; same fiscal year and additive metric; complete non-overlapping C/N/R period partition; compatible nominal/constant-currency treatment; matching accounting/recognition basis; valid guided and actual units; known source versions and allowed information cut; finite ordered ranges; and no missing required input.

A zero actual is legal if genuinely reported. Missing actuals are not zero. Annualized endpoints such as ARR, ratios, margins, EPS, backlog stocks and cumulative balances cannot use this additive revenue formula. A 52/53-week or fiscal-calendar change must be represented explicitly rather than normalized away.

If N has no earlier guidance, show the annual revision and current actual separately, but do not compute the completed-period contribution using a guessed estimate. If an old actual was later revised, retain both P_A and P_B. If the annual scope changes after an acquisition, a raw same-label subtraction is insufficient; use an accepted comparable bridge or refuse the organic interpretation.

### 8.3 Results and labels

Display annual midpoint change, newly completed-period deviation from the earlier management midpoint, earlier-period restatement contribution, and the residual implied remaining-year change. Each has the same selected unit and exact formula trace. Direction does not become a trade label. Negative results may be valid.

The first version reports midpoint decomposition only. It does not publish an uncertainty interval by subtracting independently treated guidance endpoints whose dependence is unknown. Future scenario intervals require explicitly declared admissible combinations and cannot be presented as probability bands.

Datadog is the positive development witness from R2. The exact two releases and completed-period actuals must be reacquired/admitted by the native source owner and recomputed from its retained observations. Do not copy the R2 number into a fixture and call that source-to-product proof. Include a synthetic incompatible-period/definition case and the R6 Nemetschek acquisition-perimeter case as negative design witnesses. No fabricated issuer identifier is supplied in this specification.

### 8.4 Information content and evaluation

Given the full same input vector and definitions, this deterministic bridge contains no additional conditional information. It cannot become another independent evidence vote beside its own input sources. It may improve comprehension or the representation available to a restricted model.

R5B H1 is refined accordingly: compare a simple restricted baseline, a complete-input baseline and the engineered representation with compatible populations, capacity and tuning. Include the newly issued guidance, not only a stale forecast. Report representation/task improvements separately from new information, operating prediction and tradable returns. Hand-selected R1–R6 examples are development data, never the confirmatory holdout.

## 9. Physical, materiality and valuation extensions

### 9.1 Physical demand and retained value

Keep units, system configuration, recognized revenue, bought-in content, retained gross profit, working capital and cash in distinct native observations. The HP development case must show revenue and reported units side by side; a derived revenue-per-reported-unit ratio is an aggregate proxy, not pure ASP or demand volume.

Celestica HPS is within CCS in the researched disclosure. Enforce typed containment and do not sum a parent with its child. The same applies to Samsung SDS cloud within IT services, overlapping Sage recurring views and platform subsets elsewhere. A hierarchy graph is not a disjoint financial allocation.

For a capacity hypothesis require matching product/configuration, customer or application qualification, place, time, unit, usable yield, demand and alternatives. A source-only target remains a target. The simple minimum-capacity scenario in R3 is a pedagogical case, not an admitted factory solver or new capacity engine. Quantitative prediction waits for the actual qualified-output data and the existing relationship/evaluation owner.

### 9.2 Materiality and geography

For compatible nonnegative total T and category marginals A and B, a missing intersection X can satisfy `max(0,A+B-T) <= X <= min(A,B)`. This bound needs identical periods, entity scope, accounting perimeter and currency and a valid total/partition. Validate `0 <= A,B <= T`. Signed accounting rows, eliminated intercompany values or incompatible segment totals require a different model and cannot use this rule blindly.

A compatible directly disclosed intersection takes precedence. Independence allocation `A*B/T` is a scenario, not a disclosed intersection. A bound is not a probability interval. Preserve published rounding residuals and declared precision rather than force tables to add exactly. Ship-to, customer domicile, deployment and production locations remain separate.

### 9.3 Conditional valuation

Consume the existing accepted financial, price, capital-claim and scenario owners; do not introduce a Technology valuation truth store. Display operating assumptions, reinvestment, financing, dilution, ownership and terminal conditions. Operating value and common-equity value are distinct. A missing valid equity bridge refuses per-share output without invalidating a correctly scoped operating scenario.

A reverse solution is conditional on its model, not a uniquely measured market belief. No synthetic probability, target price or rank is implied. Current company-specific valuation needs actual contemporaneous price/claim inputs and applicable authority; the fictional R4 laboratory is not an input source. Buybacks spend cash as well as reduce shares. Acquisition-led growth includes compatible acquisition funding. Equity compensation and dilution must not be omitted from both or charged twice.

## 10. Time, correction and historical modes

Every native timestamp retains its owner meaning and precision. Distinguish upstream publication, public knowability, system observation/recording, business-effective date, fiscal reporting period, computation and review. Date-only values are not silently promoted to midnight availability for intraday decisions. Unknown clocks remain unknown.

The first delivery defaults to **current accepted evidence with visible earlier source versions**. Historical public-information reconstruction and original-system replay are optional capabilities only when their native requirements are met. A source published before a cut but captured after it cannot prove the original system possessed it. A current model analyzing an old document has a separate model-era/hindsight limitation. A current event list cannot establish the historical denominator.

Distinguish these changes:

- A corrected document replaces or amends a statement in a source revision chain.
- A newly issued management outlook is a new forward statement even if it concerns the same target year.
- An actual result resolves a reporting period; it is not itself a correction to earlier guidance.
- A changed metric definition or acquired perimeter may require a new comparison regime.
- A source withdrawal or changed rights posture restricts current access without allowing the historical claim to be silently rewritten.

Use the existing event identity and revision owners. One logical issuer event can have multiple documents and issued-statement versions; do not mint a new event merely because the source bytes changed or duplicate an event for another listing. The selection receipt must distinguish source-event identity from the target period of guidance.

A dependent result records exactly which input versions it used. Corrections produce a new derived revision under the same publisher, mark the earlier view as superseded for current use, and retain reproducibility where permitted. Content returning from A to B to A must not collapse historical lineage into the first A. No ad hoc latest-state file, correction queue or alternate retry engine is added.

## 12. Freshness and failure behavior
