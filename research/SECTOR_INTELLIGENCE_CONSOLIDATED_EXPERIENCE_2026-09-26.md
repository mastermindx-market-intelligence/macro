## 2. One journey, at two levels

**Sector Central** is the group-discovery workspace. Preserve its existing parent
contract: Rotation / Discover / Market breadth, with one selected group and contextual
Overview / Members / Cycle access. **Sector Intelligence** is the expanded detail of
that same selected object, not another unrelated dashboard.

The expanded detail uses one local destination set:

**Overview · Companies · Signals · Drivers · History**

- Overview: the group's assessment, visual supporting evidence, material caution and
  next investigation.
- Companies: the complete owner cohort, searchable/comparable, with coordinated company
  inspection. A small overview preview never substitutes for the full population.
- Signals: source-issued states and horizon differences in plain language, with exact
  scope available. No newly fused confidence score or trade permission.
- Drivers: source-grounded business/catalyst evidence; a meaningful unavailable state
  when absent, not fabricated orders, pricing or narratives.
- History: actual dated observations and changes. No invented curve, replay or performance.

Evidence is contextual: a Sources control or value-level explanation opens a consistent
source panel and returns to the prior location. CI, pull-request, implementation and
admission records belong to operator/development review, not customer navigation.

This does not enact the older proposed global IA migration. Reuse the currently accepted
product-header family and inventory; no new global header, account system or route owner.
Existing URLs remain valid. Concrete cross-product URL bindings must be verified before
implementation; a plausible URL is not a working Company Intelligence route.

### Persistent context and return behavior

Carry market, canonical group identity, parent/subgroup, benchmark, date/window, selected
company and filters through applicable tab transitions. A subgroup selection must visibly
change scope, not silently relabel its parent's measures. Back restores the original
object and return position. Escape closes the current disclosure first. Keyboard focus
returns to its opener; touch supports the same actions without hover dependence.

## 3. Primary viewport composition

The visual hierarchy is **one assessment → one useful comparison → one next step**,
not an equal-weight grid of explanations.

### A. Orient

A restrained breadcrumb establishes Sector Intelligence / market / group. Put one
snapshot/as-of indicator beside scope. The actual group name is the page title, not a
generic instruction such as "What matters now". Reuse the shared shell rather than
spending a large portion of the screen on a custom banner.

### B. Answer

One short, dominant assessment is derived from available owner fields. For the stored
Sep-23 Technology demonstration, the intended reading is **leadership is strong,
participation is selective**. This is a dated design example, not a current market call.
Support it with a small aligned metric set, not explanatory paragraphs: relative
strength, participation and return, each carrying its exact period and population.

The broad risk view remains a compact contextual line rather than another hero competing
for attention. Local strength and market risk are allowed to differ. No global score
is invented to reconcile them.

### C. Show

The Semiconductor pocket provides the next level of investigation. Show relative-company
performance on a shared zero axis and keep absolute return in an aligned column. Selecting
a row coordinates one company inspector. Bars encode actual values, not decorative trend
lines. Do not call six illustrative overview rows the full cohort.

### D. Continue

The primary supported next step is **View companies**. **Compare signals** is the
secondary route for the entry/regime disagreement. These are research/navigation actions,
not a recommendation or execution promise. A downstream Company Intelligence or save
control stays explicitly unavailable until its real existing route/state owner is bound.

### Copy and material treatment

Default subtitles, descriptions and generic how-to text are removed. Use 28–38px answer
hierarchy, legible labels and aligned numerals under the existing type/token system.
Avoid tiny low-contrast footnotes, rainbow status cards, heavy borders around every idea,
and large empty expanses used to disguise missing hierarchy. Dark and light keep the
same meaning and flow while retaining their distinct existing material treatments.
