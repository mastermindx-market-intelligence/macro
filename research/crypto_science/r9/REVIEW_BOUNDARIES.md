This note identifies the consequential seams a separate reviewer must inspect before any production promotion. No reviewer is assigned, running or claimed to have accepted them.

1. Sampling and conditioning: issue times are calendar-determined, not backfilled from future incidents. The preceding24hno-D0condition is past-only. It defines an advance-to-break watch, not a cycle peak and not an absence of all prior selling. Missing-state windows remain unknown. Source-stage eligibility and future target maturity must not be confused.
2. Information timing: only candles closed by issue and the incumbent target available under the declared delay may become features. Stored source timestamps do not attest first publication. A six-hour schedule can miss fast episodes; do not present it as continuous or tick-level surveillance. Actual source latency, outages and instrument rights remain unproven.
3. Three clocks: observation issue, delayed action opening and target expiry. Model output depends on a delayed-reference label; alert-action leads must be positive to earn advance-to-break credit. Entry before a barrier is not necessarily before the first selling. No impossible peak fill or retroactive cancellation of an issued warning is allowed.
4. Two event definitions: the warning's own5%-before3%target and the independent damaging-D0catalogue are not the same denominator. An own-window hit need not match the catalogue; catalogue recall must include unsupported cases in its unconditional denominator. One warning/event cannot be multiply credited.
5. Missingness and rare-event scoring: ambiguous intrabar order is unscored; zero warnings have undefined precision. Clock rows overlap and confidence must not assume they are independent. The exposure-month denominator is conditional observed watch time, not total calendar time, so monthly coverage/counts also matter.
6. Training: raw model and recent-rate comparator use the same eligible, matured, embargoed training rows. Class counts, clipping, means/scales and fixed penalty are frozen. Hyperparameter or alarm-threshold choice based on this run would invalidate its comparison. A better base-rate forecast does not prove event-specific timing skill.
7. Economics: identical warning selections across fees, incumbent exposure already held, drift between target changes, entry and restoration costs, common expiry and no hidden future allocation. Reset per-warning returns are not an annualized compounded portfolio. Exiting alreadycash accounts is not a correctly forecast avoided loss.

No dashboard probability, risk-on/off authority, trade, paid-source or runtime collector is promoted by these research outputs.
