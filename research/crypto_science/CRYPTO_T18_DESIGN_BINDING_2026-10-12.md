## 2. Quiet styling versus the doctrine

House law: a glance must show the signal in plain words (`docs/DESIGN_DOCTRINE.md:9-15`). A null is stated on tier 1, not hidden in jargon (`:91-102`). Missing data is unavailable, never a calm substitute.

Flag these. Meaningful source failure has to stay visible.

1. R2 contract "Crypto H5 canonical allocation states" says the unavailable design "deliberately avoids a red alarm treatment" and keeps the layout stable. That sentence is about not painting a bearish alarm. It is not permission to fade the words. The candidate paints the crypto empty chart and the vector replay block in `--muted` (`templates/crypto.html.j2:107-108`, `templates/vector.html.j2:131`). The words are in the accessibility tree (`role="status"`). T19 must keep that text at body size and contrast, not only "not red".
2. Per-row missing history is 10px and `--muted` (`templates/crypto.html.j2:171`). At 320px, beside a hidden State column (`:274`, under 720px), "Building history" / "积累历史中" is the only statement that history is absent, and it is the quietest text on the row.
3. A null BTC-volume percentile is labeled "Normal" / "正常" (`engine/crypto_market_state.py:203-206`) while the number is `—`. Quiet-as-healthy. Design reference NONE.
4. A null class 30-day change is labeled "Range-bound, waiting for participation" (`engine/crypto_market_state.py:179-183`). That is a market sentence standing in for missing data. See §1.1.
5. Exclusion reasons sit inside a closed `<details>` (`templates/crypto.html.j2:345`). The summary count is visible ("Review excluded observations" / "查看未纳入的观测"). That is disclosure, which the doctrine allows, as long as the summary count and the "No ranked snapshot" line stay outside the disclosure.

`templates/illus.css:403-407` already forces `.ilx-path` to `stroke-dashoffset: 0` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. `templates/vector.html.j2:323` also sets `.ilx-path,.ilx-bar{animation:none}`. Reduced motion must not hide a status: the unavailable blocks are not inside that animation.
