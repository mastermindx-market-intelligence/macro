## 1. Root cause and why it matters

At Macro `ff3b55d3e8935d236c03cf4d74223a18fbe52b53`,
`engine/special_situations.py` has full-file blob
`4e79fc0b22045917bcb1110092ad402187cddef0`.

`build_situations()` copies `llm_role` into `role`, but it first treats every

role, or a non-target role can become a direct-stock situation. The role is not
literally deleted; **eligibility ignores its compatibility with the category**.
