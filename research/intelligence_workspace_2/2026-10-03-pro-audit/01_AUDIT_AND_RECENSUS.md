## 1. Verdict

The report is a useful architectural synthesis, but is not sufficiently precise to serve alone as the executable contract for Astra CEO. Retain its strongest decision: research identity belongs above visual composition, while facts, published theses, alerts, portfolios and domain calculations remain with their current owners. Retain visible context, source clocks, competing explanations, historical beliefs, explicit writes and deterministic-first reopening.

Do not accept its claim that this is the only possible architecture, its unsupported numerical ranking, its generic-owner assumptions, or its implicit equivalence between immutable manifests and recoverable history. The replacement program below corrects these issues without commissioning another research run. [U01; U02]

The decisive recommendation is **a thin Investigation capability inside the existing Terminal user-state responsibility, with retained owner-native references and explicit capabilities**. A new table may be appropriate for a new semantic entity, but a table name is not an ownership ruling. The first delivered outcome is a real, private, authenticated saved-and-resumed investigation with one evidence baseline—not a schema-only milestone and not a universal research database.

## 4. Material findings and dispositions

### A01 — Immutable manifest does not preserve referenced content — critical

The report proposes immutable Investigation revisions and live/pinned references, then promises baselines, fixed shares and forks. The inspected layout service overwrites the current config under CAS; it does not supply the historical read needed by those promises. A hash proves identity, not retention or recoverability. [U02 L456–490; S08]

**Correction:** distinguish inquiry revision, saved-as-seen baseline and known-then replay. Add retention capabilities to owner adapters. Require layout-owner-native retained revisions/snapshots before claiming a historical layout. Sharing a fixed manifest does not promise immutable readability of every source. Forks must detach mutable composition through owner-native copy-on-write or duplication before editing. Tests T06–T09, T17–T19 and T24.

### A02 — W1-C is security-only and has a specific pin owner — critical

The current compiler explicitly accepts only security entities and carries edge symbols; Terminal's provider always returns `pinned: []`. The report's theme/regime/event/scenario examples cannot simply be passed into that unchanged contract. [S10–S11]

**Correction:** extend the same compiler through a versioned, negotiated contract while preserving v1 behavior. Do not create a second compiler. Investigation primary subject is not automatically a Brain pin. Canonical security IDs must be mapped by the existing identity owner, not inserted into symbol fields. Tests T10–T13.

### A03 — Generic owner labels hide real missing work — major

The report admits that complete Evidence, Scenario, Alert, Vault, Market Ontology and Prophet internals were not line-audited. This audit narrows several gaps but still does not establish universal retention, stable read-by-version APIs, or a durable Scenario lifecycle. [U02 L808–810; S17–S20]

**Correction:** G0 produces exact adapter cards: owner, identity, read interface, authorization, retention, temporal capability, correction behavior, cost and active carrier. Missing capabilities become explicit owner extensions and acceptance gates—not invented endpoint names or a placeholder called an existing/future owner.

### A04 — Thesis coverage is narrower than generic hypotheses — major

Current subject kinds are issuer/theme. Routing every regime, event, portfolio or policy belief into it without contract changes is invalid. [S12]

**Correction:** published beliefs remain with Thesis. Extend its canonical subject contract when a required journey needs additional subjects. Investigation notes and open questions may persist as research intent, but do not silently become a second formal Thesis lifecycle. Competing formal hypotheses are multiple versioned Thesis references. Test T20.

### A05 — Source-admission and production grading overreach — major

A repository comment saying a migration was applied is historical evidence, not direct current schema verification. A component not being a generic inquiry object is a product-fit distinction, not evidence that its actual implemented capability is unproven. Missing current operational projection is not evidence of no live owner. [S02–S03; S14–S16]

**Correction:** separate source grade, implementation capability, dated deployment proof and current user-path proof in every receipt. Do not promote a test fixture, source annotation, merged PR or Paper board into `PROVEN_LIVE` for a broader capability. Tests T30–T32 and gate G0.

### A06 — Architecture scoring is not reproducible — major

The report labels 2.7, 4.8 and 4.3 as weighted judgments without publishing weights or a scoring rule. Their unweighted means are approximately 2.71, 4.71 and 4.00; therefore the weighting cannot be reconstructed from the displayed table. This is unsupported precision, not proof of arithmetic fraud. Its universal-graph alternative also conflates a typed graph of references with a new universal graph database. [U02 L169–179]

**Correction:** compare strongest feasible alternatives qualitatively, including a Thesis-rooted extension and a typed graph over existing owners. Select the thin Investigation on explicit invariants, reversible cost and measurable product benefit—not invented numerical certainty. ADR-01 in the masterplan.

### A07 — Analyst-action data was semantically reassigned — major

The report recasts the old analyst-action-data wave as semantic Investigation provenance. The original docket separately refers to licensed analyst-action breadth, which is financial research data, not the operator's click/research history. [U02 L619; S06]

**Correction:** preserve licensed analyst events as an owner-native data consumer with source/rights gates. Treat semantic analyst activity history as a separate research-continuity feature. Neither substitutes for the other. Downstream disposition D08.

### A08 — Idempotency and CAS lack an atomic contract — critical

`expectedRevision` plus a request ID is not sufficient unless ownership, payload identity, atomic state advancement and replay behavior are defined. A client UUID alone is not a completed recovery protocol; current saved-view code illustrates the distinction. [S12–S13; P01]

**Correction:** scope the request to principal, operation, target and canonical payload hash. Commit head/revision/receipt atomically. Same key/different payload is a typed conflict. Replayed authorized requests return the original result before new-CAS evaluation. Ambiguous response means read/reconcile the original operation, never mint a fresh key and duplicate the mutation. Tests T02–T05.

### A09 — Rights must follow derived outputs — critical

The report correctly protects source resolution but under-specifies AI summaries, titles, counts, export files and inherited fork text. An inaccessible source can still leak through an already-generated summary. [U02 L496–498; S17]

**Correction:** apply owner-native policy to input and derived artifact reads, exports, shares and forks. Record effective restrictions without copying credentials. Re-evaluate authorization; suppress disallowed metadata as well as payload. Treat retrieved content as untrusted data, not authority to issue tools or writes. Tests T22–T25.

### A10 — Time needs two different historical questions — critical

Publicly available at time T, ingested by Mastermind by T, and actually shown to this user by T are not identical. Neither saved_at nor a current corrected value answers all three. [U01 temporal requirements; U02 L443–466]

**Correction:** capture separate validity/publication/owner-version/platform-observation/user-capture clocks where available. A baseline records the vector actually captured; replay declares its knowledge policy. No current-only source is silently substituted. Missing retention is explicit. Tests T17–T19.

### A11 — Evidence disappearance is not necessarily removal — critical

A timeout, first-page-only response, changed top-K query, revoked entitlement or different cohort can look like removed evidence. A single flat diff enum is insufficient.

**Correction:** separate content revision, membership change, qualification, availability and interpretation. `REMOVED` requires an authoritative tombstone or successful complete comparable-set evidence; otherwise report unavailable, scope changed or not comparable. Background refresh never advances the saved baseline. Tests T14–T16.

### A12 — Ephemeral runtime and durable intent need separation — major

The report correctly rejects persisting every bus event, but saving no effective desired bindings can break meaningful resume. Follow/pin/local modes, intentionally pinned values, selected timeframe and replay policy are durable user intent when explicitly saved; event IDs and causal traffic are transient.

**Correction:** persist desired configuration through its declared owner and reconstruct runtime state from it. Use a per-session reducer, explicit causal suppression, late-result guards and atomic context bundles. Local user clicks are serialized, not made into a modal conflict for every near-simultaneous action. Shared saved edits use CAS; another person's viewport does not automatically follow. Tests T10–T13.

### A13 — The first implementation is too substrate-only — major

Identity alone closes an engineering gap but does not validate the renewed thesis. Forcing all semantic work behind a completed evidence-review wave also imposes unnecessary serialization. [U02 L695–708; L731–769]

**Correction:** G0 contract/owner admission, then one retained, authenticated, changed-evidence resume vertical. After contract freeze, separate context, adapter, design and QA lanes can proceed in parallel on non-overlapping surfaces. Preserve full end-state scope; the first vertical is not permission to stop at MVP. Gates G0–G9.

### A14 — Experience completeness needs actual journeys and recovery — major

The report's representative one-row examples do not define enough behavior for creation, local draft loss, fresh-session return, source revocation, wrong-route context, collaborator divergence or closure. This is particularly risky given existing context lifecycle and receipt deduplication incidents. [S09; S16; P01]

**Correction:** seven journey scripts, all entry-point noun mappings, draft conflict/recovery states, explicit archive/reopen, and a domain acceptance denominator. A polished library and a backend table alone cannot pass. J01–J07 and G8–G9.

### A15 — Design host and accessibility assumptions need correction — major

R10 contains light/dark and bilingual specimens; Terminal's admitted doctrine is dark-only. A 320-pixel/200%-text specimen does not cover all reflow or accessibility obligations. [P01; S21; E10]

**Correction:** follow the destination host's doctrine, preserve EN/ZH and canonical tokens, test 320 CSS-pixel reflow including the relevant 400% zoom setup separately from 200% text resize, and verify keyboard/screen-reader/touch recovery. Do not invent a new theme system. Test T28.

### A16 — Performance targets are uncalibrated — major

The 500 ms shell and 1.5 s first-fact figures are proposed budgets, not current measurements. Old W1-B numbers cannot be presented as current production latency. Reopen must not await deep AI. [S05; U02 L629–665]

**Correction:** define the observation point, network/device, data size, cold/warm status and sample distribution; benchmark existing harnesses; partition caches by owner, principal/entitlement, query and temporal version. Add fanout and cancellation tests, not a universal fact cache. T26–T27.

### A17 — Research support is directionally useful but not causal validation — major

External product documents establish advertised workflows, not the superiority or uniqueness of this design. Human-AI research warns that explanations alone need not prevent overreliance. The report's prior-session citations are not a portable source bibliography; FinChat also needs current Fiscal.ai naming. [E01–E09]

**Correction:** use the reconstructed source ledger, mark unrecovered background references, and test the product hypothesis against ordinary saved layouts. Measure resumption accuracy, historical/current distinction, contradiction recognition and workload; do not claim investment performance from a usability study.

### A18 — End-to-end release and retention operations were underspecified — major

Feature flags, migration ordering, data deletion, source retention expiry, backup/restore, revocation cache behavior, schema compatibility and rollback must be part of the architecture, not deferred cleanup.

**Correction:** G9 requires exact deployed identities, reversible feature exposure, no destructive rollback of user revisions, existing alert/Thesis regressions, real authorized account proof, and an operational runbook. No numerical adoption target becomes an unsupported scientific claim. T29–T32.

## 5. What survives the red team

The revised program retains question-first continuity without making every dashboard a research project. It preserves historical human belief and dated AI artifacts without calling them current answers. It preserves frozen layout v1, owner-native facts, explicit alert/watchlist/portfolio actions and existing Brain orchestration. It rejects opaque vector-memory persistence, automatic thesis publication, inferred confidence scores, generic fact caches and uncontrolled graph/queue/ACL duplication. These are stronger decisions after the audit, not concessions to the current fragmented UI.

## 6. Evidence and research-quality conclusion

The report is **good enough as a design hypothesis and background**, not as an unmodified build contract. This packet makes the recommended architecture and implementation sequence usable while exposing the remaining source/production gates. A complete byte-level audit of every adjacent owner and a current production census were not performed. Those limitations are assigned bounded commissioning work in G0 and the relevant domain gates; they are not filled with assumptions or used as a reason to restart broad research.

No statement here asserts that the product is implemented, deployed, live, scientifically validated, or that Astra has received or accepted this handoff. Publication and consumption are different states.
