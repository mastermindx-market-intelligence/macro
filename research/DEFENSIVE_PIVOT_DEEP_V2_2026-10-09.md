# Defensive Compounders — Deeper Strategy and Methodology Assessment

Research date: October 9, 2026. Final price session: October 8, 2026.

## 1. Final adjudication

The deeper tests change the research priorities. WM's original daily negative-MACD recovery deserves a higher priority than its newly optimized EMA strategy. MCD's sparse original reclaim is preferable to its expanded-grid winner. COST's daily failed-breakdown/reclaim is promising as a bounded recent-era recovery trade, not an all-regime lifetime entry rule. CTAS offers a modern daily RSI(2) candidate but fails an older-cycle challenge. PG's apparent three-day optimum is fragile to bar alignment and its daily MACD result is exit-dependent. WMT has not earned a specialized pivot-timing policy against its holding benchmark.

These are research priorities, not live trading approvals or a backtested allocation. No exact bottom/top detector, universal optimum or prospective trading advantage is claimed. This bounded deeper assessment is complete; live qualification is not. No orders, position sizes, production signals, installations, scheduled tasks, merge or deployment were performed.

## 2. Scope, method and selection

The 18-stock universe is MCD, WMT, WM, COST, PG, RSG, KO, PEP, CL, CHD, KMB, MDLZ, HSY, CLX, JNJ, ADP, CTAS and KR. The original 44 Yahoo snapshots were preserved and hash-checked. A separate acquisition added 18 earlier daily snapshots and 44,906 observations beginning in 2000; backward evaluation uses 2001–2009 after available warmup. Current-survivor and corporate-lineage biases remain. This earlier challenge is not prospective evidence.

The expanded registry contained 12 families × three settings × four frames × two stock gates × three exits = 864 complete policies per stock, or 15,552 stock-policy trials, replayed over 16 calendar-year segments. These correlated trials are not independent validation samples. Families: failed breakdown/reclaim, negative MACD, earlier volatility-normalized MACD recovery, RSI(2), RSI(14) recovery, Bollinger reentry, EMA pullback, Donchian breakout, StochRSI, MFI, a low-anchored daily-bar volume-weighted proxy, and causally confirmed bullish divergence. Frames: daily, two-day, three-day and calendar week. Gates: unrestricted or above daily 200SMA with rising daily 50SMA. The volume-weighted proxy is NOT trade-level VWAP; volume rules quarantine 260 sessions after splits.

Static selection used only 2011–2022, requiring 40 trades, eight active years, seven profitable years and positive compounded returns in each six-year half. Score: mean annual return minus half annual-return standard deviation minus 0.10 times worst annual drawdown magnitude. Six-year rolling selection used completed prior years only, requiring 20 trades, four active/profitable years and positive returns in both three-year halves. No eligible policy means cash; no gate was loosened.

The recent period had already been inspected in the preceding session. These new designs are therefore exploratory chronological replays, NOT a fresh blind 2023–2026 holdout. Earlier-only selection prevents that specific leakage path but does not erase prior exposure to the history.

### Execution model

Separate fully funded single-stock accounts, no leverage, one position at a time, zero yield on idle cash and ten basis points round-trip costs. These are research normalizations, not recommended sizing. Entries use next-opening prices after completed-bar signals. Initial stops use three prior-day daily ATR below the entry. Gaps through stops fill at the opening price; intraday breaches use the modeled stop; entry-day stops are possible. Trails use completed closes and ATR, not an unfinished candle's high.

Core exits: initial 3ATR stop plus 20-completed-session next-open timeout; the same plus daily RSI(14)>=60 next-open exit; or prior-completed-close 3ATR trail with 63-session cap. Year-end liquidation is explicit, with a continuous-position sensitivity. The initial study's twentieth-session closing-price exit is NOT this extension's 20-session next-open timeout. Different entry counts and portfolio returns must not be misrepresented as revisions of earlier event win rates.

### WM: original daily MACD is a stronger research priority

The original recovery policy's positive older challenge is more encouraging than the new daily EMA winner, which lost 7.23% annualized with 63.98% drawdown in 2001–2009. WM's recent raw-price/cash-dividend variant returned 8.84%, versus 7.24% for the adjusted-price proxy. This alternative recalculates the indicators too, so the difference is not solely a dividend-accounting correction. The holding benchmark returned 9.70% annualized with continuous exposure; timing superiority is not established merely by comparing different exposure levels.

A bounded recovery hypothesis is better supported here than automatically extending every recovery to a multi-month momentum hold. Recent original-rule returns across initial 1.5/2/3/4/5ATR stops were 2.79%, 4.73%, 7.24%, 6.72% and 7.83%. This is a positive neighborhood, not permission to optimize by selecting the highest cell.

### COST: recent bounded reclaim, not immutable personality

The recent original reclaim returned 10.88% annualized with 6.71% drawdown and 26.2% session exposure. The alternate raw-price/cash-dividend model returned 9.36% with 11.27% drawdown. Its recent annualized outcomes stayed around 9.68%–11.05% over the tested initial stop widths. But the older -8.44% annualized and 61.05% drawdown disqualify an all-regime claim.

The longer new StochRSI trade is a different strategy. Its annualized outcomes changed from -1.72% at a 1.5ATR trail to +5.92% at 2ATR, +19.07% at 3ATR, +19.47% at 4ATR and +21.92% at 5ATR. These are not equal-risk comparisons. Its gains were concentrated: +113.68% cumulative in 2023–2024, then -9.94% cumulatively in 2025–partial 2026. The periods differ in length and are not comparable annual rates.

### MCD: prefer sparse price rejection to generic oversold recycling

The original reclaim's modest +3.90% recent annualized return, 8.19% drawdown and raw-price/dividend result of +3.86% are more defensible than the expanded StochRSI winner's -10.01% and 43.46% drawdown. Its older +2.58% result still involved 32.65% drawdown. Keep it as a selective recovery candidate, not a high-certainty pivot detector.

### PG: no stable three-day optimum; stop and exit unresolved

The three-day RSI(2)<10 strategy produced +5.81% annualized in the original grouping phase, -3.63% when grouping began one trading session later, and +2.09% in the third phase. Choosing the favorable phase afterward introduces another optimization. A bar anchor is part of a strategy, not cosmetic chart formatting.

The original daily MACD with no initial stop and a 20-session next-open timeout returned 6.31% annualized with 19.57% drawdown. Adding the initial 3ATR stop produced only 1.07% and 28.94% drawdown as exits and reentries altered the path. Stops do not mechanically guarantee a smaller whole-account drawdown. Original-rule results at 1.5/2/3/4/5ATR were 3.75%, 2.91%, 1.07%, 2.69% and 6.31%. The prior 72.2% fixed-horizon positive-event fraction is not an executable tight-stop win rate.

### WMT: opportunity cost dominates oscillator selection

The expanded selected policy was approximately flat versus 26.66% holding. A simple above-200SMA timing benchmark returned 21.64% annualized, without a lower drawdown than holding in this period. A within-family daily RSI(2)<10 research leader returned 16.19%, still below holding. These are not current-price buy recommendations. Avoid forcing a dip-only strategy that systematically misses the stock's advances; specialized timing remains unqualified.

## 6. Indicator and methodology checks

Twenty-two distinct unit tests passed on both local and research runtimes: smoothing, RSI boundaries, completed bars, causality, confirmed divergence timing, gaps, next-open/delayed execution, entry-day stops, nonanticipating trails, fees, limits, nonoverlap, year-bounded selection and dividend entitlement. There were 5,184 real-history signal prefix checks, all passing. Perturbing post-2022 annual grid results did not change static earlier-only selection.

Independent expanded weighted-sum RSI/ATR formulas and direct-loop MFI/CMF identities checked 270 historical dates, 1,080 numerical comparisons, with maximum errors approximately 3e-14. Eighteen copied stop-multiplier engine checks matched the native engine exactly at its original 3ATR setting. These prove mechanics, NOT forecasting skill. Internal dividend-adjustment ratios showed no discrepancy above ten basis points across 18 stocks; this is not independent-vendor validation.

The alternative split-price/cash-dividend engine credits ex-date receivables only to prior holders, not new ex-date buyers. Receivables remain nonreinvested within each year; actual settlement/payment dates are not modeled. Indicators are recalculated on the alternative price basis. Differences mix signal, execution-coordinate and accounting effects, rather than measuring dividend accounting alone.

Extra confluence did not automatically improve performance. Positive CMF20 changed the chosen PG strategy from +5.81% to -1.03% annualized; positive 63-session relative performance changed it to -5.69%. Positive CMF changed KO from +10.12% to -1.61%. These are post-selection ablations, not another independent optimization opportunity. Related oscillators are not independent confirming votes.

## 7. Chance versus strategy selection

Each stock received 499 annual circular-shift placebos under the same exit policy. Single-rule tail fractions included KO 1%, PG 3%, RSG 5%, COST 6.2%. The shifts preserve schedule clustering but not regime membership or the executed trade count; these fractions are NOT calibrated p-values.

A separate centered circular-block maximum-t diagnostic compared all 864 policy columns to a retrospective exposure-matched stock benchmark over 15 complete quarters, using 999 resamples with 1/2/4-quarter blocks. None of the selected policies established reliable selection-adjusted advantage in that diagnostic. This is not claimed as Hansen SPA, a full Reality Check implementation, or a calibrated prospective test. Non-significance does not prove that every candidate is worthless. It does prevent treating one favorable table as statistically established skill.

Older-period failure invalidates a universal personality claim, not every possible contemporary use. The business, valuation and trading population can change. However, retrospectively inventing regime labels to discard losses is another form of fitting. Added simple stock-trend gates did not consistently repair older failures.

## 8. Next qualification and limitations

Prioritize frozen prospective paper evaluation of WM's daily MACD recovery, MCD's sparse reclaim, COST's recent-era bounded reclaim and CTAS's modern fast-RSI setup. Preserve separate recovery-trade and trend-hold exits. This prioritization is adjudication, not a new simulated portfolio allocation.

Log completed-close signal time, earliest executable order, observed spreads/opening slippage, known earnings/events, initial invalidation, exit rule, dividends and cash yield. Evaluate subsequent favorable/adverse moves rather than only hindsight-low proximity. An ATR stop cannot guarantee its fill through a gap. No 15/30-minute or continuous overnight winner was established by this daily extension.

Limitations: single public vendor, current-survivor universe, unverified point-in-time vendor vintages, no historical earnings/event controls, no taxes/financing, zero cash yield, no cross-stock capital competition, no broker-fill proof, limited microstructure and repeated reuse of recent history. The attempted secondary public vendor returned browser verification, not prices. No account credential was read or entitlement assumed.
