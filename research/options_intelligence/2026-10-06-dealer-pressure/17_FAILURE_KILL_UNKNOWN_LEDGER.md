# Failure, kill and unknown ledger

**Research cut: 2026-10-06.** A rejected interpretation, an unproved source, an inconclusive experiment and a failed production gate are different states. This commission performed source research and synthetic mechanics checks; it did not run a market backtest or make a new empirical alpha decision.

## 1. Interpretations that should not enter the implementation

| ID | Claim or shortcut | Status and reason | Evidence / consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | OI uniquely identifies signed dealer inventory | Structurally non-identifiable | Opposite dealer-capacity assignments can generate identical public trade/OI observations; report03 and witness |
| K02 | Ask-side execution means customer opening purchase | Invalid identification | Aggressor, capacity and opening/closing are separate; passive customers exist [R04] [R07] |
| K03 | Volume above prior OI proves new positions | Invalid accounting inference | Repeated turnover/closures can produce arbitrarily high volume; report03, current legacy-label caveat [I12] [R18] |
| K04 | Buying to close is irrelevant to dealer hedge change | Invalid signed-position arithmetic | Closing a short raises signed holdings just as opening a long does; report06 |
| K05 | Change in dollar delta equals hedge-trade notional | Invalid without revaluation separation | Endpoint target units must be differenced before valuation; report07 and synthetic witness |
| K06 | Futures notional is cash spent | Invalid instrument accounting | Separate contracts, reference notional, margin and variation P&L; report 08 |
| K07 | Positive gamma automatically defines a magnet | Unsupported topology | Damping is not anchored inward drift; report09 |
| K08 | A high control ratio proves dealers caused the move | Unsupported causal claim | Uncertain execution, endogenous liquidity and common news; report12 |
| K09 | A historical dark print identifies accumulation | Unsupported participant/intent inference | Reported execution and delayed ATS context do not disclose beneficial ownership [I16] [A46] [A47] |
| K10 | Every product switches from forecasting to nowcasting at 15:50 | Wrong venue model | Arca, NYSE and Nasdaq have different phases; report11 [A01] [A13] |
| K11 | Latest-dated exchange PDF is the deployed rule/schema | Wrong version inference | NYSE current/upcoming catalog and 2026-36 technology gate [A07] [A09] |
| K12 | Later OI or corrected data can repair an earlier live feature | Point-in-time leakage | Later information only enters later filtering/smoothing; report16 |
| K13 | More/wider/redrawn zones demonstrate better extreme prediction | Invalid evaluation | Frozen formation, width/distance-matched controls and proper scores required; report10 |
| K14 | A vendor replay is necessarily the historical model as seen | Unproved vintage | Input history and current-engine recomputation differ; report04 |
| K15 | One-minute refresh establishes one-minute fresh signed inventory | Unsupported freshness inference | Position inputs, Greeks and display clocks must be separated; report04 |

These are restrictions on interpretation, not assertions that the associated descriptive measurements are useless.

## 4. Future falsification and stop decisions

| Trigger in an authorized later study | Decision |
|---|---|
| Mechanical conservation, unit or causal-clock failure | Stop scoring; repair the affected source/model through the existing owner |
| Adequate power rules out useful gain from the scoped participant benchmark | Stop the corresponding costly reconstruction family |
| Confidence interval still contains a useful gain | Inconclusive; diagnose coverage/power before broader build |
| Gain disappears with true release/consumer latency | Restrict horizon/use; faster data needs a separate value-of-information case |
| Raw flow or liquidity explains the entire gain | Prefer the simpler validated input block |
| Partial signed inventories flip the pressure/node label | Scenario-sensitive or unavailable; no single authoritative label |
| Gain only in corrected history | Research finding only; captured PIT validation needed |
| Stress/close non-inferiority is unproved | No decision-policy promotion; report subgroup uncertainty |
| Rights exclude planned embedding or competing terminal use | Do not integrate under that agreement; alternative rights/source required |
| No improvement after multiple registered variants | Close the failed family and preserve negative results rather than inventing a new score |

Report 16 supplies the single primary endpoint, hierarchy, proposed useful-effect hurdle and inferential distinctions. No source-quality badge or illustrative chart can override those decisions.
