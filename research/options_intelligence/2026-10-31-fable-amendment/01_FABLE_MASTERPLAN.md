# Adaptive completion masterplan
## Options Alpha Intelligence / Intraday Flow · October 31, 2026

### 1. Mission and intended result

Finish one coherent user journey: **notice activity → understand the evidence → examine exposure and volatility → select an exact contract → author a conditional Plan → preserve the research → watch a stated condition → review what happened**. Carry the same source identities, assumptions and uncertainties through every step.

The user should be able to answer five questions without interpreting implementation details: What happened? What evidence supports it? What is observed versus assumed? What could this particular option do under my assumptions? What later happened to the research I saved?

Predictive capability is an additional, separately earned result. The programme should produce useful descriptive and mechanical functionality even when a forecast family fails or remains statistically inconclusive. It should never manufacture alpha to make a completion report look better. The existing C0 separation, candidate policy, OA3 ruler and research designs already support this distinction. ### 2. Give Fable freedom with a clear claim boundary

Fable may change the order of independent work, combine or split bounded leaves, choose the most capable admitted operator, simplify an interface, propose discarding a weak feature, develop a better hypothesis, or revise this design. A material removal of an already accepted programme obligation requires an explicit scope decision by its existing decision owner. A justified negative result, a simpler descriptive release, or removal of misleading functionality can all improve the programme.

Do not freeze a speculative architecture merely because this report describes it. The recommended designs are decision-ready starting points, not acceptance by themselves. In particular:

- A responsive layout can differ substantially from the explorer if it preserves the user's task and the source truth.
- A cohort may change from the illustrative SPY path if another currently qualified population provides the better first journey.
- Exact budgets, concurrency and task size should follow observed host capacity and return quality.
- Component boundaries should follow the existing repository's ownership and actual dependency graph.
- New research may be added with a versioned question and population, without quietly changing a preregistered primary.

The fixed boundaries are narrower: preserve existing owners and unresolved effects; do not convert absent evidence into observed facts; do not mix retrospective reconstruction with captured availability; do not equate heuristic ranking with calibrated probability; do not promote a family without its own accepted evidence; and do not call source tests production acceptance. These are the conditions that let the team work autonomously without losing trust.

Prefer precise negative findings. “Private reference installation has no qualifying terminal receipt” is actionable. “Plan blocked” hides the difference between an accepted engine, merged consumer and missing runtime proof. “Research unavailable because no actual fills exist” is more useful than a global percentage complete.

### 10. End-to-end completion standard

Close a capability only when its applicable ladder is satisfied:

1. **Definition:** user outcome, population, source/claim classes and owner are explicit.
2. **Implementation:** exact source and integrated dependencies exist, with independent review appropriate to the risk.
3. **Qualification:** current-head required checks pass and meaningful negative cases demonstrate the intended boundary.
4. **Installation:** authorized release/migration/retained-source effects have exact terminal receipts and installed identity.
5. **Consumption:** authenticated real-path proof demonstrates the actual user result under relevant natural source state.
6. **Operation:** the normal schedule/retention/correction/recovery behavior is observed over its agreed window.
7. **Scientific claim, when applicable:** the sealed family-specific empirical result qualifies the claimed target and population.
8. **Continuity:** the incumbent ledger records what is accepted, what remains unavailable, and what would invalidate the conclusion.

A descriptive feature may not require step 7. A model does. A negative study can be complete as research without becoming a prediction feature. A release-held source patch remains unfinished as production capability. A documented gate can justify pausing a dependent lane or completing this planning commission; it does not complete a required empirical or product obligation. That obligation remains open until its accepted result exists or its existing decision owner explicitly revises the programme scope. Programme closeout requires the promised admitted journeys and all remaining in-scope obligations to be accepted, with scientific limitations truthful and visible; it does not close on a large merged-file count.
