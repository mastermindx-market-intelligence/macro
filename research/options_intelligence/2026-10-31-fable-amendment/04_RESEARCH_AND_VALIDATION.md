# Research and validation plan
## Continue the accepted science; do not manufacture new authority

### 1. Research conclusion

The evidence supports building a strong descriptive and conditional-mechanics product now. It does not establish that the current Options score, exposure picture or candidate set predicts profitable option trades. The remaining scientific work is primarily source admission, causal availability, empirical binding, prospective capture and family-specific evaluation.

The checked Flow gate is a dated unscored history-building artifact. Candidate policy v2 and OA3 retain explicit activation/evaluation boundaries. The six-pilot design has a later engineering acceptance but no filled empirical seal. Q01's methods decision is already closed under O-A. These facts should determine the next experiments. [R18–R24](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#r18) [R09](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#r09)

Do not claim a null result for an unrun study. Do not claim validation because a numerical engine is correct. A source can be operationally healthy yet scientifically inadequate for a particular target.

### 2. Research evidence ladder

| Evidence question | Necessary witness | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Is the calculation correct? | Independent analytic/rational/numerical oracle with domain failures | Current source availability or predictive edge |
| Is the source measured correctly? | Identity, units, conditions, clocks, corrections, denominator and source receipts | Useful forecasting value |
| Could the system know it then? | Actual input/feature/publication/consumer availability before decision | Tradability or causal impact |
| Does it add target information? | Prespecified same-cohort comparison against the declared baseline | Actual option execution profit |
| Does the estimate calibrate? | Disjoint evaluation and appropriate support/dependence analysis | Universal probability across roots/horizons |
| Is the economic outcome evaluable? | Registered exact contract, eligible quotes/fills/costs and complete attrition | That a hypothetical quote was filled |
| Is the live capability reliable? | Installed natural operation and authenticated consumer proof | Historical OOS performance |
| Is promotion justified? | Original family-specific authority decision and rollback/drift contract | Permission for unrelated models or trading |

Use separate verdicts for artifact health, evaluation readiness, statistical acceptance and promotion eligibility, as already required by the preregistration. [R20](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#r20)

### 3. What primary research changes in the design

**Trade signing is a measurement problem.** ThetaData documents quote/trade pairing and an exclusive option for strictly preceding quotes. The documented v2 and v3 interfaces have different defaults. Pin the actual endpoint/version and ordering behavior; never infer deployment semantics from another version's documentation. [W01](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w01) [W02](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w02)

**Opening demand is not generic public flow.** Pan and Poteshman's result uses specially classified buyer-initiated opening transactions. It cannot be imported as validation of a public ask-side print heuristic, a package guess or a modern 0DTE strategy. The practical implication is to preserve the measured variable and test the actual available feature. [W03](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w03)

**Gross options volume is not net dealer pressure.** Cboe's SPX0DTE analysis distinguishes customer-flow balance and net exposure from gross activity. This supports separating observed volume, assumed-book exposure and causal hypotheses. It does not validate a particular Mastermind hedging model or establish the same result for every market. [W04](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w04)

**Better labels exist with narrower coverage and rights.** Cboe's current TBT product documents explicit side/open-close/capacity and execution linkage, delivered T+1 for C1. Open-Close covers selected Cboe venues and announces November 8,2026 schema/correction changes. These can inform source qualification or a future approved calibration dataset, but neither is whole-market real-time ground truth. Existing historical demo populations must remain separate. [W05](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w05) [W06](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w06) [R48](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#r48)

**Repeated search creates selection bias.** The primary backtest-overfitting and multiple-testing literature motivates trial accounting, sealed comparisons and untouched evaluation. It does not install a new universal threshold or justify changing the accepted programme's weight/support law after seeing results. [W12](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w12) [W13](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w13)

**Contract terms define the economic model.** OCC distinguishes exercise style and settlement conventions at the product level. An expiry intrinsic chart, an American exercise model, an exact NBBO outcome and an actual fill are different objects. Qualify the contract and the requested output before choosing a model. [W14](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#w14)

These are primary-source implications, not claims that external research proves this product's performance.

### 5. Data qualification before modeling

Build a source-feasibility report through the existing source and research owners. It should bind each required quantity to an actual retained source, units, history, availability and permitted use. Reuse the OPT-011 population work and existing AD/campaign/receipt contracts; do not create a parallel inventory service.

A minimum source qualification covers:

- Exact root/contract identity, option symbol, strike precision, multiplier, deliverable, currency, exercise and settlement.
- Source universe requested/configured/captured/qualified, with exclusions and unobserved mass.
- Event, quote, receipt, publication, consumer availability and correction times.
- Condition/cancel/correction rules and ordering at equal timestamps.
- Bid/ask validity, quote age, sizes, crossed/locked/zero cases and nonstandard sessions.
- Underlying reference basis, corporate actions, rates/dividends and versioned mappings when the target needs them.
- Source rights for the exact use: private research, product display, retained derived reference or export.
- Point-in-time population membership, history depth and terminal source gaps.

Keep source data quality and feature eligibility separate. A99.4% usable-NBBO statistic in one observed population does not establish the strict quote timing, Greek completeness, contract terms or current consumer latency required by a pilot.

If a requested source is not qualified, produce the smallest useful diagnostic or negative feasibility result. Do not lower a frozen scientific gate to fit the currently available data.

### 6. Point-in-time contract

For a decision at t, every required input must have a qualified availability time no later than t. The maximum required availability time is a useful eligibility test; it is not a replacement for retaining each original clock.

Distinguish at least:

- market event time;
- provider/source response or receipt time;
- feature construction and publication;
- first qualifying consumer admission;
- frozen selection/decision;
- label event time and label availability/cutoff.

A later repaired artifact may contain a market observation from before t, but it was not necessarily available at t. A current cohort cannot replace historical membership. Same date is not same revision. Late source correction must not retroactively change what the decision saw without creating a clearly separate restated analysis.

Audit joins as carefully as models: campaign revisions, settled OI, board membership, corporate-action adjustments, reference terms and source fixes can all introduce future information. Retain source/policy/population hashes with results, and use the existing quarantine mechanism to withdraw authority without destroying the original incident evidence.

The 103,893-row dated gate contains zero non-null published_at. That is a concrete warning against claiming historical operational performance from that artifact alone; it does not forbid every declared retrospective development use. [R24](06_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#r24)

### 11. Empirical seal and run audit

Seal the study in stages under the original owner gates. Before development fitting, fix the design, source/population admission, chronology, primary target/loss and validation/power procedure. Authorized training then produces frozen models and held-out forecasts. Matured validation losses are legitimately used for the already registered power calculation; they must not select features, bounds or hyperparameters. Only then freeze the resulting test length and final test boundary, before any final-test outcomes are inspected. V1 permits no post-validation refit. This avoids a circular requirement for a power result before the training/validation needed to compute it.

The stage-specific binding dossier includes:

- source and population versions, exact eligible-date range and all exclusions;
- actual historical feature/board/consumer-availability evidence;
- training, validation, calibration and final evaluation windows;
- horizon-aware purging/embargo and block rules;
- frozen baseline/feature/model/scaler/solver parameters;
- primary loss, effect margin, test family and multiplicity treatment;
- missingness rules before fitting, then the power/feasibility result after all required validation labels mature and before the final test;
- exact immutable input and executable identities;
- independent review and designated empirical approval.

Keep every tried alternative in the existing research record. A failed numerical optimization is an explicit refusal or failed run, not a reason to introduce an unreported pseudoinverse, clipping, fallback floor or favorable seed.

Report point estimates, uncertainty, population/coverage, attrition, effective dependence, registered primary decision and relevant stress tests. Make “insufficient,” “inconclusive,” “negative” and “positive within this scope” distinct outcomes. Avoid a bare pass/fail summary that hides the actual target or uncertainty.

### 12. Scientific falsifiers

A candidate family should lose the corresponding claim if:

- reproducing a decision requires a later publication, repaired reference or retrospective cohort;
- quote/location classification depends on an unavailable later quote or ambiguous package;
- source corrections cannot be traced to the original decision state;
- wins disappear under the registered untouched OOS or dependence-aware comparison;
- apparent improvement is driven by selective missing outcomes;
- a forecast advantage does not survive the admitted exact-option economic ruler where that claim is made;
- a calibration curve is evaluated on the data used to fit it;
- product interpretation implies opening intent, dealer inventory or causality beyond the data;
- a meaningful portion of the target population is silently dropped or relabeled zero;
- an operational replay or duplicate writer changes the source history being evaluated.

These are discriminating acceptance boundaries. They do not require arbitrary extra tests for a low-risk copy change.

### 13. Research outputs that are worth shipping

Each family should produce a small, reproducible result pack: question and registered version, population/source admission, causal availability audit, exact run/configuration, attrition waterfall, primary and sensitivity results, falsifiers, independent review, claim ceiling and next action.

Useful outcomes include:

- a source-qualified descriptive feature with no prediction claim;
- a mathematically correct conditional scenario with explicit assumptions;
- a negative study that prevents a misleading signal from shipping;
- a data-gated study with a precise source/history requirement;
- a statistically accepted result limited to its declared family;
- an admitted forecast with its own drift, withdrawal and user-facing explanation.

A data-gated or unrun empirical study remains unfinished empirical work. It can coexist with a completed descriptive capability. That distinction allows Fable to make real product progress while preserving scientific integrity.
