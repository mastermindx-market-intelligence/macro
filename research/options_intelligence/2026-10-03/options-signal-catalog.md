## Shared measurement contract

Use **q** contracts, actual quote multiplier **m**, option price **p** dollars per quoted underlying unit, and matching spot **S**. Full deliverables are mandatory; incompatible adjusted baskets cannot pass scalar arithmetic by assuming m=100. **Delta** is signed long-option delta, including negative puts; **Gamma=dDelta/dS**; **nu=dV/dsigma**; **Vanna=dDelta/dsigma**, where sigma is decimal annualized volatility. **Charm_cal=dDelta/dcalendar_year=-dDelta/dT**. The candidate reference uses **Y=365×86400 seconds**, with ACT/365F explicitly chosen rather than attributed to the vendor. A 1-volatility-point display multiplies vega/vanna by 0.01 once. [T28][T29]

For public flow, **a∈{-1,0,+1}** is inferred sell/unknown/buy. On a valid strictly prior two-sided quote, with positive sizes, an allowed condition, price inside the spread and no identified package ambiguity, sign is `sign(p-mid)`. Midpoint, invalid ordering and other ambiguity remain **a=0 with reasons and exposure mass**. Zero unknown never means neutral. Exact timestamps, source receipts and classification coverage matter; passive counterparties need not be dealers. [T06][L11][L16]

All inputs must be available before the decision. Preserve event, effective, report, receipt, revision and build clocks, then require artifact publication and actual consumer receipt/admission. A built but undelivered artifact is unavailable to the consumer. A retrospective restatement is not historical decision-time evidence. Later quotes/markouts cannot sign earlier trades. OI normally describes the previous close and arrives next morning; missing OI is not zero. Opening volume cannot be inferred from volume>OI, nor assigned to prints from next-day OI. [T08][T09][L02]

Default **RZ** is `(x-training median)/(1.4826×training MAD)` within the same root, training-defined liquidity bucket, maturity bucket and five-minute exchange-session clock bin. Use the last 60 eligible training sessions, require 40 observations and positive MAD; otherwise keep the raw quantity and mark normalized value unavailable. Fit thresholds, buckets and transforms only on training and freeze them during evaluation. Raw ratios, scenario vectors and quality diagnostics retain physical units. M0 is known same-session payoff; M1 is through seven calendar days excluding M0; M2 >7–30; M3 >30–90; M4 >90. Original-paper maturity calendars remain separate.

Each output carries counts, premium/delta coverage, quote age, package ambiguity, source/model versions and typed reasons. **Observed, inferred, scenario and unknown are evidence tags**, not numerical confidence. No invented opening probability or 0–100 score is admitted. Scenario inventory **h** is signed assumed contracts; unsigned OI and signed trading are different observables. Gamma flow is neither bullish demand nor observed dealer inventory. [L14][L15]

Cash gamma `q*m*Gamma*S²*.01` is the **gamma-driven component** of delta-notional sensitivity, not full `d(S*Delta)/dS=Delta+S*Gamma`. Signed portfolio sensitivities and the negative hedge adjustments are distinct. Repriced hedge demand may be pro-/counter-cyclical under assumptions; it does not demonstrate market price impact. **RVar** denotes realized variance and **RVol=sqrt(RVar)**; vega is per quoted price unit and per decimal sigma. OIF13 uses endpoint hedge notional at the scenario spot `S_star=S_0+δS`, positive for modeled purchases, excluding path cash. Linear Greek hedge approximations remain reference-spot sensitivities and are rescaled by `S_star/S_0` when compared with endpoint target notional.

## Exact model choices for the candidate variants

Surface interpolation uses linear total variance in log-forward-moneyness and time, with bracketing and no extrapolation. Calls and puts share qualified carry, exercise and deliverable conventions. OIF19 is **ATM IV minus the square root of forecast conditional expected variance**: `sigma_ATM-sqrt(vhat_P,H)`, where vhat targets `E[RVar_H | information]`. **sqrt(E[RVar_H]) is not E[RVol_H]**. The positive log-HAR/smearing candidate fits log forward-20-session annualized variance using completed 1/5/22-session unannualized variance and known events; use 500 eligible training formations, minimum 252, with matured/purged labels. Future RVar remains an outcome. [L08][L12]

For P3, `RVar_H=sum(r²)/T_H`, with actual elapsed `T_H=(H-t)/(365×86400)` from the eligible consumer decision. IV and physical variance share this ACT/365F horizon/basis. An external `(252/n)*sum(r²)` series for the identical interval must be converted by `(n/252)/T_H`; missing basis metadata makes comparison unavailable. OIF20 remains `sigma²-vhat` in variance units. The identity `VarResidual=VolResidual*(sigma+sqrt(vhat))` does not make their units or interpretations interchangeable. **The positive multiplicative B2-RI mapping, exact beta domain/solver and B1-RI adapter are now fixed by the accepted specification.** QLIKE scores those positive variance forecasts, including against zero realized variance, never the possibly negative residual. Causal history, data/fit qualification and empirical registration remain gates; choosing a mapping is no longer an unresolved design task.

**P6 endpoint disposition, 2026-10-03.** The principal adopted actual 60-second full-fill implementation-shortfall MAE as the sole empirical primary, strictly conditional on independently observed complete fills and fees. The previous catalogue endpoint was “Expression coverage with valid contract/clock/quote evidence plus 60-second delta-hedged markouts under stated bid/ask simulated entry, latency and fee assumptions. This does not prove actual fills, depth beyond NBBO or profitable execution.” That prior endpoint is retained as diagnostic history in the JSON disposition record; it is not an additional primary. OIF35's raw relative-spread formula and all required controls remain unchanged. Missing actual fills leaves observation/simulation work only, not simulated cost calibration.

For OIF22/24/25/28, a training OLS projection removes the conditional association with observed borrow fee, its square, maturity, dated-dividend flag, quote spread and forward/spot carry. This is an explicit candidate statistical adjustment, not a causal claim or undocumented fee-pricing formula. Fit 500 eligible daily formations, minimum 252. Borrow absent or older than one trading session makes the adjusted value unavailable. Raw, adjusted, low-fee and missing-fee cohorts must remain visible; spread/skew information without this challenge is inadequate. [L06][L07][L17]

## Limits and release boundary

Paper labels are preserved deliberately. OIF03 keeps Johnson–So’s weekly maturity/skip convention; OIF08 requires Ni–Pan–Poteshman’s actual non-market-maker categories, elasticity weighting and preceding-60-day volatility; OIF09 requires actual buyer-opening classifications. OIF25 explicitly names its common-expiry/borrow changes. L08’s monthly aggregate, chiefly quarterly-return result is not next-day single-name evidence. Event and 0DTE papers answer distinct questions about pricing, fresh trading, expiration availability and classified accumulated inventory. [L02][L03][L04][L07][L08][L12][L13][L14][L15]

The final-hour gate is **joint IV inversion plus Greeks**, including fixed-IV and same-price-refitted-IV experiments. In the audit’s zero-carry European witness, refitting IV can preserve gamma despite the time floor; vega/charm differ. No blanket gamma multiplier follows. Use economic payoff fixing, distinguish last trade from exercise and payment, and keep AM-settled fixing uncertainty explicit. [T12][T28][T30][T36]

The implementation classes identify observables, inferred proxies, model-derived values, full-revaluation scenarios, external-label benchmarks and quality diagnostics. They do not grant runtime admission. Quoted markouts and simulated costs are diagnostics and rejection/stress tests; a simulation cannot validate its own assumed execution costs. Positive realized-fill/cost/economic claims require independently observed execution evidence or a qualified external empirical benchmark. Fable may eventually receive qualified research contracts through existing owners after source/PIT/consumer/model gates, exact prior-result reconciliation, incremental validation and expression economics are reviewable. This synthesis performs no scoring activation, ranking, trade advice, production operation or owner replacement.
