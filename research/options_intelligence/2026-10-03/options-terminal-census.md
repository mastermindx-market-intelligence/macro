## 4. Flow and Alpha: exact semantics and hazards

### Legacy flow score

[flowScore.ts](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/lib/flowScore.ts) computes:

    score = round(clamp(30 P + 20 U + 15 D + 12 F + 10 M + 8 C + 5 R, 0, 100))

- P: log premium ramp from $50,000 to $5 million, clamped 0–1.
- U: premium_z / 4 clamped 0–1; null gives 0.
- D: 0DTE 0.2; 1–45 days 1; linear 1 to 0.5 for 46–90 days; beyond 90 days 0.3.
- F: volume > OI true 1; unknown 0.6; false 0.4.
- M: ATM 1, near-OTM 0.85, ITM 0.5, far-OTM 0.3.
- C: repeated base 0.6, otherwise 0.2; plus min(0.4, 0.18 log(1 + n_prints - 1)), capped at 1.
- R: mixed/floor -1; mixed non-floor -0.75; directional floor -0.4; other directional -0.2.
- Tiers: ELITE >=90, STRONG >=80, HIGH >=70, MEDIUM >=60, otherwise LOW. Positive weights total 95 points and minimum penalty is one point; maximum attainable score is 94.

This is heuristic construction, not probability/calibration. The current [FlowCard](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/components/flowdesk/FlowCard.tsx) and [InspectorPane](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/components/flowdesk/InspectorPane.tsx) render the attached score. OI exceedance does not identify whether observed transactions open or close positions. The implementation/comments nevertheless call it fresh/new positioning. Missing inputs should not be confused with weak measured evidence: score attachment converts nonfinite/throwing results to zero/LOW.

FlowCard's legacy SWEEP≈ badge requires n_prints >=3 and swept=true; BLOCK requires one print and size >=5,000; WHALE requires premium >=$1 million; UNUSUAL requires z>=2. These are descriptive rules, not package reconstruction. Fresh enrichment supplies alternate badges. ChainHeat displays upstream ask_share and accumulation/distribution/contested lean without requiring the new measured NBBO child contract. **Producer provenance of those chain fields must be checked in Macro before any measured-share claim.**

The [0DTE/Largest selectors](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/lib/optionsFlowBoards.ts) correctly state poll-batch contract aggregates rather than guaranteed individual trades. They filter declared fields, sort premium/time/id, and report additive premium/print/contract/root counts. Call premium share is call premium divided by selected gross premium, not bullish probability.

[marketStructure.ts](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/lib/marketStructure.ts) declares:
- Gamma exposure in $million dealer delta per +1% spot move.
- Vanna exposure per +1 vol point.
- Charm exposure per +1 calendar day.
- Windowed by-strike aggregates rather than full-book totals.

The first-order scenario is:

    estimated hedge demand = -(G * spot_change_percent + V * IV_change_points + C * calendar_days)

Defaults are spot shocks -3% to +3%, IV shocks +5/+2/0/-2/-5 points, and zero time change. It is explicitly a local approximation. Missing Vanna/Charm become zero in cell arithmetic with separate hasVanna/hasCharm flags. Aggregate ignores nonfinite per-row contributions and declares a Greek present once any finite contribution exists; it lacks per-Greek complete-versus-partial totals. Review consumers before allowing a partial numeric cell to look complete.

Sign sensitivity is w*call_abs-put_abs over weights [-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1]. Its "confidence" is absolute normalized call/put imbalance, and "robust" means that value >=0.12. This is a convention-sensitivity descriptor, not statistical confidence. It does not quantify actual participant inventory.

### High-value new concern: hedgeProfile's axis semantics

[hedgeProfile lines 638–695](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/a049d46fa2415d3949aae5efc0ee515b6667c7a0/terminal/lib/marketStructure.ts#L638-L695) negates a Greek column, sorts by contract strike, splits at current spot and forms running sums away from spot. The code's claimed interpretation is cumulative transactions if price travels to that strike.

Source-derived inference: sums across option strike buckets are not an integral of the whole book's spot-dependent Greek. The function uses no hypothetical spot repricing, no price-step weight and no change of the entire book's delta. Its vanna/charm branches likewise cannot infer a spot-path transaction quantity from cross-strike aggregation. The output can be retained as an explicitly named cumulative-by-contract-strike exposure display; a conditional hedge-travel field requires the existing Macro pricing path and declared scenario inputs. Parent should construct a simple invariant/counterexample witness before classifying severity or applying a repair.

DailyHedging separately composes negative charm, negative gamma times a positive one-sigma expected move, and negative vanna times a default +1 vol-point shock; it sums whichever components exist. This is a named joint-shock scenario, not the expected signed amount a dealer will actually trade today. Verify disclosure and avoid treating it as a directional forecast.

Do not rebuild old four-hour expiry-floor work: current #723 receipt records Macro #7279 already merged at fc4efb9fd9ec97cc3e65f9ff9ee157626be5683f.
