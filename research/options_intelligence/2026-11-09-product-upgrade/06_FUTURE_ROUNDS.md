It does not dispatch Fable/T3, start workers, authorize a held effect, change scientific admission or imply that a live product has been accepted.

**Current baseline:** [05_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTERPLAN.md](05_IMPLEMENTATION_MASTERPLAN.md) and [04_ARCHITECTURE_HARDENING.md](04_ARCHITECTURE_HARDENING.md). ## 1. What “more robust” should mean to the user

The system should become more dependable at depth:

- A quick overview explains what changed and what merits examination.
- Every consequential number exposes its exact source, units, population, time and limitation.
- Deep tools remain discoverable and useful beyond the principal SPY demonstration.
- The same selected object survives navigation, corrections, refresh, saved revisions and account changes.
- Mechanical calculations show their supported domain and assumptions.
- Research claims disclose what was tested, the actual result, the exclusions and what remains unavailable.
- Watches capture supported observations; reviews preserve the original question and the selected event.
- Operational failure leads to understandable recovery and retains committed work.
- Each release has an actual source-to-consumer witness.

These are product outcomes. More charts, more services, larger model names or extra pages are not acceptance on their own.

## 6. Round 8 — Contract mechanics, liquidity and deeper Plan

### User benefit

A user should understand the economic meaning and limitations of an expression, including the facts an attractive expiry curve omits.

### Domain separation

The current Plan retains its qualified ordinary SPY/QQQ, common-expiry intrinsic-payoff domain and exact source-sample semantics. Reuse #804's implemented whole-Plan v3 bridge and native Save/reopen. Do not expand the calculator's claims merely by adding fields to the UI.

Plan deeper capabilities as separately qualified domains:

| Capability | Required owner inputs | Key refusal or falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Liquidity context | Exact bid/ask, size, age, condition, underlying time and fee assumptions | A wide/old/one-sided quote is not an executable offer |
| Multi-leg cost context | Qualified simultaneous or explicitly asynchronous leg quotes; complex-order evidence if claimed | Independent ask/bid marks do not prove a simultaneously executable package |
| Exercise/settlement | Exact series terms, deliverable, calendars and product-specific exercise/fixing/settlement rules | Expiry date or ticker alone cannot establish delivery/assignment mechanics |
| Corporate actions | Versioned adjustment terms, known-by and effective dates | Adjusted contracts cannot inherit an ordinary 100-share assumption |
| Pre-expiry scenario | Accepted pricing/exercise domain, matched model/input revisions and carry/borrow/dividend inputs | Intrinsic payoff or dated Gamma is not a repricing engine |
| Model sensitivity | Explicit spot/IV/time/carry shocks, qualified units and population | Stress-grid extrema are not a statistical confidence interval |
| Empirical execution | Actual independent fill/fee/cancel records and the registered cohort | Simulated midpoint completion is not a full fill |

Use existing terms, quote, scenario and evaluation owners. Add a narrow missing adapter only after demonstrating why the existing owner cannot supply the function. Generic chart, table and numerical infrastructure should be reused.

### Design outputs

Create a supported-domain chooser, exact terms/lifecycle drawer, liquidity/cost explanation, scenario assumptions and a comparison that holds other assumptions fixed. Keep source uncertainty, parameter sensitivity, model uncertainty and calibrated forecast uncertainty visibly different. Explain omitted fees, early exercise, financing or settlement effects when they matter to the selected domain.

### Completion proof

Exact terms and clocks survive save/reopen and later correction. No silent multiplier, settlement, fee or Greek-unit coercion. Unsupported adjusted/mixed-domain inputs refuse calculation without a partial authoritative-looking result. Existing deterministic payoff and quote-ruler results remain reproducible under their original definitions.

## 7. Round 9 — Portfolio context and hedge studies

### User benefit

Evaluate what a hypothetical expression changes relative to an explicit baseline, including the cost and opportunity tradeoffs of a hedge.

### Required architecture

Consume the incumbent holdings/Portfolio/Risk owner and actual account permissions. Bind position snapshot, terms, cashflows, currencies, valuation time, model convention and completeness. Keep authored hypothetical legs outside actual holdings.

Support clear modes:

- hypothetical portfolio baseline;
- admitted actual position snapshot;
- proposed incremental expression;
- comparison under a named objective/horizon and stated scenarios.

Net risk only when units, model conventions, scope and currencies are compatible. A partial account snapshot must not appear as total portfolio risk. A scenario-selected “best” hedge is not universally optimal; show objective, search domain, carry, drawdown tradeoff and missed-upside/recovery effects.

### Concrete outputs

An incremental-risk contract, baseline-versus-proposal design, source/completeness drawer, hedge tradeoff comparison and invalidation rules when positions, prices or rights change. No new Options portfolio store, allocation authority or trading control plane.

### Completion proof

The same expression can be inspected against two correctly identified baselines without contaminating either. Missing account data, incompatible units and stale position snapshots remain visible. Hypothetical analysis neither changes positions nor implies permission to size or trade them.

## 8. Round 10 — Monitoring, replay and learning

### User benefit

Know what was being monitored, which event actually fired, what was delivered and whether the original hypothesis still holds.

### Required work

Extend existing alert/evaluator/outbox and Investigation owners for the exact supported capabilities. Ordinary watch creation needs owner-scoped reconciliation when its response is lost; do not borrow the unrelated frozen-enrollment mutation protocol or add a second retry queue.

Keep condition evaluation, firing, notification delivery and case assessment separate. The chosen firing stays fixed while an assessment is authored. Newer events offer an explicit switch. Distinguish readable empty history from unavailable or capped history; the current last-200 outbox read does not prove the whole history was searched.

Material-change watches require an exact defined observable, baseline, comparator, schedule and termination. A price-crossing watch does not satisfy exposure level-crossing, regime-change or calibrated forecast requirements. These need their own admitted source and evaluator contracts.

For replay, retain original artifact/member identity, available-by cutoff and correction lineage through existing temporal owners. Original and restated analysis are deliberate separate views. Nearest later data, digest order or a current fetch cannot fill an unknown historical observation.

### Outcomes and review design

A case can have an authored assessment, an underlying label, an eligible quote-ruler outcome, an actual fill result or a portfolio outcome. Display the applicable type, horizon and missingness. Never collapse them into one “won/lost” badge.

A useful review includes the original question, original evidence, selected event, comparable later evidence, changed assumptions, unresolved contradictions and an explicit assessment. Preserve Unicode input and the exact immutable revision after Save.

### Completion proof

Ambiguous Arm, duplicate action, re-arm, deferred/suppressed/failed delivery, missing source clock, capped history, revoked evidence and later firing all have correct observable outcomes. Original-operation recovery does not create a duplicate effect. Original-versus-restated replay preserves decision eligibility.
