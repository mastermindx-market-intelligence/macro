# Options Suite — Release Contract and Round 6 Design Brief

## 10 November 2026 UTC · Round 5 decision

**Build toward the original complete decision-to-outcome journey, using useful increments that retain their own honest acceptance boundary.** A Flow → dated Exposure → return journey is the first observational precursor. An admitted saved-case/ordinary-watch/assessment increment can follow. Neither closes original OPT-100/OPR-129, which also requires Brief, exact Trade Lab comparison, selected-node monitoring, Monitor, outcome review and measured usefulness.

This is a planning and design contract on the existing Options programme, not a new task hierarchy or implementation dispatch. Current source qualifications and holds are in [10_ROUND5_SOURCE_READINESS.md](10_ROUND5_SOURCE_READINESS.md). Original task/acceptance text is preserved under [original_2026-10-21](original_2026-10-21/README.md).

## 1. Product standard: quick orientation, deep investigation

The first screen should answer what changed, how strong the evidence is, what is missing and which investigation is useful next. Deeper tools must remain reachable without losing the selected event, source vintage, instrument, authored question or return path. Precision belongs at the point where it changes interpretation: source class beside the metric, comparison basis beside the change, assumptions beside the modeled result, and outcome type beside a review.

Use progressive detail within the existing shell and one contextual pane. A summary should be concise because its scope is clear, not because caveats and contradictory evidence have disappeared. Guided and expert presentations use identical calculations. No display score may promote descriptive observations into a forecast or trading instruction.

### Preserve the five jobs within six destinations

| Original user job | Current destination composition | Acceptance retained |
|---|---|---|
| Market Brief | Overview, linking into Flow, Exposure, Volatility and Research | Useful changes, coverage, catalysts, risk and next investigation or justified wait; OPT-029/OPR-102. |
| Opportunities | Research and Flow screening with a discoverable candidate dossier | Evidence, contradiction, shortlist and risk-comparison continuity; OPT-030/068 and OPR-103/110. Maturity cards alone do not satisfy this. |
| Workbench | Flow / Exposure / Volatility / Research Lab | Large analytical stage, contextual inspection, exact selection and all retained deep tools; OPT-027/028 and OPR-039/113/114. |
| Trade Lab | Plans & review, reached from Research/Workbench | Stock/long-option/defined-risk-vertical/wait comparison, price-IV-time loss table, payoff, terms, quotes and costs; OPT-062 and OPR-087/090/092/096. |
| Monitor | Plans & review: Saved, Watching and Review | Real selected-node monitoring, meaningful change, exact original case/firing and outcome accounting; OPT-035/037/079 and OPR-100/122/129. |

The six labels may evolve through actual usability work. The five required jobs cannot be erased by renaming navigation. A saved-case list is not the complete Monitor; an assessment form is not the outcome journal; expiry payoff alone is not pre-expiry conditional comparison.

## 2. Scope of the next useful observational precursor

### Planning-selected domain

Start with an entitled existing Terminal user examining an **SPY aggregate Flow event** and independent **qualified dated SPY Gamma**, with the actual available source session retained. Admit only the ordinary terms/source domain supported by the original owner. This is observational research, not a calibrated recommendation or executable signal.

Use current API/cache/auth/reader paths. The useful route is Flow selection → aggregate inspector → `/options?tab=gex&symbol=SPY&view=chart` → qualified dated source/known-subset inspection → exact original Flow context. Gamma may have a different source time; the handoff is explicitly root-only into independent evidence. Do not copy the Flow event’s identity into the Exposure book’s lineage.

The precursor must handle missing source or rights honestly. It cannot use the synthetic six-child fixture as natural proof, manufacture a date that is unavailable, use legacy GEX as qualified dated Gamma, infer current spot when dated spot is null, or advertise a Volatility exact-expiry handoff before its receiver exists.

### What a user can do in this increment

1. Find an actually supplied aggregate and see actual premium convention, population, source clocks and approximate classification.
2. Inspect the existing validated aggregate quote-location coverage when present, including the covered-premium denominator and age limitations.
3. Open independent dated Gamma for the same supported root, inspect the qualified subset and unavailable fields, and understand which source produced it.
4. Return to the same aggregate/source context, filters, sort, scroll and focus, or receive an explicit unavailable-original state.
5. Use keyboard/touch and the supported EN/ZH/theme variants without losing identity, signs, units or the return action.

The complete OPT-005 release cohort contract still needs its original owner’s frozen persona, instruments, source tier, devices, locales, decisions, loss cases, study N and uncertainty method before the corresponding build/study dispatch. This planning selection identifies a source-feasible precursor; it is not a fabricated usefulness verdict or silent final cohort approval.

## 5. Selection, handoff and return contracts

Every handoff has one of three results: exact transfer, explicitly root-only transfer to independent evidence, or refusal naming the unsupported field. Implement through existing owners, not a new universal context object.

| Transition | Receiver | Return and invariant |
|---|---|---|
| Flow → Exposure | Existing workspace/GEX chart decoder; independent admitted dated book | Keep original aggregate/source epoch, source mode, applied filters, sort, scroll and focus. New events cannot retarget the return. |
| Sector holding → Exposure/chart | Original SectorCompanyComparison holdings roster and origin parser | Preserve exact company, roster/content hash, method, index and return holding. Flow is not a substitute witness. |
| GEX chart/matrix/strike/expiry | Same initial/history decoder, including compatibility alias `prism` → matrix | Root/view/selection agree on cold entry, warm history and Back/Forward. Preserve meaningful history steps. |
| Exposure → Volatility, later | Real root/explicit-expiry/tenor receiver through the workspace | Independent dates remain independent; missing requested expiry remains unavailable. No generic shortcut masquerading as exact transfer. |
| Plan → new case | Original #804 explicit New/Add/native Save | New case revision 1; copied Plan detached; subsequent edits do not mutate saved inputs. |
| Saved case → exact revision | Native Analysis/embedded reader and lifetime guard | Read requested acknowledged revision; current head is not a substitute. Current authorization applies. |
| Saved case → firing → assessment | Existing alert/receipt and immutable revision owners | Selected firing fixed during authoring; later fire offers an explicit switch. No fire ID synthesized from time. |
| Futures or Equity/IEX replay | Their native contract/session/basis/cutoff/artifact grammar | Preserve their native return and time domain; no global Options replay cursor. |

Current aliases `/options`→Tape, `vol`/`screener`→Screener, `prism`→GEX/matrix and `0dte`→internal `zero_dte` retain compatibility. Proposed `research`, `plans`, `preset=doi`, Prophet `lane` and Volatility `expiry` require actual decoder/receiver proof before publishing links. Falling back to Tape is a failed intended action.

## 6. Decisive implementation proof

### Positive witnesses

Bind each witness to source artifact/member or permitted fingerprint, source/observation/publication clocks, API identity, installed application SHA/build, entitled principal scope and captured actual UI. A screenshot without this chain proves only appearance.

1. Natural aggregate Flow → independent qualified dated Gamma → exact return, including the supported aggregate measured evidence and null/partial semantics.
2. The affected existing Sector holdings roster → company → Exposure/chart → Back seam. Reuse unchanged accepted receipts elsewhere.
3. Cold URL, warm mounted GEX after another selection and browser Back/Forward with the same supported identity. Pane close does not erase selection.
4. Relevant EN/ZH, light/dark, keyboard/touch and actual device-size task success. Preserve original 1440/1280/820/390, 200% zoom and OPR-116’s 1920 readability; existing 320px component checks remain useful but do not replace them.
5. For later persistence slices, actual native operation/revision readback, explicit exact-firing review and user usefulness. Controlled fixtures cover failures; they do not replace the natural positive journey.

### Failure and recovery evidence

| Challenge | Required outcome |
|---|---|
| Root A slow, B fast, A completes last | No A values, labels, tooltip or enabled action under B. Guard assignment and render. |
| Owner A→B→A while parsing | Earlier A lifetime cannot revive through cache, callback or staged action. |
| Revoked entitlement | Values/actions withdraw; fallback cannot reopen access. |
| Same displayed date, changed source bytes | New qualification epoch; original selection does not silently mutate. |
| Removed/hidden selected event | Preserve requested identity and explain absence; no nearest-neighbor replacement. |
| Quiet, absent, failed source | Distinct states; failed read never means no activity. Last-good values retain actual date and stale status. |
| No children/packages | Useful aggregate, with no invented prints, opening intent or package economics. |
| Unequal pre/post-signing populations | Keep measured denominator separate; do not apply a quote share to incompatible event premium. |
| Null dated spot/missing member | No spot borrowing, missing-to-zero coercion or full total from known subtotal. |
| Root/class/model mismatch | Explicit refusal; NDX/NDXP or ETF/index substitution cannot make a case pass. |
| Lost Save response / committed but read failed | Reconcile original operation; retry read after known commit; no unqualified resubmission. |
| Lost ordinary Arm response | Retain original intent/operation and resolve through existing owner before another creation. |
| New firing during assessment | Original selection remains stable; switch is explicit. |
| Capped/unreadable/malformed receipt history | Scope and availability visible; never assert complete history or “no events” from failure. |
| Narrow layout/long Chinese copy | Identity, units, limitation, action and Back remain visible and operable; stable focus/announcement. |

Performance budgets must be declared for the actual cohort before measurement. Report cold/warm/failure, large books, source-to-screen versus UI latency, memory/render/reconnect and degradation. A small chart cannot close P74; passing P74 cannot close P75 or P76. Original suggested targets remain proposals until ratified.

## 7. Usefulness and claim acceptance

OPT-005 freezes persona, instruments, source tier, device, locale, decisions, loss cases, task scoring, critical errors, timing, cohort N and uncertainty before build/study. OPT-101’s original proposed budgets are ≥90% task success **per cohort**, zero critical semantic/risk misreads in the release set, ≤90 seconds useful orientation and ≤3 minutes candidate-risk comparison. These are preserved proposals, not achieved measurements.

The study must retain current Terminal and simple baselines, report N/uncertainty, abandonment, comprehension and confidence calibration. Freeze the sample-size and uncertainty method before observing outcomes; no invented population guarantee from a small convenience sample. A developer’s path check and a design review cannot substitute for this user evidence.

The first complete journey includes contradiction/abstention, stale source, illiquidity, adverse catalyst and failed-fill cases. Modeled scenario P&L, expiry payoff, quote-based research outcome, user assessment and actual fill P&L stay separately named and scored. Research provenance never grants trade authority.

## 9. Implementation order, rollout and final handoff

1. Reconcile original scope and current accepted receipts on the same programme; this round recovers the previously missing packet.
2. Complete the design and source-contract deltas required by the next bounded slice. Core Trade Lab design and qualification precede the complete first vertical. Independently ready correctness repairs, including original OPT-032, retain their own prerequisites while other Round 6 work continues; the entire design programme is not a new gate.
3. Compile bounded existing-owner packets: selection/route safety and observational precursor; volatility semantics; lossless Matrix admission; native case/Plan composition; watch creation/firing stability; core Trade Lab/Monitor/outcome; then other independently ready depth.
4. For each actual implementation, self-check the owned diff, run meaningful affected checks and required CI, preserve current source/holds and prove the installed entitled path before acceptance. Do not wait for unrelated research, or bypass a real prerequisite.
5. Roll out the admitted capability through the existing release mechanism with declared cohort/canary and rollback. Restore prior presentation or withdraw an unqualified optional capability without deleting source history, committed revisions or uncertain operations. After a persistence effect, follow the original forward-repair contract.
6. The final Fable/T3 brief belongs in GitHub and must contain exact owner/task mappings, dependencies/bindings, source/branch/effect identities, DO_NOT_REDO, actions, proof, rollback and return contract. A later explicit dispatch is distinct from this saved brief, pickup, STARTED, deployment and acceptance.

Round 7 deepens signal/sleeve qualification. Round 8 expands lifecycle/liquidity beyond the first vertical’s required core. Round 9 adds exact portfolio/hedge context. Round 10 deepens monitoring and learning. Round 11 collects real implemented production evidence per admitted slice. Round 12 reconciles the final commission; it can move earlier for a bounded implementation-ready release while later obligations remain explicit. See [06_FUTURE_ROUNDS.md](06_FUTURE_ROUNDS.md).
