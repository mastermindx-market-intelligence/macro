### 7.1 Execution cost and liquidity context

**User value:** understand spread, quote age, displayed size, stale/one-sided conditions, cost assumptions and how much a scenario depends on them before treating it as useful.

**Dependencies:** exact terms/quote identity, qualified event and availability clocks, condition/package rules, contemporaneous underlying, existing quote owner, explicit fee assumptions. Advanced empirical cost prediction additionally needs P6’s independent actual fill/fee records and frozen cohort.

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** distinguish descriptive spread from estimated fill probability; a hypothetical legwise ask/bid cost is not a simultaneous executable complex-order quote; timestamps from different legs cannot imply a common execution instant. Preserve partial/no-fill/cancel/missing categories and negative price-improvement costs. A model trained only on full fills must not claim improved routing or unconditional execution value. The current OA3 fee convention cannot become the customer’s actual fee schedule. [S04][S06]

### 7.2 Exercise, settlement and corporate-action context

**User value:** explain what the contract delivers, when relevant rights/obligations occur, and where an attractive expiry chart omits a material lifecycle effect.

**Dependencies:** the incumbent exact-series/reference owner, versioned terms and corporate-action information, known-by/effective dates, dividend/borrow/carry inputs where the model requires them, product-specific last-tradable/fixing/exercise/settlement calendars, and a separately qualified exercise-aware producer. OCC confirms standard ETF contracts commonly represent 100 shares but adjustments can alter that deliverable; it identifies ETF options as American-style. Index settlement and exercise conventions differ by product. These public facts motivate exact contract qualification, not generic root-based inference. [O1][O2]

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** adjusted series mapped to ordinary 100; publication/effective dates collapsed; option expiry date treated as the fixing/exercise/settlement instant; absence of an adjustment memo treated as positive standardness; broker/account assignment mechanics inferred from intrinsic payoff. OCC’s special-settlement representation includes multiple delivery components and separate process/settlement dates, reinforcing the need for structured deliverables. The current Plan can honestly retain its narrow intrinsic scope while this capability is developed. [O3][S15][S20]

### 7.3 Scenario uncertainty and model sensitivity

**User value:** compare “if spot/IV/time/carry change” under stated assumptions, identify which input dominates, and inspect when a result is fragile.

**Dependencies:** incumbent scenario/pricing owner, accepted contract domain, matched model/input revisions, economic clocks, unit-consistent Greeks, full required member population and explicit scenario shocks. Current v1 Plan remains expiry-only. Dated Gamma explicitly has no repricing/joint-scenario capability. [S15][S16][S22]

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** stress-grid min/max described as a statistical confidence interval; user-selected scenario weights described as calibrated probabilities; missing Vanna/Charm imputed as zero for a nonzero shock; IV points and decimal ratios confused; finite-difference Greeks fail a meaningful analytic/domain control; a non-identifiable model is rescued with an undocumented floor. Parameter sensitivity, source uncertainty, model uncertainty and calibrated forecast uncertainty require different labels.

### 7.4 Portfolio and hedge context

**User value:** compare a hypothetical expression’s incremental risk to a clearly identified actual or hypothetical baseline portfolio, including hedge carry and missed-upside tradeoffs.

**Dependencies:** incumbent D22 holdings/Portfolio/Risk owner and account access; exact position snapshot/terms, cashflows and FX; a common valuation instant/model convention; declared objective/horizon; existing execution/cost evidence where claimed. User-authored Plans must stay separate from actual holdings and allocation authority. [S11][S20]

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** summing dollar and unit Greeks or incompatible vega conventions; a hypothetical leg silently becomes a held position; partial account data becomes whole-portfolio risk; a hedge “wins” only because opportunity cost, carry or missed recovery was omitted; a best-in-grid hedge is called optimal without a registered objective/domain. Netting across accounts, underlyings or currencies needs the actual owner’s scope; no new Options portfolio store.

### 7.5 Replay, evaluation and original-versus-restated evidence

**User value:** reconstruct what could be seen at the decision, compare later revisions deliberately, inspect the exact expression, and understand why an outcome is unavailable.

**Dependencies:** incumbent source/archive/Investigation resolution, immutable artifact identity and row/member locator, owner access, original availability clocks, correction lineage and registered outcome owner. A historical read has its historical context; no continuous poller or generic replay capture system is added. [S21]

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** nearest newer frame fills a historical gap; an index in old bytes selects a different row in new bytes; equal timestamp/sequence tuples assumed globally unique; a fresh archive retrieval is labeled original live availability; a restatement changes the original campaign formation or test label; unavailable old source is rebuilt from today’s data. Show original and restated analysis as different views with explicit eligibility.

### 7.6 Useful signal explanation and evaluation workbench

**User value:** quickly understand what changed, the evidence that supports or contradicts a hypothesis, which study actually tested it, and what remains uncertain.

**Dependencies:** the existing candidate/campaign and evaluation owners, source-qualified metrics, exact study/target/population versions, observed attrition and maturity, existing capability projection. This is a consumer of evidence, not an LLM signal originator or new scoring plane.

**Acceptance/falsifiers:** one probability shown across roots/horizons without qualification; “accurate” reported from a conditional complete-case subset with concealed attrition; stock-direction performance used to promote option profitability; historical development outcomes become a new test; a signal card claims more authority than its source. A separate product-usefulness study may measure comprehension, source interpretation and successful investigation tasks; that does not add a seventh OIF pilot or establish investment returns. [S03][S05][S08]

## 10. Unresolved tradeoffs for root disposition

1. **Breadth versus depth of qualified data:** prioritize a coherent narrow population/valuation class with visible exclusions, or a wider legacy descriptive view. Support both only with unmistakable scope labels; never label the broader view as equally qualified by default.
2. **Dated terms versus current tradability:** current Plan deliberately supports a dated hypothetical. Extending its value requires a separate current terms/quote admission, not a UI label change or opportunistic new provider read.
3. **Latency feasibility:** the earlier natural acquisition receipt’s roughly 21-minute cycle does not satisfy the six-pilot ten-second consumer-admission rule. Current source owners may have progressed; actual per-pilot receipts must decide. Preserve the frozen question if data-gated, or register a materially different successor before outcomes; do not relax it silently. [S03][S04]
4. **Independent exact-expression selection:** user-authored Plan comparison is useful now, but an automated sleeve expression policy must specify an outcome-blind selection rule and feasible quote capture. Never evaluate every possible contract and retain the winner.
5. **Portfolio usefulness without action authority:** expose incremental scenario risk and explicit assumptions first. Optimized quantities, allocation and execution belong to the existing Portfolio/Risk/action owners and require their separate acceptance.
6. **Uncertainty presentation:** decide which unavailable/partial qualifications belong beside the primary number and which belong in the inspector. Any limitation that changes the permitted inference must remain visible; technical hash/endpoint detail can be progressive disclosure.
7. **Scientific maintenance:** choose how to display stale/unavailable runtime inputs for an otherwise accepted method without erasing its historical evidence or implying current eligibility. Reuse source/capability and evaluation owners; no second promotion or withdrawal registry.
