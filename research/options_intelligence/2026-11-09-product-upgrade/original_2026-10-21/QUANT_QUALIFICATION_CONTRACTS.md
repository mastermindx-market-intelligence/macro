# Quantitative qualification contracts

## Purpose and authority

This supplement makes the Terminal Options masterplan's conditional research dependencies explicit. It is a planning and acceptance contract, not an experiment activation, source admission, model promotion, new owner or new store.

Preserve original Institutional Options root **`institutional-options-iob-20261012-c1`**, original OA/AD/Intraday/Context Audit responsibilities, G0–G6, existing experiment identities and outcome/evaluation owners. `OPT-083` consumes the original research and negative results; `OPT-084` adopts only the numerical components actually required by the selected experiment.

The inspected pilot specification is **`PRINCIPAL_PROPOSAL`**, with nine null binding fields at the inspected revision. Its constants, proposed quality thresholds and sampling conventions are not automatically accepted by this supplement. Bind or amend them through the existing owner before outcomes are inspected. The current empirical adapter's refusal remains binding until the actual prerequisites are satisfied.

## 1. How to bind a conditional dependency

Under `OPT-086`, retain the following in the existing protocol/run record: selected pilot and exact version; claim class; universe and contract domain; endpoint/horizon; required source/clock/model components; baseline and cross-fit lineage; applicable task dependencies; cost/fill ruler; prospective population; and permitted consumer use. Freeze these choices before inspecting the selected evaluation outcomes.

A conditional dependency is satisfied by the **applicable, scoped accepted evidence**, not by success of every sibling pilot. A negative or inconclusive study can be a completed research task without qualifying its proposed production claim. Do not remove a failed prerequisite after observing its outcome to rescue that same claim.

Common research prerequisites are `OPT-083`, the selected population's `OPT-085` chronology/identity evidence, and `OPT-086` protocol. `OPT-011` supplies source-capability truth. `OPT-014` and the actual source-use contract apply wherever contract terms or licensed observations are consumed. `OPT-092` verifies the relevant decision/outcome denominators. Source capture work such as `OPT-018` is required only to the extent the chosen source population is not already qualified; a received-population study cannot claim whole-market completeness, but it need not invent a whole-market capture dependency either.

Production prediction or decision use additionally requires the selected family's prospective/economic qualification (`OPT-093`) and exact consumer verdict (`OPT-094`). Probability claims additionally require `OPT-091`. These conditions do not block an honest descriptive or conditional calculation.

## 3. Chronology and capacity contracts

`OPT-085` must preserve event time, original availability/receipt, actual formation, expression selection, label maturity, correction vintage and fit cutoff. The original protocol has exact local-slot/UTC ordering, delivery/quote-age tolerances, calendar rules and immutable label cutoffs. Those rules apply as originally bound or explicitly amended; a date alone cannot replace an unavailable timestamp.

For the original confirmatory pilots, captured point-in-time evidence and reconstructed development history remain distinct. P1 requires exact interval extrema. P2/P4 require synchronized residual-return and corporate-action versions. P3 requires its specified partial-session, overnight, holiday and elapsed-time sampling path. P5 must not cross an unqualified fixing/expiry. P6 preserves actual decision, quote and child-fill clocks and its separate order population.

B1-RI is a target-trained summary-information research comparator, **not the production ranker**. Required B0 forecasts must themselves be causal or time-ordered held-out predictions before RI fitting; then held-out RI predictions feed B2 training. Mature targets alone do not legitimize in-sample HAR offsets. Missing incumbent snapshot identity cannot become a zero score/stage. Beating this comparator establishes only the declared incremental claim.

`OPT-019` is the independently owned context-audit capacity successor. It is a prerequisite only when the selected study actually requires its complete-corpus operation and existing capacity cannot satisfy that contract. Do not make it a blanket prerequisite for all chronology work, a separately qualified new cohort, or unrelated AD/Options delivery. Conversely, do not evade a genuine corpus dependency by truncating, evicting or changing the population after inspection. Record the dependency judgment in the existing protocol under `OPT-086`.

## 4. Claims and applicable gates

| Intended output | Required qualification | Permitted claim ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Observed fact or descriptive comparison | Source/terms/clocks, units, population, rights and actual consumer correctness | What was observed or calculated within the specified population; no implied forecast authority. |
| Conditional scenario or modeled payoff | Supported model/domain, complete selected membership, stated assumptions, costs and numerical witnesses | “Under these assumptions”; modeled P&L is allowed and must remain distinct from realized P&L. |
| Frozen research candidate/expression | Original formation/selection identity, authentic availability and retained revisions (`OPT-069`, `OPT-076`) | Prospective research; not an issued recommendation by UI placement. |
| Point range/variance/return forecast | Family-specific protocol, independent evaluation and scoped prospective evidence | The accepted point forecast and uncertainty; no automatic probability interpretation. |
| Physical probability or target-before-stop forecast | All applicable forecast gates **plus `OPT-091`**: defined event, horizon, path resolution, censoring, proper scoring and calibration | Only the calibrated target/event/population. Delta and risk-neutral density cannot substitute. |
| Research quote-rule economic outcome | Authentic post-selection firm quotes, fixed fees/latency, supported lifecycle, missing/nonfill treatment | Conservative or otherwise explicitly modeled research economics; not an actual fill. |
| Actual-fill cost or realized trade result | Independent order/fill/fee evidence and original G5; `OPT-090` for the P6 calibration claim | The observed filled population and its selection limits; no synthesized execution. |
| Issued plan, ranking, management or sizing | Exact family/policy/consumer verdict (`OPT-094`) and the relevant original action-owner acceptance | Only the specifically admitted use. One permission does not imply another or broker execution. |

Probability calibration is not a prerequisite for every useful scenario, point forecast, conditional strategy comparison or hedge analysis. It is mandatory whenever a physical probability is displayed or used by the admitted decision policy. An expected-utility optimizer requires a qualified probabilistic/economic model appropriate to that objective; a descriptive cost/risk Pareto frontier does not.

## 6. Scientific terminal dispositions

Use these as verdict meanings within the existing evaluation record, not new runtime lifecycle states:

- **Accepted for the defined use:** applicable evidence passes; record exact limits, version, monitoring and rollback. Only the matching consumer may advance.
- **Negative / killed for this hypothesis and inspected data:** retain the complete result and failed useful-margin/family test. The research task is completed; its proposed production claim is not accepted. No same-window tuning or selective relabeling.
- **Inconclusive / underpowered:** report uncertainty and the required independent future information. Do not convert a favourable point estimate into acceptance or repeatedly inspect accumulating data outside the registered look plan.
- **Unavailable / scientifically unqualified:** preserve missing source/clock/domain/population facts. Numerical or descriptive work can remain useful; no empirical success is claimed.
- **Retired or superseded by an explicit decision:** preserve original identity and results, justify the new question/version and bind genuinely appropriate new evidence. Retirement does not erase a promised product capability; Fable must retain or explicitly revise that product obligation.

R4's exploratory result remains attached to R5/R6 qualifications and R7 source gaps. R13's failed family test, R14's worse later-period benchmarks, and R18's failed 312-week feature block remain in `OPT-083` and all relevant promotion reviews. R18's alternative lag is not a selected winner. These are scoped findings, not a universal rejection of options information. They may close those studies without blocking an unrelated qualified family; they cannot justify claiming the full predictive product is already complete.
