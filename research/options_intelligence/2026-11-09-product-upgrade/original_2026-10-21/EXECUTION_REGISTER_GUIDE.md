# How Fable should use the execution register

**Prepared 21 October 2026. Planning state: not dispatched.** These identifiers are packet IDs and proposed labels, not created external tickets, runtime jobs or accepted work.

## Read the register in three layers

1. **Outcome and custody:** task ID, accountable lead, existing carrier, bounded scope and required deliverable. Preserve the original source writer and accepting receiver. An Opus supervisory role does not create a new source owner.
2. **Readiness:** unconditional dependencies, conditional dependencies, source pins, rights and unresolved effects. Convert a ready parent packet into one or more bounded Fabric leaves only after the concrete input/output contract and resource envelope are frozen.
3. **Acceptance:** independent acceptor, exact evidence, authority boundary, failure policy and rollback. A returned artifact, merge, install, natural source, actual consumer, useful decision and qualified prediction are separate facts.

The CSV and JSON carry the same 104 work packets. The JSON is schema `options_masterplan_tasks/v2` and preserves conditional rules as structured objects. The CSV encodes those objects as JSON in `conditional_dependencies`. A semicolon separates unconditional task IDs and proposed labels.

## Conditional dependency semantics

Every unconditional dependency requires the relevant accepted receipt. Every conditional rule must be evaluated against an explicit binding frozen before dispatch and before the relevant outcomes are inspected.

- `in`: the scalar binding equals one of the listed values.
- `contains_any`: a set-valued binding intersects the listed values.
- All matching rules apply together. Their prerequisites and `additional_receipt` obligations are cumulative.
- An absent, unknown or ambiguous binding blocks the leaf. It is not false and does not waive the rule.
- A nonapplicable rule needs a recorded reason consistent with the frozen source, model and consumer scope.
- An accepted umbrella task does not qualify an unsupported member. Resolve the precise root, contract, model, horizon, quote convention and source population used by the leaf.

Required binding topics include the selected pilot/family, chronology population, source and quote scope, expression policy, structure domain, optimization objective, probability endpoint, exact consumer claims and features entering the release. The register lists supported original pilot alternatives. `QUANT_QUALIFICATION_CONTRACTS.md` provides their substantive requirements.

### Examples

**Activity-to-range pilot P1.** Apply its source, received-population, multiplier, interval-label, chronology, baseline and protocol receipts. Do not require an unrelated IV surface, HAR forecast, directional pilot or actual-fill regression. A fitted probability derived later from that range output would require its own probability qualification.

**Exact long-option research expression.** The admitted deterministic selection policy can bind exact terms and quotes prospectively. It need not wait for the structure optimizer or all additional templates. A vertical needs that vertical's model, leg, quote and lifecycle support. A portfolio of additional templates is not an automatic gate for the first supported long option.

**Physical target-before-stop probability.** An accepted return prediction does not establish this claim. Require the separately bound path-aware event, barriers, horizon, censoring, untouched evaluation and calibration receipts under OPT-091. Risk-neutral density and option delta do not substitute.

**First useful research release.** Bind the supported cohort, data cadence and lenses. Require real save/watch/review, the cohort usefulness result and applicable source, rights, performance and isolation proof. Add full intraday interval, saved-layout and sharing gates when those capabilities enter that release. The corresponding tasks remain obligations for whole-suite completion.

## Build work and activation are separate

Research construction, deterministic valuation and truthful conditional scenarios can precede predictive promotion. This allows prospective candidate/expression/outcome evidence to accrue while science proceeds.

Any production score, ranking, recommendation, issue, size or managed action requires its exact accepted successor/consumer authority, including applicable OPT-093/094 evidence. Do not turn that later activation gate into a circular build dependency. Do not infer activation from code presence, passing numerical fixtures or a successful unrelated family.

## Completion and negative results

OPT-103 requires a receipt or explicit disposition for **every OPT-001 through OPT-102** and each retained parity requirement. OPT-104 is continuing operation.

Scientific work may end with accepted evidence, a qualified negative result, inconclusive evidence, unavailable prerequisites or an explicit retirement. Only acceptance of the precise claim qualifies its production use. Preserve the failed test, inspected data and limitation; do not retune on the same evaluation window to produce a desired answer.

Product work has a different closure standard. A deliberately unsupported or deferred capability remains a visible limitation. It cannot be hidden behind a completed study or omitted from the programme ledger. Any unresolved retained parity obligation prevents an unqualified claim of full parity; an incomplete core user journey prevents whole-suite completion.
