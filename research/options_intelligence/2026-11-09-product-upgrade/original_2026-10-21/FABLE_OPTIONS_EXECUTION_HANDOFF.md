**Prepared 21 October 2026.** This is the user-commissioned audit and execution design. It has not been submitted to a runtime, published as external tickets or executed. Use the existing Options parent and original carriers when commissioning implementation.

## Mission

Complete a world-class Options intelligence and decision product across Terminal. Give newcomers a clear route from market understanding to a defensible candidate, exact structure comparison, risk comprehension and monitoring. Give experts the full chain, flow, positioning, volatility, scenario, historical and portfolio machinery, with source and model detail available at every important decision.

The north star is a complete decision dossier: **what changed; why the thesis matters; its strongest contradiction; the right horizon; exact feasible expressions; cost and loss scenarios; trigger/invalidation; what changed later; and the honest outcome**. Qualified buy/sell candidates and useful asymmetric opportunities are committed goals. They must earn their exact prediction, ranking, economics and consumer authority.

Revamp the entire suite's information architecture and layout around five jobs: **Market Brief, Opportunities, Workbench, Trade Lab and Monitor**. Retain advanced tools as lenses and presets. Preserve useful existing deep links, source ownership and state continuity. The primary analysis should occupy the primary space; multi-row navigation and explanatory chrome must not bury it. A strong 2D view and exact table are the default comparison for any proposed 3D experience.
