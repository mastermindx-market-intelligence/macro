## 1. Product thesis and architecture decisions

The product should help a person **find changing leadership, determine whether it is broad and supported, investigate the economic evidence, reach the relevant companies, and revisit the thesis with its original context intact**.

The premium quality comes from clear comparisons, coherent data and deep investigation. It does not require more top-level pages, more composite scores or an explanatory paragraph beneath every heading.

1. **One entrance and one composition.** Complete the existing Beta /sectors carrier. During migration, /discover?tab=sectors must resolve to the same accepted workspace or a state-preserving compatibility entrance.
2. **Retain analytical ownership.** Central, Leadership Rotation, Pulse, Cycles, GMI and industry research keep their current calculation, publication, rights and scientific owners.
3. **Retain four primary user jobs.** Brief, Explore, Research, Watches. Dossiers open from a selected entity; Cycles is a complete analytical workspace within the investigation, not a competing top-level product.
4. **Consolidate interfaces by function.** GMI supplies taxonomy, documented company/theme evidence and qualified relationships. Sector Central supplies market/sector readings. Industry sleeves supply specialized economic research. They do not become a single fused score.
5. **Make analytical basis explicit.** Market, universe, entity kind, membership, benchmark, return basis, weighting, horizon and source session remain recoverable throughout the journey.
6. **Use capability-level availability.** A current measured sector reading can coexist with an unavailable legacy rotation companion or an older dated filing. Do not give the whole page a false live/stale badge.
7. **Reuse generation and query owners.** Extend the existing sector-generation publication, authenticated gateway, native parsers and return-state serializer. No second data store, scheduler, graph, auth service, queue, retry service or alert evaluator.
8. **Improve insight before adding forecasts.** Prioritize change decomposition, breadth/concentration, disagreements, membership effects, evidence and economic transmission. New predictive labels require actual scientific qualification.
9. **Preserve full analytical depth.** Complete Cycles, history, exact values, methods, issuer/listing distinctions, saved investigation and uncertain-save behavior. Simplification changes placement, not meaning.
10. **Retire after parity.** Archive duplicate navigation and obsolete compositions only after their accepted jobs, links and state have a proven home. Do not delete valuable native components because they live in the Terminal repository.

## 3. The domain and data architecture

### 3.1 Keep entity kinds distinct

| Concept | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Sector / industry | Named taxonomy and classification level within a specified regional universe |
| Theme / subtheme | Potentially overlapping, source-defined grouping with membership evidence and version |
| Basket | Constructed instrument/group with its own weights, rebalance, benchmark and return method |
| Issuer / listing | Economic company identity versus exact listed security, venue and region |
| Industry sleeve | Existing specialized research vertical; retain its actual composer and evidence semantics |
| Observation | Measured condition or change, with source and time |
| Signal / call | Owner-issued rule/model result with horizon, eligibility and scientific authority |
| Research thesis | User-authored or source-grounded investigation, with evidence, contradiction and saved version |

Do not join by display name or ticker alone. Do not map a theme's companies into an industry simply because labels sound similar. Taxonomy association, an observed product connection, supplier/customer relationship and revenue exposure are different assertions.

In this program “sleeve” must not be silently converted into a tradable allocation sleeve or portfolio sizing mechanism.

### 3.2 Reuse the existing producer-to-consumer chain

The implementation should preserve this ownership path:

1. Existing licensed/source-specific ingestion and captured observations.
2. Existing identity, taxonomy, membership, rights and evidence owners.
3. Central / Pulse / Leadership Rotation / Cycles / GMI / specialized composers.
4. Existing generation/publication owner and bounded query projection.
5. Existing authenticated /api/sector-intelligence gateway and native parsers.
6. Shared Shell workspace, existing dossier/company/research/watch/return owners.

The current native gateway already reads eleven source families: sector, confluence, themes, heatmap, finviz, risk, history, events, relative_trend, holdings and intraday. Preserve its fixed upstream, authentication, body limits, cancellation, deadlines, private caching policy and per-source failure handling. Finviz retains its additional incumbent access gate. [S09]

Terminal [#1339](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/pull/1339) introduces consumption of /api/sector-generation?audience=preview through that same gateway. Reconcile the exact accepted response and audience contract before extending it. A new poller over static JSON would not close this seam. The existing 60-second visible-feed refresh is a consumer behavior, not producer freshness proof. [S10]

### 3.3 Consumer envelope: required semantics, adapted to existing schemas

Reconcile these fields with existing contracts in Round 3; do not create a parallel generic schema service:

- Canonical subject ID, kind, region and listing/issuer identity where applicable.
- Universe, taxonomy and effective membership versions; selection/cohort identity.
- Metric definition/version, units, horizon, benchmark, currency and weighting.
- Observation/session cutoff; available-at/known-at when historically supported; computed and published times.
- Generation/publication/revision and relevant native content hashes.
- Eligible, observed, usable and expected counts; exclusions and weight coverage.
- Evidence mode: observed, reconstructed, prior, model or otherwise owner-defined.
- Current rights/access scope and qualification outcome.
- Typed availability and a useful reason.
- Existing correction/supersession references where supplied; explicit absence where not.

Compatible measurements in a chart, table, dossier or comparison use the same qualified basis. Different source classes may legitimately have different clocks: a dated filing need not match a price close. The join owner must define whether the relation is current, historical or unavailable. The frontend cannot erase the difference or declare an arbitrary tolerance.

### 3.4 Availability and refresh behavior

Required user-distinguishable states: available, partial, stale, unavailable, invalid, incompatible, denied/restricted, offline, empty and changed/revised source. Use incumbent enums internally where they already exist.

- A valid zero is a measured value. Missing is not zero.
- Empty means a successful qualified query returned no qualifying records.
- Denied means the current actor may not see the result; it is not zero coverage.
- One optional feed failing must not disable navigation or unrelated valid instruments.
- Same-session revisions invalidate obsolete cursor/detail data through the existing generation owner.
- Old requests may not overwrite a new subject, window, account, locale-sensitive selection or publication.
- Do not silently replace a refused admitted-generation reading with old static data.
- Preserve a selected historical snapshot; offer deliberate movement to current data rather than silently rewriting an investigation.
- An always-available application does not imply real-time underlying financial observations.

### 3.5 Mathematical and scientific constraints

Preserve the native formulas and distinguish:

- Wealth-relative return: group wealth divided by benchmark wealth, minus one.
- Arithmetic excess: difference between returns, expressed in percentage points.
- Price return versus total return.
- Equal-weight, window-start weighted and rebalanced constructions.
- Sector coverage, constituent breadth, source coverage and company evidence coverage.
- Rank level, rank change, metric change and acceleration.
- Historical rank minus current rank is the existing positive-improvement convention; a sign count is not return magnitude.
- Membership changes, missing observations and cohort-normalization changes versus actual market movement.
- ETF fund flows versus price/volume activity proxies; no relabeling the latter as capital migration.

Current source explicitly records no demonstrated forward-return edge for Pulse heating/hot classifications. Central's US trend gate is drawdown/risk control, not a directional forecast. Current-membership baskets are not survivor-clean historical portfolios. Preserve historical hazard qualification, current MODEL parity requirements, PRIOR cells, sizing multipliers of 1.0 and the stopped lead-lag claims. A conditional grader hit rate is not a trading win rate. [S11][S12][S03]

No new “confidence” percentage or fused opportunity score enters the design without a defined owner, target, calibration and evaluation. A finite negative or underpowered result can close a research task while leaving the prediction unavailable.

### 4.3 A reusable industry research anatomy

The shared product anatomy should be consistent while the economics remain specialized:

1. **Current question:** the selected industry/company issue and observation date.
2. **Business mechanism:** how demand, supply, capacity, pricing, regulation or funding could affect the company.
3. **Evidence:** the actual source assertion, lifecycle, provenance and permitted scope.
4. **Transmission:** which linked companies/products are supported and which are unresolved.
5. **Counterevidence:** an adverse fact or missing dependency that matters to the thesis.
6. **Market response:** separately measured price/breadth/valuation behavior, without implied causality.
7. **Research continuity:** save the question and source context; monitor only actually supported conditions.

This is the shared reading model, not a new universal economic scoring model. Specialized data belongs in the vertical's own detail modules.

## 5. Unified experience and high-value capabilities

### 5.1 Navigation

| Primary job | Default responsibility | Deeper destinations |
|---|---|---|
| Brief | What leads, what changed, whether participation confirms it, and the next useful investigation | Selected entity, source date/coverage, focused evidence |
| Explore | Compare the market and inspect differences | Market map/list, rotation, breadth, flows, themes, complete Cycles, history |
| Research | Explain the economic connection and preserve the question | GMI themes/company evidence, industry sleeves, relationships, counter-theses, source-bound investigation |
| Watches | Reopen and monitor the subjects/conditions the user actually saved | Original snapshot versus latest, confirmed saves, unavailable monitoring, alert evidence |

Dossier is an entity drilldown, not a sixth competing top-level navigation job. Cycles retains its full controls and analytical depth inside Explore and entity workflows. Global shell navigation, account, language/theme, search and access remain owned by Shared Shell.

The exact query serialization is to be reconciled with parseSectorState/writeSectorState and current Macro hashes in Round 3. Do not introduce a second state router from this conceptual table.

### 5.2 Default screen composition

The first viewport should present:

- A compact subject/control header: Sector Intelligence, market and selected observation context.
- A concise measured conclusion or an honest unavailable/partial state.
- A useful leadership/participation comparison with directly readable values and small trends.
- A short set of meaningful changes or divergences.
- One clear next investigation action for the selected object.

Prefer the useful ranked comparison over a large decorative hero. Do not simultaneously show every methodology, evidence class, model state and performance table. A selected-entity inspector should add depth without replacing the analytical context. It becomes a modal/full-screen detail at narrow widths and restores its opener.

Market, window and benchmark controls must affect the appropriate measurements atomically. A control may not visually imply that it changes an independent five-session rank statistic or a dated economic source when it does not.
