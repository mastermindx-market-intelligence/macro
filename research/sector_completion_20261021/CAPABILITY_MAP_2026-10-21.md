(macro #7646, Sector Intelligence completion deliverable "all retained feature and
engine mappings"). This table records what the tree HAS, not what it should have: for

and the actual Macro route/control that renders it. The same two strings live in the

observed divergences from the archived row wording.

Conventions: `file.py::function` is the writer; `→ site/…json[keys]` is the artifact
and its top-level keys; `#anchor` / `data-view` / JS function names are the live route.
`NOT FOUND (planned)` marks completion-upgrade rows with no current producer or route.
