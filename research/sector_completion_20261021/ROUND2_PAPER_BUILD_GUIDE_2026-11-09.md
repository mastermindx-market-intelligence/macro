## Interaction contracts for all new frames

1. Controls apply only to their named metric. No global window silently alters fixed-horizon ranks or dated research.
2. Selected entity identity includes kind, region, universe/listing and opener. New selection retires stale responses. Loading cannot retain the previous entity's detail under a new title.
3. Named returns restore filters, selected entities, window/basis, scroll and disclosure state through existing serializers. Preserve the 1024-character public and 8192-character same-tab distinctions unless the owner is explicitly evolved.
4. Watch membership, research save, device-local note and alert activation are distinct. A single Saved message cannot represent all four.
5. Pending/uncertain save reads the original record. No unconditional retry while effect is unknown. Known failure may permit one explicit retry through the existing writer. Version conflict preserves both versions.
6. Unreadable account data is not an empty watchlist. Changed account retires private prior-account content.
7. Dated GMI relationship = exact documented assertion, date, lifecycle and source scope. Planned product connection is not shipment, revenue exposure, relationship confidence or trade authority.
8. Cycles timing uses confirmed anchor and same cohort; quartiles/median of half-cycle spacing are typical historical timing, not probability bounds. Window does not slide. Insufficient/cohort-mismatched state remains unavailable.
9. Current-model refusal and historical prior remain separate. Old 14% parity-failed fixture is never a current forecast; historical 33%/52% priors keep their original population/qualification.
10. China retains local taxonomy, session, listing, benchmark and rights. Unsupported taxonomy comparison refuses the comparison while preserving both separate investigations.
11. Named disclosures work through touch and keyboard; focus enters heading/first useful control and returns to exact opener. Escape closes a modal; focus is contained while open. No essential hover-only content.
12. Reduced motion removes entrance/chart draw/pulse and keeps the same final visual information. Frozen/historical reads do not pulse.
13. Light uses white panel structure on a deeper cool canvas, crisp borders and restrained shadow. Dark uses Noir luminance depth. Direction signs/text remain visible; ZH directional inks flip, health does not.
14. Mobile becomes a deliberate reading/detail path; inspector becomes full-screen depth. No horizontal page overflow; wide exact-value tables scroll within a named region.
