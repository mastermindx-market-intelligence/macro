## Direction: sophisticated charts, simple questions

The old R1 card-wall omitted much of the analytical depth already described in our earlier research. R2 restores charts as the core research instrument.

Use a quiet shared shell; one short plain-language read; one dominant analytical view; a compact interpretation column; progressive detail through a source/snapshot inspector. Thin precise chart strokes, low-contrast grids, aligned time axes, explicit units and benchmarks, direct series labels, restrained regime colors, and generous spacing matter more than ornamental glass or extra tiles. Light and dark are separate contrast compositions. Mobile must reorganize the task and preserve usable labels/targets.

Bitcoin: Overview → Cycle & risk → Strategy → On-chain → Derivatives.
Crypto: Market → Assets → Capital & leverage → Events.
Cross-links carry asset, selected snapshot and return context. Preserve useful existing Cycle Lab, strategy comparison, derivatives/on-chain research and crypto-equity dossiers rather than flattening the product into an introductory brief.

Glance answers what is happening and what the model says. Context explains what supports it, what could invalidate it and whether its evidence is available. Study depth retains full methods, original observations, model version, counterevidence and evaluation. Provenance must support—not replace—the user workflow.
