# Commission 15 — Primary source register

**Research cutoff: October 4, 2026 (UTC).** This register supports the [hardened report](COMMISSION_15_HARDENED_REPORT.md), [current-state audit](CURRENT_STATE_AND_HARDENING_LOG.md), and [validation protocol](VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md). The E01–E23 and O1–O32 identifiers match the report. URLs identify the publisher's documents; mutable pages and files must subsequently be preserved with retrieval time, version and digest in an authorized evidence store.

## How to read the evidence

**Protocol/rule** means an official specification, order or regulatory statement was inspected. **Documentation** means an official product, schema or API description was inspected. **Terms** means publicly presented conditions were inspected. **Research** identifies a primary paper, author page or abstract, with access limits stated below. A cataloged capability does not establish an executed license, Mastermind entitlement, complete historical availability, faithful normalization, or performance on an actual sample.

This commission did not acquire a new licensed dataset, inspect private vendor contracts, validate live feed samples, or execute the proposed empirical program. Existing repository findings have their own pinned evidence in the current-state audit. Vendor pages cannot prove those runtime findings. A commercial all-in cost remains **unknown** until the exact instruments, history, schema, delivery, users and permitted uses receive a dated quote and agreement review.

## Admission consequences and unresolved evidence

The source register changes what can responsibly enter the implementation handoff. Trade-with-BBO can qualify trade-location measurements and trade-sampled spreads. Continuous quote observations are a separate dependency for elapsed-time liquidity and book-event OFI. Native order events are an additional dependency for cancellation or queue claims. Participant/open-close products address a different identification problem from aggressor-side classification. These distinctions must survive the vendor comparison and acceptance criteria.

For each accepted delivery, the later owner must establish six concrete receipts:

1. **Coverage and era:** actual instruments, venues, sessions, fields and overlapping historical dates; beginning-of-window state and missingness denominator.
2. **Clock and correction:** source timestamp meanings and precision; event versus report time; quote-join ordering; original/correction linkage; sequence/reset scope; publication and collector receipt.
3. **Version:** feed/API/parser generation, effective reference definitions, historical archive vintage and immutable input/derived digests. Future changes listed here remain prospective until observed.
4. **Rights and economics:** executed entitlement and approved analytical/display/distribution uses, ongoing retention and termination treatment, complete recurring and historical charges, storage/egress and operating burden.
5. **Measurement:** an authorized fixture showing field fidelity, unsupported conditions, correction handling, gap behavior and abstention. Documentation coverage is not sample coverage.
6. **Utility:** prospective or historically admissible validation against incumbent controls, with labels and features available at their respective decision times. A classification agreement, contemporaneous relationship or smooth surface is insufficient promotion evidence.

Historical market reconstruction must identify its hypothetical entitled observer and latency assumptions. Actual Mastermind replay requires evidence that Mastermind possessed and could use the records at that time. A current archive, current license or historical event timestamp cannot retroactively prove possession. Where original availability cannot be reconstructed, prospective shadow collection is the evidence path; it remains a proposed follow-on activity, not work performed by this commission.
