# Commission 15 — Equity & Options Market Microstructure

**Completed research commission · hardened October 4, 2026**

## Recommendation

Qualify and extend the **existing** ThetaData/Flow, Terminal Quote Plane, Macro intelligence, Radar and evaluation owners. Start with reproducible measurement and a bounded source/receipt census; test one explicit entry-context question before purchasing more data or promoting new evidence.

The estate already has options trade/NBBO measurements and accepted historical observations. Its important remaining gaps are source/receipt qualification, current consumption and the distinction between measured quote location, participant intent and demonstrated decision utility. Full quote-event data is required for continuous OFI, quote duration and liquidity withdrawal; a quote attached to a trade does not supply those intervening events.

## Scope and next action

This publication completes the research and hardening assignment. It contains no production implementation, purchased data, new entitlement, licensed raw tape, private research corpus, live activation or portfolio/trading change.

Section **L** of the main report contains the exact proposed follow-on commission. It is **written, not executed**. Acceptance of this research does not itself activate a source, clear a signing/candidate gate or authorize trading. A later implementation owner must bootstrap current authority and issue the bounded wave through existing owners.

The source census is explicitly dated. Later repository changes do not alter the immutable evidence cited here; material future changes require a scoped reconciliation.
