## Why and when this kernel is useful

Commission 15 compares **the same incumbent Radar/entry candidate** with a frozen baseline B and a frozen challenger; the challenger must not invent new candidates, directions or sizing. A 30-trading-minute outcome cannot be part of the original decision. The source owner must provide original candidate and quote receipts, and the evaluation owner must freeze source/admission, costs, policy, denominator, market-calendar, missingness, testing budget and statistical design **before** examining outcome data. Do not treat `source_mode="ACTUAL_AS_SEEN"` supplied in a dictionary as evidence of genuine custody.

The pure entry point is:

```python
engine.market_microstructure.paired_entry_utility.evaluate_same_candidate(
    candidate=..., baseline=..., challenger=...,
    evaluation_cutoff_ns=..., source_watermark_ns=...,
    watermark_received_ns=..., market_health=...,
    market_health_receipt_sha256=...,
)
```

The strict input dictionaries require one `candidate_id`, ticker/session, supplied evidence mode, frozen original decision/deadline, exact 30-minute **same-session** nominal endpoint, externally supplied calendar receipt, frozen reference `Q0` as research shares (not trade sizing), and `M0` sourced from one independently supplied original **NBBO bid/ask quote** with its own original event-time/available-time/receipt, valid spread and positive size. The research denominator is **recomputed from that bid/ask midpoint and checked for exact equality** to supplied `initial_midpoint_usd`; it cannot be picked independently to amplify a result. This is only an internal contradiction check, *not* independent source authentication. The candidate source and the anchor quote must have been available strictly before decision; their receipt references and max quote-age ≤5s are carried explicitly. The reference `lib.nyse_calendar.is_session` checks the nominal full-session calendar. **Native integer nanosecond clocks are compared directly against exact New York 09:30 and 16:00 boundaries**: a 30-minute horizon one nanosecond after the nominal close is refused, not rounded/truncated down into the valid session. The same original cutoff/H rule applies even to an *unreported* frozen-cohort candidate, so unobservable rows cannot quietly evade the original session identity. This local constraint does **not** qualify early closes or special live halts: `calendar_qualified_by_this_function=false` **always**. The owner must independently prove the exact 30 trading-minute clock, unscheduled halts and early-close model. This v0 intentionally refuses cross-session windows instead of silently inventing overnight continuity.

Both policies must use the **same candidate ID** and freeze **strictly before** the original decision (same-nanosecond ties without native sequencing abstain). The challenger requires an independently supplied consumer receipt and first-available clock **strictly before** that decision; a missing or late overlay becomes **UNOBSERVABLE** rather than a retroactive success. Both hypothetical entry and exit use **strictly prior**, age-limited, crossable NBBO, source-received **strictly before** the relevant entry/horizon instant, and enough displayed top-of-book size for the same frozen `Q0`. A quote whose native event occurred before H but whose local receipt arrived after H **cannot** be simulated as executable at H; it remains UNOBSERVABLE even if a later matured response label might use it as descriptive research. **Both full-fill policies must refer to the identical terminal quote generation** (same source receipt, timestamp, bid/ask and displayed size); giving each policy a different favorable exit snapshot at the same fixed H is a consistency refusal. The kernel chooses **ASK-to-enter/BID-to-exit for longs**, **BID-to-enter/ASK-to-exit for shorts**. It never accepts caller-picked favorable entry/exit prices. These are at best *quote-side research prices*, not broker/exchange fills, impact estimates or guaranteed best execution.

Explicit `fees_usd` are positive/nonnegative fixed decimal **currency costs** only, excluding the spread already paid by quote crossing. Fractions preserve exact arithmetic for both policies:

```text
N0 = Q0 × M0  (frozen for both policies)
U(policy) = 10,000 × [ d × Q0 × (Pexit − Pentry) − fees_usd ] / N0
delta_U = U(challenger) − U(baseline)
```

All three outcomes include exact rational numerator/denominator plus 12-decimal display-only bps; rounding is **not** evidence of zero. This projection uses **pure integer half-even rounding**, not a finite Decimal context that overflows on a bounded but 1024-digit adversarial ratio. The original rational always remains unrounded, and even a tiny negative value may deliberately display signed rounded zero rather than silently losing the original sign. Malformed source modes, policy states, absence reasons and inconsistent wide cohort ratios are **typed fail-closed ValueErrors**, never unhandled host-language exceptions or fabricated measurements. A deliberate frozen no-entry decision with a receipt available before deadline and completed evaluation horizon is assigned zero utility; missing quotes/source, stale best quotes, insufficient size, market halt/unknown health, immature source watermark or a feature received too late never become zero returns. Their results have typed `NOT_MATURE`, `CENSORED` or `UNOBSERVABLE` with **null** paired numbers. Signed fractional outcomes are possible, with no automatic sign authority.

### Reported outcome maturity and censorship cannot hide study observations

An independent review found that the old cohort accountant could take an actually completed pair (including a losing challenger), erase its three utility values and falsely label it `NOT_MATURE` or `CENSORED` with a syntactically allowed reason. Both outcomes were accepted, reducing the measured subset and potentially biasing the observed mean. The **source-watermark native endpoint, source-watermark receipt time, market-health category and asserted market-health source receipt SHA** are now conserved in every evaluator output and validated by the same source-free cohort accountant. A `NOT_MATURE` row must **actually fail** the existing horizon/watermark-availability predicate; a `CENSORED` row must have a mature horizon but halted/unknown reported market health; a measured or source-unobservable outcome requires a mature horizon and normal reported health. This preserves the evaluator's priority of maturity over market status and stops *internally contradictory* retrospective label substitution, without treating loss of source as an abstention.

The cohort still **does not verify** that its supplied watermark, health value or SHA were originally received on the live source at the asserted time. A forged dictionary can forge both status and source metadata; only the original source/TrialLedger owner can authenticate those bytes and consumer delivery. This is receipt/consistency conservation within synthetic software, never a claim of completed RTH source validation or legitimate censoring rate.

The additional `supplied_input_first_knowable_ns` is a lower bound derived from the supplied source watermark and exit/abstention receipts, **not** an actual Terminal or end-user receipt. It is always accompanied by `supplied_input_first_knowable_not_consumer_receipt=true`.

The result always declares `source_authenticated=false`, `calendar_qualified_by_this_function=false`, `actual_consumer_delivery_proven=false`, `simulated_execution_not_fill=true`, `prospective_promotion=false`, `predictive_rank_trading_alert_authority=false` and `forward_outcome_available_to_original_decision=false`. It writes no data or TrialLedger. Use the incumbent `engine.trial_ledger.TrialLedger` externally for actual preregistered challenger counts; nothing here mints a trial, validation result or registry.


## Denominator-complete cohort accounting — no second TrialLedger

The same pure module also exports:

```python
reconcile_frozen_candidate_cohort(
    expected_candidates=original_frozen_candidate_manifest_rows,
    supplied_evaluations=source_free_paired_results,
    frozen_manifest_sha256=original_manifest_digest,
)
```

The **incumbent candidate registry** supplies the previously frozen complete population. This function does not create, mutate, acquire or authorize that registry. Each expected row binds candidate ID, ticker, session, evidence mode, original decision, exact same-session horizon and supplied original source/calendar receipt digests. **Critically, the externally frozen manifest must also include the original source-available timestamp, deadline, incumbent direction, research quantity Q0, anchor midpoint M0, maximum quote-age, and the exact original quote bid/ask, bid/ask sizes, event/arrival clocks and quote receipt SHA.** Those fields are validated in the original manifest even for candidates with missing or censored outcomes, and are carried *unchanged* into every individual result (including NOT_MATURE and CENSORED). Evaluations must match this complete immutable candidate economic/clock basis and the source-independent research/false-authority schema; unknown, duplicate, cross-symbol, cross-session, changed Q0/direction/M0/quote-vintage/deadline or later source-generation results are refused. The prior software version permitted nine verified after-the-fact changes to the original economic/receipt basis because it checked only candidate ID, symbol, session, source hash and T/H; the new source-free contract closes that internal inconsistency. The cohort also requires **exact native JSON types**, not merely equal-looking numerical values. Python considers `True == 1` and may consider an integer-nanosecond clock equal to its floating-point representation; accepting either would let later result schemas change original incumbent direction and source-time precision while preserving an apparently matching value. All frozen candidate, policy receipt/consumer clock, original decision/H, trading-minute and policy-count comparisons now retain the original types. Floating-point nanoseconds, booleans posing as integer direction, and floating-point counts are typed refusals even when their magnitudes compare equal. This is a software consistency gate, not source authenticity.

The supplied digest strings are internal consistency assertions, **not** cryptographically verified external source ownership. The frozen-manifest digest is carried as a caller-supplied *reference* and is not recomputed to assert equivalence to another owner's source-byte serialization; only that original registry/TrialLedger owner can authenticate the real manifest and policy freezes. This source-free improvement blocks internal candidate-basis drift **within** supplied inputs, but must not be mistaken for actual original market-source receipts or baseline/challenger policy custody.

### Original policy-freeze conservation

In addition to the original candidate's 13 immutable economic/clock fields, the externally registered study row must identify both frozen baseline/challenger policy versions and freeze instants, the challenger's original independently supplied consumer receipt/time (including explicit absence or lateness), and each policy's originally declared scalar research execution-fee schedule. `evaluate_same_candidate` conserves these asserted fields even when a candidate is censored or not mature. `reconcile_frozen_candidate_cohort` refuses changing a frozen policy receipt, freeze instant, consumer arrival or a full-fill fee after the outcome is known. A legitimate receipt-bearing `FROZEN_ABSTAIN` may incur zero fees regardless of the preregistered potential full-fill fee; it never gains utility just for not trading. The accountant also rejects a supposedly completed paired observation claiming an `UNOBSERVABLE` policy action or attaching nonzero return to a frozen abstention.

**External verification still governs:** A 64-character SHA string does not demonstrate that the purported policy rules, original entry action, consumer delivery, abstention receipt, or fee assumptions were actually recorded before T. This pure diagnostic does not compute a new policy digest, authenticate the owning TrialLedger, implement a second registration, or validate a market source. `ACTUAL_AS_SEEN` and other evidence-mode labels cannot grant that authority.

The output carries `n_expected`, `n_supplied`, `n_missing`, the exact missing IDs (private only), counts of `PAIRED_RESEARCH_UTILITY` / `NOT_MATURE` / `CENSORED` / `UNOBSERVABLE`, and the number of evaluable software pairs. Absent reports **remain in the frozen denominator**. A supplied result with a false source state, changed evidence mode, post-cutoff label, improperly formatted exact rational or `delta != challenger - baseline` is rejected. Mixing `FINAL_VINTAGE` with `ACTUAL_AS_SEEN` in one pooled mean is refused. Exact fractions and their display-only rounded strings are internally reconciled.

The optional `observed_pair_subset_mean_bps` is **not the preregistered full-population estimand**. It computes only the observed synthetic/research-eligible pairs, even when all expected IDs are accounted for, and includes no block uncertainty, untouched confirmation or causal price-impact inference. `primary_population_mean_bps` stays **null by design** until the authentic source, consumer-availability, market-calendar, trial-count, weighting and missingness owners complete their existing admission and freeze. All cohort `source_authenticated`, `actual_consumer_delivery_proven`, `denominator_and_missingness_proven_by_this_function`, `empirical_source_qualified`, `inference_or_power_established`, `trial_ledger_written`, `prospective_promotion` and `predictive_rank_trading_alert_authority` flags remain false. Passing these fixtures does **not** turn a model-created manifest into actual historical data or a verified trial.

This nonpersisting accountant is bounded to 1–1,000 frozen candidates per request; longer cohorts stay with the incumbent batch owners. It does not write the existing `engine.trial_ledger.TrialLedger`, choose policies, perform inference, create a new lifecycle, or let unobserved candidates disappear from coverage.
