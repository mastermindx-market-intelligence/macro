## 5. Historical rhythm and exact fixture arithmetic

project_next filters provisional turns and requires at least three confirmed turns. It uses the ordered intervals between consecutive confirmed turns across the retained input history, not merely the visible chart window. The central date is last confirmed pivot plus median interval; low/high add the 25th/75th percentile intervals. This is a historical interquartile duration range translated from a pivot, not a calibrated confidence interval.

The range stays anchored when elapsed; it does not roll toward today or imply that a reversal is imminent. A historical extremum date is not its first observation or confirmation time. legacy_turn_knowledge does not grant then-known history when confirmation is reconstructed from current tape.

The illustrative ordered interval durations are 0.33, 0.20, 0.46, 0.30, 0.40, 0.25, 0.36 and 0.33 years. They require nine alternating confirmed turns ending at a 10 September 2026 trough. The eight intervals have median 0.33, 25th percentile 0.2875 and 75th percentile 0.37 years.

_x_to_ym rounds the fractional year multiplied by 12; its high month is February, even though a calendar-day approximation may suggest January. Preserve the emitted month precision. Any formatter correction is a separately versioned owner change, not a frontend substitution.

period_yrs.median is twice the median inter-turn interval. The product calls it 全周期时长参考 and explicitly says it is converted from twice the interval median. It is not an independently calculated median of complete trough-to-trough cycles. The older renderer's half-cycle wording must not survive the migration.

Supporting interval count, exact turn set and confirmation/knowledge-time provenance need explicit owner projections. n_turns_all can include a provisional endpoint and cannot serve as the independent interval count.
