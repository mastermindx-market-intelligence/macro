## Product decisions

1. Preserve one shared shell, context, identity and navigation owner; use its actual available content width.
2. Give each page a distinct composition suited to its task. Do not repeat an undifferentiated card grid.
3. Open with subject, meaningful current state and useful next action. Move mechanics and provenance to named, keyboard/touch-accessible detail. Keep consequential uncertainty visible beside the affected fact.
4. No decorative colored edges, invented scores, fake live stamps, excessive badges, duplicated timestamps or builder narration in customer frames.
5. Keep a named home for every existing capability. Use RETAIN / IMPROVE / RELOCATE / REMOVE / BLOCKED_DATA at the component level.
6. Product, interaction/help states and Build references stay separated in Paper. Preserve prior studies and sole-copy requirements.
7. Desktop selected-detail panes become full-width sheets/pages on phones; closing returns focus, filters, selection and scroll. Route state is shareable and recoverable through Back/Forward.
8. Samples are design specimens, not verified current market facts. Do not turn a composed prototype into a claim of real-time coverage or production readiness.
