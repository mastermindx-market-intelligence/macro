## I. Flow semantic contract

Flow is an observation layer. It can report the exact contract, source print price/size/time/condition, measured trade location relative to a valid matched NBBO, quote age/spread/coverage, and single-contract urgency. It cannot determine customer, institution, opening/closing, intent, conviction, direction or expected return from those facts alone. The vendor's exchange sequence is not a parent-order identity. [I10–I12; E01–E04, E14]

At the analysis pin, the coalescer is single-contract. The sweep rule expresses rapid multi-venue prints of that contract; it does not discover other legs. The new semantic layer must preserve this meaning. A ratio of covered prints or premium is not a confidence score: current source checks do not impose a universal maximum quote age. A fully covered batch can therefore still contain stale execution evidence. [I10]

The canonical extension must retain raw field provenance and reason-coded nulls. The measured block's count/premium coverage, ask/bid/inside/outside fractions, spread, median/max age and related fields remain descriptive arithmetic. Quotes that are future, invalid, crossed/locked under the accepted ruler, missing or outside the eligible condition domain cannot yield a positive quality claim. A new freshness policy is preregistered by the owner; this amendment does not silently insert thresholds.

Do not equate the current default NBBO matcher with an immutable execution contract. Pin request version and `exclusive` semantics. Preserve original source observation, ingest/availability and match-policy version so a later vendor correction does not contaminate replay. Public historical event-time alignment alone is not proof of original as-known availability.

Do not change existing event identity to accommodate Package. Audit source/session/venue sequence scope and collision cases under the same identity owner. Existing raw component fields in a collector DataFrame are not proof those components survive immutably through the canonical event path. That retention edge is a prerequisite for reproducible package association—not an invitation to create another tape store.

## J. Package semantic contract

### J1. Two separate questions

**Association:** which observed components have evidence of belonging to one reported execution structure? **Economics:** what payoff can be computed from known leg identity, orientation, quantity/ratio, price and deliverables? These are not the same question, and neither answers who traded or why.

Use the following evidence states as the proposed integration vocabulary; the owner must approve the exact schema spelling/version before code:

| State | Required meaning | Prohibited implication |
|---|---|---|
| SINGLE_LEG_SUPPORTED | Positive evidence supports the execution being single-leg under the specific mechanism/data contract. | One observed contract proves the customer's whole strategy is single-leg. |
| PACKAGE_CANDIDATE | A preregistered association rule links observed components provisionally. | Parent/order identity or a resolved economic strategy is proven. |
| STRUCTURE_RESOLVED | Qualified linkage plus complete relevant legs, orientation and ratios support the stated structure. | Customer identity, opening intent, informedness or original position ownership. |
| PARTIAL | Some components or attributes are supported and others are missing. | The missing stock or option leg may be invented. |
| UNRESOLVED | Current source cannot support stronger association/economic claims. | The print was definitely independent or definitely not complex. |
| CONFLICTING | Material evidence supports incompatible interpretations. | The most attractive story may be chosen by score or LLM. |
| EXCLUDED | Unsupported condition, rights or domain prevents this use. | Zero exposure, zero value or a negative economic outcome. |

Native complex-condition evidence can coexist with `UNRESOLVED`: it identifies a mechanism without supplying every leg or common-parent map. Complex-instrument identity is not unique customer-order identity; an exchange execution ID has its own namespace/reset/partial-fill rules. A constant or auction-oriented side field is not automatically aggressor direction. [E04–E05, E08–E10]

### J2. No parallel lifecycle

A package-evidence object is a derived, rebuildable association revision referencing canonical observations and the versions/cutoff used. A content hash may identify that exact derived association payload; it is not a newly authoritative trade, order, customer or campaign ID. The candidate retains references to the existing episode/campaign owners.

The current campaign groups an exact contract within a session and is nightly-only. It is not a cross-contract package container. A package can reference several canonical component/campaign records without mutating their identity or implying they were available earlier. A narrow owner-approved extension is necessary where present canonical retention or publication cannot support the derived view. [I20–I21]

### J3. Association is preregistered and falsifiable

Freeze the eligible source population, condition domain, event-time and availability windows, contract restrictions, quantity/ratio tolerance, venue/identifier scope, correction behavior, candidate grouping and tie/conflict policy before evaluating examples or outcomes. Unknown fields are not silently neutral positive evidence. Published example thresholds in #7027 remain research proposals, not production defaults.

Use null controls that preserve relevant market intensity: within-session blocked time shifts, alternative pairings outside the registered window, unrelated-root controls where appropriate, and dense-print cases where coincidence is likely. Count ambiguous and missing-ground-truth cases separately. A source lacking qualified native linkage cannot certify resolved-package precision merely by agreement between two heuristics. No favorable-return example may select the association policy.

### J4. First lawful vertical

The first slice is **condition-aware package truth**, not a grand detector. Choose one eligible production underlying by a prospectively specified data-quality/liquidity selection rule, not by future returns or familiarity. The actual symbol remains unselected because the current runtime/atomic-retention and collision gates are not closed.

After admission: canonical observations → preserved mechanism/component evidence → typed package state → existing product drill-down → tests and natural receipt. Show a real supported or honestly unresolved case. Demonstrate missing-leg/conflict behavior in adversarial fixtures, and in natural data only when actually encountered. If resolution needs unavailable identifiers, passing acceptance means truthful unresolved behavior, not a fabricated resolved spread.

### M4. Package accounting

Freeze signed leg quantities, actual deliverables/multipliers, net entry cash flow, costs and exit cash flow. Positive quantity denotes the long leg in the accounting contract; it is not inferred from an ask-side print without sufficient evidence. For each known oriented leg, the accounting identity is quantity × multiplier × (exit price − entry price), summed across legs and reduced by costs; applicability depends on the exact structure and settlement/assignment obligations. A percentage return needs a declared capital/risk denominator. Undefined or unmeasured risk does not get an invented “return on maximum loss.”

Synchronized single-leg quotes are not the same evidence as an executable native complex quote. If the accepted ruler uses hypothetical legging, label it explicitly and apply its simultaneity, slippage and completion policy. Missing material stock/option legs or unhandled assignment/exercise makes whole-package economics unavailable. Exclude unsupported structure classes prospectively rather than dropping their losing outcomes later.

An invalidated candidate does not vanish. Missing NBBO is not automatically the same statistical censoring mechanism as an observation window ending. Report total formed, eligible, valid, mature, outcome-observable, unavailable, corrected and still-censored counts at each layer.

The 2026 survival-scoring preprint offers a useful research principle: evaluate the actually observable outcome law and state censoring assumptions. It does not justify treating liquidity-driven missing quotes as independent censoring or using untested inverse-probability weights. Standard binary calibration is evaluated only on the properly defined matured target population, with missingness and sensitivity reporting; any survival target needs its own preregistration. [E22]
