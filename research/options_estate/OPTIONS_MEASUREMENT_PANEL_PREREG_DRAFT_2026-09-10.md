# Options measurement-panel preregistration draft

**Date:** 2026-09-10. **Status:** prepared, not frozen/admitted or executed. Not a registered alpha experiment. Parent: WS:OPTIONS-ALPHA-INTELLIGENCE-RECOVERY. Existing draft carrier #7027; no new collector/storage/lifecycle authority.

## Research question and continuation

Within the licensed and supported C1 population, which attributes of observed executions can the current canonical event evidence recover reliably, and when must it abstain? The primary target is interpretation error/coverage, not stock-return prediction or profit.

This draft completes the preparation portion of the next research action in `NIGHTGLASS_HARDENING_AND_MODEL_RESEARCH_2026-09-10.md`. The remaining action is source-owner review of the rights, label and clock questions, followed by formal admission/freeze under existing OA law. Preparation is not registration, acquisition, execution or acceptance.

## Admission before obtaining rows

Confirm commercial/internal research rights, whether calibration/model development is permitted, permitted publication of aggregate/derived results, entitled execution linkage, coverage by mechanism, corrections/finality and historical field availability. The current TBT product page prohibits external redistribution. The specification and SEC filing describe execution IDs differently; obtain an explicit answer for the intended non-member entitlement.

No paid order, external contact, proprietary-row download or training job is initiated. A sample does not grant production redistribution or all-market inference rights.

## Unit and population

Primary measurement unit: an eligible execution-side record linked to a unique execution/package where supported. Report transactions, contracts, premium weights and participant-side counts separately. Determine whether both sides appear and validate pairing before aggregation.

Use the existing live-event eligible universe for the overlapping supported C1 subset, not a new profitable-looking universe. Record the unmapped population. Separate simple continuous-book, eligible auction mechanisms, floor, complex-to-complex, legged-in and unresolved/provisional executions. Source limitations may reduce supported domains but may not silently redefine labels.

Dates, roots, exchange coverage and sample sizes must be frozen before label-dependent performance inspection. Initial structural QA can determine mechanical eligibility, but cannot tune a predictor or choose a favorable evaluation window. Preserve QA changes and freeze identity under the existing research owner.

## Population, policy and source-attribution receipt

The same surface name may represent different populations and rulers over time. Every enrolled observation must therefore retain a stable `population_id` and the exact admission receipt that made it eligible. At minimum, freeze:

- `population_id`, `population_definition_hash` and observation-window dates;
- `admission_policy_version`, its immutable human-readable receipt and source commit/release;
- source channel and product lane: live alert, EOD confirmation, structure screen, combined candidate, editorial/model promotion or operator issue;
- `candidate_id`, existing `campaign_id`, exact instrument/package identity and correction/revision identity;
- `event_at`, `observed_at`, `decision_at`, `available_at` and, where measured, `client_consumed_at`;
- `entry_policy_version`, `quote_policy_version`, `outcome_ruler_version` and horizon/deadline;
- `flow_context_source`, `structure_context_source` and `editorial_or_model_promotion_source` as separate provenance fields;
- `entry_eligible`, `entry_expired_reason`, unresolved/no-entry state and final terminal state.

These are enrollment/evaluation fields on existing owners or a reviewed projection. They do not create a second event, campaign, outcome, queue, model-control or Issue Desk plane.

A later rule description, threshold exception or product redesign may create a new policy version; it may not silently relabel an earlier decision. Marketing output counts are not outcome populations. Public benchmark rows may guide questions but do not supply labels or competitor features to this study.

## Labels

Opening/closing is the source's execution-side attribute, not beneficial-owner motive. Buy/sell is not automatically aggressor. Define eligible initiating-side semantics per mechanism from exchange documentation/confirmation; unsupported mechanisms stay unknown. Label package association only where linkage semantics warrant it. Unresolved cross-mechanism/exchange connections remain unresolved.

There is no institutionally-informed ground truth merely from capacity. Future profitable price action is not a substitute label for current execution side.

## Inputs and clocks

Freeze the measured trade/quote event and revision available at the original cutoff. T+1 information supplies a later evaluation label only. Floor agreement, booking, receipt/availability and correction times remain distinct. Future label quality and later quotes must not leak into earlier inputs.

A missing contemporaneous quote produces baseline abstention. Never select whichever adjacent quote agrees with the later label. Clock-lag/spread strata and thresholds are specified before results. Current versus future correction-handling changes in the source require explicit semantic versions.

## Baselines and endpoints

Baseline A: raw contract/price facts without directional interpretation. Baseline B: frozen current measured quote-location policy. Baseline C: transparent mechanism-stratified policy only after mechanism-label definitions are accepted. A learned M1 model is a subsequent comparison, not a prerequisite.

Each policy returns established fields, unresolved fields and coverage. Primary endpoint: wrong-side interpretation among covered eligible cases, accompanied by coverage over the full preregistered eligible population. Opening/closing and eligible package association are separate endpoints, not ingredients of a new universal confidence score.

Secondary endpoints: probabilistic calibration where admitted; conditional error by mechanism/quote quality; association precision/recall on eligible linked cases; unmatched/multiply matched quantity; correction stability; and deterioration outside the development domain.

Good measurement accuracy does not establish alpha. Outcome prediction requires a separate estimand, scope ruling and economic experiment.

## Separate outcome families and attribution arms

Do not use one result column for different jobs. Preserve at least:

1. **measurement quality** — execution-side/open-close/package interpretation and abstention coverage;
2. **level quality** — touch, reaction, close acceptance, break/reclaim and time-to-event;
3. **plan viability** — first-available entry window, objective, defense, invalidation and reward/risk;
4. **exact-instrument economics** — executable quote-to-quote return, fees, quantity, capacity and management.

A target touched intraday and a support/invalidation evaluated on a daily close are different event definitions. Both may be true on one path. Neither may be translated into exact-option profit without an admitted execution policy.

If a later alpha experiment is separately authorized, preregister these comparison arms rather than one fused score:

- existing Mastermind policy;
- measured-flow-only candidate;
- first-available structure-only candidate;
- flow-plus-structure candidate with dependence retained;
- same evidence with an explicit editorial/model-promotion edge;
- explicit operator-issued/managed candidate under the existing Issue Desk.

For shared candidates, use paired comparisons to isolate incremental context. Separately evaluate the full policies, including candidates originated by only one arm, rejected candidates, abstentions, unavailable inputs and no-entry expiration. Do not count repeated notifications from one campaign as independent opportunities.

A later target or editorial selection cannot retroactively change which source originated an earlier candidate. Structure-only outcomes remain structure-only; flow context added later remains a revision with its own availability clock.

## Sweep, package-association and missingness sub-study

The current legacy enrichment makes three questions inseparable that this preregistration must evaluate independently:

1. **single-contract urgency:** did one contract print rapidly across venues or repeatedly through the book;
2. **cross-contract association:** do two or more contract executions belong to one package or coherent staged structure;
3. **evidence availability:** which quote, OI, baseline and moneyness fields were actually knowable when the event/candidate was formed.

The immutable component event remains one contract. `sweep_like` is an urgency observation and may not serve as the package ground truth. A derived package candidate must reference the exact component event IDs, evidence clocks and any source-supplied execution/package linkage without rewriting those events.

### Package-label eligibility

Positive package labels are admitted only when the entitled source semantics support the linkage for the relevant mechanism. A pair of similar-size, nearby-in-time legs without an authoritative link is a `package_candidate`, not a confirmed package. Unsupported cross-exchange, cross-mechanism or corrected cases remain unknown rather than negative.

Freeze separate test populations for:

- linked simple verticals;
- linked call/put combinations and risk reversals;
- complex-to-complex and floor mechanisms where documented;
- legged-in or partially linked executions;
- single-contract multi-exchange sweeps;
- temporal/size near-misses that are not linked packages;
- unresolved and multiply matched component events.

### Baselines

- **Package baseline D — current proxy:** `event.swept -> MULTI_LEG`. Measure both false package assignment on linked single-contract sweeps and missed linked packages whose legs are not individually sweep-like.
- **Package baseline E — transparent association:** preregistered time, quantity/ratio, right, strike and expiry compatibility using only fields available at the cutoff. It returns candidate/resolved/unresolved, never beneficial-owner identity.
- **Package baseline F — entitled linkage:** source-supported linked execution/package fields where the rights and field definitions are accepted. This is the measurement comparator, not automatically a production feature.

Primary package endpoints are linked-package precision/recall over the supported domain, single-contract false-package rate, component-leg coverage, unmatched/multiply matched quantity, structure/payoff classification accuracy and correction stability. Report coverage and supported mechanisms beside every rate.

### Missingness policies

Compare attention-ordering behavior under three frozen missingness treatments without calling any arm a probability model:

- **M0 current fallback:** retain the existing positive factors for unknown prior OI and unknown moneyness;
- **M1 evidence-only:** unavailable components contribute zero and are carried in an explicit availability vector;
- **M2 availability-stratified:** rank only within the same preregistered evidence-availability signature, then expose cross-stratum results separately.

The 20-point event-unusualness component must remain unavailable when the producer intentionally emits `premium_z=null`; a root-day baseline may not be silently repurposed as a per-contract baseline. Any new per-contract baseline requires its own point-in-time definition, minimum-history rule, version and null state.

For each missingness policy, report rank stability, top-percentile composition, missing-field concentration, coverage and any later separately admitted outcome metric. A missing-data policy that improves rank coverage is not evidence of conviction or alpha.

### Required negative and metamorphic controls

- a single contract across multiple venues must not become multi-leg solely from venue count;
- an unswept linked package must remain detectable by the eligible linkage comparator;
- dropping one leg must move a resolved package to partial/unresolved, never preserve the payoff silently;
- reversing side or swapping call/put must change the recognized structure where economics require it;
- shifting one candidate leg outside the frozen association window must break or downgrade the association;
- shuffling timestamps or roots must not preserve package precision;
- later OI confirmation must not alter the earlier package/side inputs in place;
- null OI, moneyness, quote or baseline must remain explicit under M1/M2;
- a per-event `premium_z=null` row must not earn unusualness points or an outlier badge;
- duplicate event/revision inputs must not multiply package evidence or rank weight.

### Acceptance boundary

Research acceptance requires a supported-domain confusion/coverage ledger for urgency, association and missingness, with exact clocks and source versions. It does not require or authorize a new live package service. Any later production repair must extend existing event/campaign/candidate owners, preserve component identities and prove real single-leg, package, conflicting, null and correction states through the existing Terminal consumer on mobile and desktop.

## Dependence, uncertainty and sample planning

Sides and legs belonging to a package are dependent; campaigns/sessions introduce further dependence. Use grouped evaluation and appropriate uncertainty rather than independent-binomial intervals over every leg.

As a scale illustration, an independent Bernoulli proportion with worst-case variance0.25 and a95% normal-approximation half-width0.02 requires about2401 independent observations. This is a planning approximation, not an acceptance threshold or guarantee. Mechanism strata and cluster dependence require their own precision/power plan;2401 raw rows do not establish2401 independent observations.

Error tolerances follow consumer harm, not a convenient achieved rate. A high-authority direction claim can need much stricter error control than descriptive research prioritization.

Rule-family and slice inspection must disclose population share and multiplicity. A tiny high-rate cell is not a validated strategy merely because its point estimate is above the aggregate. Candidate-policy revisions require a new frozen cohort or an explicit preplanned transportability analysis.

## Splits and controls

Development, calibration and locked evaluation are chronological, keeping whole packages/related campaigns together. Audit historical source conventions before mixing eras. No random leg-level split. Count every threshold/model trial. Disclose the full population and supported-domain result.

Required controls: time-shifted quote sensitivity; later-label leakage detector; duplicate side/leg detection; quantity-versus-volume reconciliation; missing-source abstention; and replay of an old cutoff after later correction. The audit must detect deliberately wrong treatments, not normalize them into plausible output.

For any later economic experiment, add matched-horizon/path controls, executable bid/ask or declared benchmark sensitivity, latency perturbation, unresolved-outcome bounds, campaign/session clustering, policy-version stratification, and source-attribution balance. “Ever green,” best print and perfect-exit outcomes remain descriptive opportunity measures, not primary economic endpoints.

## Acceptance and stopping

Research acceptance requires reproducible rights/field/clock mapping, eligible/missing population, untouched locked evaluation and supported-domain uncertainty. Stop for ambiguous linkage, insufficient quote timestamps, unauthorized usage, unversioned later label changes, or feature requests conflicting with OA/DNR.

Before any later economic conclusion, additionally require a frozen population/admission/ruler receipt, exact source attribution, consistent capital and P&L denominator, declared execution policy, all no-entry/unresolved cases, and correction-safe outcome lineage. Withhold the result when these cannot be reconstructed.

Production acceptance remains separate: one real canonical measured event must reach the existing candidate view with truthful interpretation, unknown/degraded states and machine-readable lineage. Dataset acquisition or an offline metric does not satisfy that end-to-end capability.
