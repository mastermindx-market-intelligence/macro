# Options Historical Signal Science: revival charter v1

Status: Chairman-authorized research scope, 2026-10-02.
Scope identifier: `OPTIONS-HISTORICAL-REVIVAL-V1`.
Decision: `DEC:OPTIONS-HISTORICAL-REVIVAL-RESEARCH-ONLY`.

The mission is a reproducible historical evidence package and empirical findings, not
just a framework or a new Options interface. Historical nulls and refutations remain
visible. Research may replicate them and test genuinely versioned successors or
interactions without assuming any edge exists.

## Existing owners and product fit

Use canonical ThetaData/live-flow inputs, existing episode/campaign/outcome contracts,
the existing Options FS/evaluation and Eval/qledger owners, and existing research artifacts.
Mastermind's signal-history ledger retains lens directions, not the raw Options feature
history; it cannot substitute for the historical data estate. No new collector, store,
scheduler, campaign identity, outcome ledger, Issue Desk or score-control plane is needed.

Execute through existing Options research owners such as
`scripts/research/options_history_gauntlet.py` and, after its required repairs,
`scripts/ops_train_flow_score.py`. These paths do not route studies through
Signal Foundry; Foundry admission is not a prerequisite for this scope. Its static
hand-curated and compiled blockers continue to govern Foundry candidates. This charter
adds no Foundry exception and does not permit renaming a candidate to evade that screen.

## Research scope and authority

Intended families include option structure; OI and delta-OI; gamma/GEX/walls;
Vanna/Charm; measured flow; NBBO/spreads; IV/skew/term structure; dark-pool context;
price/technicals; sector and regime conditioning; and registered interactions/ablations.
Candidate horizons are 5m, 30m, 2h, close, next session, 2-5 sessions and 1-4 weeks
only where the source and label contracts support them. These are coverage objectives,
not new registered statistical cells. Existing replication uses its exact registered ruler.

The six DNR amendments permit research-only replication, successor features and named
Options positioning/composite studies. They do not enable production scoring, ranking,
board ordering, gates, alerts, sizing, Issue Desk, Prophet, portfolio or trading authority.
LLMs may orchestrate and explain evidence; they do not originate runtime signals.
Keep scoring disabled. Research-pass alone is never promotion.

Fit-free code repair, metadata/coverage audits and reconstruction checks proceed now.
Within the Chairman's scope there is no second administrative permission requirement
for this work. Actual experiments follow their frozen study specification and applicable
source, review and data-access controls. Existing delivery checks remain required.

## Freeze each individual study before outcome access

A study specification must identify all of the following before evaluating outcomes:

- Exact feature/component formulas, units, source/detector/data vintage, immutable
  input hashes, population, decision clock and retained first-known availability.
- Target, horizon, sign, benchmark, actual fill/end sessions, option quote quality,
  executable-price/cost assumptions where applicable, and unsupported targets.
- Canonical exchange sessions, split units, chronological train/development,
  calibration-fit, calibration-evaluation and final OOS boundaries; inclusive actual
  label-window purge, separate trading-session embargo and within-fold root exclusion.
- Missingness/censoring rules, coverage denominators, survivorship/delisting limitations,
  dependence-aware uncertainty and effective sample size, not just row count.
- Baselines/challengers, component ablations, subgroup/regime stability checks,
  all attempted cells and hyperparameters, multiplicity family and correction.
- Acceptance/falsification thresholds, minimum support, trial accounting and a
  check-by/review date. A revised specification creates a new visible version.

This charter does not pretend those formulas, thresholds or trial counts are frozen.
Already inspected history is exploratory; fresh confirmation requires genuinely untouched
OOS or forward shadow. Never select a sign, horizon or feature after inspecting its result
and call the selected result preregistered. A missing/unfilled cell stays visible.

## Data and label integrity

Observation date and filesystem mtime do not prove historical known-at availability.
Retained attributable availability receipts are required for a historical PIT claim;
otherwise report `PIT_UNPROVEN` and describe any retrospective assumptions explicitly.
Resolve OI's publication clock and prior-session semantics from the actual source contract,
rather than assuming every date column has the same meaning.

Use recorded measured trade/NBBO evidence only for intraday/executable-option claims.
Daily EOD data cannot substitute for a missing intraday quote path. Underlying returns
are not option P&L. Preserve raw incident rows and quarantine effective authority through
the existing correction owner; never restamp historical clocks or rewrite incidents clean.

The existing session outcome corpus is explicitly research-only and training-ineligible.
Its short descriptive underlying-return evidence is not a replacement for a historical
Greek/OI panel, executable option outcomes or the FS training population.

## Finite acceptance

Complete the mission with a versioned coverage/provenance manifest; reproducible runs
through the existing owners on actual supported data; and machine-readable plus human
reports of null, negative, inconclusive or research-pass findings. Include unsupported
families/horizons, exact sample counts and uncertainty, costs, missingness and every
registered trial. A non-evaluable result is legitimate, but missing data is not a
completed empirical validation of that family. No framework-only completion claim.

The immediate source repairs are BH step-up consistency, correct historical era
classification and exact underlying/SPY label-window parity. The accepted #7401 contract
also requires trainer population separation, session/interval-safe validation, isolated
calibration/final OOS, truthful CPCV/effective-N accounting and separate artifact health
from statistical acceptance. Resolve those before a new FS fit. Reuse #7395's existing
method registration as it clears its current delivery checks; do not duplicate it.
