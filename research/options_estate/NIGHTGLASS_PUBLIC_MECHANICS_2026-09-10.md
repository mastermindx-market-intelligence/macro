## Finding: several systems, not one recovered supermodel

The observed service composes live options-print alerts, next-day OI-chain research, technical-state and anchored price-level maps, a gated research watchlist, written analysis and a separate human-trade add-on. These have different clocks and outcome populations. Their apparent unity is a product strength; it is not proof of a single predictive model or independently validated profitability.

The important reusable capability is the whole research journey: interpret activity, expose ambiguity, place it against stable price structure, withhold poor plans, track revisions and measure the exact instrument. The approved OA architecture already preserves the necessary event/campaign/Issue Desk owners. This research does not replace that roadmap.

## 1. Research Bias is an additive checklist

The Ticker Analysis guide describes `B = 50 + 10 * sum(votes)`, five votes each -1, 0 or +1. Checks concern the 10-week line, 21-day line, MACD momentum, 50/200 regime and relative structural evidence below/above price. Missing checks sit out without rescaling. Four positive price checks without the levels check reach 90, not 100. Four positive and one negative check yield the screenshot's 80.

The score is descriptive alignment, not an 80% outcome forecast. The helper treats 40/60 as weak leaning states. Exact average types, MACD periods, neutral deadbands and complete structure-check internals were not recovered; do not invent standard settings as vendor facts.

Source: `ticker-analysis-x9j-Y5TX.js`, guide around decoded offsets 197300–203700; companion `bias-display-DUTdI3VE.js`.

## 2. Price-level generation, plan promotion and rendering are separate

The guide describes completed daily and confirmed weekly/monthly structure, a session-frozen map, and a next-session rebuild at 20:00 ET. Breaches change status rather than silently moving/reordering the original anchor. High/low range crossing is distinct from close acceptance. Persisted anchor, stale anchor and unanchored partial output are separate states.

Plan selection requires real price-engine levels on the correct side and in valid roles. Proximity is the first filter; touch evidence and multi-timeframe reinforcement discriminate within a bounded band. OI may annotate a nearby zone but does not originate support, resistance, target or thesis prices; a wall belongs to one nearest qualifying zone. Same-timeframe levels are not casually merged.

Ticker KING is a dominant price-structure level, not automatically the SPX GEX King Node. Distant target-ladder levels differ from a compact plan's first objective. When genuine overhead structure is absent, a labeled volatility projection can be used; its exact estimator/multiplier was not recovered. A `kde` provenance label is consistent with a density-based candidate family, not proof of its exact bandwidth or implementation.

Client enrichment uses approximately 0.5% price-engine/analyst matching, a separate 1% OI-wall join and an analyst stale warning beyond four hours. The chart-decluttering helper has a 0.02 strength tie band and 0.5% OI-proximity rule. These are client selection/enrichment numbers, not backend pivot/clustering parameters.

Sources: `ticker-analysis-x9j-Y5TX.js` around 106730–114500 and embedded guide 196300–217900; `ticker-analysis-workstation-BXdzxPKe.js`.

## 3. Live activity and settled positioning are different measurements

Intraday gross premium is the sum of fill price × quantity × multiplier. The educational overnight method approximates net positioning premium from change in OI × average price × 100. The measures have different denominators and availability times. They must not be double-counted or retroactively substituted.

The educational four-bucket weighting discounts call selling relative to put buying: bullish = 0.7 × (call-buy premium + put-sell premium), bearish = 0.7 × put-buy premium + 0.3 × call-sell premium. Equal dollars in all four categories yield 58.33% bullish share, not 50%. This illustrates an interpretation assumption, not a learned probability.

Ask share including midpoint volume is not aggression conditional on decisively classified volume. For 5 ask, 5 bid and 90 midpoint contracts, a low ask share does not prove dominant selling. Classified coverage, aggression and net-OI retention must remain separate. Net OI change/volume is an aggregate persistence proxy, not the retained fraction of one participant's orders.

Public educational structure matching considers same root/type/expiry, differing strikes, similar OI changes and high multi-leg share. This is candidate association, not beneficial-owner or exchange parent-order identity. Sources: public `/academy/raw`, `/academy/direction`, `/academy/confidence`, `/academy/structures`, `/academy/context`, `/academy/synthesis`.

## 4. Much of alert explanation is deterministic

The alert panel builds rule-sensitive phrases from side, repetition, premium and VOI. The RAM-style explanation/share card does not require a language model. Badge thresholds around VOI 1/3 and premium $500k/$1m are post-admission descriptions, not universal upstream alert gates. A $110k alert is therefore not a violation of a separate educational $500k example.

The client validates headline-contract/leg consistency and suppresses incompatible structure/payoff fields. A matching-leg quantity floor near 20% of alert size is a payload consistency safeguard, not the complete package-discovery algorithm. Same-expiry vertical payoff requires coherent sides/right/expiry/strikes; ambiguous or mixed-expiry packages must not receive a false single-expiry curve.

Sources: `AlertDetailPanel-BEE2CEjr.js` initial helpers and 17600–24400; `api-BnBNEnz-.js` response normalization.

## 6. Human trade add-on accounting explains the UNH screenshots

Official terms identify the add-on as trades the people behind Alpha Pod are tracking. Separate options/SPX routes expose Entry/Add/Trim/Exit/Expired children and current runners. The visible convention for repeated trims halves the remaining position each time.

The allocation display maps L/M/S/Lotto to 10%/5%/1%/0.5%, respectively, with a 5% fallback. This is observed hypothetical accounting, not recommended position sizing. The marked return is the sum of each trim return weighted by successive halves plus the remaining fraction times its current return. The book view multiplies by the label's fraction; it is not verified broker equity.

For the supplied 1.61 entry and 3.00/4.05 trims, the individual marks are 86.3%/151.6%. Under two half-of-remaining trims, 25% remains and banked profit is 81.06% of original premium; if that remainder is marked at 4.05, total position return is 118.94%, not the sum of both alert percentages. Actual quantities/fills were not supplied. Add/rebasis behavior remains a backend verification question, not an established defect.

Sources: `OptionsAlertsPage-BTXppwp_.js`, `SpxAlertsPage-CP2LADoO.js`, `AddonFooter-CAEiOMmq.js` first ~3000 characters; `https://nightglass.trade/terms` v2 effective 2026-09-04.

## 7. Gamma and performance cautions that affect integration

The embedded SPX GEX guide describes selected-expiry/windowed exposure and interpolation between adjacent opposite-signed strike contributions. That strike-axis intercept is not necessarily the hypothetical spot price at which the entire portfolio's recalculated gamma is zero. Its shorthand per-1%-move formula also needs unit checking for the 0.01 factor. These are interpretation/documentation questions, not proof the backend calculation is broken.

The public performance response generated 2026-09-10T00:10:46.547583+00:00 reports 7343 tracked, 6389 matured, 954 open and 3968 target-before-stop successes (~62.1%), through 2026-09-01 with five trading days' lag. Repeat-interest rules represent 5579 matured observations. These are vendor-reported flow-contract results, not separate validation of technical levels, gated plans or human trade management, and not independent subscriber-executable fills.

The observed AI technical read has model/generated_at provenance and streaming states. No actual provider/model, private prompt or learned weights were established. Codex/Claude authorship cannot be concluded from visual style or a React build.
