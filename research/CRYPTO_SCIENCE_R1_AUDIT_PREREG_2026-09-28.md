Hypothesis H-TIME-1: a left-labeled multi-day aggregate can place a later close onto an earlier date. Test the original function unchanged on every daily cutoff in the last 366 stored daily observations, comparing the final prefix value with the full-frame value at exactly that date. Tolerance 1e-12. Publish all cutoffs/counts/deltas, including no-change cases. Dates are selected by a fixed tail rule, not market outcomes. Positive controls: single-timeframe momentum/risk recomputed from price-only inputs at the same cutoffs. These controls do not clear the untested non-price/source-vintage paths.

A mechanism-isolation counterfactual in the research harness replaces only the three-day aggregation with right-labeled/right-closed completed-date bins anchored to the fixed dataset start. ## Scientific programme following the audit

Separate four decision problems: (A) fast cascade/continuation hazard; (B) washout followed by executable recovery; (C) trend persistence and failed breakout; (D) cycle distribution/top risk. A bottom watch is not a confirmed re-entry; a high carry reading is not an immediate short; a descriptive risk-on label is not a calibrated probability.

Horizon families for subsequent preregistrations: hours (candidate 1/4/12/24h), swing days (3/7/14d), positional weeks (30/90d), cycle months (180/365d). These are research horizons, not sampling rates or promised lead times. The first operational tests should prioritize one risk-off horizon and one recovery horizon rather than optimize all of them simultaneously.

Event labels must be distinct from live features. A hindsight local maximum/minimum can be an evaluation label only; the dashboard may not display its future-confirmed timestamp as though known on that day. Measure first-passage adverse/favorable barriers, earliest actionable signal time, time-to-confirmation, subsequent excursion, false reversals and signal episodes. A future high or low is not an attainable fill. Use actual next executable bar/order constraints and explicitly pessimistic handling where both barriers occur inside one OHLC bar.

Incumbent comparators: raw allocation; final canonical allocation with contemporaneous override provenance; simple trend/cash baseline; constant-risk or volatility-target baseline. Compare equal risk and net costs, not only Sharpe or CAGR. Separate alert quality from portfolio policy. Run nested time splits, purge overlapping labels and embargo by the relevant horizon; block-bootstrap by independent events/cycles. Prior 2024+ holdouts have already been used in existing research and cannot be called untouched. Any historical rerun is retrospective diagnostic until a genuinely sealed period or issued-forward ledger supplies new evidence.

Track event-level precision/recall, false alerts per month, lead-time distribution, missed-tail cost, drawdown avoided, upside forgone, turnover and recovery participation. For probability models add Brier/log loss, calibration by regime and interval uncertainty. Register all tried variants and failures; correct selection/multiple testing. Prefer a small transparent logistic/hazard or rules baseline; nonlinear challengers must earn incremental out-of-sample value. Validation thresholds depend on the predeclared loss function and baseline, not a marketing target of 90% accuracy.

No strategy promotion from one impressive crisis, one cycle, a bar-level hit rate dominated by overlap, or a few long-horizon successes. Locked historical robustness, source-latency parity, fresh forward observation, independent review and existing authority acceptance precede any policy change.
