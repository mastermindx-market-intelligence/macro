## Direction

1. Treat G0 as the early washout lane, not an unconditional replacement for T1/T2. Keep a continuation/relative-strength lane for leaders that never provide a qualifying washout. Compare equal attention/exposure budgets and retain unmatched events.
2. Test market timing first, cohort selection second, stock selection third. A pooled advantage that disappears within the same date can motivate a market-date hypothesis but does not prove sector or stock selection. Sector timing must also beat simpler same-date/equal-exposure cohort alternatives.
3. Represent volatility level, direction and recent retreat separately. A high-but-easing VIX is not a low-but-rising VIX; VVIX measures options-implied volatility of VIX forward prices, not a realized standard deviation of VIX changes. Never substitute one silently. The incumbent VVIX/VIX ratio is partly driven by its VIX denominator; separate MA slopes avoid treating that mechanical ratio as independent corroboration.
4. Treat washout -> reduced selling -> broader repair -> persistence as an ordered hypothesis, not a discovered law. Compare the proposed sequence against the same final state and the same delay without the earlier sequence. Otherwise a complicated state story may only be a slower momentum signal.
5. Estimate behavioral traits from pre-event histories using Stock Identity, with shrinking own-history estimates toward peer priors and only matured earlier episodes. The Chairman's examples are hypotheses, not permanent labels for named stocks. Stable compounders, cyclical risk assets and crypto require separate eligible populations and clocks.
6. Explain changes in observed success rates before fitting a router: separate the frequency of market/cohort states from within-state success. Preserve the outcome definition and weighting; a descriptive decomposition is not causality.
7. Explain 63-session outcomes on exactly the same entries using the 0-21 and 21-63 gross-return segments, matched market/cohort references, and complete/censored path counts. Do not replace the frozen primary horizon because a later one looks better. A no-undercut probability cannot increase with a longer horizon on the same fully observed population, whereas positive terminal-return frequency may.

## Next registered outcome tests (not executed by this diagnostic increment)

The Fable programme should bind the exact event archive, native outcome contract and existing trial ledger before accepting any of these as trials. These are priority questions, not an invitation to run every Cartesian combination.

- Does implied VVIX MA direction add separation beyond VIX level/direction and the existing cohort washout/repair descriptor? Compare matched days/episodes and report the loss of lead and opportunity retention. Test rising and falling directions symmetrically; do not predeclare rising fear as bullish.
- Does cohort repair add information beyond market-date washout and simple sector strength? Use dated, leave-target-out peers and fixed formation-time membership; do not let membership changes manufacture improving breadth. Decompose market exposure, cohort allocation and stock selection.
- Does a hypothesized pressure-release sequence help beyond its final state at equal entry delay? A delayed entry is not free confirmation.
- Does personal washout depth add beyond current volatility, slow trend, cohort decline and generic drawdown? Do not choose a historical best indicator separately for each ticker.

Keep a compact family budget: implied volatility; price/trend; breadth/cohort; behavioral identity; economic/catalyst. Leave-one-family-out comparisons are needed because several signals can be transformations of the same sell-off. Core complementary lanes should be tested with simple shrinkage/interaction models before high-capacity routing. No confidence percentage is emitted before calibration.

## Scientific and product acceptance

Development screens, historical confirmation and prospective shadow are separate. Pre-register exact comparisons, horizon, time/episode-block inference, trial count, costs, minimum coverage and rejection conditions. Preserve negative findings and unspent holdouts. Report no action as a valid output, and separate unmeasured from measured-unfavorable.

A useful consumer explains: what is washed out, what changed, whether pressure is still accelerating, which cohort is repairing relative to alternatives, which entry family applies, what evidence is missing and the actual maturity of the hypothesis. It must never convert a diagnostic state into automatic rank, size, entry or trade authority.
