## First-principles decomposition

| Component | Actual question | Evidence it can supply | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic selection | Why own this asset rather than cash/another asset? | Changes in cash-flow expectations, discount rates, valuation and durable demand | A profitable entry time from an attractive story |
| Regime/context | Under what conditions should a specific mechanism persist? | Observable trend, participation, stress, liquidity and event state | A hindsight bull/bear label or universal risk-on switch |
| Momentum/trigger | Has the direction or persistence of price adjustment changed? | Own-price trend, relative trend, acceleration, breakout/reclaim | Independent votes from many transforms of the same close series |
| Entry geometry | What price is paid relative to invalidation and plausible opportunity? | Executable entry, adverse excursion, room, gaps and friction | A favorable payoff distribution from a drawn risk/reward box |
| Execution | Can the theoretical edge actually be captured? | Delay, spread, impact, turnover, liquidity/capacity | Free fills at signal-time prices |
| Exit/horizon | Has the reason to hold expired or changed? | Time expiry, thesis invalidation, persistent trend loss | A magical stop which converts a null entry into alpha |
| Portfolio | Is the marginal trade useful to the whole book? | Exposure, concentration, covariance, drawdown and cash opportunity cost | Higher skill merely because less risk was taken |
| Evidence/confidence | How much of the above is actually known? | Availability clocks, coverage, out-of-time performance, uncertainty | A fabricated probability or certainty from an LLM narrative |

Working synthesis: model expected net payoff conditional on setup and context, not
an unconditional bullishness score. A context may change a signal's direction, its
useful horizon, or only its risk. Distinguish these claims experimentally. A broad
market filter can hide opportunities in independent sectors; retain opportunity-loss
and coverage alongside precision. Do not infer investor intentions from OHLC alone.
