## What this matrix does not prove

- **No production data was read.** Every evidence row is either curated source prose
  (`lib/ticker_aliases.py`, cited with line numbers) or synthetic fixtures built by the
  appended tests. Nothing here attests what any vendor or the security master actually
  contains today.
- **No FIGI model exists to test.** The absence claim in row 11 is a grep of the reader at
  the pinned base; it is not a claim about any other module or the producers.
- **Successor economics are not carried.** Rows 9-10 are refusals, not handling: nothing in
  the reader maps a merged-in or spun-off entity to its successor, and the migration
  artifacts are sealed but uninterpreted. Downstream consumers must not read a stable
  `security_id` across a merger or spin-off.
- **Instants are read-clock-only.** Event validity is date-granular in BOTH namespaces
  (`_ob_date`, identity.py:970-979); the nanosecond edges pinned by I03 tests live on the
  decision/read clock (receipt eligibility and attempt selection), not on interval bounds.
- **A refusal never rewrites the family it shadows** (row 8): the reader refuses any history
  whose revisioned family changes an established security identity (identity.py:1473), so a
  BOUND→REFUSED refusal always carries the security it refuses and both lookup directions
  shadow to `REFUSED`. Refusals without a `security_id` occur only for families never bound.
- **Issuer evidence is current-only.** `IssuerMaster` answers "right now" by construction —
  no `asof` (identity.py:1720-1730); a historical issuer/CIK lineage claim is out of scope
  for every row above.
