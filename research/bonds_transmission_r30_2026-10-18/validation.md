Additional semantic checks:

- Treasury yield/price direction is explained; no arbitrary numerical funding sparkline remains.
- Alternative yield decompositions are not added as independent confirmations.
- Corporate benchmark-rate and spread effects are separated.
- The hedged number is a current-cost approximation, with future rolls open.
- Reported issuer accounting values are dated and distinct from synthetic market paths.
- Peer breadth is labeled narrow breadth, not confused with missing coverage.
- Historical research episodes are not represented by invented actual return series.
- Funding stress can challenge the Treasury bid; price direction does not prove capital transfer.
- Missing, stale or mismatched evidence cannot silently confirm a case.
- A prior confirmation can be invalidated only during its open window; closure freezes the result.
