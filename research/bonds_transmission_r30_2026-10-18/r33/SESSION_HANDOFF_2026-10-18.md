### 2. Why this project exists — preserve the north star

The original Bonds and Cross-Asset studies were visually dark, inconsistent with the Mastermind design system, text-heavy and cognitively fragmented. The product must let an investor answer a concise decision chain:

**What changed? → Which rates/credit/capital mechanism matters? → What evidence supports or contradicts it? → What could happen next? → What would invalidate it? → What case or check should I retain? → What changed since my last review?**

The product is an intuitive Daily Desk, not a wall of labeled statistics. Keep first-screen narrative sparse, expose a strong selected market reading and next check, and make deep technical evidence, source clocks, quote conventions and implementation details available through precise drawers, popovers and modals. Preserve English / Simplified Chinese, responsive 390/768/1024/1440-width behavior, keyboard use, focus return, appropriately sized touch controls, light Mastermind tokens and optional dark presentation. Do not turn technical detail into superficial descriptive clutter.
