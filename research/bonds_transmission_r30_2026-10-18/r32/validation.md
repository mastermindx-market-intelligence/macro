## Evidence and limits

The reference is an executable design artifact, not a production component release. It does not establish live-source eligibility, quote rights, application deployment, real saved research, active compound monitoring, a production accessibility audit, a historical backtest or calibrated forecast performance.

Primary-source research, dated financing facts and exact existing-owner pins remain in the R30/R31 research and binding files. Synthetic prices, spreads, cash scenarios and replay values are explicitly separate. The reported CoreWeave annual principal schedule is not represented as a current exact-date cash forecast.

The broader programme issue `#8147` was observed closed during this continuation although older body text describes it as open. This design closeout neither reopens that issue nor claims wider production acceptance. The work continues the existing draft PR `#9578`; no merge or deployment is part of this delivery.
