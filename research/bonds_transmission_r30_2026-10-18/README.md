## What changed

### A clearer analytical journey

The desk leads with a concise market read, a few meaningful signals and the next decision. The selected outlook now sits above the scenario calculator. Source explanations, quote conventions, formulas, history and implementation detail open in tools rather than competing with the primary screen.

The default is light, using the existing Mastermind token set. The optional dark and Chinese responsive specimens remain available. The reference’s phone signal grid, horizontal research tabs, internal sheet scrolling and visible close actions preserve a usable hierarchy on small screens.

### Greater depth where the decision needs it

Curve analysis separates policy expectations, term premium, parallel sensitivity and nonparallel key-rate shocks. Structure adds fixed close levels, prior-only volatility distance, horizon conflict and curve participation.

Issuer analysis separates CDS conventions, dated financing facts and actual contractual obligations. Cash timing keeps restricted funds, borrower identity, eligible draws, operating assumptions, buffers and payment cutoffs explicit. The reported CoreWeave annual schedule and the exact-date synthetic Parent A scenario remain separate.

Capital analysis compares the same buyer’s mandate, duration, currency and hedge. The transaction/revaluation lens shows why falling holdings need not mean selling. The funding view adds dealer financing demand, repo and cash supply without inventing measured capital-flow arrows.

Continuation analysis compares rate relief, supply pressure and a funding interruption. The explanation drawer includes growth, substitution, issuer-specific risk and funding stress. A matched market test, a later reversal and evidence of the proposed cause remain separate claims. The design does not turn correlated indicators into probability votes.

### Visible continuity between tools

Applying a tool creates a typed addition in the receiving case, with its source and period. Inspect, Remove and Undo maintain the effective rule or calculation. Context conflicts preserve edits and explain the actual mismatch. Main-page pending assumptions survive navigation independently from tool forms.

Save review discloses retained and excluded contents. The working reference demonstrates question-only receipts, exact-read failure, original-operation uncertainty recovery and conflict-form preservation in memory. The complete-case revision comparison remains labeled as a future admitted state. No real save or monitor is activated.
