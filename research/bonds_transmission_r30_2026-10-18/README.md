# Bonds and Transmission R30

Design checkpoint for Chris Wong’s 18 October 2026 redesign commission. The requested Paper pages are being rebuilt with the existing Mastermind light design tokens and progressively disclosed analytical tools. Forex page `p-1F-0` belongs to another active session and is excluded.

The seven main study screens have been created in Paper. Overlay, responsive, state and implementation scorecard work is continuing. This checkpoint records identity and current effects; it is not completion or production acceptance.

The analytical distinction is deliberate: observed price changes, model-derived rate attribution, proposed capital-transmission mechanisms, deterministic sensitivities and validated forecasts have different evidentiary requirements. Numerical forecasts remain unavailable until model and point-in-time validation gates are met.

The existing R26 decision workspace projection remains the integration starting point. This commission does not create a second signals, company evidence, saved-case or alert store. The issuer evidence contract remains `capital-structure-intelligence` / `capital_structure.event.v1`; its current share-count machinery is not a general bond maturity or covenant feed.
