- The prewarmer changes `Path` and `Args` on the **same** sandbox command after its cgroup FD is set. It does not create a separate command without that field in this function. [Sandbox construction](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/sandbox/sandbox.go#L1030)
- FD parking is different: its conditional fork supplies `Setsid` but no cgroup FD. When `RunInCgroup` succeeds through clone3, it passes a descriptor without moving the caller; the parking child therefore inherits the caller's cgroup. With the Join fallback it can inherit the joined cgroup. This is a source-derived placement distinction; the actual route and membership must be observed. [Parking launch](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/sandbox/sandbox.go#L1481), [placement wrapper](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/cgroup/cgroup.go#L1128), [fork implementation](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/gvisorbinaries/gvisorbinaries.go#L418)
- FD parking holds its ring descriptor until its sandbox pidfd reports exit, then intends to exit. That does not prove bounded cleanup latency or an observed absence of survivors. [Parking implementation](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/fdparking/fdparking.c#L166)

The cgroup-v2 installation path also preserves ownership of existing cgroups and only reapplies the swap setting in that branch. Some CPU queries consult the parent when the leaf is unlimited. Metadata and requested values cannot replace effective ancestor-limit observations. [Compatibility source](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/cgroup/cgroup.go#L490), [v2 installation](https://github.com/google/gvisor/blob/97e8e896904cb364319cd9758241d3f0bd27a6f4/runsc/cgroup/cgroup_v2.go#L122)
