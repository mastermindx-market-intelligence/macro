## 3. Information-set rules

A document's reporting period, publication time, observed time, effective time and correction time are independent. For example, ING's August 2022 release about the quarter ended June 30 was newly published after the ECB event but described operations before it. MUFG's May 2024 simulation uses March-end balances and cannot be retroactively imported into the March 19 baseline.

A mutable issuer blog with a later update date can corroborate historical figures, but is not by itself an immutable snapshot of exactly what the page said at the earlier publication. Use the filed original where available; otherwise flag point-in-time text certification unresolved. A blocked source is not permission to invent the missing receipt.

Required expectation distinction:

- analyst consensus: requires an actual dated consensus observation and population;
- management guidance: an attributed forecast with its own horizon and definition;
- central-bank communication: a policy statement, not an OIS probability distribution;
- a valuation-implied scenario: depends on stated valuation assumptions;
- subsequent actual: not a prior expectation.

Missing consensus is not zero surprise. Results inside management guidance are not automatically results inside market consensus.

## 4. Separate the claims being tested

The four planes remain separate: operating economics, expectations, valuation, and price recognition.

An issuer may show the predicted operating mechanism without any proof of a multiple rerating. Price appreciation can occur without multiple expansion. A higher multiple can reflect a lower earnings denominator. A raw positive return does not establish positive factor-adjusted return or causal attribution to the event.

The existing five outcome labels remain available only with a named target and sufficient evidence. Do not label a missing-price case NULL. Do not label an unmeasured market outcome SUPPORTS merely because a revenue mechanism is plausible. A narrative mechanism assessment is original research interpretation, not a registered statistical outcome. Preserve all twelve original market outcomes as UNMEASURED until the required market and expectation evidence is assembled; use explicit missing-evidence fields in interim research.

The eventual return calculation must distinguish split-adjusted price, dividends, total return, FX, per-share denominator changes, fees/taxes where relevant, and local benchmark/factor controls. A same-currency earnings-per-share and multiple identity can explain price mechanically, but attribution to the policy still needs a counterfactual.

## 5. Control-selection repair

Fix a pre-event eligible population and measured pre-event exposures before evaluating a broad cohort. Later TSE or Korea disclosers, firms that survived, successful bank migrations, and companies later reporting favourable sensitivities cannot be used as the baseline selection criterion. Later adoption may be analysed separately with explicit selection and time-varying-treatment limitations.

Allow spillovers. Nonbank mortgage lenders may be affected by an APRA rule applied to banks; financial firms outside a newly eligible exchange product can still benefit from liquidity changes. A convenient comparison group is not necessarily untreated.

Keep the original session windows. Add announcement-versus-implementation decompositions transparently; do not move an anchor or extend a window to obtain a preferred sign. Multiple post-event hikes, earnings releases, buybacks, acquisitions and regulatory decisions remain overlapping events rather than disappearing into a generic regime control.
