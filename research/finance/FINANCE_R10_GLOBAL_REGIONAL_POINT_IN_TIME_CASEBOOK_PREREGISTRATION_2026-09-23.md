## 0. Purpose

This casebook does not ask which historical Finance stocks won. It asks whether a predeclared economic mechanism, measured from information available at the event date, explains later revisions, per-share economics, valuation and price better than credible alternatives.

The fixture is event-anchored and direction-neutral. A case receives `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, `NULL`, `UNAVAILABLE` or `CONFOUNDED` only after the frozen evidence and outcome procedure runs.

## 1. Universal inclusion law

A case enters only when:

1. the event was publicly observable at a verifiable timestamp;
2. the affected business mechanism was definable before observing the outcome;
3. exposed and control populations can be frozen point-in-time;
4. legal issuer/security/listing/currency are resolvable;
5. contemporaneous operating, expectation, valuation or price inputs exist—or the missing plane is declared;
6. the event is not selected solely because a famous stock later moved;
7. overlapping events and macro shocks are recorded;
8. the source is retained/reviewed or explicitly held;
9. no later result is backfilled into the event-state record;
10. a null result is publishable.

## 2. Frozen observation windows

### Market windows

- pre-event estimation: sessions `[-252, -21]`;
- immediate: `[0, +5]` local sessions;
- short: `[0, +20]`;
- medium: `[0, +60]`;
- long: `[0, +120]` and `[0, +252]` where identity survives;
- local, sector-relative and base-currency returns reported separately.

### Fundamental/expectation windows

- last public observation before anchor;
- first comparable result after anchor;
- 2-quarter and 4-quarter comparable observations;
- consensus revisions at +5/+20/+60 sessions only from accepted point-in-time owners;
- valuation-multiple change decomposed from anchor growth and distributions.

## 3. Required controls

- local market and sector beta;
- rates/curve and sovereign-spread changes;
- currency returns and translation;
- size, business mix and pre-event valuation;
- credit/underwriting regime;
- broad value/growth factor;
- event overlap and earnings dates;
- listing/ADR conversion;
- survivorship/corporate actions;
- country-specific policy interventions.

### 5.1 `R10-EU-ECB-NEGATIVE-RATE-EXIT-20220721`

**Public event.** ECB raised key rates by 50bp, moving the deposit facility rate to 0%.

**Anchor / effective dates.** `2022-07-21` / `2022-07-27`.

**Archetype.** `RATE_NORMALIZATION`.

**Mechanism hypothesis.** asset repricing and securities reinvestment versus deposit beta, TLTRO/wholesale funding, credit demand, sovereign spreads and capital.

**Treated population.** Euro-area listed banks, segmented by deposit richness, fixed/floating asset mix, wholesale funding, country and IRB/output-floor sensitivity.

**Controls.** European insurers, exchanges, non-euro banks; within-bank matched peers by country/size/business mix.

**Falsifier / null condition.** Banks with favorable repricing exposure fail to improve normalized NII/ROTCE or price relative to matched peers once deposit and sovereign effects are controlled.

**Primary source.** `https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2022/html/ecb.mp220721~53e5bdd317.en.html`

**Required PIT fields.** Event timestamp; source availability; business/listing identity; pre-event exposure variables; latest comparable fundamentals; point-in-time expectations/valuation where available; local and base-currency prices; overlapping events; post-event comparable outcomes.

## 6. Outcome taxonomy

- `SUPPORTS` — mechanism-consistent operating/expectation/valuation/price sequence exceeds controls with sufficient evidence.
- `CONTRADICTS` — evidence moves materially opposite the preregistered mechanism.
- `NULL` — no discriminating difference after controls.
- `CONFOUNDED` — overlapping intervention or identity/data issue prevents attribution.
- `UNAVAILABLE` — required point-in-time evidence cannot be lawfully recovered.

No case is forced into positive/negative. A price move without operating/expectation/valuation support is recorded as price-only recognition or confounding, not causal proof.

## 7. Adversarial checks

- placebo anchor dates;
- time-shifted events;
- random matched peers;
- leave-one-country-out and leave-one-business-model-out;
- local versus global factor residual;
- exposure monotonicity;
- pre-trend and leakage tests;
- source-publication delay sensitivity;
- corporate-action and FX reconstruction;
- outcome-label stability across reasonable windows;
- negative-control metrics that the mechanism should not affect.
