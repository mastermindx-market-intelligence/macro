## 4. Controlling conclusions — DO NOT REDO

Unless materially invalidated:

1. Finance is a sector/system overlay, not one universal canonical theme.
2. The core Finance intelligence job is rerating/economic transmission, not bottleneck discovery.
3. Contractual flow, regulated infrastructure and public-equity economics remain separate maps.
4. Operating economics, expectations, valuation and price recognition remain separate planes.
5. There is no universal Finance multiple, score or earnings model.
6. Business-line role is more informative than a broad sector label.
7. Exposure requires a period-matched numerator/denominator and identity scope.
8. Stock/flow, average/end, gross/net/notional and reported/derived/estimated remain distinct.
9. Banks/lenders require funding, asset-repricing, vintage, credit, provision, capital and expectation/price clocks.
10. Private-credit managers and BDC vehicles are different businesses.
11. Mortgage production and servicing/MSR are different earnings engines.
12. Asset-manager AUM growth is not organic fee growth.
13. Wealth client assets are not advisory/cash economics.
14. Premium growth is not underwriting value creation.
15. Combined-ratio improvement must separate CAY, catastrophe and reserve development.
16. Life adjusted earnings are not statutory cash or distributable capital.
17. Broker reported growth is not organic when acquisitions dominate.
18. Subscription share is not retention or organic growth.
19. Disruption follows capability → permission → production → adoption → monetization → earnings → valuation.
20. Current full-fidelity Finance research remains authenticated/private.
21. Existing identity, evidence, revisions, valuation, rates, market, publication and evaluation owners remain canonical.
22. Research context has zero direct rank/gate/size/trade authority.
