## 1. Cross-regional interpretation law

1. A similar business label does not establish comparable economics.
2. `CET1`, `core Tier 1`, APRA CET1, India CRAR/CET1 and insurer solvency ratios retain their local rule basis.
3. IFRS 9 Stage 3, non-performing loans, gross NPA, nonaccrual, special-mention and impaired assets are related but not interchangeable.
4. UK structural-hedge income, Japanese policy-share gains, Chinese policy-directed lending, Indian priority-sector obligations, Australian franking and Hong Kong currency-board rates require explicit local treatment.
5. An A/H/ADR or dual-listed issuer has one legal issuer but several security identities, currencies, settlement venues and investor bases.
6. Local reported, adjusted, regulatory, statutory, economic-value and distributable earnings remain separate planes.
7. Currency translation, constant-currency growth and shareholder-currency return are separate.
8. State ownership, policy mandate and implicit support may lower one risk while increasing minority-shareholder, capital-allocation or governance risk.
9. A regional basket is a price surface, not a company-economics ontology.
10. Missing exact source or denominator remains held/null.

## 11. Cross-company comparisons that are permitted

Permitted only after period, currency, accounting, business and capital alignment:

- bank normalized return versus local required return;
- per-share tangible/book-value compounding;
- deposit and funding repricing within the same local monetary regime;
- credit formation and realized loss by product/vintage;
- current underwriting versus prior-year reserve support;
- VONB/CSM/free-surplus conversion within comparable life-insurance definitions;
- fee-paying assets and organic flows within comparable asset populations;
- venue volume × rate/mix plus recurring data/listing economics;
- capital generation, remittance and shareholder distribution after local constraints;
- local-currency and shareholder-currency total-return decomposition.

## 12. Comparisons that must refuse

- direct comparison of Solvency II, K-ICS, C-ROSS, Japan ESR, Hong Kong RBC and APRA capital as one percentage;
- direct comparison of NPL, Stage 3, GNPA, nonaccrual and delinquency without mapping;
- direct comparison of IFRS 17 operating profit, embedded-value profit, statutory profit and cash remittance;
- direct comparison of UK structural-hedge benefit with floating-rate asset beta;
- treating Japanese policy-share gains as recurring bank/insurer earnings;
- treating Chinese policy support as a guaranteed minority-shareholder return;
- treating A-share, H-share and ADR prices as one security observation;
- adding franking credits to a global total-return series without investor-tax scope;
- comparing local-currency EPS growth without FX translation and share-count bridge;
- using current curated international basket membership as point-in-time historical membership.
