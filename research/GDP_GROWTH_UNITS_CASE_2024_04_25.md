## Supplied facts

The BEA advance estimate dated April 25, 2024 reports first-quarter real GDP growth of 1.6% at an annual rate and 0.4% at a quarterly rate. Fourth-quarter real growth is reported as 3.4% annualized and 0.8% quarterly.
Current-dollar first-quarter GDP increased 4.8% annualized. The release describes real measures as inflation-adjusted and current-dollar measures as valued at current prices.
Real final sales to private domestic purchasers increased 3.1% annualized; this aggregate consists of consumer spending and private fixed investment. Imports increased and are subtracted in GDP accounting; private inventory investment decreased.
The advance estimate uses incomplete or revisable source data. A second estimate was scheduled for May 30, 2024; no later estimate or subsequent market outcome is supplied here.

## Root adjudication (judge only)

The first-quarter 1.6% annualized rate and 0.4% quarterly rate describe different units for the same growth period, not conflicting estimates or year-over-year growth. Compounding a rounded quarterly rate need not reproduce the rounded annualized number exactly. Growth slowed by 1.8 percentage points on the reported annualized scale but remained positive; slower growth is not a contraction in the GDP level. Nominal 4.8% and real 1.6% measure different price bases; their simple difference is not the released PCE inflation rate or an exact GDP deflator. Private domestic final sales exclude inventory changes, government and net exports, so its 3.1% is not a substitute GDP headline or proof that every component grew. Imports' accounting subtraction does not prove economic harm or a causal demand story. The announced revision schedule does not reveal a later value. These data establish no recession call, policy path or trade.
