## 1. Scientific decision, with a priori limitations

Factor Atlas has a defensible **measurement-product hypothesis**: properly identified, source-bound factor histories, participation, concentration, estimated flow, bounded options context, and grounded events can make market activity more intelligible. There is presently **no S6-qualified evidence** that a capital-pressure Z-score identifies institutional accumulation, that emerging breadth causes or forecasts factor rallies, that an options sign reconstructs dealer inventory, or that combining Atlas indicators improves investable prediction.

A descriptive value proposition can pass customer usability testing without predictive lift. Forecasting, risk reduction, trade economics and customer trust are different estimands. The tests must produce separate verdicts.

### Evidence levels and admissible wording

| Evidence attained | Acceptable statement | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| S0: proposal or unverified prototype | Proposed methodology; cannot compute reliably | Shipped, accurate or independently tested |
| S1: deterministic fixture and independent arithmetic | Reproduces the stated formula under supplied conditions | Correct on live source, genuine money moved |
| S2: incumbent-owner PIT, source, rights and correction admission | A measured observation as of its verified availability | Predicts returns, dealer/institution identities |
| S3: matched historical associations, corrected for multiplicity | Historical association in the specified population/era | Prospective edge or causal mechanism |
| S4: frozen untouched out-of-time and forward-paper gains | Incremental forecast/risk discrimination at declared horizon and coverage | Generalizable profit or automatic score promotion |
| S5: real-path human/product acceptance and approved release | Specific entitled users can complete accepted research journeys | Rank, veto, sizing or trading authority absent its independent owning gate |

S1/S2 are data and mathematics standards, not a lifecycle that supplants the existing authority ladder. The producer's test or report may support S1 but cannot, alone, attest S2–S5. Contradictory, missing, stale, unlicensed, or uncorrected records remain null and are counted in denominators.

## 3. Definitions, estimands and sampling geometry

**Factor:** a stable GMI-owned factor/theme or approved style/sector identity, with explicit factor version, disjoint economic factor type, roster revision, inclusion/exclusion dates, roster **known_at**, weight policy, and rights scope. A security may belong to multiple themes; per-factor exposure sums are not unique market-dollar totals. **Flow** means an estimator's signed or absolute allocated notional, not independently observed investor cash transfers or inferred investor identity. ETF creations/redemptions, traded dollar volume and derivative OI are distinct units and do not add.

**Decision cutoff C:** an actual UTC instant including source-availability, source-publication, receipt, reader-completion and finality rules. A 09:35 observation cannot use the 09:35 bar close before the bar and the source reader complete. Historical reconstruction must preserve the earliest verified knowledge clock, not retrospectively set it to event time or final corrected vendor timestamp. Always store both market event time and observation/knowledge time.

**Proposed primary observation grain:** (canonical factor ID, definition revision, NYSE session, declared decision cutoff, data revision). Separate (security listing ID, event, cutoff) for quote and catalyst validations. A group row and every stock row it contains are correlated: count one date/network event cluster, not hundreds of statistically independent firings. Censored/unavailable labels remain denominator records with reason.

**Returns:** gross total-return-index or properly reconciled cash/price basis as specified by existing price owner; log/pct-return units and calendar explicitly set before fitting. For daily EOD signals, freeze features after the owner-qualified EOD availability instant and evaluate from the **next executable regular-session observation**, never from an earlier close. Define factor excess as exposure-matched return versus the contemporaneously knowable broad market, sector and predeclared style controls; specify whether weights drift between monthly rebalances and corporate actions alter each leg.

**Intraday flow:** BVC uses minute price changes and dollar notional with a fitted empirical scale. Student-t with df=0.25 has infinite theoretical variance; a MAD-derived scale is empirical, not its standard deviation. BVC is a deterministic transform of its supplied price/volume history. A flexible price/return/volume baseline can approximate it, so new source information is *not* presumed. Quote-based trade signing is itself a proxy, not exchange-proven beneficial-owner truth. Enforce same eligible tape population, venue/sale conditions, reporting and SIP/participant clocks, bounded quote age, correction status, and quote-known-before-trade ordering.

**Factor overlap:** retain unique eligible security-minute-dollar union; sum each factor's gross allocations separately and report overlap rather than presenting their sum as market-wide net capital. Report effective breadth (1 / sum squared normalized nonnegative weights), raw breadth denominator, number missing, and stock-level concentration (HHI and top-k).

Default require at least 12 contributing independent calendar months for daily multi-session hypotheses, at least six non-overlapping full-horizon windows per held-out era and no claim about cross-regime transfer without supported distinct regimes. For intraday H01/H03 require at least 60 independent admitted trading sessions spanning multiple market conditions, valid 1-minute eligible-dollar coverage at least 80% for any broad-direction claim, and materially covered factor breadth; otherwise conclude **UNESTIMABLE / ACCRUE**, not NULL or false. Strong dependence or rare events can raise these floors. No historic availability/source proof is manufactured to satisfy a nominal N.

## 6. Temporal and inferential protocol

1. **Declare cohort and vintages:** calendar, eligible listings/delistings, source entitlements, GMI node/edge version, corporate actions, true observation and reader clocks, rights for each derived output. Reject a row if first-known feature, relation, membership or correction becomes available after decision cutoff. Current snapshot backfills do not count.
2. **Freeze feature code first:** versioned formula, BVC calibration, data requirements, graph relation semantics, options OI publication lag, zero-MAD and universe/winsor bounds. Compare fast/slow source revisions; do not reuse a corrected later vintage for an earlier prediction.
3. **Sample development/validation/holdout chronologically:** no random folds across dates or firms in correlated themes. Apply purge+embargo at least the longest forward-label overlap and relevant observed data/repair latency between partitions. Use time-ordered walk-forward only if each fold's features/normalizers train on records actually knowable at its origin. No label at time t may start at t close when prediction was emitted after close.
4. **Effective N and intervals:** primary inference paired **session-block and network/theme-event cluster bootstrap**, using complete within-date cross-sectional and shared 21-session forward outcomes together; use **calendar-month resampling** for multi-session primary intervals (at least 2,000 resamples with frozen seed) and longer contiguous date-block sensitivity; compare issuer-event clustering for catalyst/quotes. Cluster the *loss difference*, not the forecast score separately, and print sample dates, factor groups, events, effective independent months and window count. If dependence makes simple month blocks inadequate, escalate block length and withhold, do not print an artificially narrow CI. Report split-half, annual and stress/non-stress stability as declared diagnostics.
5. **Multiplicity:** only owner-accepted, preregistered primary p-values are eligible for the named BY family. Preserve trial counts, every failed alternative, missing p-value and the fact that a source-blocked trial is **UNESTIMABLE**. No secondary horizon/regime/case can rescue failed overall primary. Report false-discovery correction and the full attempted study universe.
6. **Placebos and negative controls:** shuffle factor labels within date and sector; permute historic event timestamps only within lawful clocks; use directed graph edge rewiring preserving degree/sector; use future-known article and membership mutations (must be rejected, never produce attractive score); regress estimated BVC against its exact price+volume generating inputs; invert option dealer sign assumption and report sign-fragility. Include zero true synthetic signal, correlated-noise ensemble, an oversized megacap, sector overlap, unavailable returns, and late corrections. Any placebo consistently matches or beats challenger blocks predictive admission.
7. **Prospective forward paper:** select and freeze all features/parameters before the first legal forward observation. Revisions and newly launched feeds start their own qualifying clocks. Preserve retracted original predictions, correction supersession and no-trade/noncoverage; independent review must replicate results from retained permissible inputs and hashes. Customer user testing has a different experimental population and cannot reuse statistical evidence as a replacement for comprehension tests.

**Event-time vs calendar-time:** Detect catalyst first observation by actual known-at; evaluate issuer-event outcomes once per cluster as primary, with calendar-time date-block robustness. For continuous factor panels, define one observation per eligible cutoff and avoid maturity/leadership state changes at unobservable times. Never mix fiscal report-period end with first publication; options OI relates to a prior close and an official posting clock, not an intraday inventory update.

**Stopping rule:** No inference when source basis/rights are unproven, floor N or eligible-dollar coverage is absent, trial family not sealed, available holdout already exposed for this hypothesis, or no reproducible matched baseline. A statistical null under a valid powered test is a useful result; missing data is an **unestimable question**, not a numeric zero, a negative result, or an excuse to peek.
