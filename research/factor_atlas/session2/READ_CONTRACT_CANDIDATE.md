# Factor Atlas S2-P4 — Research-only factor read contract

**Status: CANDIDATE CONTRACT / SYNTHETIC VALIDATION, NOT production reader, NOT admitted market data and NOT customer publication.** This work freezes a structural interchange candidate, not a second source, identity, calendar, membership, data-quality, auth, publication or runtime owner.

The [candidate JSON Schema](contracts/capital_pressure_read_v1_candidate.schema.json) uses the proposed discriminator `factor_atlas.capital_pressure.read.v1` from the Session 2 methodology masterplan. The [sample](evidence/capital_pressure_read_synthetic.json) is **fabricated**, and comes from an uncommitted, experimental local geometry. It is not a delivered live data snapshot, vendor result, representative market tape, license qualification or evidence of instrument ownership. The [validation receipt](evidence/capital_pressure_contract_validation.json) binds the exact bytes and synthetic falsifiers.

## What the strict candidate expresses

A factor snapshot carries estimator/version, one unambiguous UTC session and minute window, calculation cutoff and distinguishable source-known timestamp, read mode, model-based bar-proxy class, source-quality state, aggregated activity, unique-security union and *separately* duplicated basket inclusion.

Every factor carries a membership/version/mode identity, expected versus observed security-minute slots, member coverage, observed gross, nullable **full** gross and full net, model-inferred buyer and seller dollars, dimensionless pressure ratio, classified/neutral/unclassified gross shares, deterministic constituent contributions, member concentration/effective N, and per-factor overlaps. Both gross and net baseline objects preserve sample denominator, n, matched-session coverage, median, MAD, scale, floor, percentile, unclipped/display Z and explicit withholding status.

Null is not zero. A `PARTIAL_COVERAGE` factor must have null full gross, full net, inferred full buyer/seller and full pressure ratio. `ZERO_ACTIVITY` has explicit numerical zero rather than a fabricated ratio. The research schema has **no positive customer, alert, sizing, ranking or trading permission**: all authority flags have fixed FALSE values, `source_quality=NOT_ADMITTED`, `market_pilot_admitted=false`, `customer_publishable=false`, `additive_market_flow_allowed=false`, and `confidence` and `predicted_return` are null. An extra beneficial-owner or hedge-fund identity field is rejected, not silently passed through.

**Schema validation is structural, not scientific or owner authority.** It cannot prove that net equals inferred buy minus sell, that component gross sums reconcile, that the roster is truly point-in-time, that a source hash authenticates the bytes, that the corporate-action/volume basis is accurate, that a quote is an exchange aggressor flag, or that a source owner granted rights. Existing owner checkers and an independently reviewed native integrity implementation must establish those facts. A schema-valid synthetic sample must not be called product-ready.

## Exact witnessed validation

The executable research test `prototype/test_read_contract_schema.py` imports **only committed data/contract files**, never the uncommitted experimental calculation module. Twenty-four hosted-targeted test cases pass locally on Python 3.12: valid sample, twenty-one malformed/overclaimed payloads, a nonfinite JSON refusal and an explicit schema-authority assertion. A separate bounded source-generation witness confirmed one valid synthetic payload and fifteen representative rejected variants. The native factor calculation suite passed 226 earlier; the focused nine-suite **published-candidate recipe passed 250/250 locally**, with the new schema tests registered in the existing dedicated GitHub Actions research workflow and `jsonschema==4.26.0` added to its pinned research requirements.

The following are **unresolved and explicitly not published as accepted code**: the uncommitted `prototype/factor_read_model.py` and `test_factor_read_model.py` demonstrated twenty-six passing and four failing read-model integrity tests, involving constituent category accounting, factor category reconciliation, membership-version baseline key binding and source-known-before-window-close refusal. The attempted targeted patch to those exact functions was **blocked by OpenAI safety checks**. It was *not* retried, split or applied via another carrier; the original file was read back unchanged at the targeted line. It is not incorporated into the manifest, PR source, hosted CI or the schema's acceptance claim. The source-effect denial remains action-scoped, not evidence that all repository writes are unavailable.
