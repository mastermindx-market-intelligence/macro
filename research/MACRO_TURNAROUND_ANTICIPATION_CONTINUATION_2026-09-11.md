## Failures and null behavior

- Inputs not known by the cutoff are invisible rather than backfilled.
- Missing or invalid evidence stays unavailable with a typed exclusion.
- A missing independent family reduces coverage; many correlated indicators do
  not restore it.
- An infeasible family cap is disclosed and constrains confidence.
- Duplicate identical vintages are idempotent; conflicting same-vintage values
  are rejected.
- A stale economic period stays stale even if a revision arrived recently.
- Replay identity changes only when eligible source evidence, configuration,
  cutoff, or prior hysteresis state changes.
- Publication failure never falls back to overwrite, symlink traversal, canonical
  data paths, or a second output plane.
