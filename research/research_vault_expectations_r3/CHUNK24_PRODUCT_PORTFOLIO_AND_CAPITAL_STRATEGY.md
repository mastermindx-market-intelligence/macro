## 3. Two first outputs and one shared investigation interface

### Company research companion

Existing company/Prophet entry -> supported business argument -> source-reported or independently reconstructed change (correctly distinguished) -> actual opposing evidence when available -> next relevant observation -> same-source inspection.

The first single-source version can report that an institution says it revised an estimate; independent reconstruction requires the earlier comparable original. Missing counterevidence stays a coverage limitation, not invented balance. It must not mutate Prophet signals, boards, ranks, sizing or execution.

Acceptance is a real permitted source through the existing producer/reader and user surface, including correct subject, source/version/locator, allowed/denied/stale/missing behavior and correction handling. A populated field or document match is not completion.

### Selected-change brief

Use the existing brief/news/digest owner. Proposed initial attention budget: at most three lead changes, expandable in the existing Brain. This is a ceiling, not a quota or empirically optimal number. A quiet period may yield no lead item, but incomplete retrieval must be distinguished from no material change in known coverage.

A useful item can be a new supported assumption, comparable forecast change, independent counterargument, previously unanswerable question now resolved, or material correction. Repetition and formatting changes are not automatically new intelligence. Do not arm an alert merely because the model proposes something to watch.

Compose private interests under existing portfolio/context permissions. Shared research must not absorb private holdings, quantities, prompts or thesis notes. Do not copy private output into a public channel.

### Brain follow-through and later weekly review

Brain chooses bounded exact lookup, comparable arithmetic, same-source history, cross-source analysis or an explicitly labeled scenario. The existing financial bridge performs calculations. Existing gateway and source-use owners decide deeper retrieval access; the model request is not permission. Preserve the single final reader debit and current budgets.

Later, the existing private brief can review the user's starting thesis, new evidence, unresolved assumptions and relevant observations. It must not become another memory store or a portfolio execution engine. Temporal capabilities are strengthened only when corresponding historical evidence exists.
