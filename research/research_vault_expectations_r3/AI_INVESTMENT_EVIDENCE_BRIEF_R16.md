# A model warning is not a measured investment loss

Research Vault analytical control,17September2026. Assistant-authored interpretation and product design, not automated Brain output, a current trading recommendation or a published customer feature.

## What the source contributes

BIS Working Paper1367, The AI investment race, studies competitive investment and financing through a calibrated model. Its baseline table reports an investment-to-efficient-benchmark ratio of1.51 and a model bust probability of0.50. These are conditional model outputs, not a measured51% waste bill or a calendar-dated50% probability for a listed stock. Sensitivity results vary assumptions; their endpoints are not historical revisions. The paper attributes views to its author, not necessarily the BIS/member central banks. Its financing diagram distinguishes deployed equity from multi-year purchase commitments. [S1][S2]

## Mastermind's interpretation

The useful question is which mechanism applies to a company or portfolio, and what evidence would support or weaken that application. A social-efficiency benchmark and a firm's private investment return are different questions. Some companies could perform well in a sector with socially excessive investment in a model. Conversely, optimistic company demand language does not independently invalidate a model about competition or financing.

Map the mechanism to research jobs rather than average it into sentiment:

| Job | Evidence needed | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Monetization | Issuer-specific utilization, realized prices, retention and collections | No issuer probability from sector assumptions alone |
| Cost versus capacity | Comparable lease/cash definitions, unit prices, deployment volumes and timing | Unknown decomposition stays unknown |
| Financing dependency | Contract type, outstanding versus committed amount, payments, guarantees and cancellation | Relationship does not establish loss exposure |
| Challenge a premise | Independent observations/forecasts addressing that exact assumption | Repeated publication is not independent confirmation |
| Investment return | Capital vintage, attributable incremental cash flow and horizon | Total company revenue is not program return |

These are Mastermind questions, not a claim that this paper answers all of them. Different relationship types must not be summed into a current common exposure merely because they link the same firms.
