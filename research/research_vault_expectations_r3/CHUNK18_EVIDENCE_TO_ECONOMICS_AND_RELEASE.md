## 3. The first company research brief

Use an existing company/Prophet detail or Brain entry point, not a front-cover redesign. First prove one company/question; later a small named cohort tests generalization. Do not call one example universal coverage.

The brief has five parts:

- **What changed:** actual result, stated forecast revision, range adjustment, reporting-basis change, source correction, new argument or late-arriving older report. Arrival time alone is not a new economic event.
- **Why it may matter:** source-supported business mechanism, clearly attributed to the institution or Mastermind. Co-mention does not establish causality.
- **What the numbers support:** one compatible comparison/calculation, with explicit assumptions and missing inputs. Null is not zero.
- **Where the case is contested:** actual contrary evidence or a supported rival assumption. Do not invent balance or infer universal agreement from no captured dissent.
- **What to inspect next:** exact evidence plus the unresolved observation that would change the interpretation. A proposed condition is not an armed monitor.

Brain supports follow-up; Prophet receives explanation/challenge beside an independently generated signal; private portfolio composition uses authorized holdings at request time. Shared permitted evidence can be reused, but private prompts/holdings do not enter a global corpus or answer cache.

Generated answers and original-document access are different licensing configurations. Neither paraphrasing nor a citation automatically establishes a right to disclose restricted information.

## 4. Evidence-to-financial-calculation handoff

The existing flagship record correctly says its supplied calculator inputs are not automatically source-verified. Vault should qualify them rather than duplicate arithmetic.

### Dimensional identity

Map these meanings onto current owner interfaces; this is not a new registered runtime schema:

- existing issuer/security/listing identity and consolidated/segment scope;
- metric definition: cash/noncash, GAAP/adjusted, gross/net, period average/end-period;
- units, scale, currency and any accepted conversion with its source/vintage;
- observation/target period, fiscal/calendar convention, duration and scenario;
- role: actual, management guidance, broker forecast, user assumption, Mastermind assumption or deterministic derivative;
- original value/range with qualifications such as approximately, at least, excluding or contingent on;
- source/extraction versions and literal support, table header/footnote or honest locator limitation;
- actual observed/acquired/extracted/product-available clocks, separate from printed publication date;
- existing source-use decision and audience/purpose.

A capex/FCF label is not a definition. A ticker is not issuer identity. A freshly generated index does not make an old forecast current. Missing clocks stay missing.

### Analytical outcomes

**Comparable source-backed inputs:** compute the named result and preserve operands.
**Explicit assumptions:** permit a scenario under the existing financial-scope contract, visibly labeled; no promotion to actual or institution forecast.
**Incomplete inputs:** explain the gap; preserve independently valid partial calculations, not a fabricated total.
**Incompatible definitions:** explain why comparison fails and what would align it. A measurement difference can itself be useful intelligence.
**Conflicting evidence:** retain supported alternatives/version status; do not pick the convenient number without evidence.
**Changed or withdrawn source:** reconcile through current source/publication owners before recalculating dependents; no silent source substitution beneath a saved reference.

Reporting-basis change must not automatically become lower physical investment. A range midpoint is not an institution's expected value. Quarter and TTM values cannot silently combine. Pre-tax adjustments need a compatible treatment before after-tax comparisons. The existing R15 control supplies these examples; no repeat extraction/test is claimed. [S6]

A sector model's conditional probability is not a measured issuer probability or target price. Arithmetic can be exact while the conclusion is unsupported. Financial decomposition may be order-dependent and is not causal identification. Sensitivities remain scenarios unless separately evaluated. No output changes Prophet score, ranking, entry, sizing or execution; future signal use requires the existing PIT/forward-promotion process.

## 5. Disagreement as assumptions, not sentiment voting

Classify whether sources differ on facts, forecasts, definitions, mechanisms, timing or investment conclusions. Different price targets can reflect different multiples despite identical earnings estimates. Different capex values can reflect finance-lease treatment. Different margins may concern different segments or periods. These are not one generic contradiction type.

Start with a source-backed claim/comparison/dependency projection inside current owners, not a new graph database. A document co-mention is not an economic exposure. A report and several articles quoting it are not independent confirmations. Unknown independence remains unknown.

A useful multi-source answer identifies the small set of assumptions separating conclusions and the discriminating evidence. Its proposed next observation should target that disagreement, not subscribe the user to every mention. Existing monitor, editorial and alert owners retain their permission and activation rules. No new alert/risk score is created.

## 6. Acquire complementary evidence, not maximum volume

Different questions need different evidence:

| Task | Requirement | Valuable increment |
|---|---|---|
| Explain a report's stated revision | Current original with quoted old/new values and definition | Inspectable original behind a summary |
| Independently reconstruct a revision | Comparable earlier/later originals and honest chronology | Missing predecessor/version |
| Explain disagreement | Resolved distinct origins and aligned comparison or explicit mismatch | Independent view with different supported assumption |
| Compare expectations with results | Eligible pre-result expectation plus authoritative realized observation | Missing pre-event vintage |
| Relate thesis to portfolio | Supported issuer relationship plus private authorized context | Relevant business evidence, not generic mention |
| Examine qualifications | Actual appendix/table with headers, conditions and version | Recovering support already in an acquired original |

**Complementarity:** two missing reports may close a question together while each alone has little immediate usefulness. A greedy per-document score can miss that. Evaluate necessary bundles/sequences alongside individual reports; no new allocator was implemented.

For frozen task q, let A(q,D) be adjudicated supported completion using corpus D. Evaluate weighted improvement in A when adding a permitted set, with task-importance weights frozen before outcomes. This is a research utility measure, not a return model, prestige ranking or trade score. Do not trust unverified model self-grades.

Count a resolved task once. Reuse in three surfaces is not automatically three independent customer benefits. Separate redundancy, independence, continuity, freshness, task coverage and repeated use. Preserve a bounded exploratory acquisition component where permitted so current concentration does not become a self-reinforcing blind spot. The existing collector remains sole acquisition owner. No account-pooling or limit-circumvention permission is assumed.
