## Recommended decision sequence

1. **Use the existing corpus to prove one useful AI investigation.** Actual original-source discovery, permitted passage reading, honest quota behavior, and a correct answer—not only a synthetic retrieval test.
2. **Request a provider-approved business/bulk or pooled-capacity quote.** Do not assume separate user accounts may be combined to avoid a shared restriction. Separate internal research processing, generated customer analysis, original-report access and public editorial/retention permissions.
3. **Run a bounded monthly marginal-capacity pilot once permission is clear.** At the current public monthly price, one additional account is a $150 list-price increment; two are $300. A negotiated offering may differ. This is a proposed pilot, not authorized spending.
4. **Judge the second, third and fourth increments separately.** Three accounts remains a likely-capacity hypothesis from the prior inventory work, not a current contractual or complete-feed guarantee. Do not buy the fourth based on the combined benefit of earlier increments.

The existing locally observed 70-download configuration is historical implementation evidence, not a newly verified provider allowance. No new feed census or pooled allowance was measured here.
