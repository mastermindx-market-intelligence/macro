## 3. The product: three questions, not a giant collection of widgets

**Prepare the release:** Which exact statistic is about to publish? What does our frozen model estimate, what do distinct benchmarks say, what is uncertain, and which inputs have changed since the last qualified view?

**Explain the print:** What did the official source first publish, what was subsequently revised, and how does it compare with each genuinely comparable forecast? Which market prices moved in the declared observation window, and which causal explanations are still hypotheses?

**Inspect rates and repricing:** Which contracts and curve points are observed, what averages or horizons they represent, how a policy comparison is derived, and which combinations of expected policy, real rates, inflation compensation and term premia could explain the evidence?

These should be one coherent journey using existing Release Radar, Fed dialog, Rates/Curves and Forward Path seams. It is not a new operating-system shell. ## 4. The hard semantic model

### 4.1 Evidence classes travel with the value

Every decision-relevant observation belongs to a named class: official measurement; house estimate; market-derived expectation under a specified contract/model; survey/participant projection; estimated decomposition; or explicitly hypothetical scenario. Models and prose cannot convert one class into another. The class is independent of whether the number looks plausible.

Each value carries a local, versioned basis record: subject/series or contract identity; target and basket/index; observation and reference interval; unit/scale; seasonal treatment; rate index; compounding/day-count or averaging law when applicable; source/version and content reference; first public evidence and its precision; house observation/admission; publication cutoff; model/target epoch; rights; quality/missingness and inherited authority. Fields genuinely irrelevant to a target use explicit not-applicable values, not guessed defaults.

Do not impose this as a new estate-wide schema migration. Qualify the RIC fields at their existing owner boundaries and retain references to already canonical metadata. Numeric comparisons are accepted only by a documented rule that checks these bases. The output is one of **directly comparable**, **comparable after a named transformation**, **not comparable**, **not identified**, or **unavailable**. A transform changing the economic question creates a new explicit hypothesis rather than quietly changing the old target.

### 4.2 Contract-implied rates are not individual policy decisions

CME's FedWatch method uses monthly-average effective-funds expectations and explicit assumptions about meeting outcomes. Three-month SOFR futures instead settle on compounded SOFR over their reference quarter. Those observations differ by rate index, calendar interval and compounding. A point sampled from an interpolated strip is not automatically a policy rate on a particular meeting day. [E01, E02]

The current `rate_futures.implied_path` remains useful as a **reference-period curve** when labelled honestly. To derive a meeting-conditioned comparison, retain realised and remaining accrual days, the policy effective-date rule, contract month/quarter, quote/settlement time, rate-index basis and any mapping from effective rates to the target corridor. Preserve both source and quote age when falling back between strips. Do not invent a SOFR–EFFR basis, convexity adjustment, terminal meeting rate or probability from missing inputs.

Underidentified inputs must admit multiple possible paths. In a stipulated 30-day month, 15 days at 4.50% and 15 at 4.00% produce a 4.25% monthly average. The month-average decline is 25 basis points, but the second-half level is 50 basis points lower. With more unknown segments, multiple paths can match the same average. “One expected cut” is not an identified path unless the required assumptions are supplied and accepted.

A minimum viable meeting comparison may therefore report the conditional end-rate range or an explicitly model-dependent distribution, not pretend to reproduce a complete FedWatch service. Probabilities from market prices also need their risk-premium and scenario assumptions; they are not automatically physical probabilities of policy outcomes. No new policy-intent predictor is authorized.

### 4.3 SEP dots and inflation projections require matching horizons

SEP interest-rate dots are participants' appropriate-policy projections at the end of specified calendar years, not a committee commitment or a record of next-meeting probabilities. Its inflation projection convention is also not a next-month CPI target. Comparison requires a documented mapping to an equivalent market interval; a generic interpolation by integer months does not prove equivalence. [E05]

CPI and PCE differ in formula, weights and scope. Monthly, year-on-year, three-month annualized and long-horizon compensation are distinct measures. BLS seasonally adjusted history can revise. Retain the first published target and its same-vintage denominator, later revised history and any derived proxy as different records. [E07, E08]

A monthly CPI rate can be annualized arithmetically under the explicit assumption that it repeats. That calculation does not predict a year of CPI, transform it into PCE, or make it comparable to ten-year inflation compensation. A “wedge” is useful only when its operands and inference are defensible. A model/Cleveland/prediction-market comparison is not Street consensus unless that exact licensed survey object exists.

ALFRED's vintage archive is valuable for reconstruction, but observations and revisions are generally added after release, and date attribution can use fallbacks. A day-level archive receipt cannot prove a minute-level house first-print capture. First-print accuracy claims must use the admitted MRI truth basis, not a convenient later download. [E09, E10]

### 4.4 Curve decomposition is not a sum of independent causes

Treasury's published curve is a par-curve construction with specific quote/convention/methodology. A par yield is not a zero rate, and a zero rate is not a directly observed expected future policy rate. Keep the existing data owner and qualify the transformation instead of treating every series with “yield” in its name as interchangeable. [E03, E04]

There are different decomposition coordinate systems. One writes nominal yield as expected nominal short rates plus an estimated term premium. Another compares nominal and real yields to form inflation compensation, subject to instrument, liquidity and basis qualifications. Adding real-rate change, inflation-compensation change **and** a term-premium change from the first model can double-count parts of the same nominal move.

Term premia are not directly observed; KW and ACM depend on model specification, fitting and identification. Model disagreement should be shown with provenance and uncertainty, not marketed as a newly validated trading signal. [E06]

For example, an illustrative 30-basis-point nominal move is consistent with expected-short +10 and term-premium +20, or expected-short −10 and term-premium +40. The total alone does not identify which occurred. The product's job is to expose the additional measurements or assumptions that discriminate the explanations and retain the residual unknowns.

## 7. Research programme: narrow, difficult and falsifiable

The core scientific challenge is learning from sparse, revised, heterogeneous economic releases without changing the target or counting correlated evidence as independent. Existing MRI model families and sealed trial records are the starting point, not a prompt to invent a new research factory.

**H1: coherent first-print models improve on appropriate baselines.** Qualify the existing CPI bridge/factor and NFP candidates under exact target and vintage contracts. Compare strongest eligible naive/trend and existing published nowcast sources on the same forecast origin and target. Preserve claims as benchmark-only and other recorded kills. An apparent gain that disappears under coherent vintages falsifies the old claim; it does not authorize relabeling the old score as current.

**H2: basis-qualified interpretation improves research quality.** In a blinded task comparison, the corrected desk should reduce wrong-horizon, wrong-index, stale-claim and unsupported-causality answers relative to the current baseline. Measure important omissions, correct abstentions, source traceability and analyst effort—not just preference for polished copy.

**H3: conditional reaction evidence adds calibrated context.** At a declared window, distinguish the official surprise against a named benchmark from simultaneous revisions, policy statements and other news. A descriptive reaction distribution is not proof the release caused the full daily move. Existing Transmission is the read owner; no causal-DAG alpha or GLT rebuild.

**H4: additional model complexity earns its cost.** A more complex mixture, component bridge or reaction model must beat its preregistered comparator on the same admissible sample and error criterion. Forecast intervals require assessed coverage and sharpness, not decorative quantiles. Model-originated narratives cannot choose probabilities or trade sizes.

Before any new outcome work, E0 freezes: forecast origin; exact target/basket/period/first-release rule; inclusion and missingness rules; training/tuning/held-out splits; ordinary corrections; baselines; allowed trial count; meaningful margin or precision criterion; dependence treatment; and accept/hold/no-go criteria. Reuse the existing #7909 ledger rather than rerunning its 48 RD1/18 swing configurations as a “new” search. Any genuinely different study receives the existing owner's separately bounded approval/provenance, not an unbounded new queue.

Use chronological blocked validation, expanding/rolling windows where preregistered and cluster-aware uncertainty. Monthly CPI headline/core and related release-day moves are not independent observations just because there are more columns. Ragged-edge releases and annual revisions create availability changes; standardizing on the entire later dataset is leakage. Preserve cases where source evidence is too weak to score. A late archived actual cannot become a contemporaneous truth receipt retroactively.

Retain deterministic baselines before any learned explanation. Do not mix first-print accuracy, revised-history fit, direction, calibration and economic impact into one “accuracy” percentage. Prospective outcomes accrue under existing immutable forecast and target owners with original code/input/target hashes. Issued, withheld, abstained and missed opportunities are recorded under the appropriate existing census.

Natural time is a real dependency. A new forecast cannot be graded before its release; underpowered evidence stays insufficient. While it matures, execute independent basis, source, consumer, rights and recovery work. Scientific NO-GO can close a hypothesis without creating alpha; it cannot waive a mandatory useful product capability or conceal an unresolved release gate.
