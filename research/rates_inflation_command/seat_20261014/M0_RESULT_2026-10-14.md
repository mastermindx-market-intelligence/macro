## 3. What this means

- **One claim survives, and only as retrospective.** The PCE headline champion beats its baseline on the coherent first-print target by a wide margin. R≈0.46 clears Holm easily. The verdict is `QUALIFIED_RETROSPECTIVE`:
  - the evaluation is backward-looking over published vintages;
  - it is not a forward-graded promotion;
  - accuracy wording stays withheld from consumers until the nightly forward log grades it.
- **No other model is demonstrated.**
  - Headline CPI models point the right way (R 0.65–0.78), but none clears the family correction.
  - The two CPI composites with `non_pit_inputs` could not be scored point-in-time in any case.
  - Core CPI, NFP v1 and PPI are indistinguishable from their baselines.
  - "Not demonstrated" means this test failed to show skill. It does not mean the model has none.
- **The NFP v3 factor model is worse than its baseline** under the coherent target (R 1.37). The protocol therefore withdraws its claim from every consumer (§4).
- **Added complexity has not paid** wherever it was contrasted: the bridge over v2, v3 over v2, and v3 over v1 for NFP.

The CPI v2 headline and coherent_ridge results (Holm p 0.076 and 0.056) are near-misses.
They should not be read as "almost validated". The protocol's threshold was fixed in
advance; a second look at the same family would spend new budget.

## 4. Consumer consequence (the one required change)

`FALSIFIED_UNDER_COHERENT_TARGET` withdraws `nfp.v3_factor`'s accuracy claim from every
human and machine consumer.

- **What is withdrawn.** The model's display claim and its shadow block in `release_forecast.json`, together with the dashboard copy that presents it.
- **What stays.** The NFP v1 champion stays as it was, still NOT_DEMONSTRATED, so no accuracy wording is added.
- **What is unchanged.** `regime_outlook` is byte-unchanged.

Nothing is promoted.

## 5. Review findings carried forward

ORCH-B's independent review of the runner raised no blocking finding. It raised nine
non-blocking notes:

1. The `asof` defence relies on the caller.
2. Pass-1 champion errors are swallowed rather than surfaced.
3. The `--declare-budget` docstring lags the behaviour.
4. MAE subsets differ per baseline.
5. Receipt identity omits the runner hash. It is recorded here instead.
6. There is no no-clobber guard on the receipt path.
7. The `-m` CLI path has no test.
8. Sensitivity details are defined by the runner: the S1/S2 0.05 threshold, S1 parity by period, and the S2 errata heuristic.
9. H4 intervals are scored against a mixed-vintage `owner_actual`.

None of these changes a verdict above. Notes 4 and 9 qualify how the H4 contrasts may be quoted: as same-target comparisons only, never as interval coverage.

## 6. Not done here

- Forward grading of the PCE champion belongs to the existing nightly forward log. No new evaluation plane is created.
- A re-run, a re-specified family, or an added model would each be a new declared trial with its own budget.
