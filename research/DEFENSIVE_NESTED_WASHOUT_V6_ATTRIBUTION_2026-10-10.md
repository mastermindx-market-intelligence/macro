## Verified new capabilities
1. **Raw StochRSI source attribution.** A symmetric two-order exact decomposition partitions a completed-native raw StochRSI change into native-RSI movement versus change of RSI rolling minimum/maximum. Components add exactly; degenerate windows stay UNKNOWN. The decomposition is a mathematical accounting choice, not literal causality.
2. **Smoothing-source distinction.** For SMA3 K on raw StochRSI, `K(t)-K(t-1)=[raw(t)-raw(t-3)]/3`. K can rise while current raw falls because an older low rolls out. D obeys the analogous K relation. A bullish StochRSI condition is not automatically a rising RSI.
3. **Exact conditional K/D price boundary.** With prior native K values K1,K2 and prior raw r1,r2, next native K>D iff `raw_now > 1.5*(K1+K2)-r1-r2`. The current RSI rolling interval is inverted to a conditional required RSI and through previous Wilder gain/loss to a conditional native close. A new crossover separately needs prior K<=D; raw threshold >=100 is impossible, <0 has no unique close, and flat/undefined cases are held. An initial prototype wrongly flagged the unreachable case as fresh-possible; a failing test caught it and the flag was repaired.
4. **Same-native combined threshold.** Where both K/D and RSI-MACD have finite reachable boundaries and prior fresh-cross gates hold, their joint conditional close is the greater threshold. This is **not** valid as one price for a W/2W context and a daily trigger: timeframes close at different instants.
5. **Dependence-preserving paired test.** Require exactly matched (symbol, episode), arm date, evaluation endpoint and immediate baseline. Preserve skipped and incomplete episodes. Resample moving blocks of four **consecutive calendar quarters** (configurable one/four/eight), including empty quarters; stocks in the same quarter remain grouped. <8 active quarters or inadequate block coverage yields NO interval. Intervals are exploratory, not adjusted p-values or proof of alpha.
6. **Read-only offline replay.** Added source-only CSV CLI for already-legitimately-available OHLC and an explicit session calendar. Exports completed weekly/2W/lower oscillator attribution, RSI-MACD cross categories, fixed master episodes, and paired gate results with strong synthetic/empirical provenance labels.
