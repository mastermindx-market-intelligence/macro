UNDERSTAND THE CONDITIONS

Why we are waiting

The example contains both recovery evidence and reasons for caution. Neither path is confirmed.

Same moment, same horizon

A daily close and an after-hours quote cannot silently share one current timestamp.

Positioning is an estimate

Gamma can amplify either direction. Expiry and position assumptions must remain visible.

A trigger is not a trade

A condition becoming true does not establish predictive reliability, entry quality or appropriate size.

Unknown stays unknown

Missing observations, stale inputs and untested probabilities cannot be replaced with confidence.

This study uses illustrative labels and unverified sample levels. It is not the live market.
