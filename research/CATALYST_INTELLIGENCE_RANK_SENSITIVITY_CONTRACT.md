## 3. Sensitivity is not uncertainty

These are controlled perturbations, not probability distributions over model error. No likelihood is assigned to the stress or to rank reversal. No calibrated uncertainty interval, statistical confidence, measured correlation, portfolio allocation or risk-adjusted preference is supplied.

A small crossing says which assumption may be decision-relevant. It does not prove the baseline is wrong or that the perturbation is plausible. Conversely, a large crossing does not prove the thesis is sound. Domain research must establish the plausible changes and dependence between them.

In this fixture only, branches zero/one are favorable, two delayed and three negative. Production must consume explicit owner-native outcome mappings rather than guessing semantics from array position. Do not move clinical approval probability into a procurement model or compare raw clinical success with raw award success. Compare qualified equity outputs while keeping the distinct specialist targets visible.

The three controls are alternatives. A generic haircut is not a completed revised financial model. In production, funding, dilution, retained rights, prices, timing and commercial assumptions can be coupled; the existing financial owner must recompute those relationships. Price sensitivity cannot silently produce a fresh quote, a new source timestamp or entry authority.
