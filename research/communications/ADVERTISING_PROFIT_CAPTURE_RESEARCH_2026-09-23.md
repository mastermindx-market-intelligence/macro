# Communications Phase 2 — Advertising, Discovery and Profit Capture

**As of:** 2026-09-23. **Operation:** `gmi-communications-research-20260923-sol-001`. **Carrier:** Macro PR #7794 / `claude/communications-sector-research-20260923`.

**Status:** Original research and proposed product requirements. Not accepted production design, a completed sector masterplan, a securities recommendation, a predictive backtest or a Fable dispatch. Public sources only. The Phase 1 foundation remains valid; this chapter deepens its advertising/search/social/intermediation families rather than replacing the shared sector template.

## 1. The investor job this research advances

The useful question is not whether digital advertising is growing. It is whether a particular business can convert a specific change in attention, commercial intent or advertiser productivity into durable per-share cash earnings that exceed dated expectations. This requires separating the advertiser, the audience owner, the buying intermediary, the selling intermediary, the measurement provider and the content owner. Several may touch one transaction. Their reported revenues cannot simply be added to measure the economic opportunity.

The proposed first research vertical is therefore **advertising profit capture and expectation delivery**. It should explain a real operating change, identify the directly affected business, show the financial bridge and state what remains unknown. The product must remain useful when consensus, financial materiality or a causal coefficient is unavailable. It must not substitute a confident buy label for that missing evidence.

This phase supplies three deep company models, contrasting peer evidence, dated company-guidance comparisons, an AI-discovery transmission model, source-definition traps and a source-level integration assessment. It does not finish the other Communications economic families or the historical-return validation program.

## 2. An economic model, not another basket list

### 2.1 Keep the payment chain coherent

Use an illustrative advertiser budget of 100 units. A hypothetical transaction could allocate 80 to inventory/content economics, 10 to buying services, 5 to selling services and 5 to measurement/data. These are invented teaching amounts, not market fee estimates. The allocations sum to 100; counting the original 100 and all four downstream receipts as 200 creates fictitious market size.

Real arrangements can bundle these roles, net costs against revenue, pay incentives later, or charge separately. The analyst must first establish the accounting perimeter and payment responsibility. A company's gross billings, gross media spend, net revenue, contribution after traffic costs and profit are different quantities. Similar terminology does not establish comparable economics.

### 2.2 Model advertiser value before inferring pricing power

For a specified campaign and horizon, let incremental gross profit before advertising be the extra contribution that would not have occurred without the campaign. Subtract advertising and relevant incremental servicing costs to obtain an advertiser contribution estimate. Attributed conversions are not necessarily incremental conversions, and revenue is not gross profit. Refunds, repeat purchases, organic substitution and cross-channel duplication can alter the result.

Even a credible average return on a campaign does not establish the return on the next unit of spending. A platform might raise price without losing volume when advertiser value improves, but the relationship depends on the next available opportunity, competition, campaign constraints and the advertiser's alternatives. We should research marginal budget allocation rather than assume a fixed elasticity from an average return metric.

### 2.3 Model the platform's retained economics separately

An audience business can be expressed conceptually as monetizable opportunities multiplied by realized net yield. A buying intermediary can be expressed as eligible client expenditure multiplied by economically retained fees, plus separable services. A publisher or supply platform needs inventory, demand access, fill, realized yield and revenue-sharing terms.

These are reasoning decompositions, not universal accounting identities with fully observed inputs. Do not multiply every available KPI together: engagement, impressions, clicks and conversion measures can overlap. Use the fewest non-overlapping drivers that reconcile to the relevant financial line. Where an input is unobserved, expose a range or leave the bridge incomplete.

From revenue, bridge serving costs, content/distribution shares, sales/support, product investment, depreciation, capital requirements, taxes, financing and diluted claims. A margin gain obtained through a longer asset-life estimate is different from a reduction in cash required to serve customers. A repurchase authorization is different from executed repurchases and a changed diluted share count.

## 3. Meta: monetization evidence is not an AI earnings attribution

### Observed evidence

In Q2 2026, Meta reported advertising revenue of $59.363 billion on total revenue of $60.801 billion. Impressions grew 14% and average price per ad 12%. Operating income was $18.775 billion versus $20.441 billion. Expenses included $2.40 billion of legal charges and $1.18 billion of severance [M1]. Advertising thus represented approximately 97.63% of reported revenue; this is not an AI-only exposure percentage.

The 10-Q defines average ad price across objectives and describes product/geography mix effects. Reels monetizes below Feed and Stories. Management attributes stronger demand partly to ad-performance improvements but states that precise attribution of spending decisions to individual trends is difficult [M2]. The April 29 release guided Q2 revenue to $58–61 billion [M3]. The actual result was inside that range, not above its ceiling.

### Proposed business model

Research four pathways independently: additional engagement; more valuable ad selection; higher monetization of an existing format; and new business-messaging/subscription economics. Separate the familiar advertising base from any emerging non-advertising activity. Do not assume that a total Family of Apps margin can be assigned to Instagram, Reels or WhatsApp individually.

A stronger ad-selection system may improve advertiser value and price realization. It may also increase serving requirements. More short-form engagement can replace higher-yield engagement elsewhere. A newly monetized surface can create incremental revenue or cannibalize existing activity. These alternatives need cohort, mix and cost evidence, not merely an aggregate usage chart.

### Arithmetic and its limits

The rounded volume/price reconstruction is `1.14 × 1.12 − 1 = 27.68%`; rounding and scope prevent requiring exact equality with reported advertising growth. Removing only the two disclosed expense items mechanically gives $22.355 billion of operating income, approximately 9.36% above the prior reported amount and a 36.77% margin. This is a **sensitivity**, not a normalized earnings estimate: recurrence, tax effects and the prior-period adjustment basis remain unresolved [M1].

### What could justify a rerating thesis

The proposed positive thesis needs repeatable, comparable monetization strength; evidence that ad quality rather than only ad load explains it; a cost/capital bridge; and expectations that do not already embed the improvement. The adverse thesis is stronger consumption with weaker economic yield, capital requirements outrunning durable contribution, or apparent quality gains concentrated in a narrow test population.

A useful dossier should display the operating evidence first, then the cost bridge, then the expectation comparison. It should say exactly why an AI attribution remains uncertain. A vendor experiment can support a mechanism hypothesis without establishing a company-wide earnings coefficient.

## 4. Alphabet: own the business scope and the cash boundary

### Observed evidence

Q2 Search & other revenue was $63.271 billion versus $54.190 billion; YouTube advertising was $11.055 billion versus $9.796 billion; Network revenue was $7.303 billion versus $7.354 billion. Consolidated operating cash flow of $39.069 billion less property/equipment cash expenditure of $44.924 billion gives a negative $5.855 billion research cash subtotal [G1]. That cash difference is consolidated, not Search-specific.

The filing's Search & other scope includes properties beyond Google.com. Paid clicks rose 13% and cost per click 3%; Network impressions fell 12% and price per impression rose 13%. Network includes AdMob, AdSense and Google Ad Manager. TAC and other revenue costs have different scopes; YouTube content economics cannot be inferred solely from TAC [G2].

### Proposed business model

Separate search distribution, commercial-intent monetization, owned video inventory, third-party network services and non-advertising businesses. Alphabet's equity is an exposure to the combination, not a pure search instrument. A thematic sensitivity that ignores the other operations, shared expenditure, investments and financing can materially misrepresent the security.

For AI-assisted discovery, distinguish incremental sessions from substituted sessions, commercial from informational activity, and gross revenue from the cost of answering or acquiring the activity. Changes in external referral traffic should be analyzed as a publisher/distributor relationship rather than assumed to equal changes in Google's retained profit.

### Why price and revenue need a joint interpretation

The rounded Search factor bridge gives `1.13 × 1.03 − 1 = 16.39%`, compared with reported Search & other growth of approximately 16.76%. For Network, `0.88 × 1.13 − 1 = −0.56%`, compared with reported revenue growth of approximately −0.69% [G1–G2]. These are consistency checks, not causal estimates. Higher average price can coexist with fewer opportunities and lower revenue.

A lower blended TAC ratio can result from business mix as well as better contract economics. It is not an advertising gross margin. The research must preserve distribution, content and infrastructure costs separately rather than claim that every basis point of TAC improvement is incremental profit.

### Competing hypotheses to test

One hypothesis is that richer discovery increases commercially valuable activity and strengthens the economics of owned surfaces. Another is that answering costs rise or valuable commercial steps migrate to other channels. A third is that new formats expand the market while weakening some publishers and intermediaries. The hypotheses can coexist across different tasks and geographies.

The discriminating evidence should concern realized monetization, retained customer/advertiser activity, unit cost, and the resulting segment-to-consolidated bridge. Usage counts and new format announcements are insufficient. No Search-only free-cash-flow estimate or current valuation verdict is established here.

## 5. The Trade Desk: retention, reported take rate and actual economics

### Observed evidence

Q2 revenue was $715.057 million, platform operations expense $184.333 million, operating income $101.577 million and adjusted EBITDA $241.279 million [T1]. The May 7 Q2 outlook called for revenue of at least $750 million and adjusted EBITDA of approximately $260 million [T3]. The result was approximately 4.66% below the original revenue floor and 7.20% below the approximate EBITDA outlook. This is a company-guidance comparison, not a consensus surprise.

The 10-Q says new-client spending growth was partly offset by lower spending from existing clients. It also describes some supplier components shifting from deductions to revenue into platform expense. A changed useful-life estimate reduced Q2 depreciation by $4.6 million. Receivables/payables reflect gross billing obligations while revenue is generally net [T2].

### Proposed business model

The analytical unit should be the client cohort and the economic services supplied, not simply the number of retained customers. A customer can remain contracted while reducing expenditure. New clients can lift total spend while an established cohort weakens. The proposed dossier should separate count retention, comparable-cohort spend, net revenue retention when actually reported, and spend share within the client budget. Unknown cohort metrics stay unknown.

Take rate needs a specified denominator and a reconciliation for discounts, services, customer/channel mix and gross-versus-net presentation. We should not equate a higher reported ratio with stronger bargaining power until the economic bridge is known. Equally, we should not assume every reported increase is merely presentation. The filing describes several mechanisms; their individual effects are not all quantified.

### A presentation-resistant subtotal, not a substitute profit measure

Subtracting platform operations expense from reported revenue gives $530.724 million versus $543.059 million, a decline of approximately 2.27% [T1]. This research subtotal can cancel a pure shift of the same supplier cost from netted revenue to expense. It is **not** reported gross profit and does not isolate organic economics: platform operations also includes other costs and accounting changes.

For the pure illustrative case, increasing both revenue and platform expense by the same ten units leaves their difference unchanged. That algebra is useful as a consistency check, not permission to strip a company's entire supplier-cost increase. The exact reclassified amount was not established in this pass.

### Research thesis and falsifiers

A constructive thesis would require evidence that better buying decisions, accessible inventory and workflow integration increase retained client value after fees and support costs. Adverse evidence would include persistent weakness in established-client activity, price/mix changes masking volume loss, or rising query/serving requirements without corresponding monetization.

Kokai Zuma was announced on August 27, after Q2 ended [T5]. It can enter the forward catalyst register; it cannot retrospectively explain Q2 results. Adoption, advertiser results and retained revenue from the release need later observations. An announcement is not an accepted earnings contribution.

## 6. Contrasting businesses prevent a false sector-wide conclusion

### Magnite: connected-TV strength and a cash-label trap

Q2 contribution ex-TAC was $189.595 million; CTV contributed $97.133 million and grew 36%, while DV+ grew 2%. The original May 6 total ex-TAC guide was $177–181 million; the actual was approximately 4.75% above its ceiling [MG1–MG2]. This does not establish a comparable consensus surprise or prove value transferred from any particular buying platform.

The release defines headline “operating cash flow” as adjusted EBITDA less capital expenditures. Its GAAP cash-flow statement separately reports net cash from operating activities for the half-year. The labels must remain distinct, as must the periods. Its adjusted EBITDA margin uses contribution ex-TAC as the denominator [MG1]. No peer comparison may silently substitute GAAP revenue or GAAP operating cash.

### Pinterest: the denominator matters

Pinterest's rounded regional table shows $880 million of U.S./Canada revenue out of $1.180 billion, but 106 million of 640 million period-end monthly active users. Those shares are about 74.58% and 16.56%, respectively. Reported ARPU uses average beginning/end MAUs, not simply the displayed ending count [P1].

The proposed model therefore decomposes revenue by region and monetization cohort. A large audience in a low-yield region is not economically equivalent to the same count in a mature region. Nor should geography be reduced to the exchange on which the security trades. Product use, advertising billing and revenue geography need their own definitions.

### Reddit: thematic prominence versus financial materiality

Q2 reported advertising revenue of $762 million on total revenue of $805 million implies about 94.66% advertising exposure using rounded figures [R1]. Other revenue is not automatically all AI licensing. Research on content licensing and answer-engine relationships should retain those possibilities without recasting the company as primarily a licensing business.

The unresolved questions are the retention and monetization of audience cohorts, dependence on referral/distribution channels, serving/moderation costs, and any separately documented licensing economics. A popular narrative can be financially small, strategically important, or both; the record must distinguish those dimensions.

### Snap and Tencent: broaden the model, not just the ticker count

Snap reported Q2 revenue growth of 19%, adjusted EBITDA of about $250 million and a GAAP operating loss; its non-GAAP reconciliation excludes restructuring costs [S1]. The research must keep ad/subscription/product scope and adjusted-versus-reported profitability separate before comparing it with another attention business.

Tencent's issuer-distributed release reports Marketing Services revenue of RMB43.565 billion, up 22%, within a diversified business. It attributes growth partly to recommendation, campaign-management and closed-loop capabilities [C1]. This is a valuable regional mechanism contrast, not a completed China dossier or independent causal proof. Weixin-linked commerce and gaming should be investigated on their own economic terms rather than forced into a U.S. social-platform template.

The combined evidence rejects treating one intermediary's quarter as a verdict on every independent advertising business. It does not establish which company took whose budgets. Such a claim would require matched customer/spend evidence and comparable accounting scopes.

## 7. Dated expectations: reconstruct what the comparison actually means

The comparison grammar must preserve `range`, `lower_bound`, `upper_bound`, `approximate_point` and `qualitative` guidance. A lower bound has no implied midpoint or upper bound. A result above a range midpoint but inside the range is not an above-range result.

Three documented examples now anchor the design: Meta inside its original Q2 revenue range; The Trade Desk below its original Q2 revenue floor; and Magnite above its original ex-TAC range ceiling. The Trade Desk's Q2 2025 revenue of $694.039 million exceeded its original $682 million floor by approximately 1.77% [T1, T4]. This older pair supplies a comparison case, not a stock-return study.

Every comparison needs the original guidance publication, any subsequent revision, the result publication, matching metric scope, currency and period. Original-guide delivery and latest-pre-result-guide delivery are different questions. A complete revision census was not performed here, so this chapter says **original guidance**, not “the final expectation immediately before earnings.”

The same discipline applies to consensus. Minimum requirements are estimate provider, publication/observation vintage, contributor basis/count where supplied, fiscal horizon, metric accounting basis, currency and revisions. Company guidance, analyst consensus, the analyst's model and a valuation-implied scenario should appear as different reference points. Missing consensus is a useful explicit state, not a reason to invent a beat/miss verdict.

## 9. AI discovery: four distinct economic transmission paths

### A. A new place to reach commercial intent

Google announced testing new AI-search ad formats on May 20. OpenAI announced Sponsored Agents testing and advertising-workflow integrations on September 16 [A1–A2]. These are specific commercial developments, but no independently verified revenue, budget-share or causal incumbent impact is established here.

Research whether the activity is additive discovery, substitution away from another channel, or a lower-cost completion of an existing task. The model needs user task, commercial intent, eligible ad opportunity, conversion destination, payment event and retained economics. Conversation counts cannot stand in for monetizable inventory.

### B. A new interface to an existing buying system

An agent can alter campaign creation, planning or measurement while transactions still pass through existing infrastructure. In that scenario, value could migrate at the workflow layer without eliminating auctions, identity, inventory access or settlement. The alternative is an agent/platform owning both discovery and execution, reducing the need for a separate intermediary. Research should identify the actual contractual interface rather than assume either outcome.

### C. More effective production and measurement

Cheaper creative and automated optimization may reduce operating effort or increase the range of tested campaigns. Whether the savings accrue to advertisers, platforms, agencies or tool vendors depends on competition and pricing. A productivity improvement is not automatically revenue growth for every supplier.

### D. Content and referral bargaining power

Answer-led discovery can change the value of original content, referral traffic and licensing. The economic assertion should identify the rights holder, license scope, payment basis and substitution risk. Public accessibility is not ingestion permission, and a licensing partnership does not establish the value or duration of the contract when undisclosed.

### Timeline correction is part of intelligence

Google's AI Max transition article originally described a September 2026 transition. Its June 11 update moves the Dynamic Search Ads sunset/auto-upgrade start to February 2027, while distinguishing other September migrations [A3]. A parser that treats every paragraph as equally current can publish the wrong catalyst. Preserve the correction, affected feature and remaining unchanged scope; do not erase history or apply the delay to all features.

## 10. What advertising measurement research does and does not justify

Google's Conversion Lift documentation distinguishes treatment/control incrementality from ordinary attributed conversions and notes account eligibility limitations [Q1]. Discovering the documentation does not prove access to an advertiser account or experiment data.

Published research provides a reason for caution, not a reason to abandon modeling. The 2019 study comparing observational methods with 15 Facebook experiments found substantial disagreement; the later large-scale study tested richer methods across 663 experiments and still found important limitations [Q2–Q3]. Those study settings are not a universal theorem that every future causal model fails.

A 2026 PIE working paper reports strong out-of-sample agreement using 2,226 Meta experiments, but uses experimental training targets and campaign information that includes post-campaign features [Q4]. This is evidence for investigating experiment-calibrated prediction. It is not proof that Mastermind's public-company data can reproduce the result or predict stock returns. Post-campaign predictors cannot be smuggled into an ex-ante decision test.

For Mastermind, maintain three separate validation jobs: explain a reported business change; forecast a later operating outcome; and improve a governed investment decision. Causal advertiser lift can support the first two only after an exposure/financial bridge. It does not independently validate the third. Private experimental datasets are not part of this research's accessible evidence.

## 11. Proposed research hypotheses and discriminating evidence

| Hypothesis | Evidence that would support it | Evidence that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| Better recommendation produces profitable monetization | Comparable retained advertiser outcomes and yield, with manageable unit cost | Gains concentrated in attribution or ad load; adverse cohort retention or cost |
| AI search expands useful commercial activity | Incremental commercial tasks and realized revenue after serving/distribution costs | Usage substitutes for better-monetized tasks or costs outgrow contribution |
| Buying intermediaries retain differentiated value | Comparable-client spend and economic fees improve with measured outcomes | Count retention masks spend shrinkage; discounts or presentation changes lead growth |
| CTV access improves supplier economics | Repeatable inventory monetization with stable net economics and working capital | Scarce-event effects, lower retained yield or growing contractual obligations |
| Creator/content assets gain bargaining power | Documented paid licensing/retention benefits that are financially material | Public attention without durable rights or economics; monetization offset by cost |
| Agents displace workflow fees but retain execution infrastructure | Observable workflow migration and continued settlement/inventory dependencies | Full-stack agent bypass with measurable loss of retained fees |
| A new product deserves earnings weight | Dated adoption, paid usage and a credible revenue/cost bridge | Pilot status, undisclosed terms or forecasts without realized contribution |

These are research hypotheses, not installed propagation rules. No fixed coefficients, probabilities or portfolio weights are admitted. The eventual outcome record must include disconfirming evidence and abstentions, not only successful narratives.

## 12. Research-to-product proposal

The proposed first surface should show an **economic change card** with six adjacent dimensions: operating evidence; monetization; retained profit/cash; dated expectations; valuation assumptions; and existing market/entry context. The first four can be useful before the last two are qualified. A missing dimension must not silently borrow confidence from another.

The detailed journey should move from a Communications theme to a business role, then to the comparable metric bridge and the source. Show the reason a comparison is unavailable or qualified in plain language. Preserve the existing shared template, company routes and watchlist workflow. Avoid building a separate Communications navigation family, graph or score.

A relationship view should answer an explicit question: who owns the audience, who supplies the inventory, who pays, who takes a fee, and what changes if a named condition occurs? A dense graph with many edges is not evidence of insight. Unresolved identity, contractual scope and financial materiality must be visible.

Two alternative first slices remain possible. A pure company-dossier slice is easier but risks isolating facts without a cross-company question. A whole-sector live scoring rollout would require much more qualification and invites false precision. The recommended bounded research prototype is the cross-company profit-capture comparison, with unavailable expectations permitted and no investment authority. It remains a proposal pending the completed written design and its review.

## 14. Coverage and next dependency

This phase has substantially advanced the advertising economic model and resolved specific accounting, timing and expectations uncertainties. It has not established a comprehensive global company census, all customer cohorts, financial sensitivity coefficients, a point-in-time consensus panel, empirical stock rerating rules, or current production behavior.

The next broad economic research dependency is streaming/video, music/content rights and distribution. It should consume the advertising and CTV work here, not repeat it, and deepen subscriber/retention economics, content cash versus amortization, rights contracts and mixed legacy/digital businesses. Remaining advertising research includes broader historical vintages, customer-spend evidence and independent checks of public alternative-data usefulness. These remain explicit tasks before a final Communications masterplan and Fable build handoff.
