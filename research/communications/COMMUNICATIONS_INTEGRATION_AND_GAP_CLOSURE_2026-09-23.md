**Research date:** 2026-09-23. **Operation:** `gmi-communications-research-20260923-sol-001`.

## 1. The capability this phase unlocks

Phases 1–6 explained individual economic families. This phase turns them into a traversable research map: 94 source-indexed business rows across 47 issuer labels, including six new China/Korea issuer sources and two targeted agency/publishing anchors. The map can answer which business is affected by a particular mechanism, which reported financial population it belongs to, what evidence could support better economics, and what could contradict the inference. It cannot yet answer every security identity, exact theme weight, operating sensitivity or priced-in expectation.

The investor job is not to read six unrelated industry essays. It is to identify a change, understand its transmission into the right business, compare evidence across economic roles, and reach an existing company or watchlist workflow with the unknowns visible. The machine job is to preserve those scopes through composition rather than replace them with a universal opportunity score. This is the design direction for the final specification; no production capability is claimed.

## 2. Three exposures that must not be collapsed

**Current economic base** asks which receipts, profit, assets or committed capital belong to an activity. A disclosed segment can answer part of this question. It does not necessarily allocate the microtheme of interest, and a revenue denominator is not interchangeable with profit or free cash flow.

**Incremental sensitivity** asks what changes in retained earnings or cash when a driver changes. This can differ sharply from current revenue share because pricing, incremental cost, cannibalization, working capital, capital investment, financing and partner claims differ. The coefficient must be estimated from qualified evidence or left unknown; a model-generated explanation is not a measured sensitivity.

**Conditional future exposure** asks what an emerging product or deployment might contribute after technical, commercial and funding conditions are satisfied. Treating a potential service as current revenue is wrong. Treating its undisclosed current revenue as zero strategic significance is also wrong. Scenarios must state their conditions, reinvestment needs, horizon and dilution instead of borrowing confidence from a theme label.

For an explicitly hypothetical illustration, one activity with 80 units of present revenue and a 10% incremental contribution rate can produce less incremental contribution from a 10% revenue increase than an activity with 20 units and a 60% incremental rate: 0.8 versus 1.2 units. Those assumed rates are not estimates for any company. The example demonstrates why revenue-share weights cannot rank earnings sensitivity.

The first-order cash bridge is a reasoning aid, not an accepted valuation formula: incremental receipts less incremental operating cash costs, tax, working capital and required investment, with financing and partner claims handled consistently. It must include negative offsets such as cannibalization and avoid deducting development/content cash twice. A useful thesis states where the incremental cash appears and what would prevent it.

## 3. The company matrix is not a second security or theme registry

The matrix uses research row IDs and source-named companies. Every canonical issuer/security ID, live basket membership and numerical theme weight remains unassigned. This is intentional: a source-named business, consolidated issuer, subsidiary and tradable instrument are different subjects. A depositary ratio, foreign listing or investment stake cannot be resolved from name similarity.

Overlap has two separate meanings. Within an issuer, multiple research themes can describe the same receipts. Across issuers, several receipts can arise from one underlying transaction. Neither can be summed without a defined economic boundary. Likewise, a parent and a listed subsidiary do not represent two independent copies of the subsidiary's business exposure. Cash attribution, minority claims, investment holdings and instrument identity must be reconciled before portfolio-level concentration is computed.

This phase does not assert a complete legal-ownership graph. It specifies the questions the existing identity and company-intelligence owners must answer. Geography is similarly multidimensional: consumer, advertiser, operating asset, reporting currency and listing venue can differ. Missing geographic revenue weights remain unknown rather than being filled from domicile.

## 4. Regional evidence changes the interpretation, not just the company count

These are selected reported observations, not a current stock-selection ranking. Regional source IDs resolve to the companion map. Quarterly examples end June 30, 2026; China Telecom is half-year. The cases deliberately stress different analytical failures.

### 4.1 Baidu — two reporting axes cannot be added

The release presents Core AI-powered Business of RMB12.5bn and online marketing of RMB13.1bn using different groupings within General Business of RMB25.2bn. The former is a management-account measure. Its displayed 50% share refers to General Business, not consolidated Baidu. These categories must not be presumed disjoint [R:BD].

**Research consequence:** preserve the economic pathway of AI cloud, applications and advertising separately without treating their thematic labels as an additive partition. A business can be both marketing and AI-enabled. A strong activity does not establish that a declining activity has recovered, and the parent valuation still requires the full issuer bridge.

### 4.2 Bilibili — net growth can conceal opposite theme directions

The category table reports advertising revenue of RMB3,130.759m versus 2,448.888m and mobile games of RMB1,391.902m versus 1,612.333m. The four disclosed categories reconcile to the reported total. Advertising's increase exceeds total net revenue growth because other movements offset it [R:BI].

**Research consequence:** the same issuer can provide improving advertising evidence and weakening gaming evidence at the same time. The product should expose both, not let an issuer-level growth badge propagate to all its themes. Segment revenue changes do not establish segment profit changes when costs are unallocated.

### 4.3 NAVER — platform exposure is not all advertising

NAVER's current presentation reports ads of KRW1,447.2bn within total revenue of KRW3,388.8bn. Other platform services, finance, content, C2C and enterprise have separate reporting places. Its presentation also identifies acquisition-related comparability considerations [R:NV].

**Research consequence:** retain the current comparable category hierarchy. Do not apply a historic segment dictionary or attribute all group growth to search or AI. Revenue-base materiality can be computed from matched categories while the AI-specific earnings effect remains unknown.

### 4.4 Kuaishou — a quantified product is still narrower than its parent category

Kuaishou reports online marketing, live streaming and other services at 58.1%, 24.5% and 17.4% of revenue. Kling AI revenue is disclosed as over RMB850m, not an exact amount, and is not the entire other-services category [R:KS].

**Research consequence:** preserve a disclosed lower-bound statement without converting it into an exact exposure weight. Product growth should be tested against repeated paid use, serving cost and retained contribution. An emerging product and the dominant existing monetization model need different evidence windows.

### 4.5 Kakao — combined categories and transaction value require separate treatment

Talk Biz's advertising-and-subscription measure is KRW399.9bn; commerce revenue is KRW243.2bn. Their rounded sum is 643.1bn versus reported Talk Biz of 643.2bn. Commerce transaction value is a separate activity measure [R:KK].

**Research consequence:** do not label the combined measure pure advertising or add transaction value to revenue. Rounding can explain small reconciliation differences without inventing another business line. Parent-level content economics still require attributable cash rather than indiscriminate segment aggregation.

### 4.6 China Telecom — service adoption and financial improvement can diverge

Principal-business revenue is RMB244,095m, with Communications RMB212,955m and Intelligence RMB31,141m; the one-million difference is compatible with displayed rounding. Cloud is separately reported at RMB61.8bn and is not established as a third additive line. Reported profit fell while company-defined free cash flow rose [R:CT].

**Research consequence:** keep reported and management-comparable interpretations distinct. A growing intelligence/cloud activity does not automatically explain the net cash movement or qualify a sector-wide AI beneficiary claim. Do not infer the overlap between cloud and other categories without a source mapping.

### 4.7 Agency and publishing gaps were advanced in this same phase

Publicis distinguishes H1 net revenue of EUR7,229m from EUR1,505m of pass-through revenue, which together make EUR8,734m of reported revenue. Its marketing and Technology practices have different growth trajectories [R:PG].

**Research consequence:** an agency's productivity opportunity depends on the contract and delivery model. Under a time-based fee, fewer labor hours can reduce revenue; under another arrangement, better outcomes or efficiency may support retained fees. Those are alternatives to investigate, not facts established by a broad AI-powered label. Client media spend is not agency net revenue.

The New York Times Company defines digital-only ARPU over a 28-day billing cycle. It separately reports digital subscriptions, advertising and combined affiliate/licensing/other receipts; subscriber counts are not product subscriptions [R:NY].

**Research consequence:** publishing is not only a declining-print thesis or an AI-license option. Direct relationships, bundles, pricing and content investment can change recurring economics. A precise licensing exposure is still unavailable when the source combines activities. The 28-day definition also prevents transplanting a monthly-ARPU model from streaming or telecom.

These two anchors close an initial research blind spot without claiming an exhaustive agency, publishing or broadcasting census. The larger comparative population remains a scoped extension, not a reason to delay every useful build.

## 6. Cross-family propagation: conditional paths, not magic graph edges

### A. An advertiser's productivity improves

Potential paths include advertiser savings, increased audience-platform yield, buying/selling-intermediary fees, and changed demand for creative or compute services. Each needs a transaction boundary and a counterfactual. A fixed budget shifted between channels differs from an expanding total budget. Platform-reported conversion improvement does not independently prove incrementality or identify which counterparty kept the saving.

The product should ask whether the shock affects volume, price, retention, service cost, capital investment or a contractual fee. It should show the direct source observation and the conditional downstream explanation separately. A future validated coefficient could quantify a pathway; the current research does not.

### B. A subscriber's price increases

The favorable pathway is more retained contribution per surviving household. The unfavorable pathways include churn, downgrades, bundle dilution and higher rights-holder payments. A distributor's higher revenue and a rights owner's higher receipts can be two sides of the same payment. The model must preserve the contract and cohort before translating a price announcement into a profit forecast.

### C. A new network or game becomes available

Availability enables demand but does not establish it. The initial asset may require customer activation, content refresh, developer payouts or replacement capacity. Suppliers can recognize contractual revenue before the downstream service produces attractive economics. Conversely, a retailer can retain value while using a network owned by another company. The row's economic role, not thematic proximity, determines the financial channel to investigate.

### D. Financing conditions change

Long-dated cash, refinancing, customer demand and capital investment can respond differently. Mature retained-cash businesses and conditional deployment businesses should not inherit the same duration assumption. Debt, lease, partner and dilution effects belong in the relevant issuer's bridge. Correlated stock moves alone do not establish the business mechanism or calibrate the forecast.

## 7. A coherent opportunity explanation without unauthorized stock scoring

The full research objective remains timely intelligence about economically meaningful changes and potential equity rerating. The product must therefore show more than provenance. A proposed explanation has five elements: the change; why it matters economically; which business is exposed; the dated expectation and valuation assumptions where qualified; and the observation that would change the read.

Research priority, operational forecast and investment selection are different jobs. A source-rich new development may deserve research attention while failing a profitability or entry test. A strong business can be fully priced. Conversely, a weak current business could have an improving expectation gap. We should retain these differences rather than average them into a single number.

The valuation research should use matched forecast horizons and relevant capital claims. For profitable issuers, earnings revisions and multiple changes can be decomposed after definitions are fixed. For loss-making or deployment-stage businesses, scenarios need explicit future cash, reinvestment and funding assumptions. No current fair-value estimate, probability weight or validated return prediction is produced in this phase.

## 8. First useful release: advertising profit-capture comparison

**Proposed fixed research scope for the first written specification:** Meta, Alphabet, The Trade Desk and Magnite, spanning owned audiences, discovery, buying intermediation and selling intermediation. Bilibili and Kuaishou are candidate regional extensions after identity, source rights and comparability are qualified; they are not required merely to make the first slice look global.

The investor should start in the existing sector/themes workflow, compare what changed across those business roles, inspect the revenue-to-retained-economics explanation, see a correctly labeled company-guidance comparison or unavailable expectation, and open the relevant existing company/watchlist route. This is a descriptive business-intelligence slice. It does not create a new trading screen, theme ranking or automatic order.

Two alternatives were considered. A whole-sector scoring launch would hide large gaps in economic coefficients, identity and expectations. A static library of company essays would preserve research but not complete the comparison workflow. The bounded role-based comparison is the selected proposal because it proves the useful question with the evidence already assembled while leaving room for the full matrix.

A named useful output is an explanation such as: an issuer reports stronger monetization, but higher costs or investment prevent an equivalent cash improvement; or an intermediary retains clients while comparable spending weakens. These are not universal directional labels. Each explanation must be bound to actual source inputs and a coherent period, including contrary evidence.

## 11. Historical qualification with a useful descriptive release boundary

The existing archive has dated company-guidance examples and failures. They are not an original-vintage consensus archive or a stock-return backtest. The selected earnings-cache writer inspected in Phase 2 did not establish that archive; this is not evidence that no such owner exists elsewhere.

Before any forecast-use claim, define the question and horizon, collect the original observation and correction lineage, fix the matched expected measure, and preserve entities that failed or disappeared. Use historical membership known at the decision time, not today's matrix projected backward. Separate a reported-result explanation from prediction of later operations, and both from stock-selection performance.

The first descriptive release can pass without claiming forecast skill: it must correctly explain a real source transition and support the investor's workflow. Forecast and portfolio use remain separately gated. This is sequencing, not abandonment of the moat. The accepted outcome-learning owner should retain the subsequently observed operating and market outcomes so the hypotheses can be evaluated when suitable cohorts accrue.

An unavailable consensus field is acceptable; a fabricated one is not. A source-bound conditional explanation is acceptable; a numerical probability without qualification is not. These choices let the product become useful while the research quality improves, rather than hide behind unavailable institutional data indefinitely.
