## 3. Live entertainment: the same fan supports different economics

The selected Live Nation Q2 report separates Concerts, Ticketing and Sponsorship. Concerts revenue increased from USD5,946.4m to USD6,444.4m, while its adjusted operating income fell from USD358.7m to USD309.6m. Ticketing and Sponsorship adjusted income increased, leaving group adjusted income at USD817.0m versus USD798.4m. Management attributes the Concerts decline to show timing and investment effects. [CE-LN]

### Proposed economic model

Investigate three linked but non-interchangeable businesses. An event producer earns contribution after artist, venue, promotion and delivery costs. A ticketing service retains contractual fees after processing, service, platform and contract-acquisition costs. Sponsorship monetizes commercially useful access to an audience or venue. A vertically integrated issuer may participate in all three, but the same event or fan does not become three independent pieces of evidence that demand strengthened.

The useful research unit is an event or venue cohort aligned to its commercial and performance dates. Tickets sold, completed attendance, recognized fees, and cash settled can describe different periods. A venue opening changes available capacity; paid utilization and contribution must then establish whether it was a productive investment. Announced project-return targets stay management forecasts until realized cash supports them.

The favorable rerating hypothesis is that profitable utilization and ancillary contribution grow faster than the capital and operating costs needed to sustain them. The opposing hypothesis is that apparent demand strength is absorbed by artist terms, service costs, new-project ramp-up or investment. These are testable alternatives, not coefficients inferred from one quarter.

### Cash has an economic owner and an associated obligation

Live Nation's June cash balance is USD9,071.9m; its company-defined free-cash balance is USD1,933.8m after the stated adjustments for investments, client/event balances, artist obligations and prepaids. Its Q2 adjusted FCF is USD515.9m, using a working-capital adjustment, maintenance capex and distributions to noncontrolling interests. [CE-LN]

Our inference is not that the difference is all legally restricted cash, or that the proxy is an independently verified distributable balance. Rather, cash collected before performance should be assessed alongside the obligation to deliver, refund or settle. Faster presales can improve funding while saying little on their own about final event profit. A balance-stock measure and a quarter-flow measure must remain separate.

**Proposed investor output:** “Concert activity increased, but the reported concert contribution weakened; ticketing and sponsorship supported the group result. Follow full-season delivered contribution and the cash remaining after event obligations and investment.” This is descriptive research, not a recommendation or a statement about the current stock's valuation.

## 4. Broadcasting: event economics must survive the comparison period

FOX's selected fiscal Q4, ending June 30, reports Cable Network Programming revenue of USD1,670m versus USD1,532m, while segment EBITDA fell to USD728m from USD747m. Television revenue rose to USD2,482m from USD1,707m and segment EBITDA to USD705m from USD308m. The report identifies World Cup advertising and rights/production effects, with different results across the segments. [CE-FX]

### Proposed economic model

Separate distribution price and retained paying relationships from advertising activity, content licensing and rights costs. A price increase may compensate for fewer subscribers without restoring the audience base. Advertising can strengthen because a scarce event occurs in the comparison period; the cost of acquiring and producing that event must be included before interpreting the retained economics.

The analysis should use a matched event/right-season horizon where feasible, while still retaining the reported quarter. A two-year comparison can be useful for a recurring calendar effect, but it is not automatically “normalized”: rights packages, schedules, geography, audience, presentation and distribution can change. The correct response to that uncertainty is a scoped interpretation, not mechanical annualization or an arbitrary smoothing factor.

Tubi and FOX One remain narrower service questions inside broader reported businesses. A reported segment total is not a service's revenue weight, unit margin or incremental customer contribution. The model should examine whether new-format demand adds profitable activity or shifts an existing paid or advertising relationship, reusing the substitution framework already developed for search and streaming.

**Proposed investor output:** “A stronger event-related advertising period did not produce the same profit outcome in every segment. Separate the repeatable distribution/digital trajectory from the rights-season and production-cost effect.” Management attribution is useful context, not an independent estimate of the event's exact contribution.

## 5. ITV: internal activity is not independent customer demand

ITV Studios H1 total revenue rose from GBP893m to GBP912m, but external revenue fell from GBP632m to GBP628m. Internal revenue rose by GBP23m, more than the GBP19m increase in total revenue. The source's internal definition includes intra-Studios trading as well as trading with Media & Entertainment; it is not safe to relabel every internal pound as a sale to the broadcaster. [CE-IT]

The group figures reinforce the need for the reporting boundary. Studios adjusted EBITA fell GBP10m, M&E increased GBP13m, and the unrealized-profit-in-stock adjustment fell GBP3m: group adjusted EBITA remained GBP146m. The reported digital-revenue measure is within M&E, not another additive segment. [CE-IT]

### Proposed economic model

Separate new production, repeat format licensing, finished-program distribution and use of an existing library. The economics of commissioning new content differ from reusing already-created rights. Internal production can support a valuable audience product, but it does not establish equivalent demand from an outside buyer.

At issuer level, use external revenue after eliminations. At business level, retain the activity measure and internal/external split that actually describes the operation. A simplistic growth score could misread more internal production as stronger independent industry demand; an equally simplistic rule excluding all internal activity would miss the product it supports. The right model keeps both views and the reconciliation between them.

A proposed disposal or restructuring also changes the appropriate forward comparison only when its conditions and economic perimeter are understood. Announcement, closing, transition services, financing, residual costs and distributions are different stages. This phase does not certify later transaction completion or recompute the current investable business perimeter from an announcement.

### A useful scoped macro input

ITV discloses a conditional Studios FX sensitivity for the remainder of 2026 using internal forecasts: a 10% sterling move against the dollar affects revenue by GBP30–40m and adjusted EBITA by GBP5–7m, all other variables held constant. [CE-IT]

This is a concrete management scenario with a defined horizon, shock and operating measure. It is **not** a measured equity beta, a permanent coefficient or a source of calibrated probability. It can anchor a research question without implying that consumer demand, hedging, input costs and the valuation multiple remain constant in a real currency move. The companion preserves the euro ranges and the stated direction rather than compressing the table into a generic FX-risk number.

## 6. Deutsche Telekom: listing geography and reporting labels are not exposure

The official H1 report states that the United States segment contributes 65.7% of group net revenue. It also identifies a January 2026 service-revenue definition change, with prior comparatives restated. Adjusted EBITDA after leases increased, while the unadjusted measure fell. [CE-DG]

For research, the issuer's home market cannot substitute for the location of its earnings, assets or currency exposure. Likewise, counting both consolidated Deutsche Telekom activity and the same underlying US subsidiary activity as separate sector end-demand would double-count. Portfolio attribution additionally needs the actual security, minority claims and cash entitlement; those are not supplied by a revenue percentage.

**After leases** also needs its precise formula. The report's EBITDA AL reconciliation deducts right-of-use depreciation/impairment and interest and specifies its lease scope. Those accounting adjustments are not interchangeable with cash lease-principal payments. A label shared by two issuers is not evidence that their measures are comparable. [CE-DG]

### Fiber denominator and investment timing

Germany's reported fiber-based customer measures use FTTx and include retail/wholesale distinctions, whereas the 13.6m homes-passed statement concerns FTTH. The report also attributes lower H1 cash capex before spectrum partly to build timing while expecting a slightly higher full-year amount. [CE-DE]

Do not divide the 21.0m fiber-based line total by FTTH homes passed and call the result penetration. Technology, service/customer population and coverage differ. Nor does lower half-year capex establish structurally lower investment intensity when management itself describes timing.

The resulting research model combines the existing construction-cohort framework with technology, retail/wholesale population, geography and investment timing. Its leading question is how quickly a **matched** serviceable cohort converts to retained contribution, not which issuer displays the largest fiber label.

## 8. Cross-family synthesis that preserves investor usefulness

The new anchors support four recurring questions without creating a universal sector score.

**Demand versus retained economics.** Audience, attendance, traffic and capacity are potential inputs, not shareholder outcomes. Identify the payment, contribution and required investment that convert them into value.

**Economic clocks.** Presale, performance, rights season, content delivery, subscriber renewal, construction completion and cash settlement need not align with a quarterly report. Keep the legal reporting period and the operating cohort both visible; do not overwrite one with the other.

**Nested populations.** A digital service inside a broadcasting business, a studio's internal production, and a parent with a large listed subsidiary all require a defined aggregation boundary. Evidence can be relevant in multiple research views without creating multiple copies of revenue, customers or cash.

**Regime mechanisms.** Treat advertising budget demand, household affordability, FX translation, refinancing and the required return on investment as separate pathways. A yield change can affect a long-dated valuation before fixed-rate debt refinances; a currency move can change translated results without the same movement in local demand. These are hypotheses requiring matching data, not estimated betas in this study.

The intended explanation remains: **what changed -> which business is exposed -> retained economics -> dated expectations where qualified -> contrary evidence -> next observation -> existing company/watchlist journey**. Provenance supports that explanation rather than becoming the whole product. A compelling narrative with unknown coefficients remains a research hypothesis, not a forecast or trade signal.
