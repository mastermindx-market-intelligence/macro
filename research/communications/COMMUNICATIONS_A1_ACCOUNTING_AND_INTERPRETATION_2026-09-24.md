## 4. One outcome can have several explanations without several confirmations

AB06 and AB08 are two decompositions of the **same** 27,639-thousand increase. Present them as alternative views. The first separates reported revenue and deducted TAC; the second separates channels. Showing both is useful. Adding them to report 55,278 of economic improvement is wrong.

AB06 also contains a dependent input: TAC is derived from revenue and contribution. Reconciliation therefore cannot independently validate the contribution total used to construct it. A mathematically exact identity can coexist with a conflicting source table.

**Synthetic correction experiment:** change only the current contribution total from 189,595 to 190,595; this is a deliberately invented perturbation, not a company correction. Revenue minus newly derived TAC still equals the new total by construction. The unchanged channel table continues to sum to 189,595, leaving a 1,000 residual. The product must preserve the revised total, unchanged channel observations and unresolved discrepancy; it cannot invent which channel gained 1,000 or cite the algebraic bridge as independent corroboration.

This is a concrete exercise of CRV-10/21/24/51/55/56, not a new evidence, correction or confidence owner. K1 and the existing source/recipe owners retain their independence and correction restrictions. The implementation must use accepted native identity/lineage rather than treating the research labels as those receipts.
