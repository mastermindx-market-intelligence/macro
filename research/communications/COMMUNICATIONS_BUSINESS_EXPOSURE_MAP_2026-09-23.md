## 1. How to use this map

Start with a business mechanism, not a stock label. Each row distinguishes the activity that receives or spends money, the scope of available financial evidence, a proposed path to better economics, and evidence that could contradict it. The last two columns are analyst research questions, not observed forecasts or causal coefficients. A business can have several rows because it has several economic roles; row count is not breadth of independent confirmation.

**All rows have the following explicit nulls:** canonical issuer ID, security ID, live basket membership, structural sector assignment, economic-share weight, earnings-sensitivity coefficient and investment score are unassigned. Company labels are navigation, not identity resolution. A filing's ticker or depositary-receipt description does not complete the existing identity owner's join. Geography remains the cited business's reporting scope; this map does not infer demand location from a listing venue or company domicile.

**Evidence scope codes:** B = the source reports a business/category measure, not a certified microtheme share; S = a narrower activity inside a broader disclosed line, with its standalone materiality unqualified here; O = a cross-cutting activity that must not be added to overlapping rows; C = conditional development, transaction or service evidence, not realized economics. None is a confidence grade or security ranking.

A row marked B can still have an unknown profit contribution. A row marked C can be strategically material despite having no qualified current revenue. Rows are in research-family order, then source order, never ordered by attractiveness. Revenue partitions may be added only where the source proves that they are disjoint and reconciles the denominator; overlapping roles must remain non-additive.

## 8. Financial materiality and overlap rules

This map deliberately does not assign one percentage to every row. Three different quantities matter: a reported revenue or profit base; the incremental change caused by a particular driver; and a conditional future payoff after funding. They require different evidence. Large present revenue is not necessarily high incremental earnings sensitivity, and a presently small business can have substantial but uncertain future significance.

The map also contains narrower activities within broader totals. X017 shares Netflix audience/content economics with X016; X034 can share titles with X033; X050 and X052 share households with broadband; X073 crosses Baidu reporting axes; X076 is narrower than Kuaishou's other-services category; X089 cannot simply be added to X087 and X088. These relationships do not determine exact overlap percentages. Unknown intersections remain unknown.

No calculation may sum across companies without defining the transaction boundary, consolidation treatment and economic quantity. No sector-level confidence or breadth measure may count the same issuer's business rows as independent securities. Parent ownership and listed subsidiaries require attributable-earnings/cash and security-claim reconciliation before a portfolio exposure estimate. Any dual-listing or depositary-receipt arithmetic remains with the existing identity owner.
