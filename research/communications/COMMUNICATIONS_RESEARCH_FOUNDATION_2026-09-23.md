# Communications Sector Intelligence — Research Foundation, Phase 1

**Research date:** 2026-09-23. **Operation:** `gmi-communications-research-20260923-sol-001`.

**Status:** Original research and proposed design inputs, not a finished master plan, accepted implementation contract, production dataset, current stock recommendation or validated predictive model. Research remains owned by Sol under the Chairman's live commission; Fable CEO is the intended final implementation integrator, not an assigned or STARTed receiver.

## 1. Outcome and canonical integration boundary

The investor job is to discover which Communications businesses have a financially meaningful exposure to an emerging change, understand who captures the resulting economics, compare that change with dated expectations, and move into Mastermind's existing company, watchlist and governed opportunity workflow. A page full of theme labels or a beautiful relationship diagram is not the outcome.

The machine job is to connect a source-bound operating observation to the correct business, financial mechanism, period, uncertainty and potential earnings consequence, while keeping current facts, managerial explanations, research hypotheses, expectations and market confirmation distinct. The moat we are pursuing is a maintained, historically testable understanding of profit migration and expectation changes—not a scraped collection of company descriptions.

The shared sector architecture is a dependency, not a deliverable to rebuild. It already selects owner-preserving federation, separate entity types, page-specific projections and typed disagreements [C1]. Communications contributes economic content and requirements to that architecture. GMI remains the semantic/evidence owner, ThemeState the thematic-state owner, existing relationship owners the propagation-hypothesis owners, MarketOntology F04 the downstream composition owner, and the existing evaluation/selection owners retain their authority [C1–C2].

The Robotics precedent establishes a useful evidence-to-dossier execution pattern, not an obligation to force Communications into a component BOM [C3]. No source code, live taxonomy, membership, template, ranking, trade policy or publisher is changed by this research.

## 2. Research conclusions versus open hypotheses

The following are proposed modeling decisions derived from the initial source checks, not claims of historical alpha:

- Separate **demand, monetization, profit capture, cash conversion, expectations and price confirmation**. One cannot stand in for another.
- Model the **business activity before the issuer or security**. An issuer may combine search, video, cloud or other activities whose cash economics differ.
- Keep **structural industry classification, thematic economic exposure and basket membership** separate. A cross-sector supplier or competitor can be relevant without becoming a Communications constituent.
- Preserve **definition history and comparable reporting scope**. A reclassification, acquisition, fiscal-period change or cash-flow-definition change can create an apparent inflection that is not an organic business change.
- Treat statements about future adoption, launches, savings or margins as forecasts, not achieved economics. Research salience does not confer rank, entry, sizing or trading authority.

These decisions are supported by contrasting reported cases below. They do not establish which stocks are currently attractive. Consensus vintages, prices, causal identification and current basket membership have not yet been qualified.

## 3. Candidate economic ontology: eight families, forty research slices

The following forty slices are proposed research coverage. They are NOT forty newly admitted canonical themes, source-local concepts, baskets or live graph nodes. Each must be mapped to the existing ontology, retained as a narrower business assertion, or left unresolved. Similar labels do not establish equivalence [C1].

### A. Intent, discovery and commercial search

**Candidate slices:** A1 conventional search monetization; A2 AI-assisted/answer-led search; A3 commercial-intent and agent-mediated discovery; A4 search distribution and traffic acquisition; A5 publisher referral and licensed-content economics.

**Model to investigate:** monetizable opportunities × effective yield, less distribution costs, inference/serving expense, research expense and relevant capital consumption. Distinguish informational queries from commercial intent, incremental queries from replacement sessions, and owned surfaces from third-party network distribution.

**Rerating hypothesis:** more useful discovery creates valuable commercial activity faster than unit cost grows, and the resulting durable per-share cash earnings exceed dated expectations. The opposing hypothesis is that usage grows while external referrals, ad opportunities or net yield weaken.

**Evidence required:** segment revenue, eligible volume measures, traffic-acquisition-cost basis, monetization evidence, serving-cost disclosure where available, user substitution evidence and the scope of any AI attribution. Search query counts alone cannot resolve the hypothesis. Alphabet's disaggregated reporting is an initial anchor, not a finished inference model [S02].

### B. Social attention, recommendation and creator distribution

**Candidate slices:** B1 feed/recommendation monetization; B2 short-form video; B3 creator distribution and revenue sharing; B4 messaging and business interaction; B5 social communities and direct audience monetization.

**Model to investigate:** active audience × engagement × monetizable share × sold ad density × realized yield, reconciled to reported advertising revenue. Keep each factor's population and frequency consistent; do not multiply platform-wide audience by a yield measured only in one region or format.

**Rerating hypothesis:** recommendation quality improves advertiser outcomes and retention, enabling profitable monetization without excessive ad load, acquisition spending or compute investment. Counter-hypotheses include format cannibalization, weaker geography mix, rising creator payments, or engagement gains that do not monetize.

**Evidence required:** observed impressions/pricing, advertiser quality measures, cohort behavior, content and infrastructure cost, reported versus adjusted profit and cash. Meta demonstrates why monetization and margin dimensions must remain separate [S01].

### C. Advertising intermediation, measurement and connected television

**Candidate slices:** C1 demand-side buying/decisioning; C2 supply-side monetization; C3 connected-TV access and aggregation; C4 identity, verification and measurement; C5 commerce-data and agency-workflow adjacency.

**Model to investigate:** eligible media spend × economically retained fee/take rate, with explicit principal-versus-agent accounting and pass-through exclusions. Ad spend, publisher revenue, intermediary revenue and data fees may describe different stages of the same payment; adding them is not a valid market-size calculation.

**Rerating hypothesis:** an intermediary earns a larger or more durable share of advertiser value through performance, access or workflow integration. Counter-hypotheses are disintermediation, fee compression, customer-budget reduction and gains captured by another participant.

**Evidence required:** customer and spend cohorts, gross-versus-net reporting, usage/fee mix, supply-path changes, customer concentration, retention definition and incremental margin. A partnership announcement proves an announced relationship, not a material revenue contribution. The Trade Desk provides an initial public company anchor [S10]. Current formal sector classification must still come from its owner.

### D. Streaming video, aggregation and legacy-media transition

**Candidate slices:** D1 subscription pricing and retention; D2 advertising-supported tiers; D3 free ad-supported television; D4 bundling/aggregation; D5 linear-to-streaming migration and mixed-company transition.

**Model to investigate:** subscription receipts plus advertising and licensing, less content cash commitments, amortization, marketing, distribution and operating expense. Distinguish subscriber stock from average paid subscriber-months, membership from households, and gross additions from retained cohorts.

**Rerating hypothesis:** content and distribution investment produce sufficiently durable lifetime contribution to improve normalized per-share cash generation. Counter-hypotheses include increased churn after hits, promotional dilution, sports-rights inflation, weak ad-tier yield and losses in a legacy business offsetting streaming improvement.

**Evidence required:** price realization, retained cohorts, advertising contribution, content cash versus amortization, contractual obligations, bundling terms and segment mix. Netflix's reported period and outlook are useful separate observations; neither is a substitute for a point-in-time consensus comparison [S05].

### E. Intellectual property, music, sports rights and live experiences

**Candidate slices:** E1 recorded-music rights and distribution; E2 publishing/synchronization; E3 music/audio subscription distribution; E4 film, television and sports-rights portfolios; E5 live-event, ticketing and merchandise monetization.

**Model to investigate:** monetized licensed consumption and negotiated economics, plus permitted ancillary revenue, less artist/rights-holder shares, advances, acquisition costs and operating expenditure. Separate intellectual-property ownership, distribution mandates, time-limited licenses and commercial partnerships.

**Rerating hypothesis:** ownership/control, pricing, catalog durability or direct-fan access increases the retained profit pool. Counter-hypotheses include lower-yield formats, rising rights costs, mix toward lower-margin services, advance-payment needs or aggressive capitalized acquisition assumptions.

**Evidence required:** rights scope, contractual period and geography, royalty/revenue accounting, catalog versus new releases, payout obligations, pricing realization and definition-consistent cash generation. Spotify and UMG illustrate why a distributor and a rights company require separate models [S06–S08]. Their different growth rates are not, by themselves, evidence of a bilateral value transfer.

### F. Gaming, virtual economies and interactive content

**Candidate slices:** F1 premium game/franchise releases; F2 live-service monetization; F3 mobile-game acquisition and retention; F4 user-created virtual economies; F5 platform distribution and developer economics.

**Model to investigate:** payer cohorts × spending per payer, reconciled through bookings, deferred revenue, recognized revenue, platform fees, developer payments, content development and safety/infrastructure expense. Different businesses require different accounting bridges; do not impose one publisher's bookings definition on all peers.

**Rerating hypothesis:** durable player retention and monetization improve the economics of the installed franchise or platform beyond launch excitement. Counter-hypotheses include temporary hits, adverse payer mix, acquisition-cost inflation, release delays, escalating creator payments and changes in accounting estimates.

**Evidence required:** cohort retention, payer conversion, bookings, revenue-recognition method, deferred balances, title concentration, release schedules and segment cash consumption. Roblox's reconciliation demonstrates that recognized revenue and current sales activity can grow at very different rates [S09].

### G. Terrestrial access, broadband and network convergence

**Candidate slices:** G1 postpaid wireless economics; G2 prepaid/value segments; G3 fiber build and penetration cohorts; G4 cable/broadband competition; G5 fixed-wireless and converged bundles.

**Model to investigate:** average service relationships × realized net service revenue, less acquisition/retention incentives, network operations, spectrum/leases, maintenance and expansion capital, financing and tax. Equipment turnover must not be confused with recurring service economics.

**Rerating hypothesis:** retained customer value, utilization, pricing discipline or lower capital intensity improves sustainable free cash flow and balance-sheet capacity. Counter-hypotheses include subsidized additions, promotional churn, overbuilding, poor cohort penetration and lower capex caused by deferred necessary investment.

**Evidence required:** services versus equipment, churn, acquisition economics, household/line definitions, passed/passed-and-marketable/connected location distinctions, cohort build economics and debt/lease obligations. Verizon supplies a current service-versus-total-revenue example [S04]. Public network availability is not synonymous with subscribing customers [S12].

### H. Satellite access and infrastructure optionality

**Candidate slices:** H1 direct-to-device service; H2 satellite broadband; H3 enterprise/mobility connectivity; H4 ground systems and terminals; H5 launch, spectrum-access and deployment dependencies.

**Model to investigate:** usable authorized capacity × service availability × utilization × economically retained service yield, less deployment, replacement, ground infrastructure, distribution, financing and operating costs.

**Rerating hypothesis:** measurable technical and commercial de-risking converts uncertain optionality into financeable cash generation. A signed memorandum, launched satellite, successful test and paying commercial service are separate milestones.

**Evidence required:** company-specific technical service scope, coverage/capacity, signed versus deployed versus revenue-producing agreements, licenses, partner economics and funding runway. This family is deliberately included where physical bottlenecks and BOM research may be essential. **Company-level satellite research has not yet been performed in this phase; no current capability or valuation claim is made.**

## 4. Cross-cutting themes must not become duplicate sector hierarchies

AI, privacy/measurement change, content-format substitution, price increases, bundling, international expansion, consolidation, financing conditions and distribution bargaining power should be investigated as transformation mechanisms that can affect multiple families. The same mechanism can benefit one role and harm another.

Proposed AI decomposition: demand creation; discovery substitution; advertiser productivity; serving cost; content-creation productivity; rights/licensing; fraud and authenticity; creator supply; capital intensity; and competitive concentration. Each path needs a specific affected business, economic variable, timing window and countervailing effect. Neither an AI mention nor company-wide capital expenditure identifies an AI-only profit exposure.

Research should attach **conditional economic pathways**, not promote overlap into causal graph truth. A hypothetical increase in streaming ad inventory may help a service, dilute yield on its own inventory, help an intermediary, or move bargaining power to a distributor. Which path is material requires evidence on contracts, demand and retained economics.

## 5. Current primary-source stress tests

All cases below concern the quarter ended **2026-06-30**, except explicitly named half-year observations. Publication dates differ. They are demonstrations of required analytical distinctions, not a stock-selection backtest.

| Company/source | Selected reported observation | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Meta [S01], July 29 | Ad impressions +14%; price per ad +12%; revenue +28%; operating income -8%; reported FCF $784m. | Preserve monetization, operating cost and cash conversion separately. |
| Alphabet [S02], July 22 | Search revenue +17%; YouTube ads +13%; operating income +30%; net income to common +298%, alongside about $98bn of other income. | Segment operating economics and investment-related gains must not be conflated. |
| Verizon [S04], July 24 | Total revenue -0.7%; mobility/broadband service revenue +2.8%; adjusted EBITDA +7.2%. | Equipment and recurring service mixes can give opposing readings. |
| Netflix [S05], July 16 | Revenue $12.560bn; operating margin 33.4%, versus 34.1% a year earlier; Q3 revenue forecast $12.860bn. | Keep reported results, accounting timing and future guidance distinct. |
| Spotify [S06–S07], August 4 | Premium subscribers 300m; premium revenue +15%; ad-supported revenue +1%; segment histories reclassified. | Business mix and vintage-safe comparisons are required. |
| UMG [S08], July 30 | Recorded-music subscription growth 16.6% at constant currency, versus 6.7% excluding Downtown. | Acquisition perimeter can dominate apparent theme acceleration. |
| Roblox [S09], July 30 release context | Revenue $1,469m, +36%; bookings $1,557m, +8%. | Recognized revenue is not a same-period demand proxy. |
| The Trade Desk [S10], August 6 | Revenue +3%; customer retention above 95%; adjusted EBITDA $241m versus $271m. | Customer retention is not spend retention or operating leverage. |

### Five immediate analytical safeguards

**Reported versus normalized profit.** An analyst adjustment requires an explicit bridge, tax treatment, recurring/non-recurring rationale and reproducible denominator. Do not simply delete all unfavorable charges or treat all investment gains as cash from customers. Current facts can support a normalized scenario without making that scenario a reported fact.

**Acquisition and reclassification continuity.** Store the original published comparison and any restated comparable series separately. Spotify's deck says certain revenue activities moved from Ad-Supported to Premium effective January 1, 2026, with earlier periods reclassified; 2022 comparatives were not recast [S07]. UMG separately discloses acquisition-adjusted growth [S08]. A naive stitched series would not be trustworthy.

**Cash-definition continuity.** UMG revised its FCF definition in Q2 and restated prior periods [S08]. Company-defined FCF must retain its formula and vintage; the application must not assume peers use identical definitions or silently overwrite the historical series used in a past evaluation.

**Demand versus revenue recognition.** Roblox's reconciliation is $1,469m revenue + $99m deferred-revenue change - $11m other = $1,557m bookings. Its prior-year comparison is $1,081m + $365m - $8m = $1,438m [S09]. This is a concrete test fixture for truthful mixed-clock interpretation, not proof bookings always predict returns.

**Historical expectation versus subsequent knowledge.** A company beating its own earlier guidance is different from beating consensus, and neither proves the surprise was unpriced. None of the current cases has a verified pre-release consensus/price-event dataset in this phase. No causal stock-return conclusion is permitted from them.

## 6. Rerating model: separate operating revisions from valuation changes

For a profitable issuer with a meaningful positive earnings denominator, define price at a given horizon as normalized forward EPS multiplied by the corresponding P/E. Then the logarithmic price change decomposes into the change in that EPS estimate and the change in the associated multiple. This is an identity once definitions and horizons are fixed, not an explanation of causality or a forecast.

The research system must first reconcile comparable forecast horizons. Rolling from one fiscal year to another can create an apparent earnings increase without any revision to the same underlying period. Separately bridge segment revenue, incremental margin, overhead, tax, financing, non-operating items and diluted shares. A repurchase announcement is not an executed reduction in the diluted denominator.

For leveraged or loss-making companies, test an enterprise-value or scenario-based cash-flow approach instead. Keep net debt, leases, minority interests, preferred claims, investments and dilution explicit. Do not rank a satellite option, a mature carrier and a profitable search platform using a universal P/E or EV/sales percentile.

For each researched business we propose a **rerating mechanism card** containing: the economic change; measurable leading variable; affected financial line; expected lag; dated external expectation; alternative outcomes; valuation convention; balance-sheet constraint; observable contradiction; and validation status. Sensitivities must be ranges or unavailable until evidence supports coefficients.

No weights are approved in this phase. In particular, operational quality, thesis health, earnings revisions, valuation, crowding and technical entry should remain separate owner-bound dimensions. A later research-priority ordering, if useful, is not a stock ranking and cannot self-promote into Prophet selection or portfolio sizing.

## 7. Company-exposure representation and evidence grammar

A proposed company research record should answer: which legal entity and business activity; which security identities are linked by the existing owner; what economic role; what relevant geography; what revenue/profit/cash channel; how material; since when; known when; and on what evidence.

An exposure assertion needs an exact business/segment scope, mechanism, source and locator, period/valid interval, publication and observation times, reporting currency/units, evidence class, magnitude basis, caveats, and supersession relationship. These are requirements to map into existing evidence contracts—not a proposed second evidence store or automatic schema migration.

Magnitude states should distinguish **reported exact share**, **reported range**, **explicitly modeled range**, **qualitative but documented**, and **unknown**. Never encode undisclosed exposure as 0%, invent a 0–100 confidence number, or mistake a segment's total revenue for revenue attributable to one subtheme. Company-wide cloud or infrastructure spending cannot be assigned entirely to Communications or AI without disclosed allocation or a labeled model.

Evidence classes should distinguish reported financial facts, reported operating KPIs, signed agreements, announced partnerships, technical demonstrations, management forecasts, third-party estimates, and analyst hypotheses. Source publicity is not the same as ingestion/display rights. A source statement that a product can support an activity is not a statement that a customer adopted it or that material revenue exists.

For derived graph explanations, preserve each intermediate assertion and the operator that combined them. One filing repeated by ten websites remains one evidence family. Peer earnings are informative context, not an independent measurement of another issuer's result. Unknown identity or financial materiality remains visible rather than silently resolved by a model.

## 8. Leading-indicator research: useful measurements with known failure modes

This is a design backlog, not a declaration that every dataset is already licensed, ingested or statistically validated.

| Measurement family | Question to investigate | Essential guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Filings and issuer KPI releases | Are demand, pricing, margin and cash economics changing? | Publication time, original/revised definition, fiscal period and comparability. |
| Public price/plan changes | Did effective monetization improve? | Country, tax, promotion, bundle, subscriber vintage and realized uptake. |
| Search interest | Is discovery or attention shifting? | Relative interest is not absolute query volume or conversion. |
| Audience/content charts | Is a release or format gaining traction? | Panel/platform coverage, denominator, country and title availability. |
| App activity/rankings | Are adoption and retention changing? | Rank is not installs, payers, revenue or unique people. |
| Game activity and release calendars | Are player cohorts and monetization likely to change? | Platform coverage, title concentration, seasonality and payer conversion. |
| Advertising disclosures and surveys | Are budgets, realized yields or performance changing? | Sample composition, auction versus negotiated price, gross/net basis. |
| Network deployment/availability | Is addressable capacity expanding? | Availability is not penetration, subscriptions, utilization or profit. |
| Rights/contract announcements | Did economic control change? | Signed scope, territory, duration, conditions and undisclosed financial terms. |
| Revisions, price and positioning | Has market expectation or participation changed? | Point-in-time snapshots, instrument identity, horizon and owner authority. |

Google's documentation distinguishes the website's request-relative 0–100 scaling from the Trends API's consistently scaled interest series; neither is an absolute search count [S11]. The 2025 alpha announcement is a methodology reference, not proof Mastermind currently has API access. FCC documentation treats availability and subscription data separately [S12]. Both are examples of why sources need measurement contracts before they become signals.

For every proposed leading indicator, investigate coverage, release delay, revision frequency, historical availability, licensing, retrieval cost and whether it adds information beyond already-reported metrics. Backfilled recent data cannot be assumed to have been knowable at the historical decision time.

## 9. Lifecycle, geography and macro research design

A universal lifecycle label would conceal different economic processes. Proposed family-specific transitions include search product adoption to monetization; social engagement to advertiser value; streaming scale to retained contribution; rights ownership to monetized exploitation; game launch to durable payer cohorts; fiber construction to cohort penetration; and satellite demonstration to authorized revenue service. Each transition needs measurable entry and reversal conditions, not simply a theme's price momentum.

Geography must be attached to audience, payer, advertiser, operating assets, currency, issuer and listing separately. A US-listed security is not necessarily US-only exposure; a Hong Kong listing is not a complete description of operating demand. The next research passes must directly examine US, China/Hong Kong, Canada and major European/Japanese/Korean ecosystems. This phase has **not** completed those regional dossiers, current security crosswalks or regulatory research.

The macro research hypotheses are family-specific: advertising budget sensitivity to activity; household affordability and churn; financing sensitivity of capital-intensive access; duration of long-dated cash flows; and FX translation versus local economic performance. Do not pre-label an entire sector defensive or growth-sensitive, and do not treat a multiple/yield correlation as causal proof. Estimate regime-conditioned relationships only after exposure and historical data qualification.

## 10. Historical validation and falsification design

Before calling any indicator predictive, construct a point-in-time panel including weak and failed businesses, delisted/acquired companies where relevant, original filings and restatements, dated forecast vintages, corporate actions and total-return series. Preserve the difference between release date, period end, market session and model observation time.

Evaluate three separate jobs: (1) explaining reported operating change; (2) anticipating later operating/earnings change; (3) improving a governed investment decision. Success at one does not establish another. Measure added information over simple baselines, not merely correlation with a successful historical narrative.

Proposed study design: rolling or walk-forward out-of-sample evaluation; fixed forecast horizons; country/currency and industry controls; market/sector/factor benchmarks; event windows appropriate to information release; clustered events and multiple-testing controls; and explicit treatment of missingness, sample changes and reclassification. Investigate costs/turnover only if a later authorized trading-use validation actually requires them.

Primary failure checks: a leading signal vanishes after accounting for publication lag; a theme is selected using today's survivors; peer metrics reuse the same source; apparent growth comes from a changed denominator; forecast revisions merely roll fiscal periods; or earnings growth is already more than discounted by the price. Calibration must learn from missed opportunities, false positives and abstentions, not just highlighted winners.

No historical return panel or causal backtest was run in this phase. Historical case selection and data availability are subsequent research tasks.

## 11. Product implications and candidate first vertical

The proposed investor journey is: existing Theme Tracker → Communications/subtheme → what changed → which businesses capture value → operating/earnings/cash bridge → dated expectations and what would change the read → evidence → existing company/watchlist/selection path. It is a depth extension of the shared template, not another front page.

The most valuable first views to explore are an economic-driver matrix, a reported-to-comparable metric bridge, a beneficiary/counterparty explanation and a catalyst/expectation timeline. Graphs should reveal paid economic relationships and uncertainty, not decorate generic company associations. A broad sector advance with weak participation must remain distinguishable from a strong niche inside a weak parent.

**Proposed first research vertical:** advertising monetization and profit capture across owned search/social surfaces and buying intermediation. Meta, Alphabet and The Trade Desk provide source-rich contrasts that test volume/pricing, segment boundaries, cost, cash and retained economics. Netflix can later extend this into connected-TV monetization. This is a research sequencing proposal, not an accepted implementation slice, current investment recommendation or exhaustive company list.

Alternatives remain live: streaming/content economics has strong accounting and retention questions; telecom convergence offers tangible build/cohort analysis but needs more local deployment and acquisition-comparability work. The first product slice will be selected after the advertising dossiers, data rights, current template contract and practical evidence path are sufficiently resolved.

Fable should ultimately receive an approved written spec, data/rights and identity mappings, bounded implementation tasks, realistic fixtures and acceptance proof requirements. Fable must not have to rediscover the economic models. Real acceptance must demonstrate production input reaching the investor journey, clear missing/conflicting states, and required dark/light, EN/ZH and desktop/mobile browser evidence under the existing design system [C1]. No Fable delivery, ACK or START is claimed here.
