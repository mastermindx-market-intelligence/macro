## 1. Twenty-eight proposed measure definitions

Every measure requires source and locator, issuer/business/title scope, period, publication/knowledge time, observation time, revision, units, basis, population and coverage. Company-defined terms retain their definitions. Missing input is unavailable, not zero. A current snapshot is not proof of a historical vintage.

| ID | Proposed measure | Definition work required | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| GM01 | Active population | Account/person basis; daily, monthly or annual window; averaging and exclusions | Accounts or annual activity treated as distinct monthly customers |
| GM02 | Engagement | Hours, sessions or activity with matching population and period | More time automatically means higher retained profit |
| GM03 | Unique payers | Period-specific unique payer count and averaging | Monthly counts summed as quarterly distinct people |
| GM04 | Payer conversion | Eligible payers divided by matching eligible activity | Population denominators from unrelated products or timeframes |
| GM05 | Spend per payer | Purchases/bookings divided by matched payer-periods | Quarterly spending divided by one month's ending payers |
| GM06 | Bookings | Issuer-defined purchase/sell-in measure and reconciliation | Bookings equals recognized revenue or collected cash |
| GM07 | Recognized revenue | Relevant accounting period, performance obligations and reporting scope | Revenue acceleration proves current demand acceleration |
| GM08 | Recognition horizon | Durable/consumable allocation and estimate vintage | Accounting lifetime equals contractual customer retention |
| GM09 | Processing costs | Cash, capitalization and amortization timing; channel scope | Deferred expense divided by current purchases as contemporaneous unit cost |
| GM10 | Creator economics | Earned expense, payable, cash payment and incentive eligibility | Announced percentage applies to every creator or transaction |
| GM11 | Infrastructure burden | Hosting, safety, depreciation and excluded costs separately | Blended expense treated as a pure variable serving cost |
| GM12 | Cash conversion | CFO definition, working capital and explicit capital deductions | Payable timing or prepayments mistaken for repeatable profit |
| GM13 | Development investment | Capitalization, expensed development, amortization and cash | All research expense added to the development-asset balance |
| GM14 | Pipeline failure | Cancellation, impairment and project scope | Known carrying-value loss equals complete lifetime cash loss |
| GM15 | Recurrent spending | Explicit issuer definition, retention and discretionary elements | Repeat transactions become contracted subscription revenue |
| GM16 | Digital mix | Unit or revenue denominator, gross/net basis and eligible products | Peer digital percentages compared without basis matching |
| GM17 | Hardware sell-in | Units shipped to distribution, geography and returns | Sell-in directly equals consumer demand |
| GM18 | Hardware sell-through | Consumer sales, observed/estimated basis and revision | Difference between unmatched series is measured channel inventory |
| GM19 | Attach and utilization | Compatible hardware cohort, software categories and bundles | Current software/current hardware becomes lifetime attach rate |
| GM20 | Network services | Subscription, advertising and other components | Segment revenue assigned entirely to paid subscriptions |
| GM21 | Rights contribution | Ownership, license, distribution or development contract; territory | Title association implies exclusive ownership or disclosed profit share |
| GM22 | Regional economics | Revenue/payers/cost by comparable geography | Listing venue treated as location of economic demand |
| GM23 | Currency sensitivity | Reported and constant-currency bases, source method | FX-only difference becomes an operating growth signal |
| GM24 | Expectations | Original/revised guidance, analyst vintage or house scenario | Company guidance comparison labeled consensus surprise |
| GM25 | Commercial ranking | Ranking window, territory, product grouping and covered revenue | Ordinal rank used as cardinal sales or units |
| GM26 | Concurrent use | Observation time, platform, online scope and sampling | Concurrent count interpreted as unique buyers |
| GM27 | Wishlist interest | Actual access, gross/net additions, deletions and cohort | Undisclosed private counts or universal conversion coefficients invented |
| GM28 | Cohort contribution | Retained receipts less relevant incremental costs, with timing | Gross spending or activity automatically becomes lifetime equity value |

These are diligence specifications. The list does not assert that all measures are disclosed, accessible or currently ingested. A descriptive first release can remain useful with unavailable cohort coefficients and private contract terms.

## 3. Thirty future adversarial acceptance cases

No row below is an executed application PASS. The later written specification must map each necessary behavior into an incumbent owner and a real test/proof path. Some cases belong to the reusable shared template rather than gaming-specific code.

| ID | Challenge | Required future result |
|---|---|---|
| GC01 | A title operates on several platforms and in different territories | Keep title, platform and jurisdiction scope; no fuzzy title-to-security ownership assignment |
| GC02 | Latest annual activity revises an older period using later observations | Retain original vintage for historical evaluation and show corrected current belief separately |
| GC03 | Activity series is missing for one platform | Unavailable coverage, not zero activity or a negative-demand conclusion |
| GC04 | Average monthly payers are multiplied across a quarter | Label payer-period calculation; do not call it distinct quarterly people |
| GC05 | Monthly yield and ending monthly/quarterly users are mixed | Refuse exact reconstruction unless populations/time bases match |
| GC06 | Revenue rises while bookings decline | Show both clocks and the recognition bridge; do not collapse into one momentum state |
| GC07 | Durable-item recognition estimate changes | Preserve estimate vintage; distinguish accounting change from new purchases |
| GC08 | Processing expense is deferred with revenue | Do not present expense/bookings as a same-period variable-cost ratio |
| GC09 | Incentive change applies only to eligible adult/country transactions | Preserve effective date, eligibility and excluded populations |
| GC10 | Creator payout improves content supply but initially reduces retention of receipts | Present competing reinvestment hypotheses and required later contribution proof |
| GC11 | Creator-payable timing moves cash between adjacent quarters | Keep reported cash and labeled timing sensitivity; no silent normalization |
| GC12 | Infrastructure line includes safety, depreciation and other costs | Do not reinterpret it as a pure hosting cost or one AI initiative's spending |
| GC13 | Development movement is already part of CFO | Do not deduct it again in a derived CFO-minus-capital subtotal |
| GC14 | Pipeline carrying value or impairment is known, title economics unknown | Do not infer complete project cost, fair value or all-franchise loss |
| GC15 | Recurrent spending contains discretionary purchases and ads | No contracted ARR label or automatic subscription-style valuation |
| GC16 | One peer reports digital units, another software revenue | Show non-comparability; do not pick a digital-monetization winner |
| GC17 | Third-party distribution is gross at one issuer and net at another | Keep reported basis and necessary bridge; no fabricated comparable market share |
| GC18 | Bundle software is counted in units but assigned hardware revenue | Prevent misleading software ASP or attach calculation |
| GC19 | Shipments, consumer sales and activated accounts diverge | Explain populations and clocks; do not manufacture channel inventory |
| GC20 | Quarterly profit benefits from translation or refunds | Distinguish source-reported drivers from recurring operating improvement |
| GC21 | Games are a subset of a larger services segment | Keep the documented subtotal, excluding unrelated services without assumed weights |
| GC22 | International gaming growth has opposing reported/constant-currency signs | Preserve both with source method, rather than averaging or hiding one |
| GC23 | A product milestone is announced after the financial period | Do not use the future milestone to explain earlier realized results |
| GC24 | Guidance is original, revised, removed or changed in cadence | Preserve expectation kind/vintage and availability; no fictitious full-year consensus |
| GC25 | Rank improves after discount or a new package appears | Explain price/window/package scope; ordinal rank is not a sales estimate |
| GC26 | Current concurrent count excludes offline/other-platform activity | Preserve exclusions; do not label total worldwide users or customers |
| GC27 | Wishlist numbers or conversion data require publisher access | Do not invent access or infer undisclosed counts from a public rank |

## 4. Proposed first usable research experience

For gaming, the useful output is a concise explanation of growth quality, reinvestment and uncertainty. It must still work when title revenue, private contracts and cohort lifetime value are unavailable. Display source-reported observations, management interpretation and the house hypothesis as different things.

An example user question is: **“More players are active; why are earnings and current spending telling different stories?”** The answer should connect the applicable payer, recognition and cost observations, not return a generic narrative or a universal sector score. The product should name what would change the interpretation and expose the existing company/watchlist route.

This supplements the shared template and the prior advertising-first candidate. It does not select a new build carrier, alter source custody, dispatch Fable or create an independent metrics service. Browser acceptance later must follow the existing dark/light, language and screen-size requirements; source presence or green CI alone is not user acceptance.

## 5. Validation boundaries and next research unit

The local arithmetic check independently evaluates the same inputs using Decimal and Fraction. It does not audit issuer filings, test a collector, prove point-in-time data access or establish predictive usefulness. Source reference/ID checks prevent broken research references, not unsupported economic causality.

The next research unit is terrestrial connectivity, followed by satellite. The retained-economics framework carries forward, while sector-specific drivers must change: network deployment, subscriber cohorts, service versus equipment economics, utilization and financing are not game-launch metrics. Full global exposure mapping, historical qualification and an accepted written specification/implementation plan still precede Fable's final build handoff.
