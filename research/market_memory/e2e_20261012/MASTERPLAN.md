## 3. Product thesis and three modes that must never be confused

The distinctive product is an **auditable research memory of market experience**, not more historical prose. It must preserve the difference between these modes:

**A. Actual-output playback.** Show the exact retained context and output that the system actually produced at that time. No recomputation, current model, nearest-date substitute or later rule may fill a missing artifact.

**B. Historical hypothetical analysis.** Apply a clearly identified research method to the information demonstrably admissible at a historical cutoff. The method may be newer than the cutoff, but then the result is a hypothetical experiment—not what the system actually thought then. Register that distinction and the method version. An algorithm or language model developed later can contain future knowledge even when its supplied context is truncated.

**C. Current decision support.** Use a current, explicitly pinned context and a qualified historical corpus to show relevant previous situations, differences, conflicting evidence, data coverage and outcomes. This remains contextual research, not a signal, ranking, position size or executable trade.

Outcome visibility is a separate control. “Outcomes known by the original decision time” and “what happened subsequently” cannot be blended. Neighbor selection is fixed before outcome attachment. In a historical replay, future outcomes cannot set the feature normalizer, candidate pool, weights, filter, embedding or rationale used to select matches. A later-outcome inspector may show those outcomes only with the later lens explicit.

ALFRED illustrates why data vintage matters, but day-level vintages and sometimes delayed archive additions do not establish exact intraday house knowledge. FRED real-time parameters also default to current information unless specified. These sources support reconstruction at their evidenced precision, not fabrication of Market Memory’s past captures. [E01, E02]

### First useful experience

The user chooses a subject and cutoff, sees what was known and unavailable, opens a retained episode, inspects comparable prior situations and the differences, then chooses whether to reveal subsequent outcomes. Evidence links land on qualified original material, not a generic source homepage. The screen explains the sample and exclusions in ordinary language; technical receipts remain available underneath.

A successful reference release needs a real SPY operational context, a real qualified nonempty analogue corpus, all three included contextual consumers, and an entitled user journey. An honest “no qualifying matches” state must work, but a release consisting only of empty states or synthetic examples does **not** satisfy the promised product.

## 5. Production contracts to freeze before integration

The following are **proposed implementation interfaces**, not claims that these modules already exist. One O1 integrator owns shared contracts. Any action-time change must be reconciled before children code against it.

### Population and coverage

Planned module: `engine/neuralweb/market_memory_population.py`.
Planned schema: `contracts/market_memory/population_manifest.v1.schema.json`.

```python
enumerate_population(reader: ExactContextReader,
                     request: PopulationRequest) -> PopulationManifest
```

`PopulationRequest` binds subject/cohort, decision cutoff, basis, allowed source profile and exact owner generation references. `PopulationManifest` records source IDs/digests, eligibility rule/version, expected cohort denominator, captured/abstained/excluded counts, exact context IDs, coverage intervals and reasons for absence. It contains no future target labels. Independently published trusted/PIT generations are a pinned pair, not an atomic estate snapshot. An index built today can organize retained history, but cannot claim it existed or was queried historically.

The manifest's completeness claim is scoped: complete for a declared eligible population under its specified owners and intervals. It is never “complete market history” because a catalog returned 128 rows. Unobserved opportunities cannot be invented to meet a population threshold. A missing original identity or source receipt stays missing.

### Label-free state and normalization

Planned module: `engine/neuralweb/market_memory_episode_features.py`.

```python
project_episode(context_bytes: bytes,
                profile: FeatureProfile) -> ProjectedEpisode
fit_feature_normalizer(training: Sequence[ProjectedEpisode],
                       registration: FitRegistration) -> NormalizerArtifact
```

`ProjectedEpisode` contains interpretable coordinates, units, masks, context digest, measurement/availability clocks and transform version. It excludes later outcomes, future narrative, post-event regime labels and current-identity substitutions. Each feature has a source-native basis; missing is not zero and incomparable is not close.

`NormalizerArtifact` binds the exact training manifest, fit cutoff, feature definitions and numeric convention. No holdout or future candidate contributes to fitting scales, weights or cutoffs. Missing-feature behavior is preregistered; dropping difficult features or samples after seeing outcomes is another model trial.

### Retrieval and outcome attachment

Planned module: `engine/neuralweb/market_memory_retrieval_service.py`.

```python
retrieve_episodes(request: RetrievalRequest,
                  manifest: PopulationManifest,
                  normalizer: NormalizerArtifact,
                  reader: ExactContextReader) -> RetrievalResult
attach_outcomes(result: RetrievalResult,
                outcome_reader: QualifiedOutcomeReader,
                outcome_cutoff: str,
                view_mode: str) -> OutcomeProjection
```

`RetrievalRequest` binds the query context, method registration, population and fit versions, temporal policy and maximum results. `RetrievalResult` records eligibility/exclusion audit, deterministic neighbors/distances, self/overlap/purge/embargo decisions, coverage and qualified claims. It does not fabricate effective sample size or probability intervals. Retrieval may rank *similarity* inside the registered research function; that is not authority to rank investments.

Outcome attachment never changes selected IDs or the reason they were selected. Then-known and subsequently observed outcome modes have separate admissibility. Frozen forecasts, pending horizons, revisions, missing sources and censored subjects remain distinct. Current query material may not be scored as a matured historical result.

### Real evidence synthesis and consumers

Planned module: `engine/neuralweb/market_memory_operating_cortex_service.py`.

```python
compose_memory_evidence(result: RetrievalResult,
```

The same-family production packet preserves exact contexts, evidence spans, subject/time/rights, claim type, contradictions, missingness and registered falsifiers. Structural span closure is necessary, not semantic entailment. Model output proposes explanation; it cannot create source facts, permissions, a canonical identity or a historical observation.

## 7. Research design: usefulness before claims of predictive advantage

### What must be falsifiable

**H1 — retrieval relevance:** Qualified analogues improve finding decision-relevant historical evidence compared with no memory, recent cases and a transparent state-distance baseline. Failure means no advantage on a frozen evaluation, not that the team should alter the target after seeing results.

**H2 — evidence quality:** The memory-supported explanation improves source correctness, contradiction detection and important omissions without raising unsupported-claim rates. Fluent prose and valid citation bytes alone cannot establish this.

**H3 — optional conditional prediction:** Retrieved cases improve a separately registered forecast family after realistic clocks, revisions and dependence are respected. This is optional research, not a requirement that the product manufacture alpha. Do not expand into Prophet, Adaptive Signal Clock, Options or Catalyst prediction to satisfy it.

**H4 — reproducibility and economics:** The same versions reproduce the same result, and the measured utility justifies storage, fitting, serving, synthesis, coordination and review costs. More agents or a larger embedding model are not themselves evidence of value.

### Evaluation protocol

Freeze task questions, source/feature eligibility, query times, target windows, train/tune/test boundaries, baselines, trial budget, disagreement rules, meaningful quality margins and analysis plan before accessing holdout labels. Have independent reviewers approve the protocol and label guidance. Use time-held-out cases and appropriate subject/event-held-out tests; a current model evaluated on old facts may remember the subsequent history. Labeling a context “historical” does not make the language model historically ignorant. Use blinded/anonymized task conditions where valid, disclose unavoidable model-pretraining contamination, and rely on prospective evaluation for claims requiring actual decision-time ignorance. [E04]

Use chronological, dependence-aware analysis rather than treating correlated stocks/days as IID. Purge and embargo must cover the actual feature and label intervals. Three nonoverlapping neighbors are not automatically three independent trials; common market shocks, duplicated sources and related subjects still matter. Report counts, clustering and uncertainty, with effective N left unestimated until a justified estimator is accepted. Model-specific findings about correlated-sample risk motivate this care but do not supply a universal financial correction factor. [E05]

Keep selection blind to outcomes. Normalizers, learned embeddings, distance weights, candidate filters and thresholds all belong inside the registered training process. A final post-filter that removes future rows cannot undo fitting the representation on future labels or text.

Assess retrieval relevance independently of any prediction score. Assess citation precision/source support independently of answer preference. Measure answerable and unanswerable cases, missingness, source failures and contradictory examples. Report disagreement and abstention rather than dropping difficult rows from the denominator. Correctness has to pass before gains in speed or fluency count.

For forecasts, use target-appropriate proper scores, calibration and sharpness, benchmark uncertainty, maturity and censoring. Preserve failed hypotheses, changed trial versions and contaminated holdouts. A NO-GO can close a family; an immature or underpowered experiment remains pending or is explicitly adjudicated as insufficient. The current plan is not a preregistered empirical result.

### Natural-time obligations

Existing durable evaluation and source owners accrue admitted opportunities. Do not manually restart the August window, replay fixtures as natural outcomes or create extra timers to keep an agent busy. Capture issued, abstained and failed opportunities under the declared census. While horizons mature, advance independent UI, corpus, rights and integration work.
