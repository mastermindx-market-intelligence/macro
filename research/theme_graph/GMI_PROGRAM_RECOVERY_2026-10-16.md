## 5. Research verdict

### Classification and activity answer different questions

[GICS April 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-gics.pdf) assigns one principal-business classification at each of four hierarchical levels. Revenue is central, with earnings and additional analysis relevant in diversified cases; subsidiaries with separate reporting may receive their own classification. This supports a stable classification lens, not a complete decomposition of everything an issuer does.

[ICB August 2026](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/ftse-russell/en_us/documents/ground-rules/icb-ground-rules.pdf) similarly uses company accounts, business segmentation and revenue rules, with defined fallbacks and classification stability. Its levels and definitions are scheme-specific. Preserve scheme, edition, tier, code, assignment source and validity interval instead of translating every label into a generic sector field.

The [SEC SIC list](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/standard-industrial-classification-sic-code-list) explains SIC's use in EDGAR business classification and filing-review assignment. It is not interchangeable with GICS. For China, current [Shanghai Stock Exchange company information](https://www.sse.com.cn/assortment/stock/list/info/company/index.shtml?COMPANY_CODE=600768) explicitly defers to CAPCO's industry classification and results; CAPCO's [September 2026 report](https://www.capco.org.cn/sjfb/dytj/202609/20260901/j_2026090109504700017882275680267259.html) still cites its May 2023 classification guidance. The direct guidance page was not readable in this session, so its detailed rule thresholds are not adopted here. THS concepts remain a separate source-local vocabulary.

[FactSet RBICS methodology description](https://insight.factset.com/resources/factset-revere-business-industry-classifications-datafeed) illustrates a complementary approach: granular products/services and standardized reported segment exposures, supported by specialist analysts and system checks. Its cited coverage statistics are explicitly May 2019, not current coverage evidence. GMI should adopt the separation of principal classification, activity and measured exposure, without copying vendor taxonomy or assuming vendor access.

### Economic networks need typed evidence

[GLEIF Level2](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/access-and-use-lei-data/level-2-data-who-owns-whom) reports direct and ultimate accounting-consolidating parents. That is useful ownership evidence with a particular definition; it is not every subsidiary, every beneficial owner or every commercial counterparty. Preserve reporting exceptions and unresolved identity.

[IFRS 8 paragraph 34](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/html-standards/english/2026/issued/ifrs8.html) permits major-customer concentration disclosure without naming the customer. An anonymous customer must remain an issuer/document/period-scoped object. A disclosed concentration percentage does not identify the buyer, and customer labels cannot be assumed continuous between years.

[TNIC](https://hobergphillips.tuck.dartmouth.edu/idata/Readme_tnic3.txt) measures similarity in business-description text. [Vertical TNIC](https://faculty.marshall.usc.edu/Gerard-Hoberg/FresardHobergPhillipsDataSite/index.html) explicitly measures potential vertical relatedness, not observed firm-pair goods flows. [BEA input-output tables](https://www.bea.gov/industry/input-output-accounts-data) describe industry production structure. Use all three for discovery, comparative context or conditional hypotheses, never to fabricate a named supplier edge.

[Carvalho and coauthors' production-network study](https://vasco-m-carvalho.github.io/pdfs/JapanEQ.pdf) provides evidence that shocks can propagate upstream and downstream under observed economic-network conditions. It does not supply universal company-level transmission coefficients. Substitution, inventories, contract terms, capacity, geography and timing can change the effect.

[FactSet's March 2026 customer-momentum study](https://insight.factset.com/supply-chain-signals-enhancing-the-customer-momentum-strategy-with-network-centrality) uses analyst-verified relationships and explicitly substitutes network centrality where revenue dependence is rarely disclosed. Its illustrative backtest ignores transaction costs. The useful lesson is to preserve the proxy label and test a proposed predictive use separately. Centrality must not become a revenue percentage or production allocation instruction.

Keep optional parents and multiple valid paths. Every category/theme/micro needs a versioned definition, inclusion/exclusion predicate, aliases, provenance, effective and knowledge intervals, and explicit split/merge/retirement semantics. Crosswalks must name equivalent, broader, narrower or proxy relationships rather than imply equality.

Allow a diversified issuer multiple activity-specific theme memberships. Each membership binds issuer and activity/segment scope, role, lifecycle, target definition revision, source evidence, native identity, use qualification, curation decision, clocks and status. A broad parent basket is insufficient evidence for a narrow micro. If only a subset of a group qualifies, its existing group owner must supply the appropriate scope; the adapter must not create a replacement basket.

### Relations, characteristics and explanations

An economic relationship should bind direction, parties, product/service and geography, relation species, lifecycle, period, observation/adjudication status and evidence. Different species include observed supplies/purchases, contract commitment, planned product integration, licensing, distribution, ownership/control, competition, complementarity and conditional dependency. Do not collapse all of them into partnership or supplier.

Company/activity characteristics should explain revenue model, customers/end demand, principal cost inputs, pricing mechanism, capacity/capex cycle, inventory and lead-time constraints, substitution, regulation, geography/currency and material concentration. Report direct evidence, analyst interpretation and sector-level context separately. Sector averages cannot silently become issuer facts.

Keep amounts and weights distinct:

| Quantity | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Membership confidence | Confidence that the stated activity satisfies a versioned theme predicate; calibrated only where evaluated. |
| Seller revenue dependence | Revenue from this customer divided by this seller's comparable revenue, with period and consolidation scope. |
| Buyer input dependence | Input purchases, quantity or capacity dependence on a supplier, with its own denominator and units. |
| Estimated exposure | A separately labelled interval or estimate, assumptions, method revision and uncertainty. |
| Ownership percentage | Equity/voting/accounting scope, instrument/control basis and effective date. |
| Network centrality | Formula, direction, graph population, cutoff and version; no monetary interpretation by default. |
| Propagation coefficient | Scenario/model-specific effect under stated conditions; separately evaluated and not inferred from membership weight. |

Unknown amounts remain null. A company can have a strongly supported business role with unknown materiality. Known zero, not applicable, undisclosed, unavailable and stale are separate states.

For a multi-hop explanation, retain each observed edge or hypothesis, direction, mechanism, activating condition, counterevidence, horizon and uncertainty. Join paths through stable economic units. Never sum the same segment, contract, subsidiary or revenue stream because it appears under several themes or listings. Without an exposure partition, provide a qualitative pathway or defensible bound; do not invent an additive percentage.

### Time, refresh and corrections

Preserve source publication (including precision), first observation/ingestion, effective business interval, first known/admitted decision, later correction and served generation separately. Filter by requested knowledge cutoff before selection, joins or aggregation. Reprocessing an old document cannot make its contents newly current; a later correction must preserve the original decision-time view.

Use existing collection/publication schedules with per-source cadence and natural publication witnesses. Material filings and corporate actions feed the incumbent review queue. A complete authoritative empty observation may remove membership; a timeout, partial fetch, changed source shape or omitted name in a non-exhaustive roster may not. Removal is an event with evidence and clock, not deletion of history.
