**What event specifications resolve and leave open.** CSE's two-page specification [CA10] contains additions, delistings, suspension/reinstatement, symbol/name/identifier changes, splits and shares-outstanding events. It distinguishes security types and current/new identifiers, effective dates, New/Modified status, and header-only no-change days. This enables a finite reconciliation design. It does not prove historical delivery completeness, publication time of a revision or replacement of an earlier assertion. Its identifier footnote conditions CUSIP/ISIN provision on separate client licensing. An end-of-day trade-summary file [CA11] has explicit market and trade-date fields; it cannot alone certify admitted but non-trading or suspended lines.

Cboe's master description [CA14] uses a trading-eligibility population across multiple platforms. CIRO's descriptions [CA20] independently explain why trading on a book and listing admission are different facts. An issuer/security master may therefore be broader than the primary-admission roster. Required exclusions and positive primary/dual-primary evidence are not supplied by market volume, domicile, currency, ticker suffix or the presence of a CDR. The offered listing manual's foreign-issuer provisions [CA19] reinforce that foreign listings need individual status evidence.
