## 3. C01 — Micron/NVIDIA planned product integration

**Primary evidence:** [Micron's original 26 February 2024 release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2024/Micron-Commences-Volume-Production-of-Industry-Leading-HBM3E-Solution-to-Accelerate-the-Growth-of-AI-02-26-2024/default.aspx), opening paragraph. It distinguishes Micron's begun memory production from future inclusion of its 24GB 8H HBM3E in NVIDIA H200, with downstream shipment timing stated as calendar Q2 2024.

**Independent grade:** supported as a planned, product-specific integration. The memory producer is the announcing supplier and NVIDIA is the named downstream product company. The source does not establish a direct invoice counterparty, exclusivity, all-GPU coverage, delivered quantity, purchase obligation, realized revenue share or current procurement allocation.

**Native choice:** `product_integration`, lifecycle `planned`, with the original source-local labels and null magnitude/identity resolution. A `reported_use` or generic current supplier claim would overstate the original temporal proposition. The phrase indicating begun production cannot be transferred to the different proposition that customer shipments had already occurred.

**Revision/falsifier:** later product documentation, procurement changes or a changed configuration could qualify the relationship. Such evidence must be a separately dated observation. A later fulfillment cannot rewrite the earlier knowledge state. Conversely, mere passage of the expected shipping quarter does not establish fulfillment or termination. The accepted span coordinates are character `[370876,371258)` and byte `[370936,371320)`; the earlier fetch receipt's exploratory coordinates remain historical and are not the final candidate contract.

**Purpose gate:** suitable for unadmitted evidence display and bounded analyst review after the existing source binding. It is not a price/earnings propagation coefficient or admitted issuer relationship fact.

## 4. C02 — CATL framework with a Tesla buyer group

**Primary evidence:** [CNINFO issuer announcement 2021-053](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2021-06-28/1210355996.PDF), pages 1–3; the party and operative terms are on page 2. Seller: 宁德时代新能源科技股份有限公司. Buyers: Tesla, Inc. and 特斯拉（上海）有限公司. The filing reports signing on 25 June 2021, disclosure dated 28 June, and supply from 1 January 2022 through 31 December 2025. Orders determine purchases and settled amounts; authorized signatures are the stated effectiveness condition.

**Independent grade:** supported as an announced signed commercial framework. Seller/buyer direction is explicit. The collective Tesla label refers to the defined buyer group, not two independently sized customer exposures. A faithful representation preserves the legal names, collective definition, battery scope, and order-dependent quantities together.

**Native choice:** `framework_agreement` is the conceptual fit, normally preserving the historical announcement state. A current `in_force` label in 2026 is unverified from this source; an `ended` label for all CATL–Tesla commerce is also unjustified. The current single-label candidate boundary does not itself provide a canonical multiparty agreement or buyer-allocation model. Source-local collective wording can be reviewed without resolving it to a fabricated legal entity; canonical multi-party adoption needs its owning contract.

**Format gate:** the original is PDF. Its web extraction or a translation is not original UTF-8 source bytes. An approved PDF/extraction representation must preserve the original digest, page/span provenance, extraction version and original-language linkage. Until then, retain a format refusal; do not paste extracted text into a file while calling its digest the original PDF's digest.

**Revision/falsifier:** the filing describes the new framework in the context of an earlier 2020 arrangement. That does not by itself prove legal replacement of every prior term, a `corrects` candidate link, guaranteed minimum purchases, or continued supply after the disclosed term. A later order/renewal is separate evidence. Signing date is not the public disclosure clock, and neither is a historical system-observation receipt.

**Quantity and revision:** reported growth is not exposure weight; segment revenue is not a pure CPU or AI revenue measure. ## 8. C06 — Microsoft accounting flows and obligations

**Primary evidence:** [Microsoft's FY2025 annual report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html), Cash Flows, Material Cash Requirements, and Note 13. It separately reports, in USD millions: cash PPE additions **(64,551)**; finance-lease right-of-use assets obtained for obligations **20,511**; construction commitments **32,149**; purchase commitments **109,953**. The first two are FY2025 measures; the latter two are obligations outstanding at 30 June 2025. The purchase-commitment footnote describes datacenter-related orders/take-or-pay contracts outside the construction-commitment category.

**Independent grade:** supported as four separately typed accounting observations. The cash-flow sign must survive, even if a display separately derives a positive spending magnitude. Lease asset recognition is not the same observation as cash paid. A stock of future obligations is not contemporaneous realized capex or vendor revenue. These measures do not establish a named recipient or an AI-only allocation.

**Native choice:** refuse current relationship kinds. The absence of disclosed allocations must stay explicit; it cannot be filled by placing NVIDIA, an assumed GPU share, or an invented supplier into a valid-looking candidate. Nor does an aggregate commitment constitute the source-local named framework needed for `framework_agreement`. An accounting/commitment representation and its incumbent owner are required.

**Important nuance:** the objection is to unsupported mixing of recognition bases, periods and recipient attribution. It is not a blanket assertion that no financial measures can ever be aggregated. The source explicitly separates purchase and construction commitments; an approved, clearly named obligation subtotal is analytically different from inventing contemporaneous AI supplier revenue. Similarly, a capex measure including finance leases needs its defined management/accounting reconciliation rather than an assumed identity.

**Revision/falsifier:** retain the original note/statement vintage and period/balance dates. An order cancellation, delivery, lease commencement, cash payment or later financial recast changes a different state variable. Those events require their own evidence rather than a single lifecycle transition inferred from a headline.

## 9. Conditions that must block pilot promotion

The following are hard semantic failures if a proposed pilot record asserts them; they are not allegations about unseen builder records:

1. **Lifecycle strengthening:** C01's planned inclusion becomes fulfilled shipments, or C02's signed/order-dependent framework becomes guaranteed realized purchases or an automatically current contract.
2. **Party/perimeter collapse:** C02's buyer group becomes two independently sized exposures; C04's consortium share is assigned wholly to one listed security; a source label silently becomes canonical identity.
3. **Denominator substitution:** C03's aggregate coverage becomes a per-supplier share; C04 equity becomes revenue; C05 growth becomes exposure; C06 spending/obligation totals become named supplier sales.
4. **Kind laundering:** ownership or accounting observations are changed into an allowed commercial, thematic, correlation or administrative kind simply to produce an inspectable candidate.
5. **Format laundering:** extracted/OCR/translated PDF text is called the original retained UTF-8 byte stream, or its offsets/digest are attached to the original PDF without a derivative representation.
6. **Historical laundering:** signing, fiscal year, URL directory or present retrieval is used to fabricate earlier public/system availability; later versions replace the original observation without lineage.
7. **Statistical overclaim:** six purposively chosen cases or one eligible candidate is reported as a 120-case evaluation, representative coverage or measured extraction accuracy.

All pilot records should retain `NOT_ADMITTED`, null Graph1, no ranking/gating/sizing/trading/prediction authority, unknown canonical identities and absent purpose-specific rights/adoption. Independent semantic agreement does not remove these gates.
