## Working capability

`engine/company_intelligence/relationship_candidates.py` and its file-input CLI inspect a manually supplied candidate using the original UTF-8 source and the existing earnings-release flat receipt. They check full-body, span and normalized-value hashes, exact character/byte coordinates, source-local anchors, closed input shape and bounded input size.

The output keeps these propositions distinct:

1. The supplied source bytes reproduce the supplied receipt.
2. A person supplied a particular relationship interpretation.
3. The document labels may or may not be authenticated.
4. The native identity, temporal, rights and fact owners may or may not admit the candidate.
5. An admitted fact might later support an owner-approved graph projection or hypothesis.

Only the first proposition is mechanically established by this increment. The manual interpretation remains visible with its limitations. Similarity and correlation are classified as non-relationship observations. Administrative roles, supplier rosters, anonymous parties, framework agreements and planned product integrations retain distinct review dispositions. Shipment, revenue, economic weight, canonical party IDs and theme membership remain null.

Current inspection can be useful while eligibility is missing. Historical inspection requires an explicitly supplied native contract and bound row, uses native Data OS filtering before semantic replay, and still does not certify authenticated historical ingestion or served-system history. Corrections and contradictions remain explicit; no original is overwritten or automatically replaced.
