## 1. Decision

Mastermind should use **product- and activity-evidenced membership, resolved to dated issuers through Data OS, then admitted through the existing GMI curation and group-membership owners**. Discovery should be broad; admission should be conservative and evidence-specific. The system should explain why a company belongs, the exact role it plays, what is missing, and when the assertion became knowable.

Do not solve granularity by creating more ticker lists or copying parent baskets into every child. Keep the accepted three-tier hierarchy, but make each proposed member record precise enough to distinguish a manufacturer, an equipment supplier, a service provider, a distributor, a customer, and a development-stage entrant. Product role, manufacturing stage, geography, application and commercial maturity usually belong in existing evidence/vertical facets, not a universal fourth taxonomy tier.

This recommendation is an integration judgment under Mastermind's current architecture. It is not a mathematically proven optimum or a validated alpha result. Its advantage is a usable, auditable explanation layer that can later support qualified state and evaluation without corrupting historical membership or multiplying independent observations.

## 3. The membership object: seven questions, not one score

For every company–microtheme claim, retain seven distinct questions.

1. **Activity:** what identifiable product, process or service does the operating business actually provide?
2. **Role:** does it make the focal product, enable production, supply a component, distribute it, buy it, or merely discuss it?
3. **Identity:** which operating entity, consolidated issuer and listed security does the evidence bind, at what date?
4. **Time:** when was the activity effective, publicly disclosed, observed by the house and admitted by the owner?
5. **Materiality:** what numerator, denominator, accounting basis and period establish economic importance? Unknown is null.
6. **Stage:** is the evidence a capability, design, approval, sample, contract, shipment, installed operation or sustained revenue?
7. **Use:** is the claim research-only, semantically admitted, measurement-qualified, rights-qualified for this consumer, or separately approved for a decision use?

A single confidence score cannot safely replace these axes. A well-supported product offering may have unknown revenue materiality. A legally identified issuer may still have no admissible historical membership. A large commercial contract may describe future supply rather than current production. These distinctions determine what the user can reasonably conclude.

### Proposed admission predicate

For a semantic nomination to advance, require an exact scope version, role-specific primary evidence, a resolvable entity/issuer chain, original clocks, checked counterevidence and a named existing owner. Then obtain that owner's dated ratification and the required current micro-endpoint/group-admission amendment. Measurement qualification is a later predicate with a defined universe, source completeness, independent issuer treatment and consumer-specific method.

Do not set a universal revenue threshold such as 10%, 25% or 50% as a substitute for these checks. Such a number would be arbitrary for early-stage reactor developers, diversified suppliers, pure-play manufacturers and companies with undisclosed product revenue. A future measurement owner can preregister a justified materiality rule for a particular use. Until then, show factual product participation and unknown materiality separately.

## 4. Granularity: where to split and where not to

### HBM, packaging and fabrication tools

The official Micron, SK hynix and Samsung sources establish different device-development/production/shipment observations. ASE VIPack and TSMC 3DFabric support package-integration capabilities. AMD's accelerator specifications establish that its product incorporates HBM; they do not establish that AMD manufactures HBM. [MICRON_HBM4] [SK_HBM4] [SAMSUNG_HBM4] [ASE_VIPACK] [TSMC_3DFABRIC] [AMD_MI350]

Keep `theme:hbm_packaging` unchanged pending D03, but make the evidence review distinguish **HBM device makers**, **advanced package integration/services**, and **adjacent equipment/subsystems**. The research supports a split review, not an in-place historical rename. HBM generation is a product-generation facet; it is not automatically a new permanent theme. A qualified HBM cohort should not inherit every packaging company or GPU vendor.

### Interconnect and custom silicon

Broadcom's switch-silicon evidence and Marvell's optical DSP evidence address different roles. Marvell's custom-silicon architecture announcement is evidence of capability, not proof of every rumored hyperscaler customer or realized program revenue. [BRCM_SWITCH] [MRVL_DSP] [MRVL_CUSTOM]

Retain D04 scope review. Distinguish switch ASIC, NIC/DPU, optical DSP and optical-device roles before measuring an interconnect group. Do not place all optical cable makers under a silicon theme. General server CPU belongs to a separate unresolved parent/scope question; a narrowly documented host-CPU use does not make the entire CPU business AI-specific. [INTEL_XEON] [NVDA_GRACE]

### Process tools and subsystems

ASML exposure tools, Tokyo Electron coat/develop or etch systems, Lam etch/deposition tools and MKS process subsystems are not interchangeable supply roles. KLA's current inspection portfolio corroborates the inspection role without relying solely on an old launch article. [ASML_PRODUCTS] [TEL_PATTERNING] [TEL_ETCH] [LAM_PRODUCTS] [MKS_ETCH] [KLA_CURRENT]

The best additional granularity is process function and product stage, not a proliferation of supplier labels. A component vendor does not become a complete process-tool vendor merely because its component is inside a tool.

### Power and nuclear

Keep voltage class explicit: an Eaton medium-voltage switchgear page is not sufficient proof of a high-voltage transformer role. UPS and liquid-cooling offerings are different functions. NuScale design approval is not operating generating capacity. A Centrus delivery and a forward supply contract are different stages. BWXT's completed acquisition, not the earlier announcement alone, supports the ownership transition. [EATON_SWITCH] [ETN_UPS] [VRT_CDU] [NUSCALE_DESIGN] [CENTRUS_PRODUCTION] [CENTRUS_CONTRACT] [BWXT_PCG]

The fuel-cycle theme particularly needs mining, conversion, enrichment and fabrication facets. No company should inherit every stage. The future state owner must decide which homogeneous subpopulation can support a particular measurement.

### Robotics

FANUC and Yaskawa offer multiple control, servo and robot products. Teradyne's collaborative-arm exposure is through Universal Robots. A collaborative operating mode can crosscut industrial-arm identity; both roles may be true for one issuer without producing two independent observations. Bachem's peptide capability and confidential contracts do not identify an undisclosed drug/customer or product-level revenue share. Its differing forward-capacity language versions remain a documented conflict, not an opportunity to select the earlier date. ## 5. Identity and point-in-time rules

### Resolve the business before the ticker

Use the incumbent Data OS identity owner, not a new lookup table in this research package. Evidence may name a brand, subsidiary, legal entity or product. Resolve that to the dated issuer and only then to the security needed by the consumer. An ADR/ordinary-share pair, dual listing, acquired subsidiary, separately listed controlled subsidiary and joint venture are different cases.

The CyberArk closing record and Olink closing record illustrate why a brand must not remain an extra current public issuer after acquisition. Sandisk illustrates why legal separation and first trading need distinct dates. Daqo illustrates why a consolidated ownership relation is not automatically an alias. [PANW_CYBERARK_CLOSE] [TMO_OLINK_CLOSE] [SNDK_RESULTS] [SNDK_LISTING] [DQ_20F]

GLEIF parent relationships can supplement an existing identity investigation, but describe accounting consolidation with exceptions; they do not by themselves resolve products, securities or every operating entity. Do not introduce a parallel GLEIF-backed identity authority. [GLEIF_L2]

### Preserve clocks rather than manufacturing history

A research row should retain source effective time, publication time, observation time, house admission time and, where relevant, withdrawal/correction time. A current undated product page supports current observed activity only. A fiscal period end is not the date its contents became public. SEC aggregate frames can contain last-filed facts, so the current frame must not be replayed as a historical knowledge snapshot. [SEC_APIS]

No backfill is justified by today's roster. Revisions are new evidence at a later cutoff, not an overwrite of previously observed membership. Keep W-C7's nightly-only, keep-first, forward-only behavior. The research packet intentionally sets historical-availability proof to false; merely storing this packet today cannot prove that every cited page existed unchanged at an earlier decision time.

An inferred acquisition or inferred publication date is not acceptable. Conflicting date evidence remains held with both source references. Unknown is neither false nor zero.

## 6. Discovery and curation workflow

**A. Start from named consumer populations.** Recover the exact candidate/watchlist/portfolio or sector denominator from its existing owner. Separate input completeness, identity completeness, semantic coverage, microtheme coverage and measurement coverage. An attractive list of known companies is not a full-market census.

**B. Discover broadly but keep suggestions separate.** Existing filings, product pages, vertical evidence and lawful source-local diagnostics can suggest candidates. Text retrieval and models are useful for identifying product synonyms, business roles and counterexamples. Co-movement, news co-mentions and vendor rosters are discovery clues, not semantic admission.

**C. Extract a small evidence packet.** Bind the exact issuer/operating entity, product role, scope version, original clocks, source reference, supported statement and what the statement does not prove. Keep short original analysis; do not copy proprietary vendor structures or paid bodies.

**D. Challenge the candidate.** Search for a contrary role, sold business, discontinued product, contract that has not closed, customer-versus-supplier confusion, unbuilt project, generic marketing language, overlapping parent, or unqualified listing. Preserve rejected counterexamples in research so future discovery does not repeat them.

**E. Ratify through the existing owner.** The operator/delegated curation owner decides the evidence-supported semantic claim and required group/slice binding. The existing V1 restrictions on micro endpoints must be amended explicitly before materialization. A research merge does not waive this requirement.

**F. Observe one real consumer.** First prove a read-only explanation: a user can inspect a theme, understand each company's role and evidence age, and see missingness. Only after qualified membership and accepted state generation should homogeneous descriptive measurements accrue. Forecasting remains a separately preregistered use.

This is one workflow through existing owners, not a new admission service, queue, taxonomy registry or watcher.

## 7. Why the alternatives are weaker

| Approach | Useful part | Why it is not the membership authority |
|---|---|---|
| Parent-basket inheritance | Coarse context and discovery | It assigns broad proxies to every child and destroys economic specificity. |
| Vendor/ETF constituent copying | Lawful comparison or candidate discovery where rights permit | Vendor purpose, rights and inclusion rules differ; source-local structure cannot be laundered into house taxonomy. |
| Keyword or model classification alone | High-recall search and extraction proposals | Mentions conflate customers, suppliers, products and future plans; model confidence is not evidence or ratification. |
| Return correlation/clustering | A separately evaluated trading-sensitivity hypothesis | Shared returns do not establish a product role or causal economic membership. |
| Revenue-only universal cutoff | Potentially clear for narrowly disclosed businesses | Product numerators are often missing, mixed or backward-looking; one cutoff cannot fit all roles and stages. |
| Product evidence + dated identity + owner curation | Precise explanation, correction and future measurement | Requires review capacity and admits fewer names initially; this cost is preferable to a false complete roster. |

Semantic parent links and associative relations should remain distinct. SKOS's distinction between direct hierarchical relations and transitive closure is a useful conceptual analogy, not permission to import RDF or a new ontology implementation into GMI. A chain of related activities does not establish transitive company membership or return causation. [SKOS]

## 9. Validation: what must be measured before promotion

No return experiment, E1 data read or new predictive study was run for this report. The useful first benchmark is membership correctness and abstention, not alpha.

Construct a frozen, independently reviewed evaluation set spanning direct suppliers, adjacent suppliers, distributors, customers, historical sold businesses, pre-commercial plans, ambiguous brands, dual listings and conflicting evidence. Sample both obvious positives and hard negatives across regions, company size and disclosure quality. Keep the model's discovery output out of the adjudicator's initial labeling view where feasible.

Report precision among admitted claims, recall against the bounded independently assembled reference set, abstention/hold rate, role errors, identity errors, future-information errors and coverage by stratum. Do not report overall accuracy over an artificially easy or all-positive list. Log reviewer disagreement and unavailable evidence. Use a second reviewer for consequential adjudication; this report does not claim that independent review has already occurred.

Economic importance needs its own validation: issuer, product, period, currency, consolidation basis, numerator, denominator and actual disclosure. An unreported product share remains null. Sampling a product is not the same as material revenue.

Statistical cohorts need issuer-level deduplication, explicit parent/child overlap and focal-issuer exclusions. Multiple parents are not weight. A manufacturer's several products are not independent samples. A thin public cohort can be useful for qualitative research while being unsuitable for breadth, correlation or predictive inference.

Freeze consumer-specific thresholds and allowed analyses before evaluation. Existing E1 hypotheses, sample/time prerequisites, corrections and failed/held studies remain intact. Do not use this packet to select an attractive historical result and then call it confirmation.

Some first-party pages contain marketing claims, expectations or forward-looking language. Only the bounded product/business facts in the source register are used. Revenue materiality, future capacity, market share, clinical performance and technology superiority are not inferred.
