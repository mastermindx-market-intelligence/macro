## Material findings recovered so far

1. **Activity, issuer and security must be distinct.** A company can sell several products in one mechanism without being several independent issuers. An equipment supplier to HBM fabrication is not an HBM manufacturer. AMD incorporating HBM in an accelerator is not evidence that AMD manufactures HBM. Current primary evidence supports separate HBM device, packaging-service, process-tool and subsystem roles rather than one undifferentiated chip cohort.
2. **Corporate events need separate clocks.** Sandisk's May 7, 2025 results describe separation on February 21, while its Nasdaq announcement is dated February 24. Legal separation, first trading, business effective time, source publication and house admission cannot be collapsed into one date. Current NAND membership must not keep Western Digital merely because an older corporate report described the combined business. Sources: https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2025/2025-05-07-sandisk-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2025-financial-results and https://shop.sandisk.com/en-se/company/newsroom/press-releases/2025/sandisk-celebrates-nasdaq-listing-after-completing-separation .
3. **Acquisition announcement is not closing.** Palo Alto's February 19, 2026 CyberArk closing-related note confirms consummation. CyberArk product evidence is not sufficient to create a second current public issuer alongside Palo Alto. https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/company/press/2026/palo-alto-networks-announces-offer-to-purchase-relating-to-cyberark-software-ltd--s-0-00--convertible-senior-notes-due-2030 .
4. **Commercial state must be claim-specific.** Reactor design approval, a supply agreement, a sampled chip and delivered product are different evidence. NuScale's May 29, 2025 design approval supports reactor technology, not operating generating capacity. Centrus' delivered HALEU and a future supply contract must retain different stages. No quantitative exposure is inferred from those facts.
5. **Facets need not be new ontology.** FANUC's official portfolio includes collaborative robots, other industrial robots, CNCs and servos. Teradyne owns Universal Robots. Product role and application mode can be retained as evidence facets while preserving the accepted hierarchy and issuer deduplication. https://www.fanuc.co.jp/en/product/ ; https://www.teradyne.com/robotics/collaborative-robots/ .
6. **Confidential customer contracts do not disclose exact drug supply.** Bachem's peptide capacity and GLP-1 manufacturing discussion support a bounded capability candidate, not an invented Lilly/Novo customer relationship or product revenue percentage. https://www.bachem.com/knowledge-center/glp-1-demand-for-peptide-manufacturers/ ; https://www.bachem.com/articles/news/company-news/bachem-signs-long-term-cooperation-agreement-for-the-development-and-supply-of-peptides/ .
