## 1. Outcome and release boundary

The first implementation milestone is an evidence-backed economic-context vertical slice: a real source observation becomes a scoped relationship under the proper native owner, survives identity/time/rights validation, projects through the existing graph/hypothesis lineage, and appears correctly in the existing product with a matching abstention case.

The second milestone expands source and relationship coverage with measured quality, versioned corrections and defensible missingness. The third is an empirical operating-outcome experiment. Neural models are conditional challengers after that foundation; their adoption is not a prerequisite for the evidence product.

No milestone automatically grants grading, ranking, portfolio or trading authority. Preserve all four K3-D kill receipts and the sole incumbent ThemeState producer. A new product name or model architecture cannot bypass them.

|---|---|

No commercial quote, trial enrollment, procurement or license amendment was obtained in this commission. The source register records public offers and claims as such. Continue with permissible public/issuer sources where useful rather than holding the entire evidence product for a vendor decision.

These checks demonstrate specified helper and algebraic behavior, not model performance.
