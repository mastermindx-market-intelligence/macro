## Decision

A useful disclosed-relationships product is feasible with public official records, permitted issuer material, identity data and macro input-output tables. A complete, timely, quantitatively weighted global company supply graph is **not established by those sources**. Licensed graphs are advertised in vendor product descriptions, but its current eligible-universe coverage, point-in-time properties, economic weights, correction behavior and GMI reuse rights require samples and contract proof. Buying a terminal, a retrieval API or a free research app does not itself grant bulk ingestion, model training or redistribution rights.

Recommend a staged hybrid: a rights-cleared public evidence foundation; a single licensed relationships feed selected by a target-universe bakeoff; one IR/document feed if it materially saves multilingual document work; macro tables as explicitly estimated sector priors; and optional inference retrieval for research. Keep disclosed observations, vendor estimates, GMI estimates and latent statistical similarities separate. The durable business opportunity is trustworthy economic interpretation, correction lineage, disclosed versus estimated weights, multilingual entity resolution and replay—not the assertion that economic graphs are absent from competitors.

## 1. Public and official-source tiers

### US filings

**SEC EDGAR** provides unauthenticated submissions JSON, extracted XBRL and nightly bulk JSON archives. Updates follow filing dissemination; the official page describes typical processing delay below a second for submissions and below a minute for XBRL, with longer peak delays. Critically, the aggregate XBRL APIs include non-custom taxonomy facts applying to the entire filing entity; these are not a complete segment/custom-dimension feed. Read original filing iXBRL and narrative tables for products, segments, customer concentration and named relationships. Automated access requires classified/identified traffic and compliance with a shared ceiling of 10 requests/second regardless of machine count. Prefer bulk archives for bootstrap and bounded incremental accession ingestion. [S01,S02,S03]

**Architecture implication (proposal):** store accession, acceptance/dissemination timestamp where supplied, filed date, period, original taxonomy/context, document hash and exact narrative/table location. Keep those as source metadata until the owner registers the dataset/profile and native-clock crosswalk (§5). Actual first observation remains a system fact, never backdated to a filing period. Restated facts retain their later release.

Current Regulation S-K Item101 §(c)(1)(i) addresses material customer dependency and §(h) supplies smaller-reporting-company alternatives including principal suppliers and dependence on one/few customers. It does not establish a universal named-customer mandate above 10%. [S34] Exact US GAAP customer-disclosure requirements are not asserted here because direct FASB text was not inspected.

**Rights limitation:** the inspected API/access pages establish a supported public collection route, not a blanket clearance of every issuer-authored exhibit, image, third-party text or identifier for commercial redistribution/training. Collect structured factual assertions and limited evidence under a reviewed source-specific policy; separately clear retained corpus and model uses. No assertion here that all EDGAR issuer content is automatically public domain.

### Issuer IR and supply lists: high-value but sparse

Apple's currently linked supplier PDF explicitly covers fiscal 2023 and 98% of direct materials/manufacturing/assembly spend; it supplies supplier names and primary manufacturing locations, not individual supplier spend shares. Its availability on a current 2026 landing page does not make the relationships 2026 observations. [S04] This is an excellent extraction/provenance test and a poor justification for universal supply-chain completeness. A missing company in a later list can mean censoring, scope changes, threshold changes, naming/consolidation changes or termination; absence alone cannot establish termination.

IR contracts, product pages and releases can establish an issuer's assertion about a product or counterparty. They do not necessarily show shipped volumes, exclusive sourcing, substitutability, unit economics or spend attribution. A design win, memorandum, announced purchase or planned capacity must be a different predicate/status from a realized recurring supply relationship. An issuer's quoted customer and a reseller's end customer must not silently become the same economic payer.

### Identity and consolidation

GLEIF's Global LEI Index is free and CC0. Level 1 identifies legal entities; Level 2 reports direct and ultimate **accounting-consolidating parents**. This helps stable IDs and consolidation but is not a universal supplier/customer dataset or exhaustive beneficial-ownership map. [S05,S06] Preserve legal-entity, security/listing, brand, operating subsidiary and parent levels; propagate exposure to a parent only through an explicit consolidation rule. CC0 identity inputs do not remove rights restrictions on a proprietary vendor crosswalk or separately licensed securities identifier.

Companies House documents official bulk company/account/PSC datasets and an API route. [S07] It is a useful UK identity/filing supplement; no claim that it reveals company-to-company trading links. Each national registry needs its own API/license and data-quality review rather than a generic assumption that public company search means a global free graph.

### Mainland China

CSRC announcement No.3 (2025) took effect July 1, 2025. Its official attachment Article 25, printed pp14–15, requires aggregate top-five customer/supplier shares; names and amounts are encouraged generally and required under specified concentration/new-counterparty/severe-dependence, risk-warning and >10% trading-business circumstances. Article 5 preserves state/commercial secrecy exemptions. The rule also calls for material product/region/sales-mode detail and major contract progress. [S08] This supports a Chinese-language filing lane; it does **not** guarantee all counterparties are named.

**Proposed access:** issuer annual/interim reports and official exchange/CNINFO publication routes only after their automated collection and commercial reuse terms are cleared. CSRC disclosure law is evidence of what reports should contain; it is not a license to bulk collect an exchange website. Mainland registry/CNINFO production API rights and quotas were not inspected sufficiently here and remain unknown. Use published company identifiers (including registration IDs where disclosed) and documented licensed crosswalks; translation must preserve the original Chinese entity string and identifier.

### Hong Kong

HKEXnews provides issuer reports/prospectuses. An inspected December 31, 2025 prospectus uses anonymized customer/supplier aliases, revenue/purchase amounts, relationship years and dual-role disclosures; Customer F/Supplier K is shown as both customer and supplier, with distinct flows. [S09] This demonstrates that detailed quantitative disclosure can coexist with censored identity and that edge reversal is not enough to model bidirectional commerce. Do not guess named identities from a prose profile.

IFRS 8 paragraph 34 in the inspected official 2026 issued HTML requires disclosure of revenue amounts and reporting segments when a single external customer contributes at least 10% of revenue, but does not require customer identity. Common-control entities are grouped; government grouping requires economic-integration judgment. [S33] This is direct evidence that customer concentration can remain anonymous. Paragraph34’s historical amendment/effective date is addressed separately from prospective amendments elsewhere in the issued text. HKEX Rule18.40 and annual-report guidance still timed out; their snippets are not inspected proof of current listing rules. Automated HKEXnews reuse permission remains unestablished.

### Canada

Current SEDAR+ official Terms of Use §2 permits limited informational/research/internal copies subject to conditions while prohibiting commercialization, databases and mass distribution; §5 prohibits scraping/automated copying of multiple pieces. §3 recognizes that public posting can lag submission and records can be retracted. [S10] Do not propose a free public-site SEDAR+ crawler as a production GMI corpus.

**Practical lane:** SEC 40-F/6-K/other filings for eligible dual filers, issuer-hosted Canadian IR documents with cleared use, and an explicitly licensed Canadian filing feed or ASC agreement. The same filing sourced elsewhere still needs that carrier's actual rights; this is not permission to evade SEDAR+ restrictions. Public profiles and federal/provincial registries can aid identity but are not supply graphs; specific licensed/API terms remain to be evaluated.

### Macro input-output layer

BEA explains that IO accounts trace industry-to-industry/final-user goods/services flows and that estimates rely on benchmarks, indicators and balancing. [S11] OECD's ICIO current 2025 edition was second-revised mid-January 2026, covers 80 economies plus rest-of-world and years 1995–2022; its extended format separately represents China/Mexico firm heterogeneity. [S12] WIOD 2016 covers 43 countries plus modeled rest-of-world over 2000–2014 and is CC BY 4.0. [S13] These are sector/country tables, not observed firm purchases. OECD data terms allow commercial extraction/adaptation/sharing with attribution unless dataset/third-party restrictions apply; API capacity and availability are discretionary. [S14]

**Architecture implication (proposal):** pin edition, revision, release provenance/precision, monetary units, price convention and industry concordance. Map company segments to industries as explicit uncertain allocations. Keep IO coefficients in a modeled macro-exposure layer. Never label a coefficient-derived supplier/customer as observed, or use a 2026 revision as knowable in a 2019 backtest. WIOD is a historical robustness check, not a current supply map.

## 3. Rights must be machine-readable architecture inputs

**Quartr explicit terms:** US 2026:1 Internal Use §2.2–2.3 restricts use to approved internal purpose; transformed Subscriber Works may be shared under stated conditions. §3.2 prohibits AI/ML training, fine-tuning, evaluation/development; inference input requires an AI service that does not retain/train on inputs. §5.4 requires delete/return on termination, with limited unusable retained backups. [S24] Redistribution §2.2 is for an Approved Product/Authorized Users, includes attribution §3.4, and retains AI and repository restrictions §3.2; §5.4 ends user access and requires deletion/return. [S25] Hence public data-layer marketing is not GMI neural-model-training clearance.

**Bigdata.com default terms:** §§4.2,4.5–4.6 provide one-time ZDR inference grounding and conditional derivative-output survival, while prohibiting underlying-content retention, training/evaluation and systematic or competing-product extraction. Third-party product use needs express authorization. [S32] Consequently a persistent GMI graph is a written-agreement gate; the derivative-output clause alone does not clear it.

**Proposal for every source agreement:** explicitly classify automated collection/quota, raw retention duration, derived facts and graph edges, embeddings, feature vectors, aggregate metrics, inference/third-party LLM transmission, model development/training/evaluation, public UI excerpts, customer API/downloads, geographic hosting, subprocessor use, termination and surviving derived rights. A vendor's internal use restriction may bind a seemingly factual endpoint even when underlying facts appear on issuer websites. Independently sourced public evidence must have independent provenance; never relabel licensed extracted text as public.

Attach source entitlement and agreement/version IDs to documents, assertions, embeddings, snapshots and training manifests. Embeddings/features are not assumed to escape contractual data definitions. Enforce consumer-purpose eligibility before indexing, model calls, graph joins and display. On source expiration/revocation, revoke query access and remove affected indexes/features/snapshots according to contract; retain only expressly permitted audit metadata and independently justified public facts. A downstream feature or trained model with disputed deletion feasibility is a design issue to resolve **before training**.

## 4. Relationship censoring, economic weights and time

These are proposed requirements inferred from the evidence above:

- Separate relationship validity, observation period, publication, vendor receipt, actual ingestion and correction metadata; none defines an independent availability clock. Vendor relationship start/end dates require their own documented meaning; they may be extraction/maintenance dates rather than legal contract boundaries.
- Use source-attested assertions as immutable facts with resolution lineage. A current graph is a materialized view; never overwrite the old assertion or correction that supported a historical answer. “No longer in current feed” remains distinct from explicit termination.
- Distinguish explicit termination, confirmed expiration, superseded product/model, contradicted statement, no recent corroboration, feed omission and identity merge. Where contradictions are merely opposite counterparties reporting different periods/denominators, preserve both rather than marking an error.
- Named source and target, predicate, direction, legal entity scope, product/service, facility/geography, reporting period and quantity definition are independent attributes. Customer F/Supplier K is two commercial flows. Buyer spend share, seller revenue share and COGS share have different denominators. Product generation and plant can materially change relevance.
- Missing quantities are null with reasons. Unknown remainder must survive normalization. Do not make known customers sum to 100%, infer spend from geographic revenue, treat high news count as dependence, or convert a 98%-aggregate supplier list into equal 98/N spend shares.
- Aggregate/anonymized concentration can form a censored-counterparties bucket with bounded exposure, not invented legal entity nodes. Propagating a product link through a corporate parent is an uncertain aggregation operation, not an additional observed sale.
- Macro coefficients, estimated vendor weights and learned GMI weights need method/version, priors, intervals, evaluation population and provenance. A complete graph can be an estimate; calling it observed creates an avoidable information-quality failure.

- Exact quoted annual/setup/minimum/consumption, overage, redistribution and AI-license prices, currency/tax/billing/renewal terms; bounded sample acceptance criteria and exit terms. Until supplied, costs remain unknown.

**Go for internal evidence research** only when access, purpose and limited evidence retention are permitted. **Go for a persistent graph** only with positive database/derived-assertion rights, proved lineage, material target coverage, cleared owner adoption and registered native temporal compatibility for the declared consumer. **Go for embedding/inference** only with processing/subprocessor and retention terms satisfied. **Go for training/evaluation** only with explicit separate rights and a feasible revocation/model-survival policy. **Go for public display/API** only with audience/output/redistribution grants and attribution implemented. A feed may pass one use and fail another. A provider with attractive coverage but absent availability history can serve present-day exploration while failing a historical backtest; do not replace the missing proof with a guessed timestamp.

Graph size or claimed alpha alone cannot select a provider. Failure to supply observed/estimated flags, source traceability, denominator definitions or licensed purpose is a no-go for the affected use.
