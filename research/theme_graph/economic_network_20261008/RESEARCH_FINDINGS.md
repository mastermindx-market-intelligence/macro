### Findings that determine the build

| Finding | Architectural consequence |
|---|---|
| Economic relationship, operating comparability and market dependence are different evidence | Preserve Graph 1/2/3 semantics through ingestion, modeling and display |
| A company pair is often too coarse | Keep product, facility, agreement, legal party, reporting perimeter and time scope |
| Disclosed relationships are selected and often unweighted | Model unknowns and coverage; do not interpret absence as no relationship |
| A true percentage is not necessarily a propagation parameter | Preserve numerator/denominator, units, state and target variable; reject incompatible composition |
| A current graph is not automatically a historical graph | Use native TemporalProfiles and filter-before-derive or accepted replay |
| Source access does not imply training, retention or product rights | Evaluate permission by artifact and consumer purpose |
| Production shocks can propagate, but predictive performance is a separate question | Test operating outcomes and market outcomes independently |
| Neural models can learn disclosure patterns or common factors | Require same-information baselines, difficult negatives and topology/time nulls |

## 2. Research evidence and what it does not prove

The economic literature supports the proposition that actual production relationships can transmit shocks. Input specificity, inventories, qualified alternatives, duration, geography and network structure affect that transmission. The earthquake studies and related production-network research are useful for designing mechanisms and tests. They do not furnish a universal multiplier or establish that a graph model produces contemporary tradable alpha. The economic-evidence report preserves data selection, identification and source-version qualifications. [Carvalho et al. author paper](https://vasco-m-carvalho.github.io/pdfs/JapanEQ.pdf).

Customer momentum is a hypothesis worth testing, not an accepted current signal. The historical Cohen–Frazzini result and later Pinchuk counterevidence concern different sample periods and have coverage and matching limitations. The correct implication is a demanding contemporary evaluation, not automatic adoption or a claim that all information diffusion has disappeared. [Cohen–Frazzini](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf), [Pinchuk](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.11394).

Text-based networks illustrate why source semantics matter. TNIC's own documentation describes product similarity, removes vertical relationships and warns that fiscal-year data are not lagged. VTNIC's authors describe potential vertical relatedness rather than observed trade flows. These are useful Graph 2 or candidate-discovery inputs, with their original meanings retained. [TNIC readme](https://hobergphillips.tuck.dartmouth.edu/idata/Readme_tnic3.txt), [VTNIC documentation](https://faculty.marshall.usc.edu/Gerard-Hoberg/FresardHobergPhillipsDataSite/index.html).

The technical review compared deterministic typed retrieval, direct-link models, nongraph flexible baselines, sector IO, factor/covariance models, relational GNNs, heterogeneous attention, temporal event memory, higher-order representations and GraphRAG. No public benchmark establishes their value for this GMI task. A small relational challenger is preferable to committing immediately to a complex temporal graph transformer.

## 5. The recommended information architecture

Keep source observations, relationship state revisions, magnitude observations, propagation parameters and served hypotheses distinct.

An observation records what a particular source asserted and when. A relationship revision records the native owner's adjudicated state. A magnitude identifies a specific measured or estimated quantity. A propagation parameter maps one operating variable to another under conditions. A served hypothesis records what a consumer actually received and with which authority.

A qualified commercial relationship needs endpoint identity, role, direction, scope, temporal context, evidence and rights. A magnitude additionally needs its numerator, denominator, unit, currency, period and method. A quantitative path needs compatible variables and conditions across every leg. Missing requirements produce typed abstention rather than a guessed coefficient.

Multi-party agreements and complementary production inputs should be preserved as higher-order economic objects where needed. Pairwise views can be derived, but they must retain their common source and allocation references.

Keep authoritative data in native owner stores. Start with existing storage and publication machinery. A graph database is an optional query projection to benchmark if real traversal demand warrants it. GraphRAG can help retrieve evidence or propose candidates; its generated summaries are not relationship truth.

## 6. Sourcing strategy and competitive implications

Use a staged source strategy:

1. Audited issuer and original filing evidence for the first role-specific cases.
2. Existing identity/corporate sources and official IO data for their proper narrow purposes.
3. A frozen diagnostic sample to measure availability, role accuracy, missingness, time precision and analyst cost.
4. A vendor comparison only where it resolves a measured gap and the required rights are explicit.

The sourcing report reviews public and official routes for the U.S., mainland China, Hong Kong, Canada and international coverage, along with FactSet, S&P, Bloomberg, LSEG, Quartr, Fiscal.ai, AlphaSense, RavenPack and Bigdata.com. Vendor counts and advertised features are not independently validated coverage or licensing evidence.

The diagnostic design contains 120 issuers across five listing strata, with 30 difficult cases independently adjudicated. It is specified and costed as a future reference-set exercise; it was not collected in this commission. The present six issuer cases demonstrate why that exercise needs exact definitions.

Rights are a design input. The inspected Bigdata default terms allow limited inference grounding while restricting underlying-content retention, systematic extraction and model training/evaluation; Quartr's inspected standard terms likewise require careful purpose-specific treatment. A written agreement may differ. The research does not obtain or infer one. [Bigdata terms](https://bigdata.com/terms-and-conditions), [Quartr terms index](https://quartr.com/terms-of-service).

The register distinguishes document retention, assertion derivation, embeddings, features, evaluation, training, display, downstream API and termination behavior. A blanket “licensed” flag is inadequate.

## 7. Validation and predictive promotion

The first empirical question should concern one operating outcome at a defined horizon. Compare actual economic paths with direct-link, nongraph identical-information, macro/industry and appropriate IO baselines. Evaluate whether graph topology adds information beyond size, attention, own momentum, common factors and ordinary fundamentals.

Retain three separate higher-level targets: operating transmission, covariance/risk behavior and security returns. A model can succeed at one and fail at another. Public information can be economically important and already priced.

The evaluation specification includes issuer/event/time/sector holdouts, frozen transformations and entity maps, hard and unknown negatives, graph rewiring/direction/layer ablations, event-clustered uncertainty, calibration/abstention, multiple testing, practical effect thresholds, minimum detectable improvement and prospective shadow validation.

Automatic factual admission and predictive promotion are separate gates. Commission4's proposed exact role-plus-direction statistical floor must not be relaxed by an economic-cost argument without explicit owner adjudication. Rights, identity and native temporal eligibility are hard constraints, not uncertainties to average away.

Current K3-D remains a hypothesis object with no grade, rank, size or trade authority and economic_share reserved null. Its four killed-species receipts remain binding throughout the masterplan. Passing an experiment cannot silently change that contract.

It preserves the existing Terminal authentication, generation alignment and closed navigation context.

It starts with owner/schema decisions, source diagnosis and one adapter; proceeds through temporal proof, Graph 1 projection, conditional paths and an actual product vertical slice; and makes operating experiments and neural challengers conditional on data readiness.

Every package identifies existing custody, dependencies, likely seams, acceptance and rollback. Planning estimates are scenarios, not staffing commitments or vendor quotes. Prospective evidence cannot be compressed into an arbitrary delivery date.

The practical first release is a real positive economic path and a real abstention case served through the existing product. That is more useful and more testable than a large graph whose nodes and edges cannot support the meanings users infer from them.
