# Economic relationship casebook and composition rules

Research date: 2026-10-08. Status: researched architecture candidate for Sol adjudication; no production assertions admitted. This casebook is an independent source audit, not a return backtest. Entity names below are source labels. They become joinable canonical identities only after the existing identity owner resolves the relevant legal entity, security and historical interval.

The six issuer cases were selected to challenge different errors: a future product design win, a framework agreement, an aggregate supplier roster, ownership, heterogeneous segment demand, and incompatible accounting measures. They are not a representative sample of global disclosure coverage. The attachment containing the commissioning handoff was unavailable, so these cannot be certified as its prescribed cases.

## C01. Micron → NVIDIA: a product-specific announcement

**Primary evidence.** Micron's 26 February 2024 announcement says HBM3E volume production had begun and its 24GB 8H product would be included in NVIDIA H200, expected to begin shipping in calendar Q2 2024. The source is an issuer announcement, with date precision; this inspection did not establish a public release instant. [Micron original release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2024/Micron-Commences-Volume-Production-of-Industry-Leading-HBM3E-Solution-to-Accelerate-the-Growth-of-AI-02-26-2024/default.aspx), opening paragraphs.

**What can be represented.** A disclosed planned product integration linking the named memory product and accelerator, with Micron as the announcing supplier and NVIDIA as the named product company. The assertion is scoped to the particular configuration and announcement. It is not a measured shipment, recurring revenue share, exclusivity agreement, or a statement about every NVIDIA GPU.

**Contract consequence.** Preserve the original future tense and distinguish the announcement's knowledge date from the intended shipping period. Source-local product references are sufficient for evidence display. Promoting them into canonical product nodes requires the relevant native owner and a reviewed mapping. A generic SUPPLIES projection may only be emitted if its consumer retains the planned status and product scope; otherwise it would overstate the source and must abstain.

**Mechanism to investigate.** More production of a specified accelerator could require more compatible memory. That hypothesis needs a bill of materials, product mix, procurement allocation, qualification constraints, inventories, yield, production schedule and order evidence. No numerical change in Micron revenue follows from the announcement alone. Increased GPU availability could leave system output unchanged if memory or power is the active constraint.

**Falsifiers and next observations.** An updated bill of materials could remove the product; another supplier could receive the incremental order; sufficient inventory could buffer demand; a downstream constraint could prevent deliveries. A later issuer filing or product release must be recorded as new evidence, not used to rewrite what was knowable in February.

**Retrieval correction.** An old Micron prepared-remarks PDF URL surfaced a search snippet concerning wafer intensity, but opening it redirected to the investor overview. The numerical claim from that snippet is excluded. A search result is not an inspected source.

## C02. CATL ↔ Tesla entities: signed does not mean ordered

**Primary evidence.** CATL's announcement 2021-053, dated 28 June 2021, states that an agreement was signed on 25 June with Tesla, Inc. and Tesla (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. It concerns lithium-ion traction batteries for a delivery term of 1 January 2022 through 31 December 2025. Purchases and amounts depend on orders; the framework does not fix them. [Original Chinese exchange filing](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2021-06-28/1210355996.PDF), pages 1–3.

**What can be represented.** An issuer-disclosed commercial framework with a named seller, a buyer party group, product scope, signing date, disclosed date, and contractual term. It is stronger evidence of an economic role than the companies' shared EV theme. It does not identify allocations between the two buyers, completed deliveries, annual sales value, or a minimum order guarantee.

**Contract consequence.** Keep the transaction as a qualified relationship involving multiple parties. Do not create a Cartesian product in which each Tesla entity receives the entire agreement's exposure. Link a pairwise rendering back to the same agreement identifier, with allocation unknown. The two entities are not independent corroborating customers merely because both are named.

**Temporal consequence.** A historical public-information query on 26 June cannot admit the later disclosure merely because the agreement was signed on 25 June. The disclosed term has elapsed by this research date, but its expiry does not establish the end of all CATL–Tesla commerce. A renewal, another contract or continuing orders would be separate evidence. Conversely, the graph cannot silently roll an expired term forward.

**Mechanism and falsifiers.** Tesla vehicle demand may affect battery orders subject to procurement decisions, installed inventories, technology changes, qualification and geographic allocation. The downstream demand series alone is insufficient. A falsification study would compare order or shipment changes against the precommitted mechanism and an alternative procurement mix. The source's stated expectations about future benefit are management assertions, not a measured earnings effect.

**Localization control.** Store the Chinese text span, translation version and original legal names. Currency and quantity fields remain null because the source does not supply fixed contract amounts. Translating an agreement must not strengthen “determined by orders” into “committed revenue.”

## C03. Apple's supplier roster: coverage has a denominator

**Primary evidence.** Apple's supplier list says it represents 98% of direct spend for materials, manufacturing and assembly worldwide in fiscal 2023. The list names suppliers and primary manufacturing locations, including 3M and Advanced Semiconductor Engineering Technology Holding. It does not allocate that spend to individual listed suppliers. [Official supplier-list PDF](https://www.supplychainreports.apple/files/doc_downloads/2024/04/Apple-Supplier-List.pdf), cover and supplier tables.

**What can be represented.** A disclosed supplier-list membership observation, scoped to the issuer's stated fiscal period and categories of spend. Locations are manufacturing locations named by the source; they are not necessarily headquarters or separately resolved facilities.

**Contract consequence.** The 98% belongs to the roster's aggregate coverage measure. It must not be copied to each edge, divided equally across the roster, interpreted as every listed supplier's share of revenue, or applied to Apple's total expenses. Supplier-list coverage is not measured recall of all economic relationships.

**Temporal consequence.** The PDF's directory contains a year and month, but that alone is not a reliable publication clock. This research has established access on the research date. A historical replay needs a source receipt or archive proving earlier availability. Fiscal-year labeling also must not be converted into an invented calendar-year effective interval.

**Mechanism and falsifiers.** A supplier may provide a low-cost input that is difficult to replace, or a high-spend item that is readily substituted. Financial concentration and physical criticality are distinct. A claim of bottleneck status needs a scoped input, qualified alternatives, switching time, capacity and inventory evidence. Two listed suppliers sharing Apple as a customer can be retrieved together, but the roster does not establish a commercial relationship between them.

**Disappearance rule.** Omission from a later roster is an observation about that roster. It can motivate review; it is not affirmative termination evidence. A changed disclosure threshold, corporate renaming, acquisition or product mix could explain it.

## C04. Cameco, Brookfield and Westinghouse: ownership is a separate channel

**Primary evidence.** Cameco announced the closing of the Westinghouse acquisition on 7 November 2023. It described Cameco's interest as 49% and a Brookfield partnership, including institutional partners, as holding the remaining 51%. [Cameco original closing release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-and-brookfield-complete-acquisition-of-westinghouse-electric-company), closing and ownership paragraphs.

**What can be represented.** An ownership-change event and a disclosed equity interest at closing, with the acquiring parties scoped as described. The 51% cannot be assigned entirely to one listed Brookfield security. Cameco's TSX and NYSE listings do not create two independent underlying economic interests.

**Contract consequence.** Equity ownership percentage is a magnitude with an ownership denominator. It is neither the share of Cameco revenue supplied by Westinghouse nor a uranium-price elasticity. An economic read-through requires the native ownership/corporate-action evidence and, where applicable, cash-flow rights and accounting treatment. The acquisition creates no generic supplier edge.

**Mechanism and falsifiers.** Changes in the acquired business may affect the investor through earnings participation, distributions, valuation, financing or contractual obligations. These have different timing and units. Program backlog is not immediately distributable cash. A thesis that uranium spot prices map one-for-one into the investor's revenue fails if sales prices, contract terms, business mix, hedges or exchange rates dominate.

**Currency check.** Comparisons between realized uranium prices and spot benchmarks require consistent currency, delivery basis and period. This casebook deliberately does not compare a Canadian-dollar realized figure with a U.S.-dollar benchmark. An apparently simple ratio can be meaningless when currency metadata is lost.

**Next evidence.** Validate current ownership against a later filing before answering a present-tense ownership query; the 2023 release establishes a historical event. Obtain the appropriate segment or investment accounting facts before attaching an operating magnitude. Every security display must retain the single underlying issuer identity and its listing-specific market data.

## C05. AMD: one industry label can conceal opposing operating outcomes

**Primary evidence.** AMD's 4 February 2025 release reports 2024 Data Center revenue of approximately $12.6bn, up 94%; Client $7.1bn, up 52%; Gaming $2.6bn, down 58%; and Embedded $3.6bn, down 33%. Data Center encompasses both Instinct GPUs and EPYC CPUs. [AMD original full-year results](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1236/amd-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results), segment results.

**What can be represented.** Dated segment observations tied to the reporting issuer, fiscal period and source definition. The release supports substantial operating heterogeneity within a company commonly discussed through a semiconductor or AI theme.

**Contract consequence.** A Data Center total is not a CPU-only revenue measure and cannot be used as a direct allocation to a single micro-theme. A year-over-year growth rate is not an exposure percentage. Rounded segment figures should not be normalized against rounded total revenue as if the resulting weights were exact. Segment hierarchy, intersegment adjustments, changing definitions and rounding need native accounting reconciliation.

**Mechanism and falsifiers.** A semiconductor-demand model should distinguish server, client, gaming and embedded end markets, along with inventory cycles and product mix. A “shared theme implies shared growth” model is challenged by the same-period segment divergence. This is a counterexample to an overly broad mapping, not evidence that a segment-specific forecast would be profitable.

**Temporal consequence.** Full-year facts released in February cannot enter a prior December forecast simply because the fiscal period ended in December. A later recast of segment history is a new version. Historical training must use the original segment definition and disclosure vintage or explicitly qualify a reconstruction.

**Owner boundary.** This research does not change the accepted 68-micro-theme structural taxonomy or duplicate the active sibling qualification study. That study is research under the incumbent GMI owner; it does not itself own admission or prove qualified micro membership. This case supplies evidence and accounting constraints the existing owner can consume; it does not award or revoke membership.

## C06. Microsoft AI investment: stock, flow and commitment are different objects

**Primary evidence.** Microsoft's FY2025 report separately presents $64.551bn of cash property-and-equipment additions, $20.511bn of finance-lease right-of-use asset additions, $32.149bn of outstanding construction commitments and $109.953bn of purchase commitments. These are different statement and note measures; they do not identify specific supplier receipts or isolate every amount as AI. [Microsoft FY2025 report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html), cash-flow statement, material cash requirements, Note 13.

**What can be represented.** Four accounting observations with metric, currency, unit, period/balance date, scope, recognition basis and source locator. Cash outflows can be displayed as a positive spending magnitude only with a transformation recording the original cash-flow sign.

**Contract consequence.** Do not sum cash additions, noncash additions and future commitments into contemporaneous AI demand. Do not multiply that sum by a guessed GPU share and then allocate the result to suppliers. Even a valid capital-investment estimate needs separate conversions from commitments to orders, deliveries, asset recognition, installation, utilization and revenue recognition.

**Mechanism and falsifiers.** More infrastructure investment can support component suppliers, construction and power infrastructure on different schedules. A system-capacity forecast must consider the limiting input and commissioning readiness. Demand may be shifted between owned assets and leases, moved across periods, or delayed by site constraints. A narrative increase in capex does not identify the incremental recipient.

**Research use.** The typed accounting distinction is sufficient for useful product behavior now: explain which reported measures the user is looking at and why a supplier allocation is unknown. A quantitative propagation model remains blocked until the missing allocations and timing are evidenced or declared as scenario assumptions.

## C07. Earthquake evidence: mechanism support, not a turnkey company graph

The final-lineage author version of Carvalho, Nirei, Saito and Tahbaz-Salehi documents direct and indirect production-network transmission after the Great East Japan Earthquake. Its aggregate real-GDP-growth estimate is model-dependent. Earlier draft gross-output estimates refer to a different quantity. [Author paper](https://vasco-m-carvalho.github.io/pdfs/JapanEQ.pdf), data section, robustness and aggregate calculation.

The companion economic-evidence report examines identification, geography, pre-trends, reporting selection and related studies. The appropriate transfer into GMI is a testable mechanism: physical disruption can travel through production relationships when substitution and buffers are insufficient. The study does not supply a freely reusable current issuer-level dataset, a universal multiplier, or a forecast of security returns. Several earthquake studies concern the same event and cannot count as independent event replications.

## 1. The composition problem: a real edge is not a transferable coefficient

For companies A, B and C, consider two observed percentages:

| Observation | Numerator | Denominator | Why multiplication fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| A derives 20% of sales from B | A's recognized sales to B | A's total recognized sales | Describes A's customer concentration |
| B buys 50% of an input from C | B's purchases of that input from C | B's purchases of that input | Describes B's procurement allocation |

Their product is not C's exposure to A, A's dependence on C, or the sensitivity of A revenue to C output. The buyer/seller orientation, input scope, accounting basis and required target variable differ. Both percentages can be true while the proposed chain coefficient is undefined.

A composable propagation parameter is instead an explicitly defined mapping between variables, with a specified state, horizon and assumptions. For example, a derivative from changes in B's delivered units to changes in A's output units can compose with a derivative from C's available input units to B's delivered units only if the intermediate variable, unit, timing and scope match. These derivatives must be estimated or declared as scenario parameters. They are not inferred solely from observed commercial edges.

For a local linear approximation, let x be a vector of named operating variables and u a specified external shock. A structural model might posit:

$$\Delta x = J(s,t)\Delta x + B(s,t)u.$$

The Jacobian J is conditional on state s and time t. Its entries carry input/output units. Solving this system requires identified equations and an invertible I−J; interpreting an infinite path expansion also requires convergence, such as spectral radius below one. The graph alone supplies neither condition. A return-correlation matrix, a revenue-share matrix and a neural-attention matrix are not interchangeable with J.

This formulation is an analytic option, not a recommendation to construct a global simultaneous model immediately. Start with audited direct mechanisms; expand only when additional paths improve a defined outcome against simpler baselines.

## 2. A synthetic bottleneck and complementarity witness

The accompanying composition witness uses deliberately hypothetical capacities. It makes no claim about Micron, NVIDIA, Microsoft or any current market. All capacities have already been converted, by assumption, into the same units: feasible complete systems per period. Raw HBM bits, power MW and GPU units cannot be compared without such conversion.

Let output be the minimum of demand, GPU-supported capacity, memory-supported capacity and power-supported capacity. The baseline values are 120, 100, 80 and 110, respectively.

| Scenario | Output | Change from baseline | Active constraint |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Baseline | 80 | 0 | Memory |
| GPU capacity increases by 40 | 80 | 0 | Memory |
| Memory capacity increases by 40 | 100 | 20 | GPU |
| Both increase by 40 | 110 | 30 | Power |

The joint increase produces 10 systems beyond the sum of the two separate increases. This is an interaction under the assumed production function. It illustrates why additive “beneficiary scores” can miss complements and why bottleneck identity can migrate. It does not estimate an elasticity or validate this production function for real data.

The witness also compares substitution assumptions. If a substitute is already qualified and has spare capacity, an outage need not transmit fully. If qualification takes longer than the disruption horizon, the same nominal alternative may not be available. Economic alternatives must be time- and capability-qualified; a shared theme is insufficient.

## 3. Double counting and common causes

One final-demand event can reach a company through multiple documented paths. Those paths are alternative explanations of the same originating change unless the model proves distinct allocations. Summing all paths can count the same dollar repeatedly. The default product shows the paths and a shared origin, with quantitative aggregation withheld.

An event-allocation ledger within the existing hypothesis computation should identify originating event, economic unit, accounting perimeter, destination variable, interval and allocation set. A pairwise projection of a multi-party agreement must retain its common transaction reference. Cycles are not additional evidence or infinite value; a repeated edge is not an independent shock. Where a conserved quantity is being allocated, allocations must reconcile to the relevant total and unknown remainder.

Common-cause evidence also matters. Two firms can react to rates, commodity prices, currency, an industry cycle or a shared customer without trading directly with each other. Graph 2 and Graph 3 can explain these alternatives, but they cannot supply missing Graph 1 evidence.

## 4. Required claim ladder

| Level | Permitted claim | Evidence necessary | What remains unproven |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source observation | “The issuer stated this in this document” | Original source, locator, version, permitted use | Factual accuracy beyond the document |
| Relationship state | “A scoped relationship is supported at this interval” | Entity/role/scope adjudication; conflicting evidence retained | Material magnitude and transmission |
| Mechanism hypothesis | “This shock could affect this operating variable through these conditions” | Source-backed path, explicit conditions, alternatives and falsifiers | Effect size and predictive gain |
| Scenario | “Under these parameter assumptions, this outcome follows” | Unit-consistent model, parameter provenance and sensitivity | Forecast calibration |
| Forecast | “This model predicts a specified target at a specified horizon” | Frozen PIT inputs, same-information baselines, independent evaluation | Live economic value |
| Promoted consumer | “This output has this accepted authority” | Existing owner's decision, prospective evidence and permitted rights | Generalization beyond accepted scope |

The present commission reaches source observations, researched mechanism specifications and isolated logic witnesses. It does not promote a forecast or change a production authority boundary.

## 5. Machine-readable evidence and reproducibility

The research fixtures under witnesses/ contain source-linked cases and synthetic assumptions in separate sections. They deliberately leave undisclosed magnitudes null. Running the scripts verifies temporal and logical invariants, including rejected composition and bottleneck migration. A passing result establishes that the specified behavior was executed; it is not an empirical estimate of global extraction quality, causal effect or market performance.

For a later empirical program, link each case to the existing native source receipt, identity resolution, dataset TemporalProfile, relationship revision, compiler version, rights decision and served artifact. Until those receipts exist, a source-inspected case should remain a research example. Do not mint canonical IDs or backdate system knowledge to make a fixture appear production-ready.
