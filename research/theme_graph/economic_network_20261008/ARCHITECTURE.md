# Architecture: evidence-backed economic intelligence within Mastermind GMI

Research candidate, 2026-10-08. This document specifies an implementable architecture for adjudication. It does not adopt schemas, appoint owners, release held work, or authorize predictive promotion.

## 1. Architectural decision

Evolve the existing Theme Fabric into the navigation and projection surface of an economic intelligence network. Keep authoritative facts with their existing native domain owners, resolve identity and temporal eligibility through the incumbent Data OS interfaces, and compose explanations through the existing Economic Propagation/K3-D lineage. Preserve the sole incumbent ThemeState producer/publication path; this census does not establish a new naturally served release. Add evidence and differentiated contracts before adding a neural model.

The important gap is role-specific, scoped and temporally defensible economic evidence. It is not the absence of a generic graph engine. Commission4 already specifies much of the native-fact-to-Graph1 approach, propagation parameters, event exposure paths, substitution conditions, graph ablations and ownership gates. This report refines that baseline with source-backed cases, a concrete composition rule, executed temporal counterexamples, a rights-to-consumer contract, and a sequenced adoption plan. [Commission4 candidate at inspected head](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/4f4e80ca9ba9309ccb8bb211ff4d0a1ecff00272/research/economic_propagation/COMMISSION4_EFFECTIVE_DATED_ECONOMIC_EXPOSURE_SUPPLY_CHAIN_GRAPH_2026-10-04.md).

The semantic outcome should be a system that can answer:

1. What economic role connects these entities, according to which original evidence?
2. Which product, facility, customer, market or operating variable is implicated?
3. What was effective and what was knowable at the requested time?
4. Which mechanism could transmit the event, and what conditions could block or reverse it?
5. Which quantities are measured, estimated, assumed or unknown?
6. What does a validated model add beyond the evidence and simple alternatives?

Answering the first five questions is a useful product even if no return-prediction model succeeds.

## 2. Three evidence families, multiple economic layers

The three-graph separation is semantic, not a requirement for three databases.

| Family | Represents | Eligible examples | Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Graph 1: economic relationships | Documented roles, exposures and dependencies | Scoped supplier/customer relationship, ownership cash-flow change, product/facility dependency, program role | A quantitative multiplier merely from adjacency |
| Graph 2: fundamental and thematic comparability | Shared vocabulary, products, operating characteristics or research groupings | Theme membership, product competition, segment KPI comparability | An undisclosed commercial transaction |
| Graph 3: market behavior | Measured statistical or market-state relationships | Residual comovement, event response, attention state under its lawful owner | Contractual direction or causal transmission |

Within Graph 1, the following views can coexist:

| Economic layer | Useful entities and relationships | Native evidence | Required qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate perimeter | Legal entities, subsidiaries, securities, ownership and consolidation | Registry/corporate-action/filing facts | Legal entity versus listed security; effective interval; economic-rights scope |
| Production and commerce | Products, suppliers, buyers, distributors, licensors, contractual parties | Filings, agreements, issuer rosters, licensed relationships | Role, direction, product/service, contract versus execution, party group |
| Physical capability | Facilities, capacity, qualified alternatives, logistics, inventories | Facility disclosures, specifications, permits, operating data | Unit, location, utilization, available capacity, qualification and horizon |
| Demand and expenditure | End markets, final demand, procurement, sector flows | Segment disclosures, spending data, official IO | Denominator, reporting perimeter, recognition basis, vintage |
| Events and catalysts | Earnings, guidance, disruptions, policy, program funding, corporate actions | Existing native event owners | Event identity, surprise/expectation distinction, effective versus public time |
| Operating mechanisms | Conditional effects on units, orders, prices, margins, capacity | Audited path plus named parameters | Target variable, units, state, lag, conditions, falsifiers |

These are views over owned facts. They do not create a new identity service or appoint GMI as owner of all economic data. A product or facility without a canonical owner may remain a source-local evidence object. It is not thereby eligible as a universal join key.

## 3. Granularity: preserve the source's object before projecting a pair

### 3.1 Five distinct records

| Record | Identity basis | Content | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source observation | Native source receipt plus locator/version | What a particular source asserted | Evidence, with source and extraction limitations |
| Relationship state revision | Canonical role/scope key plus revision | Adjudicated current or historical belief, including contradictions | Native relationship owner after adoption |
| Magnitude observation | Metric, numerator/denominator, scope, period, source | Revenue share, procurement amount, ownership interest, capacity or range | Native metric owner; not a universal edge weight |
| Propagation parameter/path | Existing Commission4 parameter and path lineage | Conditional variable mapping and the supporting path | Research or accepted model scope only |
| Served hypothesis/model receipt | Existing compiler/version/input/decision references | What the consumer actually received | Exactly the authority accepted for that consumer |

An extraction confidence is not a relationship-existence probability. A relationship-existence assessment is not a transmission coefficient. A backtest score is not permission to display licensed content. Keeping these distinct avoids replacing uncertainty with a single misleading number.

### 3.2 Relationship key and source-local scope

A candidate relationship key must incorporate the native endpoint identities, role, product/service scope, economic perimeter and relevant agreement or facility where necessary. Two supplier disclosures with different products or contract terms must not collapse merely because company endpoints match.

The schema adoption should distinguish the relationship key from the observation identifier and state revision. Repeated announcements of the same contract create new observations of an existing relationship; they do not create independent edges or extra evidence by count. Corrections append a revision. The original observation remains available under its historical knowledge conditions.

Named “Customer A” labels are document-scoped aliases unless the source or an accepted identity resolution proves continuity. Two issuers' Customer A are unrelated placeholders. A later identification is a newly knowable mapping, not a license to rewrite the historical graph.

### 3.3 Multi-party and higher-order objects

Framework agreements, joint ventures, financing syndicates, production recipes and shared programs are often higher-order objects. Preserve the agreement/program/recipe and participant roles, then derive pairwise views only where meaning is retained.

A contract naming a seller and two buyer entities does not disclose a 50/50 allocation or two full-sized contracts. A bill of materials is a set of complementary inputs, not a collection of independent additive benefits. Multiple firms on the same program may have separate roles with no direct contract between them.

Use qualified relations or factor-like intermediate objects within the native owner's representation. A graph rendering can show these as a shared agreement or production step. It should never manufacture all possible bilateral commercial links. W3C PROV-O's qualified relations provide a useful interoperability analogy for attaching participants and attributes; they are not a replacement for Mastermind's provenance authority. [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), qualified relations.

### 3.4 Orthogonal eligibility axes

The following axes must remain separately queryable:

- Identity: resolved, ambiguous, conflicting, source-local.
- Relationship evidence: disclosed role, strongly evidenced role, candidate, contradicted, retracted.
- Effective state: planned, in term, observed activity, ended scoped contract, uncertain continuity.
- Coverage: disclosed subset, sampled source, unavailable region, unknown denominator.
- Freshness: current for the intended question, historical only, stale, superseded.
- Magnitude: measured, vendor-estimated, model-estimated, scenario-assumed, undisclosed.
- Temporal eligibility: exact native proof, conservative date-only eligibility, historical reconstruction, unavailable.
- Rights: allowed, restricted, denied or unresolved for each intended use.

These labels are contract requirements for owner adjudication, not additions silently inserted into a closed production enum. If a consumer cannot retain a material distinction, projection must be withheld or the accepted contract version must change.

A schema validator can check shape and enumerations; it cannot establish the truth of a statement. SHACL offers a comparable separation between constraint validation and knowledge content. ## 5. Time: world occurrence, public knowledge and actual system use

### 5.1 Incumbent source of truth

Data OS owns the TemporalProfile-to-clock mapping. At the inspected pin, BARS, REVISABLE_RELEASE, SNAPSHOT_SERIES and EVENT resolve publication before ingestion where provided; INTELLIGENCE uses served_at; DERIVED has no PIT clock and rejects PIT reads. The helper requires timezone-bearing instants and rejects plain dates and naive datetimes. [Pinned temporal module](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/7e6ce338a9f88aaa60834bdeaf5ee2fe90dab477/lib/dataos/temporal.py).

The temporal witness imports that exact module and executes 23 cases. It does not replace the helper. Its publication-before-later-ingestion case demonstrates public knowability under the incumbent profile, not proof that Mastermind actually possessed the information earlier.

### 5.2 Two different research questions

**Public-information counterfactual:** Could the information have been obtained at time t under the registered source's publication promise? This may support a reconstructed historical study, with explicit access and reconstruction qualifications.

**Historical system replay:** What did the actual system know, resolve, compute and serve at time t? This additionally needs the historical native source receipt, entity mapping, computation version and served artifact/replay lineage. A document fetched today and published years ago is not an actual historical system observation.

Both are legitimate research questions. Their results must not be mixed. The source-specific public clock remains canonical; actual system availability is established through existing receipts, not by inventing a new universal “maximum of all timestamps” clock.

### 5.3 Exact compatibility gap

The held K3-D contract declares date-grain cutoff semantics: evidence known intraday on the cutoff date counts as knowable at that date. ThemeState's inspected helpers preserve date precision and fail same-day date evidence as KNOWLEDGE_TIME_UNPROVEN for an intraday cutoff. These are different contracts.

Consequently, the present K3-D asof date cannot support an exact 10:30 UTC replay claim. A consumer must either label its daily semantics accurately or await a reviewed contract amendment that uses native instant proof. Do not silently reinterpret a date as midnight, end of day, exchange close or release time.

The witness executes Data OS. ThemeState and K3-D behavior is explicitly source-traced, not executed in the witness. No held code was modified.

### 5.4 Filter before derive

At an exact request cutoff:

1. Resolve the requested entity through the existing historical identity interface.
2. Read each native dataset under its registered TemporalProfile and entitlement.
3. Preserve source precision; reject unavailable or ambiguous same-day instant proof.
4. Select eligible observation and relationship revisions without reading later corrections.
5. Derive the requested graph projection and hypothesis from those eligible inputs.
6. Return the existing computation/served receipt, exact input revisions and authority.

A current DERIVED graph artifact cannot be made historical by attaching an old asof string. Either rederive from correctly filtered native inputs or use an accepted historical replay/served artifact. This applies to embedding features, graph topology, entity resolution and labels as much as to price data.

### 5.5 Corrections, dates and markets

Use append-only correction lineage. Closing a contractual interval does not delete the earlier belief. Store inclusive/exclusive interval conventions explicitly. Do not infer an exact day from “Q2,” convert a fiscal label into a calendar interval without supporting facts, or backdate a correction to the original document date.

Trading calendars are separate from source publication calendars. A post-close earnings announcement must not influence a pre-close trade. Cross-listed securities require their own sessions and currencies. Holiday sessions, stale quotes, corporate actions, delistings and security remappings must be resolved by the incumbent market-data owner before financial evaluation.

## 6. Mechanism engine: variables, conditions and falsifiers

### 6.1 Direction belongs to the mechanism

Goods flow supplier→customer. A customer-demand shock can propagate in the opposite direction, toward suppliers. An input-cost shock can affect a buyer's unit cost, selling price, volume and margin with different signs. The same commercial relationship therefore cannot carry one universal signed coefficient.

Use the existing propagation_parameter and event_exposure_path concepts. A parameter should identify input variable, output variable, unit conversion, horizon, state, functional form, estimation or assumption source, uncertainty and validity domain. The path should reference actual Graph 1 legs and the parameter application, not infer a multiplier from edge count.

The casebook supplies the composition counterexample: two correctly measured percentages with different denominators need not multiply into any meaningful exposure. The 18-case composition witness executes this refusal, state/unit/period mismatch cases, null propagation, bottleneck migration, conditional substitution, inventory buffering and duplicate-allocation accounting.

### 6.2 A mechanism record

For each candidate mechanism, retain:

- Originating event and affected economic variable.
- Target issuer or native economic object and named operating outcome.
- Admissible role-specific paths with evidence and dates.
- Conditional direction and horizon, including “mixed” where appropriate.
- Transmission conditions: input specificity, allocation, inventory, qualification, spare capacity, switching time, contract terms, pass-through, financing and geography.
- Parameter provenance and unknown dimensions.
- Alternative explanations, including common macro/industry factors and an already-anticipated event.
- Falsifiers, observation schedule, expiry and the next evidence that would change the conclusion.
- Existing authority state: hypothesis, scenario or accepted forecast, with no automatic progression.

The new information should be the auditable economic chain and its limits. A long narrative generated from company names adds no evidential value.

### 6.3 Bottlenecks and substitutability

Bottleneck status is a conditional property of a production or delivery system. It needs a target output, relevant demand, compatible input/capacity units, usable inventories, qualified alternatives and a horizon. A supplier's low revenue share can coexist with critical physical dependence. A high-spend supplier can be easy to replace.

The source-backed graph can show evidence of a dependency even when its quantitative criticality is unknown. Display “dependency documented; bottleneck not established” rather than a made-up severity score. Supplier assertions of tight capacity are evidence about management's statement; independent operational measurements may support or challenge the condition.

The synthetic minimum-input witness demonstrates complementarity and bottleneck migration. A real implementation must test the production-function choice against alternatives, including flexible substitution, allocation priorities and demand constraints. The nonlinear mechanism is not established by the synthetic result.

### 6.4 Paths, cycles and double counting

Retain the existing conservative two-hop research default. A longer path requires a specific incremental information claim and validation. Hop count is neither confidence nor economic importance.

Track originating shock, allocation set, quantity, accounting perimeter and destination variable. Do not sum duplicate views of one transaction. Do not collapse distinct flows merely because their origin matches. Where a conserved flow is allocated, reconcile known allocations plus an explicit unknown remainder. Where feedback requires a system model, solve that specified model; repeated graph traversal is not a multiplier.

Joint ventures, internal transfers and consolidated subsidiaries require perimeter controls. Counting both subsidiary and consolidated parent revenue can double count. A sale recognized at multiple production stages is not additive final demand.

### 6.5 Uncertainty without false precision

Use intervals only where their meaning is clear: disclosed range, scenario envelope, statistical interval or identified bound. Do not label a scenario range “95% confidence.” Do not multiply extraction confidence scores across paths unless they are calibrated probabilities and the dependence structure is justified.

Unknown edge magnitudes remain null. Source absence is not a zero. Corroboration is stronger when sources are independent; an issuer press release copied by a vendor and repeated in a transcript may share one underlying assertion.

## 7. Neural architecture: an optional learned consumer

The neural question is not whether a model can be trained on a graph. It is whether typed, temporally eligible relationships add useful information for a specified task under actual coverage and rights constraints.

### 7.1 Candidate ladder and selected starting point

| Candidate | Appropriate role | Principal limitations | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic typed retrieval and conditional rules | Evidence discovery, explanations, quality diagnostics | Does not infer a calibrated effect size | Build first within existing owners |
| Direct-link linear or hierarchical model | Interpretable operating outcome baseline | Nonlinearity and heterogeneity may remain | Mandatory baseline |
| Nongraph boosted/flexible model with identical data | Tests value of topology beyond feature richness | Can still overfit or leak | Mandatory comparator |
| Sector IO / factor / covariance model | Sector flow, common shock and risk baselines | Aggregation, vintage and firm allocation limits | Retain by target |
| R-GCN-style relational encoder | Typed relation representation and candidate discovery | Sparse relation types; static snapshot unless explicitly temporal | First neural candidate if graph data warrants |
| HGT-style heterogeneous attention | Different node/edge types and context-dependent representations | More parameters; attention is not causal explanation; temporal encoding alone is not PIT | Conditional challenger |
| TGN-style event memory | Frequent timestamped interaction streams | Filing events are sparse/censored; memory state and batch ordering can leak | Only if an eligible event stream supports the task |
| Higher-order/factor representation | Agreements, recipes and complementary inputs | More demanding labels and identification | Preserve data structure; add learning only after simpler tests |
| GraphRAG | Source-grounded discovery, summarized community context, proposal assistance | Default merging may lose role/scope/time; summaries are not authoritative facts | Restricted retrieval/proposal role |
| End-to-end learned causal/return graph | No admissible shortcut to production authority | Identification, leakage, coverage and killed-species boundaries | Hold; no causal-alpha resurrection |

R-GCN uses relation-specific transformations with parameter sharing; HGT parameterizes interactions using source type, relation and target type and supplies relative temporal encoding; TGN models sequences of events with memory and graph operators. Their original papers support these architectural descriptions, not a securities-performance claim. [R-GCN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.06103), [HGT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.01332), [TGN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.10637).

### 7.2 Proposed learned representation

If the data-quality gates pass, start with separate encoders or explicitly typed channels for:

1. Graph 1 observed economic roles, scoped magnitudes and source coverage.
2. Graph 2 product/segment/theme comparability.
3. Graph 3 lawful market-state features.
4. Exogenous macro/event covariates from their native owners.

Fuse representations only for the chosen target. Retain layer masks, unknown magnitudes, source-selection indicators and effective/knowledge-time eligibility. No encoder output can write back an observed economic edge. Predicted links remain candidates for the incumbent owner's review process, with evidence search requirements; a new automated review queue has not been established by this census.

A small relational encoder with one or two message-passing steps is the sensible first neural challenger. Use separate decoder heads for relation discovery and operating outcomes. Return-risk or covariance tasks must have their own target contract and validation. Avoid a multi-task loss that silently optimizes an easy source-label or popularity task while claiming operating predictive value.

Parameter sharing can help rare relations but also blur economically different roles. Review performance separately by source, jurisdiction, relation type, sector, size and newly observed entity. The model must outperform identical-information baselines; adding licensed data only to the GNN would not isolate graph value.

### 7.3 Temporal memory and leakage

TGN's original training discussion explicitly warns that updating memory with an interaction before predicting that interaction leaks information. GMI needs stricter controls because disclosure time, economic event time and actual system availability differ. Memory updates must follow the admitted knowledge sequence, with deterministic treatment of equal timestamps and revision events. Reset or reconstruct state at fold boundaries; do not carry future test state into an earlier validation query. [TGN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.10637), Section 3.2.

A relative-time embedding is not an eligibility filter. HGT-style temporal encoding cannot make a future neighbor safe. Sample neighbors only from the cutoff-eligible graph. Normalize and tune using training data; freeze taxonomies, identity maps, source snapshots and feature transformations by vintage.

Large language models introduce another historical contamination route. A current model may know later company outcomes. For extraction, require outputs grounded in provided spans, preserve citations and reject unsupported additions. For retrospective prediction, entity masking and source-only extraction are diagnostics, not proof the pretrained model is historically clean. Use a documented representation vintage or prospective evaluation after the model release before making strict predictive claims.

### 7.4 Negative sampling and missingness

Undisclosed relationships are not necessarily negative relationships. A model can learn disclosure propensity, company size or vendor attention instead of economic connectivity.

Use adjudicated false candidates where available; otherwise separate positive-unlabeled discovery from supervised relation validation. Candidate pools must match endpoint types, plausible markets and entities knowable at the cutoff. Include hard nonlinks, related-but-wrong-role pairs and historical termination cases when independently evidenced.

TGB 2.0 demonstrates that negative sampling substantially changes measured link-prediction difficulty; simple approaches remain competitive in some settings. This is an evaluation warning, not evidence of financial accuracy. Its type-aware pool construction should not be copied if it consults future entity appearances; GMI's pool must be PIT-known. [TGB 2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.09639v2), evaluation protocol and Table 9.

### 7.5 Promotion discipline

Separate four questions: extraction accuracy, relation discovery, operating transmission prediction, and market outcome prediction. Success at one does not answer the next. Statistical significance alone is insufficient: require a precommitted practical improvement, robust uncertainty, calibrated selective coverage and lawful operating cost.

The economic-evidence report gives the complete proposed protocol, including same-information baselines, temporal/entity/event/sector splits, edge/direction/layer nulls, correlated-event uncertainty, multiple-testing control and prospective shadow evidence. Current K3-D authority remains unchanged even if a research result is encouraging.

## 8. Source processing and evidence admission

### 8.1 Processing sequence

Acquire only through an approved source route. Preserve permitted original evidence and its native receipt. Parse the document and tables, retain source units/language, propose structured observations, resolve candidate entities, validate role/direction/scope/date/quantity, and route ambiguous cases to the existing reviewer. Only owner-admitted records project into ordinary economic views.

Documents, retrieved passages and vendor descriptions are data, not instructions for the system. Their contents cannot appoint an owner, grant rights, set authoritative clocks or trigger privileged actions. Extraction should propose evidence-linked fields; the existing owner and deterministic eligibility checks control admission.

Each adapter must distinguish:

- A disclosed fact from the issuer's expectation or promotional claim.
- A signed framework from an order, delivery or recognized revenue.
- A negative assertion from omission.
- Parent from subsidiary, brand from legal entity, security from issuer.
- Current measurement from a future commitment, capacity plan or historical comparative.
- A named supplier from a lender, trustee, agent or generic agreement party.

Automated extraction should fail with typed reasons rather than fill missing fields from model memory. Chinese, English and translated evidence should resolve to the same scoped observation, not separate corroborating edges.

### 8.2 Admission tiers

Begin with human-reviewed source cases. Evaluate proposed automation on an independently adjudicated sample. Retain Commission4's proposed one-sided 95% exact lower confidence bound of at least 95% for exact role-plus-direction per relevant stratum, unless Sol and the native owner explicitly adopt a different rule. This is a proposed automatic-admission baseline, not an already achieved result.

Precision, verified-support yield, unknown rate and recall within a defined reference set must be reported separately. A model can appear precise by abstaining on most hard cases. A 120-issuer sourcing diagnostic is not a proof that all source/role strata meet an automatic-admission gate.

Rights, canonical identity and temporal eligibility are hard constraints for each use. Statistical uncertainty about a coefficient can be modeled; uncertain permission to train or display cannot be averaged away.

## 9. Persistence, computation and operational design

### 9.1 Selected storage approach

Use the existing native datasets and append-only evidence/state lineage as authoritative. Produce deterministic graph and consumer projections through existing publication paths. Start with the incumbent storage and batch/query machinery; do not introduce another production graph database solely because the research object is called a graph.

Relational storage with qualified tables and temporal range constraints is a reasonable implementation option if the native owner chooses it; immutable columnar snapshots can remain useful for reproducible research. PostgreSQL documents range types and exclusion constraints, but the actual deployed version and current owner stack need inspection before DDL is adopted. [PostgreSQL range documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/rangetypes.html).

A property-graph engine can be evaluated as a disposable query projection if traversal workload, latency and cost justify it. It must rebuild from owned facts and preserve source/revision/rights references. Choosing it as the new authoritative store would add migration, duplication and temporal-governance work before evidence quality has been established.

RDF/PROV/SHACL can aid interoperability and constraint exchange. They do not require a wholesale migration, and semantic-web validity is not proof of economic truth. Select the minimum representation needed by the real consumer workload.

### 9.2 Benchmark before database expansion

Benchmark direct neighborhood lookup, two-hop filtered paths, evidence retrieval, correction replay, rights-filtered queries, and historical rederivation. Use actual admitted graph sizes and realistic degree skew. Measure p50/p95 latency, peak memory, rebuild time, cache hit rate and cost; inspect pathological high-degree “customer,” fund or country nodes.

Set service objectives jointly with the product owner. Candidate objectives should remain explicit assumptions until measured. Do not invent current production throughput or imply a few source files constitute a global capacity benchmark.

Pagination must preserve the query and snapshot identity. Truncation must be visible and must not be reported as complete coverage. Cache keys include canonical subject, query cut, purpose/entitlement scope, owner revisions, compiler and accepted representation version.

### 9.3 Rights through derived artifacts

Source rights must follow documents, assertions, embeddings, features, model artifacts, displays and exports. A permission to run an inference query is not necessarily permission to persist a corpus, evaluate a model or train on it.

Mixed-source results require purpose-specific entitlement checks for every contributing source. Restricted evidence should not leak through counts, snippets or reconstructed summaries. If allowed facts can be served independently, rederive the answer from that lawful subset and show its resulting coverage. Do not merely hide a citation while retaining a prohibited contribution.

Track revocation and deletion obligations through derived artifacts. Where a model's training right expires or is revoked, consult the applicable contract and native owner; do not claim that removing a document from a vector index unlearns it from model weights. Disable or replace affected artifacts when required.

### 9.4 GraphRAG boundary

GraphRAG's documented indexing flow links documents/text units, extracts entities and relationships, merges entities by title/type and relationships by endpoints, and produces community summaries. This is useful for retrieval but can collapse economically important distinctions unless customized. Optional claim extraction is not a substitute for the native assertion contract. [GraphRAG dataflow](https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/index/default_dataflow/).

Use GraphRAG to find relevant source passages or propose questions over an authorized corpus. Answers must cite original observations and retain the exact permitted authority. A community summary, model memory or generated relationship description never becomes the canonical relationship record.

## 10. Failure behavior and observability

The useful failure response is specific enough to direct the next action:

| Condition | Consumer behavior | Owner action |
|---|---|---|
| Identity ambiguous | Show source-local evidence, no canonical path/aggregation | Identity review |
| Supplier role not supported | Show actual role or insufficient-role abstention | Source/role adjudication |
| Same-day date lacks instant | Reject exact intraday claim; offer accurately labeled historical evidence | Temporal proof or contract review |
| DERIVED-only history | No PIT answer | Native rederivation or accepted replay |
| Magnitude undisclosed | Show relationship; quantitative impact null | Evidence collection or explicit scenario |
| Conflicting assertions | Show conflict and versions | Native owner adjudication |
| Rights unresolved for purpose | Hold the affected computation/display | Contract/entitlement review |
| Source expired or stale | Show historical state; withhold current-use inference | Refresh/review |
| Query truncates | Indicate incomplete result and continuation | Query/scale work |
| Model outside validated scope | Evidence-only answer or typed abstention | Revalidation |

Monitor evidence freshness, unresolved identities, contradictory revisions, source concentration, missing denominators, field-level rights blocks, failed temporal reads, projection loss, and consumer mismatches. These belong in existing operational and research receipts. This commission does not create a new scheduler, ledger or control plane.

## 11. What would change this recommendation

The architecture would need revision if a native owner already has a richer role-specific dataset and accepted contract than the inspected sources show; if current vendor rights make persistent graph construction feasible at materially lower total cost; if real workload benchmarks demand a graph-native query projection; or if a properly controlled model shows robust practical improvement beyond simpler same-information baselines.

Conversely, weak coverage, unresolvable licensing, poor role accuracy, temporal reconstruction dependence or no operating forecast gain should narrow the product to evidence retrieval and scoped explanations. That remains a completed useful capability rather than a failed prediction project.

The remaining decisions are concrete: native relationship custody, loss-aware projection version, daily versus instant semantics, minimum source rights, automatic-admission policy and the admissible first experiment. They are enumerated in the implementation masterplan for Sol's adjudication.
