# GMI B - OBSERVATIONS AND INTEGRATION

Operation: `gmi-theme-research-health-longevity-wearables-20260924-001`. Research proposals for the existing GMI owners; none is an enrolled interface, new ingestion service or live product capability.

## Common contract requirements, not a new native contract

Every proposed observation must preserve the user question, measured population, unit/currency, numerator/denominator or explicit non-applicability, source locator/version, applicable period, source publication and first system observation, source dependence, rights, uncertainty and correction lineage. Reported fact, attributed assertion, house interpretation, scenario and unvalidated forecast remain distinct. Missing is not zero; incompatible cohorts do not become commensurate because a derived ratio can be calculated.

The current examples have day-grain system observation. Do not manufacture intraday ordering. An issuer filing date, a policy effective date, a study's old patient-data window and today's ingestion are four different clocks. Source retrieval is not authority to acquire individual medical records, redistribute a paid corpus, or publish a protected current product body.

## Proposed observation profiles

### O1. Observed monitoring continuity

**User question:** Does the initiated cohort actually obtain usable readings, and where are interruptions?

**Population and observable:** Exact sensor/version, payer, jurisdiction and initiation cohort; retain early abandoners rather than require future survival.

**Measurement distinction:** Calendar coverage, usable reading-time coverage and interruption distribution are distinct.

**Numerator:** Days with qualifying readings; valid reading-minutes; separately, gap events and duration.

**Denominator:** Eligible observed calendar days/minutes for the declared initiated cohort; define death, disenrollment, non-upload and follow-up censoring.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S04 is a historical method witness, not available current individual data. Current matched, entitled device/cohort data have not been acquired.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: qualified cohort extract/correction. Review: quarterly feasibility/version check, not daily narrative regeneration.

**Business/information horizon:** Product-transition and payer-cohort follow-up over months; no investment horizon is mechanically inferred.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Retain initiation rules, device version, exclusions and successive available vintages where rights permit; a late upload must not silently rewrite what was known earlier.

**Strongest alternative:** Selective inclusion, upload behavior or phone compatibility can change recorded continuity without changing paid commercial persistence.

**Falsifier:** A sufficiently complete, same-definition initiated cohort shows the apparent continuity change vanishes after missing-upload and selection checks.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing Healthcare/source owner and Research Vault; Evaluation owns any subsequent clinical/forecast validation.

**Not yet measurable:** Current commercial all-starter retention, paid wear, failure causes and causal clinical benefit.


### O2. Billed and paid monitoring service

**User question:** What covered service was billed, adjudicated and actually paid for a defined payer population?

**Population and observable:** Contract/jurisdiction/service-code/period cohort; Medicare FFS cannot stand in for VA, MA, commercial or cash-pay users.

**Measurement distinction:** Supply allowance units, paid service-months and unique beneficiaries are different measures.

**Numerator:** Adjudicated eligible paid units or distinct paid member-months, net of reversals; denominator-specific counts retained separately.

**Denominator:** Eligible covered member-months only if lawfully available; no population coverage ratio from summed provider rows.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S02 defines billing; S03 describes a feasible aggregate historical source. No claims rows or current release vintage were acquired.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: payer rule effective change, annual public-data release or final-action correction. Review: quarterly availability check.

**Business/information horizon:** Service month versus claims run-out/release lag; published aggregates may answer historical context only.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Preserve code/basis versions, run-out and release vintages; suppressed cells stay suppressed/missing, not zero.

**Strongest alternative:** Policy, coding, provider mix or claim lag may change paid units while sensor use is unchanged.

**Falsifier:** Matched adjudicated evidence shows no paid-service change after the rule/perimeter and run-out are reconciled.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing payer/source admission, financial evidence and GMI composition owners.

**Not yet measurable:** Brand-specific paid patient-days and within-person continuity in the acquired public aggregate methodology.


### O3. Recurring supplier economic reconciliation

**User question:** Which part of a recurring-device revenue change reflects users, use, price/mix, channel or recognition timing?

**Population and observable:** Same product/business perimeter, channel, currency, geography and reporting period; no patient-level join assumed.

**Measurement distinction:** Reported revenue and channel composition with an explicitly conditional unit/time bridge.

**Numerator:** Reported net revenue; separately measured dispensed/paid units, returns, replacement and collection amounts when disclosed.

**Denominator:** Only a matched measured patient-time or paid-unit base; otherwise revenue-per-patient is not estimable.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S01 supplies issuer totals, not the required patient denominator; O1/O2 populations cannot be silently linked.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: earnings, product/wear-duration change, recall/correction or material channel disclosure. Review: quarterly.

**Business/information horizon:** Quarterly reporting with cohort persistence and cash-collection lags; retain both business and knowledge clocks.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Store original issuer perimeter and recasts; preserve prior interpretation when a later input changes it.

**Strongest alternative:** Channel fill, higher realization or replacement obligations can explain revenue without more sustained paid users.

**Falsifier:** Matched disclosed patient-time/dispensing and channel reconciliation support a different driver than the proposed explanation.

**Existing intended owner:** Company Intelligence/Earnings and accepted GMI/F04 composition; no new financial store.

**Not yet measurable:** Current matched patient-time, patient acquisition/support costs, channel inventory and product-attributable cash.


### O4. Staffed care capacity and delivered hours

**User question:** Can an operator turn authorized care need into safely delivered, payable hours?

**Population and observable:** Same care service, local operation, authorized client cohort, payer and comparable operating period.

**Measurement distinction:** Eligible available staffed hours, authorized requested hours, scheduled hours, delivered verified hours, missed care and reasons remain separate.

**Numerator:** Completed eligible verified hours; separately, accepted scheduled hours and clinically relevant missed-care events.

**Denominator:** For fulfillment: authorized requested eligible hours. For utilization: qualified available staff-hours. Do not substitute ending headcount, locations or licensed beds.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S05/S06 supply delivered billable hours. The staffing and unmet-demand denominators are not public in the inspected source set; partner/owner acquisition would need separate entitlement.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: qualified operating release, contract/capacity change or data correction. Review: quarterly public evidence; finer cadence only if an entitled native feed exists.

**Business/information horizon:** Scheduling/service week to payer settlement; expansion/cohort ramp over quarters.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Preserve comparable-store definitions, acquisitions, absences, skills, travel constraints and missed-care classification; never erase a failed fulfillment record.

**Strongest alternative:** More care per continuing client, case mix or acquisitions can increase total hours without additional staffed slack or more unique clients.

**Falsifier:** A matched available/authorized-hours series shows demand or case intensity, not staffing, explains the observed change.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing Healthcare/provider evidence and source-rights owners; not Capacity Fabric machine-host routing.

**Not yet measurable:** Staffed availability, fill rate, waiting referrals, quality-adjusted throughput and true staffing constraint.


### O5. Payer realization and retained operator cash

**User question:** Does delivered care generate repeatable contribution and collection after its complete obligations?

**Population and observable:** Defined operator segment/payer/service period and, separately, consolidated cash-flow period.

**Measurement distinction:** Realized revenue per delivered unit, contract rate, care costs, local/corporate costs, collection timing and capital/financing.

**Numerator:** Matched net service revenue or collections, costs, capital outlays and defined cash subtotal; preserve sign and currency.

**Denominator:** Comparable delivered units for unit economics; no denominator for an absolute cash subtotal. Segment and group measures are not automatically commensurate.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S05-S08; contract-specific allocation and normalized owner cash are not all supplied. A state fee row does not bind every national hour.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: fee effective date, financial release, contract/settlement or correction. Review: quarterly plus material policy events.

**Business/information horizon:** Service period, revenue recognition, claim settlement and cash collection may differ; a semiannual cash statement is not a quarterly flow.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Retain original and recast payer/cohort/segment definitions, receivables and funding timing; source accounting and house arithmetic coexist.

**Strongest alternative:** Rate/mix, temporary cost support or working-capital timing may explain apparent improvement without stronger underlying access or durable margin.

**Falsifier:** Comparable later collections and full costs fail to sustain the interpreted benefit after timing and perimeter adjustments.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing financial/Earnings, Healthcare payer evidence and F04 composition owners.

**Not yet measurable:** Complete normalized distributable cash, specific contract economics, staffing-quality trade-offs and age-attributable issuer cash.


### O6. Consumer engagement, paid attachment and replacement

**User question:** Is the ecosystem retained through use, voluntary paid service, future hardware replacement, or a combination?

**Population and observable:** Exact device/version and unique eligible account cohort, geography, offer vintage and channel; deduplicate multi-device people.

**Measurement distinction:** Active use, net paid service attachment, renewal and replacement motive are four separate observations.

**Numerator:** Active eligible accounts; net paying accounts excluding free trials/refunds; renewed eligible paid accounts; replacements in a defined eligible installed cohort.

**Denominator:** Eligible activated accounts; renewal-due paid cohort; or replacement-eligible installed cohort, respectively. Shipments are none of these denominators.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S09/S10 disclose commercial context and an offer, not the actual cohort numerators/denominators. Current entitled cohort data remain absent.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: quarterly results, material offer/product change or a qualified cohort release. Review: quarterly; do not treat every app release as economic evidence.

**Business/information horizon:** Activity week/month, renewal interval and multi-year replacement cycle; price/offer knowledge has a separate publication clock.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Keep trial eligibility, bundles, cancellations, device versions and old offer prices. A current JS price page failure cannot refresh a 2025 price.

**Strongest alternative:** A useful free ecosystem may support hardware retention even with low paid attachment; replacement can reflect durability, preference or upgrade cycles.

**Falsifier:** Matched cohorts show the proposed retention/attachment driver is absent or offset by acquisition, service, warranty or replacement costs.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing Consumer/company evidence and shared GMI composition; medical claims remain with Healthcare.

**Not yet measurable:** Current paid conversion/renewal, engagement cohorts, replacement share and customer lifetime contribution.


### O7. Scoped medical permission and age-linked application

**User question:** Which exact clinical/population/access claim is supported, and does an age-linked economic consequence actually follow?

**Population and observable:** Application/version, indication, comparator, age/condition criteria, jurisdiction, payer and effective interval.

**Measurement distinction:** Authorization, study findings, access criteria and age-specific paid activity are distinct assertions.

**Numerator:** Where measured: qualifying used/paid episodes by defined age/condition cohort. For a legal/clinical permission statement, numerical numerator is not applicable.

**Denominator:** Corresponding eligible cohort only when measured; a mean age, population forecast or archived ticker set is not the denominator.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** S02/S04/S08/S11/S12 illustrate different scopes. No unified current clinical/age-economic dataset was built.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: label, payer, study-analysis or withdrawal/correction. Review: quarterly known-source/version backstop.

**Business/information horizon:** Effective clinical/access interval and patient follow-up versus economic recognition period; date-only sources cannot establish intraday ordering.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Preserve old version and analysis, explicit supersession, country limits and contraindicated generalizations; do not count one decision twice.

**Strongest alternative:** A demographic association or specific app clearance may leave paid access, clinical benefit and supplier contribution unchanged.

**Falsifier:** Exact population/version evidence or matched age-specific use contradicts the broad aging/medical interpretation.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing Healthcare/BioCatalyst, identity, source/evidence and evaluation owners; no medical-advice or regulatory approval service.

**Not yet measurable:** Universal longevity benefit, complete current labels, causal efficacy, all-country access and age-specific retained economics.


### O8. Therapeutic, diagnostic and care-right qualification

**User question:** What source-backed application and economic right connects this heterogeneous leaf to useful paid care?

**Population and observable:** Asset/test/service, clinical application, territory, contract role, comparator and analysis vintage.

**Measurement distinction:** Reported evidence milestone, access state, delivered application and right/risk terms; quantitative and qualitative claims remain separate.

**Numerator:** Measured treated/tested/paid applications or contractual cash base when available; non-numerical rights have no invented value.

**Denominator:** The exact eligible or contractual base; undisclosed royalty or cost-share terms stay unknown rather than zero or a guessed midpoint.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** HEALTH2/4/5/6 selected repository studies provide reuse requirements, not a complete current external validation. New primary acquisition is needed for the named gaps.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: trial analysis, label/access, agreement, financing, launch or correction. Review: quarterly, including quiet/development-only leaves.

**Business/information horizon:** Development, rights/effective periods and commercial recognition can span different years; publication lag must survive historical replay.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Keep analysis snapshots, territories, partner roles, source availability and corrections under existing owners; no new event-history plane.

**Strongest alternative:** A clinical or platform advance may not improve a specific participant after access, rights, shared costs, financing and expectations.

**Falsifier:** A qualified current source contradicts the proposed application/right, or realized economics fail the stated mechanism.

**Existing intended owner:** Incumbent Healthcare/BioCatalyst and financial evidence owners; Meta-CEO resolves shared interface requirements.

**Not yet measurable:** Broad current therapeutics/diagnostics universe, psychedelic/cannabis-specific evidence, complete rights, valuations and forecast validity.


### O9. Component-to-application economic link

**User question:** Does an immersive/genomics component supplier have a verified, economically material role in the final application?

**Population and observable:** Specific supplier business, component/version, customer/product and qualified commercial interval, not an entire ticker.

**Measurement distinction:** Capability, named qualification, paid inclusion, shipment/acceptance and retained contribution are different relationship states.

**Numerator:** Verified shipped/accepted application content or revenue where disclosed; a design-win announcement alone is qualitative.

**Denominator:** Matched final products or business revenue only when independently supportable; no denominator from archived vendor membership.

**Source/acquisition feasibility:** INV identifies why this question is necessary; current named application and economics were not acquired. Reuse existing C/Robotics/Semiconductor evidence before new research.

**Release/event versus review cadence:** Event: qualified design/customer disclosure, product launch, financial reporting or correction. Review: quarterly and material product transitions.

**Business/information horizon:** Design qualification may precede shipped demand by multiple periods; retain order, delivery and acceptance clocks.

**Forward retention and corrections:** Keep named source status, version, customer disclosure scope and withdrawn qualifications; one source relationship reused across themes remains one event.

**Strongest alternative:** Content gains can be offset by weaker unit demand, substitution, pricing or inventory, and capability may never become a paid design.

**Falsifier:** Exact customer/product evidence rejects the presumed inclusion or shows no material commercial participation.

**Existing intended owner:** Existing Semiconductor/Robotics/C relationship evidence; B qualifies the care/consumer use only, with Meta-CEO overlap rulings.

**Not yet measurable:** Verified supplier/customer links, component content, paid volumes, retained margins and final-device adoption.

## Concrete dossier journeys

**Monitoring user.** Open a medical-wearable leaf after a company release. The dossier first states the reported business change, then places actual-use evidence beside it with an explicit population/date warning. The user can inspect why a historical VA reading cohort cannot be joined to Medicare FFS allowance billing and consolidated sales. The useful unresolved result is a named missing commercial cohort, not an empty panel or an invented retention rate.

**Care-operator user.** Open the senior-care leaf and select the operating-service role, not the property owner. The dossier distinguishes growth in delivered hours from comparable census, rate/mix and collection timing. It shows the selected cash subtotal with its H1/consolidated label and explains why staffed utilization is unavailable. A user can identify the precise next question for a public disclosure or appropriately entitled source without mistaking locations for staffed capacity.

**Consumer-wearable user.** Open the wearable ecosystem view. Reported segment performance, historical offer terms and a specific medical-app permission are separate evidence cards. Engagement, net paid attachment, renewal and replacement sections show their individual coverage states. A missing paid cohort cannot hide a useful hardware-led hypothesis, and an ECG clearance cannot turn optional wellness features into validated treatments.

**Machine job.** Retain source identity once, use it in multiple leaf views without multiplying evidence, and invalidate only dependent interpretations on correction. Preserve the prior knowledge state for later Evaluation-owned historical work. A research-priority suggestion can route review of a missing material link; it cannot directly choose a basket, size a trade or create a forecast probability.

Source rights/acquisition and immutable evidence stay with their existing source and Research Vault owners. Company/business/security resolution stays with identity; archived Finviz membership supplies neither exposure weights nor a current listing decision. Financial statements and reported/derived cash definitions stay with Company Intelligence/Earnings and existing financial composition. Evaluation separately owns clinical, causal and forecast-performance acceptance.

These are required seams, not verified signatures. Healthcare R10's shared-interface reconciliation and the #7870 implementation owner already exist; B neither adds another schema/hash/router nor repairs their code. Qualitative missing rights, signed financial measures, exact decimals and cross-domain preservation are requirements to pass to those owners, not permission to coerce a current schema or reproduce a private assertion body in public configuration.
