## 4. Four targeted primary breadth qualifications

**B41 Local Bounti.** August12,2026 preliminary Q2 release reports sales13.850m versus12.103m and gross profit1.046m versus1.472m; its adjusted margin excludes specified items. The release explicitly says the10-Q had not yet been filed and results were preliminary. Proposed implication: retain improved output assertions without certifying full-cost economics, food-safety superiority or nutrition benefits. No later finalized filing or facility-level cost audit is claimed.

**B42 Beyond Meat.** August5 release, quarter endedJune27,2026, separates U.S./international and retail/foodservice pounds and net revenue per pound. International retail volume increased while U.S. retail and international foodservice volumes declined. These are source-defined company channel shipments, not the entire category or household repeat purchases. Proposed implication: preserve geographic/channel divergence instead of one alternative-protein demand verdict.

**B43 DoorDash.** August5 Q2 release defines Total Orders across marketplaces AND Commerce Platform, while Marketplace GOV excludes Commerce Platform order value. Its membership count includes paid, trial and partnership accounts. Contribution Profit contains stated adjustments and is not full net earnings; Deliveroo changes the growth perimeter. Proposed implication: reject denominator/population substitution; use actual like-for-like cohort definitions where supplied and retain missing full-cost lifetime economics.

**B44 Donaldson.** August26 fiscal-Q4 release distinguishes weaker dust-collection new equipment from stronger replacement parts within Industrial Filtration Solutions; acquired aerospace/defense activity also affects broader Industrial results. Proposed implication: growth in one segment does not establish pollution-control installation growth, customer emissions improvement or repeat-service profitability.
