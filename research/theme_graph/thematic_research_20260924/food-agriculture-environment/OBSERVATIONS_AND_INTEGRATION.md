## 1. What this must help a user do

The user should be able to distinguish a dated order-recovery claim from an observed order return, a real financing benefit from earned operating profit, and a better reported aging ratio from measured customer collections. The machine must preserve those distinctions when composing a theme explanation, a company view and a source drawer from the same underlying event.

The useful result is not another sector score. It is an explanation with the supported conclusion, strongest alternative, exact missing evidence and a next observation capable of changing the interpretation. Source count, plausible prose, a positive cash number or a recent page build must not manufacture an additional confirmation.

## 2. A1-O1 - deferred orders versus lost demand

E01 supports only the regional management claim. Product, grower crop, original order cohort and specific application window are not disclosed; those fields remain unknown. **Observable and arithmetic.** The desired numerator is accepted/delivered quantity from the specifically identified deferred orders; the denominator is that same originally deferred quantity, with cancellations and substitutions separately explained. Neither denominator nor numerator is currently acquired. Total regional sales, total company growth and new-product sales are contextual observations, not substitutes. Product units and net consideration must remain source-specific; crop-protection products are not normalized into fertilizer nutrient tonnes.

**Source and acquisition feasibility.** E01 is publicly readable; future issuer results/filings are plausible source events. Exact order IDs, distributor stock, grower sell-through and invoice terms may be private or undisclosed. No entitled route for those fields has been established. A disclosed later management attribution can be recorded as attribution, not upgraded to a measured cohort.

**Cadence and horizon.** Observe actual issuer results, material guidance amendments and source corrections as released. Proposed structural-review backstop: once per quarter and when the relevant commercial/product definition changes. The procurement horizon is the stated Q3/Q4 window, not automatically the same crop/application horizon. E08 schedules a Q3 report; proof about Q4 remains later. No watcher or daily narrative generator is created.

**Vintages and revisions.** Retain the July29 forecast separately from later actuals and revised forecasts. Preserve original order date, revised date, actual shipment, acceptance, ownership/control and cash dates if later obtained. A changed forecast is not a source correction; a corrected source creates revision lineage without rewriting the original knowledge set.

**Alternative, falsifier and unavailable behavior.** The alternative is lost demand/share or uneconomic replacement business. ## 3. A1-O2 - financing stock versus collection quality

**Question and population.** Does a lower reported overdue burden reflect payment by the same financed customers, or changed terms/dispositions/mix? Initial observable population: Nutrien Financial's reported North America balances at June30,2025 and2026 in USD millions. This is not a fixed cohort or all agricultural credit.

**Observable and arithmetic.** Snapshot measures are all past-due buckets divided by same-date gross financing receivables, and more-than90-days past due divided by that same gross balance. Keep absolute overdue amounts beside ratios. The desired collection measure is actual borrower cash received against the SAME original-due invoice cohort divided by that cohort's original amount due. The necessary cohort is unavailable. Do not divide collections by all sales, use net receivables for a gross-basis aging ratio, or call booked advances collection of overdue invoices.

**Source and feasibility.** E04/E05 supply public aging tables and reported definitions, directly inspected. Customer-level original/revised due dates, balances sold, write-offs, restructurings, collateral realization and cash allocations are not acquired. Broader banking context from E06 is a separate source population. No legal access to private customer records is assumed.

**Cadence and horizon.** Financial-release cadence plus corrections and material term/population changes; quarterly review backstop. Compare matching seasonal dates, then explicitly qualify currency/business/term changes. One crop cycle may be a useful business horizon only when the actual customer cohort permits it; the present regional balance does not establish one.

**Vintages and corrections.** Preserve each source table, bucket definition, reporting perimeter and denominator. Retain original maturity as well as revised maturity when available. A renegotiated due date is a new contractual fact, not permission to erase historical delinquency. Cash payment, loan sale, write-off and extension remain different dispositions.

**Alternative and falsifier.** A lower ratio may reflect new current receivables or noncash removal from overdue status. A sufficiently reconciled same-cohort cash roll-forward, with term changes and other dispositions separately measured, can support a collection statement. Persistent original-term delinquency or loss absorption would contradict a simple recovery story. Unknown collateral value cannot be inserted to force an expected-loss estimate.

**Useful missing-data behavior and owner.** Display the observed aging comparison and the reason collection improvement is not identified. Existing financial evidence/measurement owners retain the numbers and definitions; existing GMI/Research Vault responsibilities retain qualified interpretation under their rights contracts. No new credit score, loan database or collector is proposed.

## 4. A1-O3 - partner advance to economically retained delivery

**Question and population.** What does the FMC-Corteva rimisoxafen advance establish, and what must happen before it evidences recurring delivery economics? The supported commercial scope is the named agreement and its source-defined geography/crops. It is not proof of a currently approved country-level application or an active grower cohort.

**Observable and arithmetic.** Distinguish cash received, deferred obligation, delivered product/control transfer, revenue recognized, remaining obligation and cash still due. A remaining balance may be shown only when a source provides a compatible bridge. A future delivery margin requires attributable consideration minus full relevant production, fulfillment, service and other obligations at the same perimeter. Price, volumes, discounts, refunds and full cost schedules are not disclosed; no numeric margin/return is estimated.

**Source and feasibility.** E02 provides accounting classification; E03 describes the agreement. They concern one event family. Subsequent issuer filings and appropriate product/approval sources may resolve milestones; actual regulatory status must be researched before use. The full private contract is not acquired or implicitly licensed.

**Cadence and horizon.** Material agreement, approval, commercialization and filing events; quarterly structural review backstop while material obligations remain. Business horizon is multi-year development/fulfillment, separate from the current quarterly procurement story. Do not reinterpret absence of a near-term launch as failure against an invented deadline.

**Vintages and correction behavior.** Retain announcement, effective date when established, receipt date at available precision, accounting period, product/version and later delivery evidence. A quarterly release and a filing can add different detail without either being a correction. True source corrections must propagate to derived explanations through the incumbent dependency owner.

**Alternative and falsifier.** The advance may provide genuine financing while later approval, delivery cost or customer economics disappoint. Contrary milestone evidence weakens a launch thesis; an unfavorable documented contract/cost bridge weakens a retained-margin thesis. Neither absence of data nor an initial cash receipt resolves future profitability.

**Useful display and owner.** Show 'cash received for future product obligations' with the reported classification and unavailable margin/launch fields. Existing financial/Earnings owns accounting facts, GMI and native relationship owners hold only admitted source-qualified relationships, and Data OS owns identity. The UI must not turn this into current farmer adoption, a clinical/agronomic claim or an investment rank.

## 5. A1-O4 - cash source, obligation and shareholder claim

**Question and population.** What changed cash in a defined issuer/reporting period, and is that the same as more recurring cash retained by the existing shareholder? The population is the source-defined consolidated entity and financial period, not a loose collection of theme participants.

**Observable and denominator.** Reconcile only disclosed like-for-like cash components, maintaining reported accounting classification. No ratio is necessary for an event classification. Where a ratio is proposed, both numerator and denominator must be explicit and compatible. Gross receivables sold, carrying value, cash proceeds and retained interest are distinct; an undisclosed bridge remains unresolved. No automatically normalized CFO/FCF is generated.

**Feasibility, cadence and horizon.** E02 and E07 support different cash/ownership event types. Later filings may establish the actual cash-statement bridge and share timing. Review on results, capital events, contract changes and corrections; quarterly backstop. Distinguish immediate liquidity, future business obligations and per-share ownership over their relevant horizons. Prices and market-return data are not acquired by this profile.

**Vintages, alternatives and falsifiers.** Preserve announced versus closed status, expected versus received proceeds, transaction costs, reporting scope and ownership-effective date. Repeated disclosures cannot re-add one receipt. A forecast of recurring cash is weakened when the improvement depends on advances, funding, asset changes or obligations not included in the forecast's original perimeter. That does not make the financing transaction itself undesirable; it changes the question being answered.

**Unknowns and existing owner.** Show the known event and unclosed reconciliation without assigning a fabricated residual to customer collections. Current source/financial/corporate-action owners remain authoritative. F04 may compose their outputs only through accepted contracts and current permissions. No new financial pipeline, forecast engine or company identifier is established.

## 6. Two concrete dossier journeys

### Journey A - 'Cash improved: has the crop-input business recovered?'

The user enters an existing theme/company route bound by the accepted identity owner. The page first distinguishes the dated operational claim from the cash event. It explains why a future-product receipt can finance the business without proving current demand. The source drawer identifies the agreement and accounting passage, their dates and one shared event lineage. The user can inspect the delivery/margin limitations and the next evidence needed.

The operational panel remains useful when future contract economics are unavailable. It must not silently overwrite reported CFO with a house-adjusted value, count a later application of the same advance as fresh cash, or infer all North America demand from a different product's corn/soy scope. The source drawer and headline must use the same retained generation and permitted content, not independently selected 'latest' records.

### Journey B - 'The overdue ratio fell: are customers paying better?'

The page shows matched-season amounts and ratios, explains the gross denominator, and labels the populations as reported snapshots. A visible limitation identifies missing original-due-cohort dispositions. The user can compare source definitions and see the difference between the regional issuer book and a lender survey. An unavailable repayment analysis is a bounded missing panel, not a blank page and not a green recovery verdict.

A corrected table must update both arithmetic and prose dependencies. A rights withdrawal must use the existing current-permission owner to prevent unauthorized content emission; an older successful cached page is not a permission fallback. Permitted metadata or an unavailable explanation may remain only as allowed by the native rights contract. This document does not enroll a new revocation mechanism.
