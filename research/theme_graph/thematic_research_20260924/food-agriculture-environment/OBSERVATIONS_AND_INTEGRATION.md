## 2. Proposed profiles for the food case

### A2-O1 - ingredient role, retained contribution and cash

Question: does the specified ingredient business retain more economic contribution rather than merely invoice more? Population: exact issuer segment/product, unaffiliated customer scope and fiscal period; unknown customer/SKU allocation stays unknown. Observable: external sales, compatible operating-cost/profit bridge and separately scoped cash/investment. Ratio: use matched segment profit/segment sales only when definitions support it; do not divide segment profit by group sales as a margin or infer segment cash from corporate CFO.

Feasibility: F22 provides selected public financial tables; customer contracts, conversion compensation, product yields and segment cash remain unacquired. Cadence: releases/corrections and material contract or reporting changes; quarterly review backstop. Business horizon: quarterly execution and multi-year qualification/capital; market horizon requires separate retained expectations. Preserve original/recast scope, units, adjustment definitions and source vintages. Alternative: favorable mix masks weak utilization or capital absorption. Falsifier: same-scope full contribution deteriorates despite higher invoices. Show the known bridge and unavailable customer-return question. Intended owners: native financial/Earnings, GMI evidence, Data OS and F04 composition; actual bindings pending.

### A2-O2 - retail customer and opening-cohort productivity

Question: does expansion add useful customer economics after cannibalization and full cash cost? Population: defined comparable stores/customer accounts, plus separate opening cohorts and store-days. Observables: matched transactions, units/basket, contribution, openings/closures, attributable cash and invested capital. Denominators: comparable prior-period sales for a comp; eligible store-days for productivity; full initial/incremental investment for a cohort return. A ticket is not a physical unit.

F21 supports group profit/cash, not complete cohorts. Entitled retailer cohort/category data have not been acquired. Observe earnings, calendar/perimeter changes and actual cohort disclosures; quarterly review backstop, multi-year maturation horizon. Retain fiscal-week definitions, original cohort eligibility, revisions and source permissions. Alternative: footprint masks mature-store weakness or new investment displaces existing business. Falsifier: same-cohort retained cash fails to cover full incremental burden against the predeclared alternative. Display unavailable payback, not a guessed margin. Existing financial/customer-evidence responsibilities retain measurements; F04 renders, Data OS binds identities.

## 3. Proposed profiles for the actual water contract

### A3-O1 - project commitment to accepted paid service

Question: has the identified project reached the stage that supports the asserted operating revenue? Population: one contract/version, customer and facility, not the vendor's entire water segment. Observable: award, signed terms, authorized scope, construction, testing, formal acceptance, eligible operation and actual invoices. No ratio is necessary for stage qualification; a conversion ratio requires an explicitly identified starting cohort, not announced project value.

W31-W34 support selected contract/progress evidence; complete amendments and actual acceptance/invoices are not acquired. Observe material milestones/amendments and financial releases; quarterly structural review backstop. Horizon: development through service commencement, with subsequent operating years separate. Preserve event availability, actual effective dates and originally anticipated milestones. Alternative: a valid award remains delayed, rescoped or unproductive. Falsifier: purported current operation is contradicted by stage evidence or missing required acceptance. Show proven stage with the pending dependency. Existing contract/financial/GMI evidence owners and F04 retain the journey; no new project lifecycle service.

### A3-O2 - full obligations, constrained cash and performance settlement

Question: how much of a source-qualified service payment remains after the contract's full obligations? Population: exact operative agreement, contract year, eligible cost classes and restricted accounts. Observable: invoices, index vintages, utilities, cost/reimbursement responsibility, repairs, reserves, credits and actual cash dispositions. Denominators: explicit compatible cost/fee basis only; no blanket margin percentage or contract-face-value/years run rate. Any efficiency comparison needs the correct guaranteed baseline and eligible actual cost.

The original contract is public; complete current terms, full index entitlements and plant records remain missing. Observe settlements, index finalizations, amendments, maintenance and financial releases; quarterly review plus annual contract settlement, no watcher created. Horizon: operating year and remaining asset obligations. Preserve preliminary/final index true-ups, term changes, termination versus ordinary expiry, original and corrected calculations. Alternative: fixed receipts conceal greater variable cost or future repair liability. Falsifier: same-contract full cash contribution fails under actual obligations. Unknown costs block numeric profit, not the explanation. Financial/rights/Research Vault retain data; F04 composes through accepted contracts.

## 4. Targeted breadth profiles

### A4-O1 - controlled-environment saleable output

Question: does improved facility output become profitable sold product? Population: specified crop, facility, area and production cycle, with packing/distribution boundary. Observe harvest, saleable packed output, sold quantities, rejection/spoilage and full delivered costs. Ratios: saleable weight/area-time and sold weight/packed weight; never substitute doors served or claimed capacity. B41 supplies limited preliminary corporate evidence; facility records and comparable conventional alternative remain unavailable.

Cadence: issuer releases, facility/product changes and finalization; quarterly review. Horizon: crop cycles and asset payback. Retain preliminary status, yield definitions, currencies, facility cohorts and revisions. Alternative: higher yield requires more packing, energy, labor or capital. Falsifier: same-cohort contribution remains inadequate after all costs. Missing energy/weight data remains unknown; no food-safety or health superiority inference. Existing operating/financial evidence and GMI owners retain facts; no agronomic prescription or new farm telemetry collector.

### A4-O2 - alternative-protein repeat demand

Question: is a specified product/channel gaining sustained paid use rather than stocking or promotion? Population: product/version, geography, channel and disclosed shopper cohort. Observe shipment pounds/net realization separately from retail takeaway, repeat buying, returns and promotion. Repeat rate requires returning eligible customers/original eligible cohort; channel shipments are not that denominator. B42 provides selected shipment geography, not household microdata; panel rights/acquisition are unestablished.

Observe releases, launches/exits and entitled panel publications; quarterly backstop. Horizon: repeat-purchase cycles and product cash contribution. Retain currency/mix, channel definitions, source dependence and revisions. Alternative: channel inventory or temporary pricing changes drive shipments. Falsifier: paid repeat cohorts/contribution deteriorate when promotions normalize. Show geographic divergence while withholding adoption verdict. Existing commercial/financial evidence owners retain observations; clinical/nutrition evidence remains separate and unproven.

### A4-O3 - pollution-control installed versus replacement economics

Question: are customers adding systems, using an installed base, or only changing replacement spend? Population: named application, new equipment and compatible installed-cohort parts, with geography and service obligations. Observe orders, accepted installation, replacement units, utilization and full contribution. Replacement attachment requires eligible active installed systems as denominator, not all segment sales. B44 supplies category-level disclosure, not customer emissions/retention measures.

Event cadence follows results, product changes and actual replacement disclosures; quarterly review, equipment-cycle versus maintenance-cycle horizons separate. Preserve acquired scope, equipment/part definitions and corrected vintages. Alternative: acquired revenue or customer replacement inflation masks weaker installations/use. Falsifier: attributed installed-base growth lacks compatible additions or profitable repeat work. Keep performance and regulatory approval unknown absent their own sources. GMI/operating/financial and identity owners retain roles; no inferred certification.

### A4-O4 - delivery cohort economics without denominator substitution

Question: does the same customer/order cohort retain value after all delivery and acquisition costs? Population: explicitly chosen marketplace/geography/cohort, separate from Commerce Platform, acquired perimeter and trial membership. Observe orders, order value, frequency, retention, realized fees, incentives, fulfillment and acquisition/retention costs. AOV denominator must be orders included in the value numerator; retention denominator must be the original cohort; net profit is not a relabelled contribution metric.

B43 supplies definitions and selected aggregate disclosures; complete cohort cash, exact food-only allocation and lifetime counterfactual remain unavailable. Observe releases, population/fee/acquisition changes and source corrections; quarterly review, cohort-month and multi-year horizons separate. Preserve paid/trial/partner status, membership vintage, acquisition assumptions, currencies and adjustment definitions. Alternative: acquisition or subsidized frequency expands activity without incremental lifetime value. Falsifier: comparable cohort full retained cash deteriorates after subsidies/service obligations. Existing commerce/financial/customer evidence owners retain truth; no rival customer database or automatic LTV model.
