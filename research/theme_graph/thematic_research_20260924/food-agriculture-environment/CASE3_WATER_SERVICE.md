## 1. Customer job and answer

**Does a long-term water-service contract create durable retained cash, or merely durable obligations?**

The useful job is reliable water supply at a manageable full cost. Economic analysis must identify which party funds construction, purchases utilities, maintains the asset, bears overruns, receives performance benefits and retains cash after obligations. 'Water exposure' and 'recurring service' cannot answer those questions.

The selected worked object is the Kalaeloa seawater-desalination DBOM arrangement between Honolulu's Board of Water Supply and Kalaeloa Desalco. It provides an actual contract to inspect, not a customer logo inferred to be a contract. The original2023 terms are a historical contract vintage; this study does not certify the absence of later amendments. Current invoice rates, actual accepted production and realized contract profitability remain unavailable.

## 5. Retained-economics interpretation

**House interpretation:** the economically important object is the matched package of payment rights and responsibilities. Stability of billing does not guarantee stable contribution when obligations persist and costs vary. A favorable efficiency incentive can coexist with downside reimbursement exposure. Funding a future repair requirement can support service reliability without creating immediately distributable cash.

For a prospective return estimate, distinguish operating compensation; reimbursed third-party expense; restricted maintenance funding; operating labor/chemicals; eligible maintenance; unfunded excess costs; performance credits; working capital; financing; and corporate/guaranty exposure. Populate this bridge only with compatible observed values or explicit scenario assumptions. Do not fill undisclosed plant costs with the services segment's average margin.

This study does NOT establish the project's realized profitability or net present value. What it establishes is why the simple alternatives fail: contract face value divided by years; all annual payments treated as margin; all cash treated as unrestricted; all output multiplied by a guessed tariff; or an announced project counted as an operating asset. The necessary additional evidence is specific and testable rather than a generic uncertainty disclaimer.

A properly framed adverse case asks whether delays, maintenance burden, operating inefficiency or financing overwhelm the retained benefit. A favorable case requires accepted service, compliant measured output and a same-contract cost/cash bridge. Neither case is settled by recurring-revenue language alone.

## 6. Four economic roles that must remain separate

Sold equipment: the customer's ownership and continuing operator obligations are not automatically the seller's recurring service exposure. A warranty is a cost obligation, not proof of profitable attachment.

Operating service: identify the actual payer, payment formula, performance conditions and included work. Do not infer a per-gallon tariff or availability-only contract from the word service.

Regulated asset ownership: a regulated return and recoverable capital base would require its own jurisdictional/asset evidence. This contractor study does not assign a utility's regulated-asset economics to the contractor.

Availability or outcome payment: capacity readiness, delivered quantity and measured outcomes are different possible payment bases. The research should establish which one the actual source supports. A broad sustainability category is not a substitute for a commercial formula.

These are analytical distinctions, not a newly enrolled ontology or legal classification of every water business.

## 7. Observation and falsification

The most discriminating next evidence is the current operative agreement/amendments, actual acceptance date, permitted performance measurements, invoices and settlement statements, restricted-account movements and same-scope delivery/capital costs. Public quarterly reporting may expose progress and aggregate results while leaving these project fields private.
