## 1. The economic question that joins the cases

A reported improvement becomes useful intelligence only after identifying **what changed, for whom, under which commercial obligations, and over which period**. Crop demand, an ingredient invoice, a retail sale and a water-service payment represent different jobs. They cannot be converted into one bullish thematic conclusion by sharing a label or a positive growth sign.

Across the three cases, the strongest reusable research discipline is to follow the same economic object into its next discriminating observation. An order-timing thesis needs the original deferred orders, not any later growth. A value-chain thesis needs matched products and transactions, not added issuer sales. A recurring-service thesis needs the operative payment and cost responsibilities, not merely a long contract term.

The proposed product therefore pairs each supported conclusion with the strongest alternative and the exact missing observation that separates them. An unresolved outcome can coexist with a useful explanation. Unknown customer cohorts, contract margins or current acceptance must not be replaced with zeros, a confidence score or a blank whole page.

**Strongest alternative:** apparently improved liquidity or aging can coexist with weaker current demand or changed obligations/dispositions.

## 4. Case 3 - water reliability and the whole obligation

The case reads an original Kalaeloa service agreement and guaranty, later issuer progress reporting and a contrasting Xylem-Dow announcement. ## 6. Anticipatory intelligence without a manufactured forecast

The useful forward step is to state the decisive observation BEFORE the outcome: which deferred cohort must return, which customer contribution must survive, or which project acceptance/obligation condition must hold. That makes later learning possible. Retained expectations must specify their population, horizon and falsifier; retrospective explanation is not prospective validation.

Simple baselines remain necessary. A price-only, sales-only or contract-value-only reading should be compared with the fuller interpretation under the existing Evaluation owner, using real retained vintages and held-out outcomes. This tranche has not run that evaluation. It supplies source-grounded distinctions and testable hypotheses, not a validated probability, alpha result, security recommendation or trade-sizing input.

Research priority, house scenarios, forecasts and trading authority remain separate. The original North-Star ambition is not reduced to a static encyclopedia: the desired result is accumulated, correctable understanding that helps a user interpret the next material event. But deeper prose alone does not establish that capability in production.
