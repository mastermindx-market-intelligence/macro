# GMI M1 research extension — selected publisher-page spot checks

**Scope:** selected five source-page checks among 58 pointers in a draft 46-candidate addendum. This is human-readable source-interpretation work, NOT an authenticated full-document archive, paid data ingest, license grant, Data OS identity, Graph membership or an E1/prospective observation. Publication dates are differentiated from review/search observation; 36 of the 46 new candidate rows lack a source-published date.

1. **MKS Instruments → wafer_fab_subsystems:** The first-party [MKS semiconductor etching solutions](https://www.mks.com/s/semiconductor-etching-solutions/mks-handbook) page documents vacuum, pressure/flow measurement and control, remote plasma/RF power, ozone and process controls. Its supported role is critical process-equipment *subsystems and process-enabling components*, **not** that MKS manufactures all complete semiconductor etchers or any lithography scanner. Page publication day UNKNOWN; stage OFFERED_PRODUCT/CAPABILITY as supported, with no issuer profit-share attribution.

2. **Nanya Technology → conventional_dram:** The first-party [DDR5 product/module catalog](https://www.nanya.com/en/Product/List/2418/2515) visibly separates standard DDR5/DDR4, LPDDR and module types. This supports the non-HBM DRAM product offer. It does not prove HBM DRAM mass production, pure-play server DRAM revenue, current available quantities, or Taiwan public-security/issuer admission. Publication day UNKNOWN; product catalog is current-context-only.

3. **Yaskawa Electric → industrial_motion_controls, precision_actuators_servo_motors, industrial_robot_arms:** [Dated April 8, 2026 primary product release](https://www.yaskawa-global.com/newsrelease/product/179182) documents MPX1010 industrial motion controller supporting up to 640 servomotors; its stated *applications* include semicap, electronics assembly, industrial robots and more. The [company's industrial robot product page](https://www.yaskawa-global.com/product/robotics) separately substantiates complete MOTOMAN industrial-robot manufacture and internally developed servo motors. The roles are simultaneously meaningful but remain **one issuer**, not multiple independent observations. A semicap application does not make Yaskawa an EUV lithography tool OEM; no customer revenue allocation is supported. The older 2020 IFR install-base statistic must not be treated as 2026 installations.

4. **HEICO → aerospace_components_aftermarket:** [HEICO's Parts Group page](https://heico.com/parts-group/) documents FAA PMA replacement engine/aircraft component parts and component repair. [Its Repair Group page](https://heico.com/repair-group/) identifies commercial aircraft component repair/overhaul and asset management. Aftermarket parts plus MRO are separate revenue activities within HEICO, not proof that the company operates airlines or builds complete new engines. Product claims are source publisher claims; financial materiality and publication day remain unverified.

5. **Basler → machine_vision:** [Basler machine-vision industrial cameras](https://www.baslerweb.com/en-us/cameras/industrial-cameras/) offers area/line-scan/3D/embedded cameras with GigE/USB/CoaXPress interfaces. This supports industrial *vision equipment* and integrations, not a claim that Basler produces industrial robotic arms or that every imaging customer creates another listed economic entity. An undated current catalog must not be back-projected.
