## 4. Leadership science

### 4.1 Exact RS benchmarks

For matching endpoints a,z:

`relative_wealth = (P_stock[z]/P_stock[a]) / (P_benchmark[z]/P_benchmark[a]) - 1`.

This is not percentage-point active return (`R_stock - R_benchmark`); label both distinctly. Performance requires total-return-adjusted levels; execution uses the proper executable price. Unknown adjustment provenance cannot be called total return.

Frozen windows: 21/63/126/252 completed sessions and exact **252 lookback / 21 skip** endpoints. A 5-session change is descriptive, not proven alpha. No shortened-history substitution. New IPOs receive explicit short-history analysis and unavailable long windows.

Benchmark ladder: broad US market → sector → industry/subsector → economically exposed theme/subtheme → issuer-excluded peer basket. Compare group-only timing with within-group stock selection. Equal-weight versus cap-weight construction is preregistered, not chosen after seeing winners. Do not add highly correlated benchmark relatives as independent votes.

RS percentiles use a frozen eligible universe, average ties and explicit denominator/coverage. Thin or zero-dispersion comparisons return unavailable. Percentile is descriptive, never probability or licensed IBD RS.

### 4.2 Alpha, established leaders and emerging leaders

Separate historical residual statistic, estimated expected excess return, and research ordering. The recovered engine provides the first; the new view must not mislabel the third as the second.

Hypotheses: residual persistence vs plain RS; within-group discrimination vs group-only timing; independent peer continuity; earnings revision interactions; and emergence without excluding established leaders. Candidate features include horizon agreement, residual persistence, downside capture, revisions and peer support; record availability and collinearity.

Emergence requires sequential comparable snapshots. Subtracting 3-month and 12-month ranks with different formation populations does not establish a rank upgrade. No backdated emergence from today's winners. INTC/MU/SNDK/ARM can be coverage examples, never privileged exceptions. Entry geometry stays separate from the selection experiment.

## 5. Earnings and re-rating

### 5.1 Exact historical bridge

For positive comparable EPS and prices: `P = EPS * PE`.

`log(P1/P0) = log(EPS1/EPS0) + log(PE1/PE0)`.

Total return is `(1+EPS_change)*(1+multiple_change)-1`; preserve interaction. +20% EPS and +25% multiple imply +50% price, not +45%. This is accounting decomposition, not causal identification.

Require same forward/fiscal period, currency, share/split basis and GAAP/adjusted definition. Rolling NTM changes mechanically as time advances: use constant-horizon estimates or explicitly separate roll, genuine revision, dilution/share and FX effects. Nonpositive/near-zero EPS, loss-to-profit, missing prices or incompatible periods yield typed unavailable. EV/revenue and EV/EBITDA need a separate accepted bridge with capital structure/margin assumptions; never fake PE.

### 5.2 Forward business cases

Evidence-backed scenarios combine estimates, defensible multiple assumptions and time/capital structure. Separate operating improvement, incremental surprise and already-priced expectations. Historical peak multiple is not an entitlement.

A scenario states the mechanism (volume/price/mix/share/capacity/margin/backlog/cash conversion), source references, assumptions, horizon, downside case and disconfirming observations. Management aspiration is not guidance; guidance is not consensus; backlog is not recognized revenue; institutional commentary is not company fact.

Until accepted source/calibration owners are bound, scenario inputs are explicitly illustrative and probabilities are null. Initial arithmetic does not independently collect consensus or issue targets.
