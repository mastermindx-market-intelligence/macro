## 1. Experience thesis

The user should feel that Mastermind is doing the watching and organizing, not handing them another task list. The first viewport answers what deserves attention, what changed, why it matters, and one useful next action. Deeper investigation is available without losing place.

Prophet is the flagship opportunity source. Macro, company, financial, catalyst and News evidence make its developments and other monitored situations more understandable; they do not turn Alert Center into another stock picker. A user without holdings or custom monitors still receives useful permitted market intelligence. A returning user sees meaningful new developments, not a fresh coat of paint on yesterday's observations.

**Experience sequence:** notice → understand → investigate → follow supported updates → receive a meaningful change → inspect the original and current evidence. Corrections, unavailable sources and quiet periods are real parts of that experience.

Premium means calm hierarchy, precise typography, readable data, useful context and predictable interactions. It does not mean giant gradient cards, a fictional AI confidence gauge, endless colored pills, or hiding qualifications to make an opportunity look exciting.

## 5. Now: useful in seconds, deeper by choice

The first viewport is not a statistics dashboard. Use one compact **What Matters Now** briefing followed by the prioritized notice queue. The briefing should select a small number of genuinely distinct developments, not fill a fixed quota when nothing material exists. Each briefing item links into the same evidence as its queue item.

A queue row contains the subject and market, a plain-language change title, one concise meaning/qualification line, one useful next action, and the relevant time/state treatment. The source authority and any decisive limitation survive the collapsed state. Separate new plan publication, current-read change, candidate discovery, material correction, company/catalyst development and monitoring limitation; do not make them all red urgent alerts.

Use a clear reason such as **Following this company**, **Matches your monitor**, or **In your holdings** only when the corresponding authorized record supports it. A watchlist is not ownership. Indirect graph relevance says why it is connected and does not claim portfolio impact.

Follow the house glance budget: short headings (normally at most four words), one short subtitle/meaning line, and mechanics behind accessible disclosure. A source headline may be longer in detail; rewrite the glance meaning without changing its assertion status or dropping a qualification. Critical negative facts cannot exist only inside hover text.

Do not show a blended confidence percentage. Source support, freshness/coverage, materiality and time sensitivity are separate concepts. At the glance tier show only the distinction that changes what the user should understand or do; let detail reveal the basis.

When new notices arrive while a user is reading, preserve scroll, focus and selected detail. Show an explicit **New updates available** control rather than constantly reordering the queue. Loading a later batch does not mark unseen notices read. Repeated observations and translation changes do not continually reset the unread count.

A truly quiet window shows a calm, positive confirmation of checked scope and the last meaningful check, not an empty error page. Partial monitoring uses a concise visible limitation with an actionable detail link. Neither an old firing nor an old successful run is sufficient to call the system healthy now.

## 6. Investigation: the useful meaning comes before the receipt

Desktop uses a detail inspector or dedicated detail composition that preserves the originating queue/filter/selection. Mobile uses a full-height detail view with an explicit, reliable return. Do not require hover for a mobile or keyboard-critical action.

The detail hierarchy is:

1. **What changed** — compatible before/after facts, source event status, relevant period and a meaningful qualification.
2. **Why it matters** — the supported implication and why this user is seeing it; not an invented instruction to trade.
3. **Evidence and disagreement** — distinguish source facts, supported calculations and interpretation; show contrary observations and missing context when consequential.
4. **Connected context** — related company/financial/macro/theme information with an explanation of each connection. Same-origin echoes are not independent confirmation.
5. **Timeline and next checks** — original development, meaningful revisions/corrections and supported monitoring choices.

Source clocks, full identifiers, methodology and run diagnostics are inspectable without dominating the default view. The original and current source timestamps remain distinct in comparison views; one general “updated” timestamp must not obscure the meaning.

A source-only alert remains complete enough to use when deeper AI analysis fails. Show **More context unavailable** only where the missing context actually matters; do not replace a valid alert with an endless AI spinner. A late response based on superseded evidence is not painted as the current explanation.

### Original and current

Opening an old notice initially shows the notice's own evidence and a visible indicator when a later update or correction exists. **Current view** is separately selected/labeled. A missing historical source returns **Original evidence unavailable**, not today's data placed in the old slot.

A correction to Mastermind's explanation says **We corrected our explanation**. A source correction says the source revised or withdrew the underlying information. These are different developments; neither should be disguised as a new investment signal.

## 7. Monitor setup: a promise the system can keep

Start from the current source context where possible. **Monitor this** pre-fills a supported plan, company, security, theme or existing watchlist scope; it does not activate silently. Natural-language intent can assist drafting, but the confirmation displays the actual finite condition or subscription the owner can evaluate.

The user sees scope, condition/event kind, data basis, check cadence, one-shot versus continuing behavior, effective notification mode, timezone/quiet hours and relevant coverage. Show the effective outcome, not just the option requested locally. For example, an immediate request under a digest-only account says it will appear in the summary.

Save states are real interaction states: saving; saved awaiting first check; active after owner-supported evaluation; rejected unsupported condition; permission expired; limit reached; save failed; and returned-to-form with values preserved. A successful save alone must not assert a completed healthy check.

Reading, archiving, following, pausing and re-arming remain separate verbs. **Archive** does not unfollow. **Pause monitor** does not delete earlier notices. Changing email preference does not re-arm a one-shot condition. A newly added watchlist member establishes its baseline rather than generating a historical notification flood.

The existing explicit-alert limit is not transformed into a portfolio-position limit. Holdings monitoring remains the existing book-level composition. Do not show a generic ability to monitor every unsupported field merely because it can be described in language.

## 8. Delivery and History: explain the outcome, not the database

History shows notices and their source revisions separately from delivery observations and personal read/archive actions. An opened email is not proof that the source condition resolved; a sent email is not proof of user comprehension.

For the currently supportable delivery facts, use language such as **Scheduled**, **Summary delayed**, **Submitted to mail service**, **Not emailed**, **Send failed**, and **Result not confirmed**. **Delivered** is used only when the actual provider path can establish that observation. These are design copy choices tied to typed backend facts, not a promise that the current sender already supports all states.

The detail explains a withheld/deferred outcome with a specific reason: quiet hours, category choice, unavailable eligibility check, expired relevance, or source access. Avoid a vague “Something went wrong” where the system knows the cause. Unknown result remains unknown; no cheerful retry-success animation without a resolved result.

An immediate and digest monitor matching the same development result in one notice and one effective channel assignment. The detail can say it matched another monitor and identify which affirmative request determined delivery, subject to the user's account limits. No model-driven override of quiet hours.

A digest distinguishes what was summarized, linked only, not emailed, or delayed. Restoring consent does not silently send old withheld history. A temporarily uncheckable digest can be visibly delayed while a separate eligible immediate notification proceeds. Do not clear the visible history merely to tidy its status.

## 9. Email composition and landing behavior

Design both an immediate update and a digest, using the existing mail design system and renderer constraints. The immediate message has a clear subject, one principal change, the qualification that affects interpretation, a concise why-for-you explanation, a visible evidence action and manage/unsubscribe controls.

A digest is an organized summary of meaningful changes, not a dump of card HTML. It shows the current relevant situation and material developments during the window. Known closed/withdrawn opportunities are not described with stale urgency. A disclosed partial/withheld population is not hidden behind an absolute claim that the user has been told everything.

Do not put a user's private holdings value in the subject. Do not embed credentials in links. Source excerpts and model-derived summaries obey the source-use rules; a readable web page does not automatically grant redistribution permission.

The evidence action opens the exact original notice through the existing signed-in route. A later correction is visible immediately after access succeeds. Authentication preserves a safe destination. A link preview or scanner causes no read acknowledgment, monitor activation or send.

Prototype at a narrow email width as well as desktop. The email artifact remains an editable design, not a tested received message or inbox-placement proof.

## 12. Visual system and responsive treatment

Reuse the actual chosen file's brand tokens, components and code-aligned typography once read. Do not choose another font system or introduce new literal colors merely to make this brief look designed. Components should use native editable frames, auto layout, text and instances; no screenshot-as-the-finished-UI.

Dark treatment: quiet deep surfaces with clear layer separation, readable neutral text, restrained accents and no excessive glows. Light treatment: clear paper-like surfaces, intentional borders/dividers and contrast hierarchy, not a mechanical color inversion. Match the house tokens; these are art-direction goals, not new hex values.

Color does not carry truth alone. Distinguish source/access status, personal unread state, selected state, delivery outcome and market direction through text, shape or icon semantics. Green “access granted” cannot be mistaken for “buy.” A blue selection outline is not event importance. Consistent monochrome action icons should not become a new notification-severity scale.

Build mobile from the user journey, not by shrinking desktop. Preserve source qualifications, usable actions and the inspection return path. Filters can use a sheet; detail becomes full-height. No clipped main action or hidden decisive caveat. Long company names and Chinese strings receive natural space; dates and amounts retain readable alignment.

Keyboard and focus paths cover queue → detail → evidence → monitor → confirmation → return. New data does not steal focus. Hover-only evidence is also reachable by focus/tap. Respect reduced-motion preferences; any loading motion indicates actual waiting, not invented system activity. Numerical charts use the existing house charting conventions and their null behavior; an ornamental mini-chart is not evidence.
