## Accepted concrete definitions and required amendments

1. **Stage:** probability of a documented native-case signed/accepted LOA at exactly house-first-seen +365 calendar days. This is a predictive binary experiment only when a complete ascertainment window admits both positive and negative labels. Otherwise retain censored/descriptive progression. No odds may be inferred from notification amount. A trained stage/age baseline is impossible until development-only ascertainment labels exist; no fabricated base rate is admissible.
2. **Financial:** year-over-year revenue growth for the same issuer, segment perimeter, accounting basis and single fiscal quarter. The endpoint is the **second fiscal-quarter end strictly after issuance**. The value comes from the first subsequent admitted report for that quarter, with amendments separately retained. Prior-year comparison must be reported comparable, and the baseline is the most recently released comparable same-segment year-over-year growth known by issuance. Management guidance is a secondary comparison unless it describes exactly that segment, period and accounting unit. Company growth cannot be labeled revenue caused by one award.
3. **Equity:** 63-session total-return excess over ITA, using a named calendar, permanent security and corporate-action chain. At the existing owner's conservative date-based convention, entry is the first session strictly after the UTC knowledge date; endpoint is `entry_index + 63` (63 intervals, not 63 bars including entry), and it must have closed by the as-of. Freeze separate public availability and house availability: late discovery may be useful for house-workflow research but cannot be called market anticipation. Require two baselines: an event-type mean estimated solely in the development set (fixed fallback to development grand mean when a type has no data), and the prior 63-session excess return entirely observable by issuance. Freeze both before holdout scoring. If no development labels or lawful adjusted prices exist, no baseline estimate is manufactured.
4. **Workflow:** 45-minute maximum task, paired product/source-reading comparison with disjoint equivalent tasks, counterbalanced order and assessor-blinded answers. A participant's repeated tasks form one cluster, and equivalent document/program tasks belong to the same leakage group. Source-reading condition may not reuse an answer learned in the product condition. Product designer self-testing is usability evidence, not a blinded workflow efficacy test. A timing benefit counts only with admitted correct-answer/risk rubrics and the registered noninferiority test.

The coordinator accepts the following **planning utility thresholds**, as explicit design judgments rather than empirical estimates: Brier-loss improvement 0.02 (unitless), financial absolute-error improvement 1.0 percentage point, equity absolute-error improvement 0.01 return units (1 percentage point), workflow time saving 5 minutes. Precision goals are respective paired-effect half-widths 0.01, 0.5 percentage point, 0.005 return units and 2.5 minutes. These numbers make the proposal concrete but **do not grant admission**, do not supply a development SD, and must be bound into the exact accepted registration before a test opens. If the principal chooses another utility criterion, invalidate this threshold acceptance and commission review of the revised criterion.

Full-input specification versus baseline is primary; the three ablations are declared diagnostic comparisons, never alternative primary winners. The initial co-primary family contains seven required comparisons: stage vs its baseline; finance vs its baseline; equity vs event and prior-return baselines; workflow time, source accuracy and omitted-risk comparisons versus source-reading. Workflow accuracy and omitted-risk noninferiority margins are each 0.01 (one percentage point), with task-level rubric denominators frozen before enrollment; lower confidence bound for accuracy difference must exceed -0.01 and upper bound for omitted-risk difference must be below +0.01. Control this exact seven-test family with Holm alpha 0.05. Power/precision planning uses conservative alpha 0.05/7; neither dropping an unavailable family after inspecting another family's results nor promoting the best ablation reduces the multiplicity budget. Target-specific tests must be retained as unavailable when their owner has not admitted them.

For a development-only variance estimate `s_c` of the **paired cluster-level loss difference**, initial 80%-power planning is `ceil(((z_(1-alpha/7)+z_.8)*s_c/gap)^2)`; precision planning is `ceil((z_(1-alpha/(2*7))*s_c/h)^2)`. For qualification against the utility null `effect <= delta`, the explicit planning alternative is `effect = 2*delta`, giving `gap = delta`; this is an assumed alternative for design, not an observed effect. A calculation that detects delta against zero does NOT prove 80% power to clear delta. For workflow noninferiority, the planning alternative is difference zero against the relevant +/-0.01 boundary, giving gap 0.01. Use the larger figure, then validate small-sample/unequal-cluster behavior with the frozen development-only simulation; do not call normal planning arithmetic final power proof. Cluster weighting is equal per independent cluster, so an issuer with 30 repeated events does not outweigh one with a single event. Decide whether any giant connected component makes issuer/program holdout infeasible before assigning rows.

There is **no admissible paired-loss development panel in the inspected data**, so do not insert a measured SD or a convenient sample size. A distribution-free bounded alternative is mathematically possible for Brier differences in [-1,1] and paired workflow times in [-45,45]: `n >= R^2 * log(2*7/.05)/(2*h^2)` independent clusters gives the seven-comparison simultaneous Hoeffding half-width.
