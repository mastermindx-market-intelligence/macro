# PAC-3: prime, supplier and market-recognition research case

As-of research date: September 16, 2026. Principal: Sol. Parent: WS:DEFENSE-PROCUREMENT-V3. This is an evidence-backed research case and proposed acceptance exemplar, NOT a production signal, a human-admitted D5 mapping, or a current investment recommendation. Existing research carrier #7175 is retained. No source, identity, financial, event or portfolio owner is replaced.

## Investor question

Does the PAC-3 production expansion create a better incremental economic opportunity in the prime or its propulsion supplier, and what additional evidence would distinguish an unrecognized opportunity from an already-anticipated expansion?

The answer cannot be determined by adding announced contract values or comparing them with market capitalization. The first required step is to identify what each announcement actually commits, which level of the supply chain receives it, the associated costs and delivery period, and what investors already expected.

## Three observations that must not be collapsed

**The government contract.** The July 30 official contracts notice identifies Lockheed Martin Corp., modification P00002 to W31P4Q-26-C-0013, awarded July 29. It describes a $53,859,843,289 ceiling increase and cumulative face value of $58,620,843,289 for a seven-year procurement conversion. The notice explicitly reports zero funds obligated at award and an estimated completion of March 31, 2035. Those fields describe this action, not every subsequent funding action or the company's eventual revenue. [S1]

**The issuer announcement.** Lockheed's July 29 release presents the cumulative PAC-3 award and production expansion. The cumulative figure is not an additional amount to add to the modification. Differences in issuer and official wording should remain linked to their respective sources; a headline describing funding cannot overwrite the action's explicit obligation field. [S2]

**The supplier award.** L3Harris's September 8 release reports a $4.7 billion, seven-year undefinitized award from Lockheed for PAC-3 MSE propulsion. It expects two Camden facilities to operate in 2027 and warns about continued funding up to the full value. This is an issuer-reported prime-to-supplier award, not a separate $4.7 billion federal obligation. Final economics, actual cash timing and legal-entity attachment still require evidence. [S3]

No amount above is asserted as recognized revenue, cash received, profit, backlog recognized under an issuer's accounting policy, or new market demand outside the related program. The older similarly rounded $4.7 billion Lockheed announcement must not be joined to the L3Harris award by amount similarity. Exact transaction identity and source scope control the join.

## Economic hypotheses to test, not conclusions to publish as alpha

The prime may gain additional production volume and multiyear visibility while committing capital and accepting execution risk. The supplier may have greater sensitivity within its relevant segment, but capacity expansion, funding uncertainty and negotiated pricing can consume that upside. Neither position is automatically superior.

A useful financial bridge needs a joint delivery-and-cost scenario: incremental deliverable units, attributable revenue, production ramp, cash margins, capital expenditure, working capital, customer advances, and the funded or conditional portion of the opportunity. Unsupported program margins and issuer exposure shares stay unknown. Do not spread a headline evenly across seven years and call that an earnings forecast.

The cost paid by the prime for a supplier's work and the supplier's revenue are linked stages in one supply chain. They can each matter to their own issuer, but summing both as independent final demand double counts the program. Comparing issuer sensitivities requires a consistent value-added/cash-flow interpretation rather than a sector total assembled from contract headlines.

An expectations hypothesis must be tested separately. A capacity expansion publicly described months earlier may already influence guidance, consensus and valuation before the supplier agreement becomes more concrete. As a separate contemporaneous control, RTX's February five-munitions-framework release says the associated investments were already contemplated in its 2026 outlook. That statement is not about LMT/LHX; it illustrates why a new announcement cannot automatically be labeled an unanticipated financial improvement. [S4]

## What would strengthen or weaken the research case

Strengthening evidence would include official funding progression tied to the exact contract, a sufficiently defined supplier agreement, independently supported capacity milestones, a favorable change in cash-flow guidance or a credible scenario above existing expectations, and market/theme participation consistent with the hypothesis.

Weakening evidence would include funding below the assumed delivery path, ramp delays, unfavorable contract economics, capital requirements consuming the profit increase, or valuation already implying the optimistic scenario. A strong stock move is evidence of recognition, not proof that no upside remains; a quiet stock is not proof that the opportunity is overlooked.

No market-relative return, valuation-implied growth rate, options signal, consensus gap or optimal entry is calculated here. That requires the existing price/financial/expectations owners and point-in-time inputs. The research watch condition is to resolve those missing economic and recognition variables, not to buy because procurement news is positive.

## Minimum machine and product acceptance using this case

1. Resolve the official contract/action and issuer-reported supplier agreement as distinct records. Preserve the different source types and legal-entity certainty. Do not make a parent-brand mention establish a subsidiary's contract role.
2. Keep modification value, cumulative ceiling, action obligation, supplier agreement value, and subsequent deliveries in distinct fields and clocks. Later funding must append/reconcile, not rewrite the original zero-obligation action.
3. Link the related program and economic relationships through the existing reviewed D5/identity owners. D5 v1's human-admission requirement is still controlling; Sol research is not relabeled as a human review. No new graph or review plane is created.
4. Show both issuers' sourced financial exposure and uncertainty without arbitrary purity, confidence, or return scores. Procurement events remain separate from financial expectations and from market recognition.
5. Present the strongest contrary case and next observable condition. Any promoted entry feature must demonstrate incremental forecasting value against sector/price/earnings baselines on true first-availability data and prospective observations.
