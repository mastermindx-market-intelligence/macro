## 3. Product and intelligence architecture

### Sector and theme participation

Compose canonical market data and Thematic Intelligence/Group Reads. Compare broad-market-relative defense performance, equal- and capitalization-weighted participation, breadth, volume, dispersion, persistence, and exposure to commercial aerospace. Separate a genuine broad defense bid from one large constituent moving after earnings.

Within the sector, use predeclared, versioned mission/product baskets: air and missile defense; tactical missiles and replenishment; propulsion and energetics; drones/counter-drone; space/sensors/communications; combat aviation and sustainment; shipbuilding/submarines; electronic warfare/cyber; strategic modernization; and enabling services/components. These are proposed analytical groupings, not a new canonical taxonomy or a claim that every constituent is already mapped. Reconcile them with existing theme/security owners.

Market leadership and fundamental opportunity are different. A strong stock may already price the improvement; a laggard may have poor economics rather than undiscovered upside. Require an explanation for divergence instead of an automatic catch-up trade.

### Event -> mission -> company -> financial consequence

Use the existing event and program graph. A relationship must specify issuer and legal entity, effective ownership interval, segment, product/program, role (prime, subcontractor, propulsion, sensor, integrator or sustainment), evidence, and limits. A broad corporate missile tag is inadequate. Unknown revenue share remains unknown; a product association is not permission to attribute all segment revenue to it.

Keep four clocks/cycles visible: demand pressure; budget/contract commitment; production and cash realization; market recognition. They need not turn together. A share price can react before an award is public, and record backlog need not imply improving margins.

For each demand development, ask what changes in quantities, timing, eligibility, production capacity, contract economics, and working capital. Model joint scenarios rather than multiplying unsupported independent probabilities. Respect fixed-price versus cost-type economics, scope changes, deobligations, price renegotiation, delivery delays, capital expenditure, customer advances, and dilution. Do not add prime and supplier contract values as independent end demand.

Illustrative financial bridge, not an admitted formula: incremental deliverable units x attributable revenue per unit x incremental cash margin, less incremental capital expenditure and working capital, timed over the delivery path. Inputs need sources or explicit scenario ranges. Compare the resulting cash-flow sensitivity with the company's size and valuation, not only with other contractors' headline award totals. Unobserved inputs must not become precise model outputs.

### Expectations and asymmetry

'Not priced in' is a hypothesis, never a field directly observed in procurement records. Compare a scenario with time-valid management guidance, licensed consensus where available, disclosed capacity plans, valuation-implied requirements, and the price/volume/option response already observed. Missing consensus must be labelled; do not invent it or scrape restricted sources.

Display separate assessments for evidence quality, economic materiality, execution risk, market recognition, and timing. Do not average them into an arbitrary 0–100 conviction score. Present the strongest contrary explanation, downside scenario, catalyst needed for recognition, and what would invalidate the thesis.

The research board should distinguish: strengthening economics with limited observed recognition; strengthening economics with substantial recognition/extension; price strength without confirming economics; and neither. These are transparent research descriptions, not automatically executable portfolio instructions.

### Broader context

Consume, do not duplicate, existing geopolitical/news, Policy Watch, energy, and company-event owners. Classify confirmed events, statements, enacted changes, proposed changes, scenarios and contested reports separately. Evaluate procurement and industrial consequences, not political preferences. Treat conflict likelihood estimates as forecasts requiring calibration, not confident LLM narratives. Public stockpile/consumption information may be incomplete; do not manufacture depletion counts or precise replenishment dates.

Oil is conditional context: distinguish supply disruption, stronger demand, inflation, and transport interruption. Neither an oil rally nor a generic escalation headline is a universal positive defense signal. RTX's commercial business, program execution risks, and supply-chain costs can produce competing effects.

## 4. Two-speed evidence without two truth systems

Official GSA documentation states that public revealed data for contracts both funded and awarded by DoD requires a signing date at least 90 days old; access to newer unrevealed data is restricted. This qualification does not say every defense-related public announcement is delayed. Source: https://open.gsa.gov/api/contract-awards/ (Revealed/Unrevealed Data and user requirements, read September 15).

Use lawful fast observations from official announcements, company releases and permitted news, with explicitly incomplete coverage. Reconcile them later against procurement, funding, filings and deliveries in the SAME event/identity system. Fast observations never become population authority merely because they are timely. Preserve D0R and D6-B source laws; a new production acquisition rail needs its own source/rights review.

For every observation retain effective/event time, source publication time and precision, system first-seen time, model-availability time, version/hash, and corrections. A backfill discovered today cannot be used as if the system possessed it at the original contract date. Current article content under an old dateline is not historical first-availability proof.

Keep notification estimates, frameworks, undefinitized actions, definitive awards, obligated amounts, deliveries, revenue and cash as distinct facts; not every procurement path follows one universal sequence. Match duplicate announcements/modifications without deleting the historical evidence. A correction invalidates dependent current conclusions and appends a new version; it never silently rewrites a frozen forecast.

## 7. Validation and learning contract

Test procurement's incremental contribution against price/market/sector and earnings baselines, not a retrospective story about a few winning stocks. Predeclare universes, themes, event families, horizons, exclusions and costs. Use time-valid security/segment histories, clustered event/conflict holdouts, and purged overlapping outcomes. Compare sector-only, procurement-only, context-only, and combined models. Include cancellations, cost overruns, disappointing conversion and rallies already reflected in expectations.

Financial forecast accuracy and stock-return prediction are different outcomes. Measure both. Report confidence intervals, drawdowns, false positives, missed opportunities, turnover, execution cost, and stability across regimes. Do not impose a decorative sample-count threshold as a substitute for power and independent observations. The earlier oil-correlation study is exploratory advisory research, not an accepted signal qualification.

Initially, useful evidence and research watch conditions can ship without claiming validated alpha. LLMs may extract, connect and explain with evidence, and propose hypotheses for review; they cannot manufacture facts, probabilities or trading authority. Promote only independently tested features through the existing model/Prophet governance, never through a freeform news sentiment score.
