## 1. Decision and the product we are recovering

Select the existing Defense Procurement & Industrial Base Intelligence V3 program. Its investor product should answer: **Which listed businesses have a credible opportunity, what evidence changes their economics, how much uncertainty remains, what has price already reflected, and what would invalidate the thesis?** It is not a contract-news dashboard and not a defense operating system. We use a descriptive product name here; the canonical program and original source carriers remain unchanged. [S02–S05]

FIF and Single-Name Intelligence are excluded. So are Grey Deer Risk, Market OS/Mastermind OS, Options Intelligence, Prophet, Market Ontology, GMI, Executive OS and Subagent Fabric buildout. Existing systems can supply read-only, admitted inputs; their implementation or repair is not silently absorbed into this mission. The old V3 D19 Prophet integration is explicitly excluded, not marked complete. Portfolio stress modeling, options research and a new universal relationship graph are also excluded.

The choice is a reasoned ranking, not a universal difficulty measurement. The assessment enumerated 92 workstream filenames and compared current evidence for Defense, Capital Structure, Rates, Liquidity, BioCatalyst and Market Memory, reusing the previous broader census where unchanged. Defense combines a particularly difficult causal chain: **public policy → funded demand → legally attributable work → feasible delivery → issuer revenue/margin/cash → expectations and price → independently evaluated research opportunity**. Every arrow can fail. Capital Structure is narrower; Rates depends more heavily on excluded options/ontology work; BioCatalyst, Market Memory and Research Vault already have substantial incumbent programs that should not receive a competing fleet. Defense itself has an incumbent reactivation carrier, #7175; this is its recovery companion, not a vacancy claim. [S02, S03, S21]

### What makes it hard—and valuable

A budget proposal is not an appropriation. An indefinite-delivery ceiling is not a funded order. A possible foreign sale is not a signed sale, a contractor award or recognized revenue. Prime and supplier revenue are not independent end demand. More orders can coincide with poor margins, execution charges and cash consumption. A price increase can reflect broad sector participation rather than incremental company economics. The product must explain those distinctions and preserve unsupported links as unknowns. [E01–E05]

The strongest rival explanation to the investment thesis is that the information arrives too late, is already reflected in prices, or is too incomplete to estimate issuer capture. That can falsify a forecast family without making the source-linked research workflow worthless. Product usefulness and predictive efficacy receive separate acceptance decisions.

## 4. The four axes that keep the product intelligible

Retain the #7175 protocol as the design input to reconcile, not as automatically merged authority. [S04]

**Participation:** Is the existing defense basket receiving broad participation or only a few large stocks? Preserve the owner’s membership vintage, coverage denominator, dates and concentration. An activity leader is not a proven alpha leader.

**Demand/theme:** What public evidence changes policy, funding, export-stage or program demand? Preserve proposal, authorization, appropriation, obligation, order, delivery and sustainment distinctions. Keep a reported geopolitical event separate from a scenario; no inferred operational readiness, classified stockpile or targeting intelligence.

**Issuer economics:** Does the specific business capture the demand after workshare, capacity, contract type, cost, capex, working capital, ownership interests and dilution? Use exact segment/company sources. Capacity announcements are not demonstrated throughput, and federal funding is not company backlog. [S03, S12, E06]

**Recognition/expectations:** What has price done, what does a stated valuation scenario imply, and which expectations are actually observed? A price-equivalent earnings hurdle is algebra under assumptions, not “the market’s expectation.” Without licensed cutoff-valid estimates or a validated estimator, “not priced in” remains an unproven hypothesis.

Do not average these axes into an invented 0–100 conviction score. The overview can say “demand supported, company capture unresolved” and show why. Later research prioritization or investment recommendations require a separately accepted policy and calibrated evidence. Existing all-false ranking/sizing/gating/signal authority remains unless its proper owner grants a specific earned change. No Prophet or live capital change is part of this commission.

## 5. Research program that can earn—not assume—an edge

### Competing hypotheses

H1: Funding or awards add useful evidence beyond price, sector and known earnings information. Falsifier: no out-of-time incremental predictive value after actual availability and revisions are respected.

H2: Execution, contract mix and supplier capture explain why similar demand shocks have different equity outcomes. Falsifier: the proposed economic features add no stable information beyond simple controls or cannot be measured before outcomes.

H3: An adverse event can be temporary rather than structural. Falsifier: the distinction depends on hindsight labels, improves only selected anecdotes, or fails unseen programs/issuers.

H4: A source-grounded research workflow reduces analyst error and effort even when forecasts do not win. Falsifier: blinded task evaluation shows no improvement in source accuracy, omitted risks or time to a defensible conclusion.

### Time and sample design

Public DoD contract data can be withheld for 90 days; early absence is not proof of no award. Event studies must use the public/house availability actually supported by the selected source, not backdate a USAspending row to its award signature. Same-day press releases may support a separate earlier observation, but do not establish complete population coverage. Record revisions and date-only uncertainty explicitly. [E03, E04]

Freeze event families, cohort eligibility, prediction timestamp, outcomes, split rules, baselines, trial budget, sample-size/precision criterion and adjudication rule before testing. Include cancellations, de-obligations, unawarded solicitations, unchanged reports and failed thesis cases. Retrospective data downloaded today is reconstruction, not prospective history. Repeated reports, multiple related issuers and overlapping event windows are clustered rather than counted as independent discoveries.

Begin with simple baselines: no-change/stage base rates, sector/market-relative return, source-quality-only prioritization and a transparent archetype model. Compare new methods only against an explicitly selected baseline and frozen outcome. Do not promote a hand-tuned backtest because it has the highest return among many tried variants.

Estimate **funding progression**, **financial conversion** and **market response** separately. Even calibrated marginal probabilities cannot be multiplied as independent without evidence. Use a joint or conditional model when justified. Otherwise present conditional scenarios without fabricated probabilities. Unresolved event progression is censored; it is not automatically failure.

### Transparent economic scenarios

Map a source-supported change to incremental issuer revenue, margin, working capital and cash by explicit period and basis. If workshare, conversion timing or shareholder capture is unknown, return a bounded qualitative scenario or typed unknown—not the entire government amount assigned to every linked ticker. Avoid double counting prime and supplier value. Report contract losses and cash requirements even when demand is growing.

Valuation scenarios must state discount/multiple assumptions, share basis and what is already in the reported baseline. A probability-weighted value requires supported probabilities; otherwise retain separate scenarios. Show downside and invalidation beside upside. None is a model-originated trade size.

### Evaluation and promotion

Use target-appropriate proper scores, calibration/reliability with uncertainty, discrimination and interval sharpness where applicable. Evaluate economic outcomes as well as returns; a good funding-stage classifier is not a profitable stock signal. Use company/program/time holdouts and benchmark paired differences with dependence accounted for. Independently reproduce the decisive tests. Preserve failed hypotheses and contaminated holdouts.

A NO-GO can close a research family. A pending horizon cannot. Produce the real observation product while prospective evidence accrues under an existing durable owner. Forecast panes remain research-only or unavailable until their acceptance gate passes. The final program report distinguishes product completion, research dispositions and remaining natural-time obligations.
