# HALO: historical oversight context, pending identity and source admission

NASA OIG memorandum ML-26-002, published June24,2026, reports that NASA converted HALO to a cost-plus-award-fee contract in September2024 and issued a stop-work order in April2026, when the reported contract value was $1.9billion. That amount is contract value at the reported event, not Northrop recognized revenue, remaining backlog or cash received.

The OIG described July2031 delivery and a Gateway operation date of at least2032 only as projections conditional on HALO continuing to completion. NASA's response says those projections use outdated architectural assumptions. The memorandum is interim; neither the conditional projection nor the historical stop-work observation establishes the current October2026 status.

For the Space investor thesis, the old award amount alone cannot support a claim of current backlog conversion. The report offers evidence of contract structure and execution uncertainty, but does not quantify HALO's contribution to Northrop's separately reported Space Systems quarterly sales or profit. Current program status, the native contract identifier and exact legal-entity mapping remain necessary before admitting a program/company claim. No program graph, capacity quantity, financial owner or theme source is changed by this preparation.
