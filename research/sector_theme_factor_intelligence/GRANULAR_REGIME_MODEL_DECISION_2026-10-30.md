## Twelve factual axes, each independent measured/null, and no mega-score

Axis ID / observations / crucial distinction:
1. `absolute_trend`: price returns/MA slopes by valid RTH or session basis — positive RS can coexist with falling absolute price.
2. `rs_market`: (1+R_group)/(1+R_benchmark)-1, aligned endpoints, measured fraction.
3. `rs_parent`: same ratio against true dated parent GICS/theme; stock-level idiosyncratic vs industry strength.
4. `rs_velocity_acceleration`: predeclared rolling log-price-ratio slope and changes of slope with volatility and reference-window receipts.
5. `participation`: k/n positive returns, positive RS, >50/200MA, plus bounds [k/N,(k+N-n)/N] (not confidence intervals).
6. `breadth_velocity_acceleration`: SAME MEMBER before/after observations; roster adds/exits and loss of coverage separate.
7. `concentration_dispersion`: top-k contributions, HHI/effective issuers, EW vs properly lagged complete weights and median.
8. `multiscale_coherence`: 1/5/15/60m, RTH, 1/5/20/63/126/252 sessions; disagreements reported, no fake simultaneous data.
9. `extension_maturity`: past-known stretch/run age/drawdown, right-censored remaining-run survival research.
10. `vol_liquidity`: qualified vol/ATR/gap/depth/spread/volume and abnormality, no unlicensed flow inference.
11. `cross_asset_credit`: observed defensive/cyclical RS, empirical rolling correlation/tail sign with stability test, dated credit spread; never 10:30 AM 'live' daily ICE OAS.
12. `evidence_quality`: exact source, asof/known/available/correction, membership PIT vs CURRENT, rights, observed/eligible/expected n/N, true vs ancestor proxy. **Not a return predictor by default**.

Each axes has `status measured|unmeasured`, `value,unit,horizon,cohort,asof,known_at,source_ids,denominator,reference,reason`. Incompatible and unknown data abstain, never zero/neutral. A single stock uses its own factors plus selected named *peer cohort* breadth; overlapping theme memberships are not independent votes.

### State representation (observed only)

At decision time `t`, group/name `g`, horizon `h`, define
`F[g,t,h] = {axis_i(value, units, source, available_at, period_end, cohort, coverage, bounds, null_reason)}`.
Never collapse `F` to a unique 4/12/24-state partition or a synthetic confidence
percent. A morphological tag is a deterministic predicate over specifically named
observations, e.g.

- `positive_parent_rs_narrowing_same_member_breadth` only if
  `RS_parent > 0` AND `(k_after/n_common - k_before/n_common) < 0`
  on the **same measured members**. Coverage arrivals, roster entries, lost
  quotes and unknowns are counted separately.
- `positive_group_return_but_majority_nonpositive` only if complete observed
  cohort equal-weight return is positive AND `upper_bound(positive-return
  breadth) < 0.5`; this proves a majority were **flat or negative**,
  NOT that a majority declined. A missing/flat member must never be
  reclassified as a decliner.
- `cross_horizon_parent_rs_sign_conflict` iff genuinely measured
  `RS_parent` has opposite signs on two explicitly labeled periods. It does
  NOT mean the numeric 5d return is commensurable with a 63d return.
- A single name can be stronger than its parent **while the group itself lags
  the broad market**. That is a descriptive divergence, not an alpha score or
  proof the stock is insulated from group weakness.

RS for aligned, basis-compatible period returns is
`RW(i,b,t,h) = (1 + R_i(t,h)) / (1 + R_b(t,h)) - 1`;
the benchmark must have strictly positive final wealth. Momentum slope is
a future factor **candidate**: fit a predeclared regression of historical
`log(P_i/P_b)` on elapsed session time *using only bars known at t*, return
slope/robust scale with exact lookback. Acceleration is the causal difference
of at least two admissible slopes, not the sign of today's simple return.
A two-endpoint breadth first difference is NOT acceleration; three or more
qualified points and a fixed clock are required for second differences.

**Effective population** must declare membership known-at, active issuer/stock
resolution, duplicate securities, overlapping theme memberships, expected N,
eligible n and missing reasons. A theme constituent appearing in two groups is
not two independent observations in uncertainty estimates. Where the canonical
GMI graph offers only ancestor proxy for a micro/subtheme, the axis is
`proxy_not_micro_membership` or null; never a fake child cohort.

### Research outcomes to freeze before any historical label access

For each (h\in\{1,5,20,63\}) *trading sessions*, outcome family definitions
must fix adjustment basis, group-vs-benchmark selection and start/end market
session, then evaluate separately:

1. **Forward relative performance:** `1[(1+R_g,next_h)/(1+R_b,next_h)-1 > 0]`
   plus actual continuous excess-relative-wealth distribution. Do not average
   a 5-day signal and 63-day target without announcing it.
2. **Forward breadth repair/deterioration:** compare the **t-cohort** members'
   next-horizon measured trend/participation; do not retrospectively substitute
   the t+h roster. Missing next outcomes remain censored/qualified, not zeros.
3. **Downside:** next-horizon worst marked-to-market adverse excursion, with
   volatility-adjusted threshold fixed in the eventual prereg (and cost/exit
   basis where strategy-relevant).
4. **Leadership retention:** rank stays in a *frozen observed peer universe*
   under a predeclared tier boundary; ties and unmeasured peers refuse ranking.
5. **Remaining run survival:** time to a predeclared RS/breadth failure event,
   treating open runs and end-of-history as right-censored. Estimate duration
   conditional on current run age; do not label all unfinished runs "success."

These outcomes can overlap. They are **separate event probabilities** and must
not sum to 100%. Only a genuinely mutually exclusive, exhaustive partition,
with all unknowns accounted for, may become a categorical transition matrix.
Macro GRI's nine authored overlapping scenarios are contextual reading paths,
not statistically estimated probabilities.

### Leakage, data maturity and statistical proof

- At each decision time create an **issue-equivalent snapshot**: everything
  has `available_at <= t`, with immutable original observation provenance.
  Retrospectively reconstructed/adjusted mutable prices may serve descriptive
  analyses only unless lawful PIT original vintages can be proved.
- Training examples enter only once their forward target has matured by
  the training cut. Purge/embargo overlapping holding/target windows and
  adjacent issuer/theme and calendar clusters. Split chronologically; never
  random-fold optimize on repeated overlapping event returns.
- Fit normalization, missing-value treatment, beta, correlations, thresholds,
  state transitions and calibrators inside each training fold only. No
  hindsight-curated basket rosters or future full-sample HMM posteriors.
- Primary proper binary metrics: Brier score and log loss versus calibrated
  base rate. Report reliability by bins, uncertainty/number of independent
  events and subgroup/market-era drift; AUC is supplemental, not calibration.
  For distributional returns, use a justified proper distribution score and
  credible predictive intervals; for survival, censored-safe measures.
- Evaluate *early warning* separately: median and tail lead time before
  prespecified confirmed transition, false alerts per 100 session-days,
  missed-events, signal churn and incremental value over contemporaneous
  price-only alarm. A faster alarm that fires constantly is worse.
- Report clustered/block-bootstrap uncertainty at independent calendar/issuer
  levels, multiple-hypothesis adjustment, ablation of each factor family,
  model selection and transaction-cost sensitivity. Too few independent
  major drawdowns -> `NOT_EXECUTABLE / INSUFFICIENT_POWER`, not optimistic odds.
- Prospective shadow forecasts must be **issued and retained before targets**
  become available. Independent non-author assessment gates any customer
  forecast, probability, ranking, sizing or alert consumer.
