## 2. Executive finding

Mastermind was not wholly blind, but the inspected architecture does not demonstrate the closed decision loop the Chairman expects. It has useful risk, leadership, rotation and entry components. Some prominent outputs answer different questions on different clocks, the live US ranker's theme evidence is explicitly absent, and the recent cross-product rotation/risk integration deliberately did not alter Prophet selection or trade behavior. This combination can produce a page full of individually plausible observations without a timely, coherent decision.

The important distinction is not simply backend versus frontend. The failure can occur at **observation, timing, candidate discovery, authority/eligibility, prioritization, publication, presentation, or delivery**. Each must receive a separate verdict. “A signal existed somewhere” is not user delivery; “a ticker was absent from six featured cards” is not proof it was never recommended.

### 2.1 Pre-event risk existed, but loud broad-market confirmation was withheld

The retained October 8 audit reports an October 5 session row issued on October 6 at 05:55:30 UTC: rates pressure 90.5, credit 81.1, internals 78.0; MOVE and credit legs confirmed; ungated risk-off, final caution, alert false. Current `risk_radar.py` still makes the live broad-context gate depend on SPY below its 200-day average AND weak breadth. Its loud alert starts at elevated. A separate, scoped advisory about concentrated leadership damage must not require the broad index to break first. Pressure, measured market state and capital policy remain different concepts. [S02]

### 2.2 The live US ranker does not actually contain the expected theme input

Current `us_prophet_fusion.py` explicitly marks F3_THEME_STRUCTURE absent because board rows carry no theme/basket/relay evidence column. ### 2.3 Fast observations exist; confirmed rotation is intentionally later

Current Rotation Command has early 1/2/5/20-session ratio context, a confirmed paired-event detector, lifecycle, velocity board and contagion fields. Its early projection uses positive two-session relative movement, reports coverage and sign conflicts, distinguishes both-down resilience, and has display-only authority. It is not a calibrated forecast. [S05]

The v1 confirmed handoff requires a completed donor blowoff/crash, a receiver turn and a confirming ratio. The donor crash floor is 10%; the receiver turn requires at least 3% off its recent low. The v2 into-strength branch requires a donor signature, a receiver at least 8% off its 40-session low, at least 5 percentage points of 20-session SPY-relative strength, at least 65% of members above their 50-day average with rising breadth, and two-session transition confirmation. These conditions can describe an established handoff while missing the earliest actionable recovery. They are not the only early mechanisms: `leadership_crack.py` already has a five-session relative-velocity route capable of firing without waiting for a 12–20% drawdown. [S06–S08]

The appropriate extension is an earlier, qualified opportunity/risk path beside the confirmed lifecycle, not relabeling early context as a confirmed trade and not silently lowering every legacy threshold.

## 4. Selected architecture: five connected decisions, not one score

### 4.1 Observation: what changed?

Reuse Data OS identity and source clocks, Ratio Lens mathematics, sector/theme membership and breadth producers. Carry absolute and benchmark-relative returns at 1/2/3/5/10 sessions alongside existing structural horizons. Same-source observations should be computed once and consumed by all surfaces.

For basket g and horizon h, breadth is the fraction of eligible, sufficiently covered members satisfying a declared condition, such as positive h-session excess return or a fresh reclaim. Publish numerator, denominator, coverage and weighting. Keep equal-weight, cap-weight and median-member views distinguishable. A two-stock basket must not look as statistically supported as a hundred-stock basket.

Candidate derivatives, for research rather than immediate trade authority:

`velocity_k = (breadth_t - breadth_(t-k)) / k`

`acceleration_k = (velocity_k,t - velocity_k,t-k) / k`.

Use session-aware windows and predeclared smoothing; measure incremental value against breadth level alone. Second differences amplify noise. Changes caused by membership, coverage or corporate-action revisions must be separated from genuine participation changes. All normalizers and beta estimates use only prior available data.

Pair context should report receiver and donor absolute returns, relative-ratio movement, participation, extension, source quality and shape. RECEIVER_UP/DONOR_DOWN is not the same as both falling with one falling less. It is not a measured flow between investors. Overlapping stocks, ETF constituents and price-derived indicators share evidence roots rather than multiplying conviction.

A receiver can be worth watching or entering under its own valid strategy without waiting for a donor to crash. A paired rotation can corroborate the opportunity but must not become a universal prerequisite for candidate discovery.

### 4.2 Interpretation: what kind of transition is this?

Risk Envelope remains the common explanation surface. Preserve measured market state, hazard and capital policy separately. Add qualified subtype evidence to the existing context, not a replacement market score.

Use potentially coexisting hypotheses: healthy broadening; defensive leadership shift; duration/inflation pressure; crowded-winner unwind; liquidity-driven liquidation; systemic transmission. Do not force a single exclusive label when the evidence supports both rotation and rising stress.

Discriminating evidence includes breadth of absolute declines, receiver participation and extension, prior-leader damage, downside correlation, credit/funding/liquidity measures when current and qualified, rates and cross-asset confirmation. Dow up and Nasdaq down alone cannot rule out financial stress. Conversely, a weak semiconductor basket does not justify a universal liquidation instruction.

Every narrative separates observed facts, inference and scenarios. State what would strengthen the working explanation and what would invalidate it. A shock/catalyst can support an explanation, but its publication time cannot be imported into an earlier warning.

### 4.3 Opportunity: what should Prophet examine now?

Feed identity-bound sector/theme observations into the existing candidate discovery/router and permitted F3 evidence path. Do not only decorate already-selected names. Keep a bounded but coverage-explicit opportunity set spanning new turns, persistent leadership and risk review; do not silently impose a six-card discovery quota.

Macro state routes strategy suitability or explicit interactions; it is not a constant per-name bonus. Sector and theme memberships are time-valid and can overlap; avoid double credit for the same underlying observation. Missing membership or breadth is disclosed, not a neutral score.

Separate early reversal/reclaim strategies from continuation/pullback strategies through the incumbent strategy-definition owner. This is not a blanket waiver of a 200-day rule. A strategy needing an earlier entry must have its own admissible evidence, geometry, horizon, invalidation and qualification. Reuse existing early-leadership/sector-rotation definitions where applicable before adding any definition.

Prophet's pipeline becomes `qualified opportunity context -> existing expert/candidate owner -> B1/B3 identity -> existing strategy definition and B4 -> priority within the permitted lane -> actual surfaced idea`. No favorable narrative, theme strength or forecast can override missing required facts or a breached stop.

### 4.4 Actionability and protection: what can be done now?

A structural leader, an emerging receiver and an executable entry are different things. Present B4's authoritative entry state next to the strategy and source clock. A current price beyond chase limits stays RAN_DONT_CHASE or WAIT_PULLBACK even when the theme is attractive.

Maintain separate new-entry and existing-idea review paths. Sector deterioration can stop adding or call for an existing strategy review without pretending every holding is invalidated. Exact candidate/Plan evidence, current thesis and name-specific invalidation must reach the existing management owner. Portfolio remains responsible for held exposure, concentration and sizing. No invented half-size rule, automatic trade or model-originated allocation percentage is introduced here.

“Aggressive” means earlier coverage, rapid qualification, prominent actionable entries and prompt risk review—not forcing a purchase after the move or announcing that every oversold technology stock is dangerous. The recovery of yesterday's losers is itself a competing scenario.

### 4.5 Continuity: what did we say, and how did it evolve?

Use the existing event/history owners. Build a read projection, not another ledger. Keep observation and recommendation events linked to their native identities; create missing event semantics only within the authorized incumbent owner.

Required distinctions: first source observation, first system availability, first issued recommendation, first publication, first delivery, latest change, correction/retraction and current validity. A reconstructed earliest date must never become an earlier issued timestamp.

The user-facing progression is watch -> approach -> entry open -> continuation/extended -> risk review -> invalidated/repaired, mapped to existing native states rather than inventing a second state machine. Show both original and current assessment. Persist unresolved risk on screen even when notification deduplication suppresses repetitive sends. Acknowledging a notice does not erase subsequent deterioration.

## 5. Forecasting program: anticipate without pretending to know tomorrow

The target is calibrated conditional prediction, not an animated historical trail labeled “where it is heading.” Predict separately: receiver continuation versus failed turn, donor further damage versus repair, and spread to broader stress. Define 3/5/10/21-session horizons, as well as any separately qualified intraday horizon. The existing >=5% SPY pullback target cannot validate a one-to-three-session semiconductor warning.

Pre-register outcome rules and economically meaningful barriers before evaluation. Use volatility-normalized return and adverse-excursion labels, and time-to-event/competing-risk treatment where appropriate. Barriers, costs and decision utility belong to the evaluation/strategy owner, not ad hoc model sizing. Unresolved outcomes are censored; overlapping daily observations are not independent episodes.

Start with transparent baselines: structural RS only; short-horizon ratio change; breadth level; breadth plus velocity; existing entry strategy; existing risk gate. Add acceleration, crowding, options, credit or change-point signals one family at a time. Compare a pooled model with horizon/regime-conditioned models; use shrinkage and abstention for sparse cohorts rather than creating dozens of untestable rules.

Industry-momentum research motivates sector-conditioned analysis; industry lead-lag research motivates testing information diffusion. Neither validates minute-level turning points or this implementation. Momentum-crash research supplies a necessary counter-hypothesis: prior losers can rebound sharply in stressed states, so blindly extrapolating donor weakness can fail. Online Bayesian change-point detection is a candidate mechanism for measuring changing behavior, not proof of directional alpha. [P02–P05]

Qualification requires point-in-time membership and fundamentals where used; split/dividend-consistent prices; actual availability clocks; no revised data leakage; delisted/failed names; untouched holdout episodes; walk-forward model selection; overlap-aware purging/embargo; trial accounting; realistic spreads, fill latency and turnover. Evaluate detection delay, missed pivots, false alerts, adverse excursion before/after warning, opportunity capture, failed reversals and portfolio-relevant utility, not only hit rate.

Use proper probability scores such as Brier/log score, calibration by cohort/horizon, and uncertainty intervals. Compare against base rates and simple baselines. A backtest selected from many trials is not independently validated by a good equity curve. The October episode is a discovery case, not an untouched test set. No predictive performance result was computed in this investigation. [P06–P07]

Do not hold clear source-backed warnings and existing-lawful entry visibility hostage to a long predictive research program. Descriptive, entry-availability and forecasting releases have separate evidence gates.

## 6. Product and interaction design

The first screen must answer: **what changed; where exposure is weakening; what is strengthening; which entries are actually open; what would invalidate the view; and when this was first identified.** Evidence and model details belong in progressive disclosure, not a wall of competing numbers.

Use the existing shared shell and Prophet cockpit. Recommended main area: one concise rotation/risk brief, a clear affected-cohort risk strip, and opportunity lanes for New Turns, Valid Continuations, Wait/Do Not Chase, and Risk Review. These are presentation groups over existing states, not new trading authorities. Empty lanes remain honest; no quota forces suggestions.

Each idea shows security/company name, economic cohort, strategy/horizon, current native availability, first signal/first entry dates, fresh quote versus decision-basis time, distance from permitted entry, invalidation and a short change explanation. Avoid truncating the name or key action. Counts must reconcile across preview and full list; “six featured” must not imply only six observed or qualified names.

Keep the rotation matrix as a secondary exploration view. Add a selected entity's historical trail and a daily comparison strip. Historical arrows are labeled historical; forecasts, when qualified, are visually separate. Default to tactical and structural views side by side or an unmistakable horizon switch, not a single mixed-horizon quadrant. The absence of healthcare in structural Leading must not hide a qualified tactical healthcare opportunity.

Provide an as-issued timeline with session navigation and meaningful changes, including inactive/invalidated recommendations. A reconstructed-price timeline is visibly a different mode. Show no fabricated old recommendation or execution result. A before/after card should explain “why changed,” not merely show a different rank.

Illustrative copy grammar, not a claim of prior delivery or a current trade recommendation:

> Leadership is shifting. AI-hardware participation is weakening while selected defensive groups are improving. Software leadership is more resilient, but strength does not make every entry timely. Review affected existing ideas; inspect only the names whose strategy-specific entry is currently open. The broader market's state and the risk of further spread are shown separately.

Production wording must replace each clause with qualified current facts, dates and evidence. When those facts are stale, say so prominently rather than using today's quote time to imply today's judgment.
