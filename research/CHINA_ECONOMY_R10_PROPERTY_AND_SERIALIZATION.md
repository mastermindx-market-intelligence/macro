## Evidence integrity

Redirected, oversized, wrong-type and mismatched-URL responses fail admission.
Multiple competing same-month links and unequal desktop/mobile article copies
are rejected. Exact acquired HTTP bytes, rather than a text re-encoding, supply
the response hash. Old row-level-only data is not silently promoted. A changed
value cannot borrow its old receipt. Original property legacy columns survive.
