## Placement rule

Product text answers the user's current question and makes the result, essential limitation and next action clear. Sources, methods and definitions have named on-demand destinations. Component/API/state/fixture/test/ownership explanations live in Build and versioned implementation guides. A description is optional; a content budget is a ceiling, never a quota.

Unknown, stale, partial, unconfirmed, unavailable and permission-limited states remain visible where they affect a decision. Long research, evidence and instructions that are the actual task are preserved. This pass does not reduce the product to unexplained labels.
