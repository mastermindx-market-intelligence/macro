# Marketing X: the first repeatable customer-acquisition loop

**Fable's decision brief and open delivery masterplan · 31 October 2026 · Internal design proposal, not launch approval**

**The question:** Will an investor who discovers Mastermind through a trusted publication use a specific piece of research before registering, return when the evidence changes, and eventually pay to keep using the product? Marketing X should make that question answerable even when the answer is no. Increasing post volume is neither the goal nor the test.

**My recommendation:** Prove one useful anonymous earnings read before making email, a partner, or billing a release dependency. Then prove the right to follow up, then a real distribution cohort, then paid conversion. These are evidence boundaries, not a required product architecture or a mandatory sequence for independent work. Fable may replace the scan, the channel, the entry screen, the audience, the creative, or the order if another route better tests the question. Preserve the nonnegotiables: truthful evidence, authorized use, consent, independent review, and honest attribution.

## Where Fable earns its time

Give well-defined tests, compatibility work and data checks to economical admitted Fabric operators and workers. Use Claude specialists for genuinely ambiguous identity, consent, source or billing boundaries. Keep a reviewer outside the authors' chain for security, evidence and cohort interpretation. Reserve Fable for choosing the offer, cutting scope, adjudicating conflicting subsystem evidence, and judging whether a real investor got value. Under the original operational root, conserve the actual granted budget/depth and reserve capacity for review and repair; no native Claude child trees or parallel work on occupied files.

**First integration decision:** keep the event-first entry as a default until a qualified alternative can show better first value or reach without an unapproved source. **First business decision:** distinguish weak editorial reach from weak product value. **First stop rule:** any unconsented send, rights leak, cross-account disclosure, unsupported financial claim or ambiguous irreversible effect stops the affected lane regardless of conversion.

**Proof ladder:** source reviewed → exact integrated checks → installed version observed → hosted user journey → first authorized pilot → repeat qualified demand → verified paid and retained cohort. Report the rung achieved, not the rung a successful PR suggests. Only those distinctions are fixed; the path Fable takes between them is open.
