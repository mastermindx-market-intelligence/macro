# Research, pilot design and commercial decision book

**Position to test:** “Show me which fact changed for a company I follow, where to verify it, and what would make me change my mind.” The offer is not generic earnings alerts, an email capture form, or a disguised sale. Public market research and private product analytics remain separate. The following recommendations are falsifiable design choices; they are not measured performance, publisher endorsement, economic terms or permission to operate.

## Audience and channel decisions

**First audience to test:** fundamental investors who already follow one or more issuers covered by the chosen filing. They have a question to answer today. An editor is a second user: give them an accurate, short, original insert they can inspect, decline, or correct without forwarding an audience list. A third user is the returning reader, who wants the difference from last time rather than another welcome message. Do not promise comprehensive portfolio risk, AI capex coverage or a trade signal on an earnings-only pilot.

The [full 25-row prospect index](partner-index.csv) points to each row of the [original prospect research](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/cdc07808f3d71818625dedb4edcdb1ccf0949aea/research/marketing_dockets/MKT_CATALYST_PROSPECT_REFRESH_20261008.md). Treat the 25 as a research denominator, not a mailing list. The five finalists' first-party pages were checked for fit or contact posture; no decision-maker, permission, comparable click volume or slot was verified by that check. The other 20 retain dated source links, not a claimed fresh requalification. No outreach was performed.

**Other routes Fable can choose:** first-party dossier placement for a quick usability study (mark existing traffic); a moderator-invited session for richer qualitative feedback (small denominator); approved X/Buffer distribution for reach once the existing outbox/media owner has an actual publish receipt; search only when indexability and evidence freshness have separately been accepted. A third-party platform's activity is not portable consent. No need to wait for SEO, affiliate payouts or daily social automation to prove one useful result.

## One pilot card ready to adapt

**Hypothesis:** An editor-approved, source-linked earnings insert gets eligible readers to a useful personalized result before asking for email, and some return for a later material change. **Alternative:** readers might like the editorial article but have no further use for Mastermind. **Unit:** new unique eligible visitor within one named editorial placement and frozen event/window. **Offer:** dated event, one supported issuer, one unsupported truth-state, optional verified update when admitted. **Comparison:** an honest plain editorial link; only a genuinely randomized within-publisher split supports a comparative causal claim. Cross-publisher or newsletter vs podcast is observational.

**Before exposure:** record channel/placement, supported issuer rule independently of the outcome, first eligible event, link ID, feature flags, rights scopes, anonymous and enrolled test results, expected denominator source, exclusion rules, maturity window and decision bands. Ask the editor only via the authorized operator, for permission covering exact asset, appearance, date, correction procedure, and aggregate comparable click data (the existing playbook asks for the median of three comparable placements). Do not count that median as observed traffic. A signed agreement/approval and an actual published placement are two separate receipts.

**Creative preview, not send-ready copy:** subject concept “Which part of this earnings story actually changed?” Body structure: one dated, document-backed change; one uncertainty; one link to the human research page. If the observation is an amendment, show the exact corrected assertion and why the old card is no longer current. Offer a link-only insert when media rights are absent. With no permitted, relevant event, skip that window rather than force an article. For a controlled comprehension study ask independently: “What changed?” “What does the evidence not establish?” “Where would you verify it?” Record answers, sample selection and denominator. Decide a pass rubric before the test (e.g., all three answered without prompting); do not use operator rehearsals as user evidence.

**Qualification before scaling:** measure one placement; repeat on an independently eligible event or separate publisher with non-overlapping source qualification. The original parent also calls for three genuinely usable packs and an independently reviewed external placement; these are not equivalent to three publishers accepting. The first 25 qualified activated leads (QAL) is a learning checkpoint, not a launch gate and not a revenue forecast. The original 100-QAL breadth milestone asks for three independent sources; the 1,000-lead strategy is not a first-release requirement. [Frozen definitions and decision bands](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/cdc07808f3d71818625dedb4edcdb1ccf0949aea/research/marketing_dockets/MKT_CATALYST_PILOT_PLAYBOOK_20261008.md).

## Measurement contract — report actual counts and denominators

| Question | Numerator / denominator | Exclusions and decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Did the placement reach anyone? | First-party eligible tagged landings / publisher-reported unique outbound clicks | Never substitute impressions/list size; separately report missing publisher counts, bots, prefetch, internal/test and repeated sessions. Unknown first touch remains unknown. |
| Did readers get first value? | Eligible sessions with a visibly rendered, supported, personally selected, dated result / eligible tagged landings | Landing/render/empty/unsupported are not equivalent; separately report input→supported hit rate, multi-symbol coverage and abandonment. Never exclude unsupported visitors from the top-of-funnel denominator. |
| Did they choose and confirm updates? | Explicit submissions / supported first-value sessions; verified unique grants / explicit submissions | No raw IDs in the report; keep errors, bounces, abandonment, suppression and pending records distinct. A trial is not required to count a QAL. |
| Was there a new qualified activated lead? | Unique new verified people who had supported personal first value and a lawful original-source join / eligible tagged landing sessions, after 7-day attribution maturation | One person globally; preserve repeat sessions in the defined session denominator. Separate prior users, unknown joins, duplicates, staff and test accounts. Never backfill with fuzzy identity. |
| Did the product earn a return? | Human reopen/qualified dossier action on a real update / eligible delivered material updates; distinct returning people / eligible verifier cohort | Show eligible-to-receive, material-event opportunities, suppressed, attempted, provider-accepted, observed-delivered where available, and human return separately. No material event is not a reader rejection. A forwarded email must not credit its forwarder as returning. |
| Did customers pay and retain? | Distinct new paying customers / matured activated persons; retained paid at D30 / eligible new paying cohort | Provider/entitlement/revenue facts, no test mode or checkout clicks. Report cancellation, refund, chargeback, activation of paid capability, unknown attribution, and right-censoring separately. |
| Was it economical? | Cash CAC = attributable incremental channel spend / distinct new paying customers. Fully loaded CAC adds attributable staff, creative and infra. Cohort contribution = recognized net revenue minus applicable channel, service and support costs without double counting. | Zero new payers ⇒ CAC undefined, not zero. State cost scope, currency and horizon. Do not book invoice totals as profit, extrapolate from open rate, or infer LTV from one billing cycle. |

**Existing experiment priors, not observed results:** fewer than 50 eligible landings per source = anecdote; 50–149 = directional. At ≥150 matured landings, first-value rate below 40% points to coverage/landing friction; ≥60% is the working aspiration. QAL rate ≥8% makes the source a candidate to repeat; ≤3% warrants investigation/redesign; middle band is inconclusive. Verification is diagnostic: below 50% with ≥30 explicit submissions, inspect the journey before changing the offer. Second-value return below 10% with ≥20 qualified delivered updates warrants review; ≥20% is an exploratory prior. Always show denominators, exclusions, dates and uncertainty. Example only: 12/150 = 8.0%; approximate 95% Wilson interval 4.6–13.5%. Two such samples would yield 24, not 25, and wouldn't prove incrementality. High-intent is a separate behavioral subset (three supported tickers and subsequent meaningful activity per the playbook); don't silently redefine QAL.

**Decision logic:** few landings → learn about distribution; enough landings but poor first value → fix hit rate or interaction; useful scans but no opt-ins → test material-update promise and clarity; submissions without confirmation → investigate delivery/trust; verified without measurable return → distinguish no opportunity, delivery failure, and weak usefulness; repeat use without payment → investigate what paid feature actually solves. Change one causal assumption at a time where possible. Record a negative result as a valid result. No attribution or reliable denominator → no rate or revenue claim.

**Privacy and assurance:** no raw recipient rows, PII-bearing URLs, partner subscription exports, portfolio fingerprints, pixel opens, or per-person webhook data in this dossier. Use owner-internal authorized readback and minimal redacted receipts; partner-visible groups need at least five individuals per cell under the existing protocol, with complementary suppression when subgroup subtraction would disclose small counts. Preserve unsubscribe function through an opaque, purpose-limited link; do not use that token for attribution. Pre-register cohort selection and right-censoring. Never replay an ambiguous real mail/payment effect as an experiment.
