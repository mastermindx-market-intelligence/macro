## Parent adjudication and implementation impact

Support-level versus candle-low is a separately identified stop-location question, and these may coincide in a particular setup. Keep setup-specific scope and the earlier previous-candle ambiguity unresolved.

Reentries and failed breakdowns are explicit reported tactics. Retain each attempted pivot, stop and later attempt in research; never replace a failed candidate with a successful later chart. The presence of these tactics is not their measured efficacy.
