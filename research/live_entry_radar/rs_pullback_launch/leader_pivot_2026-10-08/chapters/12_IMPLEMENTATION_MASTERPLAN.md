## Two prospective clocks

**Source clock:** the admitted existing producer may retain real observations as soon as its own authority and contract permit. These receipts later establish what could be reconstructed.

**Model-validation clock:** begins only after the selected feature/label/model/calibration/policy definitions and supported source are frozen and forecasts are durably issued before outcomes. Earlier data capture, simulated forecast backfills and elapsed calendar days do not count toward the 90-session model gate.

This report did not activate either clock.

## Compute and development policy

Reserve principal judgment for source/target conflicts, kill-boundary novelty and final architecture acceptance. Give source adapters, pure descriptors, conformance tests and later UI work to bounded capable existing owners under current placement policy. No new fleet or deployment project is necessary to test a named hypothesis.

Begin with the four B0/P1 launch specifications, not the adjacent planner's entire grid. Reuse cached canonical raw primitives within their existing owners. Save compact manifests and trial results, not multiple copies of the source plane. A simple null is a successful research outcome and stops optional complexity.

## Independent review

Review three things separately: causal source and label correctness; dependence, selection and economic math; and product truthfulness. The adversarial reviewer should try to backdate the pivot, leak a later membership, select only completed confirmations, exploit a higher-timeframe stub, enlarge late-entry size through a moving R denominator, and turn unavailable quotes into a favorable fill. Passing ordinary unit tests without these mutations is insufficient.

No independent external review of this new packet or product implementation is claimed. The included self-checks demonstrate only their stated mathematical scope.

## Release and rollback

A first descriptive UI release requires its own owner/release acceptance but no fabricated probability. A predictive annotation requires its separate empirical/prospective gates. Trading integration would be a new authorized capital decision, not an automatic last checkbox.

Rollback removes or disables only the optional projection/model path through existing controls. It does not erase issued forecasts, rejected trials, original bad-data receipts, old negative results or the incumbent Radar/Entry Engine behavior. No self-managed retraining or watcher loop is introduced.
