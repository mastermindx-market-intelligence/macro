# 10 | Product and UX specification

## The first user value

The descriptive MVP answers five questions: was this security an eligible leader at the relevant time; is the 30m bar actually complete; what precise rejection/reclaim was observed; where is its frozen invalidation; and what source or contrary evidence prevents a stronger conclusion?

It does not answer “buy now with 94 confidence.” The Entry Engine's verdict remains visible and unchanged. A missing optional intraday observation neither invents a bullish permission nor silently vetoes an otherwise valid incumbent decision. [I11–I13]

## Where it belongs

Use the existing Terminal tactical/dislocations, watchlist, chart and evidence/replay journeys. The LLR masterplan names the existing DislocationsView/API/lib surfaces and chart, dossier and suite-alert paths. These are integration seams to re-census at implementation, not permission to alter all of them in the first phase. No separate tactical terminal is needed. [I10]

A compact row should show: leader state at origin; pullback species; 30m state; pivot age; invalidation; source health; and evidence maturity. Optional hierarchy/context appears only when available and permitted. The default view should not become a wall of oscillator badges.

## State vocabulary

| State | Honest meaning |
|---|---|
| ELIGIBLE_LEADER | Daily owner eligibility was available and frozen at episode origin. |
| PULLBACK | The registered intraday depth transition occurred. |
| ARMED | An optional, separately validated anticipation rule fired; NOT_REQUIRED when absent from the architecture. |
| PIVOT_FORMED | A completed rejection-bar definition passed at its actual availability time. It is not a known ultimate low. |
| PIVOT_CONFIRMED | A later supported reclaim crossed the frozen structure. It is not a guaranteed launch. |
| INVALIDATED | The registered structural level was breached. Keep the original event and its history. |
| EXPIRED | The predeclared observation window ended without the required transition. |
| UNAVAILABLE / UNRESOLVED | Source or contract support is insufficient; distinguish from “no pivot.” |

Keep LAUNCH in the outcome/evaluation history. Do not let a later rally cause the earlier detector record to become a more prescient state. EXHAUSTION, DISTRIBUTION and TREND_BREAK must refer to accepted, observable owner semantics; otherwise show the underlying measurement and uncertainty rather than imply hidden participant knowledge. [I03, I19]

## Proposed typed annotation contract

This is a **proposed owner-approved annotation**, not a declaration that the current strict Radar record already accepts these fields. It references the existing episode/event ID and is persisted only through the owner-selected existing evidence mechanism. [I03, I10]

| Field family | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable security ID, episode ID, species/version and current symbol alias |
| Clocks | Session/calendar version, event time, close time, source-known time, first receipt, decision issue time |
| Source | Content digests, read receipts, basis and corporate-action lineage, source support and use/display rights |
| Structure | Frozen pivot high/low, definition version, invalidation rule, expiry and current observed state |
| Context | Origin leader state; separately labeled sector/industry/theme observations; ex-self support and residual recipe/version |
| Forecast heads | Target, horizon, units, model/calibration version, value or typed UNESTIMATED, uncertainty and support |
| Maturity | Descriptive / retrospective development / held-out / prospective validated; actual N, sessions and scope |
| Authority | `may_rank=false`, `may_gate=false`, `may_size=false`, `may_trade=false`, `may_open_entry=false` at all research/shadow stages |

For an unestimated head, numeric probability must be null and a reason must be present. A source-health field cannot be used to smuggle in a probability or recommendation. Consumer validation must reject impossible state/field combinations, unsupported calibration versions and inconsistent horizons.

## Evidence drawer

Present the observed sequence with two clocks: when the market interval ended and when the system could actually use it. Show the original frozen structure, later confirming/failing observation, elapsed confirmation time and price cost. A replay must show only information admitted at the selected historical cutoff.

Separate supporting and contrary evidence. For example, “subject lost less than its ex-self group” belongs beside “group state is deteriorating” or “residual estimate has thin support,” not above them as an overriding confidence score. Missing news coverage reads UNKNOWN, not NO NEWS. A restricted theme field is withheld with a typed rights explanation.

Show the remaining-opportunity head only at its earned maturity. Before then, factual distance already traveled and distance to a fixed registered level may be shown with their definition; they must not be presented as the expected remaining upside.

## Material art direction

Follow the existing Mastermind design system and content doctrine, not a new palette or component library. The information hierarchy is neutral and restrained. Dark treatment uses existing luminance-depth surfaces, precise structural lines and limited semantic accents. Light treatment is a research workspace with white/cool surfaces and hairline/shadow separation, not a token-swapped dark scene. These are composition requirements for the later native design owner, not a claim that mockups were produced here.

At 1440px, keep the timeline, context and evidence legible without a stack of redundant headers. At 390px, use one summary followed by an expandable evidence drawer, with no hidden invalidation or source-status field. Preserve English/Chinese semantic parity and both art directions. Use existing icons, typography and accessible contrast; no performance claim may rely on color alone.

## Required real journeys

Qualification must cover healthy formed/confirmed, invalidated, expired, no-pivot, incomplete, stale, missing identity/basis, thin group, unavailable theme rights and unestimated-probability states. Verify row → correct security/chart interval → evidence → as-known replay. Test auth/private caching, source freshness, symbol changes, desktop/mobile overflow, dark/light and EN/ZH. A screenshot of a polished positive example does not prove these journeys. [I10]

Probability display is gated on scientific/prospective acceptance. Alert integration is separately opt-in and must reuse the incumbent alert lifecycle and watermarking. No trading automation, sizing, ranking or acquisition cadence is licensed by this product specification.
