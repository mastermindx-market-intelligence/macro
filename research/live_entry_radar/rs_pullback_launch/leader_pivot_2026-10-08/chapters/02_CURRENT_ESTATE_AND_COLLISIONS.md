## Owner capability and semantic traps

**Entry Engine.** `Mastermind/portfolio/entry_engine.py` owns deterministic new-entry price assessment. Its missing-input behavior is fail-open, its supported brake is subtract-only and it does not exit or resize held positions. Several thresholds are explicitly starter priors. An intraday research input must not silently become a hard veto or upgrade these priors into validated truth. [I11]

**Daily leader/pullback.** `engine/us_leader_pullback.py` is display-only. Its highs, lows, drawdown and anchored price-volume calculation use close-basis history. Leader legs are checked at episode opening rather than repeatedly expelling a stock because the pullback lowers its RS rank. Preserve that distinction. A shallow intraday reset need not satisfy the daily lane's multiday pullback band; a new intraday species must name that population difference rather than changing the daily constants. [I12]

**Macro entry gauge.** `engine/entry_signal.py` is a different downstream context surface, not another name for the Mastermind Entry Engine. Its `_atr_pct` fallback uses close-to-close absolute moves; it is not the high/low true-range ATR proposed for this intraday study. Preserve both identifiers and units. [I13]

**Technical semantics.** The LLR census found that similarly named oscillator fields can represent different formulas. Use canonical formula/version references, not labels such as “MACD” or “StochRSI” without construction identity. Timeframe multiplication does not make correlated transforms independent evidence. [I07]

**Temporal Grain and Stock Identity.** Structural adaptation has an existing owner, but the inspected V2 protocol remains preparation, with unresolved executable cohort, rights, parameters, power and reveal fields. Stock Identity's permitted channels are a global fingerprint map, behavioral-neighbor pooling and bounded empirical-Bayes own-name residuals. An accepted method boundary is not a proven, connected intraday clock service. [I14–I18]
