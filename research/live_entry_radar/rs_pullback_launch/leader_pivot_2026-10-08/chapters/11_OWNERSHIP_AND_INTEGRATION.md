## Source, measurement and publication boundaries

A source admission receipt identifies the exact source/basis/identity/calendar/membership support. It does not validate a forecast. An empirical result identifies an exact target, data vintage and model, not a live deployment. A Terminal projection identifies actual consumer bytes and user-visible states, not economic acceptance. Maintain all three references separately.

GMI-derived measurements inherit field/use rights. Owner-approved computation does not necessarily permit public display or raw export. Contract-level fallbacks must not silently bypass a denied capture or rights scope. [I20]

## Acceptance ownership

Data owners accept source truth. TOI accepts pattern semantics. Evaluation OS and independent scientific review accept the registered comparison and outcome interpretation. GMI/Temporal/Stock Identity accept their respective derived inputs. Terminal accepts the real user journey. The Chairman/authorized portfolio authority retains trading and capital decisions. None of those decisions is inferred from the others.
