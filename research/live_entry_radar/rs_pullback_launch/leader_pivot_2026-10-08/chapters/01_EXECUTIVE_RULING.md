# 01 | Executive ruling

## The decision

**GO for a tightly scoped evidence capability. NO-GO for launching an asserted high-conviction trading system now.** The first deliverable should help an existing Mastermind user distinguish an eligible leader's completed structural rejection from an unfinished bar, a later reclaim, a failed reclaim and an unavailable source. It should not claim to know that the ultimate low is in. [I01, I03–I05]

Choose **A — Simple 30m Pivot Module, conditioned on already-known daily leadership — as the MVP**. Its output is a causal state record in Live Entry Radar. Daily leader eligibility and primitive market/sector context are inputs, not a new selection engine. The incumbent Entry Engine remains the authority for whether a new position may start. [I11–I13]

Choose **C — Fixed Multiscale Leader Pivot Intelligence — as the North Star**, with sparse, earned additions: market/sector residual repair; qualified industry and ex-self GMI theme context; optional 15m anticipation; and distinct forecasts for downside, launch, failure and entry economics. Fixed multiscale is the recommended mature architecture, not a finding that its performance has already beaten A. If those additions fail, the product remains A. [I03, I15–I16]

**D — Adaptive Temporal-Band Intelligence is a conditional challenger, not a mandatory final phase.** It can enter only through Temporal Grain's accepted structure-to-clock interface and must beat the strongest fixed and memory-matched comparisons. Ticker-specific historical best-timeframe selection is rejected. [I15–I19]

## Architecture ranking

This ranks present build priority and defensibility, not observed investment returns.

## Is 30m defensible?

**Yes as an initial engineering reference; not yet as an empirically superior structural clock.** A normal 390-minute US core session contains thirteen complete 30m bars. This makes boundaries and reconstruction straightforward. It is not evidence that a 30m rejection predicts favorable expansion. Intraday return periodicity and index-level intraday prediction are relevant context, but they do not validate this specific leader-pullback construction. [E01–E04, M01]

The decisive test is not a collage of favorable charts. It is a completed-30m increment over a baseline that already sees the same primitive price history, leader quality, market/sector returns, volatility, location and time of day. Core 15m and 60m controls, changed anchors, matched-memory filters and delayed-information controls are required before calling any result a clock advantage. [I07–I08; proposed design in 07–08]

## What has earned inclusion already?

Causal time accounting, explicit pivot/invalidation geometry, source-health disclosure and separation of forecast targets have strong methodological justification and existing owner homes. Ex-self benchmarking resolves a demonstrable construction defect. None of those facts establishes trading alpha. [I03, I07–I11, M01]

Public research motivates industry context, residual-return representations and genuine order-flow measurements, but at different populations, horizons or estimands. The reviewed primary corpus does not establish the full conjunction of durable leaders, dynamic PIT themes, a completed 30m pivot and favorable 30–120m net economics. [E02–E11]

Internal counterevidence is particularly important. Standalone residual resets failed; a subject revisiting its low after peers recovered behaved as a laggard; requiring synchronized peer advancement also failed; and several apparent bottom-localization features were generic new-low conditioning or safe-but-late confirmation. These are reasons to lower prior expectations for elaborate confluence, not reasons to stop a genuinely different, bounded experiment. They must remain named negative controls. [I19]

## Current empirical answer

There is no admitted new historical market result in this commission. The previous Phase 1 explicitly returned NOT_ADMITTED and H1/H2/H3 NOT_TESTED. Current source-repair work has advanced materially, but a draft identity reader, a merged calendar repair or a tested capture protocol does not itself supply an admitted historical cohort. [I04–I05, I21–I24]

Therefore:

- Thirty-minute superiority is **unresolved**, not affirmed.
- Dynamic themes' incremental value over sector/industry is **unresolved**, not assumed.
- Ex-self benchmark algebra is **resolved as a construction requirement**, not a predictive finding.
- Six separate outputs are **required architecture**, not six calibrated models already available.
- Adaptive clocks are **unearned**; per-name outcome audition remains **rejected**.

## Investment and delivery policy

Fund source-to-consumer admission and the minimal descriptive module before optional model complexity. Start useful prospective evidence accumulation through the existing owners as soon as its own contract is admitted; do not wait for adaptive clocks, L2/L3 or options. A frozen champion's 90-session validation clock starts with that champion's first valid prospective receipt, not with an earlier raw-data collection date. [I03, I08–I10]

The exact first phase is **P0B-30M: qualify one coherent, real source-to-completed-30m consumer path, with an admitted-or-refused result and no outcome access**. It continues the current RS Pullback owner and its source repairs rather than repeating Phase 1's old census or starting a parallel collector. Chapter 13 defines its observable completion criteria.
