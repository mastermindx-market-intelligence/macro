# 05 | Hypotheses, mechanisms and falsifiers

## Root hypothesis and its rivals

**H-root:** among point-in-time eligible durable leaders undergoing a controlled pullback, some observable states improve low localization, launch prediction or entry economics beyond primitive information. The three claims are separate. A successful downside descriptor need not identify the earliest entry; an early signal need not avoid further damage; a calibrated forecast need not pay for implementation costs. [I03]

The chief rivals are simpler: price/volatility and time-of-day explain everything; a pattern selects ordinary new lows; confirmation just enters later after risk has already fallen; a recovered peer group exposes the subject as a laggard; broad synchronized relief occurs near a local rally crest; and apparent clock selection is really slower filtering or selective coverage. [I07, I15, I19]

## Negative evidence that cannot be renamed away

**PSS-F1 and F4:** a new-low bar naturally looks close to a low; a late confirmer can naturally have low subsequent adverse excursion. Therefore the comparator must include new-low/location information and the economic comparison must charge for lateness. [I19]

**PSS-F3 residual reset:** the failed construction concentrated in systemic windows and did not improve MAE versus the raw-return analogue. A genuine new species would study leader resilience during a controlled pullback, with raw factor histories in B0, explicit systemic-exposure strata and a preregistered incremental interaction. Merely changing days to minutes, a z threshold or a peer label is insufficient novelty. Without owner acceptance of that distinction, the family remains held. [I19]

**PSS-SR2 and SR3:** both “peers stopped falling” and “peers are actively advancing” have failed in specific tested-low/held-recovery constructions. The new study must not convert those inspected negatives into a sign-reversed strategy on the same history. Do not infer durable demand from synchronized short-horizon breadth. [I19]

**Stock Identity outcome audition:** no ticker-to-best-timeframe table, including one hidden behind a cluster name or learned embedding. The scientific comparator must distinguish structural measurement from selecting the best historical outcome. [I16, I19]

**Wave B/C persistence nulls:** trailing path shape and broad sector persistence have already failed specific incremental tests. A feature that is a re-expression of returns, beta and volatility must earn representation value over those primitives; group state is not a free new alpha source. Preserve the original tested date ranges and fresh-formation restrictions when applicable. [I19]

## Mechanism classification: evidence states, not a new classifier

| Description | Observable supporting evidence | Unknowns / disconfirming evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Common shock with leader resilience | Market/group decline; subject damage smaller than trailing exposure predicts; intact prior leader context | Exposure estimate unstable; own bad event; recovery entirely market beta |
| Temporary idiosyncratic pressure, consistent with liquidity | Idiosyncratic shock with adequate event coverage; later genuine quote/spread/depth recovery when admitted | No-news coverage is incomplete; persistent residual weakness; participant identity unknown |
| Information-driven repricing | Timestamped issuer/event/analyst evidence whose content and availability match the observation | Price geometry alone is insufficient; scheduled release is not a known surprise |
| Group structural deterioration | Canonical GMI deterioration or qualified sector/industry weakness with breadth/support evidence | State is stale, shadow-only, self-dominated, rights-limited or not available at decision time |
| Laggard after peer recovery | Peers recover while the subject revisits its low and residual damage continues | Label describes observed divergence; it does not predict a bounce or authorize shorting |
| Deep washout rather than shallow reset | Admitted depth/trend observations exceed the registered leader-pullback envelope | Route to the existing different species; do not expand this one to capture a known winner |

Use the existing market-drivers, catalyst, GMI and species owners. Permit UNKNOWN, MIXED and conflicting evidence. None of these descriptions grants causal identification, institutional-intent inference, a score, or a permission override. [I10, I19–I20]

## Thesis-level kill

Stop the predictive pivot thesis when no supported model beats the strong primitive baseline on its preregistered target; when valid source timing removes the benefit; or when viable policies fail cost/tail gates. A null should not be rescued by an unregistered ticker class, a changed horizon, a new clock anchor or cherry-picked charts.

A descriptive completed-bar annotation can survive a predictive null, but its copy must say what the price did, not forecast what it will do. This is a distinct and smaller product outcome, not a renamed successful trading system.
