Confirmation requires a **later completed one-minute close strictly above** frozen high plus the declared buffer. An intraminute high touch or equality is not confirmation. Invalidation requires a low **strictly below** frozen low minus buffer. When the same completed minute contains both, low breach precedes the final-close confirmation under the explicit conservative rule; no intrabar high/low order is invented. Once invalidated, this reference never rearms after a rebound.

The projection keeps a witnessed confirmation even if a later interval is missing or the pivot subsequently fails. A source gap before a potential event prevents a first-event claim. A later gap cannot erase an already established terminal invalidation. Clean expiry requires coverage through the full evaluable interval; a deadline inside a minute remains PARTIAL_MINUTE_AT_EXPIRY rather than fabricated no-event evidence.

Each event carries both its bar-end clock and **prefix-known-at**: the maximum knowledge time of every minute needed to rule out an earlier invalidation. A delayed earlier minute can therefore delay knowledge of a later confirmation. Late corrections are invisible before their receipt, and later queries never mutate the original returned reference. A correction to the old formation bar cannot move its frozen invalidation level.
