## 1. Frozen hypothesis names

- **H0**: within point-in-time RS leaders, the 2H/3H below-zero bullish crossover offers no positive incremental post-cost per-eligible-opportunity economic utility versus a pre-scheduled matched eligible-entry reference.
- **H-RSI**: canonical RSI14→MACD(14/60/5) crosses *up* while both lines are below 0, with prior warmup satisfied; test 120m and 180m as separately counted trials. RSI-MACD is not price MACD.
- **H-PRICE**: standard *price* MACD(12,26,9) bullish cross while MACD+signal both <0 **and** Wilder RSI14 <= 40, separately on 120m/180m. Predefined exploratory proxy, not a synonym for H-RSI.
- **H-EXACT**: only after the Chairman's exact TradingView Pine code/settings or an owner-validated as-issued export of `osc_fast`, `osc_slow`, `osc_oversold` is obtained. It is **NOT_TESTED** until then; do not fit H-EXACT by picking the best historical variant.
- **H-CONFLUENCE**: the conjunction of an independently *known at entry* 30m pivot and H-RSI is a later registered challenger, **not** part of first RSI study. Do not backdate oscillator confirmation or filter only eventually successful pivot episodes.
- **H-CLOCK-SENSITIVITY**: native 130m / 195m RTH bar widths (exact divisors of 390m) are declared clock comparators; they are not retuning options selected after seeing returns.

## 2. Cohort, clock and comparability

Universe: existing approved point-in-time S&P 1500 membership/identity cohorts **including delistings and historical security epochs**, or another source-owner-frozen class recorded *before* outcome reveal. Eligibility: previous fully observed daily session's leader relative-strength and liquidity qualification, not contemporaneous/future closes; no hindsight 'best stock' samples. Stock/sector/benchmark source availability, earnings flags and session calendar must be as-of. A missing cohort or membership identity refuses that opportunity; it is not absent risk.

Bar time: RTH `America/New_York`, exchange-clock session 09:30–16:00 (exceptional sessions via existing calendar); every input bar has true UTC start/end, complete-slot evidence, and `known_at <= decision_at`. A 120m session produces **3 full bars + a 30m stub**; a 180m session produces **2 full bars + a 30m stub**; incomplete fragments **do not** become full 120m/180m observations. On early-close sessions use the actual frozen calendar and explicitly count unsupported stub/session observations, not default regular hours. 130m/195m provide session-exact but different smoothing memory and event counts. A later day is not contiguous oscillator state when the owner has a missing entire session; preserve the break or abstain. Do not label ET display epochs as Unix UTC. The first possible entry is the later owner-qualified and observable RTH minute **open**, not the crossover-bar close or an unobserved same-bar low.

Indicator initial state and warmup: use a named, versioned canonical seed. Per-series historical warmup must include sufficient complete source observations; a separate served-vs-canonical parity assertion after at least 400 bars is an explicit validation **test**, not evidence of precision earlier. H-RSI requires actual historical adjusted closes at its own timeframe, never a 30m RSI relabeled as 2H/3H. A later historical correction must invalidate a prior evaluation's source hash, not mutate original issued observations.

RSI-MACD oversold is defined as the **below-zero oscillator** with a strict bullish cross `fast[t] > signal[t]` and `fast[t-1] <= signal[t-1]` while both current lines <0; do NOT also apply RSI14<=40 to H-RSI (that is H-PRICE's separate RSI filter), unless separately preregistered. A crossing is evaluated on the fully complete/known signal bar only.
