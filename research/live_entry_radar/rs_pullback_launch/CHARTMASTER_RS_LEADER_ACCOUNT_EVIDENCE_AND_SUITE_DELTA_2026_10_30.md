## 5. New early RS leader detection: specific incremental hypotheses

Primary goal: produce useful *early recognition* even when group membership or catalyst evidence is missing.

S0 baseline: (a) current RS highs and incumbent daily RS leadership (b) benchmark-aligned momentum with 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, 63, 126 and 252-session windows, (c) liquidity and trend-age/extension flags.

S1 RS acceleration (not assumed to be alpha): slope of log(stock/benchmark) relative wealth over 3/5/10 sessions, difference of past disjoint slopes, new-high-before-price-high flag and count of distinct high sessions. Label mechanical facts and compare to the baseline that already sees raw returns and volatility. Early acceleration is extremely noise-sensitive on 1-3 day intervals.

S2 resilience and impending leadership: performance during adverse SPY/QQQ days, smaller downside excursion than risk matched peers, fast recovery of prior pivot/AVWAP, maintained weekly 8/10-week structure, volume dry-up while retracing. Distinguish actual resilience from beta, sector rotation, gap artifacts and no-trade illiquidity.

S3 group emergence: PIT member-based participant fraction positive vs market/sector, number of ex-self members making RS highs, leave-one-out member-relative return and concentration-adjusted effective N, 1/3/5-day acceleration, synchrony/dispersion and actual group breadth improvement. Consume #10213 qualified measurements, do not duplicate its producer. New group hypotheses require comparison against stable industry and parent and do not claim causality. Stock can qualify independently as STANDALONE_LEADER when group is not strong; group support is a modifier not a hard prerequisite.

S4 expansion timing: qualified first pullback since onset, first orderly weekly/daily flag near new high, post-earnings drift context, first controlled return to 8/21-day EMA, 8-week EMA and anchored volume-weighted average price; register and test no-pullback missed moves as negative/opportunity outcomes, not selected out of population.

S5 market conditional: prior known market breadth/regime deterioration, selloff/correction/risk-on filters as descriptive covariates; test interaction with group/stock resilience, do not use future eventual market state for historical event eligibility.

Stage vocabulary (presentation only): WATCHLIST_SEED -> ACCELERATING -> QUALIFIED_LEADER -> EXTENDED or ORDERLY_PULLBACK -> PIVOT_FORMED -> PIVOT_CONFIRMED / FAILED / EXPIRED, with explicit source-unavailable variants. Different source conditions can coexist; don't force a perfect one-way lifecycle on noisy real price paths.

Hypothesis hierarchy: group strength identifies search space; stock-specific RS identifies issuer leader; price/location identifies opportunity; pivot identifies possible executable timing. This is not evidence that the combined filter improves returns.
