## 6. Earlier leadership: hypotheses and measurement discipline

### 6.1 Group-first and stock-first paths

Group-first: improving breadth/RS -> qualified theme/subtheme -> constituent leaders -> accessible pullback. Stock-first: standalone RS/relative-resilience observation -> optional peer/catalyst investigation -> accessible pullback. Do not require a narrative, theme membership or catalyst to discover an individual leader.

Reuse 252-session/52-completed-week RS-high observations, but separately admit young-listing/short-history cohorts before demanding those horizons. An absence of 252 bars is insufficient history, not weak leadership. Maintain a point-in-time, liquidity-aware universe including delisted and historical members wherever the lawful source supports them; disclose survivor-only samples when it does not.

### 6.2 Candidate features, not claimed predictors

- Multi-horizon compounded relative wealth for stock vs market and sector; slope and acceleration on declared 1/3/5/10/20/63-session windows, with exact complete-window support. Relative wealth is product(1+r_stock)/product(1+r_benchmark)-1, not the subtraction of independently accumulated simple-return percentages when claiming a wealth ratio.
- RS highs before price highs, recent high frequency and duration; distinguish percentile rank from return breadth.
- Adverse-market resilience, volatility/beta-aware drawdown, time to reclaim a frozen support, and positive-return vs defensive-only relative strength.
- Group participation, new-high diffusion, dispersion, concentration, failure breadth and persistence through native #10213/GMI contracts.
- Price extension from known support/ATR, trend age, pullback depth/duration and volume change relative to time-of-day. Volume cannot identify institutional buyers by itself.
- First pullback since registered onset, earnings-gap follow-through and first orderly retest as separately declared cohorts, not a post-hoc intersection selected for profitable charts.

Do not auto-launch every possible cross-product of these features. First compare the frozen minimal RS baseline with one acceleration/resilience block, then group breadth increment on a common cohort. Expand only where evidence changes a decision.

### 6.3 Peer and theme construction

Use lagged known membership/weights and remove the subject issuer from each peer return/breadth and relevant parent contrast. Multiple share classes must not become independent peer votes. A theme hierarchy is an overlapping graph, not a forced tree; select the benchmark via a frozen pre-outcome rule, never the best-looking theme after the rally.

Report eligible, observed and expected membership/weight; effective support N_eff=(sum w)^2/sum(w^2); self-weight; concentration; stale/missing contributors; and whether a parent/ETF proxy is inclusive rather than fully ex-self. Missing prices are not zero returns. Thin groups abstain or use only an explicitly registered parent fallback. The original report's shrinkage constants are engineering priors, not estimated truth.

## 8. Scientific protocol and failure criteria

The original registered B0/P1 experiment and source-admission requirements remain controlling. New first-green and emergence families must be registered as additive comparisons before inspecting outcomes. Do not reuse a previously inspected sample as a new sealed holdout.

### 8.1 Fair comparison

Same eligible original episodes, same origin risk/notional budget, same common terminal time and costs. Baselines include actual incumbent timing and primitive price/time/volatility/support context; compare rejection-v0, one-red first-green and two-red first-green separately. A tighter late stop cannot mechanically enlarge exposure and manufacture better R. No-entry remains in the opportunity denominator, with missed rallies and capital/time use reported.

Keep finite-horizon fixed-barrier forecasts distinct from realized trade management. Entry on the next genuinely available interval/quote, not the already completed signal candle. Stop gaps, nonfills, spreads, fees, borrow where relevant, halts, missing outcome paths and same-bar ordering must be explicit. Outcomes may be censored; loss/barrier failure is not harmless censoring.

### 8.2 Chronology, dependence and selection

Chronological training/selection/calibration/holdout with purging of overlapping labels and group/date dependence; uncertainty clustered at meaningful episode/session/group levels. Account for all attempted variants, failed cohorts and reviewed parameter combinations. Hold market/sector/common-factor information comparable across arms. Measure incremental precision/lead-time at a fixed watchlist attention budget as well as returns; a 100x larger watchlist is not earlier intelligence for free.

Preserve original proposed practical margins and prospective gate (including 90 supported sessions where applicable), but compute real sample/power/calibration adequacy rather than assuming an elapsed session count proves validity. Do not promise a future validation date. Stronger features must improve the baseline at acceptable coverage and tail loss, across declared regimes and doubled-cost stress. Reject or retain descriptive-only when advantage disappears with source lag/costs or under a simpler benchmark.

### 8.3 Falsifiers

No incremental value beyond raw momentum/volatility; group effect vanishes ex-self; apparent early advantage comes from hindsight membership; returns vanish at actual first-known latency; tighter stops improve nominal R only by increasing exposure; missing-data selection manufactures performance; calibrated confidence fails out of scope; catalyst labels reflect future information; lead-time gain creates excessive false starts or unaffordable attention. Any of these blocks the affected claim, not unrelated descriptive capability.
