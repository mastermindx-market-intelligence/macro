# 12 — Microstructure, options and contextual mechanisms

## 1. Ruling and incumbent ownership

The highest-value incremental observation to qualify is the continuous equity trade/quote path, including executable spread/size and source continuity. Options, relative context, catalysts and auction indications are conditional challengers. They must explain something beyond the already observed price path and volatility, at the actual forecast horizon.

## 2. Observation ladder for microstructure

| Observation | What can actually be measured | False equivalence to reject | Proposed experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bars | Range, return, volume, close location, elapsed time | Down-bar volume = aggressive selling; wick = hidden absorption | Price/volume baseline, explicitly proxy-only |
| Ordered trades | Executed prices, sizes, conditions, reporting facilities | A large print = institution; several prints = one parent order | Eligible trade-intensity/size distribution and signed-flow sensitivity |
| Trade + contemporaneous NBBO | Trade location inside/at/outside bid/ask; spread and quote age at executions | Quote-at-trade = continuous quote path; quote rule = participant-labelled aggressor truth | Execution cost and price-location features; signing ambiguity retained |
| Continuous L1 | Best prices/sizes, quote-event OFI, time-weighted spreads, top-level replenishment | Snapshot size difference = full OFI; top book = all liquidity | Incremental low-survival/path prediction beyond price/volatility |
| L2 depth | Displayed supply by level and venue; shape, imbalance, depth recovery | Aggregated levels = individual queue priority or hidden interest | Matched depth challenger versus L1 on identical dates/names |
| L3 MBO | Native order additions/modifications/cancellations/trades and displayed queue events | Visible order = dealer/customer identity; queue reconstruction = guaranteed fill | Queue/resiliency or limit-fill model only if action/task needs it |
| Auction indications | Indicative price, paired/unpaired volume, side and publication phase | Closing-bar volume = auction imbalance | Separate late-day transition/auction-state experiment |

A near-midpoint trade is ambiguous. Trade-sign rules have errors; midpoint/tick agreement is not ground truth. Existing options bar signing reports 0.4108 net-sign recovery and unreliable direction; a narrow favorable tape comparison did not survive broader sessions. Its tape gate remains suspended/not production ready. These facts prohibit assuming “buy pressure” from a sign label, while leaving a properly sourced new measurement question open. [P40–P44]

For continuous OFI, retain the order of bid/ask price and size changes and the initial book state. Calculate events before the prediction cutoff, not over the same interval being forecast. Replenishment requires distinguishing price recovery, restored displayed depth and renewed same-side market orders; they can move differently. The classical OFI and queue studies concern contemporaneous impact or the next price movement; their large headline fits are not a 30-minute low probability. [L06–L08; L10]

## 3. Execution and adverse selection

Model quoted spread, effective spread at actual prints, depth/size, quote continuity, one-sided markets, condition eligibility, route access and latency separately. Short-run post-trade mid movement can estimate adverse-selection association at a declared horizon; it is an **outcome** for model evaluation, not a feature at the earlier trade. A measured spread recovery does not prove that information-driven selling is over.

Almgren–Chriss supplies a useful execution principle: expected trading cost and uncertainty during delay are competing objectives. Its idealized impact/price assumptions are not a fitted retail fill model. Our same-candidate capital/wealth comparison operationalizes that tradeoff without claiming an optimal schedule from that paper. [W25]

In overnight research, retain venue concentration and order accessibility. NYSE's June 2025 sample shows an extremely small, concentrated overnight market relative to regular trading; those historical percentages are not current October volumes. Nasdaq's January 2026 sample finds smaller overnight ranges for many large caps, which is not proof of lower execution cost. A recent 30-symbol/24-session BOATS working-paper abstract reports wider effective spreads and higher short-horizon impact; its narrow sample and abstract-only retrieval are explicitly limited. [W19–W21]

## 4. Options: observed, derived and inferred

| Layer | Examples | Admission and allowed initial meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Observed market facts | Contract, trade/NBBO, sizes/conditions, exchange timestamps, open interest's actual publication receipt | Descriptive only within source coverage; opening/closing/customer status may be absent |
| Derived arithmetic | Expiry concentration, distance to strike, IV surface, absolute gamma-weighted concentration, expected-move geometry | Exact formula, units, inputs and as-of required; calculation is not a hold probability |
| Scenario positioning | Signed dealer GEX, gamma flip, vanna/charm hedge demand, “walls” | State assumed dealer sign, inventory proxy, volatility response and hedge behavior; scenarios, not observed positions |
| Prediction | Conditional path variance, barrier/low survival or failure given those inputs | New preregistered ablation and calibration; no authority inherited from chart overlays |

Open interest is not opening trade flow. Call activity is not automatically bullish; buying or selling either side can reflect spreads, hedges or inventory. A volume/OI ratio cannot reveal position effect. Coalesced same-contract flow identity is not an exchange print, multi-leg package, account or dealer. Multipliers/deliverables need economic reference data; hard-coded ×100 is not safe for adjusted contracts. [P34; P42–P45]

For a scenario gamma quantity, keep the Greek per-share/per-price-unit convention explicit. A common dollar-delta change per 1% underlying move scales gamma by contract multiplier × position × S² ×0.01; sign depends on **assumed or observed** inventory. Vanna requires declared volatility units and price/IV response; charm requires time units, calendar versus trading time, and holdings. Do not add incomparable units into one strength score. Provide scenario ranges under alternative inventory signs when the sign is not established.

The empirical literature is mixed in a useful way. Gamma Fragility links a stock-level proxy to intraday momentum/reversal conditional on underlying illiquidity. A January 2025 SPX study using proprietary participant-position data estimates volatility effects at one-day and 30-minute horizons. These are positioning/variance mechanisms; neither demonstrates that public OI alone identifies the next stock low. Expiration-date clustering evidence likewise does not establish a universal strike wall. [W22–W24]

## 5. Reconcile internal options results without averaging them away

The July robustness work found conditional volatility associations after controls, while signed charm pressure weakened and apparent pinning was not uniquely OPEX-specific. The October 2 retrospective tested 60 cells with BH10% accounting and found three rejections, none replicating across all three eras. Those are different estimands and samples, not a contradiction that can be solved by choosing a preferred report. Historical PIT was unproven; no result licenses low/high localization, a dealer inventory fact or executable option profit. [P36; P38–P39]

The initial options feature set should therefore be small: known-as-of expiry proximity, clearly sourced chain/IV/liquidity state, absolute concentration and optional signed scenarios from the existing owner. Walls, gamma flip, charm and vanna remain individually labelled. Do not let a weak optional context dependency make the price head disappear, nor let a current chain snapshot leak into historical replay.

## 6. Relative and cross-market context

Construct market, sector and predeclared peer returns on common cutoffs with independent freshness checks. At minimum compare security return with index/sector ETF return, causal beta-residual return with estimation window/version, breadth within PIT membership, and peer participation. Prior-session residual recovery and true current tape improvement are separate features. Preserve the standalone residual-reset kill and test any new interaction against its raw-return analog. [P11]

Futures can provide market context when cash ETFs barely trade, but futures venue hours, roll/basis and the security's own price clock must remain explicit. A stale sector ETF is not a zero-return sector. Themes/baskets require membership known at the time; do not choose peers after observing their reaction. Current Terminal heatmap RS, sector-ratio performance and pivot-based “relative strength” are different calculations. None is automatically the needed 24H residual feature. [T68–T69; T34]

Test relative context in stages: price baseline; +market; +sector; +predeclared peers; +context interaction. Return rank or direction alone may mostly recover common beta. The old S7 mixed/harmful holdout constructions and group Trend Persistence null remain relevant constraints; untested industries or dynamic themes are not thereby killed. [P08; P18]

## 7. Catalysts and auction phases

Reuse Radar's catalyst attachment and existing EDGAR/company-event sources. Keep publication time, retrieval time, relevance time, event time and post-event price reaction distinct. “No known catalyst” requires a coverage statement and cutoff; it does not mean no information exists. A late analyst report cannot retroactively explain a prior live decision as already-informed evidence. Savor's historical ±1-day report classification illustrates that exact danger. [R16–R17; L09]

Measure scheduled versus unscheduled events; fresh versus already-digested news; earnings/guidance, filings, analyst actions and macro releases; and post-event path conditional on market/sector moves. An LLM may summarize admitted evidence, but cannot label an unobserved forced liquidation or invent a calibrated signal. Do not revive the killed OHLCV/no-news classifier under a richer narrative name. [P11; P15]

Auction context must be venue-specific. NYSE publishes closing indications from 15:50 in the ordinary session; Arca's closing imbalance publication begins earlier, at 15:00. Nasdaq NOII spans the opening and closing cross windows. Use actual auction schedules and early-close rules, including publication and order-cutoff distinctions. A 15:50 impulse may reflect auction mechanics, information or both; the feed does not identify motive. [W17–W18]

## 8. Acceptance and investment gates

R5 adds L1 only after source/clock qualification. R6 compares deeper book data to the same L1 cohort and must show useful out-of-sample improvement after cost, latency and coverage. R7 adds each options/relative/catalyst family independently and then a small registered set of interactions. Joint inclusion without remove-one-family tests is not evidence of independent contribution.

Kill an expensive challenger when matched incremental proper score and economic/risk usefulness fail their frozen gates, or when its source cost/latency erases gain. Preserve useful descriptive observations with honest labels where appropriate; a descriptive source can be worth showing without passing a trading-alpha claim. Source failure, insufficient power and a tested null receive different dispositions. No new feed purchase, collector activation or options authority is created here.
