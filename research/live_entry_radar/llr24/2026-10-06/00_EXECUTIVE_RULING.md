## 2. Scientific and economic ruling

The first proposed target is **`PRICE_L30M_30M_v1`**: whether a fixed local low, measured from30 contiguous completed RTH minutes already known at a five-minute landmark, suffers a material breach during the next30 minutes. Maximum proposed reference age is120 seconds; materiality is max(one effective tick,10% of trailing range), without a spread term. This is a price-bar label, not a buyer-executable low. The operational prospective variant freezes inputs30 seconds before a reserved grid start and requires immutable capture before that start; it has a separate label/calibration identity. [chapter06]

Build empirical clock/liquidity base rates first, then regularized price/time/volatility models and one shallow booster. Compare accepted technical families to that stronger baseline. Continuous real L1 is the next source investment because it supports spread, size, pressure, quote continuity and executable-side labels that OHLCV cannot recover. L2/L3 and deep sequence models are optional matched challengers. Literature on next-tick queue prediction and contemporaneous price impact motivates experiments; it does not establish30-minute stock-low survival. [L06–L08; M10–M11]

The economically valuable question is **how a frozen policy changes adverse path, terminal wealth, fills and missed opportunity for the same initially eligible candidates and capital**. Buy now, wait5/15 minutes, wait for reclaim, passive limit and no-entry share the same endpoint. Partial fills retain cash, fees are counted once, arrival follows actual availability plus routing latency, and source gaps remain uncertain. Fill-conditioned MAE alone is an inadequate promotion ruler. [chapters06/09; W25]

Proposed investor acceptance requires all four: at least5bp improvement in mean all-candidate adverse excursion at the confidence bound; terminal-wealth noninferiority within5bp; severe-path incidence not worse by more than1 percentage point; and at least70% share-fill coverage relative to buy-now at the lower bound. Forecasts separately require proper-score improvement and calibration intervals wholly inside the proposed±5-point band. These margins are **pre-outcome ratification proposals**, not observed performance or an assumed user preference. A day-trading profit claim requires its own positive net-wealth result. [chapter09]
