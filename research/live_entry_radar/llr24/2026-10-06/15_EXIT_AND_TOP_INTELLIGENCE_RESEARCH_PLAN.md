# 15 — Exit, top and position-health research

**Ruling:** build exit intelligence as a separate research family using bid-side liquidation economics, existing position/episode context and independently registered labels. It is not a sign-reversed low detector.

## 1. Why the problem differs

Entry can wait in cash; an existing position remains exposed while waiting. A top label is usually discovered after reversal, but a useful exit may precede the ultimate high or protect a thesis invalidation without locating any high. Spread, gap risk, overnight order access, position horizon and alternative continuation matter. A buyer-executable ask low and a seller-executable bid high are different estimands even under symmetric price dynamics.

Separate long-position health from new short-entry research. Shorting adds borrow availability, locate/recall economics and execution restrictions not qualified here. No “top risk” state grants shorting or automatic liquidation authority.

## 2. Target family

| Target | Definition at an eligible position landmark | Evaluation purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Near future seller-executable high | Current net liquidation proceeds within fixed tolerance of best supported future bid proceeds through E | Exit-location research, with hindsight label isolated |
| High survival | No material new high above a fixed observed high through H | One path question; not proof that selling is optimal |
| Giveback | Distribution of executable loss from current liquidation value or a causally known high-water mark before E | Position risk in money and percentage terms |
| Continuation versus failure | First favorable continuation barrier versus causal invalidation barrier, with administrative expiry | Competing first events; later recovery/failure remains a separate multistate path |
| Time-to-risk | Time to invalidation, gap, drawdown or loss of a declared support/reclaim level | Monitoring urgency, with censoring and undefined quantiles explicit |
| Exit now versus wait | Net common-terminal wealth for fixed sell-now, hold, staged/time, trailing/invalidation policies | Tradeoff between protected gains and foregone continuation |

The reference high, stop/invalidation level and policy horizon are fixed at each prediction or evolve only through a preregistered causal rule. A moving trailing level may update when new information arrives; do not backdate the update. Reuse chapter06's units, fee placement, support-known time, earliest-arrival constraints and gap/ambiguity treatment.

## 3. Position-conditioned population

Use canonical read-only position identity and effective quantity/cost basis where authorized. Label the source of the position: real existing position, replayed previously registered entry, or standardized research position. Do not mix them without stratification. A cohort conditioned on a successful entry can make exit results look better; compare exit policies on the same initial holdings and start time.

The first standardized exit study can start from all qualifying Radar/TOI candidate entries under one frozen benchmark policy, carrying winners and losers into the exit cohort. A second cohort may examine real held names without disclosing private position details in public research artifacts. No history is rewritten when the upstream entry model later changes.

## 4. Bid-side economics

At position landmark t₀, define equal initial holdings and cash for all compared policies. Mark holdings to qualified net liquidation proceeds and retain cash after sales. Compare wealth through common terminal E. A policy that exits immediately avoids later risk but also forgoes future gains; both belong in the same wealth path. Residual partial holdings, partial sales, fees, spread, marketable-order latency and unavailable liquidity are included.

Separate favorable realized liquidation from a midpoint chart high. For gap/halt episodes, do not assume a stop fills at its trigger price. An order can trigger when liquidity is unavailable and execute later at a different price. Use actual accessible quotes where present; unresolved execution becomes a bound or unavailable outcome. Staged exits require an explicit fixed rule; optimizing fraction-by-fraction against hindsight is a new searched strategy, not a neutral benchmark.

## 5. Hypotheses and distinct tests

**Exhaustion after extension:** diminishing signed price response to further buying, failing replenishment and increased adverse selection may precede reversal. Test actual quote/trade observations against simple return/volatility/extension controls. OHLCV wicks or oscillator overbought status alone are not observed exhaustion.

**Continuation:** orderly pullbacks, broad/sector participation and maintained support may favor holding. Compare to the momentum and volatility baseline so a model cannot “predict healthy” merely because price has already risen.

**Failed breakout / lost reclaim:** use a level fixed before the break, causal confirmation, first failure time and subsequent path. Original occurrence matters; resetting the reference to the latest break can erase failures. Preserve the existing TOI Donchian occurrence distinction. [R29]

**Liquidity and session transition:** spread/depth deterioration, auction imbalance and overnight access may affect the cost of waiting. Session-specific execution evidence is mandatory. An illiquid quote may increase uncertainty without implying bearish information.

**Options/catalyst context:** scenario positioning or new information can alter variance and continuation, but the family must pass its own incremental ablation. Existing options retrospective results and source limits remain binding; top detection receives no special exemption. [P38–P45]

## 6. Models, baselines and gates

Start with hold-to-E, sell-now, fixed-time exit and one fixed causal invalidation/trailing rule. Then use regularized cause-specific/quantile models with price/volatility/time, followed by technical and L1 additions. A joint entry/exit optimizer is deferred: jointly tuning both sides multiplies selection freedom and can hide which component adds value.

Preregister a primary horizon and consumer objective. For an investor, focus on avoiding materially adverse paths without unacceptable foregone continuation; for a tactical trader, net policy wealth after friction is central. Report giveback, captured MFE, premature exits, time exposed, tail loss, reacquisition cost if the policy permits reentry, and net wealth. No policy may receive a fresh evaluation horizon after it sells or reenters.

Use chronological grouped splits and a separately sealed final test; entry research's examined dates are known history for exit development. Dependence is at least security-day/episode and market-day. Preserve missing/censored paths in intended-population bounds. Simultaneous entry/exit claims require a declared joint family and fresh prospective validation.

## 7. Product contract

The existing-position card shows the declared position horizon, current path state, supported invalidation geometry, source freshness and what changed. Candidate copy includes “Holding structure,” “Pullback testing support,” “Extended from the latest setup,” or “Support lost,” only when the corresponding observation/rule is established. A label is not a sell order.

A user may expand to liquidation spread/size, estimated adverse-path range, probability of the precisely named event if calibrated, supportive and contradictory evidence, and the next observed condition that changes the read. If position source or bid liquidity is unavailable, explain that limitation. A normal pullback should not be called a failed investment thesis merely because a tactical window ended.

## 8. Wave and acceptance

R9 is a separate research lane after economic-price labels and the basic price/L1 infrastructure are qualified. It can run in parallel with later entry specialization, reusing source and feature implementations but independent targets and trial accounting. TOI Bottom/Top remains its later W8 owner vertical; this file supplies its concrete tactical research requirements.

Acceptance requires bid-side label witnesses, common-held-position policy comparison, causal invalidation clocks, prospective immutable forecasts and separate scientific/product adjudication. Any automatic exit, position sizing, reentry or short sale requires later explicit authority. The research outcome can truthfully be a useful position-health explanation with no demonstrated timing edge.
