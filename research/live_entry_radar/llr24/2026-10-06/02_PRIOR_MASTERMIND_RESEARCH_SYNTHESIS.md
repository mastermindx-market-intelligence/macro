# 02 — Prior Mastermind research synthesis

Research cutoff: 2026-10-06. This chapter distinguishes immutable source observations, historical experiments, reviewed research decisions and unshipped proposals. It grants no source admission, model promotion or trading authority. Its central purpose is to preserve useful prior evidence while preventing scope transfer or reconstruction of an already failed mechanism.

## 1. Scientific ruling

**Preserve entry-location and risk quality as independent objectives.** The strongest established near-low result is lower subsequent adverse excursion and greater low survival, not higher raw return. It would be a methodological regression to select a later momentum entry solely because a return leaderboard likes it. Conversely, a high near-low AUC is insufficient to claim safer executable entry: the PSS hazard work achieved strong localization while failing incremental MAE/tail gates. [P04–P05, P14]

**Existing infrastructure and kills substantially constrain the new design.** There is already an Entry Engine, a strategy-specific Prophet Availability authority, a Setup Species registry, graded outcome and claim ledgers, a trial ledger, a derived intelligence registry, Radar lifecycle ownership and an adjacent PTSE zero-authority context compiler. LLR-24 should add an explicitly registered intraday feature/label/evidence family through these owners. It should not create another ranker, candidate lifecycle, timing score, options store or evaluation OS. [P02–P03, P19–P30, P44, P48–P50]

**Neither daily evidence nor accepted software evidence establishes a 24H tactical model.** Existing studies mostly use completed daily or multi-day bars and 21–126-session labels. Microstructure data has sampling, clock and source-admission gaps, and options positioning remains partly inferred. New session-qualified empirical evidence is required; this census runs no new experiment.

## 3. Near-low risk and quality: actual results and their limits

### Entry Quality

The original study walked 110 deep-history names over approximately fourteen years using weekly evaluation steps and a trailing 600-day feature window, yielding 54,030 samples. Its outcomes were forward 21/42/63-session endpoint return, MAE and MFE. These are bar-based risk/location outcomes, not intraday executable fills. [P04]

| Result | Evidence | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Proximity to cycle low | Mean 63-session MAE approximately −7.0% at 0–3% above low, −8.8% at 12–25%, −10.5% above 25%; monotone across 21/42/63 sessions | Near-low entry reduces subsequent dip magnitude in this panel |
| Reward/risk shape | Median MFE/absolute MAE 1.31 near low versus 0.85 at +5–10% | Useful entry asymmetry, without implying return alpha |
| Raw return | Near-low + fresh score rank correlation −0.05 to −0.14, including confirmed uptrends | Strong extended momentum names often return more |
| Fresh crosses still need location | Among crosses ≤12 sessions old, MAE63 −6.8%, −7.1%, −7.8%, −9.2%, −10.9% over increasing distance bins | A recently printed cross after a large rally is still late in price |
| Rising short MA | Waiting for the “arch” underperforms because entry occurs higher above low | Curl/hold evidence can be knife hygiene; full confirmation incurs cost |
| Shipped score refinement | Strong band mean MAE −7.13%→−6.99%; count 10,344→8,978 after distance-scaled freshness | Recorded calibration refinement, not a new holdout |

The score is explicitly a risk/entry-timing-quality score. Its exact magnitudes should not be exported to other universes, bear-dominated samples or 24H horizons without new evidence. Correlated names and overlapping weekly forward windows lower effective N. The study does not establish a calibrated probability that the day's low has occurred.

### Bottom Confidence

This companion study used 109 deep-history large-cap survivors, a median roughly forty-year history, trailing 600-day calculations, weekly steps and 68,916 evaluations restricted to bottoming states. Held21 means the existing cycle low survives the next 21 sessions. The document explicitly warns that the panel is survivor-biased in a long bull sample and effective N is far below 68,916. [P05]

Before the multi-timeframe extension, entry-quality bands 0–20 / 20–40 / 40–60 / 60+ yielded held21 29.6% / 56.5% / 68.9% / 74.5%, p10 forward drawdown −14.2% / −11.9% / −10.8% / −9.6%, and average return +5.69% / +4.80% / +4.25% / +3.57%. This is direct evidence for the separation of risk quality from alpha.

Weekly turning-up state had held21 68.4% versus 49.0% without it; monthly turning up had 57.4% versus 53.0%. Confluence lifted durability, whereas drawdown depth was approximately flat near −12% in that comparison. Proximity and higher-timeframe confirmation therefore answer different questions.

Naive capitulation was rejected as a confidence boost. Above-200-day names held 61.8%, versus 36.9% for those more than 18% below; corresponding drawdown tails were −10.5% and −22.5%. Deep and still falling held only 29.6%. The adopted response was a knife-risk temper, not a bullish boost. An oversold or panic observation cannot be given a positive sign merely because it sounds like exhaustion.

## 4. Durable Bottom, COILED, RS repair and anti-chase

The Durable Bottom framework correctly separates false bottom, dead capital, and missed/late entry; requires per-fire stop-out and clean-liftoff rates with counts and event recall; prohibits treating future bottom labels as features; and records the exposure artifact created when a filter simply trades less. [P06]

The baseline gate's first wave on 223 deep-panel names, 22,458 labeled events and 8,020 base3d fires recalled only 14.8% of durable bottoms inside the perk-up window; median entry premium was +10.6% and lead +9 sessions. Earlier triggers were earlier, but this did not automatically improve the relevant total outcome.

### Positive findings that survive within their recorded scope

COILED's load-bearing ingredient was **cohort washout**, not the depth of one stock's decline. In the decisive basket replication (2,336 names, 102,408 fires), COILED had 6,842 fires, clean15 38.70% versus 31.16% (+7.54pp), stop5 40.22% versus 45.86% (−5.64pp), and dead money 6.15% versus 8.33%. Both time halves agreed; 65.2% of 492 eligible names had the desired sign. The five deep-panel folds passed in four, with the early low-volatility bull fold failing. [P06]

The recorded ship shape was a graded surfacing/ranking bonus, not a hard eligibility gate. COILED recalled only 7.35% of durable bottoms versus 59.71% for all fires in the cited basket comparison; a hard conjunction would discard roughly 88% of baseline recall. LLR-24 must not silently turn this old bonus into a universal low-timeframe gate.

China replication: 1,382 names and 10,784 COILED fires; clean15 35.57% versus 28.24%, stop5 45.72% versus 51.93%, both halves positive. Caveat: only about 3.3 years and one macro cycle. Hong Kong replication failed: clean-liftoff spread −0.84pp, halves −2.99/+1.63, only 46.1% per-name majority, and divergent STAR particularly bad. A US or China result does not authorize an HK transfer. [P06]

COILED-FIRE union of m1d/m2d inside the state passed its historical US/CN battery with display-chip plus forward-ledger disposition. US deep-panel recall was 14.22% versus 12.39%; lead 3 versus 6 sessions. Critically, the paired same-bottom price improvement was a median 0.0pp: the gain was time and coverage, not cheaper fills on the same opportunities. This is a useful precedent for separating confirmation cost from composition effects.

## 6. Trend Persistence: positive association, no incremental deployment

Stock-level V1 tested 72 feature/horizon combinations for SPY-relative returns; zero cleared all gates. V2's improved volatility controls produced 29 development drawdown/path survivors and all 29 passed the registered holdout gates. However, volatility-only simulations passed 14–29 of those same 29 holdout gates. The association was not distinguished from another volatility estimate. [P16]

B2 added the twelve features behind those tests to detailed returns/beta/volatility models. Rank IC improved only 0.001–0.002 on a 0.49–0.53 base; the highest-risk tenth captured no more severe drawdowns at the two gated horizons. Zero value gates passed; no calibrated profile, shadow snapshot or advisory field was built. Three overlapping distance-from-high tests retained about 0.01 residual IC under a retrospective decomposition; nothing was promoted from that.

A later current-pin C1 JSON exists even though the older B readout says group persistence was not attempted. C1 says C1-NULL for eleven GICS-sector, as-of-now-labelled, survivor-tilted constructions with 20/60-session horizons and a rank-linear form. Twenty-session group addition had mean rank-IC delta −0.002391, one-sided p=0.6745; twenty-session interaction increment −0.000913, p=0.9092; sixty-session addition +0.001865, p=0.3237. None carried forward. Industry groups, industries, baskets and dynamic themes remain untested, not killed. [P18]

The methodological lessons are directly relevant to LLR-24: condition on the price/volatility baseline; test incremental utility; distinguish a significant association from a useful intervention; account for overlapping labels; do not reuse exhausted dates as fresh confirmation; and do not extrapolate the null beyond the tested form.

## 11. Research consequences and integration

Near-low risk quality is the most defensible inherited objective; it deserves its own labels, matched baselines and policy consequences. Daily return leaderboards cannot adjudicate it alone. Daily probabilities, thresholds and historical held-low rates cannot be imported into a minutes/hours head.

The first empirical comparison, if later authorized after source and protocol qualification, must keep the same candidates and common terminal horizon, count waiting and missed moves, retain source unavailability, and include executable cost. Candidate selection and entry timing are separate estimands. All required price, technical, microstructure, relative, options and catalyst ablations remain proposals in this research package.

The existing registry and owner estate should receive the new horizon/session/label family. The Entry Engine, B4 Availability, PTSE context compiler, Radar lifecycle, species registry, claim/outcome grading and trial ledger already solve different parts of that ownership problem. Reconcile any later main or PR changes against the next actual commission; this source census gives neither future write custody nor automatic runtime admission.
