### 5.3 Time semantics

“First known” requires an observer and scope: first published by source, seen by vendor, received locally, usable by model, or displayed to user. These times need not agree.

Decision-time research also needs the knowledge version of identity mappings, graph relations, extraction, retrieval indexes and model/recipe. A time-filtered corpus does not remove future knowledge already embedded in a model.

Keep date-only evidence coarse. Do not turn a filing date into invented intraday availability. For macro data, separate event period, release date, vintage and retrieval time. Late backfill may retain an earlier reported publication date while local availability remains the actual receipt.

### 5.4 Numerical comparison

A valid comparison requires compatible subject, metric, unit, currency, period, basis and provenance. Separate:

- Actual result from a timestamped expectation.
- Prior published estimate from a later revised prior.
- Authorized buyback from executed repurchases.
- Contract ceiling from committed minimum, recognized revenue and profit.
- Percentage change from percentage-point change.
- Guidance midpoint/range/horizon from prior guidance.
- Management statement from independently observed implementation.

When a bridge is inadmissible, explain the missing comparison rather than emit a precise-looking surprise, EPS effect or price target.

## 6. Correction, withdrawal and access lifecycle

### 6.1 Observable behavior

| Change | Current reading | Saved research | Pending/cached derivative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corrected number on same item | Current attributed revision and material difference | Preserve original basis; expose newer revision and affected support | Old manifest cannot publish as current |
| Developing report update | Distinguish new information from error correction | Retain originally used version | Reuse only with honest coverage/applicability |
| Denial/competing account | Preserve attributed disagreement | Do not rewrite researcher interpretation | Do not manufacture consensus |
| Withdrawal/removal | Suppress content according to owner policy | Retain only permitted metadata/historical access | Prevent restoration from stale details, retries or late completion |
| Rights/audience change | Requalify current access | Honest unavailable reference when content cannot be shown | Recheck every included source on read/publication |
| Group split/merge | Trace continuity through refs/mappings | Old reference resolves to its permitted context | Grouping change is not automatically fact revision |
| Equivalent administrative renewal | Preserve lineage | No artificial new fact | No model call solely for an administrative timestamp |

A source correction means its account changed; it does not automatically settle underlying truth. A retracted rumor can remain a real historical information event for authorized research without remaining publishable current evidence.

Retention/replay obey the applicable source contract. IPTC’s status semantics are a useful reference, but do not imply every canceled article may stay readable forever. Preserve audit identities/tombstones and permitted metadata when content cannot be retained or displayed. [R03]

### 6.2 Race conditions

A correction can arrive after snapshot read, model start, cache write or user save. A separate check followed by unconditional publication leaves a race. Existing artifact/publication ownership must bind publication to the expected revision/applicability generation atomically or provide an equivalent guarantee.

Browser cancellation does not prove inference stopped or cost was zero. Preserve attempt identity and reconcile through its owner. Do not add a retry plane.

### 6.3 Useful first synthetic example

Use a fictional company and synthetic, permitted fixture text. A report says a contract is worth 500 million, then corrects it to a ceiling of 50 million over five years. A second wire repeats the original figure. A primary announcement supports only the ceiling and conditions.

The user encounters it, opens evidence and saves a note. After correction:

- Current context attributes the 50-million ceiling and does not annualize it into guaranteed revenue.
- A repeated wire does not count as independent confirmation.
- The saved note retains its version/authorship and exposes the correction.
- A pending brief based on the old revision cannot become current.
- Source/entity references remain traceable despite different local grouping.

This is a proposed mechanism test, not benchmark gold, a real event or a mandatory architecture.

## 11. Quantitative research and promotion design

### 11.1 State the claim before choosing the method

| Claim | What the evidence must establish | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Observed development | An attributed source said or reported a specific thing at a qualified time | A confident summary or a later corrected value |
| Interpretation | The proposed mechanism follows from evidence and explicit assumptions | Sentiment, repetition or unsupported industry lore |
