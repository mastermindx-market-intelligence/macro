## 1. What the program established

Mastermind should build better source-bound policy and company evidence: what was said, which instrument was chosen, what became legally effective, what money or capacity actually moved, who bears the obligation, what remains conditional, and which information was available at the decision cut. The returned research supports that product direction. It does **not** establish a general advantage for actions over words, a universal hidden-intent estimator, a coordinated equity-support mechanism, or validated T2 economic-news alpha.

The strongest quantitative baseline result is a tie. Literal-rhetoric M0 and the tested action/constraint proxy, **M2-min**, make identical predictions on all eight common primary policy episodes. They score 4/8, 7/8 and 5/8 at one, three and six months. The full commissioned M2-rich was never tested. PB-B contributes 45 abstentions, and PB-E contributes heterogeneous financing dossiers rather than comparable forecasts. The intended 40–60 comparable episode baseline therefore remains unavailable for precisely documented reasons. This fulfills the commission's data-insufficiency acceptance branch; it does not complete that larger scientific study. [Baseline reconciliation](PB_G_POLICY_BEHAVIOR_BASELINE.md)

The attractive hypotheses should stay distinguishable. Documentary cooperation is not control of another central bank. Financing support can improve access or protect an asset while diluting shareholders. A selected announcement cluster can arise from a common launch or ordinary calendar. A historical conjunction of technical state and legacy categories cannot retrospectively certify independent economic roots. These distinctions are retained in the complete [122-unit claim ledger](PB_G_CLAIM_LEDGER.json).

## 3. Hypothesis rulings

The requested labels are research conclusions. NOT_IDENTIFIED means the returned evidence does not distinguish the claim from its alternatives; it does not mean the mechanism is impossible. REJECTED below refers to a contradicted or unjustified stronger assertion. None is a production lifecycle state.

|---|---|---|
| Strategic preference for a restrictive-rate regime versus a tolerated consequence | **NOT_IDENTIFIED** | Deliberate instrument decisions and stated objectives are a separately **SUPPORTED** factual component. They do not distinguish a strategic preference for sustained restriction from inflation, institutional constraints or tolerated costs. A hidden favored-industry motive is unproved. |
| U.S. constraint over Japanese monetary/FX policy | **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** | Preserve cooperation and plausible conditional leverage over a U.S.-assisted FX option. A realized changed choice, incremental BOJ timing effect and aggregate causal share remain unproved. U.S. control/permanent veto is **REJECTED**. |
| Selective protection | **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** | Heterogeneous credit, equity, price, offtake and risk-transfer mechanisms are documented; they distribute benefits and burdens differently among assets, lenders and equity. |
| Causal two-speed cost-of-capital regime | **NOT_IDENTIFIED** | This stronger component gets its own row. There is no comparable all-in-cost counterfactual or identified causal support discount. |

### Why actions-plus-constraints remains useful despite the null comparison

The baseline does not say that actions or constraints are irrelevant. It says that the tested quantitative M2-min has no demonstrated matched advantage over M0. Separating a borrowing facility from its use, or a committed cap from cash paid, improves factual analysis without needing predictive superiority. A future rich model must show value against both simpler feature ablations and an equally informed institutional/reaction-function baseline. Only the latter can distinguish an interpretive-method contribution from the advantage of receiving more information. Even predictive success would not by itself identify private motives causally.

Institutional differences also matter. Monetary restriction, bank liquidity support, industrial grants and issuer equity transactions can coexist because they address different instruments, constraints and recipients. Treating their coexistence as an inconsistency to invert mechanically is not a valid common-target comparison.

### Election and geopolitical attribution: define missing evidence without inventing it

The parent contains these questions, but no returned identifying study answers them. Election effects require a predeclared actor/instrument/target comparison against nonelection decisions with comparable information and institutional constraints. War-response mentions in A concern uncertainty and transmission; they do not establish shock origin or intent.

The geopolitical categories are not mutually exclusive or ordinal: a shock can be exogenous to an actor and later exploited by that actor. Tolerance requires evidence of knowledge, feasible alternatives and accepted costs; induction requires a defensible link to origin/escalation. Beneficiary status alone establishes neither. A future study can use convergent documentary and behavioral evidence; private instructions are not the only possible identifying evidence. These remain lower-priority proposed research until a discriminating design is available.
