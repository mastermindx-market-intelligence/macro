# PBG-02 — Policy Watch behavior view

**Status: PROPOSED_UNLAUNCHED.** Incorporates the [shared handoff contract](../PB_G_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF_INDEX.md). Depends on PBG-01's accepted output contract.

## User capability and owner

A reader can open one policy case and compare the source statement with subsequent actions, legal state, constraints, affected parties and alternatives. They can see what evidence supports each interpretation and what would change the view. This is useful without an assertion that actions predict better than rhetoric.

Owner and candidate paths: [engine/policy_intent_desk.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/policy_intent_desk.py) for source-bound case projection and existing lifecycle references; [engine/policy_watch_current.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/policy_watch_current.py) for current official context; [scripts/build_policy_watch.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/scripts/build_policy_watch.py) for the current page. [RIC](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/rates_inflation_command.py) is a read-only measured-context dependency. Intel Hub may consume the same display facet with its existing no-vote boundary.

## Exact view

Provide a compact rhetoric-versus-action comparison with source links and dates; a case timeline referring to the canonical policy event/lifecycle; an actor/instrument reaction history with raw denominators and target/horizon; a constraints/assisted-options view; typed beneficiaries and burden bearers; and alternative explanations with conditions that would weaken or strengthen each. Label interpretations as interpretations. Use plain visible language such as **What would change this view**.

The actor history reports observations and stated objectives; it does not rank institutional predictability on PB-A's selected sample. A selected favorable example is not a skill estimate. A source statement, legal act, implementation event and measured market response occupy distinct rows. A statutory/proposed stage cannot become effective or enforced through model wording. Source dates and synthesis dates use PBG-01 qualification.

Retain the current page layout, bilingual content conventions, dark/light and mobile behavior. Scope the first slice to a few fully source-bound cases demonstrating the variation: tightening plus targeted bank support; balance-sheet-runoff adjustment; and a conditional public-support instrument. The original frozen research remains unchanged; current facts need current source receipts.

## Output and failure behavior

Emit a deterministic projection of admitted facts, linked interpretations, alternative hypotheses, constraints and provenance for the existing page. Missing actions or incomplete source coverage are unknown, not evidence that rhetoric was false. Missing instrument/counterparty identity prevents a stronger comparison. The actual lifecycle store owns corrections and supersession; the view recomposes from it.

A “beneficiary” label names the contractual/economic channel and period, distinguishing a project, creditor and common equity. “Follow the money” is not a substitute for an observed transfer. Keep intent claims explicitly qualified and out of score/vote feeds.

## Tests and real-path proof

Use cases that distinguish proposal from enforcement; loan capacity from actual draw; a dated prior view from fresh coverage; actor statement from interpretation; and source correction from historical rewrite. Test unknown/missing events and bilingual narrow-screen rendering. Prove the page links open the same admitted source/event identities as the machine projection. Verify unchanged Intel Hub votes and existing lifecycle output outside the added projection.

The acceptance path is existing retained owner evidence → deterministic case projection → normal Policy Watch build → served page. A mockup or JSON fixture alone does not complete this commission.

## Non-goals and stop

No new policy lifecycle, universal intent score, blanket inversion advice, actor leaderboard, portfolio/trade recommendation, voting authority or new research outcomes. Stop once the source-qualified behavior view is usable on the existing page with its key limitation/failure cases and owner acceptance. Broader population studies remain PBG-07.
