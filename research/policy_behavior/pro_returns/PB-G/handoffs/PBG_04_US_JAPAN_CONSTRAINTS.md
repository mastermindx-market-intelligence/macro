# PBG-04 — U.S.–Japan constraints by actor and instrument

**Status: PROPOSED_UNLAUNCHED.** Incorporates the [shared handoff contract](../PB_G_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF_INDEX.md). Depends on PBG-01/02 and exact international-source ownership.

## Capability and owner

A Policy Watch reader can distinguish a BOJ monetary decision, MOF FX-intervention authority, domestic implementation and a U.S.-assisted option. They can inspect cooperation, conditional access, market constraints and observed outcomes without collapsing these into U.S. control.

Use Policy Intent/lifecycle and the existing Policy Watch country projection with RIC's measured context. Candidate inspected paths are [policy_intent_desk.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/policy_intent_desk.py), [policy_watch_current.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/policy_watch_current.py), [build_policy_watch.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/scripts/build_policy_watch.py) and read-only [rates_inflation_command.py](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/c6c0ab36cdbf31f74a20f5ebf9304dd3fd920b05/engine/rates_inflation_command.py). The inspected White House builder imports engine.uk_policy_brain, and the Policy Watch builder consumes site/uk_policy.json through its UK view. This existing country wiring is separate from the Fed-focused policy_watch_current.py. The dated accepted UK rail is evidence of existing country-policy ownership, not proof of an already implemented Japanese collector. At admission, identify the actual BOJ/MOF/Fed/Treasury source owners and paths; a missing owner route is an explicit dependency, not permission to invent a new sentinel.

## Inputs and output

Consume dated original BOJ/MOF/U.S. statements and decision/action records, exact instrument and responsible actor, assisted-option conditions, relevant domestic constraints, and matched RIC rate/curve/FX context with its vintage/basis. The output is an actor/instrument constraint table and timeline showing the source, available option, condition, observed choice, alternatives and unresolved counterfactual.

PB-B has 15 snapshots over ten BOJ targets, with all 45 model slots abstaining. It supplies no forecast superiority or actor leaderboard. Repeated snapshots of one target are not independent decisions. A refusal or possible condition on one U.S.-assisted intervention option does not imply a veto over unilateral intervention or BOJ rates.

A strong causal statement must identify the choice/timing that changed, the relevant constraint, alternatives and a defensible counterfactual. Until then, retain reported conditionality/cooperation and plausible leverage as distinct interpretations. Do not assign aggregate H1≈H2 causal probabilities.

## Failure, tests and proof

Unknown intervention authority, ambiguous translation, missing decision date, absent historical pricing or unreconciled source contradiction withholds the affected conclusion. Preserve source-language text/translation provenance and separate date-only from precise-clock facts. Do not retrofit probabilities into PB-B's historical abstentions.

Tests distinguish BOJ from MOF, unilateral from assisted options, announcement from execution, one target with multiple snapshots, and measured FX response from identified shock. Trace retained official records through the current owner projection to the actual country-policy section; show a plausible constraint and an explicit unknown counterfactual. RIC's held repricing/keep-FIRST carriers must not be merged or duplicated for this display.

## Stop and non-goals

Stop when the qualified actor/instrument view is usable with source and unknown states. No control/permanent-veto score, causal-share estimate, trade signal, policy-rate prediction or new source/lifecycle/clock owner. A new predictive comparison belongs to a separately frozen PBG-07 study.
