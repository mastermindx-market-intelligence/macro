## Output, dependence and interpretation

Emit source-qualified arrivals, inclusion flow, scheduling/role/coverage status, frozen calendar and negative controls, and delayed matured outcomes. Group common launches and syndicated echoes. Preserve issuer/date/event dependence and multiple-comparison plan.

PB-C's NVDA exclusion is **coded issuer membership**, not every event with any Nvidia involvement. Prospective role normalization may study broader involvement only under a separately frozen definition. Do not rewrite historical exclusions to improve a selected result. Four HUMAIN releases at one launch produce six pairs; those are not six independent coordination discoveries.

A small timing-null p-value alone does not establish political coordination: PB-C's negative calendar control itself has a small null p-value. Economic direction is not a surprise label. A release may be material and negative; that does not make it evidence of price-support intent.

## Tests and real-path proof

Test a scheduled earnings release, a demonstrably freely timed material event, unknown scheduling, a mirrored joint launch, a multi-issuer article, wrong-timezone date, publication after market close, later source correction, source outage and role-based exclusion. The same source-to-event path must reproduce inclusion/exclusion and root grouping before outcomes are visible.

Acceptance requires retained real-source receipts through the owner, cut-time frozen panel bytes, coverage/inclusion denominator, reviewer access to the actual research consumer and known-result checks for planned estimators. If market-data rights or point-in-time stress data are unavailable, retain a descriptive panel and mark effect estimation unavailable.
