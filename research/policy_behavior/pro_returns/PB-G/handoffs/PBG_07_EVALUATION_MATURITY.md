# PBG-07 — Comparable evaluation, maturity and promotion review

**Status: PROPOSED_UNLAUNCHED.** Incorporates the [shared handoff contract](../PB_G_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF_INDEX.md). Freezing a method can precede accrual; estimating outcomes requires an authorized mature cohort.

## Capability and owner

A research reviewer can answer whether actions/constraints add value over literal rhetoric, inversion and an equally informed institutional baseline on comparable episodes. They can separately assess the registered T2 or announcement estimand with its actual population, dependence and coverage. This is an evaluation capability, not a shared hidden-intent model.

Reuse the existing evaluation/qledger owner and immutable Company/source events. Exact evaluator production paths and study-specific contracts require current owner admission; PB-G has not demonstrated them. The delivered [baseline reproduction](../scripts/reproduce_baseline.py) is an offline research audit of frozen PB-A outcomes, not a replacement production evaluator.

## Policy-behavior method amendment

Use [PB_G_POLICY_BEHAVIOR_BASELINE.md](../PB_G_POLICY_BEHAVIOR_BASELINE.md) as the frozen amendment. Preserve original M2 identifiers and bytes, explicitly aliasing the tested rule as M2-min. Rich M2 is a new prospective model definition. Require the same decision cut, instrument, target, horizon, probability support, information permissions and inclusion population for comparisons.

Keep fixed-horizon net rate change separate from first subsequent nonzero action and from next scheduled meeting. Freeze a representative event universe, failure/abstention rules and fame/hand-selection tags before outcomes. Exclude famous/hand-seeded episodes as a declared robustness check; the current PB-A dataset leaves zero cases after that exclusion. Abstention never becomes HOLD.

Compare M0, M1, M2-min, rich M2, persistence/base-rate controls and an equally informed institutional baseline. Freeze feature ablations that separate added information from revealed-preference reasoning. Document probabilities rather than retrospectively fitting them. Report common-support results, coverage and abstentions by actor, instrument, statement type, target and horizon. Do not pool incomparable BOJ next-meeting and Fed calendar-horizon cases to manufacture 40–60.

## Dependence, maturity and estimands

Define primary endpoint, estimand, economic minimum effect, precision/power assumptions, enrollment cap, maturity schedule, incomplete-outcome behavior, multiple comparisons and stopping rules before any start. Cluster repeated snapshots, issuers, dates and shared decisions appropriately. Nested horizons and alternate labels that coincide are not independent tests. One selected floor-bound event is not broad institutional evidence.

For PB-D, preserve first-T2 selector and frozen controls; binary attention/native-age matching does not identify intensity or momentum mechanisms. For announcements, retain source coverage/scheduling/calendar controls and valid stress/return data. Study-specific estimands stay separate rather than sharing a pooled “policy alpha” score.

Election optionality and geopolitical attribution require distinct bounded designs before implementation. Specify actor, instrument, information set and competing causal pathways. A shock may be exogenous to an actor and later exploited; do not force a mutually exclusive four-way probability vector. Benefits do not prove intent. No existing return identifies those mechanisms.

## Tests and real-path proof

Require independently computed known-result checks for scoring, common-support selection, abstentions, duplicate/cluster handling, boundary timestamps, missing outcomes, corrections, cohorts and maturity. Mutation examples must change a result when an invalid inclusion, hindsight input or duplicate event is introduced. Retain a reproducible source-manifest → frozen prediction → outcome → estimator → report path.

At outcome maturity, independent review must reproduce counts, exclusions, missingness, estimates and uncertainty on exact versions. A low p-value or minimum count alone does not establish a useful effect, robustness, executable economics or portability. Historical-source replay cannot become proof of historical system possession.

## Stop and authority

A methodology-only commission stops at the reviewed freeze and reproducible implementation under the existing owner; no enrollment follows automatically. A later outcome-review commission stops at explicit supported/unsupported/inconclusive results for its registered estimand.

Any proposed investment-authority increase needs a separate decision with independent replication, realistic costs/execution, failure behavior, stability and affected-consumer acceptance. PB-G grants none. Keep rank, entry, sizing, voting, trading and live alerts unchanged; do not tune on future outcomes or promote a minimum sample floor into automatic authority.
