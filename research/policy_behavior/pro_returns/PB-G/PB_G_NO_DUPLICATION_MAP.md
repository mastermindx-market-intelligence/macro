## 1. Reading the states correctly

- **Existing on frozen main** means the named source implementation was inspected. It does not establish fresh live runtime acceptance.
- **Dated PROVEN_LIVE acceptance** is preserved only where an existing-owner receipt was recovered, with its original date and capability scope.
- **Held branch implementation** means source exists on the named PR head but is unmerged/held as observed. It is not installed or accepted production.
- **Unmerged research proposal** is evidence and design input. It is not protected source law and is not an implementation.
- **Proposed addition** means PB-G architecture only. PBG-01, PBG-02 and PBG-03 remain `SPEC_ONLY / UNLAUNCHED`.

Open-PR searches were bounded to the relevant owners and named dependencies. They establish collision risk, not an exhaustive live source-lease census. Every later modifying build still needs a fresh exact-path/custody and effect-state check.
