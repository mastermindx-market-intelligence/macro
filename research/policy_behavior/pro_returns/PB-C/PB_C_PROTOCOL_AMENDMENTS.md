# PB-C — Protocol clarifications and deviations

This log preserves changes after protocol freeze; no change is represented as historical preregistration. The original protocol stays unchanged.

## A1 — Preliminary earnings releases (before any tests)

Source inspection found Lumentum's February 3, 2025 leadership release included preliminary quarterly results before the February 6 full earnings release. An earlier helper message incorrectly said January 29; the dated original corrected that discovery before panel acceptance.

Interpretation: retain the literal first quarterly-results disclosure as the first-2025 audit member, even if preliminary. Scheduling status must follow evidence. Preliminary status cannot be upgraded to SCHEDULED merely because it contains earnings. Keep the later full results as a distinct, linked calendar comparator. The strict timing test uses only scheduling-qualified rows. This is a clarification of an ambiguous sampling phrase, not a replacement of an inconvenient result.

## A2 — Calendar status and prior scheduling proof (before any tests)

A quarterly report is a corporate-calendar event, but that fact alone does not establish its exact publication date was publicly preannounced before stress. Preserve two fields: event scheduling type and prior-schedule proof. Use a strict subset with prior notice for the strongest calendar claim. Do not equate unlocated notice with a freely chosen date.

## A3 — Data-source route (before market testing)

FRED's displayed service terms contain an AI-development restriction, and Nasdaq data carry separate third-party conditions. The FRED bulk-input route is withheld for this product-oriented research. A single methods availability probe to a public-domain series returned header/byte metadata; no FRED series is an analytical input. Direct Treasury government data can support the prespecified 2-year nominal and 10-year real yield sensitivities independently. These cannot substitute for the primary Nasdaq drawdown exposure. Cboe and Nasdaq inputs remain independently gated pending source rights and availability.

## A5 — Negative-window selection before archive inspection

After the prespecified Treasury real-yield derivation identified April 11, 2025 as the earliest 2025 five-session exposed date, the archive-audit window is fixed to April 11–17 inclusive (seven calendar days). Issuers are the alphabetically first two in the frozen universe: AAPL and ADBE. This audit can establish zero eligible announcements only within its explicitly enumerated source frame, not across all possible worldwide disclosures. The selection was not made from subsequent price recoveries or observed archive emptiness.

## A6 — Strict cross-issuer proximity before event tests

Require disjoint observed issuer sets for the primary three-session root-pair statistic. Keep a secondary statistic requiring only distinct primary issuers. This prevents serial releases involving the same public issuer from masquerading as entirely separate firms. Same-session pairs remain unordered; missing intraday timestamps do not imply direction. The pre-event economic network is unmeasured, so pair proximity does not establish related-firm influence.
