# PB-C — Numerical interpretation review

## Recommendation

**`PROSPECTIVE_ONLY` is justified as the disposition of this research pilot.** The broad selected panel supplies no robust affirmative timing result under the reported conditional diagnostics. More importantly, it cannot estimate the central population-arrival question: event capture is incomplete, the strict freely timed positive subset is empty, primary Nasdaq/VIX stress data are unmeasured, and no pre-event matched linkage or adjusted-return panel is admitted.

This disposition means the hypothesis remains eligible for a properly designed prospective study. It is not a validated coordination finding, a demonstrated absence of coordination, a calibrated likelihood of intent, or an authorization to deploy a signal. The smaller capex-exclusion probabilities must remain visible; the evidence does not justify saying every sensitivity is negative.

## Main timing interpretation

### Broad candidates

The primary broad statistic contains **22** close pairs among **991 eligible disjoint-issuer root pairs**. The saved null mean is **21.5448**, with central simulated quantiles **15 to 30** and upper-tail diagnostic **0.4828517**. These observed pairs are close to the reference center under the stipulated month-by-weekday date-resampling model. The result does not support an excess-clustering claim for this selected panel.

Of the 22 close pairs, **11 are same-session pairs** and **6 share a directly recorded program**. The six direct-program pairs are within the same-session group. This is unordered proximity, not a verified sequence of first public disclosures. Excluding direct shared-program pairs leaves **16** observed pairs among **984** eligible pairs, against saved mean **20.0451**, with diagnostic **0.8966103**. The relaxed primary-issuer-only rule produces **25** observed pairs and diagnostic **0.3184682**.

The five-session broad sensitivity has **42** pairs against mean **40.7724**, diagnostic **0.4131587**. Excluding NVDA leaves **41** roots and **15** three-session pairs, diagnostic **0.8736126**. Excluding NVDA, AMD, and ORCL leaves **36** roots and **10** such pairs, diagnostic **0.9614039**. These large upper-tail diagnostics are absence of excess-clustering evidence under this reference model; they do not prove independence or no coordination, and they are not lower-tail tests of deliberate avoidance.

### Ordinary calendar clustering

The prior-notice-certified calendar subset has **31** roots and **95** close pairs among **465** eligible pairs. Its saved mean is **57.1754**, central simulated quantiles **46 to 74**, and diagnostic **0.00059994**. The 35-root scheduled-type subset similarly has **119** close pairs against mean **71.0872**, diagnostic **0.00029997**.

This is a useful warning about the reference model: ordinary scheduled releases can be much more clustered than independent month-by-weekday date reassignment predicts. It supports the need for richer calendar modeling before using timing as evidence of strategic coordination. It does not establish that the broad and calendar cases share identical opportunity processes, nor is a difference between their probabilities a calibrated between-group test.

For the 31-root three-session result, only **5 of 10,000** saved simulated counts reach or exceed the observation; the plus-one probability is about 0.0006 and the reported Monte Carlo-only SE is approximately **0.000245**. The probability's apparent decimal precision should not be mistaken for a finely known real-world error rate. At five sessions, both calendar cases have zero saved exceedances and therefore report the plus-one floor **1/10,001**, not zero.

## The capex-exclusion qualification must be retained

The capex-excluded sample has **35** eligible roots. Its results depend on horizon and pair definition:

| Capex-excluded definition | Observed pairs | Saved mean | Upper-tail diagnostic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Disjoint issuer sets, three sessions | 17 | 11.5772 | 0.0502950 |
| Disjoint issuer sets, five sessions | 30 | 23.0777 | 0.0453955 |
| Relaxed primary-issuer-only rule, three sessions | 20 | 12.4683 | 0.0177982 |
| Disjoint issuer sets, three sessions, direct shared programs excluded | 11 | 10.0775 | 0.4131587 |

The relaxed-rule result at **0.0177982** is the strongest noncalendar upper-tail probability among the saved outputs and should not be omitted. It allows some issuer-set overlap that the primary disjoint-set rule excludes. Under the primary three-session rule, **6 of 17** close pairs share a direct recorded program; excluding those pairs changes the observed count to 11 and the diagnostic to 0.4131587.

Keep comparisons like for like. It is accurate to contrast the three-session values **0.0502950 before** versus **0.4131587 after** direct-program filtering. It is not accurate to say that the five-session result “disappears after program filtering,” because no corresponding filtered five-session probability is present in these outputs. No new simulation is necessary to preserve that distinction.

The three-session capex probability is near 0.05 and has reported Monte Carlo-only SE approximately 0.00219. Treating 0.0503 and 0.0454 as qualitatively different findings because they lie on opposite sides of 0.05 would be especially unhelpful. The family includes many correlated cases, horizons, issuer definitions, species omissions, and rate specifications; these are exploratory diagnostics without an admitted confirmatory family error-control procedure. No post hoc adjustment or “effective number of independent tests” is asserted here.

The proper description is an **isolated, definition-sensitive capex-exclusion pattern requiring future evaluation**. It does not reverse the prospective-only disposition, but it does rule out a blanket statement that no sensitivity shows a smaller probability.

## Disposition and wording for integration

A defensible research conclusion is:

> The frozen retrospective pilot did not produce robust affirmative support for excess strategic-announcement clustering or Treasury-stress alignment. Its broad timing sample comprises 46 candidates with unknown scheduling; observed three-session clustering is near the center of the specified conditional reference distribution. Ordinary scheduled earnings releases cluster strongly under the same limited month-by-weekday reference model, demonstrating why richer calendar adjustment is necessary. A capex-exclusion sensitivity produces smaller probabilities under selected horizons and issuer definitions and remains a disclosed prospective lead. Incomplete event capture, an empty strict freely timed positive subset, uncertified globally earliest timestamps, unmeasured primary Nasdaq/VIX stress, and missing matched-linkage and return panels prevent the central population and causal questions from being estimated. The disposition is PROSPECTIVE_ONLY.

The distinction is insufficient robust support in this pilot, not proof of a null mechanism. Do not translate any diagnostic into a probability of coordination, a central actor's intent, expected returns, or production readiness.

A future authorized study should prospectively fix complete source frames and no-event coverage, scheduling evidence, public-time conventions, direct-program and conference handling, lawfully available primary stress measures, the economic linkage graph, matched opportunity calendars, and a limited confirmatory family before collecting outcomes. These are design requirements for interpreting a future test; this review does not authorize new collection infrastructure or change the research commission's custody.
