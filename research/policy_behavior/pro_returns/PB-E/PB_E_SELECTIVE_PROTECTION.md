# PB-E — Selective protection and the effective financing burden

## Conclusion and scope

The source record supports **coexistence**: targeted capital, credit, demand support and risk transfer operated alongside periods in which the Federal Reserve described policy as modestly restrictive. It also shows that public support can preserve industrial capability or lender recovery while equity holders bear dilution, restrictions or restructuring losses.

The record does **not** establish that policymakers intended high rates to benefit selected firms, that strategic status guarantees shareholder upside, or that these selected firms enjoyed a measured causal advantage over comparable controls. Monetary conditions, Treasury/market-structure relief, direct fiscal support, strategic guarantees/offtake and private financing are separate channels.

Source IDs resolve in the source register. The full five industrial case reconstructions are C09 and C11–C14 in the casebook; their terms and observation dates govern this synthesis.

## 1. Monetary context has its own clock

| Support episode | Policy range then effective | Already-public stance evidence | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| MP, July 2025 | 4.25–4.50% | June 18 press conference described policy as modestly restrictive | Targeted support coexisted with that stated stance. |
| Intel, August 2025 | 4.25–4.50% | July 30 press conference described policy as modestly restrictive and broader financial conditions as accommodative | Monetary stance and overall financial conditions must remain distinct. |
| Crane financing, November 2025 | 3.75–4.00% | October 29 cut moved toward neutral; Powell still described policy as modestly restrictive | This was an easing setting compared with July. |
| October 6, 2026 cutoff | 3.75–4.00% | September 16 decision raised the target, citing inflation; that statement did not supply a neutral/restrictive label | Equal nominal ranges across dates do not imply the same direction or economic state. |

Sources: F01–F05. Announcement dates and effective-rate dates differ by one day in the relevant decisions. No support event is reassigned to the implementation date of a rate change.

The federal-funds target is not a project's borrowing rate. A debt comparison needs the matching benchmark curve, spread, fees, maturity, collateral, covenants and guarantee structure. Real financing burden additionally depends on expected inflation and the borrower's cash-flow exposure. None is inferred from the policy range alone.

The October 2025 transcript discusses data-center investment as relatively less interest-sensitive. This is an important competing explanation: large internal cash flows, technology competition, long asset horizons and commercial demand can sustain spending without preferential policy being its dominant cause. [F04]

## 2. Five mechanisms, with distinct beneficiaries and costs

| Mechanism | Source-backed examples | Economic transmission, inference | Residual burden / who is not automatically protected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-duration public credit and guarantees | Crane FFB/DOE; Thacker Pass DOE; BlueOval SK credit | Improve funding access, tenor or lender recoverability; may allow construction before private capital is available at acceptable terms | Debt remains payable; draw conditions, collateral, reserves, fees, covenants and construction risk remain. |
| Public equity / conversion of existing incentives | Intel common equity; MP convertible preferred | Accelerate cash or improve loss-absorbing capital in exchange for government claims | Dilution, preferred return, restrictions and loss of previous grant economics can burden existing equity. |
| Price protection | MP NdPr benchmark floor | Reduce downside exposure of qualifying production receipts to market prices | Quantity rules, milestone requirements, upside sharing, political/appropriation and performance risks remain. |
| Procurement / offtake / margin facilitation | MP 10X; GM/Thacker Pass; Microsoft/Crane | Improve visibility of demand or cash available for debt service | GM and Microsoft are private buyers; they are not fiscal support merely because a project also has government debt. Volume, forecast, pricing and cancellation terms matter. |
| Restructuring with strategic asset continuity | Ford/BOSK; LAC amendments; Intel foundry ownership condition | Preserve capacity, security interests or a domestic strategic capability while changing the financial arrangement | Original ownership, strategy and common shareholders can absorb losses or restrictions. |

Talen adds a sixth channel: output- and receipts-dependent nuclear production tax credits can support cash economics without an unconditional purchase or loan. It recognized $220m of credit revenue in 2024 and none in 2025; cash collection timing remains unknown. [AI_T03, AI_T09]

Direct grants are separately evidenced by LAC's infrastructure grant. Tax-credit and regulatory/export-licensing mechanisms remain distinct categories in the taxonomy, but this commission does not establish a case-specific incremental cash valuation for them. Likewise, no Treasury market-structure relief effect is estimated. Absence of a quantified effect is not evidence of zero support.

### Credit support is not necessarily low-cost capital

Crane's facility fixes each draw at matching Treasury plus 37.5 basis points; there were no borrowings at the August 6 filing date. The government guarantee covers the relevant loan obligations, while the $1 billion disclosed amount is the principal facility limit. It is not a reported cash subsidy or a cap on every possible interest/cost component. [SEL-S02–SEL-S04]

For LAC's three draws, coupons of 4.38%, 4.41% and 4.76% compare with accounting effective rates of 6.36%, 6.45% and 6.89% after allocated financing-cost amortization. Warrants constitute much of the disclosed deferred financing cost. These are observable contractual/accounting facts. They are not a counterfactual subsidy estimate, market WACC, or proof that the project would have borrowed more cheaply elsewhere. [SEL-S21]

MP's loan is only one instrument within a larger package. Preferred dividends, warrant rights, price support, offtake and cash consideration must remain separate when evaluating the economic exchange. Calling all package elements a single grant would obscure both taxpayer rights and issuer obligations. [SEL-S05–SEL-S09]

### Demand support is not the same as private demand

A price floor can create real cash and stabilize revenue-related economics without demonstrating demand at an unsubsidized market price. Conversely, the existence of support does not prove absent end demand. MP's reported third-party sales and actual support receipts require both interpretations to be retained. [SEL-S10]

The LAC/GM agreement ties mandatory purchases to a defined portion of GM's needs, capped by Phase 1 output. A headline paraphrased as unconditional purchase of all production would conceal volume risk. A private power PPA likewise sells a productive input; it does not trace cash from an ultimate AI subscriber. [SEL-S21, SEL-S23, SEL-S01]

### Asset protection and shareholder protection diverge

Intel's exchange imposes equity claims and a warrant that can trigger if Intel’s remaining foundry ownership falls below 51%, subject to contractual exceptions. Its own disclosure warns of dilution and other financing or strategic consequences. Support for domestic manufacturing is therefore not a stock-price floor. [SEL-S12–SEL-S15]

Ford provides an observed adverse outcome within a supported project. Its original BOSK guarantee was released when the May 2026 disposition closed, and Ford assumed an already drawn Kentucky note while its subsidiary acquired the plants. The restructuring produced a charge and changed the ownership/business plan. The loan, asset transfer and sponsor capital return represent different events; the evidence does not justify an allegation of improper transfers or a claim of universal debt forgiveness. [SEL-S28, SEL-S29, SEL-S31]

## 3. What the selected cases can and cannot test

They establish the legal/economic mechanisms, their contingent versus realized states, and falsifiers. The disclosed execution, partial draws, cash receipts, dilution and restructuring are informative outcomes. They do not supply the same-security spread history, an admissible untreated population or consistent analyst-estimate vintages needed to estimate comparative financing access or revision durability.

Case selection is purposive and treatment is not random. Strong strategic importance, weak private financing, prior distress or the presence of a major customer may explain both selection for support and later outcomes. A nuclear restart, rare-earth processor, semiconductor company and EV factory cannot serve as interchangeable controls for one another.

## 4. A concrete comparison protocol for incumbent owners

This protocol is research-ready design, not a production task or permission to purchase data.

1. **Freeze one first-public event per mechanism.** Preserve rumors, conditional award, execution, close, draw and later amendment as separate clocks. A later filing cannot make its detailed terms available at an earlier announcement.
2. **Freeze eligible controls before outcomes.** Match country/currency and subsector, then project stage, credit quality, leverage, size, cash generation, collateral, maturity burden and strategic eligibility. Include delayed, refused, withdrawn and not-yet-treated projects. A later recipient is untreated only before its first support event.
3. **Prefer the same security.** Measure a consistent bond or loan against a compatible benchmark, holding seniority, currency, callability and remaining tenor fixed or explicitly adjusting. CCW g-spreads remain g-spreads; do not relabel them OAS. Stale evaluated prices or changing constituent coverage must be visible.
4. **Use disclosed terms as a fallback.** Compare comparable new/amended facilities with their own prior terms if market observations are unavailable. Capture benchmark, margin, floor, fees, collateral, guarantee, maturity and drawn amount. A coupon or facility ceiling alone cannot produce a complete cost change.
5. **Pre-specify windows.** A candidate design uses trading day −1 to +1 and a 21-session durability window, with 60 prior sessions for drift/anticipation. These are reviewable design choices, not horizons chosen for favorable results.
6. **Hold revisions to one metric and period.** Use the same fiscal period, currency, accounting basis and contributor population. If lawful historical analyst vintages are unavailable, use dated company guidance plus realization and label it as a different endpoint. Record withdrawals and fiscal-period rolls.
7. **Log concurrent information.** Earnings, ratings, commodity prices, customer contracts, equity issuance, M&A and policy decisions may share the event window. A bundled announcement may identify only the bundle; do not manufacture isolated channel effects.
8. **Retain failures and assess overlap.** Show pre-trends, missing outcomes, liquidity coverage and sensitivity to one dominant episode. With a handful of independent cases, use descriptive matched differences and uncertainty, not conventional significance claims from many securities sharing one event.

A successful later study can find better credit access alongside failed commercial execution. It can also find no coupon change while maturity or collateral improves. Both should survive the evaluation instead of being forced into a single success score.

GovRev should own official award/support events and amount semantics. CCW/capital-structure owners should own financing terms, liabilities and credit observations. Company Intelligence should own issuer/fiscal facts and public clocks. Existing relationship owners should reference admitted facts through reviewed extensions.

This return changes no production scoring, universe selection, action gate, alert, portfolio size, trade or thesis-completion status. A future implementation needs explicit admission criteria, contract review and real-path evidence. The policy finding is bounded: targeted protection and aggregate monetary restriction can coexist; their causal business/market effects remain to be measured.
