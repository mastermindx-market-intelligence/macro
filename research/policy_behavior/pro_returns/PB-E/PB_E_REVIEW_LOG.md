## Methodological conclusions that survived review

The $80bn selected financing subtotal is valid at the OpenAI-only boundary and becomes internal if its investors are placed inside a broader network. It is not all funding, cash on hand or independent operating demand. CoreWeave and LAC arithmetic is reproducible with explicit periods and units. The selected sample cannot support industry prevalence or a universal score.

The government synthesis identifies mechanisms and realized states while retaining selection, concurrent-news, missing-data and control-comparability limits. Its comparative financing/revision protocol remains unexecuted. The benign explanations have evidence and are not decorative exceptions.

The owner census distinguishes implemented source contracts from plans, reserved schema terms and historical claims. Regression test names were inspected, not run. No production acceptance is inferred from this artifact audit.
