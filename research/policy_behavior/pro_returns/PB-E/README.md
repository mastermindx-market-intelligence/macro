## Answer to the commission

The research establishes material links among capital providers, customers, suppliers, infrastructure projects and government support. Some money is demonstrably funded, some contracts are executed, and some capacity is in use. Those observations coexist with conditional financing, concentrated counterparties and incompletely disclosed ultimate customer cash.

The strongest fragility mechanism is **correlated counterparty dependence**: the same supplier may hold customer equity, sell compute/chips and backstop capacity or lease obligations. A customer shortfall can impair several exposures together. NVIDIA/OpenAI/SB Energy supplies the clearest documented contingent example; its guarantee is future, conditional exposure, not current cash expenditure. [R-S08, R-S09]

The strongest benign structural control is **ASML's customer-funded innovation program**, retaining its purchase incentives, accounting distinctions and wider market access. Meta and Talen supply additional weak-circularity controls grounded in operating businesses and identifiable outside cash routes. These are qualified economic alternatives, not solvency certificates. [R-S22; C08 and C10]

Government capital, long-duration credit, price protection, offtake and conditional tax credits can reduce particular funding or demand risks. They do not guarantee incumbent equity. Intel and Ford show dilution/constraints or restructuring alongside support. A causal funding-cost or revision-durability advantage over comparable controls remains unmeasured. [SEL-S12–SEL-S15, SEL-S28–SEL-S31]

There is **no defensible industry-wide circular-financing percentage** in this selected casebook. The report supplies explicit boundaries, actual-cash subtotals and dated measures where denominators are available. Missing aggregate cash and causal effects remain null.
