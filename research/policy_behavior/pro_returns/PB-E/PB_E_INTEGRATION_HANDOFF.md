## 8. Independent methodological challenge to PB-E metrics

### A. There is no defensible universal external-cash denominator

A set of famous disclosed transactions is neither the entire AI economy nor a random sample. Defining a network boundary changes what counts as external. State the entity perimeter, time window, currencies, types and coverage before computing anything.

Separate **external financing cash** (equity/debt from outside the selected perimeter) from **external operating/customer cash**. A capital raise can fund a real purchase without proving sustainable end-user demand. Conversely, a paying enterprise customer's own financing source is usually unknowable and need not be traced indefinitely.

Do not compute “external cash / total network cash commitments” as if it were a cash-conversion ratio. Its denominator mixes future obligations with cash flows. A permitted descriptive comparison must be explicitly labeled stock-versus-flow, have a stated horizon, and not imply realized conversion. In most PB-E cases, null is more honest.

### B. One dollar can appear many times

Investment, buyer cash, vendor receipt, revenue recognition and a reinvestment are different observations of a connected economic process. Sum actual perimeter crossings only for ingress. Link overlapping announcements and amendments through transaction/contract groups.

Do not add undrawn facility capacity to drawn balances, a revised total contract value to its earlier headline, or a parent financing commitment to subsidiaries' allocations. Do not subtract a vendor investment from reported revenue and call the remainder “real revenue”: accounting recognition and funding provenance are different objects.

### C. Warrants and guarantees are contingent exposures

A warrant headline is not automatically cash supplied, cash returned, dilution today or an unconditional ownership stake. Preserve issued/granted, vested, exercisable, exercised and expired states; underlying share count, strike, triggers and mark-to-market/fair-value figures are separate quantities.

Guarantee face value is not a funded grant. Identify the borrower, guarantor, lender/beneficiary, covered loss, trigger, cap, tenor, fees and claim payments. A price floor is a contingent support mechanism with quantity/duration/quality terms, not a bond coupon subsidy or guaranteed shareholder return.

### D. Commitments, revenue, bookings, capacity and consumption are not additive

RPO, backlog, minimum spend, purchase commitments, announced capacity and cash receipts have different recognition, cancellation and scope rules. Delivered capacity does not prove active utilization; utilization does not prove external cash collection; revenue can precede or follow cash.

A disclosed financing-linked-demand fraction is valid only for the same issuer, period and denominator definition. “Linked” means documented dual financial/commercial relationship, not that a specific dollar of financing funded a specific invoice. If reporting gives only network-level association, call it association.

### E. Prefer interpretable working-capital comparisons

“Receivable growth / revenue growth” can explode or reverse sign near zero. Show paired growth rates first, with opening/closing dates and annual/quarterly scope. DSO or average receivables/revenue can be more interpretable only with compatible periods and definition. Contract assets, factoring, acquired receivables, receivable provisions, currency changes and net-versus-gross presentation matter.

Customer concentration is supplier-side recognized-revenue exposure only where disclosed. It is not automatically concentration of ultimate demand or of funding sources. One customer may aggregate thousands of end users; many nominal customers may depend on one funder.

### F. Government-support effects need careful controls

The coexistence of restrictive aggregate rates and targeted support is a mechanism finding. It does not establish that policymakers want high rates, that a support award lowers observed borrowing costs, or that supported firms outperform matched controls.

Comparisons need pre-support leverage, credit quality, maturity, sector, country/currency, collateral, size, baseline trends and disclosure coverage. Announcement-day returns are secondary and confounded. Prefer matched descriptive cases and explicit alternative explanations over a causal effect estimate from a dozen selected successes. Retain failed, delayed, withdrawn and unsupported controls.

### G. Fragility must be conditional and falsifiable

The strongest defensible concern is an economically specified break: loss of one capital provider, non-renewal before obligation maturity, utilization/pricing below debt-service requirements, cancellation/performance trigger, or support expiry/clawback. A closed transaction loop alone is not a break.

Benign explanations include efficient vertical coordination, genuine take-or-pay demand, credible outside equity risk absorption, unused but redundant facilities, and third-party customer cash covering the contractual burden. Falsifiers should name the observation that defeats the fragility thesis: sustained independent receipts, amortization without refinancing, diversified funding, fully exercised and paid commitments, high persistent utilization, or stronger contract protection than hypothesized.
