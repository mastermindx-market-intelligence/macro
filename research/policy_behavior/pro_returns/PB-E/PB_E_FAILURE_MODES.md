# PB-E — Fragility mechanisms, counter-hypotheses and falsifiers

## Leading finding

The strongest reconstructed fragility is **correlated counterparty dependence**. A supplier can fund a customer, book sales to it, hold its equity and backstop its capacity or lease obligations. A deterioration in that customer's independent cash generation can damage several exposures at once. The customer's promise to reimburse the supplier after default may have little diversifying value.

This mechanism is clearest in NVIDIA/OpenAI/SB Energy's disclosed future guarantee and in the different, smaller CoreWeave/NVIDIA equity and capacity-purchase arrangement. The instruments have different legal obligations and should not be merged into one exposure. Neither proves current insolvency, fictitious revenue, misconduct or an inevitable unwind. [R-S08, R-S09, R-S18, R-S19]

## 1. Conditional failure map

| Failure mechanism | Observable evidence to request | Competing benign explanation | Evidence that would weaken or falsify the fragile version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier, investor and backstop share one customer risk | Separate equity carrying amount, receivables, obligations, caps, collateral and actual claim payments | Supplier has redeployment expertise, a strong balance sheet and a rational strategic return | Independent tenant cash meets obligations; robust replacement demand; guarantees expire or terminate without recurring support |
| Financing grows faster than monetized use | Cohort cash receipts, economics after service cost, supplier incentives and refresh capex | Young businesses prudently prebuild for rapid demand growth | Mature cohorts self-fund and newer cohorts converge without repeated financing subsidies |
| Long commitments conceal performance/cancellation risk | Executed contracts, available capacity, acceptance, default/cure clauses, credits and cancellations | Properly allocated performance obligations protect customers and support bankability | Consistent delivery, contractual collections and low credit leakage across stressed periods |
| Refinancing comes before collectible net contract cash | Dated principal/interest schedule, net service cash and facility conditions | Long contracts, sponsor resources and secured collateral make refinancing routine | Debt is amortized from operations; diversified funding remains available under comparable terms |
| Capacity is delivered but economically underused | Time-series operating availability, paid utilization, prices and contribution after energy/chip costs | Early ramp temporarily depresses use; spare capacity has option value | Sustained paid utilization and positive cohort economics after incentives and replacement costs |
| Asset value and guarantor strength decline together | Real transactions in used equipment/leases; tenant credit; replacement bids | Specialized equipment can be repurposed to diversified demand | Recoverable collateral/lease value proven in an adverse demand environment |
| Revenue does not become cash on expected terms | Aging, collections, credits, contract assets, prepayments and write-offs | Timing, growth and contracted prepayments explain working capital | Matched revenue-to-cash bridge and ordinary collection performance without fresh related-party funding |
| Government facility is assumed to be an immediate cash grant | Executed documents, conditions, actual draw notices and cash statements | Available undrawn credit itself has insurance and scheduling value | Timely conditional draws support completed capacity; a zero-draw claim is corrected when a draw is disclosed |
| Strategic support is assumed to protect equity | Dilution, preferred returns, warrants, collateral, covenants and restructuring outcome | Public objective is industrial capability, with shareholders knowingly sharing risk | Commercial returns exceed the full cost of capital without serial dilution; otherwise reject the equity-protection claim |
| Offtake quantity exceeds the buyer's needs | Binding orders, forecast clauses, take-or-pay terms, minimums and alternate-buyer rights | Flexible volume protects both customer and supplier as markets develop | Diverse paying buyers and resilient margins despite reduced anchor-customer requirements |

These are research tests, not live alerts. No threshold is fitted to recent outcomes and no default probability is assigned without evidence.

## 3. The strongest counterexamples remain in the result

### Customer-funded innovation can be economically coherent

ASML's customer co-investment has the opposite funding direction from the stylized vendor-finances-buyer story. Customers shared development risk; ASML retained development control and broader market access. It also supplied commercial purchase credits, and equity proceeds were distributed to previous shareholders. This is a qualified benign control, not an incentive-free program or proof that every technical subproject succeeded. [R-S22]

### Outside operating businesses can support infrastructure

Meta's advertising-led operations and Talen's operating power business offer routes to outside customer cash that do not require a young model company's monetization to be fully established. Hyperion also monetizes Meta land/construction assets and returned about $3bn to Meta; this is a financing-related distribution, not advertiser cash. Their individual project guarantees, customer concentration and costs still matter. Company-wide OCF or realized power sales must not be relabeled traced project cash without a bridge. [AI_M01, AI_M02, AI_T02, AI_T05]

### Actual execution can contradict a stale stress narrative

The record contains completed investments, repaid bridge financing, real operating revenue and collected support cash. A thesis built from an earlier announced commitment must be revised when later primary evidence confirms execution. SoftBank's completed third tranche and bridge repayment specifically prevent carrying its former bridge-maturity state forward unchanged. [R-S24]

Likewise, an unvested warrant cannot be modeled as issued common stock already owned, and an undrawn facility cannot be modeled as spent government cash. The lack of those states at the latest inspected date is evidence about that date only.

## 4. Stress scenarios are conditional, with no invented probability

**Customer cash shortfall.** Reduce collectible operating receipts at the model-company boundary while holding scheduled obligations fixed. Trace the customer's liquidity, supplier collections, equity marks and backstop triggers separately. A guaranteed payment is not automatically equal to the guarantee face amount; recoveries, remedies and coverage caps matter.

**Delivery or power delay.** Shift capacity acceptance and use later. Apply contract-specific payment commencement, credits, cancellation and financing draw conditions. Some obligations move with performance, while others remain scheduled. Without the full terms, the loss estimate is unknown.

**Higher refinancing cost or unavailable credit.** Reprice only floating or maturing instruments, with stated hedges and recourse. Do not apply a policy-rate shock mechanically to all fixed-rate debt or government-backed advances. Market-value changes in private equity are not cash outflows unless they affect collateral, funding or realization.

**Lower hardware/lease recoverability.** Reduce replacement-tenant and asset-sale proceeds on the guaranteed portfolio, then test whether tenant reimbursement remains collectible. This captures wrong-way recovery risk without assuming all modern chips immediately become worthless.

**Support withdrawal or commercial restructuring.** Apply actual expiration, covenant, appropriation and termination rules. Separate interrupted future support from clawback of previously paid funds. A borrower may retain usable assets while equity suffers; support can also be renegotiated successfully. The scenario is not a prediction of government policy.

No dollar loss, failure probability or price target is produced because key unit economics, contract schedules, claim probabilities and final cash are unavailable. Candidate scenarios should be populated only after the corresponding incumbent owner admits the required data.

## 5. Audit traps to reject

- Adding the same contract's initial amount, later increment and cumulative total.
- Adding an investor's total historic holdings to its latest completed tranche.
- Adding a loan, the guarantee of that loan, and a later assumption of its existing balance as new capital.
- Treating preferred-equity carrying gains as financing proceeds or service revenue.
- Substituting anonymous customer letters for named issuers, or assuming those letters remain stable across periods.
- Assigning an unnamed private-company investment or guarantee to a favored model company based on thematic proximity.
- Converting planned GW, contracted power or a secured site into delivered GPUs, active utilization or paid customer cash.
- Treating an initial binding-capacity share as a dollar commitment ratio.
- Calling zero Treasury spread zero all-in cost, or a government support contract an equity price guarantee.
- Backdating later disclosed terms to the original announcement or silently extending an August zero state through October.
- Treating this selected network as an independent random sample, or letting a useful research edge bypass existing closed production contracts.

## 6. What PB-G should preserve and reject

**Preserve:** typed legal/economic links, exact amount semantics, contract/version groups, separate public and economic clocks, dated execution states, explicit cash boundaries, benign controls, negative outcomes, falsifiers and existing ownership.

**Reject:** a universal circularity score; a presumption of fictitious demand; an industry percentage inferred from this case sample; a causal selective-protection premium without a valid comparison; an automatic bullish or bearish action; anonymous-counterparty attribution; any claim of production integration, model validation or trading readiness from this research return.

The appropriate synthesis is a set of durable evidence distinctions and a falsifiable monitoring agenda. It is not a completed credit or investment decision.
