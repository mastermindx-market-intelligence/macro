## Decision

The strongest evidence in the five returns is descriptive: correctly bounded model comparisons, separate policy instruments, genuine commercial and financing contracts, dated execution states, reproducible selected-sample calculations, and explicit recognition of what remains unknown. Preserve that evidence.

The package does **not** establish a superior revealed-preference policy model, U.S. control of BOJ decisions, coordinated announcements that rescue equity prices, an independent economic-quality T2 trading signal, an industry circular-financing percentage, or a causal strategic-support cost-of-capital premium. These claims require different evidence from the evidence currently available. Several are already rejected or carefully qualified by the lane authors; this review does not attribute them back to those authors.

The most decision-relevant result for Mastermind is **the need to preserve the type, clock, source and economic state of a fact through interpretation**. PB-E provides especially concrete demonstrations: a commitment, funded capital, revenue, collections, an unvested warrant and a contingent guarantee answer different questions. PB-B's intervention/FIMA distinctions and PB-D's missing-sentiment/root corrections show the same problem in other domains. This supports a bounded evidence-admission design recommendation, not immediate production authority.

The most attractive result that should **not** influence product behavior yet is a combined strategic-support/announcement/technical “confirmation” signal. Related actors, shared documents, reused outcomes and unresolved mechanisms cannot be converted into independent votes. The proposed T2 quality protocol and announcement protocol remain research proposals with activation and inferential prerequisites.

## PB-A: a real baseline audit, without a behavioral forecasting win

The central matched result is exact: M0 and M2-min give the same directions on all eight shared primary cases, scoring **4/8, 7/8 and 5/8** at one, three and six months. Comparing their different own-sample accuracies would compare different coverage, not model quality. M2-min beats the action-only rule only in the conventional-floor case of March 15, 2020; three horizons repeat one episode's contribution. [A01–A05; source method](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/a60c620b2761db74070929068c969aac8c2cee57/research/policy_behavior/pro_returns/PB-A/PB_A_METHOD_AND_FINDINGS.md)

Mechanical inversion fails on its five covered cases at all horizons, but every covered literal message is UP. It does not evaluate a balanced population of hawkish and dovish guidance. At one month, HOLD dominates much of this small sample, and the deliberately separate June 2023 pause challenge shows a case where speech beats action persistence at longer horizons. Keep that challenge outside the primary cohort. Neither “actions always win” nor “inversion never has value” is justified. [A03–A06, A19]

The forecast payload regenerates exactly from decision-only inputs; the relevant pre-score and final blobs match. These checks establish computational isolation after the retrospective freeze. They do not establish that human episode selection and directional coding were historically outcome-blind. Documentary dates are narrower than complete contemporaneous information sets. The full commissioned M2, market-pricing comparator, repeated-choice/adverse-cost contribution and market-target forecasting remain untested. [A02, A07–A08, A13–A18]

Instrument distinctions survive: reserve management, runoff caps, policy-rate changes, contingent liquidity, allocations and settlement are different events. A future cap reduction is not an executed cash injection, and coexistence of bank support and tightening does not identify hidden intent or prove support caused the rate path. [A10–A12]
