## Review boundary

The strongest supported findings concern observed decisions, contractual mechanisms, corrected sample arithmetic and limits on interpretation. Their survival does not validate a common policy-behavior predictor. Several attractive parent-level conclusions exceed what the qualified returns actually assert. The review therefore preserves author caveats while separately rejecting stronger inherited claims (X01–X12).

An intact Git freeze establishes that specified files did not change after that freeze. It does not establish that researchers were unaware of historical outcomes, that event capture was representative, or that human labels were free of hindsight. Conversely, a plausible leakage channel is not evidence that a specific forbidden input entered a calculation. These distinctions govern the findings below.

## PB-A: institutional baselines and information comparability

M0 and M2-min give identical directions on eight shared cases. The full commissioned model of actions, constraints and revealed preference was not implemented; the quantitative predictor receives only rhetorical direction, the current decision and the decided upper bound. Its own-sample performance cannot establish incremental behavioral insight, and a richer future model must face comparable information cuts and common evaluation cases (A01–A02, A19).

The automatic-inversion baseline is narrow: its five covered cases all invert UP-coded rhetoric. HOLD accounts for four of their five one-month outcomes, so inversion's failure neither establishes strong short-horizon literal-guidance skill nor tests inversion of dovish statements. The conventional-floor case contributes the only quantitative constraint gain; the June 2023 pause challenge demonstrates that guidance can outperform action persistence at longer horizons. Institutional mandates, the policy-rate boundary and ordinary decision persistence remain strong alternatives to strategic inference (A03–A06, A09–A13). Brier and log losses are deterministic transformations of accuracy under the imposed probability convention, not additional calibration evidence. All market-target forecasts abstain; descriptive yield movements cannot validate those absent forecasts (A14–A17).

## PB-B: separate assistance, stabilization and the domestic policy path

The strongest alternative to coercive control is domestic wage/price normalization combined with mutually desired yen stabilization. U.S. preferences and participation establish involvement; they do not establish accepted conditions, a refused same-terms alternative, or a Japanese policy change caused by pressure. Later anonymous accounts make conditional leverage plausible, but the return's moderate causal confidence on assisted access must be reduced. A single overall ordering across assistance, meeting timing, the cumulative rate path and exchange-rate stabilization lacks a common estimand (B02–B05, B16–B18). [Frozen constraint assessment](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/1604552006b7a91ed5d0fa89c785a983478866ea/research/policy_behavior/pro_returns/PB-B/PB_B_CONSTRAINT_ASSESSMENT.md).

Rate checks are indicative inquiries, joint intervention is an executed operation, and FIMA availability is neither an approved enlargement nor borrowing. Japan's unilateral intervention option contradicts total U.S. control. These distinctions prevent operational cooperation from becoming a claim that policy sovereignty disappeared (B07–B11, B19–B20).

Fifteen snapshots share ten BOJ targets. Reported OIS/JGB repricing can combine domestic releases, BOJ guidance, intervention and global dollar shocks. Near-full predecision pricing limits attribution to late statements, but is itself potentially post-treatment for earlier involvement. The missing observation is Japan's contemporaneous intended path without that involvement. Neither a correctly calculated repricing change nor a fully expected hike identifies a pressure effect or guarantees yen appreciation (B12–B14, B18, B21).

## PB-E: financial dependence is not a profitability verdict

The strongest fragility mechanism is correlated exposure when an investor is also supplier and contingent backstop. A closed transaction loop alone is not an economic failure. Outside risk capital, efficient coordination, replacement customers and productive early capacity are competing explanations. ASML, Meta and Talen remain substantive qualified controls; completed investments, collected cash and Ford's restructuring prevent a one-directional survival narrative (E01, E08, E12, E14, E18–E19). [Frozen failure modes](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/4720504205a7307630efe249a79c4bb02e5560da/research/policy_behavior/pro_returns/PB-E/PB_E_FAILURE_MODES.md).

Selected deal values cannot be summed across amendments, commitments, revenue, facilities, guarantees and draws. The $80bn selected financing subtotal is external at the OpenAI-only boundary and internal if its named investors are included. It is not final-customer cash or current cash on hand. Revenue, collections, financing provenance and sustainable cohort economics require different bridges. Private entities, SPVs, recourse and ultimate-cash links remain incomplete; absent joins are unknown rather than zero exposure (E03–E04, E20, E23–E24). [Frozen external-cash analysis](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/4720504205a7307630efe249a79c4bb02e5560da/research/policy_behavior/pro_returns/PB-E/PB_E_EXTERNAL_CASH_ANALYSIS.md).

## Consequence for identification and integration

Across lanes, institutional mandates, domestic fundamentals, ordinary calendars, common investment cycles and viable financing arrangements can explain much of the same evidence as strategic coordination. Preserving these alternatives prevents coherent narratives from becoming identified causes. Small selected samples, correlated horizons and version changes preclude treating the five returns as a pooled validation set.

Future gates must remain prospective: adequate source frames and counterpart roles for C; feasible coverage, frozen analysis and dependence assessment for D; comparable security/operating vintages and controls for E. Neither sample floors nor synthetic checks certify real-world power, causal identification or profitable execution (C26, D14–D16, E22–E25).

Reuse current owners without pretending their contracts already implement a financing network. Source inspection, reproducible research and draft PRs confer no protected-law status, production eligibility or trading authority (E25–E26, X12).
