## Information leakage: demonstrated, quarantined and unresolved

**Demonstrated semantic/version corrections.** The older NVDA “neutral” label is contradicted by six null values. Its later fetch does not certify an earlier cut. The old T2 “independent legs” description lacks root support. PB-D already corrects these; PB-G must preserve the correction rather than treating it as a fresh empirical quality label. [D07–D09, X01–X02]

**Quarantines that are working in the frozen returns.** PB-B keeps the later revised private account and revised domestic releases out of earlier decision inputs. PB-E's later observations and incomplete exact public clocks remain ineligible for PIT joins. In the R-S21 discrepancy, affected observation first-public fields are null, so a financial-number error or admitted lookahead effect has not been demonstrated. [B15; E26]

**Residual historical risk.** Git freezes and isolated predictors cannot certify the researchers' historical ignorance. A and C were constructed after outcomes existed; human event selection, semantic direction, timing eligibility and taxonomy can contain hindsight. B's surviving documentary chronology is not a complete original-edition archive. D's selected historical probes are not a complete contemporaneous source/quality audit. [A07–A09; C03, C13, C21; D09]

**Future selective-repair risk.** Repairing only attractive cases, allowing adjudicators to see outcomes, refreshing old source timestamps, or deleting adverse pairs as “integrity” issues can recreate lookahead even when a written protocol is careful. Freeze candidate enumeration, coverage, correction authority and original-as-known retention across exposed/control/unknown records before enrollment. [D15; C26; E26]
