## What the chronology can and cannot adjudicate

Actual assistance and reported conditional bargaining support a narrower external-constraint channel. Domestic normalization, pauses, unilateral intervention and expected decisions constrain the stronger claim. The casebook offers a temporally disciplined evidence substrate; it does not estimate a treatment coefficient, score a rhetoric-inversion model or establish a general policy-control rule.
