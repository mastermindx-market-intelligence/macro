# PB-D → PB-G research handoff

Operation `PB-D-T2-EVENT-QUALITY-20261007`. **Completed research return, not an implementation dispatch.** No cohort is enrolled and no live signal authority is granted.

## Decision

Preserve the H5 T2 × legacy-category pattern as a discovery worth testing. Do not rename it independent economic evidence. The arithmetic reproduces, but source independence, materiality and expectation change are not measured well enough to estimate their historical incremental return.

The strongest surviving result is narrow: 10/12 H5 SPY wins, five first-T2 treated issuers with 5/5 SPY and sector wins, a +4.1879-pp equal-date control gap and positive H5 issuer-omission gaps. Two issuers supply 75% of rows; first-event control tables mix selectors; outside-T2 controls include missing tiers; H10 mean SPY performance weakens; H21 has one observation; source clocks and roots are incomplete. All are part of this handoff.

## Smallest useful next acceptance slice

Under a separate implementation commission, demonstrate an **outcome-blind** path from source availability through issuer/event/root review, actual T2 emission and deterministic cohort assignment. Include failure cases: late ingestion of an old filing; one release mirrored by multiple outlets; incidental ticker mentions; multiple issuers in one article; missing expectation basis; a government proposal versus effective action; conditional funding; a correction; and missing/stale board data.

Pass on correct provenance, unknowns, clock behavior and ownership, not profitable backtest output. Freeze the analysis implementation and synthetic known-result checks specified in the preregistration before any enrolled cut. Then record the separately authorized cohort start, versions and coverage. This research commission does not authorize that next implementation or activation.

## What PB-G should not build from this evidence

- A news-positive or economic-rerating score feeding ranking, sizing, entry, board selection or live alerts.
- An independence count made by adding news/SUE/ownership booleans or publisher/URL counts without claim lineage.
- A second event/document/correction lifecycle alongside Company Intelligence.
- A T2 rewrite or execution delay tuned to the five discovery issuers.
- Historical quality labels from later manager grades, synthetic earnings clocks or post-cut news.
- An inference system treating daily repeats, overlapping tags or identical benchmark-adjusted mean differences as independent replications.
- Automatic promotion after an n-floor or p-value. A positive future study permits another research review only.

## Evidence requirement

The primary future test is first-T2 Q=1 versus known Q=0, matched on date, sector, raw attention and native age. Q requires a verified material event and two genuinely new, independently originating roots. Entry is the first regular-session close after a 09:15 ET cut; the primary endpoint is the equal-date H5 return increment.

Minimum breadth is 200 complete treated/control pairs with distinct issuers in each arm, 50 complete-pair dates, four enrollment quarters and the stated coverage/support gates during a fixed 252-session cohort. This is not a power guarantee: a 60% versus measured 50% hit-rate difference needs approximately 388 independent observations per arm at 80% power; a 2-pp four-cell mean interaction at assumed 5-pp SD needs about 197 per cell before dependence. A sparse one-year quality cohort may remain inconclusive.

The program can accept the research packet without accepting a predictive edge. Implementation, activation, merge/deployment, execution economics, replication and promotion remain separate decisions.
