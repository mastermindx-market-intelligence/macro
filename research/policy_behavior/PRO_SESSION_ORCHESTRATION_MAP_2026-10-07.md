## 2. Why this decomposition

A single research session would be forced to mix several different scientific questions:

- political / institutional reaction functions;
- cross-country constraint;
- corporate announcement timing;
- technical-signal interaction;
- financing-network fragility.

That is too broad for clean falsification and invites narrative confirmation.

The chosen split keeps shared semantics fixed while letting each lane use the research method appropriate to its question. PB-G then integrates evidence **after** independent lanes have had a chance to disagree.

## 4. Shared research law

Every session must:

- preserve **FACT / OBSERVATION / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS** distinctions;
- use point-in-time clocks for predictive claims;
- separate first-public time from later retrospective reporting;
- preserve negative cases and failed hypotheses;
- deduplicate source families;
- distinguish announcement / authorization / execution / completion;
- name competing explanations before reading outcomes where feasible;
- keep private intent unresolved when evidence does not identify it;
- model mechanical market transmission even when motive remains unresolved;
- never count article syndication as independent evidence;
- never treat government linkage alone as proof of coordination;
- never use subsequent price success to label earlier news “positive”;
- never turn a model-generated inference into rank/gate/size authority;
- prefer primary / official / issuer sources, then independent wires, then analysis;
- preserve rights and source provenance.
