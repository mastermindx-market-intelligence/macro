## Research questions

1. Does M2 provide better decision-time forecasts than M0 or M1?
2. Which statement types are most misleading if treated as forecasts?
3. Which action classes are more informative than speech?
4. Does persistence after an adverse cost becomes visible improve inference?
5. Does selective relief reveal a willingness to tolerate aggregate restriction?
6. Is apparent revealed preference actually explained by institutional mandates or feasible-alternative constraints?
7. Which outcomes can be forecast mechanically without inferring private motive at all?

- Use information available by the decision cut.
- Later retrospective reporting is archaeology-only unless it proves a fact was already public.
- Deduplicate syndication.
- Preserve failed / negative episodes.
- Do not label rhetoric as deceptive merely because action differed.
- Do not let subsequent market returns determine the “directional implication” of a statement.
- Quantify uncertainty.
- Use Brier/log score for probabilistic categorical outputs where practical.
- Report abstention and coverage.
- Keep small-n results visibly provisional.
