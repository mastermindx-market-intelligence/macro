## 4. Meaning of every output

Intensity is not probability. A historical percentile is not a frequency of future losses. Slow trend is not an all-clear. Falling intensity is not proof a price low will hold. An event that fired is not automatically a still-valid recovery. A baseline probability is not incremental predictive skill. Do not compare two countries' raw scores as though they share a calibrated unit.

The existing pullback target is a benchmark's minimum future close relative to the issue-date close, over its declared session horizon. Preserve it exactly in compatibility outputs. Intraday low, future peak-to-trough drawdown, close-to-close return, and drawdown-onset classifiers are different targets and need separate named evaluations. Display benchmark, local currency or investor currency, horizon, reference close, evidence maturity and uncertainty in detail.

No arithmetic fusion may turn disagreement into neutral. A single composed explanation may say that the long trend holds while breadth worsens, or that risk remains high while selling pressure improves. Each clause must map to a source, observation time, and method/version. Unknown critical evidence produces an unavailable/degraded qualification, not a stronger positive or negative investment conclusion.

## 5. Two-sided intelligence without a forced linear cycle

Reuse the existing orthogonal dimensions; do not create a second master state machine. Hazard families can escalate independently while repair progresses or fails. Persistent episode identity belongs to Chronicle/Reflex ownership, not a new radar database. Preserve first observation, latest material change, reason for escalation/de-escalation, and any correction under existing history contracts.

Early turning-point detection must have a fast descriptive lane distinct from slower confirmation and capital-policy release. A fresh reversal impulse may be shown immediately when the specified observation qualifies. It must not wait for the two-settled-session policy-release rule. Equally, that early impulse does not release a restriction or become a buy instruction.

Bottom recognition separates exhaustion evidence, actual price response, participation, stress repair, and durability. Reuse dislocation/washout, breadth thrust, follow-through, retest divergence, volatility-resolution, credit-rollover, fast-reclaim and liquidity organs where their target/universe fits. Count correlated breadth variants as one evidence family, not independent votes. Do not reintroduce killed standalone constructions under new names.

V-shaped repair does not require a second low; retest repair must compare breadth and credit at date-effective lows; grinding repair requires positive observed stabilization, not the absence of the other two. A relief rally remains distinguishable from broadening repair. Supportive policy is explanatory/corroborating evidence, not a mandatory prerequisite for acknowledging observed market repair. Missing liquidity evidence is unknown, not an invented veto or supportive catalyst.

A bottom call must be conditional and horizon-specific: candidate low, strengthening evidence, or confirmed repair under its declared rule. Never claim an unknowable ultimate low. Any future probability that a candidate low holds must freeze the candidate's issue time, benchmark/price definition, competing outcomes and 5/10/21-session horizon before outcome access; it cannot borrow the existing downside-probability table.

Top recognition separates extension/crowding from damage and active unwinding. Surface a pre-break vulnerability while price is above its slow trend; do not wait for the 200-day line to break before saying anything. A high sentiment or extension percentile alone is not a sell call. Preserve mechanism/exposure specificity: rates-duration, credit/funding, FX/carry, participation, and concentrated-winner liquidation are not interchangeable warnings.

Recovery can fail within the same episode. Preserve the historical impulse/reclaim event, mark the current attempt weakened or failed, retain the supporting and contradicting evidence, and notify once on material change. An independently worsening credit or contagion mechanism cannot be erased by a breadth bounce. Repair lifts only the policy that owns that release condition.
