## Main recommendation

Test **exposure-specific warnings against exposure-specific outcomes**, while treating broad-market escalation, defense selection and re-entry as distinct decisions. The highest-value scientific next step is to freeze a complete origin-date and outcome census, reconstruct real decision clocks, and compare a small model ladder on the same recipients. Do not begin with a large ensemble or a replacement market-wide score.

The first delivery improvement can be descriptive: named exposure damage, source health, observed relative/absolute rotation and explicit next observations. An early predictive or policy claim requires its own evidence. Repairing suppressed source information and proving transport can improve intelligence without converting an unqualified model into a qualified one.

## 1. What should be forecast, and what should simply be measured?

Use the existing Risk Envelope rather than a new store. Carry independent fields for environment, vulnerability, current damage, propagation, future loss, repair and permitted response; each field needs exposure, horizon, source and qualification.

| Decision | Proposed outcome or measurement | Critical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Acute recognition | First observable crossing of a frozen exposure damage threshold; recognition latency and damage already incurred | It is recognition, not preemption, when the warning arrives after damage |
| Preemptive warning | First future damage crossing within a declared actionable lead window, evaluated only while that crossing has not yet occurred | “Any alert sometime before the trough” rewards stale, permanent warnings |
| Further downside | Maximum additional executable loss from the decision origin over the inherited 5/10/21-session horizons | Preserve exact incumbent target definitions; do not substitute total drawdown from a historical peak |
| Propagation | Recipient damage after an origin exposure's signal, conditional on the recipient's own state and common shocks | Simultaneous losses establish co-damage; lagged predictability alone still does not identify causation |
| Stabilization | Deceleration, reduced new-low incidence, spread/liquidity improvement and a retest observed by time t | “Less bad” and an absent new low are observations, not proof of durable repair |
| Durable re-entry | From first executable entry, whether a predeclared recovery/gain criterion is reached before an adverse-loss barrier; then path persistence and costs | No future trough, retrospective best entry or after-the-fact leader selection |
| Defensive choice | Absolute loss/cash-relative outcome and relative benchmark outcome, graded separately at the same origin and horizon | Falling less than technology is not capital preservation |

For path-dependent recovery labels, freeze both barriers at the decision origin, using only then-available volatility if scaled barriers are used. Record “neither reached,” administrative right-censoring, delisting and unresolved daily-bar ordering separately. If daily OHLC crosses both barriers without intraday ordering, do not choose the profitable order. A recovery observation can be positive even when entry remains unattractive because price, liquidity or invalidation are unsuitable.

This decision-specific approach follows the useful principle in Drehmann–Juselius: warning timing and stability should reflect the decision being supported. Their banking-policy horizon of roughly 1.5–5 years is **not** an equity trading horizon to import. [S1]

## 2. Rotation, transmission and warning gates

### Price classifications before mechanism claims

Keep each component's absolute return, cash-relative return and relative return. A relative-strength ratio is algebraically derived from its two component prices, so test incremental value beyond the components rather than counting it as independent corroboration. Missing historical membership makes constituent breadth unavailable, not zero or modern-membership “history.”

- **Healthy broadening candidate:** participation and absolute performance broaden while originating leadership damage is limited.
- **Defensive price rotation:** defensives gain or preserve capital while a named cyclical/technology exposure deteriorates. Actual fund-flow or “escape” language requires measured flow evidence.
- **Concentrated damage:** losses remain localized after recipient/common-factor controls. “Deleveraging” requires financing, positioning or transaction evidence beyond price declines.
- **Broad co-damage / possible transmission:** losses and market-functioning stress spread across recipients. A causal contagion claim requires stronger mechanism identification.

Use these as evidence fields and competing explanations, not forced mutually exclusive global labels. A mixed condition is a legitimate answer.

### Why raw correlation or a network graphic is insufficient

Forbes–Rigobon show that changes in volatility can increase measured correlation with no change in the underlying transmission coefficient; their correction also has assumptions. Corsetti–Pericoli–Sbracia show that strong “no contagion” conclusions can themselves depend on restrictive variance assumptions. The correct design response is sensitivity to common-factor and idiosyncratic-volatility specifications, not mechanically asserting either contagion or its absence. [S2, S3]

Diebold–Yilmaz variance-decomposition connectedness provides weighted directional measures, but the authors explicitly remain agnostic about the causal structures producing connectedness. It can be a later *diagnostic* challenger. It does not turn simultaneous market declines into identified contagion. [S4]

### A bounded propagation test

Freeze origin → recipient pairs before evaluation. Use source exposure deterioration at t to forecast recipient loss after t, controlling for recipient return/drawdown/volatility, market and predeclared style/rate factors, and synchronized source availability. Compare with a recipient-only model. Require incremental future-loss skill on later recipient outcomes, economically plausible sequencing and robustness across common-shock controls. Use lead reversal and placebo origin pairs as diagnostics. If the supposed predictive link vanishes when using the first timestamp jointly observable across markets, it was a clock artifact.

Keep two episode identifiers: a nested exposure episode and its calendar/common-shock family. Overlapping country/sector/theme membership does not create independent observations.

### Compare three attention policies

1. Incumbent raw signal and existing broad-index gate, retained as separate frozen comparators.
2. Ungated market-wide escalation, to quantify its added noise and lead.
3. An exposure-qualified warning lane independent of the systemic gate, leaving systemic escalation separately evaluated.

Compare policy frontiers under the same **warning-days and warning-incident budget**, with thresholds/hysteresis chosen only in development. Do not declare #3 superior merely because it warns more often. It must identify additional recipient damage early enough to act, without unacceptable repeated interruptions or market-wide overstatement. The underlying forecast must retain its original target and calibration; changing notification policy does not recalibrate a forecast.

## 3. Small model ladder and exact falsifiers

All new models begin as research/shadow candidates. The inherited null findings rule out declaring a complex model empirically justified simply because it can be fitted.

| Rung | Candidate | What earns the next rung | Falsifier or no-promotion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | Trailing exposure/horizon base rate; persistence/no-new-information forecast; eligible incumbent raw/gated policies | Supplies honest difficulty and attention benchmarks | New model only beats an incorrectly pooled base rate or benefits from missing dates being dropped |
| B1 | Regularized exposure-level price/volatility model: own drawdown, trend/return, volatility and broad-market condition | Establishes what prices already tell us | Claimed early value disappears after conditioning on own current damage or volatility |
| C1 | B1 plus one frozen breadth/leadership block and one credit/funding or rate-shock block | Positive primary score difference on eligible evaluation, usable lead and stable counterexamples | No incremental value under ablation, common-clock evaluation, or matched warning burden |
| C2 | Small predeclared regime interactions or discrete-time competing repair/loss hazards; partial pooling only where justified | Better path-specific decisions and calibration on independent episodes | Effect depends on a single crisis, incompatible pooling, or more trial variants than preregistered |
| Optional diagnostic | One-sided change-point detector on a small residual/breadth/liquidity series; later connectedness diagnostic | Earlier recognition at the same false-alarm burden than a simple change threshold | Retrospective segmentation drives apparent lead; change-point probability is mislabeled as a loss probability |

Adams–MacKay offer causal online run-length filtering, but their original formulation assumes independent observations within segments and independent segment parameters. Financial autocorrelation, volatility clustering and heavy tails must be addressed or stress-tested. A detected distribution change need not be a crash, a bottom or a profitable trade. [S5]

Use quantile regression only for a separately scoped additional-loss distribution with sufficient tail evidence. Adrian–Boyarchenko–Giannone motivate asymmetric downside distributions rather than inferring tails from the mean. Their original GDP application is not proof of equity entry timing, and its recursive evaluation explicitly used **final revised data**. Reproduction with current-vintage macro history must not be labeled a true point-in-time result. [S6]

Do not add an unrestricted neural model, graph learner or large mixture merely to avoid a failed simple baseline. The constrained ladder is a proposed engineering choice, not a claim that literature has proved its superiority for Mastermind.

## 4. Mechanisms worth testing beyond the existing handoff

### A. Distinguish policy surprise from information surprise

A rate change can reveal policy restraint/easing and information about economic prospects simultaneously. Jarociński–Karadi use joint high-frequency interest-rate and stock-price surprises around central-bank announcements to distinguish these components under explicit sign/identification assumptions. [S7]

**Use:** preregister an announcement-window event feature and shock-mixture uncertainty when suitable historical data exist. This could prevent interpreting every decline in yields or easing announcement as favorable repair. It classifies a surprise after the window closes; it cannot count as advance warning of that same surprise. **Falsifier:** no improvement beyond yields, equity returns, volatility and the event calendar; or apparent efficacy relies on daily returns that contain unrelated news. Their high-frequency identification cannot be reproduced by casually attaching a cause label to a day's moves.

### B. Decompose common risk appetite from recipient damage

The Federal Reserve's global-risk work investigates U.S. excess-bond-premium shocks and international transmission through differentiated demand for safe assets. It motivates testing a common risk-appetite channel separately from local equity losses. Its quarterly/monthly macro results and structural assumptions are not a validated short-horizon sector alarm. [S8]

**Use:** credit/funding information, with its real publication clock, can challenge the benign-rotation explanation. **Falsifier:** no incremental recipient prediction after market/volatility controls; the information arrives after the warning window; or an expensive reconstructed feature adds no value beyond a timely, eligible spread baseline.

### C. Model recovery as a competition between repair and another loss leg

Use a small, explicit multi-state/competing-path outcome rather than one “risk score falling” trigger. Important covariates are drawdown age/depth, observed retest, participation repair, liquidity/credit repair and leadership breadth. The particular finance specification is a **new proposed hypothesis**, not an already-validated published recipe.

Daniel–Moskowitz document changing loser/winner market exposures and severe momentum reversals during market rebounds. This supports retaining high-beta rebound counterexamples and testing both new leadership and former leaders. It does not justify automatically buying the most damaged cohort. [S9]

## 5. Census, time integrity and independent evidence

Declare every eligible exposure × decision time, not only selected crises or generated alerts. Retain quiet dates, benign rotation, false warnings, missed exposure loss, gaps, holidays, delistings and recovery failures. Keep the existing five-episode atlas as explanatory anchors alongside the full origin table.

Freeze event onset and recovery rules before running the new evaluation. A past-only high-watermark/threshold rule can define current damage; future peaks/troughs may describe outcomes but cannot choose the historical decision time. Keep absolute-loss outcomes and volatility-scaled severity as separate analyses. Matching volatility is especially important when comparing sector loss rates with SPY, but should not erase an investor's actual absolute-loss concern.

Persist observation/event time, original source availability, ingestion, feature availability, decision production, publication, visible consumer timestamp and first executable price time. Training can use only outcomes that were mature and known at the retraining time. A one-sided state filter still leaks if its parameters or normalization were fitted on later data. Cross-country closes require a common cutoff or explicit lag.

Use chronological train/tune/evaluation blocks, purge overlapping label support at boundaries, and group simultaneous exposures under common shocks. Score all eligible origins, but estimate uncertainty from calendar/common-shock structure rather than pretending overlapping rows are independent. Multiway-cluster methods illustrate why non-nested dependence needs explicit handling; they do not manufacture enough independent crises from one event. [S10]

If independent episode counts cannot support the declared minimum meaningful effect, report **inconclusive / underpowered**. Do not redefine episodes until power passes. Retrospective new hypotheses must label the historical period consumed. Confirmatory evidence needs genuinely untouched data, if any exists, and immutable prospective issuance after protocol freeze. Prospective outcomes remain immature until their specified horizon and reporting lag are complete.

## 6. Metrics and promotion rules

### Forecasts

Make the primary statistical comparison the paired mean difference in a proper loss, e.g. Brier loss for the inherited binary target, with predeclared exposure weights and valid dependence treatment. Use log loss and calibration intercept/slope or reliability tables as secondary checks. For quantiles, use quantile loss and crossing/coverage diagnostics. Count origins, outcome positives and independent shock families separately.

Gneiting–Raftery support proper scoring of probabilistic predictions and explicitly caution that normalized skill scores can lose propriety. A Brier skill ratio may be a useful summary; use the underlying paired Brier loss difference as the primary inferential comparison rather than optimize a moving, sample-dependent ratio. [S11]

### Early warning and delivery

Measure separately: source-detection lead; delivered-warning lead; executable-action lead. Define executable lead as event threshold time minus first admissible action time. Report its full distribution, late/missed-event fraction, damage already incurred, share of eventual damage remaining, false warning incidents/year, warning-days/year, time under warning and repeated toggles. Match one warning incident to one exposure episode under a frozen rule; daily repetitions are not independent successes.

### Decisions and re-entry

Against frozen simple policies, report net wealth/cash-relative return, worst drawdown and tail loss, missed upside, cash drag, turnover and realistic spread/slippage/gap effects. Distinguish a sector destination outperforming the damaged origin from preserving absolute capital. For re-entry report adverse excursion after entry, false-entry incidence, time to invalidation, net recovery captured, missed rebounds and leadership persistence. Forecast superiority and decision superiority are separate acceptance gates.

### Qualification and failure

Predeclare minimum useful score/utility improvements, the allowed warning burden, uncertainty level, multiplicity handling and episode-power rule. A candidate fails promotion when its primary advantage is absent, depends on one crisis, reverses under a realistic timing/cost sensitivity, exceeds interruption limits, or lacks adequate independent evidence. Retain counterexamples; do not demote them to narrative exceptions.

Adaptive conformal intervals are optional future work, not a calibration shortcut. Gibbs–Candès' 2021 method targets empirical/marginal coverage under shift and explicitly discusses an immediate-outcome setting; 5–21-session outcomes need correct delayed feedback. Nominal interval coverage alone does not establish regime-conditional rare-loss calibration or useful entry decisions. [S12]
