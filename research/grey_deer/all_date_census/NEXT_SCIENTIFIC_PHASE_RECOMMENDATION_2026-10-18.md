## Why this phase

The [accepted T13 review](t12_frozen_sample_20261017/POST_EXECUTION_REVIEW_2026-10-18.md) establishes zero usable matched 21-session pairs. Its 5/10-session lanes retain 9/2 pairs across 4/2 event records, confined to 2022, with no established independent N or power. An exact daily calendar and outcome vector would improve later join custody but would not restore controls, chronology, source-purpose permission or inferential support. Preserve the 21-session refusal; leave the other model lanes NOT_ADMITTED. Revisit the original vector only when a separately admissible use actually requires it. Any eventual label derivation must be explicitly frozen and logged; it is not mere metadata extraction.

The [existing proposed protocol, §§1–4](../risk_radar_research_protocol_20261017.md) already identifies B0/B1 as the price baseline and explicitly says unavailable native breadth/credit features block C1/C2 without blocking those baselines. The accepted census supplies the broad denominator that the matched sample lacks: 36 fixed ETFs and 8,486 reference dates, including ordinary periods and missed damage. Its reported 212,249 eligible 21-session origins, 50,100 positive origins and 1,018 recipient episode records are **not independent observations or validated probabilities**. The four pooled overlap groups do not establish four independent crises.

The first useful question is therefore: **under the declared historical data limitations, how much further-loss information do an exposure's own price and volatility contain beyond its training-period event frequency?** Evaluate the undamaged risk set separately to distinguish preemption from additional downside after damage. This does not require FRED features and directly establishes the baseline that any richer turning-point model must beat.

## Concrete smallest development slice to freeze

Use the existing 21-session further-loss endpoint, unchanged all-date universe and joint `close` / `close_price` eligibility. Reuse the already frozen census outcome columns if their manifest and semantics qualify; do not run T12, Stage1, Stage1b or a new control sampler. The all-date dual-basis history is a different input identity from T13's admitted RAW SPY sample, even where the loss formula is shared.

For an explicitly narrowed development amendment, recommend:

- **B0:** exposure-specific event frequency from eligible, fully mature training outcomes.
- **B1:** one pooled ridge-logistic model of the existing five own-exposure features: 5/20-session total return, 63-session price drawdown, 20-session annualized log-return volatility and price/SMA200 gap. Use training-only transformations and equal exposure weight. Freeze one penalty, λ = 1 from the proposed grid, with the exact loss normalization and intercept treatment specified; do not search penalties in this first slice.
- **Chronology:** the existing annual outer blocks, 2013–2026, with 2026 ending October 15; expanding earlier history only and the full 21-reference-session outcome purge at each boundary. Retain missing-history and unsupported-training refusals.
- **No calibration fit or winner selection in this slice.** Report raw-model Brier loss against B0 on paired eligible rows and the existing fixed calibration bins. Calibration is descriptive and cannot qualify publication. Separately report own-damage/undamaged strata; do not promote a favorable subgroup.

This proposed narrowing changes the broader draft's tuning/calibration execution design and therefore requires a clearly declared development amendment before use. With 14 reporting years and the frozen 36 exposures, its maximum core budget is **504 B0 estimation units plus 14 pooled B1 fits = 518 estimation attempts**, before any refusal removes support. Enumerate every expected unit; count attempted failures and retain empty cells. There are no inner tuning fits, calibration fits, propagation fits or repair fits in this proposed slice. Any additional fitting or inferential diagnostic requires an explicit amendment to the budget. These counts do not spend T13/T14/T19 budgets.

## Limits that survive a successful run

All scanned 1993–2026 history is consumed development evidence. A chronological split of current-vintage data cannot create an untouched holdout or prove what was known at the historical release time. The fixed ETF universe also does not establish historical constituent breadth, company coverage or comprehensive country/sector representation. Preserve the rejected Stage1/Stage1b claims, failed T13 support and existing atlas counterexamples.

A B1 improvement over B0 would establish only descriptive baseline value under this reconstruction. It would not validate the live 22% figure, causal transmission, systemic escalation, warning timing or recovery. Any future warning comparison needs the actual incumbent gate, equal warning burden, expiry and real source/publication/consumer clocks. A price recovery remains a separate endpoint and decision problem.

The close-only census cannot qualify fills or net capital preservation. Executable prices after availability, spreads/commissions, slippage, gaps, cash accrual and doubled-cost sensitivity remain required before economic protection, defensive destination or re-entry claims. Numerical action authority stays with existing policy owners.

The next genuine confirmatory period begins only after immutable feature/model/calibration/protocol freezes and real issuance through the existing owner. The absent W5 forward log and its BACKFILL_ASOF/CLOCK refusals must remain intact; calendar passage or backdated predictions supply no prospective evidence.
