## 6. The principal scientific discovery: target mismatch

`research/grey_deer/gd1c/GD1C_PREREG_2026-08-19.md` freezes H1/H2 on leadership cohorts `ai_semiconductors`, `ai_infra`, `memory_storage`, `semicap_equipment`. Its outcomes are the cohort median of:

`stock cumulative return from t to t+h − SPY cumulative return from t to t+h`

at h=1 or 3 sessions, crossing −3% or −5%. This is benchmark-relative, terminal-return damage in that defined cohort. It is not the same as widespread absolute loss, actual entry-to-stop survival, or the full Prophet population.

Three logical consequences follow without a backtest:

1. If a stock and SPY both fall 4%, its specified relative outcome is zero. A severe absolute loss need not trigger the relative-damage endpoint.
2. If a stock falls 3% while SPY falls 4%, it has positive relative performance and still loses money.
3. A hypothetical executable path 100 → 92 → 98 → 102 ends up 2% while suffering an 8% adverse excursion. Endpoint returns do not identify path safety.

These examples do not invalidate the earlier research for its stated purpose. They show why that research cannot certify the different customer promise now being requested.

A second estimand distinction: the GD-1C prereg compares models on eligible, collapsed fire episodes. That can evaluate severity conditional on those triggers. A market-wide missed-event rate also needs all eligible decision opportunities, including adverse periods when no trigger fired. The new study must retain that denominator separately from the event ledger used to cluster repeated alerts.

## 8. Research architecture: three distinct prediction jobs

### A. Vulnerability before the next session

Primary scientific horizon: the next US cash session. Three-session results are secondary and separately reported. Predict the continuous distribution of broad absolute downside and participation, rather than train exclusively on a tiny number of extremely severe relative-return events. A discrete 'breakdown' threshold may be added only after its economic meaning and selection procedure are frozen before new outcome access.

Candidate observations: participation level and slope; concentration/equal-weight divergence; failed-breakout and new-low rates; cross-stock co-movement; volatility acceleration and term structure; liquidity and known catalyst exposure. Each observation retains source clocks and coverage. Breadth/complacency/technical detectors that reuse the same prices are not independent witnesses.

### B. Deterioration during the session

A separate sequential detector estimates whether the process has changed using only observations available at that time. It cannot claim previous-evening warning for a signal detected after the open. Evaluate pre-open, early-session and later-session decisions separately at the actual subscribed delay and sampling cadence. Repeated polling of one old observation supplies no additional evidence.

Compare a simple persistent threshold/CUSUM-style detector against a Bayesian online changepoint formulation only if the extra model materially improves delay versus false-alarm cost. Do not let a sophisticated algorithm name substitute for empirical advantage.

### C. Stock-specific executable path

Conditional on market/sector state and the existing stock/setup identity, estimate target-before-invalidation probability, invalidation-before-target probability, time underwater, adverse excursion and timeout payoff. Keep continuation and bottoming/reversal setup behavior distinct, with partial pooling rather than small unregularized models for every tiny subgroup.

The primary fill is the first realistically executable price after the decision became available, not the signal bar's earlier close. Preserve overnight gaps, spreads and price adjustment basis. A bar that touches both stop and target has unidentified ordering without sufficient intrabar data; use the existing conservative owner convention or an explicit bound, never assume target-first. Incomplete outcomes are censored, not wins or losses guessed at the cutoff.

## 10. Model ladder and decision utility

Start with a modest, falsifiable ladder: constant/historical prevalence or empirical quantiles; simple volatility/exposure and trend/participation baselines; a regularized, interpretable conditional model; then a constrained interaction model. Restrict search count before evaluation. Nonlinear models, neural methods, option-derived inputs and narrative features earn inclusion only by incremental value on the actual endpoint.

For stock i, the conceptual decision quantity is:

`sum over plausible named market states [P(state | information now) × expected executable net payoff(i | state)]`

subject to loss, liquidity and aggregate-exposure constraints. This is a research objective, not a shipped probability formula. The policy chooses enter, wait for a specified confirmation, retain as research-only, or refuse the current action claim. A ranked stock need not be actionable. 'No additional protection policy is active' is not synonymous with 'safe to enter'.

Preserve a positive recovery objective. A rule that avoids a selloff but remains inactive throughout the subsequent recovery can be inferior. Measure re-entry delay, false re-entry, foregone rebound payoff and turnover. New-entry permission is distinct from management of an already-held position; no automatic held-position exit is authorized here.

LLMs should extract dated claims/events, map plausible mechanisms to existing stock/theme identities, identify contradictory evidence and explain the mechanically determined state. They do not invent numeric odds, move rank, size positions or override data refusal. Model-generated evidence remains non-authoritative until the corresponding feature/policy has a valid evaluation and permission.

## 12. Missingness: uncertainty is an input, not a harmless footer

For a declared universe N with m observed names and a observed advancers, the advancing share is bounded by:

`a / N <= true advancing share <= (a + N - m) / N`.

This is an identification bound, not a statistical confidence interval. With N=175 and m=9, even if every observed name declines, the advancing share could be anywhere from 0% to 94.86%. The example uses the screenshot's reported denominator only to illustrate the information loss; it is not an estimate of actual market breadth or an assertion about which nine names were observed.

Therefore, no reassuring whole-universe conclusion follows from that read without further assumptions. Renormalizing over the survivors may conceal a systematically missing population. Preserve separate candidate coverage, market-context coverage and per-name input validity. A data outage does not prove that the market is risky; it proves that the affected claim is not established.

## 15. Primary research: what transfers and what does not

- Cong, Feng, He and Wang, **Mosaics of Predictability**, NBER w35158, April 2026: motivates asset- and state-dependent predictability and interpretable partitions. Its reported portfolio results are not evidence of next-session Prophet performance; the abstract-level source was reviewed, not a reproduced experiment. https://www.nber.org/papers/w35158
- Daniel and Moskowitz, **Momentum Crashes**, NBER w20439 / Journal of Financial Economics 2016: emphasizes that long-short momentum crashes can occur during rebounds after panic states. It supports testing recovery/opportunity costs, not a claim that Prophet's long-only board shares that exact mechanism. https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439 ; https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/momentum-crashes
- Adams and MacKay, **Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection**, 2007: supplies an online filtering method, not foreknowledge of market shocks or a trading guarantee. https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742
- Gibbs and Candes, **Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift**, 2021: motivates adapting uncertainty to changing distributions. Long-run coverage is not a guarantee for tomorrow's crash, every subgroup or portfolio profitability. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170

The research does not depend on any proprietary competitor code or reconstructed private trading rule. Existing external-method research and licensed source boundaries remain separate.
