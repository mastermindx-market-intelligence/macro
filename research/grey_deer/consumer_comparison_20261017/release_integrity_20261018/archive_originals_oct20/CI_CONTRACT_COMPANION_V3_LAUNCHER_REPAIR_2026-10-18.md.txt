## Small shared launcher repair

The generator previously placed `sys.executable` directly in a shebang. Its whitespace split prevented the transport shim from starting under the owning virtual environment.

The new `_write_python_executable` uses the existing executable-file writer to create a `/bin/sh` launcher and writes the Python body to a sibling fixture file. The launcher uses `exec` with `shlex.quote(sys.executable)` and the quoted exact program path, followed by `"$0" "$@"`. The interpreter path is preserved exactly; it is neither resolved to a different executable nor looked up through `PATH`. Quoting accommodates spaces and apostrophes in the interpreter and program paths.

A two-line Python prelude removes the program-file argument from `sys.argv`, restoring the invoked alias and original arguments before the existing body runs. This preserves the command-name dispatch used by the fake `ssh`, `rsync`, `python3`, `systemctl` and other aliases. The full transport body is byte-for-byte identical to v2, including path containment, real-rsync peer binding, fixture observations, and checks of the received forward-CLI root and deployment SHA. This change adds no application or production release mechanism.
