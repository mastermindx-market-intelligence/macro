Date: 2026-10-20 UTC. Reviewer: `/root/release_scope_judgment_oct20`. Scope: the seven parent-supplied, immutable source files at Mastermind #1610 head **`7b5ccfffef4e90b89496751dd7bc0f55b711ed25`**. ## Verdict

**Accept this conditional static effect map. Production startup and final-release acceptance remain OPEN.** The seven files establish the registered startup handler, its order of operations, the scheduler's paused-start/preflight/resume sequence, nineteen job-registration sites, three conditional first-run workers, and several finite dynamic targets. They are enough to reject an effect-free-restart claim. They are not enough to prove the effective production gates, the pending-target quarantine implementation or the complete runtime import closure.

The most consequential finding is that the application imports the scheduler before its authoritative-auth assertion and serve-only branch. On the non-serve-only path, `scheduler.start()` can create a directory and initialize a jobstore; it registers jobs, starts paused, removes three retired job IDs, and calls the pending-target preflight before resume. The caller expressly accepts a `quarantined` result as safe to resume. Its actual quarantine implementation is outside the supplied files. First-run flags are checked later and do not govern this earlier sequence or the ordinary cron jobs. [S1], [S2]

These are existing candidate behaviors, not a newly demonstrated regression in the test-only #1610 successor. No software patch or wider operational change is proposed by this review.
