and portfolio families. At 4412–4417 the receipt marks a family contradicted whenever
that reader returns any nonempty `contradictions` or `conflicts`. It does not inspect

do not satisfy primary coverage.

The reproduction deliberately supplies a contradiction explicitly scoped to
`macro_rates`; it still produces `portfolio: not covered (contradiction present)`.
This proves a scope-loss counterexample. The actual live contradiction payload was
