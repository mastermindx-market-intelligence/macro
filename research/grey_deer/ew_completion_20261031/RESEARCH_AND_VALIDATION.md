## 3. Types and objective math

- **Measurement:** group relative performance over a *matched* start/end: relative wealth equals (1 + receiving group return) / (1 + donor or benchmark return) minus 1. Show both absolute leg returns and distinguish BOTH_DOWN relative shelter from actual gains. Do not label relative wealth as capital inflow.
- **Relative-wealth drawdown:** compute the trajectory of the same-clock ratio of group wealth to benchmark wealth, and its peak-to-trough decline under a registered peak rule. **Do not** subtract independently timed maximum drawdowns, which measure different endpoints.
- **Categorical watch:** no issued probability, so no Brier/log loss. Score observation coverage, alert-episode frequency, time in watch, false-alarm/missed-event burden, issued and delivered lead, comprehension and downstream misuse.
- **Sector event probability:** only for a separately issued immutable model/definition/target/horizon; score with proper probability methods and a baseline for the *same sector outcome, same horizon, same issued rows*. Broad SPY market-loss probability is not a sector-loss probability.
- **Authorized action:** a third product with its own owner, mandate and safe policy scope, possibly no market forecast at all. It cannot inherit ranking/size/execute rights from description or statistics.

## 4. Flexible preregistration before scoring

For each candidate, Fable/the science owner should decide and freeze:

1. **Primary estimand:** one registered market or sector event family, benchmark, eligible universe/member identities as known, price adjustment/corporate actions, market session calendar, onset, existing-damage exclusion and horizon(s). Market drawdown, sector drawdown and leadership/relative-strength shifts are not interchangeable. Register separate families if studying multiple claims; account for multiplicity.
2. **Clock chain:** source session/observed time, original vendor available_at (UNKNOWN permitted), ingested, first-writer, model issued, published, served, user notified and outcome onset. Lead_measurement, lead_issued_product and lead_delivered use distinct real timestamps; do not backdate k-session confirmation to its first component.
3. **Model/version isolation:** source rights, definition and model hash, frozen training/calibration vintages, original vs reconstructed history, corrections, member universe, trial/parameter-selection budget. October-discovered hypotheses require *later* unseen confirmatory observations.
4. **Baselines:** same-eligible-universe unconditional sector event rate; conditional sector event rate using market information; simple lagged RS or other credible “cheap” comparator. All must predict the identical target on identical rows, including exposure/coverage restrictions.
5. **Dependence:** prospective holdout eras, label embargo across longest forecast horizon plus confirmation/lookback and shared data ancestry, overlapping event windows, sector family nesting and episode cluster count. Set power, practical effect margin, finite looks or a justifiable anytime-valid process before outcome examination.
6. **Missingness and rights:** holidays (US/CN/HK/CA), future/malformed dates, historical source-available timestamps, original cohort membership and Yahoo price revision/redistribution rights. Unknown/unavailable never becomes a correct negative; no currently revised price is relabeled original PIT.
7. **Scorecard:** for probabilities, proper score, calibration, baseline paired differences, uncertainty and abstentions. For categorical watches, false alarm time/episodes, coverage and lead; keep unserved event labels out of fake historical “delivered skill.” Prevent unsupported policy inference.

Statistical options are open to Fable. Fixed looks are one route; [time-uniform confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08240v9) may be valid for a correctly specified sequential process, but do not automatically solve sector overlap, delayed labels, selection or dependent data. Power can remain inadequate even after simple sample-count floors are met.

## 6. Research literature: primary methods, not Mastermind proof

| Primary publication | Inspected evidence | Application here |

These are carefully scoped methodological sources, **not a systematic literature review of every market crash model**, and no paper supplies evidence of Mastermind signal-specific predictive success.
