## Exposure, clocks and action authority

The central exposure distinction is supported by the supplied measurements: the small SPY drawdown coexists with much larger semiconductor damage, while RSP, XLP, XLV and XLE supply evidence against a claim that all equity exposures are deteriorating together. The map does not promote those overlapping proxy counts into constituent breadth or independent event counts.

The instrument qualification is explicit and material. QQQ is treated as Nasdaq-100 exposure; it is not presented as the Nasdaq Composite. The map therefore leaves the literal Composite comparison and the requested dated September–October decision-time reconstruction open. DIA's older observation is also acknowledged. USD country ETFs are not presented as local-currency national indexes or independent country populations. Acceptance of this snapshot cannot close any of those reconstruction gaps.

Preparation on 20 October is visibly separated from observations dated 16 October and the four rows dated 14 October. The absence of a 19 October close, current executable prices, historical `available_at` and first-user publication is stated. The action priorities consequently remain a dated review agenda. They are not evidence of what the system actually published at a past decision time.

Protection/reduction wording is tied to excessive concentration, existing limits, current confirmation and policy-owner checks. Hold/add language for XLP/XLV is conditional on mandate, current quotes, absolute performance and concentration. Cash/bills require instrument and eligibility refresh, and the older BIL/SGOV date is disclosed. Numerical allocations, order size and execution remain with Portfolio/Prophet and their validated owners. No new numerical sizing or executable instruction is introduced.

The defense discussion preserves the distinction between relative opportunity and absolute capital preservation. Positive observed defensive-equity returns support a candidate review; the map does not claim guaranteed protection. Its explicit failure case—relative winners can become absolute losers—is useful. Recovery remains a separate problem: the map does not turn a rebound, positive week or descriptive stabilization flag into a validated re-entry signal. The horizon labels are qualitative review horizons, with no measured lead-time or holding-period performance established by this artifact.

## Competing pathways and limits

The alternatives and falsifiers are adequate for this bounded diagnostic. Contained rotation is retained alongside wider damage. Concentrated deleveraging/defensive escape is presented as an interpretation consistent with prices, with fund flows explicitly unmeasured. Continued leadership repair weakens that interpretation; synchronized deterioration strengthens a wider-damage concern. Systemic contagion is left unconfirmed and requires evidence beyond pooled ETF counts. Credit ETF prices are not silently treated as credit spreads, funding conditions or causal transmission.

No updated credit spread, funding, MOVE, earnings-surprise or options-positioning measurement is claimed. Their absence limits the inference rather than being filled with a score. The supplied primary-source record rejects the mismatched Nasdaq date and does not admit a daily value from the unavailable historical table. Its SPY check is narrowly bounded to a single close; it does not authenticate all endpoints, all other ETFs or original information availability.
