- D: Leadership/rotation production input not advancing (cache, collector lag, failed nightly cluster, bad source rights/credits, valid no-data or cadence constraint), and envelope degrades correctly. Inspect input last natural valid as-of and original failure. Repair upstream source only after qualifying source legal rights/PIT; no risk forcing.
- E: Mirror pair differing by design due intentional special archival/release mode. Locate source-owner **accepted** decision that explicitly supersedes the docs saying same bytes.
