- A: Risk envelope builder ran successfully on recent session and wrote **both** same bytes, but Git commit only updated public. Look for commit staging exclusions, follow-up post-rebase restore, artifact cleanup and cross-lane writer; outcome is a **publication/commit integrity** repair, not risk engine recalibration.

Look for original warning with destination path, same-run source hash/digest and final publish; outcome is verify two successful target writes and fail-closed **this projection** without taking unrelated market outputs hostage.

Inspect input last natural valid as-of and original failure. Repair upstream source only after qualifying source legal rights/PIT; no risk forcing.

If legitimate, amend contract with versioned consumer scope; do not silently declare incident or copy public back onto old durable.
