## Delivered mechanism, not another integrity cleanup

internal read of individually accepted, digest-pinned policy records to the exact
board object and source-owner read window. Supported actions are only
`SUPPRESS_NEW_ENTRY` and `NO_NEW_LONG_RISK` for the US board's
`NEW_LONG_RECOMMENDATION` lifecycle. This consumes a rule; it does not invent a
hazard, learn a threshold, mint a grant, create a registry or allocate a portfolio.

registry/grant owner. Passing both the proposed records and their expected hashes
from an untrusted request would defeat this prerequisite. A hash or Python type
is not authority. No production policy, expected-hash allowlist or activation
configuration is installed by this change.

`engine.prophet_bridge.originate_plans` accepts the internal read explicitly. It
keeps the original selector and ranking, preserves existing duplicate/open-plan
checks, and enforces the disposition BEFORE constructing a new plan. Rejection
is counted as `market_policy_suppressed`, never hidden as an invalid chart or
random score. Named per-candidate dispositions carry every policy ID, rule,
version, grant reference, expiry and restoration condition. Lossless accounting
includes the new category. The raw research board is untouched.

Every applicable restriction is retained; policies do not vote or offset each
other. Revoking or expiring one rule cannot lift another. Missing fresh source
evidence cannot erase a still-valid pinned active rule. Missing expected records,
unsupported action/scope, altered digest or invalid read window withholds the
explicitly controlled new-entry path. NO_POLICY_CONSTRAINT is not buy approval:
the native admission, entry, duplicate and plan checks still apply.
