## 4. Ranked card H1 — independent peer-participation acceleration

**Version:** H1 v0.1. **Disposition:** first and only numerical intake.  
**Crosswalk:** Q05 primary, Q02 attribution, Q10 selection. B09 produces group evidence, B10 owns the bounded research model, B06 owns measurement, B11 is a held downstream consumer. Q03 earlier discovery, Q09 payoff timing and Q12 routing are not silently added to this trial. [S3–S4,S13]

### Mechanism and prediction

After controlling for a stock's own move, price-paid context and ordinary industry information, an increase in the fraction of *other issuers* participating in its economic group may predict additional H10 opportunity. The hypothesis is about **acceleration beyond the current level of peer breadth**, not simply a preference for strong groups.

For the owner's predeclared primary group at decision t, hold the membership and weights fixed across two adjacent five-session windows. Exclude every listing of the focal economic issuer. Let `b_now` be weighted peer breadth outperforming SPY over t−5..t and `b_prev` the analogous breadth over t−10..t−5. The proposed feature is:

`a(i,t) = b_now(i,t) − b_prev(i,t)`.

No “best theme” selection is permitted. No peer group or issuer mapping may be reconstructed from today's winners. A true observed breadth of zero is a value; unqualified membership, clocks, source rights or compulsory price coverage are typed missing states. A singleton after issuer exclusion cannot generate peer confirmation.

### Comparator and one-experiment budget

The proposed configurations are C0 (own-price/industry/coverage controls), C1 (C0 information plus current ex-issuer breadth), and C2 (C1 plus acceleration). The **only primary comparison is C2−C1**. C0 and the genuine incumbent-domain comparison are explanatory controls and cannot rescue a failed primary. All configurations count in the same recorded experiment; this is not three untracked trials.

Start with date-weighted ridge, an unpenalized intercept, training-only standardization and proposed fixed lambda1, without a tuning sweep. That is a design proposal, not a claim of optimal regularization. B10 must freeze the exact implementation, units and feature manifest. Required controls include own5/20-session relative movement, own-price extension/volatility, liquidity, ordinary industry level/change, group size and source-coverage indicators. C1/C2 share training rows and source access; unavailable peer evidence leads both to the same C0 fallback. Coverage-only effects and within-covered effects must be reported separately.

### Population, target and falsifier

Use original **US curated, explicitly momentum-labelled candidate-date landmarks**. This is not a hand-picked semiconductor basket and not a newly minted B1 episode population. Source-default `other` does not establish a valid strategy label. Preserve issuer/listing identity and the native `(stamp_date,ticker,board_definition)` key. Off-list/unscored and unknown observations remain accounted for; they are not assigned last rank or zero performance.

The proposed primary is the equally date-weighted difference in **decision-frozen top-five H10 SPY-excess marks**. Candidate, feature, prediction, selection, maturity and usable-paired-date denominators are separate. A missing selected outcome leaves the date unresolved. It never promotes the sixth-ranked name into the original top five. A small date does not switch to K1 after outcomes. H10 corresponds to the native momentum ruler, not a new entry holding rule. [S7]

Reject the acceleration mechanism when it does not beat the static-breadth comparator under the frozen rule, or when the apparent increment is attributable to self-inclusion, source coverage, ordinary industry exposure or a few dominant groups. Gains confined to current-only memberships remain diagnostic. Selection cannot authorize earlier entries, and gross mark improvement cannot be described as executable net alpha.

## 6. Ranked card H3 — signed economic propagation with original-shareholder capture

**Version:** H3 v0.1. **Disposition:** untested alternative; no Cycle diagnostic.  
**Crosswalk:** Q13 primary; Q05 group-control and Q09 payoff interpretation; Q08 original-equity/funding is a dependency, not a newly seized research seat. Existing GMI/Graph/Fusion B04/B09/B11/B15; Financial/Data OS/Portfolio-Risk B17/B18/B19 where capture is claimed. [S3,S15]

### Mechanism

Start with one **dated, directed, one-hop edge species**, such as a disclosed customer-to-supplier revenue exposure. A source-defined customer operating/demand change may reach a supplier after the customer has repriced. Edge direction and economic sign matter: a buyer's investment can benefit some suppliers but expand future capacity and pressure others. A generic “connected to AI” label is not the feature.

An interpretable candidate feature is an exposure-weighted source shock over edges that were eligible at the decision. Direct exposure and proxy membership stay separate. Do not combine unverified graph confidence with exposure size as if both were measured probabilities. Compare the transparent edge feature with own-price, industry, node-degree/popularity and source-coverage controls before contemplating a temporal graph network.

### Equity capture is a separate necessary claim

For ordinary operating issuers, test whether the edge increment predicts the original listing's supported horizon outcome; proposed H63 uses an existing ruler but is not yet a registered horizon. Operating follow-through is a mechanism check, not a second promotion primary. For distressed/recovery names, a valid enterprise-demand forecast is insufficient. Track the original shareholder's claim after funding, dilution, senior claims, restructuring and possible cancellation. Financing proceeds must not be counted twice in net debt and equity value. A deterministic recovery scenario is not a calibrated forecast.

Include unsuccessful and cancelled securities when the lawful identity/terminal source supports them. Where that source is missing, the original-equity claim is unestimable; silently limiting to surviving listings cannot establish it. This is a real blocker, not a reason to manufacture terminal values. #7868's separate Cycle protocol/manifest/outcome restrictions remain unchanged.

### Controls, falsifiers and dependency cost

Same eligible edge cohort and same source budget for no-edge/industry/own-price controls; degree-preserving edge shuffles and availability-shift controls must be registered before results, not chosen to flatter the graph. A future-known edge must be refused. Fresh duplicate articles about the same event are not independent shocks.

Reject graph attribution if ordinary sector exposure, degree or source coverage explains the increment, if shuffled relations perform comparably, or if historical edges were selected after the event. Reject an equity-capture claim when economic recovery does not reach original holders after funding/claims. Do not let an economic-only success rescue a failed shareholder outcome.

Minimum evidence comprises originally dated directed edges, exposure basis, source-defined shocks, lawful original-listing outcomes and a broad enough independent shock/issuer set. One industry boom cannot establish a general graph effect. D03/D06/D07/D08 and the funding/identity owners remain prerequisites. **Why third:** it addresses a genuinely different information channel but has the heaviest source/terminal-outcome dependency. A one-hop tabular feature is computationally modest; graph acquisition and identity coverage are likely more costly, an engineering judgment rather than a measured budget. Historical linked-firm predictability motivates the test, not a current-alpha promise. [P1]
