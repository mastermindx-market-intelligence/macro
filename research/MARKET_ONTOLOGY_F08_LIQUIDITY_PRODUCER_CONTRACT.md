# F08 per-stock liquidity producer contract

MO-PAID-036 / MO-DELTA-014 needs a qualified per-stock input for actual-holdings risk readouts. The native owner already exists: `engine/liquidity_chip.py::compute` computes the 20-observed-session median of close times share volume and its existing tiers/build heuristics. The separate legacy 21-day mean, board metrics, scoring and UI keep their existing behavior.

The stock-library writer adds `liquidity_profile` to its existing per-ticker JSON. `scripts/stock_liquidity_profile.py::write_record` is the directly testable one-record publication seam used by `scripts/build_stock_library.py`; there is no second store, risk formula, collector, public page or user-state read. The engine job moves its existing late cold-runner raw daily R2 restore into the preamble before its dashboard/stock-library builder, with one bounded restore shared by consumers; the collector and its gated publication remain the original owners. This closes the materialization ordering gap without launching new collection. Terminal Portfolio PR1044 retains its route/UI and actual-holding consumer scope. This producer release does not claim full MO-PAID-036 acceptance.

The qualified projection requires raw close and raw share volume from the existing `equity.bars.daily.massive` store, the exact 20 NYSE sessions through the completed-session cutoff, finite valid values, source fingerprint and explicit USD evidence covering that window. The native formula and thresholds are reused unchanged. Current-only currency reference, country/ticker spelling, a profile financial currency and an adjusted-price proxy cannot establish this quote-currency window. Missing information yields null metrics and null `thinly_traded`, with a typed coverage/unavailability reason. An unknown value is never a false thinness flag. The raw producer currently fills missing source volume with zero; its parquet cannot distinguish that from real zero activity. The reader therefore refuses a qualified window containing zero instead of treating erased missingness as observed turnover.

Licensing is already recorded in `research/licenses/MASSIVE_ENTITLEMENT_RECORD.md`: the operator-confirmed enterprise instrument covers daily bars, derived redistribution, export and portfolio analytics. This release records adoption of that existing evidence, grants no new access, exposes no confidential agreement, and gives no forecast, ranking, sizing, gate or trading authority. The Yahoo `equity.bars.daily.stocks` contract remains adjusted and redistribution-not-granted; it is not a fallback.

Clocks remain separate: `source_asof` is the actual final daily bar; `recorded_at` is this projection's observed build instant; `store_updated_at` is only the native store manifest stamp. A manifest rewrite does not establish ticker ingestion, first publication or historical availability. `ingested_at`, `published_at` and `known_at` remain null when not evidenced. Filesystem mtime and source stat tokens are not freshness or content hashes. Currency evidence retains its own source date and validity bounds.

Read states follow F08's native vocabulary: `READ_OK` only for qualified values, `READ_NO_COVERAGE` for missing/insufficient/unqualified coverage, and `READ_UNAVAILABLE` for malformed/unreadable input. Zero activity is not silently interpreted as a liquid or illiquid instrument. These are descriptive turnover/build heuristics, not order-book depth, independent episodes, causal estimates, executable-size guarantees or forecasts.
