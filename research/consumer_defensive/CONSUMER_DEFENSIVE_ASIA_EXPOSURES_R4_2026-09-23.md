## 1. Main result

Asia-facing coverage must preserve business, geography, channel, fiscal period, unit scale and shareholder claims rather than attach a country label to the same global template. The proposed user job is to identify the specific business producing a change, connect that change to retained economics, and distinguish local commercial performance from consolidation, currency and financial presentation.

Seven current-period issuer dossiers and one deliberately historical measurement case follow. These are selected examples, not complete China/Japan/India coverage or a certification of the latest filing for every company. Facts are dated and source-attributed. Calculations are explicitly derived. Economic implications are Sol's proposals, not established causal coefficients or investment recommendations.

The most important new distinctions are category-versus-company growth, category share versus aggregate share, internal versus external revenue, profit versus cash, issuer reporting currency versus customer geography, and equivalent labels with different definitions. Strong reported performance can coexist with weak demand or cash; weak reported growth can coexist with healthy volume or local-currency performance.

## 2. China-facing business dossiers

### R4B-C01 — Nongfu Spring: the product generating growth is not necessarily the familiar headline category

**Reviewed disclosure.** The H1 2026 issuer announcement reports tea revenue of RMB13.122bn within RMB29.718bn total revenue. Packaged water revenue is RMB9.641bn, compared with RMB9.443bn in H1 2025; its segment result fell from RMB3.347bn to RMB3.257bn. The source does not supply segment assets/liabilities for resource-allocation review. Original tables are in RMB thousands. [B01]

**Derived reading.** Tea represents 44.16% of reviewed-period revenue and approximately 74.07% of the group's absolute revenue increase. Water revenue increased about 2.10% while its segment result fell about 2.70%. These are dated accounting calculations, not future revenue weights or a recommendation. Product segment results must not be presented as attributable common-share earnings.

**Research design.** The relevant question is whether repeat category demand, distribution productivity and net realization persist after promotional and production costs. Refute a simple company-wide growth thesis when a legacy category's economics deteriorate, or when revenue gains fail to translate into cash. No segment ROIC is fabricated without compatible capital data. A Hong Kong listing cannot substitute for actual consumer geography or reporting-currency exposure.

### R4B-C02 — Mengniu: group profit is not a substitute for product operating economics

**Reviewed disclosure.** H1 2026 external revenue is RMB44.795bn versus RMB41.567bn. Matched-period gross margins round to 40.8% and 41.7%. Shareholder-attributable profit is RMB2.371bn; the source separately reports associates and minority interests. The segment table totals RMB45.790bn before eliminating RMB0.996bn of internal sales. Liquid milk includes multiple formats, not only fresh or protein-enriched products. [B02]

**Research design.** Separate category sales, gross profit, segment results, central expenses, associates, tax and shareholder attribution. A rise in parent profit must not be assigned automatically to improving product margins. A segment's share of external revenue cannot be calculated from internal-inclusive sales. The source's named operating-profit measure and the segment-results subtotal need their own reconciliations rather than being renamed identical metrics.

**Rerating conditions and falsifiers.** Research price realization and mix by actual format, production/route economics, promotional investment and working capital. A thesis of premiumization needs repeat demand and retained contribution, not merely a larger share of a smaller denominator. Lower agricultural input prices do not establish benefit after packaging, formulation, promotional and channel costs. Source-native definitions precede comparison with global dairy or nutrition businesses.

### R4B-C03 — China Resources Beer: premium-format growth has to be scaled to the whole business

**Reviewed disclosure.** The H1 2026 announcement reports group revenue of RMB24.240bn: beer RMB23.670bn and baijiu RMB0.570bn. Beer volume is 6.6 million kilolitres, up 1.7%; beer revenue grew 2.2%. The release gives nested premium-category growth descriptions, which are not additive independent segments. [B03]

**Research design.** A premium brand can grow rapidly from a small base while total physical demand grows slowly. The useful exposure has a denominator, product scope, volume basis and retained earnings path. Separate premium category shares from group growth, and beer from baijiu economics. A licensed brand's growth is not an independent demand confirmation when already counted in its partner's global system.

**Rerating conditions and falsifiers.** Test whether premium mix, unit economics and operating efficiency improve absolute recurring contribution, with any nonrecurring property/disposal effects separate. Refute a generic premiumization thesis where weaker mainstream activity, promotion or distribution costs offset the higher-tier gains. A sales-only read cannot establish which shareholders receive a brand's economics.

### R4B-C04 — Wuliangye: earnings recovery and collections must remain distinct

**Reviewed disclosure.** The English H1 2026 report gives revenue growth of 20.87%, shareholder-profit growth of 89.30%, and operating cash outflow of RMB2.154bn. It attributes the profit comparison partly to the prior low base and the cash comparison partly to collection policy and bank-acceptance maturities. The document is dated August 29; its English archive appeared later in September. [B04]

**Research design.** Strong profit and negative operating cash are a question to investigate, not automatic proof of either excellent cash economics or misconduct. Compare contractual collections, bills, customer advances, distributor inventory and subsequent cash realization over matching periods. Keep the issuer's sell-through explanation attributed; it is not an independently observed household panel.

**Rerating conditions and falsifiers.** The research should test demand during comparable occasions, channel profitability, cash collection and required aging/production investment. A favorable comparison against a depressed base does not establish a return to the former earnings trajectory. Announced or catalog product attributes are not evidence of final consumer use. The authoritative-language document and later translation need separate availability clocks for any historical study. This mirrored report remains research evidence pending native retention/source admission, not a production-authorized fact feed.

## 3. Japan and India dossiers

### R4B-C05 — Japan Tobacco: gaining share inside categories can coexist with losing share overall

**Reviewed disclosure.** JT's July 30 H1 release says Taiwan total tobacco market share fell 1.0 percentage point to 51.6%, despite share gains within both Combustibles and RRP. The same release distinguishes reported from constant-currency performance and applies a Canada-related adjustment retrospectively to selected comparative metrics. [B05]

**Research design.** Changing category weights can reverse the aggregate direction; both statements can be true. Therefore, category mix is a necessary part of competitive-share interpretation. Do not classify this as a source contradiction or delete one observation. Retain each share's market definition, denominator, product coverage and time window.

**Rerating conditions and falsifiers.** Analyze which category produces retained contribution and whether transition investment offsets displaced legacy cash. The relevant commercial market need not match the issuer's country of listing. Reported JPY growth can differ from local business growth, while a later restatement can improve comparability without having existed for an earlier investor. Neither FX translation nor an adjustment label independently proves demand or recurring cash improvement.

### R4B-C06 — Tata Consumer: volume, cost pass-through and currency answer different questions

**Reviewed disclosure.** For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, Tata Consumer reports INR5,349 crore consolidated revenue, up 12%. Its India Branded underlying volume growth is 13%, not a group-volume measure. India tea volume grew 2% while lower costs passed to consumers reduced revenue; international reported growth of 16% differs from constant-currency growth of 3%. [B06]

**Research design.** The quarter is Q1 FY2027, not a 2027 calendar observation. A crore is ten million currency units. A selected domestic volume statistic must not be used to explain the entire consolidated sales bridge. Lower sales realization under pass-through is not proof that the consumer franchise deteriorated, while currency-supported growth is not additional physical demand.

**Rerating conditions and falsifiers.** Test whether distribution, productive new categories and conversion economics generate incremental cash after investment. Refute a broad growth claim where an acquired/perimeter or currency effect explains the headline without comparable underlying improvement. Brand associations and joint ventures must preserve actual consolidation and commercial rights; no group revenue is inferred from outlet count or retail system sales.

### R4B-C07 — ITC: even the label net revenue needs a native definition

**Reviewed disclosure.** ITC's July 31 standalone presentation shows Q1 FY2027 gross revenue +28.1%, named net revenue -14.4%, and EBITDA -27.9%. **Reconciliation boundary.** Subtracting statement excise from statement gross product/service revenue yields a different constructed series: INR16,758.84 and INR19,612.71 crore. That arithmetic is not the publisher's complete net-revenue KPI bridge. Preserve the native KPI separately; do not overwrite it or allege a source error. This research has not resolved every additional adjustment behind the named measure.

**Research design.** Consumer-facing FMCG, cigarettes, agriculture, paper and other activities have different economics. Standalone and consolidated numbers are not interchangeable, nor are a segment's EBITDA and segment profit. The dossier should identify what the operating business retains rather than interpret tax-inclusive turnover as demand. Investigate pricing, product mix, cash collection and competitive response using compatible measures; policy explanations remain neutral and source-attributed.

## 4. Four cross-market models that now have concrete tests

### 4.1 Category composition and apparent contradiction

An aggregate share is a weighted combination of category shares only when category sizes and share bases are compatible. Both shares can improve while the company loses total share if demand moves toward the category in which it is relatively smaller.

An explicitly synthetic illustration uses initial category weights 80%/20% and company shares 60%/20%, yielding 52% total share. Later weights are 50%/50% and shares improve to 61%/21%, yet total share falls to 41%. These are not estimates of JT's categories. They demonstrate a mathematical failure mode and explain why a typed scope split can be more useful than a universal positive/negative share label.

The research question is then whether aggregate profit follows the same direction; category contributions, prices and investment can differ again. A volume share decomposition must not be silently promoted into an earnings share decomposition.

### 4.2 Country, currency and shareholder outcome

Keep the location of consumers, production and procurement separate from functional currency, reporting currency and the investor's security currency. A locally improving operation can translate into weaker reported results. A change in an exchange rate may also alter costs, competitiveness and balance-sheet items, rather than act solely as a translation factor.

Only consistently defined measures support a currency bridge. Do not divide a reported total-revenue measure by an adjusted core-revenue measure and label the entire residual FX. Even a valid matched bridge compounds: reported-factor equals local-factor times conversion-factor under the simplified model, not always the sum of displayed percentage changes. No currency sensitivity coefficient or forecast is estimated here.

For valuation, company cash flow and the chosen discount rate must be in compatible currency and nominal/real terms. A revenue share in China cannot be multiplied by a USD discount-rate change and called a measured sensitivity. Country research is economically granular or it becomes another misleading label.

### 4.3 Collections, channel health and seasonal comparison

Revenue recognition, customer advances, bank instruments, receivables and operating cash each have their own clock. A cash collection policy can change the timing of reported cash without proving a permanent demand change. A high prior-period collection base can make growth comparisons extreme. Conversely, persistent collection weakness can materially weaken an earnings thesis.

The next evidence should distinguish timing from persistent conversion: comparable-window collections, receivable/bill maturity, customer concentration, net price after rebates, returns and subsequent cash. Seasonal occasions need matching calendar windows, not an automatic Gregorian quarter-to-quarter comparison. No historical adjustment is invented for holiday effects without a documented calendar and method.

A direct channel may improve visibility or economics, but it can also transfer marketing, fulfillment, receivables and inventory responsibilities to the producer. Compare retained contribution after these costs, not only the producer's higher selling price. Channel names are source-specific and may include different commercial arrangements.

### 4.4 Classification changes and translation vintages

A later restatement or reclassification supplies a useful comparable view. It does not prove that an investor had those same metrics historically. Preserve as-published and latest-comparable versions under the existing correction/point-in-time owners.

Likewise, a Chinese original, an English translation and a third-party mirror have different availability and retention provenance. A translated report's financial period does not identify its publication date. A URL folder is not an authoritative date. Do not pretend that a public mirror is an authenticated native acquisition receipt or that two mirrors provide independent confirmation.
