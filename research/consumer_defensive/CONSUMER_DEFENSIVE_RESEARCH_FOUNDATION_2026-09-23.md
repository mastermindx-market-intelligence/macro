## 1. The outcome and the first research conclusion

The user job is to start with a Consumer Defensive opportunity, understand the actual category and company exposure, identify what changed in demand and unit economics, see how that could change earnings and cash flow per share, distinguish what the market already expects, and find the exact evidence and invalidation conditions. The machine job is to preserve those distinctions across companies, periods, geographies, channels and source revisions instead of converting every positive headline into the same bullish theme score.

The governing analytical chain proposed for this sector is:

`consumer occasion / category demand -> distribution and competitive share -> realized price, volume and mix -> gross profit and operating investment -> earnings / cash flow per share -> expectations and valuation -> market recognition`

Supply, procurement and capacity enter this chain when they affect its economics. They are not the universal starting point. A bottleneck can benefit an upstream seller and harm a downstream processor; lower input prices can coexist with a worse processing spread. A mature category can contain a rapidly gaining brand. A strong reported sales number can reflect acquisitions, inflation or channel inventory rather than consumption.

R1 establishes a sector-native research grammar, twelve provisional coverage families, eight economic archetypes, ten issuer anchors with eleven dated cases, an expectations/valuation bridge, leading-data feasibility boundaries and twenty-four proposed adversarial acceptance cases. These are research outputs, not an accepted production schema, complete company census or calibrated predictive model.

## 3. Taxonomy: a hierarchy plus separate analytical facets

A single deep tree is insufficient. Use the existing governed hierarchy for navigation, then preserve separate research facets. The names below are proposal vocabulary, not newly registered graph types or canonical identifiers.

| Dimension | Question answered | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Structural category | What is sold or distributed? | Pet food is a category; premiumization is not its structural parent. |
| Demand theme | What consumption change is being investigated? | Protein adoption, value seeking and reduced-sugar preference may cross categories. |
| Business role | Who earns money and how? | Brand owner, license holder, manufacturer, ingredient supplier, bottler, distributor and retailer cannot be interchangeable. |
| Economic archetype | Which profit bridge is relevant? | A membership retailer and a branded-food manufacturer need different operating models. |
| Channel / customer | Through whom does demand reach the business? | Grocery, club, convenience, foodservice, direct and marketplace activity have different measurement boundaries. |

## 4. Eight economic archetypes to investigate

These are analytical templates proposed from accounting structure and the contrasting cases below. Their predictive usefulness remains unvalidated.

| Archetype | Operating bridge | Rerating hypothesis to test | Main falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Established brand compounder | Category demand x share x net realization, less production and brand investment | Volume/share stabilize without giving back all price or cutting necessary investment | Apparent margin gain depends on reduced support while repeat demand deteriorates |
| Distribution-led challenger | Productive outlets x within-outlet velocity, with retention and repeat purchase | New distribution converts into repeat sales and profitable scale | Initial pipeline fill is mistaken for persistent consumer demand |
| Brand / concentrate / licensing system | Contract-defined monetization of a wider commercial system | System growth translates into attributable retained earnings | Retail system sales or partner revenue is counted as issuer revenue |
| Commodity-exposed branded producer | Realized price less lagged procurement and conversion costs | Margin recovery occurs with tolerable elasticity and sustained reinvestment | Hedging, inventory, promotions or weaker mix absorb the expected benefit |
| Biological / processing spread business | Output/byproduct revenue less livestock/feed, conversion and utilization costs | Spreads and asset utilization improve across the relevant cycle | Output prices fall faster than inputs; inventory losses or underutilization offset relief |
| Membership / value retailer | Merchandise gross profit plus membership/services, less operating and fulfillment expense | Traffic/retention and incremental service contribution improve cash returns | Revenue growth comes from uneconomic fulfillment or nonrecurring benefits |
| Distributor / private-label / pass-through supplier | Throughput and service margin, contract resets and working capital | New business or richer service mix improves absolute profit/cash returns | Lower revenue from pass-through is mislabeled share loss, or contract wins lack profit |
| Mature cash generator transitioning to new formats | Legacy cash flow plus new-format contribution minus conversion and reinvestment costs | New economics offset decline without undermining cash conversion | New-category volume lacks retained profit, cannibalization is ignored, or rights lapse |

The same company may span several archetypes. Business-unit analysis comes before a consolidated valuation conclusion. A label such as quality, growth or defensive does not substitute for the earnings bridge.

## 5. Eleven dated cases from ten issuer anchors

These deliberately contrasting examples test semantics, not stock attractiveness. All growth comparisons below retain the publisher's own basis. Source IDs refer to section 14. Percentages of growth and percentage-point contributions are not interchangeable; rounded bridges are not forced into exact arithmetic.

### C01 — P&G: reported growth without organic growth

In fiscal Q4 2026, P&G reported net sales up 2% while organic sales were unchanged; volume, pricing and mix were neutral at the consolidated level. Its table separately identifies FX and rounding. Beauty organic sales rose 4% while Baby, Feminine & Family Care declined 2%. Preserve segment disagreement rather than infer broad demand growth from the consolidated headline. [S01]

### C02 — Coca-Cola: system volume is not the revenue bridge's shipment measure

For Q2 2026, organic revenue grew 6%, with concentrate-sales growth of 4% and price/mix of 2%; unit case volume grew 5%. Asia Pacific reported case-volume growth of 8%, concentrate-sales growth of 11%, price/mix of -9% and organic revenue growth of 2%. The source attributes shipment differences to timing. Preserve issuer definitions and geography; do not relabel price/mix as pure pricing or unit cases as company revenue. [S02]

### C03 — Hershey: margin recovery with weak volume

Q2 2026 organic constant-currency sales rose 3.6%. The rounded bridge shows price +12 points and volume/mix -8 points; the narrative describes volume decline and elasticity. Adjusted operating margin rose 450 basis points to 20.2%. The release separately attributes reported-margin effects to derivative mark-to-market gains. This supports separate demand, price realization and margin observations, not an assertion that all three improved. [S03]

### C04 — Mondelez: reported EPS and underlying operating performance disagree

Q2 2026 organic net revenue rose 2.2%, with volume/mix +0.7 points. Reported diluted EPS rose 144.9%, while adjusted EPS declined 2.7% at constant currency. Keep each accounting and currency basis intact. A large reported EPS increase alone must not trigger an underlying earnings-acceleration label. [S04]

### C05 — General Mills: acquired pet revenue and channel inventory

Historical FY2025 Q4 North America Pet sales grew 12%; acquisition contribution was 9 points and organic growth 3%. The company reported that organic sales outpaced retail sales by about 3 points, largely from retailer inventory ahead of customer activations. Segment profit declined 3%. This is a deliberate older counterexample: neither acquired growth nor pipeline fill establishes accelerating consumer consumption or earnings. [S05]

### C06 — General Mills: portfolio contraction versus organic deterioration

FY2026 Q3 sales declined 8%, including a 6-point net divestiture/acquisition headwind; organic sales declined 3%. Management described price investment and anticipated later improvement. Keep actual observations, corporate-perimeter changes and the forward recovery thesis separate. The release is a March 2026 case, not claimed to be the latest company result. [S06]

### C07 — Costco: comparable growth and membership need separate denominators

In the twelve weeks ended May 10, 2026, company comparable sales grew 9.8%, or 6.6% excluding gasoline-price and FX effects. Net merchandise sales were $69.154 billion; membership fees were $1.373 billion. Fees are revenue, not a disclosed standalone profit figure. Do not compare headline comps directly with a manufacturer's organic volume, or treat the gasoline/FX adjustment as a full inflation adjustment. [S07]

### C08 — Freshpet: volume-led growth still needs a cash bridge

Q2 2026 sales grew 15.5%, with volume +15.7% and price/mix -0.2%. Gross margin improved, but SG&A as a share of sales increased. First-half net income also benefited from an equity-investment disposal gain. Distinguish demand, plant leverage, distribution expense, operating earnings and investing proceeds; growth or an accounting gain alone does not establish sustainable free-cash-flow economics. [S08]

### C09 — Walmart: service mix and accounting presentation matter

Walmart's Q2 FY2027 communication reported global e-commerce growth of 23% and advertising growth of 38%. Its footnote says advertising is recorded in net sales or as a reduction of cost of sales depending on the arrangement. The operating discussion separately identifies nonrecurring benefit effects. Do not add all advertised business volumes to consolidated revenue or attribute the entire profit change to retail operating leverage. [S09]

### C10 — Tyson: cheaper inputs can accompany worse spreads

In its September 3, 2026 outlook update, Tyson described pork product values falling faster than livestock costs, compressing processing spreads. It also discussed beef margin pressure and expected live-cattle inventory valuation effects. The announced full-year segment ranges are guidance, not reported annual results. This directly refutes a universal rule that lower agricultural inputs improve every food company's profitability. [S10]

### C11 — Philip Morris: category transition must reach retained economics

The July 22, 2026 issuer results presentation states that smoke-free business supplied 42% of first-half net revenue and describes approximately 8% organic quarterly top-line growth. It separately discusses shipments and different regional/category performances. Revenue mix, unit equivalents, consumption, segment profit and reinvestment are different measures. No health claim or product recommendation is inferred from the company's commercial terminology. [S11]

## 6. Accounting and measurement bridges

### 6.1 Revenue quality

The underlying identity is `revenue = sum(price_i x quantity_i)` for consistently defined products, channels and regions. This does not mean every company's reported price/mix/volume table uses a universal decomposition. For one unchanged product, `(1 + price growth) x (1 + quantity growth) - 1` includes a cross-term; a company's percentage-point bridge can instead use its own allocation and rounding convention. Preserve the reported bridge first. Derived calculations need a formula, source inputs and a visible residual where applicable.

Require the following distinctions: reported versus organic perimeter; constant versus reported currency; pure price versus combined price/mix or volume/mix; shipment versus consumption; pounds/liters/cases/transactions versus dollars; total versus same-store/same-outlet activity; period totals versus average daily rates; retail scanned channels versus all-channel estimates; actual versus guidance; calendar/holiday and extra-week effects.

Demand decompositions can use households x penetration x purchase frequency x quantity, or productive outlets x outlet velocity. These are alternative views with overlapping factors. Do not multiply both complete decompositions and double-count distribution or penetration. Household penetration and outlet distribution each need their own population denominator and observation window.

### 6.2 Inventory and consumption

A valid physical inventory identity can connect beginning inventory, receipts, shipments, losses and ending inventory only for a reconciled chain segment, unit and period. Issuer sell-in revenue and retailer sell-through value generally differ in pricing and scope. Therefore do not subtract those two dollar series and call the difference inventory. Without compatible data, label shipment/consumption divergence as an observation or management-attributed explanation, not a fabricated stock reconciliation.

Retailer inventory, manufacturer inventory and distributor inventory are distinct. A promotion can move purchasing across weeks without changing long-run demand. A product launch can initially fill shelves and later disappoint. A recall can reduce shipments, cause provisions and disrupt measured distribution on different dates. These are required research questions and future controls, not automatic accusations about the case companies.

### 6.3 Profit and cash

Separate revenue, gross profit, operating expense, operating profit, financing/tax effects, minority interests and diluted share count. A gross-margin improvement can be spent on marketing, price investment or logistics. EPS growth can reflect share-count changes rather than operating growth. Cash from an asset disposal is not customer cash generation. Free-cash-flow definitions must retain treatment of capital expenditure, leases, restructuring and other adjustments rather than assume comparability from a shared label.

Map advertising both as current expense and as an investment hypothesis; neither capitalize all advertising nor presume all cuts are harmless productivity. For retailers, membership/service revenue and merchandise margins remain distinct; without an accepted cost attribution, membership fee income is not a standalone profit pool. For ingredient and distribution businesses, test absolute profit, throughput and cash needs before interpreting revenue deflation as deterioration.

### 6.4 Costs and timing

For each relevant input, research the physical exposure, buying contract, hedge instrument, inventory method, conversion process, output-pricing rule and commercial reset. Distinguish a market quote, contracted cost, delivered cost, inventory carrying amount, recognized cost of sales, derivative mark-to-market and cash settlement. A universal three- or six-month lag is not defensible without company-specific evidence.

The proposed cost-transmission view is a set of dated links, not a weighted cost map filled with guesses. It must support unknown shares, ranges and conditional directions. Where one unit buys an input and another sells a related output, show the business units separately. Procurement region, sales region and reporting currency must remain independent.

## 7. Rerating: four separate panels, not a fused score

Propose four adjacent research panels under existing owners:

1. **Operating fundamentals:** demand, share, pricing/volume/mix, costs, margins, investment and cash conversion.
2. **Expectations:** dated management guidance, same-fiscal-period consensus where entitled, revisions, dispersion and market-implied scenario assumptions.
3. **Valuation:** the relevant earnings/cash-flow anchor, capital intensity, balance sheet, reinvestment needs and conditional discount-rate scenarios.
4. **Market recognition:** existing price leadership, breadth, participation, concentration, liquidity, crowding and timing observations.

Evidence coverage and data quality are a separate overlay, not an attractiveness score. Well-documented deterioration should have high evidence quality and poor operating momentum without contradiction. Strong economic fundamentals and weak price action can coexist; they do not automatically prove a mispricing.

### Rerating mechanisms to test in subsequent tranches

| Mechanism | Evidence sequence worth investigating | Counter-hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Sustainable category adoption | Repeat demand and penetration -> profitable expansion -> stronger forward cash expectations | Distribution fill or novelty without repeat purchase |
| Share capture | Comparable category and company volumes -> durable share gains -> incremental profit | Promotions purchase unprofitable share or panel coverage changes |
| Pricing durability | Realization holds -> elasticity normalizes -> brand support remains adequate | Volume/share deterioration requires future price reversal |
| Margin repair | Recognized input relief/productivity -> retained margin -> cash conversion | Hedging delays, reinvestment, mix or inventory offsets the benefit |
| Channel / format advantage | Retention/trips/service mix -> contribution after fulfillment -> capital returns | Convenience growth is costly or funded by unsustainable discounts |
| Structural business transition | New-format repeat revenue and contribution -> legacy decline offset -> improved duration | Cannibalization, rights restrictions or excessive acquisition cost |
| Capital allocation / portfolio change | Better business mix or returns -> attributable cash per share | Deal price, leverage or share dilution destroys the expected gain |
| Defensive / rates repricing | Market-relative leadership consistent with observed macro/valuation conditions | Business deterioration or a broad market move is misread as sector-specific demand |

These are hypotheses and comparative-research designs, not proven return predictors or calibrated classifiers. The direction of rates sensitivity is not assumed invariant. Test rates, inflation, currency and risk conditions jointly with business fundamentals, rather than call every staples rally a bond-proxy trade.

### Valuation discipline

For a positive, consistently defined earnings measure, the identity `price = earnings per share x valuation multiple` permits a clean decomposition. A synthetic example of earnings +10% and the multiple -15% produces a price change of -6.5%, before dividends. This is arithmetic, not an observed stock result. It shows why faster earnings and a higher share price are not equivalent claims.

For forward multiples, preserve both the fiscal period and forecast vintage. A rolling next-twelve-month denominator can increase merely because the horizon advanced; it is not automatically an upward earnings revision. Split-adjust price and earnings consistently. Negative or near-zero earnings require a different anchor rather than a misleading P/E change. Compare enterprise value with firm-level operating cash measures and equity value with equity cash measures; do not mix their financing bases.

A defensible scenario asks what growth duration, incremental margin, reinvestment, capital return and discount rate are needed to support the observed valuation. R1 has not assembled current market prices or licensed consensus histories and therefore supplies no current fair-value targets, upside percentages, surprise scores or rerating rankings. Missing expectations are UNKNOWN, not an LLM-imputed consensus. Buyback benefits already present in per-share earnings must not be added again as an independent price-return component.

## 8. Theme lifecycle without category errors

A single enum mixing Emerging, Pricing-Led, Defensive Bid and Margin Recovery would combine four unrelated questions. The proposal is to preserve independent dimensions:

- structural lifecycle: emerging, scaling, established, mature or declining, only where evidence supports the state;
- current operating change: demand, share, price realization, margin and investment separately;
- market phase: native owner readings for price leadership, crowding and timing;
- evidence state: current/dated, insufficient, contradictory, superseded or rights-restricted under accepted owner vocabulary.

A mature category can contain a scaling format and a margin-recovery company while its equities receive a defensive bid. This should be representable without forcing one winning label. Research labels remain descriptive until their definitions, measurement windows and evaluation are accepted. No replacement lifecycle owner is proposed.

## 10. Economic dependency view and evidence packet

The broader map should distinguish physical, commercial and analytical relationships. A proposed user view can follow `input -> processor -> brand -> channel -> consumer occasion`, while a separate explanation follows `observation -> economic mechanism -> affected financial line -> forward uncertainty -> valuation/price context`.

Use solid source-backed relationship labels only for what is actually documented; use explicitly hypothetical presentation for transmission paths. Product compatibility is not supply, supply is not material revenue, and membership overlap is not causality. No new graph edge types are admitted by this document. Missing attributable quantities produce an unweighted map, not invented Sankey widths.

### Proposed research-packet fields

Before any contract adoption, a portable research record should retain: source identifier and locator; issuer/business/source-native label; optional validated canonical identity; metric's exact reported name; value/range and unit; level/growth/contribution basis; price/volume/mix semantics; reporting/perimeter/currency basis; segment/category/channel/geography; observation period and comparison period; upstream publication and observed dates; fiscal calendar/grain; actual/guidance/derived/attributed status; source lineage and revisions; formula/inputs where derived; precision and missingness; limitations; review/admission state; and rights/retention state.

Not disclosed, unavailable, not applicable, stale, non-comparable and zero must remain distinct. Do not create a new source owner merely because the native schema does not yet accommodate a field. Financial event facts belong with their existing financial owners; the accepted curation extension, if any, should reference or complement them rather than copy them into competing truth.
