- **R76 (years; amends R72).** A bare year is `year` when it is the admitted FISCAL year, `calendar` when it is the quarter-end calendar year and not the fiscal year (a Q1/Q2 release), `prior` when it is the year before either, `foreign` otherwise. `prior` is neutral only BESIDE the admitted year ("2026 vs 2025"); alone it is foreign everywhere — heading, label, ancestor and every route (w01–w05: "Net Sales Change Drivers 2025", a "2025" label, a "2025 Results" section). `calendar` alone is ambiguous: unknown in a heading or label, the current column in a band. The drivers heading is year-level (R35) only when it names the bare admitted fiscal year, alone or beside its prior; "Net Sales Change Drivers 2026" in Q1 FY2027 admits nothing (w06), exactly as "… 2026 vs. 2025" is foreign there (c06). An untailed ordinal quarter names the admitted quarter beside the fiscal year, alone, or beside the fiscal year and its prior ("Fourth Quarter Segment Results 2026 vs. 2025" binds, f31); any other year makes it foreign. PLAN FORMS: the fiscal year is always a current form and its predecessor always a prior form; the calendar spellings are forms only when they differ; a spelling shared by both periods ("2026" in Q1 FY2027) means the observation's OWN period. BAND READING: a band is read in ONE year reading — when the admitted fiscal year appears among its bare years every bare year is fiscal and the calendar year is the PRIOR column; otherwise bare years are calendar years — and the other reading's bare-year form is dropped for that table (`_band_headers`), so "2027 | 2026" in a Q1 FY2027 release binds 3.20 and never 3.07 (w07, w08) while the frozen "2026 | 2025" band still binds.
- **R77 (hierarchy and labels; amends R73).** The TITLE (the first heading block) is exempt from the topic rule only: a forward-looking word with no scope form, or a period outside the admitted quarter, still opens a section (`<h1>Outlook</h1>` first refuses, w15; the frozen "… Results and (Fiscal Year 2027) Outlook" keeps binding through its scope form; the frozen masthead stays exempt). An ANCESTOR heading governs by POSITIVE evidence: it must be admitted by a route, be a pure label of the admitted quarter, or consist of neutral topic words from the closed set {overview, highlights, highlight, summary, key, financial, financials, business, results, result, company} with no excluded period; a forward word, a foreign or cumulative period, or any other topic — "Ambitions" (w16), "Cumulative Results" (w17), "Priorities" — opens a section no table binds from, while "Overview" (frozen s23) and "Financial Highlights" do not. A period LABEL is a paragraph carrying a strong period token whose CONTENT words — what is left after recognised forms, decoration, glue and period tokens are removed — number at most `PG_LABEL_CONTENT_WORDS` = 3, whatever its punctuation or literals: "Q3 2026.", "January to March 2026." and "Q3 2026 (in $000s)" are labels and refuse (w20), "Diluted EPS was $3.07 for the quarter." and "Net sales grew 4% in fiscal 2026 on strong volume." are prose (frozen s16, s17), and the frozen unreadable labels of r13 still refuse.
