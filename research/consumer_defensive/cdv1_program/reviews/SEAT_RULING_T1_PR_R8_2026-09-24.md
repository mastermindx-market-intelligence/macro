- **R55 (the parser owns the HTML row, the attribute and the digit).** `disclosure_diff.py` records on every cell the ordinal of the HTML row it sits in, counting rows that emit no cell (`TableCell.row_ordinal`, additive, outside `to_dict` and ids), so an empty `<tr>` covered by a rowspan from above occupies a row exactly as HTML lays it out and the cell is never carried into the next real row (p01, p06b). An attribute map keeps the FIRST occurrence of a name, as HTML does (`colspan="1" colspan="2"` is 1; p02), for hidden-markup detection as well as spans. A span attribute is 1 unless it is one to three plain ASCII digits within 1..64 — Unicode digits and underscores that Python's `int()` accepts are garbage (p03). `_grid` places rows by that ordinal; a band cell spanning several band rows counts once in every stacked form, label list and band verdict.
- **R56 (fail-closed everywhere a period can be named).** The "unknown" verdict of R50 applies to captions, label-column band cells and headings, not only to value-column stacks: a caption "Three Months Ended March 31st, 2026", a band label "Quarter ended 31/03/2026" and a heading "Segment Results for the Three Months Ended March 31st, 2026" each refuse what they govern (p12, p13, p14; the ordinal-suffix date stays unreadable on purpose — p15 pins it). A fiscal-year label naming another year makes a heading foreign even when a quarter verdict was already reached (reviewer g7). A period label keeps its meaning under decoration: a leading "For the", a trailing parenthetical such as "(Unaudited)", a trailing colon or period (p18 ×3). A reconciliation paragraph under a heading whose period cannot be read is refused like one under another quarter.
- **R57 (section labels).** A section label is a row with exactly ONE distinct text-bearing cell, wherever that cell sits and however many columns its colspan copies it into — the usual EDGAR `<td colspan="3">Twelve Months Ended …</td>` opens the section it names (p21; second-column label p22, reviewer g4).
- **R58 (spanning headers).** The column key is the set of band cells that COMPOSE the matched form — every stacked cell for the joined form, the one cell for a per-cell form — so a stack in which only the top row spans (`Three Months Ended …` over `""` / `(unaudited)`) shares its key across the spanned columns and the single-literal `$` / amount rule applies (p05). One literal whose colspan covers several value columns with different header stacks names no single period and is absent (p09, reviewer g1).
- **R59 (forward-looking headings).** A table is admissible only when EVERY governing heading — the topic and the period sub-label alike — is free of a forward-looking term (p31: `Outlook` over a period paragraph), and the term list carries its inflections: outlook(s), guidance, forecast(s/ed/ing), expect(s/ed/ing/ation(s)), project(ed/ion(s)), target(s/ed), estimat(e/es/ed/ing) (p32 ×4).
