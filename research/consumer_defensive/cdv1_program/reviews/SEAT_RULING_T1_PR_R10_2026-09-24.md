A group opens on a `thead`/`tbody`/`tfoot` start tag and closes only on ITS OWN end tag (a stray end tag is ignored, as HTML ignores it; r04, r05); rows after a closed group sit in an implied `tbody`. `_grid` lays rows out in the order a browser DRAWS them — every `thead`, then the `tbody` groups, then every `tfoot` — so a `tfoot` written before `tbody` cannot escape a later section (r06); a rowspan carries down its own group only; `rowspan="0"` runs to the group's end (r02). Span parsing skips ASCII whitespace only (r01). - **R67 (ONE period classifier, fail-closed as a rule; replaces R62's constructions).** `period_context` recognises the period FORMS — cumulative and full-year forms with their ended-date clause, "Three Months / (Fiscal) Quarter Ended|Ending <date>", Q-FY in every spelling (`Q4 FY26`, `Q4 FY'26`, `Q4 FY-26`, `Q4 FY ’26`), an ordinal quarter with or without an attached fiscal-year tail (`Fourth Quarter Fiscal-Year 2026`, `Fourth Quarter Fiscal '25`), a fiscal-year label in every spelling, the drivers heading, a bare date, a bare year — removes them, and reads the RESIDUAL against a period-token lexicon: any year, month, quarter number (`Q3 2026`, `3Q26`, `2026 Q3`) or period word (quarter(s), qtr(s), quarterly, month(s), year(s), fiscal, fy, ytd, ended, ending, period(s), annual, half, interim, trailing, prior, previous, to date) left over makes the text "unknown" (r10 ×3, "Third Qtr.", "January to March 2026", the mixed captions and headings of round 9). A bare "quarter" beside a recognised quarter form is a caption word (frozen r19c). Precedence: unknown, annual, scope, foreign; a bare fiscal-year label alone is annual; the drivers heading and a bare admitted year or quarter-end date are YEAR-LEVEL (consistent, never quarter-binding: frozen r27c/r35b), and a bare year is current when it is the fiscal year OR the calendar year of the quarter end (a Q1 FY2027 column reads "2026"). `band_context` is the same classifier for a table's own texts, where a bare current year names the current column; table-level texts (caption, label-column cells) refuse the table when foreign, while a foreign value column is the prior column beside a current one (frozen q09, r37g). A paragraph is a period LABEL when, decoration stripped, it is at most 120 characters and carries a strong period token (a year, month, quarter number, or quarter/month/fiscal/fy/ytd/ended/ending word); what it says is judged by the classifier, so an unreadable or long-prefixed label refuses instead of being ignored (r13 ×5), and prose that merely says "prior year" is not a label. Section rows are judged by `band_context` (a repeated band row mid-table names the current column).
