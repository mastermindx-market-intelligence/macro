### R4.1 — the fiscal scope comes from the admitted calendar and the actual source clock (bar c4)
One private function in the Task 2 section replaces `_PREPARED_FISCAL_SCOPE`, the Q1 fallback, the report-date equality check and every year, quarter and date literal in the preparation seam:
- **Calendar.** The fiscal calendar is the `fiscal_year_end_month` of the `IssuerIdentity` that `pg_private_registry().get(company_id_for_cik(<acquisition cik>))` returns. A missing CIK, or one the admitted registry does not resolve, is the typed refusal `unadmitted_issuer` (plan: "absent authority is a source gate, not a synthetic default"). Never default the CIK to `PG_CIK`.
- **Clock.** The source clock is the acquisition's `acceptance_datetime`, parsed strictly as UTC (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`; anything else is the typed refusal `malformed_source_clock`).
- **Candidate.** The current quarter is the admitted calendar's fiscal quarter whose end date is the latest quarter end strictly before the acceptance date. The prior interval is the same fiscal quarter one fiscal year earlier. The result is Task 1's four-date shape, and it must pass main's `_scope`.
- **Labels.** The fiscal year and quarter come from main's `_fiscal_identity(current_start, current_end)`. They feed `FiscalPeriod(year=<fiscal year>, quarter=<quarter>, calendar_end=<current end>)`, the identity passed to `document_period_verdict`, and `presented_fiscal_label`. Never derive a fiscal year from a calendar year.
- **Document validation.** `document_period_verdict(parse_release_blocks(body), identity)` then decides:
  - `"scope"`, or `None` (no period signal; Task 1 R35 binds such a document by the admitted scope), admits the candidate;
  - `"annual"` or `"foreign"` is the typed refusal `document_period_not_admitted`.

  Do not search other quarters.
- **`asof`** passed to `build_event_workspace` is the acceptance date.
- **Report date.** The report date is never an input to the scope. It stays trace data.
- **Tests.** Prepare the same synthetic acquisition with four acceptance datetimes, 2026-07-29, 2026-10-24, 2027-01-23 and 2027-04-22 (all at 17:00:00Z). Assert the four-date scope, `workspace["fiscal_period"]` and `document_metadata["presented_fiscal_label"]` as literals written in the test from the calendar:
  - Q4 FY2026: `("2026-04-01", "2026-06-30", "2025-04-01", "2025-06-30")`;
  - Q1 FY2027: `("2026-07-01", "2026-09-30", "2025-07-01", "2025-09-30")`;
