## SEAT RULING R4 — the Task 7 display contract, exact (binding)

### R4.1 Input: `selection` becomes a closed mapping
`build_economic_interpretation(..., selection=...)` takes `selection` as a mapping with EXACTLY two keys:
- `facts`: `None` (all native rows) or a list/tuple of native handles, validated exactly as today. The existing 24-item cap applies to `facts`.
- `currentness`: `None`, or a mapping with EXACTLY two keys, `state` and `source_clock`:
  - `state` is one of `up_to_date`, `newer_source_pending`, `currentness_unverified`;
  - `source_clock` is a UTC timestamp string of the form `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`. It is REQUIRED for `up_to_date` and `newer_source_pending`, and must be `None` for `currentness_unverified`.

`currentness=None` means `{"state": "currentness_unverified", "source_clock": None}`.

Each of the following raises `EconomicInterpretationError`, never `KeyError`, `TypeError` or a silent default:
- any other `selection` form: a bare list, `None` itself, or extra or missing keys;
- any other `state`;
- a missing or malformed clock where one is required;
- a clock given for `currentness_unverified`.

Task 3 never imports Task 2. Task 4 builds this mapping from Task 2's currentness context.

## SEAT RULINGS R5–R13 — the review findings (binding)

### R5 (B2) — uncertainty is provided, never inferred from precision
- The signature becomes `compare_eps(current, prior, *, precision, uncertainty=None)`. The plan's call `compare_eps(Decimal('1.20'), Decimal('0'), precision=None)` stays valid, and the plan test stays verbatim.
- `precision` (`None` or a non-negative int) only quantizes the result, as today. It never refuses.
- `uncertainty` is `None` or a provided, finite, non-negative half-width in the prior's own unit. A `bool`, negative, NaN, infinite or non-numeric value raises `EconomicInterpretationError`.
- The order of checks is:
  1. `prior <= 0` gives `nonpositive_prior` (unchanged);
  2. otherwise, `uncertainty` provided and `prior - uncertainty <= 0` gives `not_comparable`, `value` `None`, reason `uncertainty_interval_touches_zero`;
  3. otherwise the pair is comparable.
- No other threshold exists. A small positive prior with no provided interval is comparable.
- `_comparison` passes no uncertainty, because Task 1 rows carry none.
- Remove the `uncertain_prior_eps` body case from the fixtures: a $2.00 prior carries no uncertainty. The plan's "uncertain prior EPS" is proven at `compare_eps` level (test 8).
