## 1. Product destination

Mastermind should explain how changing consumption becomes attributable earnings, cash flow, expectations and equity-market recognition. Consumer Defensive is not a static list of staples companies and must not inherit a universal Robotics BOM score.

The primary user is an investor who wants to discover a material change, identify the businesses genuinely exposed, understand what the market may already recognize, inspect the evidence and return to existing company, watchlist and opportunity workflows. The machine job is to preserve source meaning, economic scope, time and learning across those steps so each new release adds understanding rather than another disconnected summary.

The target journey is: existing Theme Tracker or sector entry -> economically meaningful research slice -> business/company exposure -> dated operating and earnings explanation -> expectations/valuation/market context -> exact source and competing explanation -> existing research/watchlist workflow. A group label never grants an individual stock eligibility. Prophet and the existing entry/portfolio owners keep their own authority.

The moat is the combination of granular business understanding, longitudinal source-bound economic interpretation, explicit alternative explanations and evaluated read-through across related businesses. A source archive alone is not the moat. A large model producing confident narrative without compatible inputs is not intelligence either.

This masterplan preserves the full global destination but selects one small complete release. It is a delivery roadmap and design synthesis, not the subsequent task-by-task implementation plan. The latter follows written-design review and current source-owner admission.

## 3. Sector model: facets over existing identities

Retain the existing sector/subsector/canonical-theme/source-local-subtheme/basket/company/security distinctions. Add reviewed economic research facets through the existing semantic owner; do not create another global taxonomy or treat each facet combination as a tradable basket.

A usable exposure describes business, product or brand, commercial right, territory, channel, period and accounting scope. Ownership, license, manufacturing, supply, distribution and retail relationships mean different things. A familiar brand does not establish worldwide rights or material revenue. A listing country does not identify customer geography. Undisclosed brand sales remain unknown.

Proposed research facets answer different questions:

| Facet | Meaning | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Category | What is consumed? | Calling premiumization a physical category |
| Demand change | What consumer behavior may be changing? | Calling social attention unit demand |
| Commercial role | Who earns which type of revenue? | Equating brand owner, processor and retailer |
| Economic archetype | Which profit/capital bridge applies? | Applying one multiple to every business |
| Channel/geography | Where and through whom are economics realized? | Using listing currency as operating exposure |
| Structural maturity | How established is the category or format? | Inferring maturity solely from share-price momentum |
| Current operating state | What is changing now in demand, pricing, margin or cash? | Mixing margin repair and category adoption |
| Market state | What do existing market owners observe? | Turning theme participation into stock buyability |
| Evidence state | What supports or limits the interpretation? | Treating more citations as a more attractive stock |

Facets are filters and explanations. Newly proposed canonical identities, relationships or memberships require the incumbent GMI owner. Source-local terms remain local until an accepted mapping exists. Region and format expansion must not clone issuer identities.

## 4. Family-specific economic research map

The following is the synthesized research agenda, not a set of fitted causal coefficients or investment recommendations. Each family uses the common four-panel product grammar but retains its economic model.

| Family | Operating mechanism to track | Earnings/valuation question | Discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonalcoholic beverages | Consumption occasions, package/channel mix, distribution and within-outlet demand | Is brand/system growth retained by the issuer after partner economics and support costs? | Separate concentrate, bottler, licensed and consumer measures; repeated outlet demand |
| Snacks/confectionery | Realized price, elasticity, input recognition, portions and promotions | Does margin recovery survive volume/share effects and required brand investment? | Price versus combined volume/mix; hedges/MTM versus recognized costs |
| Packaged meals/pantry | At-home occasions, convenience, private label, shelf productivity | Is growth share capture, price realization or channel inventory? | Retail takeaway versus sell-in, portfolio changes and contribution |
| Dairy/nutrition | Commodity exposure, branded differentiation, cold chain and product mix | Does higher-value formulation improve retained returns? | Volume/price bridge and business-level capital needs |

## 5. Four analytical panels and a separate evidence overlay

### Operating fundamentals

Show the source-defined price/volume/mix bridge, competitive/distribution evidence, retained margin, investment and cash. Different issuer definitions are allowed; false comparability is not. Observation, management attribution, deterministic arithmetic and house hypothesis must remain distinguishable. Company shipments are not household consumption.

### Expectations

Preserve exact management guidance vintages, target fiscal periods and metric bases. An actual above a previously issued management range can coexist with year-over-year deterioration. It is not automatically a consensus beat. Licensed consensus, estimate dispersion and historical snapshots remain optional unavailable inputs until an existing entitled owner actually supplies them. Rolling a forecast horizon is not the same as revising a fixed-period estimate.

### Valuation

Use a suitable native earnings/cash anchor, attributable shareholder claims, reinvestment and capital requirements. Distinguish price change from dividend-reinvested return and reported EPS from adjusted EPS. An ex-post annual multiple based on results released later is not an observable historical forward multiple. Do not capitalize temporary procurement gains, tax effects or working-capital releases as permanent cash. No current fair values or target prices are authorized by the initial release.

### Market recognition

Consume, do not recompute, the accepted leadership, participation, concentration, entry and crowding outputs. Strong theme context does not select every member. Good business news at demanding expectations may have poor equity outcomes. Economic strength and market weakness do not automatically establish mispricing. Missing identity/period joins remain visible.

### Evidence overlay

Track source scope, lineage, period, publication/observation, corrections, retention and rights. Evidence quality is not attractiveness. Two articles repeating one release do not provide two independent legs. Uncertainty should narrow an interpretation, not force the product to become only an absence catalog.
