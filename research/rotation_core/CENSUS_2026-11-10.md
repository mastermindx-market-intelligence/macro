## 1. The ten findings that matter most

1. **Nothing the organisation computes has yet shown rotation or risk-timing skill out of sample, after costs, against an
   exposure-matched baseline.** Every honest test so far lands in one of two places. Some are drawdown control that
   costs return. Others are statistically indistinguishable from the passive alternative. No prior study claims more
   than that and survives its own controls.
2. **The sister team's preregistered sector comparator, 2008-01 → 2026-09 (236 twenty-session periods, 5 bp/side, next-close fills):**
   - Cycle-state sector allocation (C3) returned **−1.82 %/yr** against SPY [90 % CI −3.54, +0.05].
   - Against an exposure-matched reference it returned −0.53 % [−1.73, +0.59].
   - SPY-above-200-day (C2) returned −2.73 %/yr against SPY [−6.87, +2.35], with max drawdown −17.6 % against −44.6 %.
   - Emergence recall was 76.1 % against a 78.3 % base rate.
   - Source: `research/sector_completion_20261021/B12_V11_HEADS_RESULTS.md:55-67` at PR #10924 head `f1337bdd87`; verified by the lead. It is unmerged.
3. **Sector-ETF momentum over 27.9 years** (`data/strategies/thematic_rotation_phase0.json`, 11 SPDRs 1998-12 → 2026-10, monthly, 10 bp; verified):
   - Equal-weight sectors: CAGR 8.86 %, Sharpe 0.66, max drawdown −49.1 %.
   - Top-3 12-1 relative momentum: 9.00 % / 0.65 / −43.3 %.
   - Top-3 6-month momentum: 8.23 % / 0.62 / −39.7 %.
   - Top-4 12-1 plus 10-month-MA gate (dual momentum): 7.26 % / 0.66 / −24.1 %.
   - 1-month rank-IC 0.0094 (t_HAC 0.44).
   - Above-trend versus below-trend forward 1-month return: identical means (p 0.99).

   The artifact has no intervals, no exposure matching and no SPY column, and it is regenerated nightly.
   W3 transposed the two CAGRs in its report; the figures above are corrected.

   *Reading:* relative sector momentum adds nothing on this universe. The trend gate halves drawdown and costs about
   1.6 pp/yr of CAGR.
4. **The best-documented market de-risk object is the S&P/Macro vector (spvector, `engine/equity_alloc.py`).** It is a
   weighted 0–100 macro de-risk score (drawdown gauge, recession, NFCI, HY-OAS widening, net liquidity) with publication
   lags and a 4-band glide.
   - Over 33.4 years of SPY: Sharpe 0.91 against 0.65 buy-and-hold and 0.77 for the 200-day rule; max drawdown −34.1 % against −55.2 %.
   - Initial-release-vintage recheck: Sharpe 0.90, max drawdown −32.5 %.
   - Its own adversarial verdict: *"a DRAWDOWN / SHARPE engine, NOT a CAGR-beater. The headline CAGR-beat is entirely the T-bill carry"* (`reports/spvector-phase1.md:5`, `reports/spvector-pit.md:8-17`; verified).
   - The weights were declared before the run, but the band and confirm-day settings were selected on the same 1993–2026 span (DSR at n_trials = 12). **It has no clean historical window.**
5. **Risk Radar v2 has no out-of-sample historical evidence.**
   - **Reconstructed history:** everything before 2026-06-23 is replayed with parameters chosen in 2026 on the same 2006–2026 window, and the "2020+ hold-out" was inspected in at least four studies. The one preregistered walk-forward returned `not_promotion_eligible` (Brier worse at every horizon; `research/grey_deer/RISK_RADAR_PROBABILITY_WALKFORWARD_RESULTS_2026-09-23.md`).
   - **Live forward ledger** (78 rows since 2026-06-23): **zero gated alerts**. The ungated counterfactual was 2 true / 9 false positives.
   - **Recall trade-off:** the context gate raises H21 precision from 0.085 to 0.249 but cuts recall to 28–37 %.
   - **Leg stability:** the predecessor composite flipped from 2.93× lift to 0.68× in 2020+. Several legs flip sign across eras. Only HY-OAS 21-day widening is era-robust (1.93× / 1.94×).
6. **Classic cross-sectional momentum collapses once the stock panel is de-biased point-in-time.** Plain IC goes 0.0036 → 0.0008 (t 0.08); the residual long/short Sharpe is −0.29; the best BH q is about 0.40 (`research/RESIDUAL_ALPHA_MOMENTUM.md`, per W6/W7). Sector-neutral residual momentum keeps some IC (0.012 vs 0.0008) but is FDR-null in the modern era, and its de-contaminated long/short is negative.

   Two further facts from the same study (`research/RESIDUAL_ALPHA_MOMENTUM.md:1-15,105-115`):
   - Stock-level "velocity/acceleration" of momentum is **anti-predictive** (t −2.7), and 1-month horizons are reversal (sector-neutral t −9.5). That is a skeptical prior for RS-velocity rules, though it was measured on stocks, not sectors.
   - Its PIT S&P 500 test recovered delisted prices from Yahoo for only 199 of 567 missing names (`scripts/residual_alpha_pit.py`, 69 % coverage). The missing 31 % are the catastrophic losers.

   **No study has shown that single-name RS selection works better inside leading sectors** (J4's hypothesis).
7. **Regime / cycle / rotation-state machinery is null or falsified on its own preregistered controls.**
   - Cycle-map turn precision 0.075 [0.054, 0.103]; forecast cones 8× too tight.
   - Cycle position has no forward-return edge (8,344 PIT stamps).
   - `rotation_state_daily` autocorrelation is −0.04 against a > 0.5 gate.
   - Family × regime reliability: the interaction is 3.8× smaller than the main effect, with era sign-stability at 62 %.
   - Country-cycle turn precision 0.109, skill −1.198.
   - Defensive-rotation-leads-vol-shock was falsified: CI ∋ 0, false-alarm rate 0.80.
   - Rotation events: n = 13, null-to-negative.
   - Subsector rotation IC is negative (−0.12 / −0.14).
8. **Cross-asset and international trend overlays are confirmers, not alpha.**
   - Repo cross-asset TSMOM (2007 →, 8 bp): net Sharpe **0.54 against 0.56** buy-and-hold, DSR 0.795 fail (`reports/cross-asset-phase0.md:9-18`).
   - International 200-day overlay: max drawdown −18.5 % against −57.0 %, but the split-half Sharpe edge fails.
   - Country-ETF trend: DSR 0.85 fail.
   - Pooled international macro sleeve: re-graded against its declared target, DSR 0.83, verdict CONTEXT.
   - **No GTAA, dual-momentum or risk-parity engine exists anywhere in the estate.**
9. **Point-in-time inputs are the binding constraint, and they differ by job.**
   - Sector-ETF, cross-asset ETF and index prices are PIT by nature, available decades back.
   - Macro: true ALFRED vintages exist in the repo only from 1997. The public ALFRED endpoint allows further vintage pulls.
   - S&P 1500 membership is PIT only per leg: S&P 500 from 1996-01-02, S&P 400 from 2012-01-13, S&P 600 from 2019-12-17. Each starts with a left-censored block.
   - Sector labels are as-of-now (`era_correct_count: 0`), and **most early leavers have neither a label nor a cached price** (§9.4).
   - Theme baskets are PIT only from 2026-08-13; the Finviz subsector snapshots from 2026-06-26.
   - W6 attributed a "vector allocation family vanishes under SPA p_c = 0.283" finding to spvector. It is about the **BTC** `vector` family (`research/quant_assessment_2026_10/Q20_dependence_aware_model_comparison/VERDICT.md:8-10,111-131`), not the equity spvector.
10. **Binding house law already fixes much of the method:**
    - **Trial budget:** declare it in `data/trial_ledger.jsonl` before screening (`epistemics.trial_registration`).
    - **Confidence intervals:** time-block CIs only (`DNR:LAW-TIME-CLUSTERED-CI`).
    - **Era split:** no era-pooled inference across 2010 without a split (`DNR:LAW-ERA-SPLIT`).
    - **Retrospective limit:** retrospective results can falsify or qualify but never promote (`DEC:RIC-E0-RETROSPECTIVE-FALSIFIES-PROSPECTIVE-PROMOTES`).
    - **Composites:** no fused composite in any scored path (`DNR:KILL-FUSED-COMPOSITE`) and no composite regime scorecard with tactical ETF allocation (`DNR:KILL-REGIME-SCORECARD`). Both bind production; research tests of transparent rules are lawful but grant no authority.
    - **Kill list:** rotation × cycle-position confluence is DON'T-TEST; RS-dispersion gates and absolute-VIX thresholds are killed.
    - **Burned formation dates:** 2022-07-06 → 2026-06-02 have been scored three times for sector/group → member persistence, and *"no further claim may rest on them"* (`agentos/workstreams/WS-TREND-PERSISTENCE.md:48`).

**What has been honestly tested and what it showed:** sector-ETF relative momentum (null), the trend gate (drawdown
control, CAGR cost), cycle-state sector allocation (null to negative), the cycle and turn ontology (falsified),
cross-asset TSMOM (≈ buy-and-hold), the international trend overlay (confirmer), defensive rotation as a lead (falsified),
cross-sectional stock momentum on a PIT panel (null), and family × regime conditioning (null). **What has not been honestly
tested:**
- a multi-factor risk-on/off pivot rule (trend × credit × macro) on a clean window against exposure-matched references;
- RS-velocity sector rotation;
- conditional sector rotation (sector choice given risk state);
- GTAA or dual momentum on this estate;
- the amplification hypothesis (J4);
- any combined stack.

## 6. Macro / liquidity / credit / policy state (J1 inputs)

- **Credit:** HY OAS is the repo's only era-robust risk leg. The repo store `data/fred/BAMLH0A0HYM2.parquet` runs 1996-12-31 → 2026-11-05 (7,795 rows; lead-verified). Public FRED now serves this series only from 2023-11-13. Moody's Baa yield: repo `DBAA` from 1986-01-02, FRED `BAA10Y` from 1986.
- **Real-time recession:** FRED `SAHMREALTIME` (real-time Sahm rule, 1959-12+, monthly) is constructed from real-time data, so it is PIT by construction.
- **Revision-prone macro:** PAYEMS, INDPRO, CPI and similar series have ALFRED vintages in the repo from 1997 (`data/fred_vintage/`). The public ALFRED CSV endpoint allows single-vintage pulls without a key (verified 2026-11-10).
- **No vintages:** NFCI, ANFCI and EBP have no ALFRED vintages; they need a publication-lag rule.
- **Null or retired:** global liquidity (GLT), fed_path / fed_stance, credit_cycle, flow_regime, narrative regime (falsified), sector rate-inflation. None is shortlisted.
