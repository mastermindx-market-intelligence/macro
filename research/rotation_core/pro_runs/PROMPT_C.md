THE CHAIRMAN'S REFRAMING (v2 must answer this)
- "Beating the S&P 500" means beating 500 companies weighted heavily to a handful of technology mega-caps. That is close to asking us to beat the Nasdaq. The equal-weight index (RSP) must sit beside SPY. Cash is a legitimate position.
- The rotation that matters is getting out of high-beta technology when conditions stop favouring it and into the defensives that lead then, and back again. In 2026: semiconductors peaked mid-year, NVDA fell about 30% from 6 October, data-centre names more; health care made all-time highs and staples rebounded hard; energy and defence led earlier on a supply shock and war.
- Often only some themes inside a sector carry it. Breadth diverging inside a sector or theme, key names rolling over, and competing sectors topping are candidate clues for washout bottoms and for tops.
- Rates, real rates, credit spreads and the competition for capital between AI investment, sovereign bonds and corporate bonds condition the seesaw.
- Single names and thematic expressions come after the beta is right.
