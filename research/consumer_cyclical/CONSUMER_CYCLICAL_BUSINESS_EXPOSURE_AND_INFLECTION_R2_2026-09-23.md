## 2. A business-exposure map, not a list of thematic tickers

**Proposed representation.** A research record should bind an issuer to a source-reported business, a source period, the economic right, an operating population and a measurement. A separate reviewed view can associate that record with research questions such as affordability, renovation, performance footwear or value retail. Association alone must not become basket membership or a causal relationship.

Four exposure questions must remain separate:

- **Revenue:** what proportion of the explicitly stated revenue denominator is represented?
- **Profit:** what contribution belongs to the parent after relevant costs, eliminations and minority interests?
- **Capital:** what assets, working capital, obligations and reinvestment support that contribution?
- **Optionality:** what future opportunity is plausible but not yet represented by measured economics?

An undisclosed answer is unknown, not zero. A revenue percentage cannot substitute for a profit percentage. A consolidated business is not fully attributable to the parent merely because all its revenue is consolidated. Conversely, a franchisee's system sales are not the franchisor's revenue.

The companion JSON contains source-local research records, not newly enrolled company, product or security identities. Company names identify the research cases; live listings and corporate-action joins must still use the incumbent identity owner.

## 3. Housing: affordability transmission and capital turns

### D.R. Horton — four different economic roles

The annual filing distinguishes homebuilding, rental, Forestar and financial services. At 2025-09-30, the company owned 62% of Forestar, consolidated 100%, and treated the remaining 38% as noncontrolling interests; intercompany transactions were eliminated. That is a dated observation, not a refreshed September 2026 ownership assertion. [R2-S01]

The July 21 release reports a June-quarter cancellation rate of 20% versus 17% and defines the ratio using gross orders. Its trailing homebuilding return-on-inventory measure uses pretax income and a five-quarter average inventory denominator. [R2-S02]

**Proposed demand mechanism.** Model the decision to buy in terms of the financed payment and required cash, not only the house's quoted price. Relevant variables include down payment, term, mortgage pricing, closing assistance, insurance/taxes and qualification. A builder can support conversion by absorbing financing costs. That can improve closings while leaving incremental cash capture weak.

A useful research sequence is: lead or order evidence -> cancellations -> backlog quality -> delivery -> realized margin -> inventory release -> parent-attributable return. Each transition can fail. A gross order announcement is not a closing, and fewer cancellations in one quarter need not establish a sustained recovery.

**Proposed re-rating mechanism.** Investigate whether financing conditions permit the builder to reduce concessions while retaining conversion and acceptable inventory velocity. This is different from forecasting a fixed positive stock response to lower interest rates. Demand, competitive supply and financing costs can change together; a rate decline associated with weaker employment could have a different net effect from a decline with stable household purchasing capacity.

**Capital and moat questions.** Test whether lot access, construction execution, local scale or financing distribution improve returns after the full capital commitment. Contract-controlled lots may reduce some holding exposure while retaining deposits or other obligations. A related developer's assets and transactions cannot be treated as wholly outside the consolidated group. Also assess the flexibility to slow starts without incurring disproportionate penalties, the mix of finished unsold homes and the cost of maintaining ready inventory.

**Disconfirming evidence:** more cancellations; weaker conversion despite unchanged concessions; aging completed homes; rising land losses; deteriorating parent-attributable return despite volume growth. An attractive homebuilding thesis requires more than a large lot count.

### Illustrative affordability calculation — not a market quote

For a hypothetical $320,000 fully amortizing, 30-year loan, monthly principal and interest is `L*r/(1-(1+r)^(-n))`, with `r` the monthly rate and `n=360`. Changing the assumed annual rate from 7% to 6% reduces that payment from approximately $2,129 to $1,919. Taxes, insurance, fees, qualification and subsidy cost are excluded. Neither rate is asserted to be a current offered mortgage rate.

The proposed product should show separately the consumer payment benefit and the issuer's cost of providing it. A subsidized payment is not free economic value for the builder.

## 4. Home improvement: category recovery versus distribution expansion

### Home Depot — preserve reporting scope and purchase purpose

Home Depot reports Primary separately from Other, which contains SRS operating segments including GMS. GMS was acquired on 2025-09-04. Its customer-transaction and average-ticket statistics exclude HD Supply and SRS including GMS. Average ticket means price paid per transaction. Comparable sales use locations/websites open more than 52 weeks, with specific acquisition and closed-store treatment. [R2-S03]

**Proposed economic lenses.** Separate necessary repair, discretionary remodeling, turnover-associated work and professional distribution. These are research views; the source read does not provide a complete numerical partition into those four purposes. A professional purchase can be necessary maintenance or a discretionary large project. A customer label does not resolve the economic driver.

A housing-turnover hypothesis should be tested against transaction-sensitive categories, not imposed on every item the company sells. A repair hypothesis needs evidence that the installed base is generating work irrespective of moves. A major-project hypothesis needs project size, financing and conversion evidence. Price inflation and product mix can raise transaction value without proving either mechanism.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** A stronger case would combine renewed project activity with improvement in contribution after delivery, labor, inventory and acquisition-related capital requirements. Buying a distributor can expand reach but is not evidence that an existing store cohort has recovered. Synergies should be tied to observed cross-selling, route economics or retained contribution, rather than the deal's announced addressable market.

**Moat research:** availability at the required time, job-site delivery reliability, breadth of trade relationships, inventory allocation and contractor workflow. These are hypotheses about competitive advantage, not accepted measurements of switching costs or pricing power.

**Disconfirming evidence:** comparable transactions remain weak; larger tickets arise primarily from mix; acquired revenue masks an unchanged core trend; gross-margin improvement is offset by delivery or financing costs. The appropriate output may be “expanded business footprint, core activity unresolved,” not one sector-wide bullish or bearish label.

## 5. Athletic brands: rebuilding demand without confusing channels

### Nike — observe the channel, then ask who retained the economics

The geographic/channel cells above establish real revenue scope. Nike's store-comparable measure excludes digital and applies store-age and footprint restrictions. Wholesale revenue is not by itself an observation of the retailer's consumer sales. [R2-S04]

**Proposed mechanism.** Separate product desirability from channel allocation. A shift back toward wholesale can improve distribution while reducing the proportion of direct revenue; the direction of the mix alone does not establish worsening economics. Conversely, direct-channel growth need not improve fully loaded contribution when fulfillment, returns, stores, promotions or customer acquisition absorb the margin.

For each product/category hypothesis, investigate full-price sell-through, replenishment quality, inventory age, repeat purchasing and promotional breadth. A shipment rebound after depleted retailer stocks could represent a one-time replenishment event. A sustained product cycle should have evidence that consumers absorb subsequent replenishment without escalating discounting.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** A plausible rehabilitation case combines stronger product demand, fewer clearance pressures, productive distribution and sustainable contribution after ongoing brand investment. Cost cuts that temporarily increase profit while reducing product development or customer relevance may not support the same valuation duration.

**Exposure research advantage.** The disclosed regional/channel joints let the analyst ask narrower questions: is a wholesale change concentrated in one geography, and does it coexist with improving direct demand there? This is more discriminating than treating all footwear, all China exposure or all digital commerce as one factor. Exact product-by-channel customer cohorts still need their own disclosed joint or lawful representative data.

**Disconfirming evidence:** retailer inventory accumulation; a short shipment rebound without repeat demand; persistent markdown dependence; returns or marketing costs offset the gross benefit; growth concentrated in a temporary distribution fill. These are tests to investigate, not a conclusion that they currently describe Nike.

### lululemon — growth geography and mature demand are different dimensions

The regional revenue partition above is reported. Its comparable-sales definition includes all e-commerce and eligible stores after at least 12 full fiscal months, with particular relocation and channel exclusions. This differs from Nike's store-only comparison. [R2-S05]

**Proposed mechanism.** Decompose expansion into mature demand, incremental locations, geographic development and new categories. Research local brand adoption through retained customers and sustained unit contribution, not just opening counts. A new market can have a strong initial launch but weak subsequent productivity; a mature market can temporarily suffer from product or distribution choices while another expands.

For new locations, require opening investment, ramp period, mature sales/contribution, cannibalization and maintenance needs. When those disclosures are unavailable, the appropriate conclusion is that reported expansion is visible but its incremental return is unmeasured.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** Investigate whether mature demand stabilizes without heavy discounting while newer markets add profitable volume. An apparent revenue runway should be assessed against product reinvestment, competitive access and the capital needed to establish distribution. This is not an inference of current market share or intrinsic value.

**Disconfirming evidence:** weaker comparable demand under rising location count; diminishing sales productivity; higher acquisition costs; profitability relying on unusual items. The dated guidance study below provides a concrete way to keep operating delivery and expectations separate.

## 6. Off-price retail: inventory opportunity is not automatically consumer weakness

### TJX — distinguish purchasing optionality from customer demand

The annual filing describes opportunistic purchasing and packaway practices. It defines average ticket as retail price per unit and average basket as value per transaction. Fiscal 2026 comparable sales include e-commerce and use an eligibility convention based on the third fiscal year; that definition must travel with the observation. [R2-S09]

The August 19 quarter release reports pretax margin of 13.3% versus 11.4%, and company-adjusted margin of 11.9% versus 11.4% after specified refund-related effects. Its adjustment also recognizes associated compensation expense. [R2-S10]

**Proposed mechanism.** Study two sides independently: the supply of attractive merchandise and the customer's reason to visit. Distressed inventory at other merchants might create purchasing opportunities, but only successful selling and inventory rotation can convert that option into profitable sales. Lower acquisition cost is not automatically higher retained margin if markdowns, freight, shrink or labor consume the difference.

A trade-down hypothesis needs evidence of customer substitution, not only a value-oriented brand description. A separate value-seeking or assortment-discovery hypothesis may be plausible even without broad consumer distress. These mechanisms should be allowed to coexist or remain unresolved.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** A stronger case combines recurring demand, productive buying and distribution, and attractive new-location economics. It is not enough that reported margins rise during a one-time recovery. The inspected quarter supplies an important positive control: excluding the company's specified adjustment still leaves a margin improvement. An honest model must be able to retain supported improvement, not mechanically reject every business with an unusual item.

**Disconfirming evidence:** inventory aging without adequate future-season rationale; weakening transactions under higher merchandise prices; purchasing advantages that disappear after freight or markdowns; expansion at falling store-level returns. Packaway should be assessed against its intended selling window, not automatically classified as obsolete inventory.

## 7. Restaurants: traffic recovery, operating leverage and who bears the costs

### Chipotle — a demand recovery can precede a profit recovery

For 2025, the annual filing reports comparable sales down 1.7%, transactions down 2.9%, and check up 1.2%. It separates 4,042 company-operated restaurants from 14 partner-operated locations at year-end. [R2-S11]

For Q2 2026, the July 29 release reports comps up 2.2%, transactions up 1.0%, and check up 1.2%, while restaurant-level margin fell to 25.2% from 27.4%. Its comparable cohort requires 13 full calendar months; average restaurant sales uses a trailing-year measure with a different eligibility rule. Restaurant-level margin excludes items that still matter to shareholder economics. [R2-S12]

**Proposed mechanism.** Decompose the operating system into transactions, price/mix, capacity to serve peak demand, labor scheduling, product costs, occupancy and new-unit maturation. Faster throughput can matter if there is unsatisfied demand; adding capacity without demand may simply increase costs. Digital mix needs incremental contribution evidence because channel substitution, fees and fulfillment can alter the economics.

The cited observations support a narrow historical statement: transaction growth in the selected quarter has a different sign from the prior full-year comparison. They do not demonstrate an enduring recovery, explain its cause or establish a stock opportunity. The weaker margin is a separate dimension, not a reason to erase the demand observation.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** Investigate whether positive transaction activity persists and translates into margin or cash contribution while new restaurants achieve attractive returns. Evaluate the capital required to replicate the format, not just the store-count target. Price-led growth and transaction-led growth can produce different cost behavior.

**Disconfirming evidence:** short promotional response; added labor consuming incremental sales; cannibalization; weak new-location maturation; rising capital per opening; digital orders replacing rather than adding demand. A small sample of disclosed quarters is insufficient to estimate these elasticities.

### McDonald's — system activity, fees and restaurant-owner economics

The annual filing reports approximately 95% of restaurants franchised at year-end. Conventional franchise arrangements include rent and royalties; developmental license and affiliate structures differ. Comparable sales cover eligible company and franchise restaurants, including temporarily closed locations, with currency and hyperinflation exclusions. [R2-S13]

**Proposed mechanism.** Trace system activity through the specific contract. Royalty revenue, rent, equity-method income and owned-restaurant contribution are different rights and obligations. A restaurant-count proportion must not become the same percentage of consolidated revenue, operating profit or cash.

A franchisor can have attractive incremental fee economics, but the operator must still fund wages, occupancy, inventory and investment. Persistent operator weakness could impair expansion, maintenance or the sustainability of the fee stream even before it is obvious in the parent's reported margin. Do not infer operator health solely from parent profitability.

**Possible re-rating mechanism.** Investigate sustainable system growth, healthy restaurant-owner economics and profitable reinvestment with appropriate parent capital requirements. Loyalty participation or a new store target would be supporting evidence only after retention, incrementality and return questions are addressed. No exact restaurant-specific contract rates or operator profit census were acquired here.

**Disconfirming evidence:** franchise closures, strained operator cash, refurbishment obligations, fee relief, concentration in unusually strong markets, or owned-property costs eroding apparent asset-light economics. Compare these with Chipotle through economic roles, not by directly ranking differently defined restaurant margins.

### A controlled operating-leverage illustration

Assume hypothetical sales of 100, variable costs of 65 and fixed costs of 25: operating contribution is 10. A 5% volume increase at unchanged price and proportional variable cost produces sales 105, variable costs 68.25 and contribution 11.75. A 5% price increase at unchanged volume and unchanged costs produces contribution 15. These are deliberately restrictive scenarios, not observed restaurant elasticities. Wage response, menu mix, promotional behavior, demand elasticity and step-costs can invalidate them.

The useful product feature is an explicit scenario bridge with assumptions, not a universal coefficient applied to every company or a hidden promise that price increases drop through at 100%.

Do not estimate an earnings-surprise return, investor positioning, market incorporation or attractive entry from these releases alone. A later study needs a predeclared price window, a licensed or otherwise lawful dated expectation comparator, corporate-action treatment and an appropriate sector/peer comparison. It must include failed recoveries and avoid selecting cases only after their stock performance is known.

## 10. From an economic hypothesis to a testable re-rating study

**Proposed research design.** Do not start by training one sector score. Define a separate hypothesis and observable chain for each mechanism, then assess whether its information adds value beyond common exposures.

For housing, the discriminating question is whether effective affordability improves conversion and retained return, not simply whether yields decline. For brands, it is whether stronger product demand survives replenishment and lower clearance dependence. For off-price, it is whether buying opportunities and customer activity combine into retained contribution. For restaurants, it is whether transaction improvement converts into durable cash economics at both mature and incremental locations.

For each mechanism, predeclare the event definition, expected intermediate outcome, observation horizon, competing explanation and minimum evidence needed to assess it. Also define which observation would invalidate the thesis before looking at the investment result. Keep an explicit “unresolved” state when an essential denominator or comparison is unavailable.

### Valuation hypotheses, not preset multiple targets

A higher multiple could be consistent with greater confidence in recurring earnings, a longer productive reinvestment runway or reduced financing risk. It could also merely reflect different rates or a broad market move. These possibilities require measurement, not narrative certainty.

Use business-appropriate value bridges: normalized earnings and capital turns for builders; contribution and reinvestment for merchants and brands; operator and franchisor obligations for restaurants; consolidated ownership and debt for parent equity. Where accounting earnings are negative or heavily adjusted, do not force a conventional P/E comparison. Show the cash-flow and financing assumptions instead.

The research should eventually compare operating hypotheses with multiple outcomes: demand, margins, cash conversion, future guidance, revisions and security returns. A feature that predicts next-quarter volume but not retained profit may still help explain the business without deserving ranking authority. A feature associated with returns in one selected episode is not necessarily predictive in another regime.

### Dependence and failure controls

Avoid treating a filing, press release and transcript repeating the same result as independent confirmations. Distinguish manufacturer shipments from downstream purchases and parent consolidated disclosures from related subsidiary disclosures. Where multiple companies share one macro driver, measure the common component rather than claim several independent signals.

Historical cohorts should retain exited, acquired and failed businesses when relevant; account for restated definitions, unknown original publication times and delayed data. A retrospective explanation is not proof that Mastermind could have observed the evidence early enough to act. The prospective cases accompanying R2 specify these requirements but have not been executed against an application or return dataset.

## 11. Product direction and a discriminating first research vertical

**Proposal, not an approved specification.** The most useful first Consumer Cyclical vertical is an earnings-inflection dossier inside the existing theme/detail workflow, with a compact exposure view rather than another top-level dashboard.

The user journey should be: existing theme -> concrete business exposure -> what changed -> demand/contribution/expectation decomposition -> counterevidence and next observable condition -> existing company and market workflow. Source detail remains accessible but does not replace synthesis.

Use contrasting fixtures rather than one favorable example:

1. **lululemon:** distinguish a reported quarter and item-specific adjustment from a lower full-year outlook.
2. **TJX:** preserve an improvement that remains after the company's disclosed adjustment, without promoting it to alpha.
3. **Chipotle:** preserve improving transaction direction alongside a weaker margin.
4. **Nike exposure:** show genuine regional/channel joints while leaving unsupported intersections unknown.
5. **D.R. Horton ownership:** preserve related-party consolidation and minority interests rather than count independent demand twice.

These are research acceptance scenarios, not executed product tests. No new UI, identity contract, collection job or live model is created by specifying them. Integration must consume the actual current shared template and evidence contracts when implementation is authorized.

### Proposed reader-facing synthesis

A dossier should say which layer is improving and which is not. It should explain the mechanism, identify the relevant business and source period, show the most consequential counterevidence and state what would change the interpretation. It must not bury the investor in a checklist or output only a confidence number.

A useful positive result is “the disclosed adjustment does not remove the observed margin improvement; persistence and valuation remain untested.” A useful mixed result is “transactions improved in the selected observation, but the profit bridge has not yet followed.” These statements are deliberately narrower than buy/sell instructions while still materially advancing research.

## 12. Coverage, data feasibility and handoff readiness

Seventeen public primary-source documents/pages support the selected work. The source-local JSON retains locators, periods, definition caveats and observation types. Public accessibility does not establish bulk redistribution rights, complete acquisition history, a production feed or licensed market-consensus access. No paywalled corpus was copied and no new provider was purchased.

Still missing: full business-unit cash/capital allocations; customer-cohort exposure; many contract-level economics; genuine unit maturation histories; actual comparable valuation distributions; consensus histories; relevant security identity/corporate actions; prospective and historical investment evaluation. These omissions are explicit research obligations, not reasons to abandon independently useful work.

The sample remains narrow. Autos, household durables, travel/lodging/capacity, luxury, global platforms and wider China/Hong Kong/Canada coverage need comparable depth before a sector master plan is mature. The next principal tranche should broaden into **autos and household durables**, testing replacement demand, affordability, dealer/channel inventory, captive finance and retained economics. Then integrate the cross-family driver matrix and historical failed-recovery studies.

No Fable handoff has been issued. The eventual handoff must include reviewed architecture, owner/interface reconciliation, feasible data contracts, explicit unknowns, bounded implementation slices, real-path user acceptance and governed evaluation obligations. Fable should not inherit a generic instruction to research the consumer sector from scratch.
