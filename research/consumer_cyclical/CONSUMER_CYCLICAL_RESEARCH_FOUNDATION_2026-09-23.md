## 1. Mandate, product outcome, and architecture boundary

The investor should be able to investigate a discretionary-spending development and determine which consumers and categories it concerns, whether underlying activity is improving, which businesses retain the economics, what expectations already assume, and what evidence would change the interpretation. The machine must preserve the identity, measurement basis, source, time, uncertainty, and authority behind each conclusion.

The proposed analytical chain is:

**Household capacity and preferences -> spending job -> category demand -> brand/channel choice -> delivered units -> realized economics -> attributable cash flow per share -> expectations and valuation -> separately governed market confirmation.**

This is a research framework, not a claim that all relationships have been measured. Its proposed advantage is identifying changing demand and economic capture before a broad sector label or backward-looking basket return tells the whole story. Supply-chain research is subordinate to that job. Neither Robotics component scarcity nor Healthcare payment logic is a universal Consumer Cyclical model.

## 2. Consumer Cyclical needs intersecting views, not one tree

**Proposed design direction:** preserve provider-specific structural sector membership separately from research exposure. Consumer Cyclical and Consumer Discretionary labels must not silently imply identical constituent universes. A basket is a measurement vehicle, not the economic ontology. A cross-sector supplier can matter without being reclassified or admitted to a basket.

Use linked dimensions for: household cohort; spending purpose; price tier; category; business model; company/business unit/brand; channel; economic ownership; geography; competitive/product lifecycle; evidence clocks; and market state. Customer geography, selling geography, listing venue, manufacturing location, and currency are different fields.

A proposed cohort view distinguishes income dependence, liquid resources, wealth exposure, debt burden, housing tenure, life stage and purchase financing. These are aggregate research populations, not inferred personal characteristics of users. Do not allocate aggregate disposable income to individual cash budgets or infer a company's customer mix without disclosure or licensed representative evidence.

### Five simultaneous states

Model household demand, category replacement/inventory/capacity, company competition/product cycle, earnings expectations, and market valuation/positioning separately. A company can gain share in a shrinking category; strong orders can coexist with falling unit profitability; a sound thesis can coexist with an unattractive entry. One universal lifecycle label would conceal those distinctions.

## 3. What the primary evidence actually shows

Sources are public primary HTML documents or pages, inspected at the cutoff. Figures below are limited examples, not a comprehensive current sector call. Source IDs resolve in Section 12. Interpretive requirements are proposals, not accepted causal findings.

### A. Aggregate spending is not one quantity signal

The September 16 Census release reports August retail/food-services sales up 1.2% month over month, adjusted for seasonal/calendar effects but not prices. BEA's August 26 July release reports real PCE growth below 0.1% and a 3.0% saving rate; its August-period release was scheduled for September 30. Different periods, categories and adjustments prevent treating these as contradictory same-period measurements. [S01, S02]

The NY Fed's Q2 page reports $18.8 trillion of household debt and 4.7% in some stage of delinquency. Census's Q2 e-commerce release reports 17.1% of retail sales through e-commerce. Debt balances are not new consumer credit, and channel share is not total demand. Neither aggregate identifies an issuer's customer cohort. [S03, S04]

**Research consequence:** require category, population, units, price treatment, period, publication vintage and coverage before comparing observations. A macro average may inform a hypothesis but cannot directly establish a company's demand or earnings outcome.

### B. Housing volume can improve while its economics weaken

D.R. Horton's quarter ended June 30, released July 21, reports home closings up 4%, home-sales revenue up 1%, and homebuilding pretax income down 10%; cancellations were 20% versus 17%. Management discussed elevated incentives. More closings did not establish stronger profit capture in that period. [S06]

**Research consequence:** housing needs an absorption-to-cash bridge: active communities, gross orders, cancellations, backlog conversion, closings, realized price/incentives, construction/land costs, capital turns and financing. Investigate whether apparent demand was purchased through concessions, and whether lower selling price reflects mix rather than a uniform price cut. Evidence needed next includes incentive definitions and comparable regional/community cohorts.

### C. Home-improvement growth is not automatically renovation recovery

Home Depot's quarter ended August 2 reports total sales up 5.7%, comparable sales up 1.7%, comparable transactions down 1.0% and comparable average ticket up 2.8%. Its transaction/ticket measures exclude HD Supply and SRS. The scoped transaction measures cannot explain every dollar of consolidated growth. [S05]

**Research consequence:** separate professional/DIY, maintenance/remodeling, acquired/disclosed scope, transactions, units per ticket and price/mix. Do not manufacture a full-company bridge by multiplying incompatible denominators. A smaller-project recovery need not establish a big-ticket replacement cycle.

### D. Brand improvement must survive normalization and channel checks

Nike's May 31 quarter, released June 30, reports gross margin up 890 basis points, including approximately 900 basis points from expected tariff recovery. Wholesale revenue rose while Direct declined; inventory value was flat with more units offset by product mix. Reported margin and inventory dollars alone do not establish restored brand demand. [S07]

**Research consequence:** distinguish sell-in from end-customer sell-through; value from units/aging; headline profit from recurring operating economics; and recognized recovery from received cash. A refund is not new product pricing power. A channel shift needs fully loaded acquisition, fulfillment, returns and inventory costs before calling it better economics.

### E. Geographic growth can hide comparable-store weakness

lululemon's August 2 quarter, released September 3, reports international revenue up 4% but international comparable sales down 3%; the corresponding constant-currency figures were +2% and -6%. Its 200-basis-point gross-margin increase included a 560-basis-point refund benefit. New space, currency and unusual items need separate treatment. [S09]

**Research consequence:** investigate openings, maturity cohorts, closures, relocations, channel inclusion and cannibalization. Preserve fiscal labels and actual dates: lululemon calls this fiscal 2026 while TJX calls its nearby August 1 quarter fiscal 2027. Fiscal-year labels alone are not comparable-period keys.

### F. Value retail needs traffic and sourcing evidence

TJX's August 1 quarter reports 4% comparable-sales growth, supported by basket and transactions. The filing identifies $331 million of tariff refunds with $112 million of related incremental compensation, a $219 million net benefit. Normalizing only the gross refund would ignore the associated expense. [S08]

**Research consequence:** treat trade-down and advantageous sourcing as hypotheses requiring evidence, not automatic recession protection. Research full-price realization, aged stock, packaway, traffic, repeat customers and supplier availability. Rising consumer demand, share gains and better buying opportunities are distinct explanations. Do not infer a specific supplier relationship from a brand and off-price retailer sharing a theme.

### G. Restaurant sales do not equal restaurant profit

Chipotle's June quarter reports revenue up 9.3%, comparable sales up 2.2% from transactions/check, and restaurant-level margin of 25.2% versus 27.4%. McDonald's separately reports $37 billion of systemwide sales, 1.3% comparable-sales growth and 4% consolidated-revenue growth. These are different scopes and business measures. [S10, S11]

**Research consequence:** distinguish mature-unit traffic, check, new units and closures; do not call average check pure menu inflation. Company-operated four-wall margin is not corporate cash flow. A franchisor's economic share must be established from royalty, rent, fee and ownership contracts rather than assigning the entire system's sales to its shareholders. The McDonald's HTML summary was inspected; its linked full-release PDF was not acquired here.

### H. Used vehicles combine retail and credit economics

CarMax's May 31 quarter reports comparable used-unit sales down 0.8%, used-vehicle revenue up 4.7%, and used gross profit per unit of $2,177 versus $2,407. Auto-finance income included a $25.1 million allowance release on loans reclassified as held for sale. A credit-cost change need not represent better borrower performance. Q2 fiscal 2027 was scheduled for September 29, after this cutoff. [S17]

**Research consequence:** distinguish retained/sold loan economics, finance penetration denominators, loss recognition and servicing. Vehicle age/mix, financing approval, monthly payment, residual value and inventory turns all deserve investigation. Lower interest rates alone cannot establish affordable financing or healthier underwriting.

### I. Commerce participation is not pure consumer exposure

Amazon's June quarter separates North America, International and AWS, as well as online stores, third-party seller services, advertising and other revenue lines. Consolidated growth includes cloud economics; third-party service revenue is not the seller's entire merchandise value. [S15]

Alibaba's June release introduces new segment reporting, distinguishes reported customer-management revenue from a like-for-like adjustment, and reports operating cash inflow alongside free-cash outflow. Historical segment definitions, contra-revenue programs and capital spending cannot be silently overwritten by current labels. [S16]

**Research consequence:** model a business-unit revenue/profit/exposure matrix, with undisclosed allocations left unknown. Keep retail-media monetization, seller incentives, logistics, cloud and AI capital distinct. An AI-commerce thesis needs incremental conversion, retention or cost evidence; product announcements or investment gains do not establish consumer operating profit.

### J. Travel requires booking, consumption and capacity clocks

Booking's June quarter reports room nights up 5%, gross bookings up 9% and revenue up 8%. Royal Caribbean defines occupancy so that it can exceed 100% with more than two passengers per cabin, and distinguishes net yield from gross-margin yield. Generic retail denominator rules would misread these businesses. [S12, S13]

**Research consequence:** retain booked/stayed/refunded/cash cohorts, currency, available capacity and operator-specific definitions. Do not compute an economic take rate from mismatched booking and revenue periods. A higher yield need not overcome fuel, labor, distribution, dry-dock and financing costs. Fee earners and capacity owners require different valuation and risk models.

### K. Luxury needs currency and client context

Hermes's inspected H1 2026 figures show revenue growth of 1.6% at current exchange rates and 6.1% at constant rates. Those aggregate figures do not isolate price, units, geography or customer mix. [S14]

**Research consequence:** test whether growth comes from client acquisition, repeat purchasing, distribution, price, mix or new supply. Scarcity becomes an economic claim only with evidence of binding demand, constrained qualified supply, substitution limits and retained margins. A prestigious label alone is not that evidence.

## 4. Proposed economic engines and measurable bridges

These are analytical requirements, not newly implemented engines. Each should consume existing canonical facts and keep its own measurement basis explicit.

| Economic role | Proposed bridge | Main failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory-owning retail | Matched transactions x average basket; realized gross profit less fulfillment, occupancy, returns and overhead | Ticket/mix or acquisitions mistaken for volume; cash released by liquidation mistaken for durable earning power |
| Brand/IP owner | End-demand x owned/contractual economics; channel-specific costs and reinvestment | Wholesale shipments or licensee sales mistaken for consolidated demand/revenue |
| Restaurant/location operator | Mature/new cohorts x transactions/check; contribution less rollout and corporate costs | New-unit growth hides weak mature units or poor return on incremental capital |
| Franchisor/manager | Eligible system activity x contractual fee/royalty base plus other rights, less obligations | Systemwide turnover assigned entirely to parent; franchisee distress ignored |
| Homebuilder | Orders/cancellations -> closings x net price -> contribution -> capital turns/cash | Incentive-funded absorption, backlog erosion or land commitments omitted |
| Auto OEM/dealer | Retail/wholesale units x realized economics plus separately reconciled finance/service | Factory shipments, subsidy/mix and credit risk combined into a single margin |
| Marketplace/platform | Cohort merchandise/travel activity -> earned fees/ads/subscriptions less incentives and service costs | Gross/net reporting changes or bookings timing presented as monetization gains |
| Capacity-owning leisure | Capacity x occupancy x realized revenue; operating costs, maintenance and financing | Capacity growth or nonstandard occupancy interpreted as pricing power |

For all roles, distinguish incremental growth capital from maintenance, acquisition spending from organic investment, stock compensation from cash settlement, and debt-funded buybacks from underlying demand. Consolidated income, minority interests, finance subsidiaries and share counts must reconcile before calculating equity value per share.

## 5. What could cause re-rating — research hypotheses and falsifiers

A re-rating is not synonymous with revenue growth. Investigate separately changes in expected normalized earnings/cash flow, duration of growth, reinvestment returns, financing risk and the valuation paid for those expectations.

| Hypothesis | Evidence that would support testing it | Important falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Affordability/transaction recovery | Comparable demand improves as effective payments ease, with stable credit access | Concessions absorb all margin benefit; job/credit deterioration offsets payment relief |
| Replacement-cycle recovery | Cohort replacement and retailer sell-through improve after prior pull-forward | Restocking or channel loading without end demand |
| Brand rehabilitation | Better full-price demand, repeat purchase and sustainable channel contribution | Refunds, aggressive discounting, new space or lower marketing create apparent improvement |
| Value-seeking/share gain | Traffic and repeat cohorts migrate with sustainable basket contribution | Mix, competitor closures or temporary promotion explains the gain |
| New-unit compounding | Mature demand holds while incremental sites earn adequate fully loaded returns | Cannibalization, weaker cohorts or increasing capital required per unit |
| Platform monetization | Like-for-like monetization improves with retention and seller/customer economics intact | Higher fees subsidized by incentives or accompanied by weaker participation |
| Experience yield/operating leverage | Demand absorbs supply at profitable net yields and adequate cash generation | Fuel/capacity/maintenance/capital costs overwhelm the revenue gain |
| Deleveraging and risk reduction | Durable free cash retires debt and reduces refinancing exposure | Asset sales, working-capital timing or equity dilution drive the apparent improvement |
| Mix toward attractive economics | Disclosed business-unit contribution shifts toward durable high-return activities | Allocation assumptions or reporting changes manufacture the shift |

None is a calibrated trading signal. A causal narrative should name its counterfactual: what would be expected absent the proposed mechanism? Compare matched categories, regions or cohorts when feasible. Correlation, shared seasonality or common macro exposure is not proof of propagation.

### Valuation discipline

For positive, consistently defined earnings, the arithmetic identity is `P1/P0 = (EPS1/EPS0) * (multiple1/multiple0)`. Hypothetical EPS growth of 20% with a 25% multiple decline gives a 10% price decline. For losses or changing denominators, use a documented cash-flow/scenario framework rather than a nonsensical P/E.

Proposed family-specific lenses include mid-cycle earnings/cash for cyclicals; land/capital turns and normalized return on equity for builders; unit economics and reinvestment for chains; fee durability and operator health for franchisors; retained platform economics; and net cash-flow/capital demands for capacity owners. These are alternatives to evaluate, not fixed universal valuation rules.

A useful sensitivity model must reconcile leases consistently between enterprise value and earnings, separate financing businesses, avoid double-counting debt/minority claims and adjust corporate actions. A low multiple on peak earnings is not automatically cheap; a higher multiple may reflect duration assumptions that must be explicit. Current security prices, analyst consensus and valuation distributions were not assembled in this tranche. No current cheap/expensive verdict is made.

### Expectations are a separate dataset

Retain prior guidance, its timestamp, fiscal period, currency and adjustment basis. Distinguish management expectations from licensed market consensus. Normalize a revised outlook on a comparable basis before calling it an upgrade. Repeated preliminary and final disclosure is one information lineage, not two independent surprises. Never reconstruct an earlier expectation from subsequent prices or revised analyst data.

## 6. Conditional macro transmission and demand substitution

**Proposed model:** macro observations alter hypotheses through exposed cohorts and contracts, not through a universal sector multiplier. Interest rates affect loan payments, refinancing, discount rates and wealth channels; the signs can differ. In a purely illustrative fixed-rate amortizing loan, payment is `principal * r / (1-(1+r)^(-n))`. The same rate move has different effects with different principal, term, approval and down-payment constraints. Fees, taxes and insurance require separate treatment.

Create research read-throughs for: essential repair versus replacement; goods versus experiences; premium versus value; new versus used vehicles; owned versus rented housing; domestic versus international travel; and brand/direct versus distributor/platform channels. They need evidence of actual substitution and affected issuer economics. Do not assume all categories share one fixed wallet or that a decline in one category transfers dollar-for-dollar to another.

Supply constraints deserve their own test: identify the constrained resource, geography, dated capacity, demand queue, substitute, relief horizon, contractual owner and actual financial benefit. Consider the opposite explanation—excess stock, spare capacity, deteriorating utilization or promotional intensity. A capacity announcement is not qualified output, and concentration is not automatically pricing power.

## 7. Company exposure and subtheme construction

The proposed exposure record binds a company/business unit/brand to an economic activity, region and time, with source-backed role and a declared measurement basis. Store revenue, operating contribution, capital employed and future opportunity separately; never turn an optimistic addressable market into present revenue exposure. Unknown values remain null; qualitative statements remain labeled qualitative.

For each reviewed mapping, record: identity owner reference; source-local business/brand descriptor; direct versus enabling exposure; customer/cohort evidence; owned inventory/assets or contractual rights; reported value and denominator; period/currency; disclosed versus analyst-derived status; confidence limitations; and contrary evidence. Cross-listed instruments should resolve through the existing issuer/security owner, not be counted as separate economic companies.

Basket eligibility requires separate identity, rights, point-in-time membership, liquidity and measurement acceptance. Research relevance does not grant membership. Overlapping baskets need concentration and overlap disclosure; they are not independent confirmations. Conglomerate performance should not be attributed completely to one consumer subtheme.

## 9. Product composition and intelligence requirements

Three approaches were considered as research directions: a static taxonomy page; one generic consumer strength score; or a family-specific economic dossier composed from existing owners. The third best preserves the user's job. A taxonomy alone cannot explain profit capture; one score hides unlike denominators and conflicts. This is a proposed approach, not written-spec acceptance.

The desired workflow is `Theme Tracker -> Consumer Cyclical family -> subtheme -> economic driver and company exposure -> source/contrary evidence -> existing company and market workflow`.

Proposed page content: what changed; affected household/category; activity-versus-price/mix bridge; inventory/capacity/credit state; exposed business units; recurring contribution and cash; expectations/valuation assumptions; evidence quality; market confirmation; and conditions that would invalidate the thesis. Use the shared template rather than design another microsite.

Candidate visual requirements include a cohort/category matrix, company-exposure view, contribution bridge, dated catalyst timeline and conflict panel. These are information requirements only; no chart or UI implementation was created. Preserve unavailable data and disagreements instead of filling them with synthetic scores.

A useful explanation might say: “Revenue grew, but matched transaction counts did not; the profit change includes a separately identified adjustment. The recovery hypothesis therefore needs evidence of end-demand and recurring contribution.” It must also support the positive case when those bridges genuinely improve. Provenance alone is insufficient; the analyst synthesis should explain significance and the next discriminating observation.

## 10. Evaluation before Prophet authority

Start with explanation and research/shadow context, not rank, gate, sizing or trade origination. Separate evidence quality, fundamental forecast skill and investment outcomes. No score calibration or historical return study was performed here.

Proposed evaluation program: predeclare family-specific hypotheses and failure criteria; use actual publication timestamps and point-in-time metric definitions; retain delisted/acquired issuers and adverse cases; resolve share-class/corporate-action history; and prevent overlapping events from becoming independent samples. Measure underlying outcomes such as matched units, realized margins and cash before claiming stock-selection skill.

For market studies, predeclare horizons such as 1, 5, 20 and 60 trading days and relevant business quarters; compare sector/market-adjusted outcomes with parameters estimated only on prior data. Record announcement timing, trading sessions, costs, missing observations and multiple-testing exposure. A later stock rally does not prove the proposed economic mechanism. An accurate explanation can coexist with a poor entry.

An accompanying set of 32 prospective counterexamples specifies traps in scope, clocks, denominators, cash, ownership, expectations and authority. They are **NOT_EXECUTED research requirements**, not application tests. Local document/JSON/arithmetic checks, if reported separately, establish artifact integrity only.
