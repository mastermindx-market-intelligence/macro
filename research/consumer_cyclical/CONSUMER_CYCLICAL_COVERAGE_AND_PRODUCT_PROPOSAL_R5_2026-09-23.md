## 1. The decision this research supports

The proposed first capability is an **economic-change dossier within the existing theme and company workflow**. It explains what changed in a business, which part of the change could reach common-shareholder cash, what remains uncertain, and what evidence would change the explanation. It does not launch another sector dashboard or automatically select securities.

The complete mission remains broader: granular themes and subthemes, measured company exposure, causal economic reasoning, valuation and expectation analysis, and integration with existing market and opportunity systems. A descriptive first slice preserves that destination without pretending to possess data or predictive authority that has not been established.

R1–R5 now provide examples spanning every original research family, but this is not complete coverage of every industry, country, company or historical cycle. Five waves of illustrative research cannot be converted into a census by adding a percentage-complete label. The next bottleneck is native source/contract fit and a reviewable design, not a sixth unrestricted set of similar company summaries.

## 2. Alternatives and recommendation

**A. One Consumer Cyclical score.** Attractive for a simple headline, but rejected as the organizing model. Household conditions, company competitiveness, accounting effects, cash economics, valuation and entry quality can disagree without any one observation being wrong. A fused score would hide the reason for the disagreement and encourage premature decision authority.

**B. Fourteen independent products.** Detailed sector-specific dashboards could preserve domain depth but would duplicate definitions, source ingestion, customer state and workflows. They would also force users to reconcile the same issuer across several pages. This is rejected as a parallel architecture.

**C. Shared owner-preserving read views with economic-specific models.** Recommended. Preserve the existing GMI identity/evidence spine and STSI page composition. Let domain-specific research identify the relevant units, contracts, growth bridges and falsifiers. Display these through the existing theme/detail/company journey. One company can have several economic roles without becoming several securities.

This recommendation is an architectural proposal. Current continuation authorizes research and design development; it does not approve a product specification that has not yet been reviewed or transfer source custody to this session.

## 4. Economic models to reuse without flattening the sector

The common reasoning path is **demand or service need -> competitive choice -> contractual activity -> recognized economics -> cash/capital -> expectations/valuation -> separately governed market confirmation**. The models below determine what belongs at each step.

**Purchase and replenishment.** For brands and retail, distinguish end-consumer purchases, wholesale shipments, retailer inventory, returns, promotion and repeat demand. An inventory restock can be real revenue without proving a new consumption cycle. Scarcity matters only at the product, location and time evidenced, with substitutes and relief in view.

**Installed-asset and replacement.** For autos and durables, identify asset age/use, repair, replacement, upgrade, financing and disposal. A large aging population is a candidate demand pool, not an inevitable purchase schedule. Repair events can repeat; a terminal replacement choice needs a defined unit.

**Location or capacity conversion.** For builders, restaurants, hotels and leisure, connect available capacity to utilization, realized price/mix, contribution and the capital needed. Openings, acquisitions, conversions, mature units and closed units have different implications. The research must retain failed or closed cohorts in historical comparisons.

**Fee and franchise economics.** Separate system activity from the parent fee stream, operator profit and capital required to keep the network viable. A parent can receive cash while the operator funds investment. That may be sustainable or damaging; the contract and operator evidence decide.

**Access and finite-service economics.** For membership, passes and education, distinguish the customer account, entitlement, actual use, outcome, renewal and successful completion. Do not reward an indefinite customer lifetime where the intended job ends. Additional engagement needs an incremental contribution analysis, not an automatic positive score.

**Platform intermediation.** Separate gross transaction activity, net revenue, payment flows, acquisition/support costs and retained contribution. Customer and supplier cash may relate to future fulfillment. A lower percentage margin can coexist with more contribution dollars; a lower expense ratio can be a presentation change.

**Financial-claim bridge.** Across all models, reconcile common income, shares, debt, leases, minority interests, retained finance businesses and sale proceeds consistently. Financing an improvement is not equivalent to operating improvement. Enterprise and equity valuation bases must not double-count obligations.

These are model families for research and composition, not seven new engines or registries. Existing specialist calculation owners should be extended only after native-fit review identifies the actual gap.

## 5. The first user journey and visible result

The stable entry remains `state_of_themes.html` and its existing accepted detail/company navigation. The design must resolve exact target theme/group and issuer identifiers through GMI; it must not invent a Consumer Cyclical canonical theme or a route merely to make the prototype convenient.

A compact economic-change section should answer five questions in order: **What changed? Who economically benefits? Why could it persist? What evidence contradicts it? What must happen next?** A user can then open the measured exposure, growth/cash bridge, dated expectations and source trail. Narrative should lead with the useful conclusion while showing its limits; the source drawer is support rather than the product itself.

The top view should separate a directly observed fact, the company's attributed explanation, Mastermind's research hypothesis and an untested investment implication. It should retain positive and mixed cases. It must not become a screen whose only useful output is missingness or a long compliance checklist.

Proposed first comparison set: **Planet Fitness, Life Time and lululemon**. They provide three distinct economic explanations: revenue with offsetting advertising throughput; operating improvement with property-financing cash; and a delivered quarter that differs from the direction of annual guidance. This is a bounded acceptance set, not a recommended portfolio, a claim of semantic equivalence, or a demand to place all three in one price basket.

Theme linkage and source availability determine which existing pages can lawfully expose each case. If a link is unresolved, keep the company dossier available only on an accepted company path; never fabricate a theme membership to satisfy the demonstration.

## 7. Data feasibility: what is available versus what is merely desirable

The research has actually acquired bounded public disclosure facts, metric definitions, selected business/ownership descriptions, management guidance and some historical counterexamples. That can support explanatory research after source admission and identity binding. It is not equivalent to an automated live collector, a complete history or a license to republish every source.

Customer retention, cohort contribution, seller economics, visit-level behavior, full-price sell-through and site-level capital returns are often absent at the required grain. The first build must mark these unmeasured. A management statement may be useful but remains attributed; it cannot become a measured independent series.

Point-in-time consensus, corporate-action-consistent valuation histories and fully versioned historical company universes remain substantial requirements for later investment studies. A historical filing alone does not supply pre-event expectations. A current stock price must not be used to manufacture past valuation or imply that a story was unpriced.

For each proposed field, the eventual design needs source owner, retrieval path, rights, retention, expected cadence, observation/publication/recorded clocks, revision handling, unit/denominator and failure behavior. Freshness budgets must follow the source's actual cadence and job. An annual definition is not stale because it is not daily; an earnings-event observation is not current merely because the page rebuilt today.

No new major paid source is assumed. Any later source purchase, restricted corpus or external service activation remains a separate budget, rights and authorization decision.

## 8. Valuation and early opportunity research: preserve the destination

A useful explanatory dossier is not the final moat. The intended advantage is detecting changes in economic quality early enough to improve research and eventually validated decisions. To make that testable, the proposal separates **business improvement, expectation revision, valuation compensation and technical entry**.

For example, a repeat-service hypothesis might predict better renewal-adjusted contribution, not simply revenue growth. A capacity hypothesis might predict a higher return on the next unit of invested capital, not merely higher occupancy. A financing hypothesis might predict a lower burden on future common cash after all claims, not merely less headline debt.

Later research should translate each into explicit scenarios: units or active entitlements, price/mix, retained margin, reinvestment, working capital, taxes, financing and shares. Reverse valuation can then ask which scenario the observed equity value requires. Unknown inputs must remain intervals or unavailable values, not precise invented point estimates.

Leading candidates include matched order or booking cohorts, renewal/collection transitions, less promotional support at stable volume, profitable replenishment, improving unit-cohort cash and genuinely lower financing risk. Each has counterexamples already represented by the research. The candidate must be defined before testing, with a date and horizon and a result that could disprove it.

The program should not use a language model's narrative score as a forecast or a direct input to position size. Explanations can improve the user's workflow immediately; investment authority requires independent accepted evidence.

## 9. Acceptance and learning: interpretation before prediction

The first slice's acceptance must be production-path and task-based. A paid authorized user should move from an accepted theme or company entry to a correct economic explanation and its exact evidence. An anonymous or ineligible user must not recover restricted full-fidelity data. A source correction must propagate through the incumbent owner without appearing as an independent second confirmation. Missing denominators must not become zero or a confident comparison.

The three golden cases should demonstrate all of the following: correct entity and business scope; correct time/period; a reproducible arithmetic bridge; a useful leading explanation; visible competing explanation; honest missingness; and unchanged incumbent selection/entry outputs. Desktop/mobile, supported languages and themes, access, empty/stale/partial cases and return navigation belong to the eventual browser proof.

Local research checks are not this acceptance. Likewise, a schema round-trip, HTTP 200, synthetic fixture, green CI or a merged PR does not prove the user can complete the task.

Interpretation studies should use blinded, predeclared cases with positive, negative and mixed controls. Business-outcome studies should freeze definitions, horizons and available-at-the-time inputs. Investment studies should add appropriate sector/style/earnings baselines, transaction costs, survivor and revision control, and correction for repeated hypothesis searches. Each stage is a separate authority decision under existing evaluation ownership.
