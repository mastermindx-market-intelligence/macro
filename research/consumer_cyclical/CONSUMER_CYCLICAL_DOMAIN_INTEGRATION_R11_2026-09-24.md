## 4. Input qualification before arithmetic

Every production input must bind to an admitted native fact and its exact issuer, event, document revision, locator and measurement context. An input key in the companion examples is only a local research label. It is not a source receipt.

Required comparison context includes measurement period and grain, actual/outlook role, target where relevant, metric definition, accounting basis, business perimeter, currency/unit, scale and sign convention. None/None is not evidence that an omitted context matches. Equal values or labels do not prove equal definitions.

Exact decimal source text must remain available through a qualified native representation. The illustrative oracle accepts decimal strings and rejects floats, booleans, exponent notation, non-finite text and oversized inputs. This is a deliberately narrow research representation, not a proposal to replace every numeric field in the platform. A native producer may use a different accepted exact representation if the Consumer output preserves equivalent meaning and precision.

Before any sum, input periods and scopes must match. Before a year-over-year bridge, each side must have coherent event/revision and measurement context; comparability of the periods must be supported by the source owner, not inferred from calendar arithmetic. The research examples explicitly stipulate that comparison and do not prove it for real companies.

Before an outlook revision, both inputs remain outlooks, their target and compatible definitions match, each range is ordered, and the original reporting events remain distinct. This first slice does not pretend to cover every same-quarter guidance-update situation.

## 5. Complete invented worked examples

The companion JSON and `research_oracle.py` use ONLY invented numbers, issuers, dates and `example.invalid` locators. All native references are null and admission flags false. They are deliberately unlike a production source package. Real acceptance sources remain the four releases identified in R6 and the qualified refinements in R8/R9; they were not reacquired here.

### A. Growth with offsetting fund expense

Assume total revenue changes from 230 to250, fund revenue from40 to48, and fund expense from40 to49, all on a declared compatible quarterly basis. The required result is total revenue change20, fund-revenue change8, fund-expense change9, fund-net change−1 and revenue change excluding the fund12. The fund share of the positive revenue change is40%.

The explanation must say that12 is a revenue difference, not profit or organic growth. It must retain the −1 contribution change, not round it to an exact offset. The next useful observation is recurring contribution in the non-fund business.

For zero or negative total revenue change the useful differences remain available, but the “share of revenue growth” ratio is withheld. The oracle explicitly checks that degraded case rather than deleting the whole result.

### B. Cash with property proceeds

Assume operating cash120, signed capital cash−150, asset proceeds70, and a disclosed company-defined subtotal40 on one half-year interval. The pre-proceeds subtotal is−30; adding70 reconciles to40. Both views must be shown.

Neither is labeled normalized owner cash or maintenance-only cash. Growth investment, property obligations and unit-cohort returns remain unanswered. A positive capital leg, a wrong total or a quarter/half-year mixture must refuse this calculation.

A separate exact-decimal example uses0.30−0.20+0.10=0.20. It establishes that the research arithmetic is not routed through binary float, not that native ingestion already supports the required representation.

### C. Two forecasts for one annual target

Assume an older annual outlook of1000–1100 and a newer outlook of900–950, issued by different reporting events for the same target. The lower bound changes−100, the upper bound−150, and the new upper bound is50 below the old lower bound.

No annual actual is supplied. The explanation may report a downward management revision; it may not report a consensus miss, a realized annual result or a corrected original document. Neither a midpoint nor a forecast-success score is required.

Passing the new forecast in an actual role, changing the target, reversing a range or silently mixing definitions must fail the requested comparison. The known source statements should remain available through their properly entitled native views.
