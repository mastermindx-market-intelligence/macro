## 1. Outcome, scope and acceptance boundary

The investor should move from an existing company or governed theme entry to a useful explanation of what changed economically, see the actual calculation and contrary evidence, and know which observation would change the interpretation. The machine must preserve the issuer, business scope, period, source version, calculation dependencies and authority behind the same explanation.

The selected first slice covers Planet Fitness (PLNT), Life Time (LTH) and lululemon (LULU). They are contrasting acceptance cases, not a new basket, a claim of comparable business models or recommendations. PLNT tests growth with offsetting advertising flows; LTH tests positive operating evidence alongside a capital-funding bridge; LULU tests changed management expectations without confusing a later forecast with a correction or an actual result.

The primary display answers: **What changed? Which economic exposure is affected? What supports persistence? What contradicts it? What comes next?** Evidence supports that job; a list of citations is not the product. Positive, negative and mixed evidence must survive. The page must distinguish a reported observation, management attribution, deterministic calculation and research hypothesis. It may not manufacture a forecast or verdict from an appealing narrative.

The first slice is complete only after the four selected real releases reach the lawful native source path, the three paid company dossiers show correct useful results, a source revision invalidates dependent results correctly, and required access and browser proofs pass. The complete theme-integrated slice also requires a real accepted theme-to-company navigation chain. Unresolved thematic membership cannot be invented to pass it. A company-first release can be a separately labeled useful milestone, not full theme integration or completion of the sector program.

R1–R5's 14-family map, seven economic models and investment-research destination remain controlling design inputs. Global coverage, customer/site cohorts, failed recoveries, actual market expectations, valuation histories and predictive validation are still owed by the parent mission. The descriptive slice does not erase those obligations.

## 2. Selected architecture and rejected alternatives

**Selected:** extend the incumbent Company Intelligence event/fact profile seam; compose deterministic economic comparisons under existing F04/STSI ownership; publish a typed paid view through the existing private earnings publication and API owners. No separate Consumer database, source collector, issuer registry, semantic graph, metric catalog, event log, revision index, publisher, queue, authentication layer or evaluation ledger is introduced.

A consumer-specific spreadsheet/JSON fact warehouse is rejected because it would duplicate source corrections, identity and periods. Renaming the synthetic Financial Intelligence Packet issuer is rejected because its fixture contract does not establish real-company KPI admission. Coercing releases into fake transcript segments or untagged operating KPIs into fictitious XBRL is rejected because it changes the meaning of the source. Putting the full dossier in public static JSON and relying on a visual paywall is rejected because hiding a panel is not access control.

The first three formulas do not require an LLM to calculate or extract numbers. The factual clauses and arithmetic should be deterministic. Human-reviewed interpretation templates consume only accepted facts and explicitly named limitations. A later model may draft cited explanatory prose, but cannot create values, overwrite facts, select an unsupported peer comparison, rank securities or size positions.

## 5. Direct-fact and guidance contract extension

The current top-level `event_workspace.v1` key set remains unchanged. Existing profiles and their published objects remain byte-compatible. New issuer profiles produce literal values through the existing direct-fact seam; extraction never performs the economic calculation.

**Proposed, not yet enrolled:** an optional, closed `measurement_context` on new profile facts and guidance items, accepted by the Company Intelligence contract owner and explicitly understood by the new consumer. It records period start/end and kind, actual versus guidance role, issuer/business scope, metric-definition reference/version, source basis, currency, unit scale, canonical decimal text, source precision, and receipts for context/definition. Guidance additionally records its target fiscal period and paired bounds. The existing `metric`, `value`, `unit`, `period`, `basis` and source-span fields retain their current meanings. The additional object does not become a global metric registry or overwrite an existing scalar to fit the Consumer view.

Unknown scope or precision remains explicitly unknown. The consumer may display a raw source observation with its limitation but cannot calculate a comparison whose required context is missing. New-context validation is closed: malformed optional data must not be ignored while the calculation proceeds. Legacy payloads remain readable; absence of the new context means the new comparison is unavailable, not that legacy evidence is invalid.

For a table, the profile must bind the table title, row label, column period/year, unit header and the uniquely located numeric token. A valid number receipt in the wrong year or year-to-date column is still a wrong fact. Matching only the first repeated number or the first row with the right label is insufficient. Literal values retain sign and precision. Numeric values at the comparison boundary use canonical Decimal text; the compatibility adapter must be tested against existing consumers rather than assuming their number/string handling is interchangeable.

Release-guidance extraction is a separately typed callback receiving `BoundRelease`, document ID, event ID and fiscal period. The existing transcript-guidance callback remains unchanged. This requirement overlaps the already-recorded #7331 proposal; its source owner must reconcile it once. A release must not be disguised as a transcript to reuse the other callback.

## 6. Deterministic economic comparison

A proposed `economic_change.consumer.v1` is a derived read representation owned by the existing F04/STSI composition layer, not a new canonical store. The exact contract name remains a proposal until owner enrollment. Each result identifies the company, original native event and fact references, source revision/generation hashes, calculation/version, source and system cutoffs, comparison basis, typed limitations and all-false decision authority.

A dependency reference is not just `fact_id`: the native fact IDs can recur across events. Bind owner, issuer, event, generation, document revision and fact ID. The derivation must be reproducible from those exact references. Persisting a paid rendering is publication of a derived view, not creation of source truth.

The first slice uses only named operations: matched-period subtraction, a signed cash sum, range-bound comparison and a labeled ratio of two matched changes. There is no expression supplied by the browser, arbitrary `eval`, unconstrained graph traversal or model-generated formula. Dependencies must agree on units, scale, economic scope and the applicable interval. Explicitly allowed differences, such as current/prior years in a year-over-year difference, are part of the formula contract rather than accidental mismatch tolerance.

A zero denominator suppresses the ratio without suppressing the underlying values. Missing and measured zero differ. Negative or tiny denominators require an explicit display limitation; they must not produce a persuasive contribution percentage. Source precision is carried through the calculation; presentation may round only at the final display boundary. Reconciliation tolerances derive from source rounding, not an arbitrary epsilon that hides an inconsistent sign or period.

Do not use the current workspace's consensus `deltas` array for management-guidance comparisons. Its current validator expressly reserves beat/miss and `basis_match` for licensed consensus behavior. The Consumer view records `comparison_kind=management_guidance_revision` and never claims a consensus surprise or market-implied expectation.

## 7. Golden outputs and useful synthesis

### 7.1 PLNT: advertising flow versus retained growth

S1's table, in USD thousands, reports total revenue 365223 versus 340879; advertising revenue 32922 versus 22781; advertising expense 32922 versus 22777. Derived changes are 24344, 10141 and 10145. The advertising net change is **-4 thousand**, while its current-period net is zero. The advertising revenue change is approximately **41.66%** of the reported total revenue change. These are arithmetic relationships, not organic growth, a complete profit bridge or a conclusion about franchise health.

**Precision refinement of R5:** the narrative rounded the two advertising changes to the same $10.1 million. At the displayed table precision they are not identical. Preserve both source bases and the -4 thousand residual. Do not edit the historical R5 evidence to pretend it originally used table precision.

Suggested lead: a substantial part of the revenue increase is associated with advertising flows with nearly matching expense, so it does not translate one-for-one into incremental profit. Counterevidence/context: the business also has franchise, corporate-club and equipment economics that require their own analysis. Watch condition: comparable dues and retained operating contribution, together with franchisee economics where actually disclosed. Do not call dues billed observed visits or subtract advertising alone and label the remainder organic growth.

### 7.2 LTH: operating evidence versus funding the expansion

Select S2's **six-month** free-cash-flow reconciliation, in USD thousands: operating cash 408351, signed capital expenditure net of construction reimbursements -523276, sale-leaseback proceeds 200191, reported company-defined free cash flow 85266. The pre-sale-leaseback subtotal is **-114925**; the full sum is **85266** with zero reconciliation residual. Do not mix quarterly operating cash with first-half capital expenditure, or subtract an already negative cash-flow leg twice.

Keep the separately reported favorable operating evidence visible; it is not invalidated by this cash bridge. Suggested lead: operating performance improved, while the positive company-defined cash figure also includes property monetization. The pre-transaction subtotal is not normalized or maintenance-only free cash flow. Counterevidence/context: growth capital can support future earnings; sale-leaseback changes future obligations and asset control. Watch condition: matched-period capital requirements, mature/new-center contribution and lease-adjusted economics when available. No undisclosed lease NPV or customer return estimate is fabricated.

### 7.3 LULU: reporting event versus expectation target

S3 gives FY2026 revenue guidance of 11000–11150 USD million; S4 gives 10350–10500. Both bounds moved down by **650 million**. The later upper bound is **500 million below** the earlier lower bound. Optional midpoint arithmetic is explicitly illustrative: 11075 to 10425, approximately **-5.87%**, not a company-issued point forecast.

The EPS ranges must be displayed with their different stated treatment of the identified refund benefit; do not call their raw difference a like-for-like normalized revision. Optional arithmetic of reported quarterly EPS 2.92 less the identified 0.86 benefit is 2.06, labeled a single-item subtraction, not a comprehensive adjusted EPS or consensus result.

Suggested lead: management reduced its full-year revenue range; the reported quarter and its identified benefit remain separate from that change. Counterevidence/context: future operating responses and specific regional/product developments require new evidence. Watch condition: actual activity and the next same-target guidance, not an assumed recovery or share-price forecast. This case needs no fabricated single annual guidance event or new identity scheme: compare the guidance observations attached to their original reporting events.

## 10. User-facing states and failure behavior

Use the existing STSI dimensions and conflict grammar. A source/scope/time split is not automatically a genuine contradiction. Missing data is not zero; an unsupported comparison is not a bearish finding. The view must identify its analyzed reporting event separately from the freshest discovery check and show whether a newer event is known but not yet analyzed.

Default layout: a short economic conclusion, the reporting period and source status, a compact calculation, counterevidence, then one primary watch condition. Expandable detail shows exact facts, units, periods, versions and source context. Do not overwhelm the sector landing page with full company tables or change its dashboard height materially for this feature. All three company cases need desktop/mobile, supported language and theme treatment. Numeric meaning and signs cannot change in translation.

No match shows not covered; valid identity with no eligible source shows source unavailable; missing measurement context shows not comparable; rights or entitlement failure stays in the existing private error behavior. A producer failure does not become not applicable. A deterministic calculation failure leaves the raw accepted facts visible to an entitled user when safe, with no stale calculated number substituted. No page claims that an unstarted fetch, worker or repair is running.
