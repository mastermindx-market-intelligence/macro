## 5. Repricing science: freeze the questions before examining winners

Separate six measurements:

- **Anticipation:** eligible pre-event return and expectations changes.
- **Announcement reaction:** last eligible pre-release observation to the first genuinely available post-release observation, preserving session, halt and timestamp precision.
- **Tradable drift:** first executable entry after public availability AND system decision, then 1/5/21/63/126 trading-session outcomes.
- **Persistence:** prespecified excess-return distributions, downside tails and later-catalyst contamination, not the best later price.
- **Valuation rerating:** price/enterprise-value change versus then-known earnings/sales/asset revisions, dilution and distributions. Price appreciation alone is not multiple expansion.
- **Investment utility:** net return, benchmark/hurdle success, payoff ratio, loss severity, coverage, turnover and capacity—not clinical or award accuracy relabelled as alpha.

Define universe, labels, executable pricing, benchmark/factor convention, costs, event time and model-selection rules before evaluation. Preserve confounds: earnings, financing, macro releases and simultaneous company events. Dependence-aware temporal splits must prevent the same issuer, asset or transaction family leaking across train/test when that violates the intended generalization claim.

Do not delete delisted losers or treat original-equity extinguishment as benign missing follow-up. Acquisition cash/share settlement differs from total loss. Original-target misses survive later revised-target success. A later disclosure of an earlier economic fact cannot enter an earlier information set. Model weights can themselves contain future historical knowledge; retrospective retrieval cutoffs alone do not establish an outcome-blind LLM backtest.
