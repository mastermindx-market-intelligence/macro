## What materially changes

The corrected program starts as an **action-conditioned evidence adapter over existing market and candidate owners**, rather than assuming a new canonical market-regime classifier is needed. It distinguishes descriptive observations, qualified forecasts and permitted decision effects, and does not force six named states or claim that the dimensions are empirically orthogonal.

B0 gets an independent price-qualified path. Original C1 retains its event-qualified experiment and exact target. Each incremental family is compared with B0 on matched dates with its coverage cost disclosed; no universal intersection with the weakest source. Real prospective observations can accrue and a useful read-only workflow can be proven before a model winner exists.

The October 2 Options research reopening and actual newer retrospective results become required inputs, without converting old failures into successes. The newer vanna-relief return target is not a direct retest of the older volatility association. Existing internal flow `market_tide`, MAS-260 and Terminal Market Structure computations retain their identities and owners.

The six action contracts now specify pre-decision applicability, candidate/strategy/episode joins, costs, maturity, path-dependent de-risk/re-entry evaluation and no-mutation tests. Statistical defaults are explicit **proposals requiring ex-ante owner ratification**, not claims of accepted law or observed efficacy. Shadow simulation is not labeled causal live uplift.
