                          TREASURY POSTPONES 4-WEEK BILL AUCTION

   The 4-week Treasury bill auction originally scheduled for today is postponed until tomorrow

September 12, 2001. Treasury took this action because most major dealers closed as a result of the

incident in New York’s financial district. Except for the auction date, the terms and conditions of the

auction announced on September 10 remain the same. The closing time for noncompetitive tenders is

noon eastern time with a competitive tender closing time of 1:00 p.m. eastern time.
