                      TREASURY CANCELS AUCTION OF 4-WEEK BILLS


       The Treasury announced today that it is canceling the auction of 4-week bills, originally

scheduled for today, and earlier rescheduled for Wednesday, September 12, 2001. There are no

plans to reschedule this auction.
