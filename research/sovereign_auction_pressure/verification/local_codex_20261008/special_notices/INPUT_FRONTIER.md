# Special-notice source frontier

Three original official PDFs now establish concrete cancellation/postponement input shapes. The raw bodies, request/body/verification clocks, text extraction hashes and parent visual/text normalization are retained here. These are October 9 captures; the 2001/2015 document dates, source HTTP headers and raw embargo minute are not historical system receipt clocks. Exact publication and original first seen remain null.

The 2001 four-week Bill notice moves the September 11 auction to September 12 and states explicit noon/13:00 eastern deadlines. A separate same-day notice cancels that original/rescheduled auction without a replacement plan. The actual intraday publication ordering is not measured by these current captures. The 2015 two-year Note notice expresses an intention to postpone its October 27 auction because of settlement constraints; it leaves the five- and seven-year auctions unchanged. It supplies no replacement date and does not identify the separately mentioned FRN as postponed.

`SPECIAL_NOTICE_CASEBOOK.json` preserves those scopes and unknown CUSIPs. Its normalized semantics are parent checked, not independently accepted parser output. No episode is joined only by a reused CUSIP or guessed term. No disappearance from a snapshot is treated as cancellation. A buyback-operation cancellation is a different accounting operation and cannot cancel an issuance auction or imply funded cash zero.

The held v1 Mastermind and Terminal readers admit only TENTATIVE, ANNOUNCED, AWAITING_RESULT and RESULT_OBSERVED lifecycle states. Directly inserting CANCELLED/POSTPONED would make both strict readers refuse the context. The next source transition must therefore be independently accepted as one additive notice/reconciliation contract, with explicit original/replacement episode binding, same-clock ambiguity, clock eligibility, terminal cancellation, retained superseded terms and unknown replacement deadlines. It must be composed with the separately admitted consumer owners before release. No v1 validator is weakened here.

Required discriminating cases include postponed-then-cancelled, unspecified replacement date, scope excluding unaffected auctions/FRN, multiple matching same-class episodes, contrary same-clock notices and future notice receipts. Historical challenge feature eligibility cannot be reconstructed from today's downloads. No cancellation parser, consumer transition, feed publication or full Phase A completion is claimed by this evidence.
