     TREASURY ANNOUNCES AUCTION SCHEDULE FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 24, 2025

Given the upcoming holiday, Treasury announces its securities auction schedule for Wednesday,
December 24, 2025.
The noncompetitive and competitive closing times for the 4-week and 8-week bill auctions are 9:30 a.m.
and 10:00 a.m. ET, respectively.
The noncompetitive and competitive closing times for the 17-week bill and 7-year note auctions are
11:00 a.m. and 11:30 a.m. ET, respectively.
Offering amounts and other details for the 4-week, 8-week, and 17-week bills will be announced in
regularly scheduled announcements on Tuesday, December 23, 2025.
