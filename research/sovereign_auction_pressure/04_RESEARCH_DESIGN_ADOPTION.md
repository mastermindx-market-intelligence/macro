## Adjudicated decisions

1. **H3 is the first new empirical implementation target.** Build the private settlement-cohort ledger and release-aware funding baseline before fitting. Current official DTS accounting can support descriptive reconciliation; it cannot stand in for a complete private cash forecast. The October 6 outcome-blind accounting audit is a data-semantics test, not a market-performance experiment.
2. **H1 requires actual expectation evidence.** Prior official plan changes can be published as `official_plan_revision`; last-auction differences are `offering_change`. Neither is relabeled a market-expectation surprise. A price-derived announcement factor is observed after its construction window.
3. **H2 requires a different test from failed D2 V1.** The proposed own-tenor intraday burden × fragility experiment requires entitled quotes and actual cutoff clocks. Daily ETF returns are not a substitute replication.
4. **H4's old composite remains NO-GO.** A genuine WI-tail branch needs the same security's entitled pre-cutoff quote and result-known time. Current result allocations are post-result context only; held #7320 retains denominator-fix ownership.
5. **H5 remains an independent gate.** It must improve the actual prior-known Macro baseline, outperform in-regime controls and satisfy proper-score, calibration, alert-budget and realistic opportunity-cost requirements. Higher rates volatility alone does not establish equity downside or authorize cash defense.
6. **Historical exposure is recorded honestly.** Prior null studies already inspected substantial history through June/July 2026. A new split of those dates is retrospective, not an untouched organizational holdout. Forward time begins only at the first successfully logged decision after final code/data/specification freeze and admitted collector readiness; the current date is not backfilled as a launch.

## First executable work and deliberate absence of results

The implemented W1 source eligibility gates reject later-known observations, corrected latest records and future result-bearing contradictions. The funding audit independently reconciles original cash and debt tables while keeping private net cash and S−1 forecast eligibility unavailable. The W2 primitives admit only externally qualified input observations and source-complete cash categories.

No market outcome model, new auction backtest, calibration fit, forecast probability or forward performance report was run in this initiation phase. The quantitative thresholds in the design are prospective research choices, not estimates of predictive skill, risk limits or a policy authorization. Independent source/math review is engineering evidence and does not substitute for independent predictive validation.
