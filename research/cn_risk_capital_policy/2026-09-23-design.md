# China Risk Radar capital-policy validation — design

Status: preregistration design for `cn-risk-p4-capital-policy-20260923-solpro-001`; research only, Draft/HOLD, no live consumer.

## Outcome and authority boundary

The study separates two questions that the current product prose partially blends: whether the China Radar identifies unusually hazardous market states, and whether a particular gross-exposure response creates enough protection after opportunity cost, turnover, execution lag, and uncertainty. Existing detector evidence is an input, never proof that the live `1.00/.97/.90/.78/.62` ladder is a valid sizing rule.

The implementation may read the exact live state construction and mapping but may not edit `engine/risk_radar_intl.py`, UI/templates, `can_force`, Market State, ranking, execution, Prophet, or any published sizing behavior. Outputs live only under `research/cn_risk_capital_policy/`, the research harness, and tests.

## Data architecture

The state reconstruction calls the exact pinned-base `engine.risk_radar_intl.composite_series(CN_PROFILE)`, `_band`, profile bands, and context gate. Its usable reconstructed history begins 2003-03-03. The primary return series is the engine benchmark `china/000001.SS` (Shanghai Composite stored close series). A secondary investable-proxy robustness lane uses `china/510300.SS` from 2012-05-04 while retaining the same ex-ante Radar state.

The issued forward ledger is evaluated separately and labelled issued-forward evidence. At freeze time it contains 34 rows through 2026-09-23, only 16 matured, including one matured `elevated` and four matured `risk-off` rows. It cannot estimate or optimize five gross coefficients.

## Replay architecture

A policy maps an observable state at close `t` to gross applied to the next close-to-close return; the primary convention is one-session lag and the realism sensitivity is two sessions. Uninvested gross earns zero. Gross never exceeds one. Turnover cost is charged only on post-start absolute gross changes, at 10 bps per unit in the primary run and 25 bps in stress.

The current ladder is evaluated first and exactly. Alternatives are frozen, sparse, and economically interpretable: constant full gross, constant 0.75 gross, an exposure-matched constant derived only from the current ladder's mean executed gross, a binary loud-state policy, an otherwise-current ladder with only the risk-off floor changed to 0.75, and a simple 15% trailing-volatility target clipped to `[0.50, 1.00]`. No coefficient search is permitted.

## Independence and crisis architecture

An independent loud episode joins `elevated`/`risk-off` observations separated by at most ten non-loud sessions; an eligible episode must contain at least three loud sessions. Episode evaluation runs from the first loud session through 21 sessions after the last loud session. Risk-off-containing and elevated-only episodes are reported separately.

Nine benchmark-only crisis windows are frozen before policy outcomes: 2004-04-06→2005-07-11, 2007-10-16→2008-11-04, 2009-08-04→2010-07-05, 2011-04-18→2012-12-03, 2015-06-12→2016-01-28, 2018-01-24→2019-01-03, 2020-01-13→2020-03-23, 2021-09-13→2022-04-26, and 2023-05-08→2024-02-05. Recovery participation uses the next 63 sessions after each trough. Era stability is split at 2016-01-01.

## Evidence and adjudication

The report must include CAGR/return, realized volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum drawdown, 95% historical expected shortfall, reduced-gross time, average gross, missed upside, avoided downside, turnover and exposure-change count, crisis effects, recovery participation, leave-one-crisis-out, split eras, lag/cost sensitivity, independent-episode outcomes, and concentration of benefit in the top crises.

Uncertainty uses a deterministic 2,000-draw circular moving-block bootstrap with 21-session blocks and seed `20260923`. Exact five-step authority is impossible unless there are at least 40 eligible episodes, including at least 12 risk-off-containing and 12 elevated-only episodes, and the preregistered protection/cost/robustness gates all pass. A simpler binary policy needs at least 20 eligible episodes and its own stricter comparator and stability gates. Failing these floors is evidence against precision, not permission to tune the floors.

The final product verdict is selected from the commission's five allowed outcomes. Any shadow candidate is inert data with no live import or consumer. User-facing language is adjudicated separately from statistical detector quality.
