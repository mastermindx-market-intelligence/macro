The important new distinction is **economic level versus economic direction**. Improving a loss is not the same as becoming profitable. A positive margin can deteriorate. An acquisition can grow reported profit while comparable activity weakens. A contract can reduce the holder's share while the underlying operation remains unchanged. The product must explain these distinctions before it attempts to prioritize research or influence stock selection.

## 2. Four economically different situations

The following taxonomy is a proposed **explanation of two observed dimensions**, not a new lifecycle/ThemeState owner or a buy/sell classification.

| Level of the comparable unit result | Change in that result | Investor research question |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | Improving | Is better realization/efficiency durable, and what cash and valuation already reflect it? |
| Negative | Improving | Is the operating recovery sufficient to fund the business before liquidity or dilution becomes binding? |
| Positive | Deteriorating | Are strong headline profits concealing a normalization or a worsening future input layer? |
| Negative | Deteriorating | What can change the cost, volume, financing or asset-exit problem, and what claims bear the risk? |

Examples EC02–EC06 explicitly separate these dimensions. EC03 improves from -20 to -10 but remains negative. EC04 declines from +40 to +25 but remains positive. EC06 improves to zero: it is neither positive nor negative. A zero must not be represented as missing, and a missing observation must not become zero.

This framing applies only to a named comparable measure. A reported gross-margin improvement does not establish an equal change in cash, EBITDA, enterprise value or common-equity value. It may also reflect composition rather than same-product price attainment. The explanation must identify what it can actually establish and the next financial bridge that is still needed.

### 3.1 Nutrien: scope-specific margin, not a commodity verdict

The manufactured-phosphate table combines fertilizer and industrial/feed products and compares Q2 2026 with Q2 2025. It reports average net selling prices of 781 and 714 and cost of goods sold of 812 and 646, in US dollars per product tonne. The table's gross margins are -31 and +68. [E01]

EC01's independently specified answer is: price change +67; cost change +166; gross-margin change -99; current level negative; direction deteriorating. EC07 verifies the signed difference. That is an exact arithmetic bridge over reported displayed precision, not a causal attribution of all cost growth to sulfur or evidence that every phosphate producer has identical economics.

**Expected concise explanation:** Reported blended phosphate realization rose, but the corresponding cost increase was larger, leaving a negative unit gross margin. The scope is this issuer's manufactured-product table and this reporting interval.

**What would change the read:** a comparable later table; actual consumed-input costs rather than only a spot quotation; changes in volume, utilization, mix and investment that reconcile to cash. A later favorable sulfur quote is a research lead until its contractual and inventory timing is known.

**Disallowed conclusion:** a buy/sell recommendation, a pure-DAP price, a complete global balance, a current September margin, or attribution of the entire 166 cost change to a single cause.

### 3.2 Weyerhaeuser: both cash views are useful, but neither erases spending

The Q2 exhibit reconciles operating cash of 399, capital spending of 139, an adjustment of 63 and adjusted FAD of 323, all in millions of US dollars. The adjustment is described in the source's facility-investment footnote. [E05]

EC23's answers are 260 after the stated total capital expenditure and 323 under the issuer-adjusted presentation. Both belong on the explanation, with different labels. EC24 refuses a request to subtract working capital again when the starting measure is already reported CFO. EC25 refuses the wrong sign convention for this deliberately specified positive-outflow-magnitude recipe; it does not rewrite a source statement's original parentheses.

**Expected concise explanation:** The business retained 260 after the stated investment spending in this quarter. The issuer's adjusted distribution measure adds back 63 of specified spending; that may illuminate the investment's purpose but does not mean cash was never paid.

**What would change the read:** subsequent collections, sustaining and expansion requirements, realized returns from the project and a comparable issuer reconciliation. A single quarter does not identify recurring distribution capacity.

### 3.3 NOVONIX: test results, acceptance, shipment and cash remain separate

### 3.4 Wheaton/Antamina: the financial right has its own economics

The April 1 closing announcement specifies an initial 33.75% payable-silver participation, a later 22.5% share after the stated delivery threshold, a 90% payable factor and ongoing payments of 20% of spot, alongside an upfront payment. [E04]

EC36 applies those dated terms to **hypothetical** output of one million ounces and a hypothetical price of 30. It calculates deliveries of 303,750 versus 202,500 and contribution before excluded costs of 7.29 million versus 4.86 million. **What would change the read:** attributable delivered volumes, contract amendments, settlement obligations, remaining thresholds and actual receipts. Contract valuation requires its own completed source and financial perimeter.

## 4. An economically relevant event needs an explicit follow-through condition

A useful Materials opportunity explanation should have six components:

1. The source-backed change, with its product/business scope and dates.
2. The business or financial claim that could gain or lose value.
3. The transmission into realization, costs, volume, investment, funding or cash.
4. Offsetting factors and missing measurements.
5. A concrete next observable condition and the horizon on which it matters.
6. The separate existing market/valuation/entry context, including an unavailable result when appropriate.

These are requirements for useful synthesis, not a requirement to wait until every uncertainty disappears. For example, a newly contracted feedstock improvement can be worth attention before it reaches reported earnings, provided the explanation identifies the lag and does not describe the benefit as already realized.

The planned early-discovery mechanism is thus a **testable, conditional economic hypothesis**, not a generic bullish sentiment tag. It needs both a supportive observation and a way the interpretation can fail.

### Three proposed channels for useful intelligence

**Economic improvement already realized.** Comparable unit profitability, saleable volume, cash or retained financial participation has changed. The job is to establish the financial scope and distinguish continuation from peak-cycle or one-off effects.

**An evidenced precursor with an explicit lag.** Contract resets, customer acceptance, improved input quality, project milestones or inventory changes may precede cash. The job is to estimate the relevant transmission under scenarios, preserve conditions and track the next milestone without promoting it prematurely.

**A divergence requiring explanation.** Commodity and issuer economics, earnings and cash, headline growth and organic activity, or published outlook and equity behavior disagree. The job is to identify whether the disagreement is genuine, a period/mix issue or a data problem.

The system should not force these into a single scalar. The initial consumer can display concise context and a next-evidence question through existing workflows. Any subsequent prioritization policy must be accepted by its current owner and evaluated separately from trading authority.

## 5. Counterexamples that test the interpretation, not just arithmetic

The reference set covers more than the first four subjects because the same mistakes recur across Materials.

**End-market units versus material use.** EC37 assumes vehicle units rise 20%, average battery size changes from60 to55, nickel-bearing chemistry share moves from60% to45%, and intensity stays at0.6. Energy rises10% while modeled material requirement falls17.5%. These are hypothetical coefficients, not a nickel forecast. EC38 compounds20% application growth with15% thrifting to obtain2% material growth; adding the percentages would be wrong.

**Reimbursement versus incremental profit.** EC39 adds the same20 to revenue and cost: contribution remains35. This is a simple pass-through scenario, not a substitute for a company's contract terms, lag or volume obligations.

**Channel shipments versus consumption.** EC40 starts with100 of inventory, receives120 and sells90. Closing inventory is130. The extra30 is not simultaneously downstream consumption. A build could precede demand or become excess stock; subsequent sell-through matters.

**Asset value versus shareholder value.** EC41 and EC42 use the same hypothetical operating value, financing gap and debt, but issue new equity at different prices. Modeled per-share values differ even though assumed asset economics do not. The scenario explicitly assumes remaining construction spending is funded and paid at the valuation date. It is not an actual issuer valuation and does not imply an attainable issuance price.

**Outlook improvement versus surprise.** EC47 has higher management-guidance midpoints but no consensus input, so the comparison with consensus is unavailable. EC48 supplies a hypothetical same-definition consensus121 against new guidance midpoint120: the guidance increased relative to earlier guidance but remains1 below that specific supplied expectation. This is named `guidance_vs_supplied_consensus`, not a realized earnings surprise.

**Economic sign versus growth percentage.** EC26 and EC27 refuse percentage-growth claims from zero or negative baselines. The explanation can still show an absolute change; refusing an unsuitable percentage does not mean refusing the research job.

## 6. Comparability is specific to the requested calculation

The example difference recipe requires known, matching scope, unit, currency, period and ownership basis. EC08–EC14 demonstrate mismatches and missing fields. In particular, two absent denominator fields do not establish a match.

This is intentionally narrower than a production comparison service. A year-over-year comparison is not forbidden merely because years differ: EC01 is explicitly a reported-blended year-over-year bridge. A current-quarter price minus half-year cost, however, is not an eligible same-period margin. The production owner must preserve the intended comparison relationship instead of using universal equality or universal permissiveness.

Conversion is a separate evidenced act. A product tonne, a contained-nutrient tonne, a short ton and a troy ounce are not labels to normalize by guesswork. The test corpus does not allocate unit IDs, estimate grades or obtain FX rates. It refuses when a required conversion or scope is missing. A later accepted native conversion must name its input, factor, validity period and rounding basis.

Precision is also two different things. EC15 preserves an exact decimal difference at the18th decimal place to catch destructive float coercion. That does not claim any real source measures an economic quantity that precisely. Source precision and calculation precision must both remain visible. Storing more digits cannot improve measurement quality.

## 7. Three evaluation layers, with different claims

### Layer A — faithful explanations

The four-company real-source proof and48 reference cases are a development set for semantic and numerical correctness. They are deliberately adversarial examples selected after reading their documents, not a representative investment sample.

For this layer, verify compatible calculations, allowed signed values, missingness, current rights, original evidence, valid identities and actual page behavior. The offline checker covers only some numerical and supplied-premise parts. Native storage, rights, source extraction, company linking and browser paths remain untested by it.

A useful annotation record should retain both the allowed explanation and prohibited inference. Two independent reviewers should resolve disputed source meaning through an adjudication record under existing review ownership. This session's labels are principal reference judgments, **not independent ground truth**.

Metrics should separate numerical errors, wrong scope, unsupported certainty, dropped caveats, incorrect history and inability to complete the user task. Do not merge these into a score that lets excellent arithmetic compensate for a leaked private source or a fabricated company identity.

### Layer B — economic forecasting

Before assessing predictive value, freeze the target, horizon, information cutoff, reference universe, exclusions, baseline and decision policy. The outcome must be distinct from the signal.

| Proposed study | Exact target to define | Earliest sensible evidence of usefulness |
|---|---|---|
| Matched margin bridge | Next comparable reported unit margin, on the same definition and perimeter | Out-of-sample error versus prior-quarter, seasonal and simpler price-only baselines |
| Commercial milestone | Customer acceptance or documented first/repeat shipments within a named horizon | Better probability calibration or discovery lead time without interpreting nondisclosure as failure |
| Cash conversion | Next defined CFO-after-identified-capex result or working-capital release | Improvement versus simple seasonal/lagged cash baselines without unsupported normalizations |
| Financial participation | Receipts and contractual share under a complete source-defined phase | Accurate threshold/settlement treatment before claiming incremental prediction |

For a numeric target, report absolute error in the original unit, coverage and performance relative to the baseline on the same observable records. Do not hide hard cases by comparing the complex method's easiest subset with the baseline's full universe. For probability targets, use a proper loss such as mean `(p-y)^2`, alongside calibration, discrimination and the number of observable outcomes. The formula is an evaluation proposal, not an executed forecast study.

A missing report or an unobserved milestone is not automatically a negative target. Preserve an unobserved/ambiguous outcome and report how much of the eligible universe could actually be scored. An explicit customer rejection is different evidence from silence. Changing the source coverage or outcome definition can change the study population and must be recorded.

### Layer C — discovery and security outcomes

Only after economic targets and useful workflow are supported should the system test whether its observations improve research discovery or security-level outcomes. Separate the economic feature's incremental contribution from existing momentum, sector exposure, valuation and selection mechanisms.

A proposed comparison should hold constant the eligible historical universe, liquidity constraints, execution timing, costs and existing policy. Compare the incumbent method with an explicitly bounded added-information version, and record all attempted variants. The displayed research view itself remains read-only; this document does not authorize replacing stock rankings, entry or sizing.

The evaluation should measure lead time, false-positive burden, missed events, useful actions a user can take, and subsequent outcomes. It must include negative and uneventful cases chosen by a rule before seeing returns. Four memorable corporate examples cannot establish an investment edge.

## 8. Historical information boundaries and leakage control

EC43 and EC44 use the same entirely hypothetical public-availability and system-recording times. A source publicly available before a cutoff but recorded later may support a reconstruction of public source history. It cannot establish what Mastermind itself knew at the cutoff. The two queries have different answers.

EC45 refuses an unknown availability time, and EC46 refuses to turn a date-only field into an invented instant for a query that requires one. A native implementation may support a conservative interval rule through its existing clock owner, but this offline utility does not invent that rule or backdate collection.

For a future evaluation, retain:

- the exact source version and original publication/availability interval;
- first system observation and recording;
- the business period and target horizon;
- the transformation/interpretation version;
- the eligible identity, listings, exposure and basket membership at the cutoff;
- later corrections as later information rather than replacements inside a historical sample.

Train/test splits must respect time and dependency. Multiple listings of an issuer, repeated reports on the same asset, and syndicated copies of the same announcement must not appear as independent supporting examples across train and test. Decide the grouping boundary before evaluation. A source-source repetition is not extra confidence.

The R12 corpus is public development material. It cannot double as a concealed evaluation set. A separate frozen holdout and prospective shadow record must be created under existing Evaluation OS ownership. Do not build a new evaluation service, queue or outcome ledger in the Materials repository folder.
