## 3. Dossier A: Nutrien manufactured-phosphate economics

### Source and company correspondence

The original August 5, 2026 issuer release identifies Nutrien Ltd. and both TSX and NYSE NTR. Its Phosphate manufactured-product table contains fertilizer and industrial/feed products. That is the relevant measurement population. The release's date/time heading does not establish a timezone for the displayed 1:00 p.m.; this worksheet therefore retains a date-level publication observation, not an invented exact instant. [S1]

R13 independently supplies the NYSE candidate: `co:us:NTR` -> `ISS:US-XNYS-NTR` / `SEC:US-XNYS-NTR` / `US-XNYS-NTR`, with CIK `0001725964`. This is existing identity evidence, not a freshly allocated ID. The corporate-page declaration supports the proposed correspondence; a native reviewed source-to-issuer relationship is still required before production use. [I1]

### Proposed primary card

**Phosphate: higher realization, weaker retained unit margin**

On the stated manufactured-product basis, realization rose by $67 per tonne while COGS rose by $166. Gross margin moved from +$68 to -$31 per tonne. The explanation concerns this product population, not the whole company's cash flow or a pure DAP benchmark. [S1]

The detailed panel keeps reported rows separate from calculations:

| Q2 comparison, USD/product tonne | 2025 comparative | 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average net selling price | 714 | 781 |
| Cost of goods sold | 646 | 812 |
| Reported gross margin | 68 | -31 |

The local reference bridge checks price less COGS against each reported margin, then derives an absolute change of -99. It intentionally does not manufacture a percentage growth rate across a change in sign. The table values retain their source display precision; exact decimal transport does not make the economic measurements more precise.

### Useful interpretation and change conditions

The investor question is what could turn price strength into a better retained unit margin. The next evidence should be another comparable realization/cost result, supported by consumed-input information and operating context. A lower spot input quote can be relevant without proving that purchased inventory or recognized costs have already improved.

An explanation may discuss that question without ranking the stock. It must not extrapolate the single product table across the issuer, assume its entire business is unprofitable, infer an exposure percentage, or turn a calculation into source-attributed causal certainty.

No current market forecast, entry setup or valuation result is generated here. The record is explicitly an analysis of a dated disclosure.

## 4. Dossier B: Weyerhaeuser cash and an adjusted distribution measure

### Source and company correspondence

The July 30, 2026 exhibit names Weyerhaeuser Company and NYSE WY. Its SEC directory CIK 106535 agrees with the R13 resolved issuer axis. The selected cash table is the consolidated company reconciliation, not a Timberlands-segment statement. [S2, I1]

The observed chain is `co:us:WY` -> `ISS:US-XNYS-WY` / `SEC:US-XNYS-WY` / `US-XNYS-WY`. Neither that chain nor the matching SEC directory constitutes a native curation-admission receipt. [I1]

### Proposed primary card

**Cash: distinguish investment spending from the adjusted distribution measure**

Q2 operating cash of $399 million, less the listed $139 million capital expenditure, leaves $260 million. The issuer's $63 million adjustment produces $323 million Adjusted FAD. Its footnote links that adjustment to specified facility spending. [S2]

The detailed panel preserves two different answers:

| Question | Local reconciliation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| What remains after the listed capital spending? | 399 + (-139) = 260 | Explicitly defined research residual; not an issuer-named universal free-cash-flow metric |
| How does the issuer arrive at Adjusted FAD? | 399 + (-139) + 63 = 323 | Reconciliation of the issuer's named presentation, not reversal of the actual expenditure |

The expenditure is stored as a signed cash-flow contribution, matching the parentheses in the source table. The calculation must not subtract that negative number again.

### Useful interpretation and change conditions

The research should explain why the company separates the spending, what future operating benefit might result, and what spending remains. The adjustment may be analytically useful; that does not make the cash available twice or turn one quarter into a sustainable distribution rate.

The source link is company-level, but this does not make the result a measurement of timber economics alone. The proper next observations are actual subsequent cash, remaining investment, and operating evidence for the investment's contribution. A positive project narrative is not a replacement for those outcomes.

The card is not a dividend recommendation, current valuation or forecast. It provides a coherent basis for further company research.

## 8. Historical meaning and correction behavior

These 2026 disclosures include older comparatives. A 2025 number in an August 2026 document is evidence from the 2026 publication, not proof that the identical value was available in 2025. A source-date cutoff and a system-recording cutoff answer different questions.

R13's actual Nutrien example remains decisive: its record known at the August 20 cutoff was unresolved, while a later historical-name computation using September inputs resolved the older date. This is the documented identity function's behavior, not system-known-at replay. The worksheet carries the R13 evidence reference; it does not repeat the native call.

Three kinds of change need separate handling. A corrected table value invalidates dependent calculations and explanations. A new unrelated announcement adds a proposition without rewriting the table. A rights restriction affects access without claiming the source event never happened. None should become an automatic stock-direction change.

The proposed acceptance checks also require a changed identity version to requalify company navigation independently of a valid financial subtraction. Arithmetic correctness cannot certify identity or permission.
