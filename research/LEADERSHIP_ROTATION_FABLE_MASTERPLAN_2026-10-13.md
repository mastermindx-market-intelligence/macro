## 7. Scientific ruler: finite and future-only

The core trap is rolling-window carry. A trailing return can improve because an old loss exits the window even if all new returns are zero. A target retaining those known observations cannot establish future prediction. Define the outcome entirely after the actual decision/confirmation origin, on fixed target sessions. If confirmation takes c sessions and the future target is h sessions, maturity is c+h from the earlier trigger; there is no free earlier fill.

Select a small eligible subset of twelve proposed questions, not an unlimited hypothesis hunt. Before outcome access freeze source/use eligibility, formation/cohort rules, information cutoffs, target and primary horizon, model/transform family, baselines, independent units, joint practical margins/power, finite look budget, contamination and sealed outcome custody. Current motivating incidents stay inspected history. A no-blank plan with invented rights/cohort values is not an executable protocol.

Required comparisons: fixed current-strength/simple momentum, simple trend/volatility, breadth level versus change, existing eligible detector, same-information unpaired receiver/donor facts, fixed multiscale/consensus where relevant, delayed-confirmation control and zero-new-return carry placebo. Compare identical groups/origins/targets; unmatched coverage must be reported, not turned into a favorable paired metric.

Separate early-event delay/misses/alarm burden, future-only continuation/false starts, calibrated probability/ranking where actually claimed, and costed economic utility. Report all-event and supported denominators. Repeated windows, shared stocks and multiple target horizons are not independent episodes. Preserve time/common-shock dependence, multiplicity, concentration and era sensitivity. A favorable subset cannot rescue a failed frozen practical condition.

Explicit donor–receiver pairing must add information beyond the separate legs and shared factor exposures. Even a useful lagged association does not prove investors moved capital between those groups. Independent issuer flow claims are not created from prices. Regional transfer requires native calendars/currency/groups and separate evidence; do not inherit US calibration.

Use the existing research/evaluation owner and retain all trials. No new model was fit, no market backtest ran and no protected outcomes were opened in this planning assessment. A negative result can close an accepted finite question, not a promised working predictor.
