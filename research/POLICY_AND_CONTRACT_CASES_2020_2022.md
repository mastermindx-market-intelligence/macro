## BOJ yield-band and policy-rate distinction

Cutoff: 2022-12-20 (date only). Dependence group: japan-ycc-december-2022.

### Supplied facts

The December 20, 2022 BOJ statement retains minus 0.1% for Policy-Rate Balances and a target around zero for 10-year JGB yields.
The permitted 10-year yield fluctuation range expands from about plus/minus 0.25 percentage point to plus/minus 0.5 percentage point. The Bank offers daily fixed-rate purchases at 0.5%, unless bids are highly unlikely.
The announced monthly JGB purchases rise from 7.3 trillion yen to about 9 trillion yen. The statement describes the change as improving market functioning while maintaining accommodation.

### Root adjudication (judge only)

The upper edge rises 25 basis points; the full interval widens from 50 to 100 basis points. Neither is a 50-basis-point short-rate hike or a new 0.5% central target. Announced purchases are about 1.7 trillion yen higher per month, not an observed purchase total. A stated objective does not establish realized effects, exchange-rate direction or a trade. No later policy change is supplied.

Source: [official release](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2022/k221220a.htm).

## ECB rate-level, effective-date and instrument distinction

Cutoff: 2022-07-21 (date only). Dependence group: euro-area-policy-july-2022.

### Supplied facts

On July 21, 2022 the ECB announces 50-basis-point increases in its three key rates, effective July 27: main refinancing 0.50%, marginal lending 0.75%, and deposit facility 0.00%.
The release describes future rate decisions as meeting-by-meeting and data-dependent.
The Transmission Protection Instrument is approved and can be activated against unwarranted, disorderly dynamics threatening policy transmission. Its purchase scale depends on those risks and has no ex-ante limit.

### Root adjudication (judge only)

Fifty basis points means 0.50 percentage point, with distinct resulting levels for three facilities. The announced changes take effect July 27, not automatically July 21. A zero deposit rate is not a zero rate for every ECB operation or every borrower. Instrument approval is not observed activation or purchases; no ex-ante cap does not guarantee unlimited realized buying. The future rate path and market returns are not supplied.

Source: [official release](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2022/html/ecb.mp220721~53e5bdd317.en.html).

## BOE financial-stability purchases and monetary-policy distinction

Cutoff: 2022-09-28 (date only). Dependence group: uk-gilt-dysfunction-september-2022.

### Supplied facts

On September 28, 2022 the BOE announces temporary, targeted purchases of long-dated UK government bonds to restore market functioning, with HM Treasury indemnification.
Auctions are planned from September 28 through October 14; purchases will be unwound once risks to market functioning have subsided. Scale is described as whatever is necessary for that objective.
The annual 80-billion-pound stock-reduction target remains unchanged, while the start of gilt sales is postponed to October 31. The MPC will assess developments at its next meeting and can change rates as needed for its inflation remit.

### Root adjudication (judge only)

Temporary financial-stability purchases and a postponed sales start do not by themselves announce a policy-rate cut or abandon the annual stock-reduction target. The open scale statement is constrained by purpose and the stated auction window, not perpetual buying. October 14 is the planned end of purchase auctions, not a promised date by which every holding is sold. The release supplies plans, not proof of subsequent execution, solved dysfunction or returns.

Source: [official release](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/news/2022/september/bank-of-england-announces-gilt-market-operation).

## WTI contract, delivery and storage-availability distinction

Cutoff: 2020-04-27 (date only). Dependence group: oil-delivery-dislocation-april-2020.

### Supplied facts

An EIA article published April 27, 2020 reports that May-delivery WTI futures traded as low as minus $40.32 per barrel around 2:30 p.m. ET on April 20. June-delivery Brent closed at positive $19.33 per barrel on April 21; longer-dated WTI prices were also positive.
WTI contracts held through expiration require delivery arrangements at Cushing unless other arrangements were made in advance.
For April 17, the article reports 76 million barrels of working storage capacity and about 58 million barrels in tanks, with another roughly 2 million in transit. Physically unfilled capacity may already be leased or committed.

### Root adjudication (judge only)

The negative observation is an intraday price for a specified WTI contract, not a quoted settlement or a universal oil price. The positive Brent comparison differs in benchmark, delivery month and day. Eighteen million barrels of arithmetic tank space does not establish that an unprepared trader could rent it. Physical emptiness and commercial availability differ. This April 27 retrospective cannot be represented as a proved April 20 decision-time input, and supplies no forecast or trade.

Source: [official release](https://www.eia.gov/TODAYINENERGY/detail.php?id=43495).
