This stage tests your proposed **two-day RSI-MACD bullish crossover with a daily StochRSI bottom within the larger W/2W StochRSI washout**. Earlier V11–V27 studies were preserved; no other indicator species, price vendor or 2W phase was chosen by looking at favorable returns.

## Findings

- **V28:** At a fixed two-day crossover with W+2W washout, 14/14 core-five cases with recent daily Stoch K<=20 within five completed sessions had positive 126-owned-session outcomes; nevertheless six hit a −5% stop before reaching a +8% target. Nine of the fourteen already had daily K>70 at 2D entry.
- **V29:** Keep rejected signals; check 20/63/126 session paths and 63/126 session embargo. Core accepted-versus-rejected advantage has an exploratory block interval crossing zero, and cross-sectional stock-selection outperformance is not better.
- **V30:** Hold identical 51 complete core master washout episodes. First2D signal averages +9.29% all-watch return; first independent daily Stoch +9.40%; strict daily-then2D condition just +3.89% because it accepts fifteen and skips thirty-six episodes. The accepted group is not the whole opportunity set.
- **V31:** Reuse the accepted V12 one-position-per-stock account executor (54 matching accepted account outcomes reconcile within 2.22e-16). Core SPY/cash-free four-policy endpoint accounts range 4.45% to 8.15% CAGR, below same-five buy-and-hold 14.25%.
- **V32:** With audited SPY reserve while waiting, core first2D ~16.66% CAGR and daily Stoch ~16.40% versus strict daily then2D ~15.30%. This is *historical*, factor-exposed and not selection-adjusted.
- **V33:** Paired differences (daily Stoch minus2D) average +0.11pp across51 core original watches and +1.85pp across35 watches with 126-session spacing; exploratory intervals include zero. Strict daily then2D has -5.39pp versus first2D all watches and -3.21pp with spacing; intervals remain negative, but the whole historical program has already been searched.

The most useful distinction is that **recent oscillator confluence does not guarantee good entry timing or a safe stop path**, and all-watch coverage matters more than an accepted-trade hit rate alone. See [full report](./RESEARCH_V28_V33.md).
