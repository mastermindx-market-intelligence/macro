## Why this change is needed

International Markets should explain each country's economic condition, projected path, policy choices and market implications immediately. This change delivers a country intelligence cockpit with country-specific analysis for seven economies, in English/Chinese, light/dark, desktop/mobile. Comparison, history and saved research remain secondary tools.

The experience connects a concise national thesis to attributed growth and inflation paths, historical context, policy decisions, fiscal/external conditions, independently dated equity/currency evidence, transmission channels and conditions that would change the interpretation.
