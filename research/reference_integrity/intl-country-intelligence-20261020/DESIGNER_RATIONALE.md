# Designer rationale — revealed only after frozen first-pass findings

The primary job is to help a reader understand a country: what is happening in its economy and markets, what the published economic path says, and which conditions could change that path. The user's direction makes comparison, saved research and alert mechanics secondary. The design therefore starts with interpretation and supporting evidence.

## Hierarchy and reading sequence

The world brief establishes the common backdrop. Seven country summaries then show a thesis, a two-period growth path and a dated equity state. One country opens into a consistent sequence: official outlook, policy decision, fiscal/external position, latest observations, market conditions, transmission and conditions to watch. The same structure lets a reader build knowledge without learning a different interface for each country.

The forecast plot distinguishes published history from projection. The growth values, inflation path, unit conventions, source release and information cutoff stay with the claim. Country-specific fiscal-year conventions and differing equity geography are stated where they affect interpretation. The short thesis does not replace its sources; the evidence disclosure provides the deeper record.

The separate stronger and weaker paths are conditional research statements. They are not predicted asset returns, probabilities, portfolio recommendations or measured causal effects. Global connections likewise names demand, price and financing channels to examine, with country links where coverage exists.

## Retaining research tools

The existing workspace and its charts, inspectors, calculation controls, scenario arithmetic, historical qualifications, library and larger research boards remain in the source. They move behind Data & tools. Existing view URLs and section fragments open the required disclosures. The stock route remains separate.

This choice has a cost. A user who came for the old aggregate regime boards or full tables now opens an outer disclosure, and the initial page gives those boards less prominence. That task is explicitly marked WORSE in the proposal. It must be adjudicated as a hierarchy tradeoff against the user's stated priority; the additional action is not denied or hidden by the fact that production currently has resource failures. The tools remain available for readers who need them, without occupying the primary country-analysis reading path.

## Data boundaries and constraints

The main country assessments combine separately dated sources. An official economic projection, a national central-bank decision, a monthly policy-series cross-check, an equity observation and an FX receipt can have different clocks. The interface must not collapse those into one universal “live” claim.

The complete review render uses an unchanged ordinary builder and a read-only copy of existing production stores. The copy is not a new acquisition. Existing FX observations without qualified producer receipts therefore appear unavailable. Normal full-history acquisition after code integration is a release obligation. Retained Macro, History and chart sections can also show their pre-existing unavailable input states. A mounted panel is not evidence that every underlying series is supplied. The new national policy decision is not silently substituted into an older tool's different policy-series contract.

Forecast archives preserve their original acquisition lineage. A correction does not retroactively make the corrected record known earlier. Market-state labels describe the observed window; they are not forecasts of returns. Some countries have additional independent views and some do not; missing evidence is not invented to make the cards look equally complete.

## Visual decisions and acknowledged limits

The shared navigation and Mastermind identity remain. Neutral surfaces, narrow rules, aligned numeric columns and semantic market colors separate interpretation, official figures and evidence. The country summaries and charts compress the initial scan; detailed prose remains available within the open assessment. English and Chinese use the same semantic structure, with wrapping adapted for narrow widths.

An expanded assessment is still substantial on a phone. This is research content, and the design accepts vertical reading while exposing the country and section structure early. That density merits criticism where a shorter expression can retain the same meaning. The design should be assessed for scanability and usefulness, not accepted because it has more information.

The actual production baseline has failed public workspace assets. Its 13,888 visible English token count is not a fair measure of a normally working old card layout, and it must not become an advertised percentage-compression claim. The source capability ledger, real before images and explicit hierarchy tradeoff are all part of the comparison.

Approval of this reference does not prove deployment. Hosted integration checks, normal governed acquisition, covering render, updater adoption and actual served browser journeys still have to succeed. No earlier reviewer conclusion is offered as a reason to disregard a fresh criticism.
