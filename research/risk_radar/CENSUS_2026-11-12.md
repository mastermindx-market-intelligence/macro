## 1. What it computes (engine/risk_radar.py, "Risk Radar v2")

- **16 legs → 7 scare types.** Each leg is a trailing 504-session causal percentile (`:52`, `:675-678`).
  - Tier A (can originate a state): **credit** (HY OAS 21d change 0.85, HYG/TLT 20d 0.15),
    **rates** (MOVE level 0.80, 10y real-rate 5d change 0.20), **bubble** (SPY distance above
    200dma 0.85, SMH/SPY 63d 0.15), **growth** (XLU/SPY 0.50, XLY/XLP 0.50) — `:173-177`.
  - Tier B (can only escalate a Tier-A state by one step): **vol** (VIX term, put/call, GEX,
    COR1M floor-break), **global** (country-ETF breadth 0.7, JPY carry 0.3), **internals**
    (new-high contraction, AI-vs-rest breadth divergence) — `:183-204`.
- **Sub-score** per scare = weight-renormalised mean of available leg percentiles × 100 (`:813-856`).
- **State ladder** calm / watch / caution / elevated / risk-off on bands **55 / 68 / 78 / 88**
  (`:224-225`). Max Tier-A band → "armed + confirm" conjunction (a validated leg ≥ its 0.90
  threshold inside a hot scare **and** ≥ 2 Tier-A scares ≥ watch) → +1 step; Tier-B can add +1 →
  **context gate** caps the state at caution unless SPY < 200dma **and** %>200dma breadth is in
  its bottom 40% (`:866-885`, `:962-1050`). Alert = state ≥ elevated (`:1199`).
- **Displayed probability** = lookup table `_PROB_CAL[state][h5|h10|h21]` + 1.2/2.0/3.0 pp per extra
  hot Tier-A scare, cap 0.95; target is a ≥5% SPY pullback (`:234-243`, `:1306-1349`).
- **Authority**: "binding" only if loud **and** the state's own lift > base **and** a confirmed
  validated Tier-A leg is firing; otherwise "advisory" (`:1358-1410`). Gross multipliers come from
  `engine/regime_one.RISK_STATE_GROSS` (`:249-256`).
- **Validation bar for a leg**: `lift_2020 >= 1.20` (`:160-166`). Only `credit_oas_roc` is marked
  era-robust (`:102`).
