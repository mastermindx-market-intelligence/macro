## Analytical contract

The first build provides conditional research scenarios, not forecast probabilities. Each scenario records asset/universe, observation cutoff, horizon, expiry, enabling condition, proposed sequence, confirmation, invalidation, supporting and opposing evidence, and data limitations. “This becomes plausible if X occurs” is distinct from “X is likely to occur.”

Technical levels may be deterministic display context from an explicitly dated, contiguous trailing close range. They are not support/resistance certainty, trade orders, intraday highs/lows, or the official model's invalidation level. Never bridge a missing day; reject nonfinite/nonpositive prices, future dates, unordered/duplicate dates, mismatched arrays and invalid source contracts. Record the exact lookback and basis in evidence. No current observation leaks into a prior-range threshold.

Evidence is grouped by price structure, demand/capital, derivatives, holders/network, macro and participation. Related indicators do not become independent votes. Do not create a fused confidence or probability from those families. “No clear advantage” and “Evidence incomplete” are first-class states.

The btc.decision/v1 record is the sole canonical model authority. Preserve its identity, effective time and integrity. The existing H5 contract can project the model crypto envelope and its admitted split; this redesign does not reinterpret or recompute that scope. A saved hypothetical scenario does not change the model.

Temporal policy: source observation times, release availability times and generation times are distinct. Mixed clocks remain visible at the relevant claim. Daily context must not be labeled Live. Stale flows cannot confirm a new setup; null funding cannot become neutral leverage; measured zero is a real value. Broad market breadth stays unavailable below the accepted eligibility floor.

## Research expansion

Research basis:
- IMF, The Crypto Cycle and US Monetary Policy (2023): https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2023/08/04/the-crypto-cycle-and-us-monetary-policy-534834
- BIS, Crypto carry (working paper 1087): https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry
- Coinbase, Advanced Trade API: https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/coinbase-app/advanced-trade-apis/overview

The IMF study motivates testing the monetary-policy/risk-taking channel, not assuming a fixed contemporary coefficient. The BIS study motivates venue-specific carry, margin and positioning research, not promoting funding into a crash probability. Exchange interfaces require current venue/product-specific contracts; spot and perpetual markets must not be silently spliced.

Next analytical waves:
1. Point-in-time evidence capture and event-time replay: price/volume, source-specific spot demand, ETF subscriptions, stablecoin issuance/redemptions, futures basis, perpetual funding, open interest, liquidation stress, holder cost basis and macro surprises.
2. Conditional mechanism research: rates/dollar/liquidity to risk appetite to crypto demand; price/spot demand versus leverage; issuance versus deployed capital; holder/miner supply versus absorption; breadth/leadership persistence. Distinguish shared causes from independent corroboration.
3. Multi-horizon candidate models: tactical 1–3 days, swing 1–4 weeks, regime 1–3 months, with horizon-specific features and labels. Calibrate independently; an interesting macro story is not an intraday forecast.
4. Historical review: point-in-time universe, delistings, event availability lags, revision-aware macro vintages, distinct episodes, simple baselines, walk-forward evaluation, embargo/purging for overlapping labels, calibration and proper scoring, false-alarm cost, lead time and stability by regime. Show counterexamples alongside matches.
5. Live shadow ledger before promotion: freeze issue-time scenarios, expiry/invalidation, data state, outcomes and user alert load. No retrospective edits to improve a hit rate. The accepted R6–R12 studies do not establish a promoted live edge.

## Meaningful velocity

Velocity means a material state transition, acceleration, proximity to a dated condition or an upcoming catalyst—not a faster blinking number. Capture observations continuously where admitted, then evaluate each family at its native cadence. Deduplicate by asset + condition + horizon + scenario revision, add hysteresis/rearm rules, expire old scenarios, and preserve sequence gaps as degraded states. Do not relabel current delayed snapshots as streaming.
