## 0. Executive ruling

The next Prophet science program is **not another universal score** and is **not a relaxation of the incumbent technical vetoes**. It is a same-platform, multi-sleeve research program designed to answer three separate questions:

1. **Discovery:** did we surface the eventual leader early enough?
2. **Selection:** among lawfully visible candidates, which opportunities deserve research priority for a named sleeve and horizon?
3. **Availability:** is that opportunity actually enterable now under deterministic B4 facts?

Discovery maximizes lawful recall; Selection orders research attention; B4 alone decides present entry truth. No model, LLM narrative, theme excitement or historical outcome can waive B4.

The user journey remains:

`market/regime -> strengthening group -> appropriate member -> usable entry or explicit wait -> maintained thesis/invalidation -> honestly measured outcome`.

## 3. Discovery architecture: doors, not dilution

The incumbent miss audit shows that a single confluence/washout-resumption family cannot be treated as the complete discovery plane. The repair is an **independently measured expert union**. Each expert emits a typed observation with its own owner, clocks, population, missingness and scientific status. Experts may increase discovery recall; they do not enlarge B4 authority.

### D1 — Group leadership / industry momentum

**Hypothesis:** cross-sectional strength is easier to detect when common industry/group information is separated from idiosyncratic noise; strengthening-group state should therefore be an explicit discovery door, followed by member selection inside the group.

Primary research reference: Moskowitz & Grinblatt's industry-momentum result, summarized and decomposed in Dai et al., *Reversals and the Returns to Liquidity Provision* (NBER w30917): https://www.nber.org/papers/w30917

Owner reuse: Sector Intelligence, GMI/ThemeState, existing relative-strength/breadth data. No new sector truth store.

### D2 — Price-location / persistent leadership

**Hypothesis:** proximity to a prior high and persistent relative strength can identify leadership that a washout/reclaim door structurally misses, but must be measured separately from industry momentum and from present entry state.

Primary research reference: George & Hwang, *The 52-Week High and Momentum Investing*: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00695.x

This is a discovery/selection feature candidate, **not** permission to chase extended securities. B4 may still emit `RAN_DONT_CHASE` or `WAIT_PULLBACK`.

### D3 — Fundamental / earnings momentum

**Hypothesis:** earnings surprise and related fundamental change carry information not safely reducible to price momentum and may provide a more stable discovery species for the Quality Earnings sleeve and a corroborating expert for Early Leadership.

Primary references:

- Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok, *Momentum Strategies* (NBER w5375): https://www.nber.org/papers/w5375
- Novy-Marx, *Fundamentally, Momentum is Fundamental Momentum* (NBER w20984): https://www.nber.org/papers/w20984
- DellaVigna & Pollet, *Investor Inattention, Firm Reaction, and Friday Earnings Announcements* (NBER w11683): https://www.nber.org/papers/w11683

Owner reuse: Earnings Intelligence OS and correction-safe episode evidence. No second earnings parser or consensus store.

### D4 — Economic-link / propagation momentum

**Hypothesis:** economically linked firms and theme/supply-chain neighbors can react with delay to information, creating a propagation discovery door distinct from same-name price momentum.

Primary references:

- Cohen & Frazzini, *Economic Links and Predictable Returns*: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01379.x
- Ali & Hirshleifer, *Shared Analyst Coverage: Unifying Momentum Spillover Effects* (NBER w25201): https://www.nber.org/papers/w25201

Owner reuse: Theme Graph / Market Ontology relationship edges and source clocks. Graph centrality alone receives no recommendation authority.

### D5 — Dislocation / liquidity-provision reversal

**Hypothesis:** a short-horizon reversal door should be modeled as a different species from momentum. Industry-relative residual moves, volatility and turnover can help distinguish liquidity provision from simply buying weak groups.

Primary reference: Dai, Medhat, Novy-Marx & Rizova, *Reversals and the Returns to Liquidity Provision* (NBER w30917): https://www.nber.org/papers/w30917

This door belongs to Catalyst/Dislocation and Range/Reversion research. It must not contaminate the Early Leadership score or revive a generic oversold rule.

### D6 — Context-conditioned momentum risk

**Hypothesis:** market context can change the risk of a momentum expression, but broad regime routing is not licensed. Only preregistered mechanism-by-context interactions with adequate effective N may later influence sleeve eligibility or abstention.

Primary reference: Daniel & Moskowitz, *Momentum Crashes* (NBER w20439): https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439

This is an evaluation/context hypothesis, not a regime scorecard.

## 4. Multi-head research target

Do not train one opaque return score. The challenger architecture carries four separate heads over one point-in-time feature vector:

1. **Selection** — probability/ordering of favorable sleeve-specific outcome at the registered horizon;
2. **Asymmetry** — upside-vs-downside path distribution, including MFE/MAE and target-before-invalidation ordering;
3. **Fragility** — probability of invalidation, tail loss, gap/liquidity failure or thesis break;
4. **Confidence** — data completeness, evidence agreement, calibration support and epistemic uncertainty.

The four heads may disagree. Product priority is a transparent policy over those heads plus deterministic Availability; it is not a fifth learned fused score.

Minimum baselines remain simpler than the challenger:

- current V3 priority/control;
- raw price momentum / relative strength;
- industry-relative momentum;
- 52-week-high proximity;
- earnings/fundamental momentum where lawful;
- equal-weight expert union;
- within-sleeve linear/logistic and tree baselines before any neural/listwise challenger.

A more complex model must beat these same-tape baselines after costs and calibration, not merely fit the historical sample.

## 6. Discovery and ranking labels

### 6.1 Discovery label

A discovery expert is judged on whether it surfaced a future strong outcome **before too much of the eventual move was consumed**, not on whether it happened to be `ENTRY_OPEN` that day.

For each registered sleeve/horizon, record:

- first lawful expert observation time;
- first candidate-episode visibility time;
- first B4-evaluable time;
- fraction of eventual registered-horizon favorable excursion already consumed at each timestamp;
- subsequent MFE, MAE and benchmark/sector excess;
- whether invalidation occurred before favorable excursion;
- whether the observation was unusable because of stale/missing/rights/basis facts.

No per-name winner audition. Thresholds are fixed before reading the prospective cohort.

### 6.2 Selection labels

Selection research uses registered fixed horizons plus path-aware labels:

- 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 sessions for tactical sleeves where applicable;
- sleeve-specific longer horizons for Quality Earnings and Cycle Capture;
- benchmark- and group-relative return;
- net return after measured-or-conservative cost floor;
- MFE / MAE;
- target-before-invalidation ordering;
- time-to-positive and time-to-invalidation;
- thesis survival / correction state.

Off-horizon verdicts remain forbidden.

## 7. Evaluation scorecard

### Discovery

- recall of registered future-strong outcomes;
- recall before 10%, 25% and 50% of eventual favorable excursion is consumed;
- median lead time in sessions;
- incremental recall over the incumbent door;
- union overlap/Jaccard by expert pair;
- false-observation burden per 100 eligible names;
- coverage and missingness by date, sector, market-cap and liquidity cohort.

### Selection

- rank IC and confidence interval by date;
- P@K and recall@K against within-date baselines;
- NDCG/listwise utility where labels support it;
- top-minus-bottom and monotonicity by score bucket;
- benchmark/group excess;
- calibration/Brier or log loss for probabilistic heads;
- MFE/MAE and invalidation ordering;
- net results after decision-time costs;
- stability across eras, groups and liquidity cohorts.

### Scientific power and multiple testing

- report both raw N and clustered/effective N;
- date/episode clustering is explicit;
- preregister the finite hypothesis family before outcome reads;
- no threshold mining on named winners;
- hold out a final untouched temporal block and then require prospective accrual for promotion;
- any interaction/routing claim needs its own effective-N floor and cannot inherit significance from the parent expert;
- null/negative experts remain in the registry as controls rather than being silently deleted.

## 8. Frozen first hypothesis family

The first discovery study is intentionally finite:

- **H1:** group/industry leadership adds early future-leader recall versus incumbent technical discovery without widening current candidate-admission authority;
- **H2:** 52-week-high / persistent-leadership location adds incremental recall or within-group selection information beyond H1;
- **H3:** earnings/fundamental momentum adds independent selection information beyond price/group momentum for the Quality Earnings sleeve and as a corroborating Early Leadership expert;
- **H4:** economic-link/theme propagation adds incremental early recall for newly strengthening groups/members beyond same-name and same-industry momentum;
- **H5:** industry-relative dislocation/reversal is a separate short-horizon species whose value should disappear or invert when mixed into the momentum sleeve;
- **H6:** context modifies the reliability/risk of a specific expert only where a preregistered interaction clears power, OOS and prospective gates; no broad regime router is tested.

These hypotheses can fail. Failure does not trigger threshold retuning in the same cohort.
