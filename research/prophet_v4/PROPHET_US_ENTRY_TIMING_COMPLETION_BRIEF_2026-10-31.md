The inherited populations, claim boundaries, accepted evidence, source ownership and registered scientific looks travel with the work.

**Success for the user.** Open Today, find an early candidate even when it is absent from the featured cards, understand why it surfaced and what would falsify the thesis, and see whether a new entry is open for a specific strategy *now*. On return, recover the original judgment and its changes. **Success for the machine.** Reproduce the user-visible state from the same entitled source generation at the recorded cut, reconcile every candidate to a disposition, and measure earlier executable entries and their cost on held-out episodes. A scientifically sound rejection is a completed experiment. It does not establish the requested performance improvement.

## Scientific audit and research design

**Measure three delays separately.** For every supported episode, identify the first source-knowable observation, first persisted discovery, first honest user visibility, first policy-authorized entry, and first executable opportunity under the declared execution model. A later portfolio fill, if independently observed by its owner, is a sixth fact. Attribute lateness to the interval that actually lengthened. Keep unknown ends of intervals null. A price trough is an after-the-fact diagnostic reference.

## Product and engineering design

### One opportunity, viewed across time

A researcher opens **Today** after the close and sees that a group has strengthened. The row names the strategy, the new evidence, the stock’s present availability and the time of that determination. A promising stock can be too extended to enter. Opening **Setup** shows what caused the first observation, the strongest contrary fact, the current price basis, the target or invalidation relation when supplied by the owner, and the next event that could change the judgment. **All Candidates** explains why a visible observation did or did not progress. **Model Plans** links to the native plan or explains its absence. A saved watch returns to the same episode, original decision and later revisions. No new portfolio, watchlist or alert-delivery owner is needed.
