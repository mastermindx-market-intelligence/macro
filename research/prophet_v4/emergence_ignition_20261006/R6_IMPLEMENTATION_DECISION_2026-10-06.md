## 1. Decision

**Build the descriptive B03 capability now, through the existing owner. Preserve the frozen emergence and ignition hypotheses for source-qualified future accrual. Do not promote a predictive score, a convergence-based entry permission, or a leadership forecast.**

The evidence supports a concrete product need: an early source observation can be useful to preserve while entry is closed, even when its predictive value is unknown. The historical study does not establish that a count of the current board flags measures independent, point-in-time-valid convergence. It also does not establish incremental predictive power beyond the incumbent technical state and selection process.

There is one material correction to the inherited implementation shape. The current `candidate_pool` is lossless over the **cascade-eligible pool**, not over all `watch[]` observations. Only 3 of 21 reconstructed first emergence baselines appear in that same-snapshot pool; the early NVDA, FORM, and PWR observations are outside it. A projector that reads only that pool would miss the cases it is intended to preserve. This finding changes the required source composition, not the B1 anchor law.

The existing B03 composition must therefore expose the eligible-pool read model **and the already-existing relevant watch/candidate observations**, with exact source references and honest eligibility state. Do not enlarge cascade eligibility, synthesize a B1 episode, alter ranking, or make an unanchored observation masquerade as a canonical episode merely to display it. ## 3. B03's minimum truthful product contract

### 3.1 Preserve the observation before requiring an episode

B03 should consume the existing source-owner observations through its existing all-candidate/searchable-field mandate. Where the current pool excludes a watch observation, preserve a source reference to that observation in the B03 composition. The minimum reference is the existing source owner and artifact/generation identity, immutable revision/digest, and native observation/row reference. A row locator in an exact source snapshot is a locator, not a new economic identity.

The read model should preserve:

* first preserved observation time and current source version, with the distinction between source event time and system first observation;
* the exact source evidence references and their direction, active/stale/unknown state, and independent-origin qualification;
* current technical state, provisional/freshness status, and the incumbent Entry Availability reference;
* candidate lane, admission/suppression status, and reason without changing them;
* canonical B1 relation where the owner proves one, and a reasoned missing-relation state otherwise;
* later confirmation, disappearance, and expiry only when supported by the corresponding source/owner receipt.

Do not reset first-seen on each nightly echo. Do not infer continuity across security changes or unrelated episodes from the ticker string. Use the existing owner relation to maintain continuity when available; retain unresolved observations without fabricating that relation when it is absent.

### 3.2 Identity absence has several meanings

The current cohort contains six retrospective exact B1 associations, two identity-unresolved cases, and thirteen cases without an exact source relation. The archive cannot certify that those six IDs were known at the original historical decision cuts. It provides **zero proven `NOT_YET_ANCHORED` classifications** for the other fifteen observations. An unmatched archive search is not a source-owner assertion that a structural anchor did not exist.

The display/evaluator must preserve the owner-supplied distinction among a present canonical relation, proven not-yet-anchored state, unresolved security/issuer identity, and unavailable source relation. Reuse the owner vocabulary; these descriptive categories do not establish a new lifecycle schema. Exact source observations remain inspectable while relation-dependent metrics remain unavailable.

This supports the R6 B02 ruling that the first B03/B04 versions do not require another anchor species. It does **not** support conditioning the entire emergence study on B1 presence: only 6/21 have a recoverable exact association today, and even that is retrospective. Report B1 coverage and later mapping as outcomes. Do not drop the harder-to-map early observations from the primary denominator without an explicitly separate, narrower estimand.

### 3.3 Explain attention without turning it into an entry instruction

Use a descriptive label whose explanation reflects qualified source facts, such as research attention with entry still closed. If independence is unknown, expose the source facts and the uncertainty; do not show a confident two-family convergence badge merely because two legacy booleans are true. A provisional technical state must remain provisional. Entry Availability continues to come exclusively from its existing owner.

B03 has no authority to promote the name, reserve a plan slot, move the chase ceiling, relax a stale source, change sizing, or convert a historical observation into a current recommendation. Those are existing owner decisions and were outside this commission.

### 5.3 A bounded schedule, not a search-until-significant rule

For implementation planning, this report proposes **two consecutive 60 scheduled US trading-session enrollment blocks beginning at S0**. Lock both blocks before outcomes are read. Publish source-coverage/clock health during enrollment; do not use outcome peeks to redefine exposure or choose the better horizon. The primary comparative read occurs after both blocks' primary outcomes mature, with H21 and H20 observed-board follow-up clearly marked mature or censored. This schedule is a proposed execution detail for owner acceptance, not an already-adopted new preregistration or a promotion threshold.

At that read, report the effect and uncertainty separately in each block and pooled with date/issuer/common-origin dependence preserved. Report leave-one-ticker, leave-one-sector and leave-one-origin sensitivity; never call resampled nightly echoes new episodes. A single pooled point estimate with five treated names is not sufficient evidence of generalization.

Report distinct ownership disclosure cycles and source origins, including origins that cross the block boundary. Two calendar blocks do not create two independent ownership-information samples.

This observational accrual schedule does not replace Conditional Fusion's existing model-training, fold, embargo, challenger or promotion rules. It supplies no permission to fit a model on inadequate folds or to weaken those gates.

As a **precision-planning illustration only**, independent Bernoulli outcomes at worst-case variance need roughly 120 exposed episodes and 480 controls for an unclustered risk-difference interval about ±10 percentage points. Date and common-origin correlation, poor matching, and missing source coverage make the effective requirement larger. This is not a required sample-size claim under the actual dependent design, an expected event yield, or a numerical shipping rule. A fixed cohort that is smaller or imprecise returns **INCONCLUSIVE**; it is not rescued by a favorable point estimate or by extending collection only until significance appears.

The unresolved empirical question is now narrowly specified: does a **decision-time, source-version-qualified, genuinely independent exposure** improve later valid confirmation / H5 T2 follow-through over equally covered, comparable controls across future temporal blocks? Historical re-aggregation cannot supply the missing source and negative-coverage proof. B03's descriptive implementation does not need to wait for the answer.

## 6. Falsifiers and retained negative controls

For emergence, an apparent advantage that vanishes under first-preserved versions, valid technical-state rules, or complete follow-up is not robust support. If coverage or B1 conditioning explains the result, report that rather than selecting a more favorable denominator. Repeated research attention that never confirms and disappearance without a proved expiry remain product-cost outcomes.

For ignition, the frozen hypothesis fails on adequate future data if it does not improve on same-date T2 controls, if a single origin/issuer/sector drives it, if the gain is restricted to stale/non-executable episodes, or if one family or generic T1/convergence explains it equally well. Positive excess with poor absolute returns or worse MAE/large-loss risk is not sufficient practical success. The current retained-price comparison is diagnostic; source basis, spread, and execution receipts still govern any later executable study.
