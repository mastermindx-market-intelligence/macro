## 1. Final answer

**The September NVDA journey is a real, useful observation of the proposed sequence. This research does not establish that independent early convergence → technical ignition → sustained leadership is a generalizable predictive mechanism. It does establish enough to build truthful B03 observation-preservation semantics and a precisely qualified future test through the existing owners.**

The distinction matters because the strongest new findings concern measurement. The historical flags do not carry the complete event, value-version, clock and independence proof needed for the frozen scientific hypothesis. The emergence advantage is sensitive to provisional states, incomplete follow-up and which version of a daily board is used. The five-name ignition H5 result survives several reasonable comparisons, but all five carry ownership, share three filing origins, occupy four dates, and include restricted or late-published observations. Leadership's cumulative-retention headline partly reflects gains already earned during the first interval.

The result is therefore more specific than either “NVDA was luck” or “the mechanism is validated.” Prophet preserved an early NVDA research state and later technical transition. That is evidence of a working observation path in one strong case. The broader source-qualified predictive effect remains unestimated. There is no calibrated future win probability and no basis for a live rank bonus, entry override, sizing change or leader forecast.

**The next product action is B03 continuity.** Its existing composition must preserve relevant watch/candidate observations even when they lie outside the cascade-eligible pool or lack a current canonical episode relation. This is a correction to the parent pool-only assumption, not a new candidate architecture. The exact parallel blockers are B04/source identity, source/value versions, independent origins, observable negative coverage, and A2 publication lineage. More historical threshold combinations cannot resolve those blockers.

The repair must bind the value revision, not only the timestamp. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).
