## Scope, specificity and adoption

This prepared record transcribes the current live Chairman delegation. It is included
for source validation/adoption under the existing Agent OS writer. Its inclusion in a
local package does not claim a merged source record or a native receiver assignment.

The delegation supersedes the named Sol/Astra principal recipient and mandatory return
dependency of the prior September18 scoped Prophet mandate and R5 chapters21/26, for
this full US build only. All product/scientific boundaries of those records survive.
Existing technical owners remain owners; the workstream need not acquire a fabricated
runtime role or owner enum to express the scoped delegation.

Fable resolves D01–D12 and progresses B00–B28/Q01–Q24 with external specialists and
independent reviewers. Program review and acceptance do not require a new Astra turn.
Fable may adopt/version in-scope design and scientific decisions through existing owners,
not bypass their evidence. A prior HOLD-FOR-SOL cannot create a permanent dependency on
a departed principal once its real gates are met.

## External execution and reserved authority

No built-in/native Opus/Sonnet/Haiku, Agent Teams or internal-agent fallback is permitted
for this mission, including descendants and indirect wrappers. Ordinary deterministic
tools and Fable's principal reasoning are not child agents. Actual current external
route eligibility, budget, custody and return evidence are required before effect.

No new provider, API, credentials, model slug, queue, lifecycle, registry, workstream,
research warehouse or publication authority is created. Existing STARTed or
EFFECT_UNKNOWN work remains on its original carrier. This mandate grants no brokerage
execution, leverage, discretionary trade sizing or new unapproved spending.

Genuine reserved human/credential/legal/new-budget or materially expanded/deleted scope
questions go directly to the Chairman or their formal owner. Routine research, debugging,
architecture, design, review and execution sequencing return to Fable, not Astra.

## Full outcome and receiver truth

Retain the complete early field, three initial strategy sleeves, six product views,
current entry versus holding distinction, original/current thesis, lawful data and model
vintages, private user actions, honest outcome/control measurement, normal refresh and
rollback. US first; CN/HK/CA remain deferred.

A deliberately delivered execution handoff to the actual eligible Fable session supplies
receiver assignment; merely finding this record does not. ACK, START, running children,
source acceptance, deployment and final product acceptance remain separate facts. The
preparation session has not claimed Fable pickup or execution. Runtime and Agent OS
continuity must survive later context rotation without replaying the entire chat.
