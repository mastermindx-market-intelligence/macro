# Decision-resolution playbook — no Astra return dependency

Fable is accountable for all twelve decisions under the current Chairman delegation. The existing technical and evidence owners still supply and validate their own facts. These procedures define how to resolve unknowns, not fictional values selected without data. None grants a model, policy or runtime effect merely by being written.

## D01 — Source-session validity and revocation

External source/entry specialist; independent integration reviewer; Fable adjudicates the versioned composition.

1. Read the exact #7581 adapter, incumbent confluence producer, accepted session policy, emission receipts and original review carrier. First determine whether a genuine current-session predecision receipt exists; do not infer this from a scheduled workflow name.
2. If it exists, prove that receipt across actual normal and shortened sessions. If it does not, specify an explicit source-session to valid-decision-session interval with emission/knowledge cutoff, expiry, revocation and the owner responsible. The last completed session remains its actual source date.
3. Have the existing owner implement only the minimal versioned composition on the incumbent carrier after custody. Require old-session-valid, stale/expired, late-emitted, retracted, basis-mismatched, closed-session and compact-UNKNOWN cases.
4. Accept the exact native adapter-to-B4 behavior and subsequent normal-session proof. Until then, preserve an honest unavailable result and let independent research/UI work proceed.

**Closure artifact:** Accepted validity contract, exact source/head, independent review and real owner-chain evidence. No date relabeling, naked equality-check removal or invented PASS.

**Affected units:** B05, B12, B15, B19

## D06 — Economic margins, endpoints and formal-read law

External quantitative-design specialist plus independent statistics/risk reviewer; Fable is the final program scientific decision owner.

1. Read the existing accepted evaluation protocol and trial ledger. Separate development/pilot evidence from untouched confirmatory or already-protected outcomes. Record all previously inspected eras and variants.
2. Select the economic quantity the user needs improved, realistic costs and review/coverage burden. Define the minimum worthwhile gain, maximum tolerated degradation and coverage floor from that job and permitted development evidence, not whichever holdout result later looks best.
3. Estimate variability/dependence using only the permitted pilot or historical development partition. Count dates, episodes, issuers and market cycles separately. Choose the uncertainty method and sufficient independent-information or precision requirements. If no defensible numeric margin can yet be set, commission that bounded design analysis instead of opening the holdout.
4. Before results, persist exact numeric margins, primary/supporting endpoints, population, benchmark, sample/landmark rules, model/policy variants, comparison family, multiplicity and formal-read schedule through the existing evaluation owner. Where existing law fixes an alpha/error rule or sample floor, preserve it; amendments are versioned and future-bound, not retroactive.
5. Obtain independent challenge, resolve it and authorize only the registered read under current Evaluation controls. A nonsignificant harm estimate is not non-inferiority. The #7751 aliases do not add sample/test credit; its 300-episode floor is not alone a power proof.
6. Record PASS, REJECT or INCONCLUSIVE against the frozen rule. A failed head stays shadow/absent or is replaced by a separately registered hypothesis. Do not cherry-pick a favorable horizon or ask Astra to make the verdict.

**Closure artifact:** A fully numeric, versioned, pre-outcome analysis and interpretation contract accepted by its existing owner. No universal invented win-rate target or permission for real-money risk.

**Affected units:** B06, B10, B11, B12, B15, B18, B19

## D07 — Source and model-vintage eligibility

External temporal-data/model-validation specialist; Fable decides the admissible claim.

1. Pin the input body/revision and its source, observation and correction clocks. A document published before a cut but captured after it is not observed-as-run evidence.
2. Pin feature code, fitting data cutoff, normalization, model training/selection vintage and calibration. Current date-filtered retrieval does not prove a modern model has no later knowledge in its weights.
3. Label unknown historical model knowledge as retrospective or use an admissible model vintage and explicit replay assumptions. Start a frozen prospective study where historical provenance cannot establish the intended claim.
4. Add real late-capture, corrected-body, current-only event-set, future membership, overlap and fitting-label-maturity tests. Keep the original forecast distinct from later landmarks.

**Closure artifact:** Per-study evidence class and model/source eligibility, with tested leakage controls and no hidden future knowledge claim.

**Affected units:** B04, B08, B10, B11, B15, B18
