# Prophet US — finite research docket

These are proposed work packets, not dispatched jobs or newly registered trials. Existing protected outcomes stay closed until their owner permits the formal read. Numerical policy/economic decisions must be frozen through D06 and the applicable study owner.

## Q01 — Decision-time integrity and usable source history

**Decision question:** Which exact source revisions and owner clocks can support each proposed historical or prospective claim?

**Population:** A source-readiness census of supported identities, dates, source families and coverage strata; not an outcome-selected ticker list.

**Inputs:** Data OS identities, immutable source generations, observation/publication clocks, adjustment basis, episode generations, current model-vintage records.

**Controls:** Late capture, same-date correction, current-only event discovery, missing sessions, renamed/reorganized security and unknown model vintage.

**Method:** Trace representative source-to-decision paths with explicit cutoff joins. Measure coverage without looking at protected strategy outcomes; map irrecoverable history to prospective-only use.

**Required return:** An approved evidence matrix specifying observed-as-run, PIT replay or diagnostic eligibility for each input/date; missing compulsory inputs block only affected claims.

**Reject or narrow when:** Any declared PIT path depends on a current body, later membership, future label or unproven clock. Repair or exclude it before fitting.

**Existing owner:** Existing Data OS/source owners with Evaluation adjudication

**Build consumers:** B01, B02, B04, B06, B08, B16. **Evidence:** R2, R3, R4.

## Q02 — Winner/loser and missed-opportunity attribution

**Decision question:** Which part of the pipeline most often destroys useful opportunity or introduces avoidable downside?

**Population:** A complete dated supported cohort with original decisions and all dispositions; individual winners/losers illustrate rather than define it.

**Inputs:** Nominations, suppressions, B1 relations, original board rank/visibility, availability, accepted publication, entry/management and outcome references.

**Controls:** Cohort/era separation; control-only V3 tape; no ticker-ever conversion substitute; unresolved joins remain counted.

**Method:** Build a layered failure decomposition before recommending a rule. Separate discovery, ranking, delivery, entry, management and common exposure. Quantify attribution uncertainty.

**Required return:** Select the next largest identifiable capability loss and its corresponding research/build edge; do not tune V3 from a pooled headline.

**Reject or narrow when:** The diagnosis reverses under original-time joins or depends on hindsight-selected names. Return a measurement correction rather than a trading rule.

**Existing owner:** Evaluation / Prophet program research

**Build consumers:** B03, B06, B10, B22. **Evidence:** R1, R2.

**Existing owner:** Technical / B1 / Evaluation owners

## Q04 — Same-origin daily/multi-day/intraday entry timing

**Decision question:** When does earlier lawful observation improve the complete entry policy rather than just the surviving filled trades?

**Population:** Same original eligible episodes across early-lawful, slower-confirmation, pullback and cash policies; no eventual-confirmation selection.

**Inputs:** Registered bar anchoring, source/decision validity, first observable episode, quote/basis and geometry, declared fixed management.

**Controls:** Same episodes and exits; never-confirmers/no-pullback cases; latency and cost sensitivity; incumbent next-close convention disclosed.

**Method:** Date-weighted paired full-opportunity H10 proposal with H5/H15 supporting for this new study. Separate episode-clock and fill-clock results and dependence.

**Required return:** Identify a bounded species/timeframe policy worth qualification, with exact availability requirements; do not grant confluence waiver via adapter.

**Reject or narrow when:** Apparent early-entry gain disappears when failures, no-entry, gaps or real latency are retained, or depends on provisional bars treated as final.

**Existing owner:** Technical / B4 / Evaluation

**Build consumers:** B06, B12, B13. **Evidence:** R2, R3.

## Q05 — Group leadership, member selection and self-confirmation

## Q07 — Cycle proving-domain and economic-turn identification

**Decision question:** Which domain offers an interpretable, historically measurable economic recovery mechanism without choosing recent winners?

**Population:** Source-readiness candidate industrial capital goods/machinery; final universe selected by coverage/mapping criteria before return inspection.

**Inputs:** Vintage orders, backlog, shipments, inventories, production, issuer segments and failure histories; common definition changes documented.

**Controls:** Domain breadth and composition; inventory-versus-sales interpretation; latest-revised versus vintage evidence; sector distress is not recovery.

**Required return:** One admissible pilot domain with explicit coverage and falsifiers, or a documented alternative chosen by the same readiness rule.

**Reject or narrow when:** Apparent turn exists only in revised series, coarse proxies, survivorship-selected names, or future-known cycle troughs.

**Existing owner:** Macro/GMI/financial source owners and Cycle research

**Reject or narrow when:** Recovery value vanishes for original shares, or outcomes improve only after failed firms and canceled securities are excluded.

## Q09 — Multi-head remaining opportunity and payoff timing

**Decision question:** Can separate payoff, adverse-tail and time-to-event forecasts improve decisions beyond stage labels?

**Reject or narrow when:** No incremental policy value, incoherent first-event probabilities, poor selected-cohort calibration or hindsight path ordering.

## Q10 — Ranking objectives and economic tradeoffs

**Decision question:** Which ordering best serves a declared review/entry task without compressing all strategies into one score?

**Inputs:** Qualified features/heads, current availability references, workload limit and declared risk preferences.

## Q11 — Calibration, abstention and user-visible confidence

**Decision question:** Which forecasts remain reliable after top-K selection and across evidence coverage?

**Population:** Held-out strategy/horizon/coverage cohorts and meaningful adverse events, retaining unavailable outputs.

**Inputs:** Frozen predictions, calibrator vintages, proper labels, risk/coverage and selected-population information.

**Controls:** Uncalibrated model; simple calibration; base-rate/shrinkage; coverage-matched comparisons.

**Method:** Assess proper scoring rules, reliability, interval width/coverage, tail sample counts and selective risk/coverage. Avoid automatic conditional guarantees.

**Required return:** Expose only qualified heads and meaningful uncertainty; unsupported precision stays absent.

**Reject or narrow when:** Pooled calibration hides top-K failures, abstention drops hard cases without coverage reporting, or rare-event N is inadequate.

**Existing owner:** Evaluation / Conditional Fusion / UX

**Build consumers:** B11, B15, B18, B22. **Evidence:** R1, X02, X08.
