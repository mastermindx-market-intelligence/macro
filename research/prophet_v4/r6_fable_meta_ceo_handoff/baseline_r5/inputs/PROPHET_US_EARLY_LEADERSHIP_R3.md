## 1. The concrete outcome

The first user should be able to open the existing Prophet workspace during a US session, find an emerging stock even when it is not Featured, understand the evidence and the appropriate tactical strategy, distinguish current entry availability from research interest, inspect the episode's history and risks, and deliberately save the symbol to an existing watchlist. After selection or a real plan, the same workspace should maintain the relevant thesis and management context without converting a losing tactical trade into a long-term investment by narrative.

The machine should preserve the full opportunity path: nomination, suppression or canonical episode, evidence known at each observation, hypothesis and control identity, availability, exposure to the user, user action where actually recorded, and subsequent outcomes. It should distinguish a useful research sighting from an actionable entry and from a captured trade.

The final flagship target is not merely an accurate timestamp or another attractive card. It is timely, risk-aware, intelligible opportunity selection. However, neither user usefulness nor the strategy's name establishes predictive edge. A truthful research product can be accepted before a new model is promoted; a model can perform well in research while the production product remains unacceptable.

Three accomplishments therefore need separate receipts:

1. **Useful observational delivery:** the real user can complete the research journey on current, coherent data.
2. **Accepted decision mechanics:** owner-bound entry and management policies operate correctly under their declared scope.
3. **Earned predictive authority:** the candidate-selection or ranking method improves the registered outcomes under appropriate out-of-sample and prospective evidence.

These are milestones inside the existing Prophet program, not three new platforms.

## 2. What the present source permits—and what it does not

### 2.1 The strategy name is ahead of the economic selector

The inspected `prophet.strategy_definition/v1` defines `EARLY_LEADERSHIP_SECTOR_ROTATION`, strategy version `1`, entry-policy version `early-leadership-sector-rotation-entry-v1`. Importantly, sector/theme context is **optional context**, not a required leadership selector. The definition explicitly refuses to widen B1, waive confluence, or originate a plan. Therefore the current definition cannot be described as a tested high-recall sector-rotation stock picker merely because it bears that title.

The first integration should preserve this control verbatim. A new research eligibility predicate or entry policy must be separately accepted and versioned through the existing strategy owner. The current validator compares the full payload to the frozen definition; adding a new strategy by changing a label or an undocumented dictionary is not a valid extension. [S1]

### 2.2 A source union is not an independent episode-origination union

Current B1 intake accepts TURN WATCH, Context Vector candidates, Doors, and Radar observations. The inspected normalizer provides a structural anchor only for TURN WATCH. Candidate, Door, and Radar observations are unanchored. The canonical reconciler suppresses an unanchored observation when no active episode exists, using `MISSING_STRUCTURAL_ANCHOR`. This is a binding design dependency for high recall. We cannot claim every independent expert can currently originate a candidate. We also cannot solve the gap by manufacturing a ticker/date episode, borrowing a later trough, or opening a second episode registry.

The product must account for both sides of the boundary: owner-native nominations/suppressions before B1, and canonical episode observations after B1. A pre-B1 research sighting can remain useful, but its unanchored status must be visible. Any additional anchor-producing expert needs its own source-valid structural-anchor and re-arm contract, accepted by the existing B1 owner. Persisted suppressions must not be silently reopened under reused source keys.

## 3. Architecture choice and rejected alternatives

**Recommended:** one episode-centered Prophet workspace, an immediate coherent research view over existing owners, and a separately evaluated Early Leadership selection/entry program. The current V3 control remains frozen; new intelligence and entry policies earn their own authority.

**Alternative A: improve V3's weights and card styling.** This is smaller, but it cannot solve unanchored discovery loss, original-versus-current belief, missing strategy economics, multi-clock validity, or explicit holding policy. It could still be a useful control or narrow maintenance change, not the final design.

**Alternative B: launch a new standalone AI leader finder.** This might appear faster but would duplicate identity, quotes, lifecycle, evidence, outcomes, and entitlement. It would also fragment the user journey and contaminate the comparison with the incumbent. Reject it.

**Alternative C: wait until every advanced model and data family is complete.** This avoids partial labels but delays useful candidate visibility and feedback. Reject waiting as the sole delivery approach. Ship a truthful integrated research capability while the scientific work proceeds; do not market the partial capability as the finished flagship.

The recommended approach has a real cost: precise interfaces and separate acceptance gates require integration discipline. Its advantage is that each bounded release can help users without inventing authority, and later quantitative results can be attributed to the actual component that changed.

## 4. Strategy semantics: three clocks and two questions

Early Leadership asks whether emerging group and company-specific evidence creates a useful tactical opportunity over sessions to weeks. It does not assume that every relative-strength leader is a buy or that every washout is a bargain.

The **economic clock** concerns the mechanism and expected duration: participation broadening, earnings or policy transmission, positioning adjustment, or another declared process. The **structural clock** concerns formation, confirmation, persistence, reset, and deterioration. The **execution clock** concerns the present quote, session, zone, basis, liquidity, and invalidation.

The first integrated desk answers two questions side by side:

- Why does this stock deserve research attention now?
- What does the accepted entry owner permit at the current decision point?

Attention is not order authority. A highly interesting but extended stock can be a valuable watch candidate. A technically available setup with weak evidence can be correctly de-emphasized without rewriting B4. Absence of a fresh-entry opportunity is not a command to exit an existing position.

The live v1 control must retain its exact common-stock and B1 boundaries. ADRs, ETFs, REIT representations, and other instrument classifications require explicit identity and strategy support; ticker appearance or informal regional labels cannot establish eligibility.

## 5. Discovery, research eligibility, and preservation

### 5.1 Retain two distinct populations

**Population N:** existing owner-native nominations and suppressions, including observations that never become B1. Store no new identity; reference the producer's original identity and reason.

**Population E:** canonical B1 episodes and their immutable generation references. Research ranking, repeated observations, and strategy eligibility bind to these owner identities.

A complete product can provide separate filters for these populations. A joined table must label them rather than quietly assigning a surrogate episode to each ticker. A supported-universe count, nomination count, episode count, selected research count, entry-open count, and plan count are different denominators.

### 5.2 Preserve before prioritizing

Capture the full permitted population before Featured, sector concentration, pagination, or display ranking. A presentation preference cannot remove an observation from the audit. Unknown, stale, and unpriced cases remain counted with their reasons.

For the first observational view, retain current owner order or use an explicit user-selected observable sort such as observation time. Do not introduce an unreviewed predictive composite under the name of UI sorting. Existing V3 ranks are shown only for rows that actually received them, with the definition and scored population identified; an unscored displaced row stays unscored.

### 5.3 Define research eligibility without freezing a convenient heuristic as truth

A proposed Early Leadership research selector should require valid identity, an actual supported technical/event observation, and a measured basis for the leadership hypothesis. These predicates need owner acceptance and an era before they affect a trial population. Missing specialist context does not suppress general research visibility.

Use upstream GMI leadership decisions and their measured dimensions where available. Do not create a second ThemeState or arbitrary threshold such as "top five groups" merely to make the sleeve executable. The exact research predicate belongs in the existing strategy/evaluation registration, with an explicit control and full rejected-population accounting.

Different economic roles—first leader, continuing leader, reset candidate, and lagging participant—are hypotheses, not automatic grades of desirability. Each must beat an appropriate control. The existing negative evidence against naive leader dips, unconditional freshness widening, generic relay transport, and killed washout/rotation constructions remains intact.

Do not infer confidence from how many signals agree. Correlated price-derived features are not independent sources. A theme chip, its basket score, and a narrative generated from that basket must not receive three votes.

A generally covered base model should be evaluated against enriched models on compatible populations. Report the complete base population and the enriched subset separately so richer coverage cannot win through hidden selection of easier names. Missing an optional transcript may lower certainty; missing mandatory quote or basis evidence can block entry. These effects are not interchangeable.

### 6.1 Separate group selection from member selection

For matched endpoints and return basis:

`stock excess vs market = group excess vs market + stock excess vs group`.

This identity motivates two diagnostic questions. Did Prophet choose a useful group? Did it choose a useful member within that group? It does not mandate a market-neutral product or erase profitable sector allocation from total performance.

Industry-momentum research provides a reason to measure these separately, but the cited historical study focuses on intermediate horizons of six to twelve months. It does not validate a two-to-fifteen-session rule. [P1]

### 6.2 A new anti-self-confirmation control

When a stock contributes materially to its group's return, its own strength can make the group appear supportive. For a fixed-weight simple return over a matched window:

`R_group = w_i * R_i + (1 - w_i) * R_group_excluding_i`.

In an invented equal-weight five-member group, one stock rises 20% and the other four are flat. The group rises 4%, but the other members supply no positive return support. Counting the stock's 20% and the group's 4% as two independent bullish facts would be misleading.

Propose a leave-one-out peer-strength and breadth ablation through the existing group owner. It is a research control, not a replacement published index. Use actual admissible membership and weights; report a one-member group as not estimable rather than zero. Rebalanced, time-varying groups require their native path construction rather than applying the one-period formula indiscriminately.

Test whether any incremental feature value survives removing the subject stock, conditioning on ordinary sector effects, and controlling for group size and concentration. Overlapping groups share evidence lineage. Never choose the historically best-performing group for a stock after its outcome is known.

### 6.3 Price incorporation is a hypothesis, not mind-reading

The evidence dossier should compare the timing and magnitude of new information with the observed price response, extension, peer moves, and remaining geometry. It may identify uncertainty about whether an event is already reflected in prices. It may not declare exact market expectations from a narrative or equate a low price with undiscovered value.

A slower event or economic thesis can remain interesting even when the first tactical entry is gone. Preserve it as a research hypothesis with concrete future conditions; do not invent a guaranteed pullback date or a replacement long-term thesis to excuse a failed trade.

## 7. Original-call and remaining-opportunity models

The same episode creates two legitimate forecasting tasks.

**Origin model:** evaluate the opportunity at its first genuinely observable and owner-valid origin. This is the anchor for comparing discovery and early-versus-later entry on the same cohort.

**Landmark model:** at a predeclared later observation, assess the remaining opportunity conditional on what has happened so far. This answers the user's current question, not what the original model should have predicted with hindsight.

The landmark record must not overwrite the original prediction. Its features are known at that landmark, and its labels begin at the declared landmark/fill convention. Multiple observations of the same episode remain statistically dependent. A model trained only on first-entry rows cannot be assumed calibrated when repeatedly applied to mature or deteriorating episodes.

The existing owners should retain original and current observation references. Do not create a second episode lifecycle or pretend that a new rank is a new independent trade.

### 7.1 Required outputs, earned separately

The intended model interface supports absolute net outcome, market and group-relative outcome, upside/downside quantiles, severe-loss risk, target-before-invalidation, and time to event. Evidence applicability is separate from economic risk. A forecast interval, confidence interval for a mean, ranking uncertainty, and entry permission are not synonyms.

Not every head must be promoted together. A weak time-to-target model can remain absent while a useful calibrated relative-return model progresses. The UI must disclose which outputs are accruing, uncalibrated, unavailable, or accepted. An empty probability field is preferable to a fabricated 78% confidence label.

### 7.2 Baseline and challenger sequence

Within Conditional Fusion, retain the frozen V3 comparator on compatible populations. Add robust simple controls: market/group returns, own-stock behavior, evidence-free/base-rate predictions, and regularized linear/logistic/additive models. Use real intercepts and shrinkage. Compare sparse family-level interactions only where estimability is adequate.

The next model class is a date-grouped boosted ranker alongside separately calibrated payoff/risk models. XGBoost documents query-group inputs and LambdaMART ranking; its output is a relevance score, not a return probability. Generic grouped cross-validation examples do not replace R2's chronological embargo and label-availability requirements. [P2]

Additional conditional, sequence, or graph models may be researched in parallel where inputs are lawful. They must beat the strongest surviving simpler method on common decision populations, costs, labels and periods. Model complexity is neither a reason for automatic acceptance nor for rejection.

Training windows, hyperparameter budgets, aggregation, feature selection and formal read rules belong in the existing preregistration. No new model was fit in R3. Modern-network calibration research is a reminder that confidence outputs are not automatically reliable; calibration must be tested in the actual top-K, sleeve, horizon and coverage cohorts. [P3]

### 7.3 Ranking without a universal stock score

Research interest and actionable priority should be separate purpose-labeled projections. The final active ranking compares like strategy/horizon/availability candidates; it does not compare a tactical ten-session trade and a two-year cycle thesis on an unexplained universal scale.

During observation-stage delivery, do not create a hidden predictive rank. Once accepted, a decision policy may combine a calibrated expected net outcome with declared downside preferences within a specific sleeve. Its risk preferences and coverage tradeoffs must be transparent and evaluated. B4 cannot be overridden by a large utility value.

Top-K is a review allocation, not deletion. Saved filters should state both included and total counts. Low confidence can justify abstention from a model claim without erasing the candidate. Selective-prediction research motivates measuring coverage versus error/risk together, but its benchmark results do not validate a stock-selection policy. [P4]

## 8. Entry policy: preserve the owner and close the actual seams

B4 remains the sole computational current-entry evaluator. Geometry remains with its incumbent owner; Data OS and quote/basis owners retain identity and adjustment truth. The research composer does not accept a caller's preferred stop, confluence verdict, or model-derived exemption.

Current B4 requires a canonical candidate projection, strategy identity, decision/quote clocks, owner geometry and the closed deterministic gate set. It emits a content-identified result. Terminal/retracted/breached facts, unavailable required evidence, chase, risk, liquidity, gap, session and confluence are not averaged into a score. [S8]

A computational `ENTRY_OPEN` generated under a control-only strategy must be labeled as an experimental/control evaluation in the research product. It must not silently become a production recommendation, a green BUY button, plan origination, or sizing authority. The same output field can be structurally valid while its strategy has not earned commercial decision authority.

### 8.1 Source age is not the same as expiration

A valid last-completed-session technical fact can legitimately be considered during the following live session under an explicit owner policy. Conversely, a same-date artifact may be late, provisional, or stale. Therefore source date equality and genuine decision validity are different questions.

The exact #7581 finding must be resolved on that carrier with the source owner. Until then the research desk can show the truthful refusal and its cause. The builder must not make the intended demo pass by changing a date, forcing a candidate, or weakening a guard.

Current session policy explicitly covers 2026. Any historical replay outside that era requires an accepted historical-session policy rather than calling the live helper and treating refusal as a market signal or bypassing it. NYSE's official hours and holiday information is the external reference; the existing calendar owner remains the implementation source. [S7, P5]

### 8.2 Earlier discovery does not yet create an earlier licensed entry

The existing strategy definition does not waive incumbent confluence; B4's binding currently proves positive T1/T2/T3 facts. The observation roster includes earlier daily and two-day states. Connecting their cards does not, by itself, permit entry before the accepted confluence condition.

A genuine earlier-entry experiment needs a declared expert/species policy, accepted geometry, current-data validity, and R2's same-origin cohort design. Its method may differ from the incumbent, but it must be a versioned research challenger and pass the standing construction-equivalence review. It cannot be introduced as an adapter refactor.

## 9. Management and the maintained thesis

The new-entry horizon is not a holding contract. The current general Prophet plan book remains a control; this design does not silently alter its horizon or close existing plans.

The tactical sleeve needs an explicit management definition identifying entry/fill, initial risk, invalidation, permitted review conditions, terminal horizon, re-entry, and treatment of no-entry and missing information. For research, a simple owner-bound reference should precede optimized management. A proposed fixed entry-clock H10 reference and the R2 common-origin H10 policy valuation answer different questions; neither becomes a live rule through this packet.

Distinguish four events in the user view:

- **New-entry unavailable:** the current price may be extended; existing holding policy is unchanged.
- **Thesis evidence weakened:** explain the measured change and the relevant review condition, without fabricating an execution command.
- **Binding invalidation occurred:** report the owner fact and execute only the separately authorized existing management process.
- **Data unavailable:** identify the gap; absence is not proof the thesis failed or remained safe.

A rank decline is not automatically a sell. A strong group is not sufficient reason to hold a broken company. A tactical loss cannot acquire a Cycle Capture label after the fact. A missed first entry should remain observable with lawful re-entry conditions rather than vanish; any new episode still requires the canonical anchor/re-arm law.

Maintain the original thesis, expected path, disconfirming evidence, current view, and action history through existing plan/feedback owners. Narrative should summarize measured evidence and explicitly identify uncertainty. It must not originate rank, size, entry or trade authority.

### 10.2 An integrated desk rather than disconnected screens

The proposed default shell includes a compact context header, complete candidate table, focused review queue, and an episode drawer. Strategy, observation class, availability, freshness, and scientific status are independently filterable. Existing plan and track-record views are linked, not cloned.

The header shows market session, technical source session, quote clock, covered population, missing/partial sources, strategy/control definition and current generation. A page rendered today from yesterday's evidence says so.

Each row should answer, without opening five screens:

**Why surfaced?** Exact early observation and time.

**Why this company/group?** Measured own-stock and peer/group evidence, with coverage and counterevidence.

**What remains?** Current extension, zone and declared forecast if accepted; otherwise research-only/accruing.

**Can a new entry be considered?** Exact owner availability and blockers, visibly qualified by strategy authority.

**What could change the answer?** Observable owner conditions, not a guaranteed date or price path.

**What has the user done?** An actual saved action or plan reference, never a success checkmark inferred from a click.

### 10.3 A synthetic walkthrough, not a recommendation

Consider the fictional symbol TEST-A. A daily early event is present, a measured group observation is supportive, and the current quote is above the owner's chase boundary. The row remains in the research field and shows the early event's source time, current price time, group coverage and `RAN_DONT_CHASE`. It offers research details and a deliberate Watch action. It does not print Buy because the evidence is persuasive.

On a later observation, a source-owner-defined pullback might produce another valid evaluation. That is a new observation of the same episode unless the canonical lifecycle says otherwise. The original sighting and earlier refusal remain in history. If the quote feed fails, the page changes to data unavailable; it does not silently retain a green action from the previous generation.

### 10.4 Watch is not a position or a cloud thesis record

The current WatchStore distinguishes attention membership from Portfolio positions. It has list-scoped symbol operations; its documentation says notes are local-only by ruling. [S13]

The first user action should therefore select an existing watchlist, call the existing owner operation, and confirm actual persisted membership. It must not create a new default Prophet list on page load, create a portfolio position, or claim cross-device episode notes merely because a symbol is saved.

Episode-specific Watch/Pass/reason/thesis history requires mapping to an existing feedback/plan schema or an accepted extension by that owner. This is a named integration requirement, not permission for a second feedback database. Until available, the interface accurately distinguishes saved symbol membership from any local-only or unavailable episode decision history.

### 10.5 Updates must be safe to read and act on

Separate price freshness from rank-list stability. Material changes in permission, source validity and invalidation must be reflected promptly; rank changes should not move a row out from under the user's pointer or keyboard focus. A visible update indicator can let users apply a new coherent ordering while retaining the selected episode.

The client must not recompute B4 or invent a score. It consumes a coherent accepted projection. Mixed candidate generation, quote/geometry basis, preview/private tails, or stale source receipts are refused or explicitly degraded. Corrections on the same calendar date still require a new content identity and correct refresh.

Accessible status messages should announce important changes without forcing focus to jump, and without reading every tick aloud. W3C's status-message guidance informs this interaction requirement; this packet makes no WCAG conformance claim. [P6]

### 10.6 Entitlement and data rights are separate

Use existing authentication and paid entitlement for private data. A paid subscription does not automatically confer vendor redistribution rights. Field-level source rights and user entitlements must both be satisfied. Anonymous previews cannot receive hidden private rows through HTML, script payloads or mismatched-generation hydration.

Real acceptance includes an entitled production user, desktop/laptop/tablet/390px mobile, light/dark and supported languages, keyboard/focus, failures and partial data. Component screenshots or a simulated authorized callback are useful development evidence, not production acceptance.

## 11. Evaluation and the proof ladder

R2's measurement design remains controlling input: same-origin policies, complete denominator, original and landmark records, true exchange-session endpoints, basis-compatible costs, meaningful missingness, label availability, chronological/grouped splits and dependence-aware inference.

Operational tests must prove a real path before any new policy result is useful: source → canonical identity/state → evidence and owner availability → accepted private projection → actual entitled browser → user action receipt. The next ordinary scheduled refresh must retain coherence; a hand-triggered success alone is insufficient.

Scientific tests compare discovery at fixed review burden and ranking on fixed candidate populations. They retain cash/no-entry, unresolved data and failed episodes. They report absolute net and market/group-relative outcomes, adverse tails, giveback, timing, turnover, costs, coverage and uncertainty. Top-K improvements that depend on missingness or fail large-loss controls do not pass automatically.

Do not infer alpha from our eight binding-probe checks, an authored acceptance catalogue, a merged schema, a backtest, or a beautiful UI. These are different proof classes. Performance claims require the existing experiment owner, protected evidence boundaries and separate adjudication.
