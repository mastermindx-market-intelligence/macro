## 1. What this round adds

R3 specified Early Leadership. R4 makes two genuinely different economic jobs concrete. Earnings/Revision asks whether newly disclosed operating evidence and changed expectations leave an attractive opportunity. Cycle Capture asks whether an industry turn can reach a surviving company's existing equity holders before financing pressure or permanent impairment consumes the recovery.

Neither is a reskin of a technical score. Both reuse Data OS identity and source facts; Earnings and Filing Forensics for issuer evidence; GMI/Group Reads for economic exposure; B1/B3 for candidate identity/state; D5 for source-bound evidence; B4 for accepted strategy-specific current entry; existing Prophet plans, feedback and WatchStore for user workflow; and existing Evaluation/QLedger and Portfolio/Risk for outcomes and allocation. No additional authoritative event, episode, quote, price-geometry, model-registry, outcome, thesis database, or publication system is created by this design.

Three clocks remain separate: the economic mechanism's clock, the technical observation clock, and the execution clock. A strong economic case can coexist with a poor current entry. A tactically unavailable new entry is not an automatic exit from a valid existing core. Conversely, a tactical loss cannot acquire a structural thesis retrospectively merely to excuse holding it.

The proposed interface is one Prophet opportunity workspace with sleeve-specific evidence and decisions. A user selects a job and horizon, not an unlabeled universal score. Research attention, present entry validity, predictive qualification, and actual user actions remain distinguishable.

## 3. Architecture choice

I reject three shortcuts. First, attaching earnings and cycle bonuses to V3 would preserve the wrong mixture of jobs and would not establish when the economic evidence was known. Second, building independent Earnings Prophet and Cycle Prophet applications would duplicate identity, entry, plan, and learning authorities. Third, requiring a complete macro graph, a licensed consensus history, and a finished universal model before showing any useful research would postpone user value unnecessarily.

The recommended design is incremental extension through the existing owners. Establish a source-comparable research dossier first, then accepted entry and management mechanics, then earned model influence. A release-only Earnings view can be useful while an analyst-revision branch is unavailable. A current Cycle dossier can be useful while a multi-cycle historical study is still being qualified. Neither may be mislabeled as calibrated alpha.

The first ER proving population should be US-listed domestic operating-company common stock with comparable ordinary revenue, earnings, and cash-flow semantics available through accepted sources. Banks, insurers, REIT-specific metrics, funds, foreign-accounting variants, and binary clinical outcomes require separate semantic contracts rather than being coerced into this first template. This is a proposed applicability boundary, not permission to remove those names from the broader research field.

## 4. Earnings/Revision: three research constructions, not one bullish event bit

### 4.1 Post-release economic repricing

Start from a verifiable earnings release or subsequent relevant disclosure. Ask whether the new information changes a plausible forward operating path, whether the improvement is economically durable, and whether an attractive opportunity remains at the first actually usable entry.

Potential evidence includes revenue composition, margins, earnings and cash conversion, segment performance, changes in guidance, and funding requirements. Where compatible pre-release consensus exists, surprise is additional evidence. Where it does not, the model can assess issuer-reported change without asserting a consensus beat.

Historical studies support researching this mechanism. Novy-Marx relates earnings momentum to price momentum; Jegadeesh and Livnat report stronger drift where revenue and earnings surprises point in the same direction. These are historical findings in their specified constructions, not proof that a new Prophet threshold or horizon works. [P1, P2]

### 4.2 Issuer-guidance or commitment change

A company can materially change its outlook between routine releases. This branch compares the newly observed guidance with the previously observed guidance for the same metric, period, scope, units, and accounting definition. A wider range, withdrawn guidance, or a changed basis is not an ordinary numerical raise/cut.

The analyst should distinguish management assertion from corroboration. Orders, pricing, backlog conversion, customer statements, and later reported results can reinforce or contradict guidance, but a supplier repeating the issuer's press release is not independent confirmation. Commitments must retain their original horizon and conditions, so missed milestones remain visible.

### 4.3 Analyst expectation revision

This branch requires genuinely licensed, timestamped analyst estimate history with identifiable target periods and, for contributor-level claims, stable contributor identifiers. It asks whether comparable forecasters revised the same future quantity—not merely whether the headline consensus value moved.

When only aggregate snapshots exist, label the observation aggregate consensus change. Do not invent revision breadth, contributor persistence, or dispersion from one mean. When no suitable consensus exists, this branch abstains; the post-release issuer-evidence branch can continue.

Pre-event favorable-result prediction is not included in the first post-release construction. Calendar proximity provides preparation and risk context, not knowledge of a future beat. The original pre-event thesis and a later post-result update can coexist without borrowing each other's knowledge cutoff.

## 5. The expectation object must say what was expected

The comparison identity should reference the existing owner's issuer, canonical event, source revision, and security mapping. The analytical measurement needs metric, numerator/denominator, unit/currency, accounting basis and definition, consolidated/segment scope, exact fiscal-period dates, continuing-operation scope where material, share/split basis for per-share values, and source/observation cutoffs.

There are at least four different comparisons: reported actual versus compatible analyst expectation; new issuer guidance versus prior issuer guidance; a fixed-period analyst estimate versus its prior estimate; and actual versus a separately declared statistical forecast. None should be silently renamed as another.

Livnat and Mendenhall find different drift behavior when surprises use analyst forecasts rather than time-series expectations. That supports keeping the two expectation types separate; a seasonal model is not a free substitute for licensed Street consensus. [P3]

SEC Company Facts provides access to standardized filing facts, while the frames endpoint selects a last-filed fact that best matches a calendar period. Company fiscal calendars can differ. Consequently, downloading a current frame does not establish a decision-time expectation panel or even identical fiscal periods. The builder must retain accession, filing availability, period, and source-revision identity through the existing filing owner. [P4]

Non-GAAP measures require definition-level comparison. The SEC explicitly warns that such measures may differ across companies or become inconsistent between periods. The design should preserve reported GAAP and issuer-adjusted views and their reconciliation, not imply that one generic adjusted EPS is economically identical everywhere. [P5]

A percentage surprise is also fragile near a zero or negative expected value. The release must not display enormous precision from division by a nearly zero number. Report an absolute difference with units where appropriate; standardize only with a declared, training-fitted scale and adequate history. An unavailable scale is not permission to insert an arbitrary epsilon and print a dramatic score.

## 6. Two false revision mechanisms we must isolate

### 6.1 Contributor composition

In an invented example, three analysts forecast 80, 100, and 120. The mean is 100. The analyst forecasting 80 leaves coverage; the others change nothing. The new mean is 110.

The headline consensus rose 10%, but continuing analysts revised by zero. The useful output is both facts: the aggregate changed, and contributor composition explains the movement. This is elementary arithmetic, not evidence that a particular data provider has this defect.

A matched-contributor series should use the intersection of eligible contributor identities at the two cutoffs, retain excluded/entered/departed counts, and require a common target period and basis. The raw aggregate remains visible; matching should not hide genuine entry of an informed new analyst. These are different evidence channels, not one corrected truth.

The research ablation compares raw aggregate change with matched revisions, contributor-change indicators, and coverage. Results must not win by excluding difficult or thinly covered names after observing returns. Multiple analysts at one broker or clustered responses to the same event also do not supply independent sample size.

### 6.2 Forecast-horizon rollover

Suppose fixed fiscal-year estimates are 100 for the nearer year and 140 for the following year. A forward mixture with weights 75/25 equals 110. Later, weights 50/50 equal 120 even when both fiscal-year estimates are unchanged. The moving forecast rose about 9.09% with no revision to either target.

For a consistently defined weighted forecast, decompose the change into estimate change, calendar-weight change, and their interaction. Compare fixed fiscal-period estimates first; report a constant-horizon construction only with its transformation and source periods disclosed. An ordinary roll cannot become a positive economic-news event.

The same discipline applies to seasonality, 52/53-week years, acquired/disposed segments, currencies, and stock splits. Maintaining comparability is the owner's job. The model cannot repair an unknown basis by learning a favorable correlation.

## 7. Earnings quality: identify the source of improvement

The proposed quality analysis is a structured operating bridge, not a summed list of bullish words. A profit improvement can result from more units sold, higher prices, favorable mix, sustainable cost change, a tax movement, financing changes, a smaller share count, or a non-recurring item. These explanations carry different prospective hypotheses.

Start with owner-measured revenue and margin changes. Then separate reported operating improvement from effects of acquisitions, disposals, currency, and revised definitions where the issuer provides enough information. Missing decompositions stay unmeasured. Do not reverse-engineer a precise organic growth rate from insufficient disclosures.

Cash conversion is essential, but not all positive operating cash flow means better demand. Inventory liquidation or stretched payables can temporarily release cash while the business contracts. Conversely, working-capital investment can consume cash during a healthy expansion. Preserve cash-flow components and the expected operating mechanism rather than apply a universal cash-positive/cash-negative judgment.

Share count matters separately. In a synthetic example, profit rises from 100 to 120 while diluted shares rise from 100 to 150. EPS falls from 1.00 to 0.80. A company-level recovery and a per-share improvement are not synonyms. Share estimates used in scenarios must be consistent with financing assumptions and avoid double-counting conversion effects.

For stable profitable firms, cash-flow and margin quality can be informative. For temporarily loss-making firms, the design must not require a high current margin to recognize a turn. Applicability and uncertainty should describe why a metric does or does not apply. The objective is economically meaningful discrimination, not a rigid quality screen that excludes every genuine recovery.

## 8. Evidence sequence and price response

A release, conference call, filing, and later estimate revision are separate observations. The first release can support a limited dossier before a transcript exists. Call-derived statements cannot be assigned to the release cutoff merely because both share an event ID. A later filing correction cannot rewrite the earlier forecast.

For each event, retain the original source-timed baseline and successive current-landmark assessments. A post-event analyst revision is new evidence for the later landmark, not the expectation that existed before the event. A current-event manifest cannot discover all historical events; a complete event-set claim requires the existing Earnings owner to support that set, including superseded and withdrawn events.

Separate the price response already experienced from future opportunity. An overnight gap after a release cannot be credited to a policy whose first usable fill is the next morning. Conversely, a flat price after improving evidence may indicate slow incorporation, a competing negative fact, or a flawed economic interpretation. It is a research question, not automatic undervaluation.

A useful original model pair is economic-change-only versus economic-change-plus-observed-price-response, on the same admissible event set. Compare both with own-price-only and compatible-surprise-only baselines. Do not define the event cohort by a favorable subsequent price reaction and then claim that economic evidence predicted it.

Price incorporation remains an inference. The system may show the pre-event run-up, post-event move, revision change, and implied scenario range. It must not pretend to observe the market's true belief or assign a precise percentage of the news already priced without an explicitly validated model.

## 10. Cycle Capture: three necessary links in one thesis

A serious Cycle case connects three questions: is the relevant industry mechanism turning; can the particular issuer fund its path through that turn; and what value reaches the originally held equity after debt, financing, and dilution? Technical repair and current entry determine how the idea can be expressed, not whether the economic story is automatically true.

The old Cycle architecture already distinguishes washout, base, pivot, accumulation eligibility, entry, core hold, maturity, invalidation, and exit. R4 adds the economic measurement contracts needed beneath those words. [I5]

The strongest alternative hypothesis must remain present: the apparent trough is a pause in secular decline, margins recover only temporarily, discount rates or competition offset operational improvement, or the business survives at the expense of existing shareholders. Campbell, Hilscher and Szilagyi's US distress research is a direct warning against treating distress as an unconditional reward. [P6]

The July 2026 Industry Distress Anomaly working paper is also cautionary: its abstract reports lower expected returns in more distressed industries, distinct from firm-level distress. It is preliminary research, not evidence for buying the weakest industries. The implication for this design is to distinguish a measured recovery mechanism from the level of distress itself. [P7]

We do not require every successful case to experience the same macro recession, oscillator, or drawdown percentage. That would recreate the killed simplistic seed. A registered species must state an economic mechanism and failure path that add information beyond drawdown and the incumbent technical observation.

## 12. The cycle evidence chain

For the industrial pilot, the proposed observation chain is: order quality and demand → production/shipments and inventory normalization → pricing and margins → operating cash and capital requirements → funding through recovery → value per share.

The chain is not a rule requiring every arrow to turn positive simultaneously. That would confirm too late. Instead, classify each observation's economic role, timing, scope, and uncertainty. Some indicators can lead, others corroborate, and others arrive after the move. A forecast of later improvement must remain a forecast, not be filled with the eventual observed result.

An inventory decline alone is not improvement. In a synthetic example, inventories decline from 120 to 100 while comparable-period sales fall from 100 to 60. The inventory-to-sales ratio rises from 1.20 to 1.67. The company holds less inventory but a larger burden relative to sales. No trade conclusion follows from this arithmetic; it identifies a necessary interpretation check.

Orders should be separated from shipments, and gross backlog from cancellable or financed backlog where measured. Nominal sales should not be equated with unit demand; rising input prices can also make gross inventory dollars rise. Profitability may improve because demand recovers, costs fall, assets are impaired, or product mix changes. These are distinct mechanisms with different persistence hypotheses.

Peer confirmation must exclude the subject's own contribution where possible, as in R3, and preserve common roots. Four vendors redistributing one survey do not establish four economic confirmations. Overlapping subthemes need exposure lineage. The model should test whether a feature adds information beyond industry exposure, own-price movement, and the other measures of the same mechanism.

Public industrial data are revised. ALFRED/FRED supports vintage queries but defaults ordinarily return today's view; exact vintage coverage must be checked series by series. The Federal Reserve's November 2025 revision changed the estimated manufacturing contour, illustrating why later revised history is not the same as a decision-time signal. Daily vintage dates do not by themselves prove an intraday public-release or system-capture time. [P9, P10, P11]

## 13. A financing calendar, not an optimistic cash-runway ratio

The issuer analysis should use a dated liquidity waterfall. Starting unrestricted cash and facilities that are actually usable are compared with operating burn, mandatory maintenance/growth commitments as defined in the scenario, interest, debt maturities, covenants, and a minimum liquidity buffer. Currency restrictions, collateral conditions, and revolving-credit availability are constraints, not cash equivalents.

For each declared scenario, compute cash or liquidity at each relevant milestone and identify the minimum. A possible future refinancing can be modeled as an uncertain scenario, not assumed available in the base evidence. Exclude double-counted obligations: if operating cash flow already includes a cash item, subtracting it again is wrong.

Synthetic example: cash 400, operating outflow 50 per quarter, debt 300 due in quarter two, and minimum cash 100. A simple burn ratio says eight quarters. The cash path is 350, then zero after the maturity. The company hits the liquidity constraint before the assumed recovery, despite the long headline runway. If a committed usable facility is included, show how it changes both liquidity and future debt service.

The objective is not to build a separate credit platform. Filing/Capital Structure/Portfolio owners retain facts and policies. Prophet consumes their dated facts and presents an explainable scenario. Unknown covenant terms or refinancing eligibility create uncertainty rather than an invented probability of survival.

A hard source-known financing constraint may prevent a new strategy action under its accepted risk policy. A model's high recovery score cannot override it. Optional absent information should not become a false economic failure, but a missing fact essential to this sleeve's survivability case cannot be called resolved.

## 14. Translate recovery into the investor's actual claim

A company's profit or enterprise value can recover while existing shareholders lose. The research must therefore preserve security identity and the actual claim held through recapitalization, merger, spin-off, suspension, or delisting. A reorganized business's new equity is not automatically a continuation of the original shares.

A simple scenario bridge is enterprise value minus net debt and other explicitly modeled senior claims, divided by the scenario's fully diluted shares. This is a transparent scenario identity, not a valuation oracle or an automatic fair-value estimate. Cash proceeds, dilution, debt conversion, warrants, and fees must reconcile consistently. Do not count financing cash without counting the claim issued to obtain it.

In an invented example, enterprise value rises from 10 to 12, net debt after all scenario cash flows remains 6, and shares rise from 1 to 2. Equity value rises from 4 to 6, but per-share value falls from 4 to 3: minus 25%. The fixed post-financing net-debt assumption is explicit; this is not the claim that every equity issuance leaves net debt unchanged.

This has a direct modeling implication. Do not multiply separately trained industry-recovery, survival, and upside scores as if they were independent. Research a joint outcome distribution or a mutually exclusive scenario decomposition. Operational recovery, refinancing, dilution, and shareholder payoff are often causally linked. The scenario weights remain hypotheses until calibrated.

Use price and total-return labels deliberately, with dividends and corporate actions attached through their owners. Include original-equity wipeouts and recoveries, not only surviving tickers. A deeply distressed issuer that restructures into a successful new business can be an economic success and an original-shareholder failure simultaneously.

## 15. Cycle Capture entry and hold contract

The first control comparison should be unlevered and simple: an earliest qualified strategy entry, a later structural-confirmation entry, and a declared no-position reference on the same original economic opportunities. Each actual entry still requires accepted B1 identity/anchor and B4 strategy-specific geometry. A new policy cannot be created by disabling a tactical veto inside the existing adapter.

Technical repair is relevant because a plausible economic case can remain unenterable. Conversely, prices may turn before reported earnings. The scientific problem is the tradeoff between earlier participation and false pivots—not the impossible promise to know the exact low.

I propose 252 sessions as the initial primary measurement horizon, with 126 and 504 supporting, to operationalize a 6–24-month research envelope. These are proposal numbers, not accepted holding limits or amended ledger horizons. The exact historical data must support them; delisted/merged outcomes and rolling exposures need their own treatment. A long fixed horizon does not excuse an earlier binding invalidation.

Separate four policies: research eligibility, new-entry/add availability, core-hold validity, and tactical management. A core may stay valid while a new add is beyond the chase boundary. A daily oscillator rollover is not an economic thesis break unless the accepted strategy specifically makes it one. New supportive evidence can justify a new review of add eligibility; price appreciation alone is not independent confirmation and does not automatically increase capital.

Holding must have a disproof path. Financing failure, loss of economic exposure, persistent demand/pricing deterioration, structural invalidation, or exhaustion of the declared residual-opportunity thesis can trigger the appropriate existing-owner review or exit rule. Exact numerical policies must be registered before performance interpretation; the phrase long-term conviction cannot replace them.

Milestones should be tied to observable reports and economic conditions. If the thesis expects inventory normalization or cash improvement at a specified future disclosure, keep that expectation and its original date. An absent disclosure is data uncertainty; a published adverse result is contrary evidence. A changed reporting schedule should be represented as a schedule change, not an automatic success or failure.

## 16. Economic models that are ambitious but testable

The first Cycle baseline can use a sparse, interpretable state vector: demand/production, inventory burden, pricing/margins, capacity/capital commitments, financing distance, price repair, and exposure confidence. Test the economic state beyond simple drawdown, price momentum, and sector-only controls. No predetermined sum converts these into a live universal cycle score.

A later model may estimate state transitions or competing outcomes such as durable recovery, stalled recovery, funding stress, and permanent impairment. Inference at a historical decision must be filtered using information available then. Full-sample smoothing that uses later observations can diagnose historical cycles, but it cannot be called a live leading signal.

Use parameter sharing only when justified by economic similarity and effective sample size. Thousands of firms inside one recovery are not thousands of independent cycles. Hold out complete periods and, where feasible, industries or cycle episodes to distinguish a portable mechanism from memorized crisis history. Uncertainty should widen for unfamiliar structural changes rather than force a confident label.

A research bridge from physical measures to revenue/margins to cash/per-share outcomes could become an important moat. But it should be tested in layers: does it forecast the economic milestone; does that forecast improve equity outcomes after price response; and does the entry/hold policy capture useful net returns? Success at one layer is not proof of the next.

Transformer, graph, or hierarchical sequence models are not prohibited research directions. They become useful when they beat strong common-data baselines under honest model selection and preserve interpretable failure modes. The first production phase does not need them to make the dossier coherent and informative.

## 17. Evaluation contracts and stop conditions

Earnings and Cycle studies inherit R2's whole-opportunity denominator, source/model vintage rules, date/episode dependence, cost treatment, no-entry accounting, and no-lookahead temporal splits. They do not inherit the tactical strategy's horizon or every numerical operating constant.

For Earnings, register each construction and its primary endpoint separately. The essential contrasts are issuer-change-only versus compatible consensus augmentation; aggregate versus matched-contributor revision; fixed-period versus rolling-horizon effects; and economic evidence with and without already-observed price response. Avoid an unrestricted grid that selects whichever event window looks best.

For Cycle, distinguish industry selection, issuer selection, financing survival, shareholder dilution, entry timing, and hold policy. Retain evidence of candidate mechanisms that never recover. Compare with simple industry exposure and cash as well as the current Prophet control where populations are comparable. Market and sector excess answer attribution questions; absolute net return and drawdown remain relevant user outcomes.

Large losses and incomplete data require special care, not omission. A known delisting should use the accepted terminal treatment. An unpriced unresolved security is not cash. Finite-horizon cases not yet matured cannot be called flat. Repeated quarters and landmarks retain issuer/cycle dependence. Non-inferiority and coverage claims need predeclared economic margins and uncertainty bounds; an insignificant difference is not proof of safety.

The models should be rejected or narrowed if improvements disappear under vintage-clean inputs, after excluding calendar/contributor artifacts, after accounting for financing/dilution, on held-out cycles, or after realistic entry and exit costs. These falsifiers are part of the design, not optional commentary after a disappointing test.

No trial in this packet is registered. Before a run, existing owners must resolve exact source versions and date ranges, event/candidate mapping, active review/custody, numerical policy and economic margins, endpoint hierarchy, power, and multiplicity. The packet must not cause a worker to fill unresolved choices from observed winners.

## 18. The actual user experience

The Earnings drawer should have four compact views: what changed, what was previously expected, what the market has already done, and what remains actionable. It should show actual/prior/guidance/consensus only where comparable, with known times and source reasons. A user can distinguish a true issuer-guidance raise from an aggregate estimate change, see missing consensus, and inspect contrary cash-flow or margin evidence.

The Cycle drawer should show the economic mechanism, issuer survival path, per-share scenarios, next observable milestones, technical/entry state, and separate core/add status. The central question is not just where the oscillator is. It is why this economic process can work, when it needs to work, who captures the value, and what would make us wrong.

Source-backed facts, estimated outcomes, and user decisions should be visually different. Do not print an 87% recovery probability before a model has earned that claim. A stress-case range can be useful if its assumptions are explicit, even when it is not a calibrated probability distribution.

Both views use the existing list-scoped Watch action. Watch is not a filled position, a new plan, a cloud thesis note, or a trade. Episode-specific user decisions and review history require existing-owner mappings and real persisted readback. A local-only note must be labeled local-only. Shared issuer evidence can inform multiple sleeves without multiplying the portfolio exposure or outcome sample.

The dashboard should preserve the user's selected episode across source corrections and ranking updates. Critical invalidation changes should be visible promptly; noncritical reordering should not move the row under focus. Entitlement and source rights both apply. A rich consensus model cannot leak unlicensed estimates through generated commentary even if the raw numbers are hidden.
