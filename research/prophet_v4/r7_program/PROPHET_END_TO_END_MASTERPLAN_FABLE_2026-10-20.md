## 1. Executive assessment

**Prophet is not unfinished because it lacks a sufficiently ambitious masterplan. It is unfinished because the already extensive plans have not converged into one accepted, installed, continuously operating product.** There is substantial working infrastructure, meaningful accepted research, merged repairs, and detailed design. There are also unmerged consumer integrations, missing native entry composition, unresolved source and effect custody, regional correctness work, and scientific claims that still have not earned promotion.

The next commission should be a completion program over the existing estate. Its unit of progress must be an observable user or machine capability, supported by the exact accepted source and actual operating path. A design board, a passing test suite, a green workflow, a merged PR, and an authenticated successful user journey are different pieces of evidence.

### 1.3 What “completely fixed and built out” means

The goal is a coherent opportunity-and-thesis workspace:

- Discover the complete qualified research field, including names outside the featured shelf.
- Understand the economic sleeve, source evidence, uncertainty, maturity, risks and what changed.
- Distinguish research interest, historical setup, current entry permission and held-position management.
- Compare candidates on compatible meanings without manufacturing a universal score.
- Take a deliberate supported action through the real account, list, Plan or Portfolio owner.
- Return later to the original decision, current evidence, actual outcome and correction history.
- Receive useful state-change alerts and a truthful performance record.
- Repeat those tasks across the advertised markets, languages, themes and device sizes.
- Operate through ordinary producers, publication, authentication, monitoring, refresh and rollback.
- Promote a model or policy only when its specific scientific contract passes.

A useful research release can precede promoted models. Full flagship completion cannot be claimed by permanently returning “unavailable” for every promised core decision, by quietly dropping an advertised capability, or by relabeling open research as validated performance.

## 3. Why the program has not finished

This section is a causal interpretation of the inspected evidence, not a judgment about individual effort or a claim to have measured all labor.

### 3.1 The definition of the upgrade expanded

The July plan emphasized governance, accountability and history. August added discovery, ordering, learning and composition of disconnected intelligence. September R1–R6 expanded to a multi-sleeve opportunity, prediction, entry, management and learning system. October added more explicit four-market journeys and source-integrity repairs.

Those ambitions can coexist, but “complete” cannot keep moving without a current scope-to-proof ledger. Earlier local completion often describes a smaller predecessor. The next plan must preserve the accumulated commitments while assigning each one a clear release boundary.

### 3.2 Plans and component tests have outpaced accepted consumer integration

R6 already supplies **29 build units, 24 research packets and 12 decision procedures**. The missing ingredient is not another set of general ambitions. It is the connection from each existing component to its actual consumer and operating owner.

Concrete examples include a private research route still absent from current main, a native B4 reader that returns no availability, a news-output verifier not adopted by its downstream reader, a stdout-only HK projection, and a Canada shadow write behind a zero-buy early return. These are observable integration obligations. [S03], [S04], [S05], [S06], [S07], [S14], [S17], [S19], [S20], [S21], [S22]

### 3.3 Source truth, decision authority and UI language have drifted apart

Current price is not current assessment. A historical setup is not present permission. An issuer match is not a security/episode/generation match. A research ranking is not predicted return. A save is not a purchase.

When those distinctions are expressed in isolated components but not enforced across the entire journey, the user sees contradictory or overstated actions. More cards or more evidence alone cannot resolve that. Contract-level acceptance must cover all surfaces that express the same decision.

### 3.4 Ownership conservation has not consistently produced timely convergence

Conserving the original effect and writer is necessary. It prevents duplicate work and corrupted recovery. But obsolete personal HOLD labels, stale PR-body heads and stale TODOs can make accepted work look unaccepted or make a nontechnical naming dependency look permanent.

The plan therefore distinguishes:

- A real active or unknown-effect operation, which requires original-effect recovery.
- A source or permission boundary, which remains with its actual owner.
- A scoped CEO decision, which Fable can resolve under the existing delegation.
- A stale record, which should be corrected from actual source and evidence.
- A genuine reserved human gate, which is escalated precisely.

Fable must not wait forever for an archived person-name when authorized succession exists. It must not seize a live writer or bypass an explicit retry denial.

### 3.5 Useful product delivery and scientific promotion have been coupled too broadly

Some constraints are real: point-in-time history, source rights, independent effective sample size, maturity of outcomes and a predeclared formal look. They constrain particular scientific claims. They do not prevent truthful full-pool search, supported dossiers, durable watch actions, or a clearly unavailable entry state.

Conversely, delivering those features does not prove alpha. A plan that collapses both into one global “finished/not finished” flag either stalls useful work or overclaims scientific success.

### 3.6 The operating model has too much reconciliation at the top

Repeated broad context loading, principal-led repairs, polling and re-auditing can consume the attention needed to settle cross-domain decisions and close releases. The requested Fable → Opus → Fabric structure can improve this if each coordinator owns an integrated outcome and workers return bounded evidence. It will not help if every leaf acquires another relay or if every branch invents an independent budget.

**The remedy is delegated outcome ownership with reusable acceptance evidence, not maximum simultaneous activity.**

## 7. Fable → Opus → Fabric operating model

### 7.1 Fable's job

Fable owns the end state, priority order, cross-domain contracts, scientific and product claim boundaries, resolution of scoped decisions, and final program acceptance. It also owns the obligation to continue to the next useful authorized step after a local task closes.

Fable should spend most of its attention on:

- Selecting the next capability that materially advances completion.
- Resolving a dependency or semantic disagreement that spans domains.
- Assigning bounded outcomes with the actual incumbent owners.
- Consuming integrated returns and independent challenges.
- Deciding release scope, research verdicts and remaining obligations.
- Maintaining a concise current frontier in the existing program record.

Fable should not become the default implementer, repeated CI poller or manual messenger for every leaf. An exceptional principal intervention is appropriate for a hard unresolved decision or a broken contract; routine implementation returns to workers.

### 7.2 Opus domain responsibilities

These are **logical responsibilities, not six automatically started sessions or new runtime identities**. Activate, combine or rotate them according to actual capacity, custody and review availability.

| Opus responsibility | Accountable integrated outcome | Typical Fabric assignments | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source and current decision | Lossless, source-bound generation through identity/state and native B4 | Capture adapters, clock/basis joins, negative tests, producer/consumer integration | Existing source/B1/B3/B4 owners retain authority |
| Product and design | Coherent discovery, study, setup, evidence, comparison and saved-state journey | Frontend/backend slices, bilingual/responsive implementation, accessibility and persistence proof | Current compact baseline and shared design system; no rival shell |
| Intelligence and strategy | Useful Early Leadership, Earnings and Cycle evidence/management capabilities | Dossier readers, comparable facts, source readiness, thesis and strategy integrations | No source invention, universal score or model promotion by prose |
| Science and evaluation | Admissible, reproducible, independently adjudicated studies and honest records | Dataset/capture verification, registered analysis, replication, evaluation interfaces | Protected outcomes, trial accounting and existing scientific owners govern |

Each Opus coordinator decomposes its domain into useful deliverables, reconciles worker outputs, settles local questions and returns an integrated result to Fable. It should not forward raw logs as its main contribution. If an added coordinator would only relay one small task, use a direct admitted worker and reserve Opus for a useful judgment role.

Preserve root identity, true parentage, budget conservation and source custody throughout descendants.
