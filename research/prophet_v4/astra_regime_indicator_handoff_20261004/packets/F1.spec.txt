MISSION. Decompose severe failures of served Prophet US picks into entry-state (selection) versus path (management) components using the retro-grade ledger, with honest small-N inference. Descriptive evidence for a management-law proposal; nothing is promoted.

(i) ENTRY-STATE MODEL. Logistic regression of SEVERE on one-hot(rotation_tercile, breadth_tercile, dfii_tercile, quad_hard_label, vol_regime, band, archetype, rank_by, sector_etf) + standardized(off_high, score). PURGED out-of-sample: order episodes by as_of; for each as_of ISO week w from the 5th week onward, train on episodes with as_of ≤ start(w) − 22 calendar days (a 21-session embargo so training outcomes are realised before the test entries), test on week w; collect OOS probabilities; AUC = Mann–Whitney statistic over all OOS episodes (implement by rank). (iii) ATTRIBUTION SPLIT. Among SEVERE episodes: share with excess_h10 ≥ 0 ("deteriorated after a non-negative H10"), share with excess_h5 < 0 and excess_h10 < 0 ("immediate failure"), remainder "other"; with week-cluster CIs; overall, by rotation_tercile, by rank_by.

(iv) PROPHET LEDGER DENOMINATOR. From ledger.jsonl: counts by outcome; mean/median stock_result_pct and days_held by outcome; join rotation_tercile at signal_date (if C1 available) and give counts of outcome × tercile. Small-N: report only counts and medians, no model.
