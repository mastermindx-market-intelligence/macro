## Product problem closed by this slice

The current Early Leadership strategy definition names a useful tactical job but does not itself contain an economic stock selector. Prophet also must not solve that gap by copying ThemeState into a second local graph or by adding another opaque points score.

This slice provides a pure research feature envelope that B10/Evaluation can compare against the incumbent on the **same candidate population and clocks**.

## New research contract

`engine/prophet_early_leadership_evidence.py` emits `prophet.early_leadership_evidence/v1`.

### Anti-self-confirmation

The peer receipt must prove:

- the candidate security is excluded;
- all aliases of the same issuer are excluded;
- exact dated membership vintage;
- exact return window and price basis;
- original peer denominator, priced denominator and coverage;
- equal-weight return, median return and positive-member share only when at least two priced peers exist.

A singleton/empty peer group is **UNAVAILABLE**, never zero support.

### Return decomposition

For matched basis/window:

`stock excess vs market = peer excess vs market + stock excess vs peer`.

This separates choosing a strengthening group from choosing an exceptional member. The identity is checked in code; it does not create a market-neutral product.

### Theme dynamics

The compiler projects owner-produced raw ThemeState legs:

- velocity / acceleration / persistence / decay risk;
- positive breadth / rising relative strength;
- leadership entropy / leader-median spread;
- exact coverage and receipts.

It requires ThemeState authority to remain `display`. It never recomputes a Theme Score.

### Economic exposure

Membership alone is not economic confirmation. The consumer distinguishes:

- `CONFIRMED_DIRECT`;
- `CONFIRMED_INDIRECT`;
- `MEMBERSHIP_ONLY`;
- `UNAVAILABLE`.

Only the first two can make the evidence package ready for a research comparison, and even that gives no live ranking authority.

### Setup and remaining opportunity

Setup lifecycle remains categorical: FORMING / ARMED / TRIGGERED / CONFIRMED / FAILED / EXTENDED / UNKNOWN.

Owner geometry produces transparent research quantities:

- risk to invalidation;
- room to the owner's chase boundary;
- target room where a target is supplied;
- gross reward/risk geometry.

A negative room-to-chase value may describe an extended entry. It does not mint `RAN_DONT_CHASE`, Buy/Sell, holding management, or B4 Availability.

## Anti-feedback and clock law

The candidate observation is closed. Board rank, score rank, display rank, Featured, manual action, plan status, future return, target outcome and similar downstream outputs are refused.

Candidate, peer, theme, exposure, setup and geometry must all be knowable by the declared decision time. Candidate/peer/theme measurement clocks and membership vintage must compose exactly.

## Output status

The envelope may say:

- `READY_FOR_RESEARCH_COMPARISON` — source facts are sufficiently coherent for B10/H1-style research;
- `ACCRUING` — useful evidence exists but economic exposure or setup support is incomplete;
- `UNAVAILABLE` — the independent peer or source/rights basis is not estimable.

Those are **research data-quality states**, not recommendations.

Every authority flag is false. The output intentionally contains no scalar score, probability, rank, recommendation, plan, B4 state, size or trading instruction.
