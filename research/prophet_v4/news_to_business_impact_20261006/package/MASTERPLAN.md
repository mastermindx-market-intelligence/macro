# Prophet News-to-Business-Impact: Evidence, Expectations and Strategic Change

Date: October 6, 2026
Status: RESEARCH AND IMPLEMENTATION PROPOSAL. Not an accepted production contract, registered trial, worker dispatch, funding approval, or predictive result.

## Executive decision

Build a budgeted, event-driven research capability that answers: what changed in a company's economic prospects, through which mechanism, against what prior expectation, with what evidence, and what remains unresolved? Do not build a frontier LLM that assigns a universal bullishness score to every headline.

The unit of expensive reasoning is a material change to an existing event or issuer thesis, not an article and not each article-ticker pair. Articles supply claims; event clusters remove redundant reporting; a maintained thesis links genuinely different milestones. Economic impact, market reaction, research priority and entry permission remain separate.

The target edge is better recognition and maintenance of consequential changes over hours, sessions and quarters. It is not winning a sub-second news race or proving that an LLM can instantly calculate the correct stock price. Useful research delivery and incremental predictive performance must be accepted separately.

Use Astra for concentrated mechanism design and adversarial scientific judgment. Retain the existing Fable-led Prophet R6 delivery responsibility, subject to fresh runtime/custody checks before any implementation. Delegate deterministic implementation and bounded extraction to economical qualified workers. This proposal does not transfer an active operation.

## 2. What the output means

A story can be important without being bullish. A bullish business development can be expected. A surprise can be priced immediately. A valuable long-run investment can reduce next-quarter cash flow. An excellent thesis can have no safe current entry.

Publish separate fields with definitions and evidence:

1. **Event materiality:** economic size relative to the issuer, exposure and horizon, or an explicitly unquantified strategic significance.
2. **Expectation change:** difference from an admissible prior, with the baseline type named.
3. **Impact mechanism:** revenue, margins, share count/capital structure, financing/survival, durability of excess returns, strategic option, or market-only sympathy/positioning.
4. **Evidence maturity:** what is verified about the announcement, implementation and outcomes. Official publication confirms what management said, not that management's forecast will occur.
5. **Market response:** observed response through the current decision cut and uncertainty about remaining incorporation.
6. **Research priority:** attention allocation, not trade permission.
7. **Entry availability:** the unchanged, separately owned B4 result.

A 99 research-priority percentile would mean top one percent in a declared reference population and period, not a 99 percent probability of appreciation. Do not ship the percentile until the sorting policy itself is accepted. Early delivery can show owner order and named, observable filters.

A future probability must name its event, horizon, threshold and population. Examples are probability of a fiscal-period-matched EPS consensus increase above a preregistered threshold within 60 days; probability of positive net residual return over 21 sessions; or probability of an explicitly defined commercial milestone by a stated date. These targets are not interchangeable. Rare top-tail confidence is unavailable until data support it.

## 3. Per-story analysis: ordered, cheap-first, loss-aware

### 3.1 Capture and eligibility

Use lawful existing feeds. Preserve publisher time, source availability, first observation, processing time, document revision, rights and language. Do not label a headline-only feed as full-text understanding. Quarantine corrupt data or uncertain rights rather than sending it to a model.

Apply existing low-value rules to articles, not blanket company/event classes. A routine roundup is different from new quantified guidance embedded in a roundup. An opinion piece can point to a primary filing: mark it secondary and investigate the filing; do not turn an editorial into fact. Preserve rejection/defer reasons and sample rejects for missed material events.

### 3.2 Resolve who did what

Use issuer/security identities and as-of aliases. Resolve whether a company is the subject, counterparty, competitor, supplier, customer, or incidental mention. Mention count is not economic exposure. Unknown direction or role remains unknown.

Extract an atomic fact packet: actor, action, object, amount, unit/currency, fiscal period, geography, commitment status, conditions, dates and source spans. Use deterministic parsers for structured filings and quantities where reliable; a compact model handles ambiguous language. Bind every extracted number and claim to its exact source revision. Validate negations, ranges, basis, total versus incremental amounts and planned versus completed actions.

### 3.3 Identify the real change

Perform exact/near deduplication through qbus first. A new article may repeat an old event. A revised article may contain one crucial new condition. Cluster membership should not suppress novel facts inside a familiar story.

Compare against the previous owner-valid facts and known commitments. Separate:
- new reporting about an old fact;
- a corrected fact;
- a new magnitude, timing, commitment or counterparty;
- an independently observed implementation milestone;
- commentary or a prediction about those facts.

Reporting breadth remains useful as an attention measure. It cannot raise economic confidence merely because syndication created 30 URLs.

### 3.4 Attach the expectation baseline

Use a type-tagged hierarchy: period/basis-matched licensed consensus; prior company guidance or contract; prior owner-captured analyst estimate; a disclosed model baseline; historical base rate; or UNKNOWN. These are distinct objects, not interchangeable fallbacks for consensus surprise.

Attach baseline timestamp, contributors, coverage, period, basis and age. Comparing FY+1 snapshots across a fiscal rollover can create a fake revision; use the revisions owner's period anchors. Post-announcement analyst upgrades cannot be inserted into the pre-announcement baseline.

No price response is not proof of market ignorance. The event might have been expected, small, offset by other news or expensive to exploit. Without a defensible baseline, say that surprise or mispricing is unmeasured.

### 3.5 Source text is data, never tool authority

A news article, filing attachment or linked repository can contain malicious instructions. Extraction runs without write tools, arbitrary network access, trading tools or credential access. Source text cannot change the system prompt, select a provider, request another download or authorize a trade. Use closed output schemas, bounded quoted evidence and allowlisted retrieval through the existing owner. Validation rejects unknown fields and unsolicited instructions; suspicious items remain quarantined with a reason. A cited sentence is evidence for what the source says, not evidence that the sentence is true.

## 4. Translate facts into economic channels

Use a small, reviewed mechanism library rather than unconstrained price-target prose. Each mechanism has allowed inputs, a causal chain, contrary cases, required observations, costs, lags and falsifiers. Novel events remain eligible for strategic review instead of being forced into the nearest template.

| Channel | Main analytical question | Necessary discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue / demand | Does this create incremental funded demand, pricing, share or capacity utilization? | Distinguish addressable market, pipeline, backlog, bookings and recognized revenue; check cancellations and cannibalization. |
| Margin / cost | Does the change improve gross profit or reduce sustainable cost? | Separate one-off accounting effects from recurring cash economics; include integration and ramp costs. |
| Capital allocation | Do execution, financing and share-count changes improve per-share economics? | Authorization is not execution; include dilution, price paid, cash opportunity cost, debt and taxes. |
| Moat / duration | Does evidence change how long excess returns might persist? | Track switching costs, distribution, complements, pricing power and competitive response; do not convert adjectives into multiple points. |
| Strategic option | Does the company gain a credible, funded path to a previously uncertain market? | Track feasibility, adoption, monetization, retained economics, resource constraints and deadlines. |
| Financial incentives / survival | Does an award, subsidy, tax change or refinancing alter cash flow or failure risk? | Separate award ceiling, obligation, disbursement, eligibility conditions and possible clawback. |
| Market-only effect | Is there common-factor, attention, positioning or index demand without an operating change? | Keep it out of the earnings-impact head. It can still be a separately tested market hypothesis. |

A simple driver bridge can structure a demand claim: incremental eligible units x realized revenue per unit x incremental margin, less operating costs, cash taxes and required capital. Inputs can be intervals or unavailable. The multiplication does not make unsourced assumptions factual.

For long-duration claims, represent multiple scenarios including failure, niche adoption, broad adoption and displacement/cannibalization. Their probabilities must come from accepted empirical or explicitly judgmental assumptions. No numerical fair value is required when the ranges are indefensible. Do not count a new market's cash flows once in the core forecast and again as a separate option premium. Likewise, avoid counting the same moat extension as both persistent excess profit and an unexplained extra multiple.

The system should report a hypothesis as economically interesting but not yet quantifiable when that is the truthful result. This is useful if it also names the next discriminating evidence.

## 5. Clusters: an evolving thesis, not a bag of bullish stories

Use three levels, retaining existing owner identities:

- article/source manifestations of an event;
- distinct facts and revisions within that event;
- a thesis view linking multiple events and milestones over time.

A strategic cluster might progress through a product release, an independently confirmed customer deployment, recurring usage, paid bookings and reported economics. The key information is the changing bottleneck, not the article count. The order matters: an announced partner name before deployment has different implications from independently verified production use.

For every thesis view retain: original proposition and decision cut; supporting and contrary claim IDs; root source families; exposure paths; required assumptions; current milestone evidence; next expected observation; deadline; and conditions that weaken or invalidate the thesis. This is derived through existing event/claim/plan owners, not a new lifecycle service.

Do not apply naive independent Bayesian updates to correlated reports. A partner announcement and a vendor release can share one originating claim. Even an independent measurement may be correlated with another measurement. Start with conservative provenance groups and qualitative updates; fit likelihood ratios or calibrated models only with adequate labeled data.

Recompute only for material deltas: changed numeric terms, a new independent evidence source, a milestone, contradiction, missed deadline, source correction, exposure change, or relevant market-state change. Cosmetic wording and unchanged mirrors should not trigger another frontier call. Scheduled deadline checks must use an existing admitted scheduler; this research creates none.

## 6. Worked example: NVIDIA's $150 billion authorization

The issuer's September 28, 2026 release announces an additional $150 billion of buyback authorization, bringing remaining authorization to $235 billion, with planned execution through fiscal 2028 [P1]. Those are distinct quantities and an intention, not a receipt for completed purchases.

The correct packet should identify capital return and prospective per-share effects, not a revenue/operating-earnings uplift. Important questions: incremental versus replacement capacity; actual execution pace; price paid; expected free cash flow; lost cash income or financing cost; employee dilution; existing market expectations; and competing investment demands.

Illustrative arithmetic, NOT a valuation of NVIDIA: with a hypothetical $5 trillion equity capitalization, $150 billion is 3 percent of capitalization. If that cash actually retires 3 percent of shares at a fixed price, no new shares appear, and net income is unchanged, the denominator-only EPS change is 1/0.97 - 1 = 3.09 percent. Execution timing and the weighted-average diluted share count matter. Net income can change because of cash income, financing and tax effects.

Per-share EPS accretion is not automatic value creation. In a frictionless fair-value illustration, a company worth 100 with 100 shares repurchases 10 shares at 1 each. Equity value falls to 90 after spending 10, shares fall to 90, and value per share remains 1. Valuation gains require additional assumptions such as undervaluation, altered financing or improved capital discipline.

Expected initial output: verified authorization; capital-allocation significance requiring issuer-relative context; operating revenue impact not established; prospective denominator effect conditional on execution; surprise unknown without a valid baseline; no automatic semiconductor read-through; entry unchanged. The next useful evidence is actual repurchases and net dilution, not a twentieth reprint.

## 7. Worked example: an NVIDIA open-model release

NVIDIA has issued public open-model announcements, including Nemotron 3 on December 15, 2025 [P2]. Treat a specific release as a specific event: announced models, actually downloadable artifacts and their licenses, supported hardware, deployment availability and separately evidenced adoption. Do not equate the phrase open source with unrestricted rights or every promised variant being available.

A positive mechanism to test is: lower development friction -> more production applications -> more relevant compute demand or software attachment -> retained NVIDIA economics. A durability mechanism is: greater developer familiarity and integration -> switching costs or distribution -> longer persistence of attractive returns.

Contrary mechanisms must be equally explicit: more efficient models lower compute per workload; deployments shift to non-NVIDIA hardware; benchmarks fail to translate into adoption; adoption is subsidized; competitors capture the economics; maintenance and training costs exceed monetization; or the strategy was already well anticipated.

Track a staged evidence bundle, not a single rising confidence counter. Useful measures can include independent deployments, repeated active use, production renewals, appropriate hardware mix, support revenue, incremental orders, gross margin and competitive retention. Downloads, social engagement and repository stars are attention proxies, not profit receipts.

The first release may warrant high strategic research interest while near-term earnings impact remains unquantified. A later cluster can become more consequential when the weakest link gains credible support. For example, new paying demand plus independently verified deployment can resolve a monetization uncertainty that benchmark announcements cannot.

A strategic thesis can remain alive when entry is unavailable, but a missed commercial milestone must weaken it. A losing tactical trade must not be relabeled a long-term platform thesis after the fact.

## 8. Group, theme and supply-chain transfer

Consume the existing three-graph separation [R5]: economic relationships; fundamental/narrative comparability; and residual market behavior. These answer different questions. MAG7 or megacap membership may support a factor/attention explanation. A semiconductor label does not prove shared end-market demand. GPU and memory participants can have different bottlenecks, contract economics and competitive exposures.

For each source event-target pair, require the target generator, role and direction, economic mechanism when supportable, exposure magnitude or UNKNOWN, timing, alternatives, and the existing graph's provenance. A customer capital-spending increase can help a supplier; a supplier shortage can hurt the buyer; a competitor's product improvement can hurt an incumbent. Same-group moves are not automatically positive economic transmission.

Limit the pilot to direct issuer effects and source-backed one-hop relationships. Second-hop expansions require a separately justified path and uncertainty treatment. A graph path is a research candidate, not authorization. Do not propagate a score recursively or permit cycles to amplify it. One macro shock touching many firms is not many independent shocks.

Preselect eligible peer sets by documented exposure, not by which set rallied most. For market response, use coefficients fitted before the event and matched windows. Leave the target out of peer-return/breadth measures where practical so its own move cannot confirm itself. Collinear market, sector, MAG7 and GPU terms cannot simply be subtracted or summed independently.

Current K3-D PR #6514 explicitly refuses participation-only target admission and new predictive authority [R6]. Its restrictions remain in force for its candidate contract. Any future tested predictor is a separate accepted consumer of allowed facts, not a hidden score smuggled into this compiler.

## 9. Market incorporation and the achievable edge

Maintain two distinct observation modes. At-origin features use only data available before the decision. At a later landmark, features may use price and news observed up to that landmark; its outcome window starts afterward. Never use the full day's move to justify a forecast supposedly made at 09:35.

Measure absolute and residual response, gaps, liquidity, options information when lawfully available, revisions and later operating outcomes. Attribution remains uncertain when multiple events coincide. An abnormal return is not proof of causation, and a flat price is not proof that information is unappreciated.

Historical research motivates testing stale-news treatment, investor attention and economic-link transmission [P3-P5]. It does not establish that those effects remain profitable for today's NVIDIA, at our execution speed and costs.

The proposed advantages are: persistent issuer-specific understanding; reduction of repeated reporting; patient tracking of strategic evidence; typed economic-versus-factor explanations; explicit counterevidence; and detection of meaningful changes that survive our latency. Our own label set, correction histories, exposure verification and measured routing failures can become valuable accumulated assets. None is a demonstrated moat or alpha result yet.

Treat seconds-scale announcement trading as outside the pilot. Proposed operational targets are timely factual visibility after first admissible feed receipt, selective reviews within a bounded operating window, and nightly thesis consolidation. Measure achieved latency. Test several realistic delayed-entry policies rather than assuming the release print was executable. Source delays, research time, spreads and gaps all enter the evaluation.

## 10. Model economy and capacity

Proposed processing order: existing ingestion -> cheap deterministic screening and dedup -> compact extraction on distinct material deltas -> validation -> issuer/exposure lookup -> deterministic mechanism calculations -> selective stronger-model adjudication -> rare frontier thesis review -> existing evidence/Prophet consumers.

Precompute issuer driver dossiers and reusable mechanism cards. Cache by document revision, event facts, baseline, relationship generation, model/prompt/schema version and rights state. A source correction invalidates dependent analyses. A model update does not retroactively rewrite old predictions.

The expensive review packet should contain the new evidence, old thesis, relevant driver/exposure facts, alternative mechanisms and the unresolved decision. It should not contain months of duplicated articles. Analyze a macro or industry event once, then derive issuer-specific exposures; do not run the same frontier prompt for every company sharing a tag.

Start with transparent escalation rules: material issuer-relative change; disputed high-consequence fact; unfamiliar mechanism; contradiction to an active thesis; or time-sensitive adverse information. Reserve 10-15 percent of review capacity for random/stratified low-score and rejected-item audits so the cheap router cannot hide its mistakes. These are proposed workload controls, not capital-allocation rules.

Use the existing router, metering, concurrency admission, queue and retry owners. No shadow router or per-chat multiplied concurrency budget. Preserve all inputs when model budgets are exhausted; mark inference deferred and maintain basic factual visibility. Do not turn unprocessed into neutral or silently truncate the tape. Adverse-event visibility should not depend on an expensive analyst call. A novel low-confidence story can be more deserving of review than an obvious duplicate high-confidence one.

## 11. Research and evaluation plan

Use broad cheap discovery over the currently lawful available universe, with deep thesis maintenance initially for 100 issuers across several economically distinct clusters. Include less-followed names and failures, not only megacaps and famous winners. Related source events outside the 100 remain admissible inputs. Coverage counts must distinguish discovered items, unique events, extracted facts, issuer impacts, deferred reviews and evaluated episodes.

Create 600 adjudicated event-issuer-cut examples: 200 capital-allocation events, 200 demand/guidance/contract events, and 200 strategic product/partnership events. Mix positive, negative, ambiguous and no-change cases; include indirect-transfer targets and deliberately misleading near-duplicates. This is an extraction/routing benchmark, not proof of 99th-percentile return forecasts. Duplicate articles and related issuers from one event stay in the same train/test group.

Freeze questions and primary endpoints before outcomes:

- Fact integrity: critical amounts, units, periods, negation, conditionality and source spans.
- Clustering integrity: pair precision/recall, false event merges, missed novel terms in familiar clusters, and root-source independence.
- Routing quality: cost at fixed material-event recall, coverage of strategically important nonnumeric events, reject-audit miss rate and calibration of defer/escalate decisions.
- Economic reasoning: whether a proposed mechanism follows from source-backed facts, represents negative channels and names a falsifier. Independent reviewers adjudicate disagreements; LLM self-confidence is not the label.
- Business outcomes: period-matched revisions where legally available, reported operating changes, actual buybacks, and preregistered strategic milestones. Missing outcomes remain missing.
- Market outcomes: net returns at declared delays and horizons, market/group-relative results, drawdowns and coverage. Compare identical populations and actual execution conventions.

Baselines: current news stack; deterministic materiality plus existing price/factor information; a cheap extractor with no frontier review; the proposed cascade; and frontier-only analysis on a small stratified audit set. Ablate novelty, source independence, economics, group transfer, strategic milestones and price-conditioned updates separately.

Use chronological holdouts and an untouched prospective cohort. Cluster uncertainty by source event and calendar period; treat repeated issuer landmarks as dependent. Preserve delistings, corrections, data failures and rejected/deferred cases. Do not select the historically winning group or parameter after reading its returns.

Current LLMs can know the outcomes of famous historical events even when the prompt contains only old documents. Historical extraction/reasoning QA is useful, but it is not clean predictive proof. Use dated model vintages where feasible, constrain extraction to source spans, and put the strongest predictive weight on prospective performance. Masking company names is a diagnostic, not a cure. Financial-LLM evaluation literature explicitly raises look-ahead, survivorship, narrative and cost biases [P8].

Proposed acceptance thresholds, to be frozen by the owner before measurement: at least 98 percent correctness on critical numeric fields among auto-published outputs; at least 95 percent material-event routing recall on the labeled set; zero unsupported ticker-to-supplier assertions in the accepted test set; complete processing/defer/reject accounting; no duplicate mirror-driven confidence increments; and successful correction replay. Report uncertainty and sample size: zero observed errors is not a guarantee of zero future errors.

Predictive promotion needs economically meaningful improvement versus the strongest simple baseline after costs and latency, a preregistered dependence-aware interval, and acceptable adverse-error/coverage tradeoffs. Determine sample requirements from power and event dependence rather than a universal small N. A short pilot cannot validate multi-quarter strategic payoff. If prediction fails, keep useful factual/research features, demote the predictive head, and investigate only evidence-motivated alternatives.
