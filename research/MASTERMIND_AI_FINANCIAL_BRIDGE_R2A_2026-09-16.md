## Contract
`analyze_financial_bridge(payload)` accepts one closed object: basis `supplied_scenario`, a common uppercase three-letter currency, amount_scale from units/thousands/millions/billions, and prior/current objects. Both periods declare the same literal integer `period_months` (1–24). Optional values are revenue, gross_margin_pct, operating_expenses, depreciation_amortization, cash_interest, cash_taxes, working_capital_increase, capital_expenditures, other_operating_cash_adjustments, eps and earnings_multiple. Omitted values remain null, never zero. Numeric strings represent decimal inputs, not executable expressions; booleans, non-finite, oversized, structural, and unknown inputs are rejected without echoing their contents.

No natural-language extraction is claimed. All quantities are `caller_supplied_unverified`. The common unit/scale and comparable periods are supplied assumptions, not an identity or period verifier. No date is inferred from the wall clock. Mismatched period lengths and unknown per-row units are rejected. EPS is always currency per share, never scaled by amount_scale; a P/E product requires positive EPS, positive multiple and a 12-month earnings period. Quarter EPS is not silently multiplied by four.

Gross profit = revenue × gross_margin_pct / 100. Operating profit = gross profit − operating_expenses. Simplified operating cash = operating profit + depreciation_amortization − cash_interest − cash_taxes − working_capital_increase + other_operating_cash_adjustments. Cash after capex = simplified operating cash − capital_expenditures. Every cash adjustment must be supplied explicitly; this is not a reconstructed GAAP/IFRS cash-flow statement or a claimed standardised free-cash-flow measure.

The gross-profit change bridge separates sales at the prior margin from margin change at current sales; the terms exactly sum to gross-profit change. The price comparison separates EPS change at the prior multiple from multiple change at current EPS. This decomposition ordering is explicit and has no causal interpretation. Missing/zero/negative denominators retain a change amount but not a misleading growth percentage. Negative operating profit/cash remains negative, not clamped away.

The result contains exact input decimal strings and calculation strings (up to 12 significant digits, half-even, with explicit rounded flags; tiny nonzero losses never round to zero), named formulas, operand references, transitive missing-input lists, mathematical observations and fixed limitations. Computation uses a local Decimal context, no eval, no imports chosen by the caller, no filesystem/network/provider access, no persistence and no clock. Output is bounded by the fixed two-period, closed-field calculation set. The CLI reads at most 32 KiB from stdin, rejects duplicate JSON keys/non-finite constants and emits exactly one JSON result.
