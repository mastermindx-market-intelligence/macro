## 5. Principal economic and scientific decisions

### Measurement is not a universal money score

Distinguish central-bank assets, reserves, broad money, private/foreign-currency credit, funding conditions and market liquidity. BIS global liquidity concerns ease of financing and its indicators emphasize non-bank credit; the incumbent three-CB asset factor is a different measurement. Keep the vector and exact units instead of averaging unlike quantities.

For dollar value V=qL, preserve the exact decomposition `ΔV=q_previous×ΔL + L_previous×Δq + ΔL×Δq`. Unchanged local assets plus stronger FX is not domestic money creation. Freeze treatment of the interaction before viewing returns. For reserves use full assets less non-reserve liabilities and capital; assets−TGA−RRP is an incomplete proxy, not a global accounting identity or automatic risk-on signal. Forecast financing mix and other liability changes explicitly rather than treating assumptions as observations.

Global credit remains null until comparable release-qualified coverage exists. Do not splice US C&I and China TSF into a supposed causal global scalar or revive discontinued monetary histories without current source qualification.

### Historical knowledge and forecast components

Bind economic/reference, release, first-system-known, vintage/correction, source version, use rights and decision time. Conservative date-lag alignment is not a historical vintage archive. A date-level FRED vintage is not an intraday publication timestamp. Treasury actual cash data, published plans, assumptions and statistical residuals are separate roles. Current historical revisions cannot be retroactively made first-known by this producer.

Begin future-liquidity work with the existing US Treasury/funding sources identified on board #123. Deliver announced/conditional accounting context independently of a fitted forecast. A statistical residual must beat persistence, announced-only and simple seasonal/autoregressive baselines on identical mature origins before being described as a forecast improvement. Composed component intervals must propagate correlated errors; sums of marginal quantiles are not automatically aggregate quantiles.

### Prediction and causal identification differ

An expanding z-score or prior-only residual ensures an information-ordering property, not exogeneity. A threshold-defined event is an observed state change, not automatically an external policy shock. State whether the target is predictive association, total causal response, direct effect or publication surprise.

Policy reaction to growth, central-bank information effects, anticipation and simultaneous funding changes are explicit threats. Specify the causal diagram, pre-known conditioning set, relevance/exclusion assumptions and placebo/pre-response tests before outcomes. A weak or indefensible instrument yields NOT_IDENTIFIED. Do not force causality to satisfy an ambitious product title; a separately qualified predictive association can still be useful.

The funding composite contains dollar, real-yield and HY spread changes. Target/input overlap can produce mechanical explanatory success. Registered direct-input and leave-target-family-out comparisons must isolate incremental information. Lagged inputs can be legitimate predictors; they are not automatically independent transmission discoveries.

### Dynamic responses require honest uncertainty

Reuse the existing response skeleton as a baseline. A richer local-projection/state-conditioned model must justify its incremental purpose. Local projections estimate horizon-specific responses; identification still comes from the design. Report full registered paths and distinguish pointwise intervals from simultaneous bands or an overall effect test. A noisy maximum at one lag is not a precise repricing clock.

Count independent macro episodes/time blocks, not targets×horizons or repeated weekly rows. Purge overlaps and preserve common-shock dependence, era concentration and all failed cases. Hierarchical borrowing requires an explicit exchangeability argument and separate target/prior support. Use existing canonical statistical judges; no new evaluation platform.

### A gap is not expected remaining upside

Let original reference path m0(u) and actual path a(u) define an elapsed discrepancy d(u)=m0(u)−a(u). The distinct quantity is expected return from the current admissible origin u to the original fixed exit H, conditional on current information. Simply using m0(H)−a(u) is not an accepted forecast of that quantity.

Retain the original forecast, append any update with its own known-at facts, and keep the exit fixed. New information may reverse or eliminate the expectation. Withhold percentage-complete when the expected denominator is near zero or sign-uncertain. Before incremental out-of-sample qualification, describe an unexplained/reference-path divergence rather than investable mispricing.
