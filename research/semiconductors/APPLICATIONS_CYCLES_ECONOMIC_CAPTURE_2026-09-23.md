## 1. Material conclusion

The correct scope is **beyond AI processors**, not a permanent list of AI-free companies. Application, semiconductor function, business, product configuration and economic mechanism are independent facets. The first two installments supplied physical/manufacturing/commercial/capacity/economic views and upstream material/IP dependencies. This installment connects those views to user jobs and the operating mechanisms that may or may not improve recurring earnings.

Three explanations of increasing supplier sales must remain distinct: stronger final consumption; inventory replenishment; and more or different semiconductor content per end product. Pricing, share, currency and acquisitions add further explanations. We must not label all of them secular demand growth.

The proposed user journey is: application or research slice -> required functions -> source-scoped implementation -> alternatives/displaced content -> observed operating changes -> economic interpretation with falsifier -> existing company and theme workflow. More source links or a larger ticker list is not the outcome. No research result automatically originates a trade.

## 4. Eight economic distinctions, with real source examples

### A. Inventory turnover is not inventory liquidation

Microchip reported inventory days falling from 185 to 175 between March and June 2026, while inventory dollars rose from $1,035.4m to $1,047.3m. Its FY2027 Q1 actually ended June 30, 2026. [NA01]

Keep company, distributor and end-user inventory separate. A ratio can improve through its denominator. Falling days are not necessarily falling dollars or units. Qualitative management commentary is not a independently measured final-demand series.

### B. Revenue can grow through price without more shipped bits

Nanya's second-quarter release reports 68.2% sequential revenue growth, flat bit shipments and ASP growth above 60%. Its first-phase 2028 capacity remains a plan. [NA16]

Preserve the inequality rather than setting price growth to exactly 60%. Do not solve missing quantities from rounded prose or generalize a supplier observation into a universal memory price. Price-cycle and volume-cycle theses need different falsifiers.

### C. End markets can diverge inside one company

Sandisk's quarter ended July 3 shows Datacenter revenue up 103% sequentially, Edge up 48%, and Consumer down 32%. ADI separately reports Industrial, Automotive, Communications and Consumer. [NA14, NA03]

Match vendor definitions, period, currency and consolidation before thematic mapping. Edge is not automatically all edge-AI hardware; Industrial is not all robotics. Consolidated growth cannot be assigned to every subtheme.

### D. Cash flow needs a definition bridge

TI's trailing twelve months ended June 30 show CFO $8,667m, capex $3,312m, incentive proceeds $1,179m and issuer-defined FCF $6,534m. The separately derived CFO-minus-capex figure is $5,355m. Preserve both labels and the reconciliation. [NA02]

Tower Q1 separates $290m increased customer prepayments from $225m CFO excluding them, alongside $156m net PP&E. Derived cash-minus-investment comparisons are $359m including that funding effect and $69m excluding it. These are explicit arithmetic comparisons, not new GAAP measures. [NA10]

Identify incentives, customer funding, asset transactions and gross/net spending before comparing apparently identical cash-yield metrics. Reported definitions are not overwritten by house analytics.

### E. Net profit is not pure foundry operating performance

UMC reports NT$14,950m operating income, NT$30,236m non-operating income including NT$30,045m investment gains, and NT$42,260m net income attributable to the parent. The retrieved release stops partway through a later geometry table. Unrecovered capacity counts are not imported. [NA09]

Tower's $680m silicon-photonics annual run rate cannot be divided by $460m total quarter revenue as a quarterly exposure ratio. The periods/measurement concepts differ. [NA11]

### F. Parent/child financial aggregation can double count

ST segment leaves sum to $3,487m: 1,426 + 464 + 1,147 + 445 + 5. Group subtotals 1,890 and 1,592 are contained revenue, not additional sales. [NA06]

Company representations need financial containment as well as physical containment. Never sum parent group and child segment values into one total.

### G. Accounting bases and rounded rows are distinct

Renesas reports IFRS revenue JPY418.4bn versus non-GAAP revenue JPY405.3bn; the release also provides rounded reconciliation rows for non-recurring revenue and expenses. Do not force displayed rounded values into an invented exact unrounded identity. [NA05]

A normalized analytical measure must retain its definition, source inputs and adjustment policy; it is not a replacement for the issuer's reported measure.

### H. Local tightness can coexist with spare capacity

ST attributes tightness to several categories while retaining unused-capacity charges. Infineon's reservation narrative combines concluded and in-negotiation agreements. [NA06, NA08]

Scope constraints to product/process/facility/time/status. Global spare capacity does not refute a specific qualified bottleneck; a local bottleneck does not prove universal shortage. A combined negotiation-plus-agreement amount is not wholly signed backlog.

## 5. Economic capture and valuation: explicit reasoning, not automatic stock conclusions

NXP's centralization narrative includes removing modules, and the Bosch relationship includes samples. The SiC package agreement is an MOU, not universal qualified substitution. The common Si/GaN driver announcement is sampling-stage evidence. [NA19, NA25, NA26]

The proposed analytical chain is:

`End-system activity -> qualified content/configuration -> supplier share and recognized sales -> price/mix and cost -> recurring profit -> cash/capital needs -> per-share expectations -> valuation`

Substitution can redistribute value rather than increase every layer. An integrated stage can replace separate components; a different bus can eliminate a conversion step; central compute can displace modules. Determine what actually remains in the selected implementation before assigning economic exposure.

A simple stock-flow illustration is supplier shipments = customer consumption + change in downstream inventory, provided units, product, period and perimeter match. It is not a fitted sector model. Unknown channel movements stay unknown.

For positive comparable earnings and a consistent P/E definition, price = EPS × multiple. A hypothetical 20% EPS increase with 20% multiple contraction produces a 4% price decline: `1.20 * 0.80 - 1 = -0.04`. This is arithmetic, not a return forecast. Negative/discontinuous earnings require another explicitly chosen framework. A technology-growth narrative is not sufficient for valuation or trade conclusions.

A useful future card should state which evidence changes expected units, content, prices, costs, duration, capital needs or uncertainty; what was already expected; and what would disprove the interpretation. Point-in-time expectations, corporate actions, market inputs and accepted evaluation remain required before predictive/ranking use. No model is built or validated here.

## 6. Sensor and configuration requirements

Hall mechanism, acceleration-only power, full-IMU operating modes, image bit depth and downstream HDR compositing are meaning-bearing conditions. Removing them can select a component unable to complete the task. [NA20–NA24]

Keep chip/device/package/board/system bases separate. A supported system-current range is not current flowing through the sensor IC. An STO-enabling circuit proposal does not certify a complete machine. Shared footprint does not remove firmware, demodulation, electrical, thermal or qualification work.

Parsed page furniture can contain mutually exclusive stock-availability messages. It cannot be treated as an observed live inventory state without the relevant bound context and accepted admission. Source freshness alone does not fix scope or measurement errors.

## 10. Shared-product requirements

Preserve existing GMI evidence/curation/identity, K1 references and F04 presentation. No duplicate template, product master, latest-state file, scheduler, queue or publisher. Source-scoped configurations remain source-scoped until the accepted identity owner supports stronger binding.

The existing Themes workflow should answer: what changed; which application/function/business is affected; through what economic mechanism; what is the strongest alternative explanation; and what observation is needed next. Coverage counts do not measure attractiveness.

Financial, physical and commercial containment need typed views. A source's extraction confidence cannot become revenue purity. Live aggregates require validated security, period, currency, scope and a non-overlapping methodology. Unknown values remain unknown, not zero or a model-authored percentage.

Preserve source publication, business-effective, observed/retained, correction and first-known clocks separately. Later facts cannot leak backward into evaluation. A stale configuration can be a useful historical example; a fresh out-of-scope claim can still be unsuitable. Comparisons should visibly refuse or separate incompatible operating modes, conditions, fiscal periods, accounting bases and run-rate measures.

These are proposed requirements for the later written specification, not an implementation plan approved by this research. User-facing acceptance still requires admitted real source -> native owners -> existing authenticated product -> useful visible result and browser proof. None occurred here.
