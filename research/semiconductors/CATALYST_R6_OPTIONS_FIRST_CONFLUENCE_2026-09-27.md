## D51–D56: selected product and method decisions

**D51 — options-led discovery.** Options can originate a research candidate before the fundamental catalyst is known. The existing security/case universe retains options-led, fundamental-led and joint origins as metadata. A later discovered catalyst enriches that case; it does not create another investment exposure. No new candidate store or queue. Lack of a named catalyst is not a blanket exclusion.

**D52 — options-first is a real competing strategy.** Compare price/context-only, options-plus-price/context, fundamentals-plus-price/context, combined additive and combined interactions. An options-first model that independently qualifies for a declared return/timing target need not wait for a fully built fundamental model. Conversely, adding fundamentals must earn its contribution over the stronger options baseline. This is a path to qualified signals, not permanent display-only treatment.

**D53 — observable proxies can be useful without perfect actor identification.** Unknown institution or dealer inventory does not invalidate measured volume, skew or concentration as candidate predictors. It withholds the unsupported actor/hedge claim. Unknown dealer sign blocks inventory-dependent scenarios, not all options features. The classification and source limits of the feature remain explicit.

**D54 — confluence uses outcome-specific joint information, not a badge count.** A flow tide, unusual-activity card and heatmap built from the same prints are not three independent confirmations. Initial production-method proposal: regularized logistic inside the existing Alpha/evaluation owner, training-only standardization, elastic-net l1_ratio{0,.5,1}, C{.1,1,10}, chronological nested selection and initial Platt calibration on a later disjoint block. Five predeclared interactions: directional pace×event-expiry concentration; flow change×skew change; node persistence×signed regime×price response; disclosure change×skew change; prior run-up×activity×remaining expiry. No coefficient is forced to agree with a bullish story. Boosting is a deferred challenger requiring adequate data, not another service.

**D55 — direction and payoff are different outputs.** Stronger upward probability can coexist with less remaining upside, larger tail loss or expensive option premium. Keep probability, reliability, conditional equity payoff and entry economics separate. A stock-direction model does not establish the profitability of buying calls; an option expression needs its own pricing/decay/spread/deliverable model under existing owners.

**D56 — evaluate discovery before the story as well as confirmation afterward.** Preserve every eligible scan origin in the existing source/evaluation lineage, including unranked/rejected and no-known-catalyst cases. Keep optionable and non-optionable coverage visible. Main market target remains the proposed21-session net benchmark-relative total return; positive net absolute return and5-session direction are separate labels. Intraday node first-passage is a distinct family, initially60 trading minutes with an end-of-session secondary view. E1 earnings and volatility magnitude remain distinct. No retrospective case becomes a prospective forecast.

|---|---|---|
