## 3. The central modeling result: five distinct views

A single generic SUPPLIES line loses the meaning that makes industrial research useful. The proposal is five views over accepted existing ownership, not five databases:

| View | Question | Examples of required relations | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical composition | What is actually inside a specific product/package? | Contains die; contains memory stack; mounted on substrate; connected through interposer | A fabrication tool is a component of the finished chip |
| Manufacturing route | Which operations and qualified technologies produce it? | Requires etch step; patterned by lithography; bonded by a qualified process; electrically tested | All suppliers in a process category are interchangeable |
| Commercial ecosystem | Which businesses have documented relationships? | Named development agreement; disclosed supply; licensed IP; manufacturing partner | Product compatibility proves a customer contract |
| Capacity and constraints | What qualified output is available, where and when? | Facility/line capability; qualification milestone; reported output; planned expansion | Investment or an opening ceremony proves customer-qualified output |
| Economic exposure | Which business and eligible security capture the economics? | Business-to-issuer mapping; disclosed segment exposure; source-backed sensitivity | Semantic relevance equals revenue share, basket weight or expected return |

Physical containment should be acyclic within a configuration. Commercial relationships can legitimately be reciprocal. A material can be part of a package while also enabling a manufacturing step; role and scope, not label similarity, determine the relation.

## 5. Initial primary-source findings

### 5.1 HBM is a supplier-specific manufacturing stack, not one ticker label

Micron's 16 March 2026 release separates **36GB 12H HBM4 volume shipment beginning in calendar Q1** from **48GB 16H samples**. [S10] Samsung's 12 February release describes HBM4 using **1c DRAM and a 4nm logic base die**, while SK hynix's TSMC collaboration describes a different base-die supply relationship. [S08, S06, S07]

Research consequence: record supplier, memory generation, DRAM process, base-die process/provider, stack configuration, interface, intended customer and milestone separately. Do not apply one supplier's base-die relationship to all HBM. Do not convert a displayed model, a sample, a designed-for platform or a volume-shipment statement into the same commercial fact.

### 5.2 CoWoS is a family of different interconnect constructions

TSMC distinguishes CoWoS-S silicon interposers, CoWoS-R redistribution-layer interposers, and CoWoS-L combining RDL with local silicon interconnect. [S03] Research must therefore identify the package variant rather than assume that all CoWoS expansion consumes the same full-area silicon interposer.

A capacity observation needs variant, package/configuration, size convention, qualified line, time period and output unit. Reticle multiples describe a size convention, not finished packages per month. Product-page historical milestones are not the latest universal size limit.

### 5.3 The substrate chain contains different economic businesses

Ajinomoto's ABF is a dielectric build-up material, whereas Ibiden describes fabrication capacity for high-performance IC package substrates. [S11, S12] These roles should appear at separate manufacturing levels. A category connection does not establish a specifically named supply contract between the two companies.

Ibiden's February 2026 program totals approximately **JPY500 billion over FY2026-FY2028**. The **JPY220 billion first phase is included**, with sequential operation/mass production planned from FY2027. [S12] The observation is a dated investment program and planned milestone, not a measured number of qualified substrates.

### 5.4 Bonding technology must be tracked by application and generation

Besi's TCB Next orders and its hybrid-bonding portfolio are separate evidence. Its Applied Materials collaboration combines complementary process and placement capabilities. [S14, S16, S17] Samsung discusses hybrid copper bonding as a future direction in its GTC material. [S35] None of these sources establishes that every HBM4 stack already uses hybrid bonding or that TCB demand disappears on a fixed universal date.

A useful hypothesis is that tighter integration increases bonding, surface preparation and inspection requirements. The falsifier is an accepted alternative process or lower qualified intensity on the relevant product, not a generic negative headline. Procurement, installation and qualified utilization remain separate tests of economic capture.

### 5.5 Tool capacity and qualified fab capacity must not be conflated

ASML's July 2026 results discuss planned additional low-NA EUV and immersion production capacity. [S05] Besi's catalog lists dry-cycle throughput for a particular tool. [S15] One is a supplier production plan; the other is a catalog measurement under stated conditions. Neither alone measures good customer packages leaving a production line.

Lam's conductor-etch and cryogenic NAND-etch disclosures describe different process applications. [S20, S21] Tokyo Electron's catalog spans distinct resist-processing, deposition and related operations. [S22, S36] The equipment basket must retain process content and not merely tag all front-end vendors as identical AI-capex exposure.

### 5.6 The hidden supply chain is not always directly listed

ZEISS describes EUV optics and actinic mask-metrology capabilities; TRUMPF describes an explicit ASML EUV-laser relationship. [S24, S25, S26] Those are upstream equipment dependencies rather than chip contents. The operating business must be resolved before attaching a security; a similarly named listed company is not a valid substitute for issuer mapping.

### 5.7 Test is a multi-stage chain, not one generic equipment category

FormFactor describes known-good-die probing and a named manufacturing partnership with Keystone Microtech. Advantest's April 2026 release concerns a digital test instrument on V93000. [S27, S28, S29] Wafer probe cards, tester instruments and final/system-level tests have distinct functions. The cited FormFactor product's listed HBM generation must not be silently relabeled HBM4, and an AI/HPC tester announcement must not be treated as proof of a named HBM supply contract.

### 5.8 Power semiconductor manufacturing is a necessary independent branch

Infineon's SiC and GaN disclosures distinguish material, wafer diameter, manufacturing location and pilot/product-release milestones. [S31, S32] Its monitoring-IC release includes robotics and server applications. [S33] This supports a cross-theme research route, not a claim that every power device uses the same material, process or manufacturing economics. Those historical milestone sources need newer operating evidence before a present capacity conclusion.

## 7. Capacity and bottleneck reasoning proposal

A technology can be indispensable without being short of supply. Preserve five independent questions: technical necessity, observed supply-demand imbalance, qualified substitutability, switching delay, and economic capture. Do not collapse them into an unsupported single bottleneck score.

For a specified finished package, a conceptual constraint calculation can compare capacities only after conversion into compatible finished-package units. For example, good logic dies divided by required dies per package, qualified HBM stacks divided by required stacks per package, and qualified assembly/test output impose separate upper bounds. These are analytical definitions, not numerical estimates in this installment. Unknown dies-per-wafer, yield, stack allocation or package count makes the estimate unavailable.

Do not multiply correlated yields as if independent, sum capacity across unqualified substitutes, treat different wafer diameters as equivalent counts, or mix bits, stacks, wafers, revenue and packages. A full flow model also needs product mix, routing, shared-tool contention and qualification; a simple minimum is only a bounded necessary-input approximation, not a whole-industry equilibrium model.

Each candidate bottleneck should have a dated observation, comparison basis, alternative explanation, missing measurements, resolution milestone and falsifier. For example, a substrate expansion announcement may support a future supply response, but not current relief; qualification, usable output and customer allocation must be observed before declaring it resolved.

The economic hypothesis is separate: greater process intensity could support supplier demand, but realization depends on content, share, qualification, price, cost, capacity and timing. No numerical company benefit, stock target, expected return or trade follows automatically.
