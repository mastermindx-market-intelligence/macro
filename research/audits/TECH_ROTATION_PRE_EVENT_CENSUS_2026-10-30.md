## Root-cause mechanism and distinct outcomes

1. **Measured early evidence was present.** The September 30 legacy risk and AI-hardware damage monitors predate the Oct6 market high; the retained Oct9 early-context pair observations identify a specific defensive-versus-semis turn after the high and before the larger Oct12–16 leg.
2. **Risk gate mismatch:** In `engine/risk_radar.py`, `context_gate_live` requires **SPY below its 200-day average AND weak breadth**. `_resolve_state_row` caps `state` at caution when not met. The later `alert` flag is only true at elevated/risk-off, and `can_force` additionally requires above-base historical lift and a validated confirmed leg. This is a conscious whole-market false-alarm gate, not sufficient for a distinct industry-crack watch. Do **not** remove it wholesale; introduce/qualify an independent descriptive sector watch that cannot masquerade as validated broad-market crash probability.
3. **Unreconciled consumers:** `engine/rotation_events.py` produced `early_context`, but before Oct23 `templates/rotation_events.js` did not show pair-level facts. Sector Central had a compact state/coverage reading, not the same evidence rail. The October9 artifact had zero confirmed-event lifecycle events, so a confirmed-event-only notification path was empty despite measurements.
4. **Risk voices contradict in presentation:** `risk_state.v1` ELEVATED, `risk_radar.v2` Caution, `macro_risk` Low at Sep30. These may measure different risks and horizons; a reconciler must explain those axes rather than average them or silently prefer the calmest. `leadership_crack` was BROKEN Sept30 but INTACT Oct6/8; direct prospective grading must account for this reversal and false positives.
5. **Cross-product integration and alerts unproven:** `scripts/build_feeds.py` explicitly references a July 2 prior incident where the Mastermind bot missed Risk Radar even while Macro computed it. Feed packaging exists but current model/user consumption has not been proven. The source-to-per-user-rotation-outbox bridge is still absent from accepted production path. Do not count `data/subsector_rotation/alerts.jsonl` as emails; it is Alert Command Center context. No user-level receipt inspected.
6. **Live/historical freshness:** Git static `site/live/breadth.json` fallback is Jul27 even at Oct30 main; its existence does not prove the separate VPS dynamic poller lacked fresher data. Tracked rotation event JSON on current main is as_of Oct20; tracked subsector rotation as_of Oct26; turn_watch has as_of Oct29 but data_session Oct23. This requires live-source/served-byte comparison before claims about intraday warning precision.
