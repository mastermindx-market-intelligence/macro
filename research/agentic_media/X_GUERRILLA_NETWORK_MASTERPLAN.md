## §1 Why this shape — the arithmetic of borrowed distribution

The parent programme uses useful replies and profile conversion to seek audience growth. This proposal explores an additional, openly affiliated cohort; its incremental effect has not been established.

The original business hypotheses were targeted discovery, parent-content redistribution, substantive conversations and attributable links. Claimed follow-back rates of 5–15%, ranking lift from a reply band and distribution gains from a larger account count are **unvalidated hypotheses**, not operating evidence. Following people merely to induce reciprocal follows, automatic engagement and coordinated amplification face the platform constraints in the [follow brief](research/X_API_FOLLOW_LIMITS_BRIEF_2026-11.md); the proposed growth mechanism cannot be activated by lowering its cadence.

Retain the goal of useful content and measured parent conversion. Do not count organisation-owned followers as independent audience growth, manufacture independent-looking endorsements, or treat account plausibility as proof of permission or value. Any future permitted cohort must contribute distinct material and disclose its relationship to the parent.

## §9 Halt and health

The existing health/action owner must distinguish transport status, action outcome and permission. A cooldown or an HTTP response alone cannot settle an applied write.

| Observed state | Required disposition |
|---|---|
| Definitely not applied, with reliable evidence | A bounded retry may use the existing authorization and original carrier; do not expand scope |
| Applied, with canonical receipt | Retain the result and do not repeat the logical action |
| EFFECT_UNKNOWN: timeout, interrupted call or ambiguous response | Stop that logical action and reconcile its original target before retry, failover, account change or identity replacement |
| Explicit safety/permission denial | Stop the action; no alternate route or account bypass |
| Rate limit or authentication failure | Classify the actual response and settle any preceding write first; cooldown/login repair is not settlement |
| Challenge or suspension | Freeze the affected scope and require the appropriate operator/platform resolution |

The vendor follow documentation describes success for an already-following or pending relationship and an error for a rejected action. Do not map generic numeric `326` to success or infer universal semantics from string fragments. A generic HTTP 403 has several possible causes and does not prove Enterprise-only access. Billing for rejected or ambiguous calls remains unresolved until the applicable contract/usage evidence establishes it.

**Halt ladder retained:** L1 pauses an affected child after two rate-limit responses or an auth error; L2 freezes on challenge or three L1 events in a day; L3 pauses a cohort when at least 20% reach L2 within six hours; L4 stops the network on suspension or two L3 events in a week. The previous automatic-resume and one-blind-retry instructions are removed. Resume requires settled effects, valid authorization, healthy credentials and the incumbent owner's explicit eligible state; elapsed time alone is insufficient.

Preserve the existing halt controls and per-account override owner. Proposed `HALT`/`COHORT_ACTIONS_ENABLED` aliases must bind that owner rather than create a second control plane. A write-ahead intent row precedes a call; its outcome receipt follows observation. Neither row may be restamped to invent success, execution absence or live health.

## §13 Measurement and attribution

Vanity metrics are the default failure of a bot network: counts move, revenue does not, nobody can say which half of the spend worked.

**Per-child action view** (proposed `data/marketing/cohort/actions.jsonl` schema): timestamps, child ID, action, target, recipe, score, outcome, error and latency, plus a stable logical-action identifier and original carrier/receipt reference. Materialize this as a view/extension of the existing action owner. Intent, applied result and unknown result are separate states; no independent ledger or restamped health authority.

**Proposed dashboard view:** per-cohort attempts and observed outcomes, follow-back rate over comparable windows, follower/following ratio, replies and responses, cost and halt state. A falling rate warrants investigation; it does not by itself prove audience drift, platform suppression or a cause.

**Parent effect** — the only numbers that justify the spend:

1. **Follower delta per parent**, weekly, split into "arrived while cohort active" vs baseline.
2. **Engagement contribution**: measure substantive, permitted conversations against a matched baseline; exclude self-generated network activity from independent audience lift.
3. **Profile-visit proxies** where available from the read rail.

**Proposed attribution path:** a child-specific ref through the existing redirect/consent owner into the existing lead ledger. Record source and permission before adding a consumer; no endpoint, tracker or new publication/control plane is deployed by this document.

**Retire trigger:** a child below 50 % of cohort-median follow-back for 30 days, or below 15 followers at day 45, is retired (§17.4), not nursed.
