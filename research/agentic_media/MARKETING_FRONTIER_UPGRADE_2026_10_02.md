## Executive decision

Build an evidence-led editorial operation, not a larger automatic posting machine.
The always-on component is the existing deterministic runtime. Frontier bots are
stateful editorial collaborators invoked for bounded work; they do not need to
reason continuously. Mastermind retains authority over data, trading candidates,
work identities, publication, money and deployment. Bots may investigate a material
change, develop a novel explanation, propose art direction, draft, critique and abstain.

The commercial objective is qualified readers who experience the product's first
useful watchlist/research result and return, then pay. Followers are an intermediate
measure, not the optimization objective. There is no promise of reach or investment
returns. Better models are a hypothesis to evaluate, not proof that distribution or
conversion will improve.

Preserve the positioning already in config/marketing.yml: accountable AI market
intelligence; what changed, why it matters, what to watch next. Start with the
flagship and founder identities, subject to their current consent/approval state.
Do not expand the account network or paid campaigns before repeatable acquisition.
This is a proposed pilot allocation, not a change to live account configuration.

## 2. Supported bot access and three separate trust domains

Current Grok Bot documentation describes persistent cloud computers, shared files
and context, and event-triggered routines through separately configured Slack or
GitHub account integrations. That supports the proposed concept but does not prove
our registered target, credentials, delivery, consumption or return path.

Separate bots under one Grok account share a computer and credentials. They are
not security isolation. Keep publication credentials in the existing publisher's
isolated service principal, outside the editorial bots' shared computer.

Use admitted Dot/Astra or Grok workers only through supported, authorized integrations.
A provider API is an alternative worker surface, not an excuse to route around a
refusal or rate limit. Do not make the production loop depend on scraping consumer
ChatGPT output, clicking X's website, or cycling accounts to evade usage limits.
Pin the qualified model/route in the existing registry; a marketing name or a model's
self-reported identity is not runtime proof.

The trust domains are:
1. Market/data authority: existing engines own observations, candidate rankings,
   signal qualification, timestamps and deterministic calculations.
2. Editorial intelligence: bots own proposed explanations, counterarguments, words
   and art direction. They cannot promote a trading score or approve their own copy.
3. Publication authority: existing approval/outbox/publisher owns account, final
   text/media binding, transport and delivery reconciliation.

An honest lane can be delayed or abstain. It must never silently replace a failed
frontier assignment with weak template copy just to fill a slot.

### 3.1 Event selection: deterministic facts, non-deterministic insight

The deterministic layer decides whether there is a material, fresh change with
adequate evidence, entitlement and budget. It must not predetermine every headline
and thesis. Let a frontier editor inspect several plausible interpretations and
request a bounded amount of additional evidence when an important comparison is missing.

Start with six event families: earnings/forward-guidance changes; material candidate
or invalidation changes; theme/subtheme leadership changes; multi-factor regime
changes; price dislocations with a documented catalyst; and matured outcomes for
prior public claims. Proposed family names are not claims of registered event schemas.
Map them to current producer contracts during integration.

Reuse the existing attention_source/content_studio/story owner. Coalesce repeat
notifications from one underlying event and respect cross-account story ownership.
A corrected event supersedes the old draft; a second publisher or a second event ID
namespace is not the solution. An outage must not trigger a flood of expired backlog.

Select on reader relevance, evidence, freshness, explanatory opportunity and the
existing deterministic quality/attention measures. Do not let marketing fabricate
or rescore Prophet recommendations. Social conversation helps identify questions;
it does not establish financial facts or institutional flows.

### 3.2 Evidence packet

A packet should reference existing immutable/source-versioned records, including:
- canonical source/event identity and revision; exact company/security mapping;
- observed time, publication time, ingestion time, valid-until time and market session;
- facts with unit, period, GAAP/non-GAAP basis, denominator, currency and source locator;
- consensus snapshot and timestamp, where licensed; guidance versus prior guidance;
- price/reaction window, benchmark and corporate-action treatment;
- admitted thesis, qualification level, invalidation and uncertainty;
- prior public claims and outcomes, including mistakes and expired theses;
- account voice/policy versions, already-used angles and allowed media specifications;
- model-processing and public-use entitlements, separately assessed.

Do not treat eight syndicated versions of a press release as eight independent sources.
Do not turn a privately licensed institutional report into a public excerpt merely
because a model can summarize it. Exclude non-admitted material before model access;
then independently verify the final public claims and any quotations/illustrations.

Use existing market/news/earnings/vault producers first. Useful additional source
classes are issuer IR and regulatory filings, SEC submissions/XBRL, official central
bank releases, and revision-aware FRED/ALFRED observations. SEC provides public
submissions and financial fact APIs; FRED vintage dates distinguish releases/revisions.
These do not supply a complete real-time global market feed, licensed consensus or
all transcript rights. Those are separate capability and entitlement checks.

### 3.3 Frontier editorial work

A bounded assignment asks the writer to identify the most useful change, compare
plausible explanations, give the strongest counter-case, draft for one account,
propose an evidence-bearing visual and list every material claim with its sources.
The worker may return ABSTAIN when the material is stale, crowded, insufficient or
not useful. It may request a specific missing observation through an allowed data
tool; it may not browse unrestricted private data or execute instructions embedded
in news, PDFs, X posts or source excerpts.

Start with one writer and one reviewer, not an always-spinning department of bots.
Reuse context across a case without freezing old facts. Expensive research belongs
on a few unusually valuable cases, not every market tick. Cost and latency must be
recorded through the incumbent model accounting path, including repairs and searches.

### 3.4 Correctness is not taste

Mechanical guards cover identity, revision, expiry, supported numbers, links,
account permissions, rights, media integrity and publication binding. Semantic
review checks whether each assertion actually follows from its sources, whether
all material assertions are covered, whether causality is overstated, and whether
the investor implication is honest.

Editorial review asks whether a reader learns something specific and useful,
whether the explanation is differentiated, and whether the visual earns its space.
A generic restatement can be rejected even when every number is accurate.

Reconcile the current v2/v5 contradiction repair first. Later, evaluate soft style
rules such as sentence patterns and number density as hypotheses. They are not
financial safety rules. Any policy change needs a versioned, held-out comparison;
no self-healing job may weaken factual guards to make a dashboard look green.
Model agreement alone is not evidence, and the writer cannot self-certify quality.

## 4. Content that deserves distribution

| Franchise | Reader value | Evidence-bearing visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earnings: what changed forward | Separate a backward-looking beat from guide, margin, orders and cash changes. | Actual/consensus/prior-guide comparison with period and basis labels. |
| The leadership map | Explain which subtheme is improving and what would negate that conclusion. | Point-in-time relative strength plus breadth/revision comparison. |
| Regime -> portfolio implications | Translate rates, dollar, liquidity and dispersion into conditional risks. | Small causal diagram plus a few sourced series, not a fabricated risk-on score. |
| Dislocation autopsy | Distinguish new impairment from a plausible temporary shock without promising a bottom. | Price reaction versus catalyst timeline and explicit invalidation. |
| Receipts, including misses | Make prior public reasoning auditable. | Timestamped original thesis, fixed evaluation horizon, benchmark and outcomes. |
| Research disagreement | Show where source-backed interpretations disagree and what observation would resolve it. | Argument/evidence/counter-case map with permitted attribution. |

Illustrative, NOT a claim about a real company:
A weak draft says an earnings beat is bullish. A better draft explains that revenue
beat expectations while guidance was unchanged and gross margin weakened, so the
next confirmation is a guide increase with margin stabilization. The content earns
attention by separating historical surprise from forward improvement. Every real
instance needs its actual period, consensus source and publication-time evidence.

Avoid generic market recaps, decorative ticker collages, unexplained screen outputs,
unsupported confidence language and highlight-only performance cards. Do not say
that rising relative strength proves institutional buying or that a historical
backtest establishes a safe entry.

The frontier model supplies the editorial question and art direction, not invented

visual inspection must look at the rendered image, not merely trust its JSON spec.
Use immutable asset hashes and the existing public-fetch/MIME/digest validation;
late image replacement invalidates approval for the previous text/media payload.

The X create-post API accepts previously uploaded media IDs and exposes a
made_with_ai field for AI-generated-media disclosure. Qualification must check the
current endpoint, account entitlement and active transport. The present source
slice accepts only an existing media-spec ID or an art-direction suggestion; it
cannot attach a new arbitrary URL or upload a picture.

## 7. Learning and self-development

Use existing exemplar_store, persona memories, thesis/receipt history and the
nightly learning owner. Store approved successes, instructive failures and outcomes
with their evidence/prompt/model/account/format versions. Separate stable voice
preferences from currently true financial facts. Bot memory is a convenience,
never the authority for a claim, a permission or a publication decision.

Run two distinct evaluations. First compare current v2 versus the frontier route
on identical evidence to isolate editorial benefit. Next compare the complete
frontier research process against the baseline to measure incremental evidence
benefit. A proposed starter benchmark has 60 representative cases across the six
franchises, with 30 held out from prompt tuning. Include stale, corrected,
contradictory, unlicensed and no-story examples, not only easy winners.

Use blinded human pairwise judgments, independently verified financial claims,
and deterministic aggregation of results. Report sample sizes, ties, uncertainty,
cost per accepted artifact and the rate of abstention/critical errors. A model's
self-score is not the promotion criterion. No claim that 60 cases prove safety.

For live experiments, publish only one assigned version of a story; do not create
duplicate posts for an A/B test. Compare matched event/account/time windows and
predeclare the measurement horizon. Do not chase single viral outliers, treat
low exposure as proof of bad writing, or open a bandit before adequate observations.
Source freshness, writing quality, distribution and conversion are separate failure
hypotheses with separate interventions.

Self-development means bounded repair proposals in isolated branches, regression
and negative tests, independent review, and controlled rollout through the existing
ship path. It does not mean a bot editing credentials, budgets, production routing,
hard safety checks or its own approval requirements. A repeated failure should
create an accountable repair obligation; a blocked repair stays visible.

## 9. Implemented first slice and its exact limits

New module: engine/marketing/frontier_editorial.py.
New offline tests: tests/test_marketing_frontier_editorial.py.

build_brief consumes an existing context plus canonical operation/attempt/source,
account, persona and policy identities, evidence refs and existing media-spec IDs.
It deep-copies bounded JSON, enforces aware timestamps, seals the payload with SHA-256
and explicitly declares draft-only intent. This is an interchange digest, NOT a
new event identity, cache owner, signature, credential or authorization mechanism.

The trusted incumbent job owner must supply current_brief using the original
attempt/window and current source/context/policy. The model cannot supply that
current snapshot. evaluate_return rejects drift, corruption, expiry, cross-attempt
returns, unknown evidence/media IDs, malformed claims and new arbitrary fields.
It invokes the existing copywriter.validate_copy lazily, or an explicitly supplied
integration validator. Missing/malformed/crashing validators fail closed.
Successful output retains the request digest and says REVIEW_REQUIRED, with
publish_authorized false. An abstention never produces a fallback template.

This does NOT prove semantic truth or complete claim coverage, source rights,
provider/runtime admission, model quality, prompt-injection resistance, image
correctness, publication or growth. Rights filtering precedes packet construction;
semantic/media/approval checks follow it. Production callers have not been changed.
No credentials, network calls, new queues, databases, workers or approval state were added.

Offline evidence: 36 unittest cases passed in the isolated supplied source slice.
Tests use an injected copy-validator double; they test the integration contract,
not the deployed validator's full behavior or the complete repository suite.
Four mutation checks intentionally disabled binding, evidence membership, expiry,
