## 4. First-class reasoning: five distinctions before any number

A financing dossier must distinguish **documented**, **registered**, **currently eligible under evidenced conditions**, **economically executable under a scenario**, and **actually issued/paid**. These are product semantics, not a replacement runtime lifecycle.

For each route or instrument, the engine must answer separately:

1. What was directly observed, in which exact source and at which time?
2. What identity, rule and contractual conditions apply?
3. What amount or share quantity can be derived under the stated assumptions?
4. What other routes consume the same constraint or represent the same underlying claim?
5. What remains unknown, and which future fact would change the answer?

A filing form is not a financing instrument. An EFFECT observation is not a generic capacity approval. A registration may concern primary issuance, resale of already outstanding securities, underlying shares not yet issued, debt, units or mixed components. The resolver must retain those distinctions and the transaction that links them. Ambiguity produces an unresolved candidate, not a guessed instrument.

A company's economic supply, securities registered for resale, presently exercisable securities and shares outstanding are different quantities. The product must never present their sum as fully diluted shares. Even a mathematically correct result needs a named basis: issued-and-outstanding, assumed as-converted, scenario incremental issuance, or potentially resalable existing shares.

## 5. The hard architecture: a joint financing-constraint model

The key design improvement is to model **shared resources and mutually dependent choices**, not compute each route independently and add its maximum. Use an owner-controlled derived constraint structure inside the Capital Structure compiler; this is not a new global graph.

For a fixed time and scenario, let nonnegative quantities represent a proposed use of each supported financing route or instrument action. Accepted documentary and rule predicates determine eligible actions. Constraints may limit dollars, shares, voting power, holder ownership, registered classes, programme balances, reserved authorized shares, covenant headroom or time windows. Every quantity has a unit, security/share basis, source/version and applicability predicate.

A mathematical model may use linear inequalities where the documents actually support them. Piecewise conversions, resets, cashless elections, notice periods or mutually exclusive settlements require explicitly enumerated branches or another reviewed exact representation. Do not assume every contract is linear or choose a heavyweight optimizer merely because the project is difficult.

**Required result:** per-route conditional bounds; a jointly feasible witness where sufficient evidence exists; shared constraints that bind; blockers; unknowns; and a reproducible calculation receipt. An apparent optimizer maximum is not a statement that management can or will execute it. Unsupported routes remain outside the proven envelope, not silently assumed impossible or unlimited.

### Illustrative check, not an issuer or legal conclusion

Suppose two hypothetical routes have individual limits of $8 million and $7 million but share a remaining $10 million pool. At an assumed $2 per new share and six million shares available under a separately supplied share constraint, each route is individually feasible at its own limit. Their $15 million sum is not. The shared dollar pool limits the joint result to $10 million, with one feasible split of $8 million plus $2 million. The share constraint is not binding in that example.

Now reduce the available shares to three million while keeping price fixed. The share constraint makes the joint amount at most $6 million. If the share count is unknown, do not substitute infinity or zero: any complete-capacity claim is indeterminate even if a narrower documentary dollar bound is known. The packaged illustrative cases check this arithmetic; they do not validate the production engine or encode securities law.

### Keep stock and flow quantities apart

Outstanding principal, authorized shares, reserved shares, remaining registration amount and programme availability are state quantities. Sales, exercises, conversions, repayments, redemptions and repurchases are events that change them. A cumulative usage figure and the individual transactions explaining it must not both decrement capacity. A resale by an existing holder normally does not itself fund the company; any underlying exercise proceeds are a separate action under its own terms.

Exercise/conversion does not automatically occur because it is economically attractive. Holder blockers and market participation conditions may limit immediate execution without retiring the lifetime claim. Cashless exercise and debt conversion can create shares without bringing equivalent new cash. Pre-funded warrants must not contribute their already-paid exercise economics again.

Reverse-split changes require separate effective-date and contract-adjustment evidence. A proxy proposal is not an effective split. Authorized share capital may not change in the same proportion as outstanding shares. Never apply a universal split factor to every denominator, reserve and instrument without its rule.

## 8. Share basis and funding: mandatory mathematical foundations

The existing share-count ledger records observations; it does not select today's outstanding shares. Qualify its accepted external-head/materialization path before using it as an admitted source. Its documented pre-production and provider-conformance limitations must be reconciled through actual owner actions; a unit test or schema is not provider proof. No new signing, storage or authentication plane is authorized. [S12]

The share selector must distinguish common shares outstanding, weighted-average EPS denominators, public-float dollars, non-affiliate shares, treasury stock, preferred equivalents, reserved issuance and corporate-action basis. A balance-sheet share figure, cover-page figure and later transaction can refer to different dates. Resolve the supported timeline or display a conflict; do not silently choose the largest or newest-looking number.

Select funding inputs only at their supported accounting basis. Unrestricted cash, restricted cash, securities, receivables, revolver availability, accrued interest, maturities, minimum cash covenants, committed expenditures and financing costs cannot be collapsed into a headline cash balance. Use the existing financial/source owners and record gaps instead of completing FIF inside this mission.

Runway is a scenario schedule, not automatically cash divided by the latest net loss. Freeze the burn/working-capital/seasonality assumptions and show contractual payment dates. Negative or unknown burn does not imply infinite runway. Debt conversion may relieve a future obligation without providing current cash; an undrawn commitment may have conditions that prevent treating it as money available now.

Financing scenarios join the cash schedule with the shared issuance constraints. Fees and discounts use explicit assumptions; gross financing and net usable cash are distinct. Price-to-share conversion is a scenario input. Price impact, manager intentions, investor demand, likelihood and entry timing remain unmodeled rather than guessed.
