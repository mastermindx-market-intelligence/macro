Verified first-party documentation:

- [Massive dated overview](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/tickers/ticker-overview): report-period indexing can incorporate later-filed information. The same endpoint describes SIC, not GICS.
- [Massive ticker list](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/tickers/all-tickers): dated listing and active/inactive selection; response completeness still requires evidence.
- [Massive ticker events](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/ticker-events): experimental timeline; ticker reuse needs immutable historical identifiers.

- [S&P GICS History brochure](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/documents/119749-gics_history-collateral_ltr.pdf): active/inactive company history and dated from/thru fields are advertised. The illustrative brochure is not a delivered historical feed or an operator entitlement. CUSIP has a separate stated license condition; no CUSIP data was obtained.
