### T+1 and suspensions

The controlling wording is more exact than a generic T+1 label. SZSE 2026 **§3.1.4** prohibits selling purchased securities before settlement, except permitted turnaround products; **§3.1.5** lists same-day exceptions, none of which are ordinary A shares (printed pp7–8) [M3]. SSE 2026 §3.1.4 has corresponding wording [M2]. BSE 2026 **§3.1.4** directly prohibits selling on the purchase day except turnaround products [M5]. HKEX's current Connect FAQ **Q1.15, printed p19** explicitly interprets the mainland rule as no day trading for Connect securities [M6].

Evaluator consequence: an alert generated after Tuesday's close and bought at Wednesday's open cannot treat a Wednesday stop/target touch as a sale of those newly bought shares. The first permitted sale is the next applicable trading session, subject to suspension, daily limits, and route eligibility. An intraday mark-to-market adverse excursion may still be recorded, but it is distinct from a realized exit. This is an implementation implication of the cited rules, not a claim that the entire settlement infrastructure has a single uniform T+1 cash/stock clock.

SZSE **§4.3.3** says actual suspension/resumption timing follows notices; **§4.3.4** applies ten-minute intraday halts to non-price-limited stocks at first ±30% and ±60% movement from opening price. **§4.3.5** says suspended stocks remain in quotation information [M3]. Thus quotation presence is not proof of tradability. BSE's analogous ten-minute IPO/non-price-limited halts are **§4.2.5** [M5]. Corporate-action reference prices also matter: SZSE **§4.4.3** uses the ex-rights/ex-dividend reference as the limit calculation base [M3]; adjusted historical returns and raw executable prices require separate fields.

BSE risk-warning §§4.5.1–4.5.4 were initially deferred in April; an August 21 exchange notice activated them **2026-08-31** [M7]. Other initially deferred BSE provisions should not be assumed active without their own commencement evidence. This is a concrete reason to version rules by provision and effective date rather than one global `rules_2026=true` switch.

### October 2026 clock

SSE and SZSE official annual calendars close **October 1–7**, reopen **October 8**, and explicitly keep **Saturday October 10 closed** despite the civil make-up workday [M8–M9]. The research-date October 9 is consequently an ordinary scheduled trading day. Mid-Autumn closed September 25–27 and reopened September 28. Calendar arithmetic must be exchange-session arithmetic, not weekdays or civil workdays.

HKEX's 2026 Stock Connect calendar, **PDF p2 October table, visually inspected**, shows both Connect directions closed October 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 **and October 19** [M10]. Mainland exchanges can be open when the Connect route is closed. Domestic-market freshness, A/H valuation synchronization, and a user's accessible entry date are separate questions.

### Data availability and accessibility

HKEX's current Northbound holdings page says holdings became **quarterly from 2024-08-19**, published on the **fifth Northbound trading day after quarter-end**. It warns that its holdings list is not an eligible-securities list and that shareholding-percentage denominators may not reflect corporate actions [M11]. Its implementation circular distinguishes the May 13, 2024 real-time-turnover/quota changes from the August 19 remaining changes [M12]. These sources support checking that the existing exclusion of daily Northbound directional products has not regressed. Do not silently splice old daily holdings, estimated vendor flows, and quarterly official positions into one daily contemporaneous factor. A closed-channel/missing observation is not zero institutional demand. No new Northbound infrastructure is proposed.

The Connect FAQ Q1.3 distinguishes domestic-market access from Connect access: STAR and ChiNext Connect trading is restricted to institutional professional investors. Q1.4 and Q1.4.1 describe security eligibility and sell-only transitions [M6]. These access facts should be user-facing qualifications if the product serves offshore individuals; they are not reasons to erase those companies from a domestic research universe.

Masking is a useful diagnostic, not proof that a model has no hidden future knowledge. Historical extraction should be limited to source-supported fields; predictive evaluation needs either an appropriately dated frozen model or prospective shadow outputs after model freeze. Narrative plausibility is not an outcome label.

## 3. Eight prioritized falsifiable experiments

These are proposed tests, not measured improvements. Keep current production unchanged. Use the existing evaluator/intelligence/candidate owners and the parent source census to assign concrete paths.

1. **P0 — Repair the actionability and benchmark clocks.** Hypothesis: the reported win/excess figures are materially biased by stock next-open/HL2 versus benchmark fill-day-close mismatch and same-day exits. Freeze publication cutoff; simulate earliest permitted entry and T+1 exit using rule-effective board/ST/calendar/suspension facts. Compute stock and benchmark over identical timestamps, entry convention and horizon. Separate gross marks, cost-adjusted proxy execution and unresolved fills. Acceptance: every sample can recover its issuance, knowledge cutoff, earliest entry, permissible exit and matched benchmark; no same-purchase-day sale; no holiday synthetic session; no assumed limit-queue fill. Report the before/after delta without optimizing parameters against the corrected sample.

2. **P0/P1 — Isolate the four-row global fallback.** Hypothesis: a small missing-data cohort displaces the intended ranking for otherwise supported eligible candidates. On each stored date, replay v3, intended v4 on the common covered eligible set, and an explicitly qualified partial-coverage policy. Show overlap@24, displaced candidate count, board-wide mode changes and matched excess outcomes. Do not mix incomparable raw v3/v4 scores silently or infer outcomes for excluded rows. Acceptance: disappearance of one unsupported nonselected row cannot silently reorder all supported rows; all missingness reasons and ranking modes are recoverable. Alpha acceptance still requires corrected out-of-sample comparison. This is primarily product-contract integrity.

3. **P1 — Attribute incremental value of the actual score terms.** Hold the candidate pool and clock fixed. Compare v3, alternative-data core alone, core×`edge_remaining`, plus falsifier term, plus leading-desk count; also leave out one component at a time. Predefine 5/10/20-session primary outcomes. Test whether leading-desk count adds information beyond correlated/repeated evidence and whether a flat penalty merely rescales many rows. Acceptance: retain a term only when its incremental direction and effect survive block-by-date uncertainty checks and reasonable cost stress; log null/negative terms. A higher model-originated interest score is not independent confirmation of an underlying thesis.

4. **P1 — Separate reversal from continuation and audit the MACD–RSI gate.** Compare matched eligible stocks before/after confluence, using sector, size and liquidity as controls. Cross stock-level short-horizon return rank with 20–60-session momentum and event status. The falsifiable question is whether the existing positive technical gate combined with inverse RS20/off-high penalties selects a coherent opportunity at the intended horizon. Acceptance: report gate selectivity, missed-opportunity baseline and signal decay; choose one primary horizon before the final holdout. This is an ablation of the current gate and score, not a recommission of the killed subsector-state reversal gate. Avoid retrospectively choosing the best horizon for every candidate.

5. **P1 — Earn complexity against inexpensive China baselines.** Run sector-neutral equal-weight ranks of earnings yield, quality, reversal, momentum and liquidity; matched random draws should use the exact same eligible universe and released K. Compare v3/v4 on rank IC, excess precision@24, adverse excursion and rank churn. Keep benchmark/matched selection symmetric. Acceptance: score complexity earns promotion only if it beats prespecified simple baselines on the corrected sample and prospective shadow evidence. The factor literature motivates baselines, not arbitrary new weights or an automatic small-cap exclusion.

6. **P2 — First-disclosure earnings/catalyst qualification.** Start with available official Chinese event history. Distinguish forecast→revision→quick report→final report; extract sign, magnitude, denominator, period, novelty and invalidation. Compare technical-only candidates, event-qualified candidates and matched event-only alternatives. Keep event-window returns unavailable until the event window ends; count preannouncements only once. Acceptance: expert-reviewed extraction precision, source/clock coverage and incremental benchmark-relative outcomes are evaluated separately. No effect supported only by an LLM explanation counts as event evidence. Reuse existing intelligence owners.

7. **P1/P4 — Calibrated variable coverage and abstention.** Test a few frozen eligibility/quality thresholds against the current board capped at 24, keeping an all-issued audit log. The cap is not a compulsory quota. Plot realized error/excess-loss versus percentage admitted, with sample sizes and regimes. Treat source completeness, tradability and predictive confidence as distinct fields. Acceptance: thresholds reduce a prespecified risk/error measure at comparable opportunity cost in holdout/shadow, and the product can truthfully show zero supported picks. The current 0.85 penalty and desk-count boost are not calibrated probabilities.

8. **P3 — Conditional learner only after history qualification.** If a sufficiently long point-in-time universe becomes available, compare regularized linear/boosted LTR with a small lagged-regime interaction model, then graph aggregates only if relationship timestamps are supported. Do not create a new pipeline merely to run a paper model. Acceptance: common folds, same labels, nested fitting and calibration, lagged regime features, matured outcome feedback, and improvement on an untouched later holdout. Reject complexity that helps only an old paper universe, random split, or one regime. Prospective capture should proceed now, but model promotion waits for evidence.

## 4. Evaluation constraints for this history

Approximately 60 board dates and 42 candidate-store dates do not support a strong claim of broad regime robustness. Thousands of stock rows on the same day share market/sector shocks; overlapping 10/20/60-day labels share returns. Count unique issuance dates, unique ticker episodes, matured horizons, and effective cohort coverage separately. Repeated daily appearances should not become dozens of independent successes. Cluster/block inference by issuance date and, when feasible, ticker/sector; show confidence intervals and parameter-search count. A horizon must remain unreported when the actual data cutoff does not support maturation.

Maintain immutable issued records and current-board records separately. Use vintage universe membership, first-known features, original financial-report versions, board/ST status, adjustment factors, suspension notices, endpoint/field coverage and response/ingestion timestamps. Preserve raw legal limit/reference fields independently from adjusted prices; do not infer legal limit states from adjusted historical returns. Restated reports and current concept maps may be useful for explanation, but not retroactive predictors. Do not fill missing features/returns with zero. Cross-sectional normalization must use only the decision-time eligible cohort and be frozen with that cohort. Statistical significance alone is insufficient; a prespecified economically meaningful effect after costs and a viable prospective result are needed for promotion.
