## 2. The live path is now a concrete implementation problem

The integration experiment uses the exact 5.87 MB canonical board, projects all 180 primary names, exercises native gate logic on three actual names, runs the actual evaluator driver across six synthetic mainland-session times, and invokes the native ledger writer on temporary Parquet. The retained run contains four distinct events and four native daily rows, because the key includes event kind. The membership receipt has two confirmed names and one dropped name. Technical confirmation is independently taken from the same-session settlement pack; neither observation certifies a fill. [Evidence: [integration repair decisions](repairs/integration_contract/REPAIR_DECISIONS.md), native inputs and results.]

The first prototype's nominal pass was insufficient. Independent review found seven failures: loss of a whole event key could escape detection; readable duplicate ledger rows could be silently collapsed; equal-time conflicting close snapshots were chosen by digest; raw basis was stamped without source evidence; a tampered first price survived; huge integers raised an untyped overflow; and pack score bounds were incomplete. Later challenges exposed backdated events supported only by a later receipt or later-built pack. Tightening those checks then exposed a legitimate fractional-second input that the native second-truncated serializer rejected. All failures and intermediate candidates remain retained.

The final repair preserves the supplied evaluation instant in the existing timestamp fields after checking agreement with the native evaluation second. Source receipt and archived pack must each be known by the event's own full timestamp. Distinct event identities at the exact same retained instant refuse chronological ambiguity; a digest cannot decide which came first. Exact event replay coalesces. Authentic later events update last/count fields and preserve the full first-observation projection.

The producer passes 136 full-run controls plus a separate retained-fixture replay. Independent acceptance passes **80 checks**, including five actual native writes, four readable hostile Parquet refusals with zero writer calls and unchanged bytes, and fractional-time positive/negative cases. This accepts a bounded research contract. It does not certify the installed schedule, actual R2 listing, natural provider basis or authenticated consumer. [Evidence: [independent integration acceptance](reviews/integration_repair_review/REVIEW.md).]

The most important architecture decision is to keep the **archived N−1 armed pack**, **N settlement pack** and **canonical N board** separate. They respectively supply event thresholds/frozen values, technical confirmation and membership comparison. The current pack key cannot stand in for all three. The incumbent event spool and archived-pack store already exist; this requires compatible evidence at their boundaries, not a second ledger or transport.

Natural quote adjustment basis remains unqualified by the pinned parser evidence. The synthetic resolver demonstrates exact source-byte, parser, field, price and clock binding; it deliberately does not certify Yahoo, Tencent or generic local snapshots as natural unadjusted prints. A field named `regular` denotes the quote rung in this code, not an adjustment-basis receipt. Implementation must resolve that contract through the existing source owner before making the stronger claim.

## 3. Historical price differences must remain decomposed

There are 1,948 unique legacy entries behind 2,632 episode rows. Of 656 changed unique entries, **274** are basis-selection-only differences, **206** match the current raw price numerically, and **176** remain unresolved. All 1,948 retained original/current T+1 session fields agree. This finding supports preserving the current session identity; it does not support replacing original prices. [Evidence: [vintage findings](vintage_lab/VINTAGE_FINDINGS.md).]

For the 289 mature V4 H10 observations, 136 entries change: 61 basis-selection-only, 35 raw numeric matches and 40 unresolved. The original accounting has 133 wins; recomputing entry against the current data has 129, with 16 sign changes. The aggregate mean difference is only about +0.0090 percentage points, because material individual changes cancel. These are comparisons at fixed current exits, not recovered original economic P&L. The public original denominator remains the original denominator.

Git archaeology found 82 reachable price-changing commits and retained before/after witnesses. Among 1,332 present witness comparisons, the accepted classifications are 418 identical, 636 open-only rewrites, 226 uniform positive scales and 52 mixed-field revisions. Those are witness rows, not unique changed entries. A positive uniform scale is a shape, not a certified corporate-action explanation. Action-title leads without effective dates, ratios or a verified factor bridge do not resolve the disputed `.SZ` basis. A Git commit time is also not a producer publication or serving receipt. [Evidence: [independent vintage acceptance](reviews/vintage_repair_acceptance/ACCEPTANCE.md).]

The benchmark search found 38 reachable `510300` versions, all Close/Volume only. The native collector's extractor drops optional OHLC. The repaired retention fixture therefore keeps the native required Close/Volume projection unchanged, retains optional raw fields without coercion, and uses the same opening-quality validator at retention and mark construction. Invalid Open never becomes a valid opening mark merely because it was retained. Missing benchmark Open remains unavailable; Close cannot silently replace it.

The coupled source/mark contract passes **104 producer** and **86 independent** checks, closing all 13 original variants and both later interface findings. It resolves exact source bytes, row, field, calendar anchor, price basis and clock relationships again when comparing serialized marks. Every finalized row in a blob must be consistent with its declared availability. Output remains `ALIGNED_MARK_DIAGNOSTIC` / `MARK_ONLY`, with matched stock and benchmark endpoints. It is not a fill, total-return series or proof of what the system knew at the old decision.

The original-entry fixture authenticates the entire stored list before replay and preserves uniqueness on `(decision_id, ticker)`. Corrections are separate records strictly later than the referenced original; they retain the original entry session. The fixture does not implement replacement-session migration, a total order among corrections or a latest-correction view. A wholly rewritten history with consistently recomputed hashes requires external durable custody to detect. Those boundaries are explicit implementation premises, not omitted conclusions.

## 4. Stronger comparison makes the alpha claim weaker

The stricter control study requires distinct-name assignments, exact sector matching and ADV between 0.5 and 2 times the selected name, while excluding all selected names from the control pool. Uniformity is over valid slot assignments; it is not uniform over unordered portfolios. Only **9 of 17 dates** support a complete six-name assignment. On that support, selected mean excess is −1.029524 pp, expected control −0.921376 pp, and paired difference **−0.108148 pp**. The IID interval is [−1.949733, +1.910396] pp; five- and ten-session block sensitivities also cross zero. [Evidence: [matched-controls decision memo](matched_controls/DECISION_MEMO.md), [independent review](reviews/matched_controls_review/REVIEW.md).]

For the other eight dates the strict-policy estimate is unavailable. The report's [−43.9798, +50.1379] pp envelope describes hypothetical-completion **precision differences** under explicit assumptions; it is neither a finite return bound nor an identified full-cohort bound for a strict policy that had no valid assignment. The declared sector-only sensitivity produces an attractive exploratory reversal result on a different six-date support. It was specified before this pass's calculations, but remains a retrospective sensitivity on already-examined history. Unequal support and that evidence status make it unsuitable for promotion.

Missing outcomes also change inference. The September 1 intelligence control graph has 308 assignments; 170 contain unresolved `601059.SS`. Its missing outcome carries 9.20% of expected portfolio weight. Sweeping a hypothetical missing **excess return** from −20 to +20 percentage points moves the estimated difference by 3.6797 pp. The implementation must keep those assignments and their missing weight. Resampling until a chosen control happens to have an observed outcome changes the policy after the fact.

The broader H10 diagnostic has 288 episodes across 21 signal dates and 249 issuers. There are 135 overlaps among 210 date-window pairs, 39 repeated-issuer episodes and at most three nonoverlapping date windows. That last number is a structural illustration, not a literal effective sample size. Ten staggered phases split five positive/five negative; removing one large winner reduces the mean from +0.163738 to +0.011839 pp. A block-plus-issuer sensitivity also crosses zero and is not advertised as a calibrated two-way confidence interval.

The illustrative power calculation uses an 11-date paired standard deviation of 4.811819 pp, a true effect of +0.50 pp, a required lower-bound hurdle of +0.25 pp, a two-sided 95% interval and 80% power. The normal approximation gives about **2,908 independent date-equivalents**. This is a warning about precision under those assumptions, not a forecast of trading days, a release countdown or a requirement satisfied by adding stock rows. The original H10/H20/H60 cohort and common-support comparisons remain in the first report; nothing in this pass warrants changing their promotion conclusion.

## 5. Intelligence is numerically present but not historically qualified

At the frozen board, measured intelligence exists for 1,597/1,601 scored names, 179/180 raw-eligible names, all 164 signal-buyable names and all **125 reconstructed qualified pre-cap competitors**. The last population is reconstructed from 24 featured rows and 101 exclusions caused only by the caps, not a complete private qualification rerun. Incumbent ordering reproduces all 24 featured names; intelligence-first ordering on the same population overlaps four. That is a selection effect, not a return benefit. [Evidence: [intelligence decisions](intelligence_lab/INTELLIGENCE_DECISIONS.md).]

The accepted contract requires measured finite numeric values in range; zero is valid, Booleans and numeric strings are not. Missing intelligence for a genuine competitor triggers coherent whole-population incumbent fallback. Missing data outside the competitor set does not. Conflicting baseline generation, population, scores or ranks requires refusal/quarantine: a fallback label cannot authenticate a corrupted baseline.

Temporal replay requires true publication and system first-seen timestamps, an authoritative revision relation and owner-specific expected sessions or periodic applicability. Select the latest known revision before deciding whether it is valid; a known retraction or invalid newer version suppresses the observation instead of reviving a convenient old value. A revision not yet known at the cutoff leaves the earlier replay unchanged. The final helper passes **79 independent** cases after the original and intermediate failures were repaired and preserved. It still depends on the existing generation and source owners to supply authentic premises. [Evidence: [final intelligence acceptance](reviews/intelligence_contract_review/acceptance_f2f5aeeb/ACCEPTANCE.md).]

The actual analyst duplication hypothesis is falsified within the tested feature: 475 duplicate groups contain no conflicting payloads, and full row reversal has zero effect on native consensus, raw rating, percentile or tested qualified/featured projections. Current source dates and complete numeric coverage do not supply missing historical first-seen clocks.

The native calibration audit and its independent replay agree byte for byte across seven scenarios and twelve checks. The actual current card leaves normalized priors unchanged. Synthetic future, wrong-target, weak negative-evidence and malformed numeric/Boolean cards reveal missing admission checks. A date-only control confirms that later timestamp text alone does not cause a coefficient change; altered evidence fields do while the cutoff gate is absent. Both up-weighting and down-weighting need feature/target/horizon/basis matching, matured labels and appropriate evidence units. Preserve the current signed coefficients until that qualification exists. [Evidence: [independent weight assessment](tradability_lab/DECISION_MEMO.md).]

The margin addendum resolves the window rather than inferring it from scaling. All 155,688 stored pairs are 20 positions apart in the pinned index. The producer permits 21/22-position fallback and has a synthetic short-history slicing failure. A bounded replacement preserves the valid window and leaves underfilled history unavailable. Retain the actual date pair through the consumer. This does not qualify a whole-market financing/CSI300 timer as evidence for the per-issuer feature. [Evidence: [margin source trace](intelligence_lab/MARGIN_HORIZON_TRACE.md).]

## 6. Tradability and display have bounded, testable repairs

The complete existing pack stock universe has **1,716 names**: the incumbent predicate admits 1,638, rejects 77 for ADV and one for ST. One usable market-cap value is missing and passes the incumbent unknown-value policy. The caller supplies no tradability map. The predicate returns a rejection reason or `None`, so the compatible Boolean map is `predicate(...) is None`; using raw return values reverses admission. A six-name native pre-gate witness demonstrates the omitted, complete, inverted and partial-map behaviors. This is not a full-universe gate/armed-board or return recalculation. [Evidence: [tradability decisions](tradability_lab/DECISION_MEMO.md), 77 controls and immutable input census.]

Reuse the existing nightly metadata loading and precedence. Assert complete map coverage, while preserving the incumbent treatment of genuinely unknown values. Keep current price age and session guards distinct. Older deep ADV data for three names is not an official suspension flag. Current ST observations are fresh in this census, while synthetic stale/future-name tests expose the separate source-time premise. Do not invent a new suspension or expiry policy inside a caller repair.

Display experiments execute the exact native IIFE with synthetic DOM/fetch/timers. The original source fails twelve comparative cases involving HTTP errors, absolute artifact expiry, request races, hanging requests and clock boundaries. The first research repair then failed two independent delayed-timer variants. The final repair checks absolute request age at response consumption as well as through a timeout. It passes **30 producer** and **38 independent** controls. The paired scripts retain 120-second polling, 15-minute artifact age and the existing bilingual vocabulary; 30-second request lifetime and 60-second future-clock allowance remain explicit settings for owner ratification. Real browser, authenticated API, deployment identity and the separate server-renderer age repair still require implementation proof. [Evidence: [display repair decisions](repairs/display_contract/REPAIR_DECISIONS.md), independent display repair review.]

## 7. Why this is a valid handoff boundary

The feasible offline questions now have discriminating experiments, repaired candidates, independent challenge and explicit negative results. Further rank searches on these same short, overlapping and partly unrecoverable cohorts would not resolve the missing original facts or produce independent future evidence. The next productive work is implementing these contracts at the existing owners and collecting natural receipts under those contracts.

Research readiness is therefore separate from production readiness. The executable prototypes are **BUILT_NOT_PROVEN for production**; the broader claim of improved candidate quality remains unproven. The final handoff carries concrete unresolveds and their falsifiers: original publication/basis custody, natural quote evidence, actual complete store reads, installed scheduled/durable behavior, real browser/API behavior and untouched future outcomes. No new operational store, source-law change, data rewrite, ranking activation, deployment, collection or trading occurred in this pass.
