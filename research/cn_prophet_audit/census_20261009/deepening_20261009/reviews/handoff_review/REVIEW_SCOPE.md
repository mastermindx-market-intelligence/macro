# Independent cross-component handoff review

This is a bounded documentation and claim-to-evidence review after the component experiments and independent acceptances. It does not rerun those suites, revise an accepted candidate, collect a source or exercise production. Only this review directory is writable for the assignment.

## Acceptance questions

1. Does every accepted implementation contract identify the exact reviewed version, distinguish producer evidence from independent acceptance, and preserve superseded failure evidence?
2. Are the archived armed pack, source quote receipt, settlement technical verdict and canonical membership receipt kept distinct? Is a first-event timestamp compared at its full retained precision?
3. Is incumbent fallback allowed only when the baseline population, scores/ranks and generation are independently coherent? Does invalid optional intelligence refuse its own unqualified decision without authorizing a structurally conflicting baseline?
4. Are complete tradability-map coverage and native unknown-metadata semantics distinguished? Are six-name frontier results kept separate from an unexecuted full-universe gate/board effect?
5. Are source receipt, publication, decision, event, finalized-bar availability, grading and rendering clocks applied at their respective owners? Are synthetic clocks/codecs distinct from real source and calendar truth?
6. Are original-entry identity and event-forward daily identity kept separate? Do append-only correction claims retain their narrow tested scope: later than the original, no total correction ordering/latest view, no implemented replacement-session migration?
7. Do market outputs retain `ALIGNED_MARK_DIAGNOSTIC` / `MARK_ONLY` labels, with unresolved original basis and missing benchmark Open preserved? Do quote/source hashes avoid claims of external authenticity or execution?
8. Does matched-control synthesis retain uniform complete slot-assignment weighting, missing-label mass and strict-policy unavailability on infeasible dates? Are unsupported precision completions and power illustrations qualified?
9. Are accepted live-display behavior and untested renderer/browser/deployment boundaries separated? Are proposed timeout/skew parameters identified as owner decisions?
10. Are the final implementation and release gates concrete, assigned to incumbent owners and distinct from a ranking-promotion or alpha claim?

`INITIAL_INPUTS.json` preserves the first frozen document set. The interface timestamp wording had already been corrected by its owner when that snapshot was taken; the earlier read-time wording concern is retained in the review history without inventing a byte identity for the earlier unsaved file. Later margin-horizon evidence and the final handoff will receive separate exact input records when available.

The review will record accepted claims, required qualifications, resolved drafting observations and any remaining blocker. A passing result is acceptance of the handoff's internal consistency and evidence scope, not additional experimental or production proof.
