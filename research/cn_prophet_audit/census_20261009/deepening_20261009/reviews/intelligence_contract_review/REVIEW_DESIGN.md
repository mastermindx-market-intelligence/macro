Scope: read the fixed intelligence laboratory and challenge its implementation-readiness claims. Write only within this review directory; do not alter the laboratory, matched-controls package, production sources or original records. This is adversarial contract research, not installation authority.

Preserve the laboratory's real controls: 125 currently qualified rows, exact 24-name V3 reproduction, four retained names under the experimental intelligence order, and 475 identical analyst duplicate groups with zero conflicting payload groups or row-reversal effect. Synthetic malformed records are not allegations about current stored data.

The challenge checks:

1. Exact-duplicate coalescing must be independent of input order and type sensitive enough that Boolean `true` cannot impersonate measured numeric `1`. Numerically equivalent representations must either canonicalize consistently or fail as conflicts; they cannot produce different selected digests by row order.
2. A newly appended record whose valid availability timestamps are after an earlier cutoff must not break the earlier selected state just because its identity or revision payload is malformed.
3. A higher revision already seen and published before cutoff, but with invalid applicability metadata, must not silently restore an older fact. A known retraction is the clearest witness. A legitimately future-effective correction remains a separate valid control.
4. Fast-source session qualification requires an actual well-formed expected session and observation session. Missing values must not qualify through `None == None`. Row-declared cadence must not override the owning source family's cadence contract.
5. Canonical issuer identity must be consistent across temporal selection, normalization and ranking. An alias with whitespace cannot create a second normalization observation for one issuer.
6. Invalid numeric payloads, including JSON integers too large for a finite float, must produce a defined refusal/suppression/fallback rather than an uncontrolled exception.
7. Normalization must operate within a compatible feed/measure population after source-time qualification and qualified issuer aggregation. Distinct ticker keys alone do not make unrelated units/families comparable.
8. Qualification and incumbent-rank consistency remain explicit supplied premises. The current population positive control must reproduce, while mixed source generations or contradictory rank metadata must not be claimed safe solely because every intelligence value is numeric.
