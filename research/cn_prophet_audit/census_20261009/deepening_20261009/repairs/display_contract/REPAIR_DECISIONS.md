This candidate closes the independently reproduced request-deadline race while preserving the original display laboratory's bounded behavior. It passes **30 producer controls**: all prior 28 plus two direct comparisons against the previous candidate. The previous candidate paints an overdue response when timer callbacks have not run; the repair refuses both late headers and a late JSON body at consumption, aborts that current transport and leaves the chip absent. The repair stores each request's absolute deadline at launch and uses one current-request/deadline predicate before reading headers and after JSON completion. The deadline is inclusive: consumption at or beyond the deadline is too late. A timer that runs late is no longer able to erase the deadline contract by losing a race to response processing.
