**Deliverable F · 2026-10-09 · Research proposals, not live-change authority.**

This plan follows the actual pipeline and negative results in [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), the [mechanism census](MECHANISM_CENSUS.md), and the [historical evaluation](HISTORICAL_EVALUATION.md). The existing `china_prophet_shadow`, `china_standout_track`, `track_scoring`, `cn_prophet_audit`, `china_validation` and `china_signal_lab` owners remain the storage, grading and feedback owners. PR [#6871](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/pull/6871) retains R0 replay custody. None of these experiments changes live rankings in this commission.

## 2. Common experimental contract

### Population and clocks

Freeze the complete scored population, raw-eligible population, qualified pre-cap population, chosen names, order, cap exclusions and actual publication receipt **before** observing outcomes. The same timestamped population must feed every comparison. A board date, a first-seen date, a field observation date, an ingestion time and a publication time are different facts. Reuse the existing producer generation identity and source fingerprints to connect them; do not invent another identity or candidate store.

Freeze selection before joining forward prices. If a selected name later lacks an outcome, keep that name and mark its result unresolved. Never promote the next name with an available return into its place. Compare arms on common qualified dates and feature coverage; print every excluded date and its reason. Distinguish outright unavailable features from measured zero. No current company map, sector label, weight scorecard or revised filing may silently serve as an old input.

Keep each `board_definition`, horizon, entry rule, benchmark and price vintage separate. Daily repeats are not independent trades. Use existing contiguous-membership episodes and identify readmissions with overlapping holding periods. A changed definition is a new shadow comparison under the existing owner, not a rewritten old grade.

### Outcome instruments

Use three named views: (1) immutable as-published records; (2) signal research marks with fully specified matched timestamps; (3) execution-feasible outcomes only when raw prices, legal restrictions and fill evidence support them. They may disagree honestly.

The current audit's matched-close diagnostic enters at the next CSI300-proxy session close and measures **h elapsed market sessions after that entry**. The existing H10 header instead includes the fill session in its ten stock-close observations and starts the benchmark at the fill-day close. The difference between their aggregate results is not an isolated clock effect. A clock-only experiment must keep stock trades, cohort and endpoints fixed. Current stored `510300.SS` data contains close and volume only, so the benchmark-open counterfactual is unavailable.

For a future open-entry benchmark comparison, require stock and benchmark opens on the same session and compatible adjustment basis. For an HL2 proxy, a daily benchmark bar cannot identify a matching intraday time. Retain the ambiguity rather than invent it. Purchased ordinary A shares cannot be sold during that purchase session; same-day excursions are observations, not realizable exits of newly purchased shares. Legal-limit and queue feasibility must use effective-dated rules and raw reference data, never inferred adjusted-price bands.

### Metrics and uncertainty

- Primary quality metric: mean benchmark-relative return at a prespecified horizon, with the cohort and cost convention named. Include absolute return and loss frequency; beating a falling market is not the same as making money.
- Priority IC: Spearman correlation of **higher-is-better score** with forward excess return. If numerical rank is used, lower is better and the favorable correlation is negative. Print the convention beside the result.
- Precision@K: proportion of the frozen K picks with positive excess return; also print absolute-return precision. Always show frozen K, observed outcomes and unresolved outcomes. Primary comparisons use complete-K dates; an incomplete date stays unresolved. If reporting observed-only precision, label it conditional and provide fixed-K bounds from `observed_wins/K` to `(observed_wins + unresolved)/K`. Never drop or replace unresolved names silently. Compare the same K and release dates.
- Top-decile lift: mean excess of the frozen top decile minus that of the same eligible pool, with equal-date aggregation. The hindsight best K may be a regret diagnostic, never a deployable baseline.
- Risk: close-based and intraday adverse excursion separately; gap loss, suspension duration, unresolved exit, downside tail and cost sensitivity. Do not call an episode excursion portfolio drawdown. A portfolio claim would require portfolio construction, capacity and capital assumptions outside this commission.
- Stability: membership replacement, rank changes for surviving names, signal duration and readmission overlap. Report counts of actual decisions, dates and issuers, not just a large row count.

The current date-cluster bootstrap captures same-date cross-sectional dependence but incompletely captures serially overlapping holdings and repeated companies. Its intervals are exploratory. Future acceptance should use contiguous time blocks at least as long as the longest evaluated horizon, with issuer/sector robustness checks and an explicit multiplicity budget. Tune neither thresholds nor block lengths until the desired result appears.

Use actual route-specific fees when available. The 10/30/60 basis-point deductions in this docket are **round-trip cost scenarios**, not measured commissions, stamp duty or demonstrated fills. Freeze a normal-cost scenario and a stress scenario before testing; include participation, turnover, spread and limit-queue feasibility rather than a universal haircut alone.

### Chronology and promotion

All history examined in this audit is now research history. It is not an untouched holdout. The baseline arms were fixed diagnostics, not fitted models, and their chronological subwindows are descriptive. A later model study must fit transformations and weights on past data only, validate hyperparameters on a later window, and evaluate once on a still later untouched window. Purge overlapping labels across boundaries and delay outcome-derived feedback until labels mature. Use long histories only when universe and features are genuinely first-known at those dates; five years of price files alone do not supply five years of event or candidate history.

For a promotion proposal, preregister an economically meaningful minimum gain, not just a positive point estimate. A reasonable **decision proposal** is at least +0.25 percentage points of net H10 excess versus both incumbent and the strongest prespecified simple baseline, with a paired interval excluding zero under the accepted dependence treatment and no material tail-risk degradation. This is a proposed hurdle to be ratified, not a promised result or a threshold optimized in this audit. Report sensitivity to that hurdle. Preserve existing per-leg maturation gates; do not substitute “60 rows” for independent evidence. Estimate needed effective observations using the measured paired-difference variance: approximately `(1.96 + 0.84)^2 × variance / target_gain^2` for a conventional 80% power planning calculation, then adjust for dependence. If the available sample cannot discriminate the target gain, return **inconclusive**.

Prospective shadow capture should use the existing owning lane and stores. It must verify natural publication, stable source clocks, matured feedback, degraded states and executable-label integrity before rank promotion. It is not permission to create a new background runtime, publisher or grader. Existing ratification and release authorities still apply.

## 3. Bounded experiments to commission in order

### E0 — Establish publication and outcome identity

**Hypothesis:** Different artifact/ledger generations and current-vintage fills prevent reliable comparisons. **Implementation:** Extend existing builder, candidate, board and latch receipts to bind the same generation, source fingerprint, definition and publication cutoff; preserve keep-first history and append corrections according to existing ownership. **Dataset:** All recoverable stored episodes plus a small consecutive set of new natural Asia bakes. **Control:** Exact as-published header and original latch; current-vintage recomputation remains a distinct view. **Pass:** Every accepted experimental row has a unique traceable publication/fill identity; unresolved old rows are explicitly quarantined, not repaired by assumption. Reproducing public 133/289 v4 wins must remain a positive control. **Reject/rollback:** Any re-fill silently overwrites historical economics, any old `.SZ` price-vintage dispute is hidden, or a cross-generation join is accepted on date/ticker alone. **Owner:** #6871 and existing tracking/publication owners.

### E1 — Define lawful entry, benchmark and exit clocks

**Hypothesis:** The outcome label can change with clock and execution conventions independently of selection quality. **Implementation:** One existing grade path exposes entry, earliest lawful sale, endpoint and benchmark basis explicitly. Add benchmark OHLC with compatible vintage through the existing price owner; retain HL2 ambiguity. **Method:** Paired, fixed-stock-trade comparison changing only the benchmark basis; separate paired entry-policy comparisons. **Pass:** Zero impossible same-purchase-day sales, future bars, duplicate trades or mismatched benchmark intervals in accepted execution labels; all exclusions counted. **Performance threshold:** No improvement required for correctness. A more pessimistic but correct result passes. **Shadow/rollback:** Parallel named view only until independently reconciled; reject results requiring adjusted-price legal-limit inference.

### E2 — Repair the real live path

**Hypothesis:** Real nightly frozen records, events and confirmations do not pass through the scheduled pack/reconciler path. **Implementation:** Repair the three existing joins (path, lane schema, score/rank fields), invoke the intended stock screen, and pass real event/close-board inputs through `reconcile_cn_live`. **Dataset:** Actual canonical nightly artifact and a recorded quote/event episode, then one natural owning-lane cycle. **Control:** Current source reader returns zero on the real artifact. **Pass:** Exact frozen identity/score/rank survives; one valid event reaches the existing forward ledger once; replay is idempotent; missing/stale/malformed/non-tradable cases remain refused. Read back the durable confirmation and real published overlay. **Rollback:** Any new stock admission authority, duplicate event ledger, future quote, missing teardown or silent healthy state. This is live-path correctness, not alpha promotion.

### E3 — Resolve the intelligence-coverage population contract

**Hypothesis:** Coverage outside the population competing for featured slots unnecessarily disables intended ordering. **Implementation:** In `china_board_rank`, compare the incumbent global policy with a clearly versioned, atomic policy scoped to the already qualified competing population; maintain excluded names and reasons in existing records. Do not blend v3 points with missing intelligence values. **Method:** Same eligibility, same caps, source-time-qualified inputs, frozen selection then outcomes; include all-zero measured and missing-unbuyable controls. **Pass for behavior:** Adding an unrelated non-competing missing record cannot change the competing cohort under the proposed contract; complete coverage preserves exact intended ordering. **Pass for rank promotion:** E0/E1 plus economic and prospective criteria above. **Known result:** Same-day 20-name replacement is established; the 11-date historical race does not establish benefit. **Reject:** Switching on intelligence merely because coverage rose or because the resulting names look more plausible.

### E4 — Qualify source time and age by feature

**Hypothesis:** Clockless or stale inputs distort ranking and historical validation. **Implementation:** Existing extras/Hub/signal-lab consumers retain publication, observation, first-seen, ingestion, revision and expected-cadence information; `build_interest_map` receives the decision cutoff. **Dataset:** Actual heterogeneous valuation, margin, analyst, block, LHB and special-situation records. **Controls:** Future publication, old fast-moving valuation, legitimate slow-moving filing, missing feature, source correction, holiday and stale price plane. **Pass:** No future evidence; no unqualified source clock; explicit feature-specific expiry and missingness; learned weights use only matured pre-decision evidence. **Quality test:** Compare qualified versus incumbent inputs on the same eligible cohort without changing selection rules at the same time. **Reject:** Treating every old observation as worthless, every missing record as zero, or private TuShare compliance as newly unsatisfied.

### E5 — Attribute score usefulness and benchmark complexity

**Hypothesis:** Score components and the intelligence multipliers add little or redundant predictive information. **Implementation:** Read existing candidate fields; preregister one-component removal, altdata core alone, core×remaining-edge, falsifier penalty and desk-count terms. Keep all transformations and rounding faithful to source. **Baselines:** Same-K incumbent, sector/size-relative momentum and reversal, earnings yield/quality where vintage exists, eligible equal-weight context and sector/liquidity-matched random selections where feasible. **Pass:** Incremental net performance survives common feature/date controls, chronological corroboration, dependence/multiple-test correction and a prospective shadow. **Known negative:** The currently complete 11-date comparison does not distinguish intelligence, momentum, reversal or quality from the score baseline. **Reject:** Promoting the apparently stronger incomplete-feature reversal table, rerunning the killed A-share subsector reversal gate, or training current v3 weights on a favorable old-definition cohort alone.

### E6 — Test opportunity coverage and timing without erasing safeguards

**Hypothesis:** The top-800/index-union universe and 300-bar requirement miss attractive newer/smaller leaders, or the patience-oriented entry ladder misses sustained continuation. **Implementation:** Reuse search and existing continuation-watch records. Record names excluded by membership, history, ST, liquidity, raw signal, execution, timing and caps. Distinguish information coverage from tradability. **Method:** First test missed opportunities on recoverable contemporaneous membership; compare short 1–5-session reversal and 10–60-session continuation under the same clocks and sector/size/liquidity controls. **Pass:** Incremental eligible opportunity capture and net outcomes at acceptable risk; no current-member-only survivor replay. **Reject:** Removing ST/liquidity/age checks simply to increase count, retroactively treating IPO first-store dates as listing dates, or forcing semiconductor/other favored names into the board.

### E7 — Add one verified event feature through existing CIE owners

**Hypothesis:** First-disclosure earnings revisions or well-qualified catalysts add information beyond technical confluence. **Implementation:** Reuse current official disclosure, IRM revision, contract, visits and communiqué owners. Start with one event family whose identity, economic fields and first-public timestamps can be recovered. Preserve `may_feed_prophet=false` until independently promoted. **Method:** Technical-only versus event-qualified versus matched event-only cohorts; no counting the same information again at forecast, revision, quick report and final report. **Pass:** Human-reviewed extraction precision and source lineage first, incremental returns second; a proposed ≥95% precision and ≥99% entity/time linkage threshold is an engineering target to ratify, not a measurement already achieved. **Reject:** Metadata interpreted as contract materiality, post-event prices used before the event window closes, LLM-generated facts, current graph edges in historical training, or a new vendor purchase without a separate need and authority.

### E8 — Test abstention and only then adaptive models

**Hypothesis:** Selective release or a simple regime interaction improves quality at lower coverage; additional model complexity may not help. **Implementation:** The existing qualifier may produce zero supported candidates and distinguish source failure from weak opportunity evidence. Compare a few frozen confidence/quality thresholds before any transformer or graph model. **Method:** Coverage-risk curves, missed-upside cost, absolute and relative outcomes, identical eligible populations, nested chronological calibration and lagged regime information. **Pass:** A prespecified loss/risk improvement at a stated coverage and opportunity cost; then outperform simple linear/boosted ranking on an untouched window. **Reject:** Full-feed quotas, heuristic score relabeled as probability, a single Q4 cohort marketed as regime robustness, or complex models whose gains disappear with timestamp-correct data. The current history cannot validate broad adaptive intelligence.

## 4. Required experiment return

Each return must contain the immutable source and input hashes; exact frozen choices; all candidate/date exclusions; metrics by definition/horizon/regime/benchmark; paired comparisons and uncertainty; cost and missing-outcome sensitivity; negative results; source-time and mutation controls; existing owner/carrier; and an explicit **reject / inconclusive / eligible-for-independent-promotion-review** verdict. A green test suite proves code behavior. It does not demonstrate investment value or grant release authority.
