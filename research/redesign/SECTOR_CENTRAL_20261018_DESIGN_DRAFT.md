## 2. Master workflow

### Brief — “What changed, and where should I investigate?”

One short source-bound conclusion on the canvas, one primary analytical visual, and at most three priority investigations above the fold. Use native change clauses; do not generate a new recommendation. A clearly separate action summary quotes the existing board's stances and counts, with a path to all rows. Do not compress five full boards into five narrow columns.

Keep Bottoming Watch visible as an early-sign investigation, explicitly separate from entry qualification. Show one source session in the page header and a meaningful missing/stale state. A build timestamp does not make an old observation current.

### Explore — “Where is leadership moving, and how broad is it?”

One selected group persists across the map, list and inspector. A switch of representation never changes the selected subject, population, benchmark or time window. Controls use plain labels and expose the active analytical basis near the chart.

Explore uses the same six lens names in the navigation, reference and migration contract:
- Rotation: observed relative strength/momentum and retained trails, with exact axis definitions and separate sector, industry and theme universes.
- Turns: native dated transition observations and watch states, with their original eligibility and explanation; a turn is not a stock-entry verdict.
- Leadership: measured relative performance, discovery, readable group tables, filtering and comparison under each producer's actual cohort and coverage contract.
- Money: participation, concentration, measured/expected counts and missing coverage. Actual fund flows have their own labeled source/basis and do not borrow the meaning of relative price strength.
- Confluence: existing class, entry and regime evidence, with separate native universes, qualification, coverage and dependencies.
- Cycles: native cycle and trend/macro-gate context, with its existing history/replay controls, preserved as a separate analytical instrument.

Theme discovery is a universe/depth choice; comparison, membership/overlap, Time Machine, breadth and methods remain within the relevant lens or dossier. They do not introduce a second set of top-level lens names. Only expose membership history where its current producer has qualified data.

Do not invent unsupported menu tabs. A producer-gated instrument has an explicit available/unavailable state or stays at its verified existing destination until its new equivalent is complete.

### Dossier — “What explains this group?”

A reusable selected-group depth view, not a second dashboard:
- Overview: measured leadership, formation/transition, entry conditions and evidence quality as independent facts.
- Companies: the actual dated structural roster; membership kind, population, measured coverage and ordering basis are explicit.
- Signals: producer-owned calls and transitions, with their original stance and eligibility.
- Drivers: source-bound explanations and contradictory evidence; no synthetic independent vote.
- Record: observed versus reconstructed history and call grading, with missing periods retained.

ETF proxy, equal-weight structural group, industry and curated theme are separate identities. XLK is not interchangeable with verified XLK holdings or us_sector_tech. A stock chosen from a strong sector does not inherit a stock-entry verdict.

### Research and Prophet — “Continue this investigation”

Research carries the selected entity, question and source references into existing research capabilities. The dossier keeps provenance and disagreements available without forcing them into the Brief.

A company-level “Open Prophet” action names the stock and enters its existing authoritative route. If the receiver cannot accept or show the context yet, call the action a stock deep link and retain the sector context in the origin. Do not label it “context-aware integration” until the receiver demonstrably consumes it.

Return restores the selected group, lens, period, benchmark, filters, scroll and focus using existing navigation conventions. Preserve unrelated URL/history fields. Auth retains the permitted intended destination through the existing shared session mechanism.

- 28px page title; strong, readable headline; 14–15px body; 12.5px secondary table content. Numerals use tabular figures. No ticker-style monospace body copy.
- One primary question and at most two supporting modules above the fold. At most three priority investigations. Study receipts live in disclosure unless they change the headline's meaning.
- Names wrap. A compact table may scroll within its own container; the page never does.
- The dossier becomes an inline full-width detail route/sheet at narrow widths, with a persistent named back action and sensible focus restoration.
- Color is never the only signal. Chinese directional colors follow the existing up/down token convention; structural group identity remains neutral.
- Complete focus outlines, clear selected/current attributes and keyboard-operable list alternatives. Motion supports selection and time continuity, with reduced-motion behavior.

No colored edge accents:
