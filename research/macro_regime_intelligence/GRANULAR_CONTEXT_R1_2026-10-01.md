## Synthesis

The analyst must explain simultaneous dimensions, not select one universal bucket.
A rising price or relative-strength spread does not establish literal capital transfer.
Earnings-estimate growth is not an expected investment return, and a high real yield
is not by itself an observed short real policy rate relative to neutral. Distinguish
implied from realized dispersion/correlation and do not subtract unlike measures.
Mechanisms are hypotheses with counterevidence and observable invalidation, not new
engine hazards or unearned forecasts. Quiet indices can coexist with meaningful
cross-sectional regimes. A correct description does not establish timing skill.
