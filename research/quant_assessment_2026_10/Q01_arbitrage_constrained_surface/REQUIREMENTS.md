# Q01 — acceptance requirements: test and evidence map

* **Module:** `engine/options_arbfree_surface.py` (`RESEARCH_ONLY = True`, `VERDICT = "INSUFFICIENT_DATA"`).
* **Suite:** `tests/test_options_arbfree_surface.py` (18 hermetic tests; imports only stdlib, numpy,
  scipy, pandas and the module).
* **Focused run:** exit 0. The command and output are recorded in `_handoff/PR_BODY.md`.
* **Status meanings:**
  * **PINNED (synthetic):** the property is enforced by the reference and asserted on analytic or
    synthetic inputs.
  * **EMPIRICAL:** additionally shown on real retained local data.
* No requirement has an empirical leg on a market quote chain, because none exists locally (verdict
  INSUFFICIENT_DATA, see `VERDICT.md`).
