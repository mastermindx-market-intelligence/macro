## Recommendation

Start with one authorized source and one temporal provenance demonstration. Register a KPI study only when a defensible target exists. Commercial priorities are conditional: merchant transactions → comparable restaurant sales, then app activity → reported users. A semiconductor mandate should first establish an issuer monthly-KPI comparator and reconcile existing TIL/GMI physical evidence.

Keep observations, economic exposure, features, forecasts and expectations inspectable. Evaluate the sensor against a strong same-origin baseline, with first-release targets, actual availability, full-coverage fallback, dependence-aware uncertainty and an explicit inconclusive outcome.

## What the hardening corrected

- Existing DOL hiring-intent and aggregate Census trade-flow owners were missing from the earlier census.
- The committed trade-flow projection is an empty-data state; a fresh render is not fresh evidence.
- Older trade data can be revised. Existing developer snapshots can overwrite same-day observations.
- App ratings are not usage; an earnings-expectation risk lane is not a general operational-nowcast interface.
- October alternative-data outcomes supersede the earlier August-only description, while all relevant authority cohorts remain ineligible and clock vintages must stay separate.
- Vendor event history, reconstructed history, archived source state and actual client receipts are different evidence.
- Engineering proof, measurement usefulness, expectation information and investment utility need separate acceptance gates.
