# Commission 16 — Operational Sensor Source Diligence Appendix

**Research date:** October 4, 2026, UTC. **Scope:** payments and SKU sales; web and app usage; hiring; foot traffic; pricing and ranks; manifests, customs and AIS; advertising; developer adoption.

This appendix reviews public primary sources. It establishes what providers document or advertise and the questions an actual delivery must resolve. No commercial licensed feed, historical sample, vendor contract or account entitlement was inspected. Product claims, model accuracy and historical coverage have therefore not been independently validated. No purchase, account login or vendor contact was undertaken.

## How to interpret the evidence

A long historical time series and a usable point-in-time archive are separate properties. A transaction, visit or shipment can have an old event date while first appearing in a vendor product years later. An old observation can also be recomputed using today's model, panel or corporate ownership. Neither its event date nor its availability in today's download establishes that a historical decision could have used it.

Four questions must remain separate: what happened; when the provider learned it; when the customer could receive it; and which version was actually used. Historical completeness is useful for understanding measurement and seasonality. Historical replay requires additional evidence about publication, corrections and mapping versions. Forward collection can establish that evidence prospectively when the retrospective archive cannot.

The source register below records concrete definitions and limitations alongside unresolved entitlement questions. An advertised point-in-time product is a promising diligence candidate; the actual contract, sample schema and replay demonstration determine whether that label applies to a particular delivered series.

## Focused commercial shortlist and KPI calibration examples

### Merchant transactions → Chipotle comparable sales, transactions and check

**Primary candidate:** Facteus Arbiter 2.0/merchant-level transaction delivery (S31), with Earnest Orion as documentary/procurement challenger if its vintage evidence is superior. Arbiter's public schema provides deterministic spending/cardholder/transaction inputs; its PRO_FORMA field must not be used as historical issuer ownership. Do not substitute Onyx SKU history for a restaurant payment feed. Anchor the initial target on **CMG**, then require a small preregistered restaurant cohort before cohort-level or generalizable inference. Chipotle's July 29, 2026 earnings disclosure reports comparable sales and its transaction/check decomposition; it defines comparable company-owned restaurants as operating at least 13 full calendar months. [Issuer primary source](https://ir.chipotle.com/2026-07-29-CHIPOTLE-RAISES-FULL-YEAR-COMPARABLE-SALES-GUIDANCE-ON-STRONG-Q2-MOMENTUM); [definition](https://newsroom.chipotle.com/2026-07-29-CHIPOTLE-RAISES-FULL-YEAR-COMPARABLE-SALES-GUIDANCE-ON-STRONG-Q2-MOMENTUM).

Test weekly same-location/same-panel spending, transaction count and ticket growth against separately disclosed KPIs. Require store IDs/open dates or explicitly lower target to chain-level sales with an expansion control. Estimate mapping and seasonal parameters on earlier periods only. Keep cash, gift-card reload/redemption, refunds, tax/tips, aggregators, delivery platform descriptors, and international operations in a coverage bridge. Purchases through a third-party delivery merchant can be attributed differently than food sales; blind merchant aggregation can miss the transaction. A franchisor's system sales are a separate KPI from consolidated royalty revenue, so avoid transporting the CMG causal model to franchise-heavy names without changing accounting bridge.

Selection rationale: low causal distance and issuer-provided decomposition. **Missing gate:** neither vendor's inspected public docs establishes sufficient frozen historical vintages to run a decision-time backtest. If unavailable, begin forward-only KPI shadows with immutable receipts; historical corrected data is exploratory only.

### Sensor Tower app usage → Duolingo DAU/MAU and a separate monetization bridge

**Primary candidate:** Sensor Tower App Performance/Advanced Usage with its advertised PIT Scheduled Data Feed. **Initial issuer:** DUOL. Confirm the app package separately from advertising, web and gaming entitlements. Duolingo's 2025 10-K describes DAU, MAU, paid subscribers, subscription bookings and total bookings as operating metrics; its Q1 2026 letter excludes free trials and nonpaying family members from paid subscribers. [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm); [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm).

Target app-active-user direction and country/platform weighted growth first; exact global unique users require cross-device/cross-platform deduplication and browser coverage. Downloads are acquisition, not active users. Keep app-estimated revenue separate from bookings and recognized revenue, whose payment channels, fees, taxes, FX, free trials, annual subscriptions and timing differ. Older official Sensor Tower methodology reports explicitly exclude mobile-commerce spending and in-app advertising from app revenue estimates; request current metric definition rather than carrying that historical definition forward untested ([official report explanation](https://sensortower.com/blog/app-revenue-and-downloads-q3-2022)).

Selection rationale: issuer KPIs are observable and the source advertises PIT. Require vintage sample before granting that label to actual observations. Stratify validation across the 2025 combined-panel/model transition. Similarweb is a sensible alternative for a web-mediated cohort, but its explicit revised-history behavior makes forward collection or separately proven vintage delivery necessary.

### Evidence required before continuation

Predeclare universe/targets, feature transformations and forecast cutoffs; use initial forward earnings cycles to assess operational feasibility, while recognizing that a small cohort cannot establish statistical reliability. Grade KPI error against seasonal issuer baselines; grade incremental information against contemporaneous consensus only where licensed PIT consensus exists. Keep two outcome tracks: KPI accuracy and investment information. A nowcast can be accurate yet already priced. Costs/rights/history/coverage are acceptance gates; price-relative evidence does not fix measurement errors.

## Historical coverage, vintages and correction diligence

For a commercial source's claimed historical-vintage/correction capabilities, require a small actual licensed sample demonstrating: event/effective time; vendor first publication; delivery time; local receipt time; stable record IDs; two revisions with changed fields and correction reason; deletion/tombstone behavior; method/panel/source/POI/entity-map versions; historical availability start distinct from earliest event date; exact replay semantics; source-specific coverage and missingness. Distinguish (a) newest corrected historical series, (b) historic event log, (c) frozen historical vendor state, (d) actual client delivery receipt. Only (c)/(d), with proper mapping versions and permissible usage, establish decision-time availability. If a source exposes no archived corrections, certify only demonstrated immutable client receipts and retain the supplier archive limitation. A targetless existing-source provenance pilot does not require a commercial purchase or a fictitious KPI.

Ask actual contracts for internal investment research/automated processing, storage of raw receipts, derived features/model outputs, post-termination audit/reproduction, affiliate/contractor access, use in an LLM or hosted compute, displayed evidence and redistribution. Record these as rights fields, not assumptions based on public marketing. Applicable rights must be resolved from the order and governing contract. No exact commercial prices were established in this research.

## Shipment shortlist decision

**Company import/supplier attribution:** compare Panjiva versus ImportGenius and Datamyne with the same frozen issuer/supplier sample. Panjiva has the most accessible public technical specification inspected; IG has clearer plan-specific U.S. lookback; Datamyne distinguishes company/cargo/market analytics. None earns historical PIT approval from those facts alone.

**Physical commodity flows:** Kpler is the best documented technical diligence candidate because dated snapshots and correction operations exist. Its AIS lineage may support inputs, but avoid independently counting Spire Maritime. Require installation→operator→issuer exposure plus seller/buyer/charterer roles, scheduled/completed state and then-known cargo/product/destination.

**Aggregate controls:** use Census commodity/country/port history, with archived publication receipts; the live revised API alone cannot stand in for an as-of release archive. No generic shipment adapter should imply shipment arrival equals company revenue.


## Cost and permissions: questions for an actual order

**Exact commercial cost is unknown.** Public entry-level search prices, where visible, would not price enterprise API delivery, historical snapshots, redistribution or model use. No quotes were requested. The evaluation should record annual commitment, minimum term, renewal/cancellation rules, countries, issuers, metrics, lookback, update cadence, query or row limits, export charges, support and required infrastructure. Ask for the total cost of the specific research workflow, including an initial historical extract and ongoing correction delivery.

Compare costs at the level of a reproducible decision: the fixed issuer/KPI cohort, forecast frequency, retained receipts and permitted users. A cheaper dashboard may become an expensive manual data pipeline. A broad bundle may add no incremental information to a narrow KPI target. Neither consideration justifies assuming an unavailable entitlement. Documented government controls can be evaluated separately from commercial nowcasts.

The permissions discussion should resolve these concrete uses:

- Can the named legal entity use the feed for internal investment research and automated KPI forecasts? Are affiliates, employees and contracted implementers included?
- Can it retain immutable raw responses, correction records, map versions and model inputs for audit? Does that right survive expiry or termination, and what deletion obligations apply?
- May it store derived features, fitted parameters and forecast outputs? Does the provider distinguish reversible derived data from non-reconstructable analysis?
- Are cloud storage, managed databases and external processors permitted? May limited observations be supplied to an LLM for interpretation, under what provider/account controls, and with which retention restrictions?
- May research reports display individual values, aggregates, charts or inferred KPIs? Are source attribution, numerical limits or preapproval required? Who may see those reports?
- May the same observations be combined with competing feeds or used for independently retained evaluation results? Is a vendor sample restricted to evaluation or allowed in a production history?

These are required design inputs rather than presumed prohibitions. Public accessibility of an underlying record does not resolve the rights to a vendor's cleaned, matched or modeled product. The Panjiva agreement in S29 is an example of why the actual order matters; it does not establish the terms of other suppliers.

## Demonstration protocol for vendor vintage claims

Request an authorized sample covering a fixed issuer, metric and period, delivered at two different historical knowledge dates. Ask the provider to explain the differences at record level: newly discovered observations, amended amounts, reassigned entities, deletions and methodological recomputation. A sample containing only two files generated today is insufficient unless the files are demonstrably frozen representations of the old provider states.

The acceptance demonstration should make a forecast cutoff reproducible. Select a timestamp, reconstruct exactly the eligible observations and effective mappings, and show which later records were excluded. Repeat after importing corrections. The old forecast must reproduce from its retained inputs; a new forecast may use the revised information. Keep raw data and derived features separately so a model change cannot silently rewrite what the earlier decision saw.

For provider snapshots, verify how the requested date is interpreted: calendar day, publication batch, UTC instant or last snapshot before the date. Record time zone, batch completion and incomplete-period handling. For change feeds, verify ordering, idempotency, deletion semantics, cursor expiration and recovery after a missed window. For modeled panels, request the historical model version and whether the provider can return earlier weights rather than recomputing them now.

Coverage also needs a replay test. Choose an issuer that changed ownership, a product that changed identifier, a low-coverage entity and a record that was later removed. Check whether the historical result reflects the then-existing entity and coverage, or today's surviving universe. An empty query must distinguish insufficient coverage, confidentiality, discontinued collection, no eligible events and a failed request.

## Decision boundaries

A provider can pass access and schema review while failing historical-vintage review. That result supports forward observation collection and exploratory measurement analysis; it does not authorize historical decision-grade performance claims. Conversely, frozen historical data can pass replay review while failing the causal link to the issuer KPI. Both properties are necessary, and neither establishes tradable value by itself.

Maintain a narrow source-dependent scope. Do not infer actual hiring from posting certifications, active app use from ratings, exact sales from platform ranks, recognized revenue from arrivals, or paying customers from developer interest. These proxies can motivate a hypothesis; their empirical bridge to the target KPI must be demonstrated separately. Shipment and commodity products deserve dedicated exposure mapping rather than inclusion in a universal operational score.
