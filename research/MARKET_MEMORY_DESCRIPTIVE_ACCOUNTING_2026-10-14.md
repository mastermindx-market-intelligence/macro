# Market Memory descriptive evidence accounting

`engine.research_factory.market_memory_evidence_accounting` accounts for caller-supplied
assessment records without making a research decision. It is preparation for the
existing A0/E1 evaluation, not an installed study owner or an admission grant.

Each registered task has one cell in each of three conditions: no memory, recency,
and fixed L1. Missing observations remain explicit cells. The answerability/response
ledger is exhaustive, including incorrect and unresolved answers, abstention,
source unavailability, pending and censored responses. Implicit missingness retains
`record_present=false`; an explicitly supplied missing record retains true.

Material-claim counts retain unsupported, contradicted, attributed-inference and
unresolved judgments in the denominator. A support precision is unavailable while
material judgments remain unresolved. Identification bounds describe possible
resolved support shares; they are not confidence intervals. Empty denominators
produce null values. Critical claims remain visible even in the no-memory or
recency baseline. Caller ratings are not authenticated or generated by this API.

Retrieval coverage counts the union of registered task facets supported by relevance-2
items. Abstention may retain retrieved evidence without asserting any claims.
Unknown facets, including those registered only to another task, are rejected.
Missing observations have unobserved coverage, rather than a zero-performance score.
Contradiction recall explicitly remains `NOT_REGISTERED` because no gold contradiction
inventory enters this interface.

Paired coverage is signed fixed-L1 minus recency. Only observed pairs contribute to
its point estimate, while excluded tasks and their statuses remain in the output.
Cluster means give each supplied cluster equal weight and are unavailable when an
included task has unknown membership. Both task and cluster point estimates preserve
exact integer fractions. This module estimates no independence, confidence interval,
effective sample size, power, threshold, or acceptance disposition.
