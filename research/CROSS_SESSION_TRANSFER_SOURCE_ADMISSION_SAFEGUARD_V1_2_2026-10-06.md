## 5. Full-source-day admission gate

For every UTC source day containing a potential qualifying event:

1. keep the target HSI endpoint sealed;
2. wait until the complete UTC source day has elapsed;
3. run the bounded source-only census for all frozen narrative families;
4. record every candidate, exact source clock or honest unresolved-clock state, source quality, family,
   parent cluster, and inclusion/exclusion disposition;
5. persist that completed-day source receipt;
6. only then may the U.S. +5->+35 legs of already-admitted rows be opened from historical minute data.

Therefore source discovery no longer races same-day U.S. measurement.

An unresolved earliest public clock may be logged, but its U.S. leg stays unmeasured. If the clock is
resolved only after any relevant market exposure, that row remains late/non-evaluable.
