## 1. The capability we need

An investor investigating a regional technology development should be able to distinguish where a company is listed, where its customers buy, where its products are deployed, where it produces or delivers, and which currencies affect cash generation. The machine should not turn a headquarters address into a revenue map, a shipping destination into an end-user location, or separate product and geography totals into an invented joint exposure.

The proposed capability is **evidenced economic exposure, including valid bounds when a precise answer is not identifiable**. An honest bounded answer can be more useful than either a fabricated percentage or an uninformative missing-data label. Bounds remain mathematical consequences of particular assumptions; they are not probability intervals or certified current theme weights.

The research must also recognize that a vendor can sell enabling products into an excluded industry without transferring that industry's research program. Semiconductor tools, chip design and fabrication remain with their incumbent owner. In mixed businesses, isolate the non-semiconductor product/workload, preserve the customer-industry dependency and disclose unallocated scope.

## 2. Eight separate location and currency questions

| Dimension | Native meaning to retain | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate and listing identity | Legal issuer, ownership, security, listing venue and effective date from the existing identity owner | A venue or ticker suffix defines the company's entire economic exposure |
| Customer location | The issuer's stated customer-location or domicile rule | The buyer's address identifies the ultimate users or deployment sites |
| Ship-to and distribution | Shipping destination, distributor/reseller status and period | Distributor destination equals final demand geography |
| Deployment and workload | Evidenced installation, operating site or customer-use location | A local office, event, launch or contract signature proves production deployment |
| Production and supply | Facility, product/configuration, qualification, output and current/target status | Announced floor area or regional sales equals usable manufacturing capacity |
| Delivery workforce and cost base | Labor/service location, function, period, headcount definition and cost currency | Customer revenue geography equals employee geography or payroll exposure |
| Financing and cash | Currency and location of obligations, receivables, available cash and remittance constraints where evidenced | All balance-sheet cash is freely transferable excess cash |
| Measurement and valuation currency | Reporting, constant-currency, listing and investor currency; nominal/real basis | Same percentage growth or monetary multiple is comparable without a currency/period bridge |

These are facets on existing source and identity owners, not eight new tables with independent authority. Retain the original geography label and its definition even when a display crosswalk groups it. APJ, APAC and Asia excluding a home market are not automatically interchangeable.

## 3. Representative company evidence and proposed research consequences

The source register in section 10 supplies locators. All analytical consequences are this research's proposals. Issuer statements remain attributed and period-specific, including management estimates and targets.

### 3.1 Ubiquiti — product scope and shipping scope

The June 2026 annual filing separates Enterprise Technology and Service Provider Technology and explicitly bases geography on customer ship-to destinations; distributor resales may reach other countries [S01]. The exact marginal figures used below do not disclose the joint product-by-region table.

Research consequence: label a North America exposure as ship-to, not end demand. Retain platform identity, distributor inventory and actual end-customer evidence separately. The mechanism to investigate is paid deployments and retained product/platform economics, not a presumed AI or hyperscaler exposure inferred from a networking label.

### 3.2 SAP — a disclosed intersection is better than an inferred one

SAP's half-year report supplies both regional revenue and regional cloud revenue, using customer location [S02]. That permits a real cloud-by-region calculation. It does not identify every country's deployment or hosting region. Its employee table is a different population and cannot be used to allocate customer revenue.

Research consequence: use the actual compatible intersection where available; otherwise show missing or bounded values. Preserve a published rounding residual rather than modify reported totals until a table appears exact. Contract and operating-metric definitions from R2 remain applicable.

### 3.3 Infosys — the reporting currency changes the growth headline

The same June 2026 quarter is presented as revenue of INR48,211 crore versus INR42,279 crore, and USD5,082 million versus USD4,941 million [S03]. These yield approximately 14.03% and 2.85% growth from the displayed amounts. The calculation does not isolate price, volume or a single realized exchange rate.

Research consequence: preserve currency and translation basis before comparing companies or feeding a regime hypothesis. Customer-budget, delivery-labor and financing sensitivities require separately evidenced inputs; this financial snapshot does not establish their regional shares.

### 3.4 Kingdee — native AI revenue is narrower than cloud exposure

The issuer's August 11 release reports H1 revenue of RMB3,625m, including RMB296m under its AI-native definition; associated subscription revenue is RMB146m and AI contract value is RMB605m. Cloud revenue and ending subscription ARR are different measures. Approximately 140 international customer signings are reported, not an overseas revenue percentage [S04].

Research consequence: a derived AI-native share of about 8.17% is legitimate only under that issuer definition and period. Do not substitute the much broader cloud share, add contract value to revenue or convert overseas customer count into sales materiality. Localization and actual paid adoption must be established by product/customer evidence, not by listing venue.

### 3.5 Samsung SDS — a technology-and-logistics perimeter

The July 30 preliminary release reports Q2 IT services of KRW1,762.5bn and logistics of KRW1,955.3bn. Cloud revenue of KRW779.4bn is within IT services, not a third division [S05].

Research consequence: a company-level software multiple would require a justified sum-of-parts and shared-cost bridge. The calculated cloud share changes with the denominator: about 44.22% of IT services versus 20.96% of group revenue. Neither is a cloud-profit share. Infrastructure expansion targets remain targets, and this issuer must not be confused with another business in the same corporate family.

### 3.6 Lenovo — consolidation comes before geographic materiality

The June-quarter statement reports three business revenues totaling USD28,499.224m before USD1,556.458m of eliminations, giving USD26,942.766m consolidated revenue. Its geography table totals to the consolidated amount. The statement also separately identifies warrant valuation effects and potential shares [S06].

Research consequence: do not multiply a pre-elimination segment share by a consolidated regional share. A later sum-of-parts must allocate eliminations and central items explicitly. Adjusting a noncash earnings item does not establish that associated financial claims or dilution have no economic value; R4B specifies that boundary.

### 3.7 Advantech — distinct sector, region and product-stage evidence

The August presentation provides H1 regional and sector revenue tables, each totaling USD1,470m; sector operating profits reconcile to USD280m only after USD41m of unallocated expense [S07]. Its edited call transcript says WEDA is supplied without a separate charge alongside hardware, while IWS has limited current contribution and was not a principal first-half growth driver [S08].

Research consequence: do not promote a product architecture into a separately monetized software business. A hardware-enabled ecosystem can still support differentiation, but the paid unit must be identified. The tables include semiconductor-linked customer applications; retain the program boundary instead of calling all reported revenue non-semiconductor. Geographic allocation basis requires further filing-level confirmation beyond the presentation labels.

### 3.8 Fujitsu — price architecture is a testable transformation thesis

September 16 investor-day materials describe output/usage-based pricing, including fixed-duration development-output contracts. A separate CFO presentation sets long-horizon margin and cash targets and defines cash measures that exclude specified growth investment or one-time effects [S09, S10].

Research consequence: track realized contract adoption, retained unit economics, renewal and investment against the stated ambition. A management target is not achieved earnings or analyst consensus. Research the services/application scope without importing the chip-development portion of the wider corporate vision.

### 3.9 Constellation Software — acquisition-funded growth

The August release presents results in USD although the shares are listed in Toronto. Q2 revenue growth is 17%, with 3% issuer-defined organic growth and 1% after its FX adjustment. FCFA2S is USD345m and expressly precedes acquisitions, investments and debt repayment; acquisition cash consideration is separately disclosed and includes acquired cash [S11].

Research consequence: do not forecast acquisition-driven growth while treating acquisition funding as permanently free. Do not mechanically subtract the two reported cash quantities without reconciling acquired cash, consolidation, timing and noncontrolling interests. Reporting currency also cannot be inferred from the listing.

### 3.10 CGI — operating growth and per-share growth differ

The July release uses CAD. Its adjusted net earnings grow 1.7%, while adjusted diluted EPS grows 9.0%; it separately reports currency-adjusted revenue growth [S12].

Research consequence: per-share improvement requires a denominator and capital-allocation bridge. The rounded percentages are not a precise share-count disclosure. A buyback can benefit remaining holders, but its cost and the foregone use of cash cannot disappear from valuation. No conclusion about the quality of an actual buyback is made here.

## 4. When product-by-region exposure is mathematically identifiable

Let T be compatible total revenue, A revenue for one product scope and B revenue for one geography. Under nonnegative revenue cells on the SAME entity, accounting, period, currency and consolidation perimeter, the intersection X obeys:

`max(0, A + B - T) <= X <= min(A, B)`

Proof: X cannot exceed either marginal. The union is A+B-X and cannot exceed T; X is nonnegative. These constraints are sharp for the two-way table: every X in the interval gives cells X, A-X, B-X and T-A-B+X that preserve the marginals and remain nonnegative.

The assumptions matter. Product revenues including intersegment sales are not compatible with a consolidated external-revenue geography total. Negative contra-revenue cells, overlapping non-partitioning scopes, different periods, currencies or consolidation perimeters can invalidate this simple construction. Rounding creates an additional tolerance problem. Do not silently repair incompatible inputs or apply the formula to an arbitrary graph edge.

### Case C01 — Ubiquiti marginals

Displayed USD thousands for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026 [S01]:

- T = 3,274,162.
- Enterprise Technology A = 2,972,302; the other product category is 301,860.
- North America ship-to B = 1,743,978; other regions total 1,530,184.

Thus X lies from **1,442,118 to 1,743,978**, or approximately **USD1.442bn to USD1.744bn**. This is a bound on the disclosed ship-to/product intersection, not on final North American enterprise demand.

An independence assumption would yield about USD1.583bn. That is an illustrative allocation, not a reported fact, a best estimate, or the midpoint of a probability distribution. Both endpoint contingency tables were constructed and checked. No evidence currently supports assigning probabilities inside the range.

### Case C02 — SAP's direct intersection

For H1 2026, regional total/cloud pairs in EURm are EMEA 9,082/5,321, Americas 7,597/5,147 and APJ 2,753/1,775 [S02]. Cloud shares within the respective regions are about **58.59%, 67.75% and 64.48%**. The published group cloud total is 12,244; the rounded regional cells sum to 12,243. Preserve the EUR1m difference as a disclosed-table reconciliation residual.

Multiplying the group cloud ratio by Americas revenue would ignore the directly available intersection and produce a different answer. The interface should prefer the observed compatible cross-table, not the most convenient general formula. Neither method proves the location of cloud-hosting assets.

### Case C07 — Advantech's missing intersection

H1 iSystems revenue is USD546m and North America revenue is USD432m, against a common USD1,470m total [S07]. Marginals alone permit a range of zero to USD432m. The wide interval is useful: it shows why a precise iSystems-in-North-America percentage cannot be recovered from the two tables. Qualitative cross-market descriptions may establish a relationship, but they do not supply the missing monetary cell. The formula does not erase such qualitative evidence; it reports what the numerical marginals alone establish.

## 5. Practical representation in the existing product

A proposed exposure cell should retain these semantic requirements, mapped into accepted native owners rather than a new global registry:

`subject/business scope; product/workload facet; geography value; geography basis; source and publication; period; currency; numerator; denominator; consolidation; method; bounds or value; rounding; comparability; exclusions; correction; review/rights; evidence limitations.`

Method should distinguish a reported intersection, a direct ratio, an identified bound, a scenario allocation, a qualitative relationship and unavailable coverage. The labels are research proposals, not newly enrolled enums. Historical values retain the original source vintage; newer geographic definitions do not retroactively rewrite what was known.

The visible sentence should identify the method. For example: a bounded intersection should read as a possible range consistent with disclosed totals, not 'estimated exposure' without qualification. A qualitative installation reference should not acquire a numeric materiality badge. Unknown is not zero. Confidence about the source and precision of the economic estimate are different dimensions.

One company can participate in multiple themes, while one source may supply evidence for several facets. Reusing that source does not create independent confirmation. A parent business and its nested product should not be added as separate revenue. A residual unallocated share is necessary when known disjoint allocations do not cover the whole business.

## 6. Regional thesis construction without regional stereotypes

Each dossier should construct a mechanism from its actual buyer, product and cost base. Candidate research mechanisms include localized enterprise workflows, multi-country deployment of existing customers, outsourced development, regional hardware configuration, industrial edge deployment and changes in service pricing. These are questions, not predetermined national advantages.

For each mechanism specify:

- the economic buyer and paying unit;
- evidence of current product capability versus signed, implemented or renewed business;
- revenue materiality and its allowed precision;
- customer geography versus delivery and production geography;
- dependencies, substitutes and original-source relationships;
- pricing, support, localization and deployment costs;
- currency, financing and working-capital channels;
- the next observation that could support or refute the thesis.

The falsifiers should be commercially meaningful. A localization initiative may fail to convert contracts into durable paid use. Output-based pricing may be offset by price concessions or delivery risk. A regional hardware ramp may fail to become qualified shipped capacity. A new software layer may remain bundled without separately monetizable economics. A serial acquirer may grow reported sales but fail to earn sufficient returns on incremental acquisition spending.

Neither headquarters nor listing location predicts these outcomes. A regional government program or industry forecast is not company revenue; any future use must preserve its native stage, rights and political-neutrality boundary. This chapter makes no policy recommendation or geopolitical forecast.

## 7. Comparability and valuation handoff

The R4B valuation work must consume the same perimeter, not simply attach a multiple to a research label. A cloud subset is not necessarily a standalone profit pool. A reported segment may include internal transactions; corporate expenses and shared assets must be allocated or explicitly left unallocated. Joint geographic exposure bounds should not become point estimates inside a discounted cash flow without a named scenario.

Currency handling requires four separate choices: the operational currency mix, the reporting presentation, the investor's currency, and the nominal or real valuation basis. A fixed-currency managerial growth statistic is useful operating context but is not cash translated into an investor's currency. Do not use a home-market nominal discount rate merely because the issuer is listed there.

Periods must also align. June-quarter, June half-year and June fiscal-year observations are not equivalent despite the shared date. Annualizing a quarter requires an explicit seasonality assumption. Market-price timestamps cannot precede a fact's public availability in an as-known analysis; later filings and revised definitions cannot be backfilled into a historical signal.
