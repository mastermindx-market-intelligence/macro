## 1. Outcome: turn domain knowledge into a usable economic-change dossier

The user must be able to follow a material change from a company product/workload to its paid economics, inspect compatible evidence and uncertainty, compare management expectations where possible, and return to the existing company/entry workflow. The first delivery must answer a useful question without pretending to possess every research input.

The architecture should compose existing owners, not build a Technology database beside them. The feasible direction is an **economic-change projection with owner-native observations, versioned definitions, deterministic comparisons, explicit interpretation, and bounded evidence navigation**. Shared sector/theme pages supply the shell. The research supplies the economic grammar; existing financial, company, graph, identity, access and evaluation owners retain their authority.

A complete first slice is not an empty schema or another list of tickers. It must show a real, admitted source through native persistence and an authenticated visible result, plus a correction or unavailable-input case. The first software slice should explain a management-guidance change after accounting for already-reported results. The next physical slice should distinguish unit demand, price/mix and retained value. Their common display grammar must not force their different economic models into a universal score.

## 4. Required observation semantics

Every quantitative value must answer: **what was measured, for which business, over which period, on which basis, in which units, according to which source and definition, and when could each version be known?**

The semantic requirements below are to be mapped to native schemas before implementation. The labels are not proposed production field names and do not authorize an independent universal observation object.

- Subject: validated issuer where available; source-scoped business/product/configuration; ownership/perimeter date; comparison cohort.
- Measure: native metric identifier and definition revision; unit/currency/scale; stock, flow, rate or annualized run-rate; reported, derived, estimated or conditional status.
- Scope: fiscal start/end and calendar presentation, consolidated or segment basis, gross/net recognition, customer/ship-to/deployment geography, denominator and overlap constraints.
- Evidence: native source object/revision/digest, source selector, text/table receipt class, original-source dependence and applicable rights profile.
- Time: upstream publication precision, public availability if established, collection/observation, native recording, computation, business applicability and review trigger.
- Change: original versus corrected presentation, predecessor references, metric/perimeter/FX reconciliation, and a reason a comparison is either lawful or refused.

**Unknown precision is not midnight.** The inspected company helpers can normalize date-only values to midnight UTC. That is a representation convention, not evidence that an original disclosure was available at that exact instant. Any new intraday eligibility calculation must retain original source precision through an accepted owner extension or refuse the stronger timing claim. A consumer cannot reconstruct precision after the native record has discarded it.

**Address-only is not byte-replayed.** A source_span table-cell or slide locator can establish where a reviewer looked without establishing that native stored bytes reproduce the number. The product should say which form of evidence it has. In the first automated numeric slice, prefer actually retained and verified text/filing context. A table-only input remains a reviewed source observation with its stated receipt limits until the proper owner supports stronger extraction and replay.

**New forecasts and corrections differ.** A company issuing a new outlook changes the expectation history even when the underlying fiscal event identity remains stable. A correction fixes a particular prior source version. The later actual is a third object. Preserve the event identity and distinguish source revisions, issued outlook versions and realized results through the existing owners; do not improvise an additional guidance-event plane.

## 5. Feasible data classes and their limits

### Official filings and issuer releases

The SEC's aggregate XBRL APIs select non-custom taxonomies and facts for the entire filing entity [E01]. That makes them useful for some standardized quantities, but not a complete supply of custom ARR definitions, product-by-region cells, non-GAAP reconciliations or company-specific operating measures. Retained filing packages and issuer documents remain necessary. The absence of a custom metric from CompanyFacts is not proof the company never disclosed it.

The same SEC documentation says frame facts can have different period boundaries and data.sec.gov has no CORS support [E01]. Therefore the browser should not fetch or normalize SEC data at page-load time. Existing collectors and native packet/query owners handle lawful acquisition, comparison and retention. Provider dissemination speed is not a measured Mastermind source-to-visible service level.

### Catalogs, pricing and technical specifications

Use official product documentation to establish product scope, charging units, allowances, configuration and stated capabilities. Catalog price is not realized enterprise price. A standard is not a purchase; a product launch is not qualified volume. Dated source retention and configuration specificity are essential. For non-event catalogs, use the accepted GMI/house curation source route rather than fabricating an earnings event to fit the existing document types.

### Geography and operating statistics

Use native statements of how regions are defined. Customer address, ship-to, manufacturing and deployment are separate. Joint exposure is exact only when disclosed on a compatible basis. Mathematical bounds can be displayed with clear assumptions; they do not become confidence intervals or tradable basket weights. Ratios require known nonzero denominators and disjointness where totals are added.

### Market/regime vintages

ALFRED can retrieve observations by historical vintage, but its own help states that release-date provenance can come from the originating source, a provider, or first availability in FRED, and that revisions are generally added after release [E02]. Its release-dates API explicitly warns that source release dates need not be FRED/ALFRED availability dates [E03]. An archived vintage is useful but does not by itself prove a precise original-system ingestion clock. Use the existing macro owner, retain these distinctions, and label reconstruction appropriately.

### Consensus and forecast history

The current repository's VEND-0 record documents sample and rights requirements and does not select a vendor [N14]. It explicitly warns that a missing adapter/sample in that historical audit does not prove the company has no subscription elsewhere. This project has not queried account entitlements. Therefore the first build must work without a licensed historical consensus panel, show that absence, and avoid earnings-beat language unless a compatible accepted estimate is actually supplied.

Management outlook, house scenario, external consensus, actual result and market-implied conditional requirement remain five different objects. They are not fallback aliases for one another.

### Alternative data

Developer activity, job postings, pricing changes, customer references and traffic can be useful hypotheses or corroboration candidates, but they do not establish paid revenue. Their rights, historical coverage, source dependence and selection bias need source-specific admission. They should not delay the first source-backed economic-change slice or acquire automatic forecast weight merely by being easy to collect.

## 6. Selected delivery direction and alternatives

Three approaches were considered as research choices:

**A — Editorial research panels only.** A source-backed account of what the company does is useful and relatively easy to publish safely, but it leaves the most important economic comparisons to the user. It is suitable as a bounded first data-admission witness, not the final destination.

**B — Owner-composed economic-change dossiers. Recommended direction.** Source-scoped product/business evidence is composed with accepted native financial/earnings observations, deterministic comparisons and existing sector/company navigation. Unknown metrics, rights and identities degrade narrowly. This preserves the full investment job while avoiding a new data platform.

**C — A universal product, economic and expectation graph immediately. Not selected for initial delivery.** It offers eventual expressive power but would require wider identity, source, rights and inference ownership decisions before proving one useful workflow. Preserve versioned source-scoped objects and relationship types so later accepted identity promotion is possible; do not make that future graph a hidden prerequisite.

This research recommendation is not approval of a written implementation specification or shared-owner interface modification. It is consistent with the existing shared federation direction. The separate Semiconductor program has its own B-first/C-later decision; that decision is evidence of compatibility, not a delegation of its source custody to this session.

## 8. Identity and composition behavior

A source-scoped assertion can remain useful without a security join: a reader may inspect a named product, catalog role and evidence while the stock link is visibly unresolved. It may not acquire live valuation or portfolio relevance from an approximate name match.

Where a native financial/earnings adapter proves an issuer binding, consume that exact scope. Where K1 requires exact canonical subject equality and lacks a bridge slot, a declarative mapping is not enough. A required combined output refuses; an optional leg may be excluded with a visible reason. Showing two independently valid panels next to each other is permissible only when their limits are explicit; it must not look like a successfully compiled unified security recipe.

The native D5 owner has a narrower episode-bound bridge and allowed lanes [N13]. It is not a general permission to import all Technology numerical facts. New research evidence must obtain its own accepted adapter/version before entering that envelope. Missing adapter means no invented family, not a neutral row.

## 9. Freshness, correction, access and publication

Use existing source refresh and publication owners; do not introduce a Technology poller, queue or retry service. The meaningful freshness unit differs by input. A quarterly operating disclosure is not stale simply because it was not published today; a supposedly current price can be stale within a much shorter period. Store upstream period and publication beside computation time.

Define expected observation cadence from the selected native source and announced event calendar where supported. If a new release is expected but not admitted, show the last accepted version and the precise missing stage. Do not replace its source date with today's build date. If the source is corrected, preserve the earlier answer and publish a new version through the existing lineage. A numerical input correction triggers recomputation of dependent derived views; an unrelated document change need not regenerate unrelated observations.

Authentication and rights are separate tests. Follow existing `require_user -> enforce_site_full(always=True)` before private reads and retain private/no-store behavior on success and errors [N10]. Do not embed the detailed current paid payload in public generated HTML/JSON, source maps, public Git, prefetches, service workers, localStorage or IndexedDB. These requirements apply even when the source material originated publicly. The publication owner must explicitly determine what house-authored editorial subset may be public.

No source family inherits a grandfathered path's rights by sharing a template or label [N03]. Current policy overrides old mint-time licensing snapshots. Source revocation or entitlement failure must be represented in the access/retention owner's permitted manner without rewriting historical analytical truth or leaking the withdrawn bytes through diagnostics.

A generic API 200 response is not acceptance. Proof must show the actual admitted source revision, stored observation, derived output, authenticated browser rendering, evidence drawer, error path and unchanged legacy theme/Prophet outputs.

## 10. Historical research modes and evaluation handoff

Three questions require different evidence:

1. **What is the best supported answer now?** Current accepted sources and current definitions, with earlier versions accessible where supported.
2. **What could a researcher reconstruct from information publicly available at a past cut?** Historical source versions plus explicit reconstruction/model assumptions. This is not evidence Mastermind actually possessed or processed that information then.
3. **What did the original system know and compute at the decision cut?** Native historical event universe, identity era, source availability, observation/recording and computation versions all need to exist before the cut.

The D5 census explicitly records current-generation event discovery and no full historical event-set reconstruction [N13]. Its absence from today's event list cannot prove that no event existed at a past cut. The first Technology study must therefore label its mode and refuse an as-executed claim when those prerequisites are missing. This is a denominator problem as well as a value-timestamp problem.

R5B will specify the frozen feature/label definitions, source dependence, evaluation cohorts, falsifiers, prospective observation and decision gates. Evaluation belongs to Evaluation OS/QLedger and applicable existing episode owners, not a new Technology scoreboard. A correct explanation can be accepted for display before there is sufficient evidence to change a selection or rank.
