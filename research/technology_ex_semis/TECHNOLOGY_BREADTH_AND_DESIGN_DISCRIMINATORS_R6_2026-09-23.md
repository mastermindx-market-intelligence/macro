## 1. Why this supplement exists

R1–R5 established the economic models and native integration boundaries. This supplement broadens the design witnesses beyond large US software and AI infrastructure. Eleven additional company/business cases cover accounting, logistics applications, construction software, physical authentication, Chinese enterprise applications, industrial edge systems, peripherals, mixed IT/logistics services, security delivery costs, infrastructure engineering and electronic-paper displays.

These are deliberately selected design examples. Their usefulness is finding a requirement that a generic dashboard would miss, not demonstrating predictive investment performance. They remain development material and cannot become an unselected statistical holdout. Source publication, report period, subsequent event and current web observation are kept separate. Company descriptions below are business context, not a licensed sector-classification crosswalk.

Each case separates an attributed observation from an original mechanism hypothesis and its falsifier. Public source URLs do not prove native immutable retention, production rights, issuer/security binding or automated historical coverage.

## 2. Eleven business cases

### B01 — Sage: finance-workflow software and overlapping recurring views

**Observed.** The nine-month update for the period ended 30 June 2026 presents underlying and organic revenue, recurring revenue, subscription revenue, Business Cloud and cloud-native revenue as different reporting views. Cloud-native is within Business Cloud; the other views must not be assumed to be additive. The report describes growth in products including Intacct. Exact publication day was not established in this read [S01].

**Research mechanism.** Distinguish new paid organizations, migration of an installed base, additional workflows, realized pricing and churn. A cloud migration can change revenue timing and gross margin without being a new customer. The customer budget is the finance/operations job, not a generic enterprise AI budget.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** A migration thesis weakens when new recurring economics do not compensate for displaced licenses, implementation cost or concessions. Require a definition-aware recurring-revenue panel and cohort bridge where disclosed; otherwise show separate reported measures rather than manufacture a reconciliation.

### B02 — Descartes: network software is not a freight carrier

**Observed.** The 10 September 2026 release covers the quarter ended 31 July. It identifies logistics-network software and separates services revenue from professional services and other revenue. The same release reports acquisitions of TAI on 21 August and Extensiv on 1 September—after the reported quarter [S02].

**Research mechanism.** A logistics application's charging units may be transactions, connections, subscriptions or implementation, whereas its customers' freight spend is a different quantity. Research changes in shipment activity, customer adoption, network participation and paid functionality without treating all customer freight value as vendor revenue.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** Subsequent acquisitions cannot explain the completed quarter as though they had already been consolidated. Preserve transaction-effective dates and operating periods. The word “services” cannot select a labor-utilization model without the issuer's revenue definition.

### B03 — Nemetschek: construction software, unchanged organic guidance and acquired growth

**Observed.** Its 30 July 2026 release states that HCSS legally closed on 1 July and that the prior organic outlook remained unchanged. The expanded annual outlook includes an estimated acquisition contribution of about 600 basis points to currency-adjusted group revenue growth and acquisition-related margin dilution. The group reports distinct Design, Build, Manage and Media businesses [S03].

**Research mechanism.** Investigate project design seats, construction execution, recurring subscriptions and media workloads separately. An acquisition can widen distribution and the addressable workflow, but purchased growth is not evidence of a stronger organic customer cycle.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** A raw higher annual growth forecast is not an organic acceleration signal. Display acquisition perimeter, first consolidation date and the unchanged organic expectation beside the enlarged group forecast. Preserve purchase-accounting, integration and financing effects rather than adding a generic acquisition premium.

### B04 — Yubico: physical products with user-based subscription economics

**Observed.** The August 2026 interim report separates net sales, bookings, subscription revenue and ARR. Official purchasing documentation distinguishes outright hardware from end-user subscription arrangements, including tier and replacement/backup rules, with a January 2026 policy change and treatment of existing contracts [S04, S05].

**Research mechanism.** Units shipped, people covered, entitlement renewals, backup devices and replacements need not move one-for-one. Security adoption can increase the installed device base while the revenue bridge depends on the purchase model and recognition period.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** A replaced device is not necessarily a new paid subscriber. A subscription label does not turn the whole company into software. The dossier must carry product, entitlement, charging unit and period before selecting a growth or margin comparison. Current catalog terms are not actual enterprise contract prices.

### B05 — Kingdee: measurable but issuer-defined AI revenue

**Observed.** The 11 August 2026 issuer announcement for the six months ended 30 June reports approximately RMB3.625 billion total revenue, RMB296 million of source-defined AI-native product revenue, cloud revenue, subscription revenue and an annualized subscription ARR measure. It names the included AI-native product combination [S06].

**Research mechanism.** The correct choice is neither “all AI exposure is unknowable” nor “all cloud revenue is AI revenue.” A reported product-family numerator can support a scoped materiality calculation, while paid conversion, margin contribution, customer adoption and comparability remain separate questions.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** Changes to the included product family can invalidate growth comparisons. AI-native, cloud and subscription views cannot be added without an explicit partition. Preserve the original-language metric definition and unit scale; a translated short label must not erase the measurement boundary. The indexed issuer statement is research evidence, not a retained native source package.

### B06 — Advantech: industrial edge systems and regional order ratios

**Observed.** The issuer's account of its 5 August 2026 investor conference reports first-half results, a quarterly aggregate book-to-bill ratio and different ratios for North America, Europe and China. It describes an integrated industrial software/system strategy [S07].

**Research mechanism.** Customer qualification, embedded design cycles, integration, software attachment and replacement lifetimes can matter more than consumer-device shipment counts. Semiconductor dependencies remain inputs to this system-level research, not a transfer of chip research ownership.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** Regional book-to-bill ratios are not regional revenue shares, unit capacity or proven delivery lead times. A region with a higher ratio is not automatically the largest exposure. Keep numerator/denominator definitions, duration and cancellation risk visible before interpreting a demand imbalance.

### B07 — Logitech: ordinary peripherals can face binding upstream constraints

**Observed.** The 28 July 2026 release identifies USD61 million of tariff refunds within reported margin and profit measures. It separately describes an incident at an unnamed semiconductor supplier and estimates different revenue effects for subsequent quarters. The release provides product categories, including peripherals, gaming and video collaboration [S08].

**Research mechanism.** Separate replacement demand, channel behavior, product mix and temporary accounting/economic benefits. A supplier disruption can restrict conversion of demand into sales even when brand/product demand is intact. Investigate substitution qualification and mitigation rather than infer a sector-wide shortage.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** The supplier is unnamed: no guessed supplier edge, location or chip technology may be created. Management's estimated future lost sales are not realized losses. A benefit that remains in non-GAAP results is not automatically recurring merely because the label says non-GAAP.

### B08 — Samsung SDS: cloud inside IT services alongside logistics

**Observed.** The 30 July 2026 preliminary results separate IT services and logistics revenue, with cloud revenue inside IT services. Their reported KRW-billion values are 1,762.5, 1,955.3 and 779.4 respectively; group revenue is 3,717.8. The release discusses data-center projects and future capacity objectives [S09].

**Research mechanism.** Cloud operations, implementation work, customer data-center projects and logistics execution have different revenue, capital and risk units. A common corporate platform does not make them a single SaaS economic model.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** Cloud cannot be added again to IT services when calculating group revenue. Customer-project capacity is not automatically owned, available cloud capacity. Preserve Samsung SDS's own entity binding rather than join to Samsung Electronics on a similar name.

### B09 — Trend Micro: AI-related costs can outrun the sales plan

**Observed.** The 13 August 2026 release reports Vision One platform ARR growth separately from overall ARR. In its annual-outlook discussion, management says sales are near the initial plan while cloud-related expenses, including AI token costs, are above the original projection, contributing to a revised earnings outlook [S10].

**Research mechanism.** Platform adoption must be connected to paid revenue and cost to serve. A feature that improves customer usefulness can reduce near-term retained economics when inference, reliability or other delivery costs exceed the amount monetized.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** Do not label platform ARR growth as equivalent total-company growth or use it to erase the cost warning. Attribute the explanation to management; do not infer an independently verified token-cost model, undisclosed cost per user or a causal stock-price forecast. This is a real disclosure case for the earlier efficiency/cost scenarios, not proof of their general predictive validity.

### B10 — Bentley: an engineering-software ARR definition is not a generic SaaS constant

**Observed.** Its 6 August 2026 release describes ARR using contracted recurring amounts and an annualization of recent consumption for relevant shorter arrangements. Its recurring category can include particular auto-renewing fixed-fee professional-services contracts. The constant-currency ARR business-performance measure treats platform and programmatic acquisitions differently [S11].

**Research mechanism.** Infrastructure engineering can involve recurring seats, consumption, project complexity and service obligations together. Research backlog of customer projects, paid utilization, renewal and execution costs without substituting national construction spending for the software vendor's own demand.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** A metric described as business performance is not automatically a universal acquisition-free organic series. A comparison with another issuer's ARR requires definition, acquisition, annualization and service-scope compatibility—or a visibly non-comparable display.

### B11 — E Ink: electronic-paper retail applications are a non-AI hardware witness

**Observed.** An official April 2025 explanation discusses electronic shelf labels, remote price updates and electronic-paper display characteristics. It is a product/application source, not a 2026 financial or deployment series [S12].

**Research mechanism.** The investment question is whether customer savings and operating benefits justify rollout, what display/module content the supplier captures, and what replacement, installation and qualification costs remain. Link retailer workflow to paid displays and retained supplier economics only when evidence supports each step.

**Falsifier and design consequence.** A catalog application does not prove a customer contract, installed base or revenue share. Driver semiconductors remain an upstream dependency. The source's age should be shown without declaring the physical capability newly discovered or treating an old application description as current adoption momentum.

## 3. Design implications across the full sector

These cases suggest five refinements to the integrated design.

First, **economic archetypes attach to businesses and products**, not mechanically to a sector label or a revenue-line word. Services can mean hosted software, fixed-fee work, continuing operations or physical logistics. Hardware can carry user subscriptions. ARR can include different contractual and consumption observations.

Second, **growth decompositions must keep overlapping views distinct**. Business Cloud, cloud-native, recurring, subscription and AI-defined product revenue answer different questions. Ratios and concentrations need explicit denominators. A customer-region ratio must not become a geographic exposure weight.

Third, **ownership and report timing are part of the comparison**. Subsequent acquisitions, first consolidation, organic versus group guidance, changed contract terms and reporting definitions need separate dates and states. A press release is not a single timeless truth object.

Fourth, **cost, capacity and monetization deserve equal visibility with adoption**. A capacity constraint can limit an ordinary peripheral. A software platform can grow while costs exceed plan. Neither case warrants an automatic positive or negative market verdict.

Fifth, **coverage should broaden by missing mechanisms, not ticker count**. The next useful issuer is the one that challenges an unsupported assumption: a different charging model, geography, reporting convention, product lifecycle or source type. An additional large SaaS example should not substitute for an uncovered physical/vertical workflow.

## 4. What breadth is and is not closed

All fifteen R1 families now have representative economic mechanisms in R1–R6. That means the written design need not assume one software or AI-hardware model for the whole sector. It does not establish exhaustive company coverage, formal classification completeness, paid-source rights, deep historical metrics or regional parity.

The rollout should use the existing governed issuer universe and explicit coverage denominators. Each family records which businesses have source-backed roles; which also have quantitative economics; which have comparable history; and which have only qualitative context. These are independent coverage dimensions, not one completion percentage. A family remains visible when its measured population is zero.

Outstanding depth includes regional small/mid-sized businesses, vertical applications, telecom and storage configurations, electronic components, non-US disclosure retention, privately held ecosystem participants, and actual customer pricing or qualified-output time series. Address these through existing research/source intake after the first useful vertical, with named unanswered questions. Do not require a global census before making the admitted subset useful; do not label that subset the whole Technology sector.

## 5. A correction to the interpretation of the R5 evaluation question

The remaining-year guidance bridge is a deterministic function of its input guidance and actuals. Given the complete same input vector and definitions, it supplies no additional information in the mathematical sense. It may improve interpretation, task performance, or a restricted model's representation, but it cannot be counted as a new independent evidence source.

This refines R5B H1, not its underlying arithmetic. Compare a restricted baseline, the same raw inputs in a flexible baseline, and the engineered representation. Freeze comparable populations, model capacity and tuning. Any empirical improvement must be described as a representation or modeling result under that comparison, not newly acquired fundamental information. The updated company guidance remains in the baseline. Accuracy at explaining a headline is a different acceptance question from prediction or returns.
