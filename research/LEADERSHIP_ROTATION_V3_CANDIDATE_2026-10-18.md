## 2. H1 candidate — detect real transitions earlier than an *independent* arm

**Old structural impossibility:** A1 versus A2 is effectively comparing an alarm with a five-session lagged version of itself. That test has no room to reject a null quantile equal to its own margin.

**Candidate estimand H1-V3:** compare A1 with incumbent already-frozen **B2** trailing-63-session momentum crossing (not A2). Use the same zig-zag onsets (amplitude .08 log units, min 40-session up-phase) solely as retrospective *evaluation* labels, never prospective inputs. For each qualifying onset `T` and each arm, take the first kept `MATCHED` alarm under existing `leadership_rotation_episodes.classify_alarms`. Set `d_arm(T)=t-T` if matched in [T,T+40], else **41** (right-censored miss penalty). Report the per-group common-onset statistic

`D_H1 = mean_T[d_B2(T)-d_A1(T)]` measured in NYSE sessions, **including every missed onset** in both denominators.

The `D_H1 >= 5` practical hurdle is not automatically true: B2 can beat A1, both can miss, and delays vary independently. Retain H1's other arm-comparison safety clauses:
- A1 detection rate no more than 5 percentage points below B2; A1 unmatched-alarm burden per pair-year no more than B2 +0.5.
- Against suppressed A0, A1 reduces *unmatched-alarm burden* by ≥25%, and detects no more than 10 pp fewer qualifying onsets. Print A0 raw daily-alarm burden separately.
- All late, repeated, false-start, superseded and censored episodes count under the existing first-match precedence; do not cherry-pick the events both arms found.

The **statistic is a detection/efficiency contrast**, not a tradable excess-return forecast. P3 may support only that narrower statement. A1/A2 5-session delay, A0 raw stream and B2 relative delay remain separate descriptive diagnostics; no one retroactively relabels V1's invalid H1 as supported.

## 3. H2 candidate — positive future-only advantage against risk-set comparators

Old `mean(Y20 after A1)-max(mean(Y20 after all B1 days),mean(Y20 after B2))` was undersized for the positive-control regime at the fixed +.0050 economic margin; the unconditional B1 calendar exposure and B2's differing alarm opportunities are not equivalent samples. This is a question of **estimand**, not evidence that the margin should be weakened.

**Candidate H2-V3 test:** for each A1 onset `t`, form a risk set on the **same registered pair** with B1 control origins `c ∈ [t−63, t−21]` and an available 252-session warm-up. The entire control's `Y20` outcome matures before `t`; nothing about the A1 outcome at `t+20` enters control selection. Require its trailing 63-session realized-volatility quintile, **computed at c**, to equal the A1 quintile computed through `t`; exclude controls with an active A1 state *at c*. Choose the most recent eligible `c`, tie-break by earliest original row index (never by realized future return). Require a contemporaneously observable formation-date/volatility receipt for each selected control. B2's already-frozen raw onset path is computed separately at each B2's own information date; B2 does not borrow the A1's future outcome. No retrospective best-outcome matching or after-`t` control can enter the risk set.

The proposed pair/group statistic is the *higher* of B1-matched and B2 genuine standalone baseline means subtracted from A1's mean `Y20`, with full future-only outcome states, separate honest-N, and the baseline reselected in every bootstrap replicate. ## 4. H3 candidate — distinguish two-leg transfer from a common rising tide

Old A1 pair-return versus B3 receiver-only residual `Y20` had no detectable positive effect under G2. **Important generator correction:** G2 already applies drift *symmetrically to two physical legs* (`r_num=β_num·m+u/2+v`, `r_den=β_den·m−u/2+v`, with drift inside `u` in `scripts/leadership_rotation_p0.py:161–210`). It is incorrect to claim G2 lacked two-leg movement. What G2 lacks is a separately planted **donor-weakness effect-modification contrast** that can distinguish pair structure from receiver-only B3. A new candidate must test this contrast, not merely rename G2's existing ratio drift as migration.

**Candidate effect modifier, known at t:** `donor_weak_t := (log(P_donor,t/P_SPY,t) - log(P_donor,t-20/P_SPY,t-20)) < 0`. Equal price basis and both-leg source timestamps required. C2 both-up/both-down/mixed classifications remain printed, and missing donor/receiver bars are SOURCE_INVALID or UNMEASURED, not donor_weak=false.

For each predeclared donor_weak stratum, compare the subsequent **future-only SPY-beta-residualized 20-session pair return** `Y20^res` between original A1 onset events and frozen B3 receiver-only entries. For an A1 onset at `t`, eligible B3 control onset `c` must be for the same registered pair, with `c ∈ [t−63,t−21]`, and carry the same receiver's trailing 20-day SPY-relative momentum sign and 63-session volatility quintile **as they were known at c**, not inferred after t. Donor weakness is classified at *each event's own origin*. Choose most recent eligible B3 onset using timestamps alone, never its matured residual return, and record missing strata and controls without imputation. This is an observational *historical matched comparison*, not an executable synchronized pair trade. Then evaluate the **difference in uplift by donor-weakness**:

`D_H3 = [mean(Y20^res|A1, weak)-mean(Y20^res|B3, weak)] - [mean(Y20^res|A1, not_weak)-mean(Y20^res|B3, not_weak)]`.

Margin remains **+0.0025 log units**, with clustering and Holm. If any of the four cells has no measured/matured support or the matched reference is absent, it is NOT_EXECUTABLE (not an extrapolated zero). This asks a **new effect-modification question**: does pair structure improve subsequent relative movement *specifically when a donor was already weak?* It is still observational, not proof of capital flows or causality.

**Necessary negative control:** a common-factor/both-up model with the same *pair-ratio return law, volatility and planted regime dates* but **no conditional donor-weakness effect** must not appear powered for the two-leg-specific claim. **Positive control:** a separately declared G3 effect-modifier generator in which *past-observable* donor SPY-relative weakness occurs before the formation alarm and the **subsequent** receiver–donor residual difference has a planted conditional advantage, while unconditional ratio-drift magnitudes and event probabilities are explicitly matched to G2. Crucially, simply splitting `u` +/− across the receiver/donor—as G2 already does—is **not** a novel positive control. Match null/G3 risk sets on information available at the original alarm date; expose/score only the unchanged frozen A1/B3 arms; never let the planted regime or future outcome be a feature. Document calibration of ratio-drift, volatility, donor/receiver residual correlations and missingness before frozen trials. G3 is a candidate until independently specified and accepted; no synthetic generator establishes actual investor fund flows or prospective alpha.
