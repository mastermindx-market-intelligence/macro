## 1. Outcome and completion ruler

Build one evidence-driven system that discovers, tests, compares and operates diversified systematic strategy sleeves through the EXISTING research, risk, portfolio and publication owners. Macro Dashboard is the research/decision surface; Mastermind remains the downstream accounting/execution owner; Terminal consumes the same qualified research contract rather than another trading engine.

The economic north star is persistent positive net contribution at a stated capital scale and risk budget. No return, win-rate, Sharpe or Medallion-imitation performance is promised. **Operational correctness, economic implementability and demonstrated investment value are three separate verdicts.** A correct release may conclude that no candidate has earned capital.

The full build mandate remains active; the first strict research slice is not the parent outcome. The mandate does not implicitly activate live brokerage orders, capital movement, new subscriptions, unrestricted data redistribution or overrides of active portfolio-program holds.

### Human journey

Open Systematic Strategies -> understand the current research stance -> inspect a candidate's mechanism/cost/evidence -> distinguish historical, pre-outcome and paper records -> see incremental portfolio contribution or why it is blocked -> trace the same decision to its actual outcome.

### Machine journey

Qualified owner snapshot -> causal features -> registered trial -> chronological evaluation -> eligible forecast or abstention -> separately admitted isolated shadow/paper path -> reconciled outcome -> existing evidence owner -> bounded product projection.

### Parent DONE_WHEN

1. Selected inputs have rights, point-in-time limitations, availability clocks, action/basis conventions and reproducible versions.
2. Complete trial history and candidate discovery use existing research/evaluation owners; no hidden winner selection or duplicate registry.
3. Timing/accounting invariants, realistic costs, chronological separation, dependence-aware uncertainty and comparator parity are tested.
4. Forecasts and abstentions are verifiably persisted before outcomes; backfills cannot masquerade as prospective evidence.
5. PBR-compatible isolated shadow/paper economics reconcile shares, cash, income, actions and expenses without modifying live histories or cohort markers.
6. Macro/Terminal consumers show freshness, limitations, evidence maturity and attribution correctly, with actual desktop/mobile and authenticated-path proof.
7. Existing schedulers run the accepted pipeline with tested restart, duplicate-run handling, bounded failure, rollback and next-day return.
8. Capital-bearing promotion is separately approved for an exact strategy/data/risk/execution generation. Investment value requires observed evidence, not build completion.

## 4. Research portfolio and sequencing

Every family is a hypothesis, not a disclosed Renaissance recipe. Each gets a mechanism, falsifier, input contract, comparator, trial history and measured marginal portfolio contribution. Differently named correlated indicators do not create independent risk budgets.

| Family | Mechanism / horizon | Required evidence | Main falsifier / treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trend baseline | Sustained own-price movement; weeks/months | Qualified total-return bars, calendar, cash convention | No benefit against passive/risk-matched controls. First transparent comparator. |
| Pullback timing | Temporary dislocation within a trend; sessions | OHLC, events, executable opens | Gap/cost correction removes effect. Overlay, not presumed standalone alpha. |
| Residual reversion | Move unexplained by market/sector; days | PIT exposures/universe, events, borrow for shorts | Structural repricing or turnover consumes edge. |
| Relative-value baskets | Economic linkage and temporary divergence | Train-only hedge ratios, funding, synchronized marks | Relationship breaks or apparent convergence is untradeable. |
| Cross-sectional momentum | Relative persistence; weeks/months | PIT membership/delistings, neutralization, costs | Selection bias, concentration or beta explains result. |
| Macro response | Post-release transmission; later hours/days | Release/receipt clocks, vintages, lawful historical expectations | Revised values or hindsight expectations create effect. |
| Earnings/revision drift | Information diffusion; days/weeks | First publication times, lawful estimates, actions | LLM memory contamination or unrealistic first fill. |
| Carry/term structure | Compensation for exposure | Contract rolls, collateral, financing, FX | Hidden crash/short-volatility risk. Deferred. |
| Liquidity/rebalance | Noninformational flow | Timestamped flow/auction rights, capacity | Unobservable in time or execution competition erases benefit. Deferred. |
| Risk overlays | Improve loss profile/capital use | Risk-matched controls and stress regimes | Reduced beta marketed as new alpha. Risk result only. |

## 5. Data architecture and feeds

### Bitemporal observation contract

Preserve instrument/venue/currency; session/observation time; source release time when known; actual received/available time; vintage; adjustment basis; immutable source reference; correction lineage; quality; and storage/computation/redistribution rights. Use existing identity and Availability owners.

Every consumed observation must satisfy `available_at <= decision_cutoff`; session date alone is insufficient. Unknown availability remains unknown. Current adjusted history may support a labelled reconstruction, never a historical knowability claim. Freshness uses the expected completed exchange session, not calendar-day age.

### Feed sequence

1. Existing daily OHLC + capture receipts: qualify opens, volume, currency, actions and basis per cohort. No fake opens or synthetic records in real evidence.
2. Existing macro ingestion: bind vintage-aware reads where available. ALFRED's historical-date vintages are not low-latency arrival proof.
3. Existing event/filing and sector/identity producers: retain source timing and rights; no implicit consensus purchase or unsupported scrape.
4. Optional borrow/spread/depth/futures/intraday inputs stay unavailable until their existing entitlements and quality are verified. Block only dependent strategies.
5. Record storage, derived-use and redistribution rights separately. Technical access is not a license. No new paid acquisition in initial waves.

### Deterministic quality gates

Column/schema checks; chronological unique sessions; no future timestamps; finite positive prices; OHLC inequalities; zero/missing volume distinction; action/basis consistency; exchange holidays/incomplete bars; stale instruments; symbol changes; duplicates; cross-source disagreement; source hashes. Never forward-fill executable prices through suspensions or closures. Retain batch and per-instrument reasons.

### Strict economic simulation

The new helper remains inside `engine/validation.py`, not a second portfolio ledger. Existing holdings retain overnight risk until the executable open; a new post-close entrant does not receive that earlier gap. Track shares/cash or an explicitly equivalent model; charge initial buys and sales; preserve nonnegative cash and unleveraged bounds; disclose terminal marking/unliquidated exposure.

Declare dividend/split treatment, total-return basis, cash yield, fees/taxes, spread, delay, borrow/funding and impact. Included versus absent components are explicit. Missing a component blocks claims requiring it. Capacity is never certified from a flat-cost calculation.

## 6. Experiment and evaluation architecture

### Protocol before evaluation

Use actual TrialLedger contracts and compatible PBR protocols after implementation inspection. Record hypothesis/falsifier; complete tried-variant family; immutable data/code/config; universe and historical eligibility; feature/label horizon; decision/fill timing; train/validation/test windows; purge/embargo rationale; benchmark; economic minimum; uncertainty method; stopping rule; bounded compute; and output refs.

Changing a filter, parameter, metric, comparator, universe or inspected holdout creates another trial/amendment. Failed and abandoned variants remain counted. AI cannot erase trials, call a reused holdout untouched or manufacture expected returns.

### Evidence ladder

- E0 mechanics: synthetic adversarial tests; no performance claim.
- E1 reconstruction: real historical inputs, explicitly bounded knowability; no prospective claim.
- E2 frozen chronological study: train-only transforms, overlapping-label purges, justified embargo, complete search history.
- E3 robustness: cost/delay/capacity stress, parameter neighborhoods, subperiods, concentration, joint date-block resampling, negative controls.
- E4 prospective observation: persist predictions and abstentions before outcomes; retain late/missing/corrected data and exclusions.
- E5 isolated shadow/paper: identical qualified economics for incumbent/challenger; simulator generations remain separate.
- E6 capital decision: existing authority approves exact model/data/risk generation; a historical statistic cannot promote itself.

### Metrics and comparators

Predeclare one primary economic objective for each job: e.g. net active return for selection, loss-profile improvement for an overlay, or incremental portfolio utility after costs. Report gross/net, turnover, exposure, cost attribution, drawdown/tail loss, concentration, uncertainty, effective independent date clusters and failure regimes. Sharpe/DSR are not probabilities of future profit. Statistical corrections cannot repair leakage or impossible fills.

Compare to declared cash carry, passive/equal-weight same-universe exposure, risk-matched baseline and incumbent policy. Match horizon, capital, missingness, costs and eligible instruments. Correcting an incumbent simulator error is not policy alpha. No universal Sharpe/trade-count/duration threshold: declare meaningful effect and dependence-aware precision before outcomes, then report insufficient evidence honestly.
