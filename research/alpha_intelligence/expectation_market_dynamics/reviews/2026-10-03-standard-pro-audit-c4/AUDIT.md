## 4. Findings that must change the plan

### A01 — Critical: the exact next action duplicates an active incumbent

**Report locations:** executive summary, capability census, exact-next-action section, limitations. The recommendation to begin SRC-A1 natural-wrap closeout omits #8312 and #8309, despite those records predating the report's stated final source observation. A main-only census cannot establish absence of active implementations.

**Correction:** treat #8312 as the source-acceptance carrier; treat #8309 as current continuation/effect context; retrieve the reported EXP-1 child's actual paths, owner, source and review. Reuse these before producing new repair work. A later change to #8064's collector must invalidate the relevant version-bound proof, not erase historical acceptance. [I02–I05]

### A02 — Critical: schema richness was mistaken for usable economic data

**Report locations:** source ledger, typed interface, minimum vertical. The collector has columns for identity, units, currency and basis, but the current observed rows do not populate those facts. A count of valid floating-point values does not make revenue comparable across issuers or EPS comparable across currencies, accounting bases or share conventions.

**Correction:** separate four gates: physical capture integrity; declared-capture diagnostic comparability; economic/identity comparability; purpose-specific rights/publication. Unknown facts remain unknown. A current identity lookup cannot silently backfill historical identity. A provider-native two-cutoff diagnostic may be valid at a weaker scope than a canonical security product; its receipt must say so. [V01; I03–I04]

### A03 — Major: the proposed initial product seam conflates Finance with general financial intelligence

The inspected Finance Intelligence program is a **financial-sector** dossier, not a ready universal issuer financial-distribution service. Its current source explicitly leaves its read URL disconnected. Its four-node information grammar is reusable design inspiration; it is not proof of a production-ready K3E landing zone.

**Correction:** first extend the actual security-level Terminal Company Intelligence/Financials owner or another owner-approved existing security surface. Keep the Finance sector dossier as a later consumer and preserve its separate foundation gates. Never replace either product. [I08–I09]

### A04 — Major: contributor science exceeds the observed grain

An aggregate average, range and covering count do not identify who revised, how old each contributor forecast is, a matched-contributor mean, individual refresh latency, analyst skill, or analyst-level diffusion. Provider snapshot recency is not analyst freshness. Changes in consensus can reflect entrants/exits or changed composition.

**Correction:** v1 emits provider-observed consensus changes at stable metric/period identity. Contributor breadth, age weights, skill and arrival distributions require contributor IDs and vintages with rights and point-in-time qualification. Label provider-reported reviser counts by their exact windows; do not sum nested 30/90-day windows or equate them to covering analysts. [I06–I07; E09]

### A05 — Major: cadence and public availability limit the estimands

The source observation clock can support what this system captured, not what every investor knew earlier. A changed value between snapshots usually localizes the change to an interval; it does not reveal its exact publication minute. Missing collection attempts are not flat expectations.

**Correction:** distinguish capture-cutoff replay from public-information replay; use interval-censored revision arrival where appropriate. No sub-cadence lead/lag, exact event reaction or arbitrary pre-capture history from these rows. Do not resurrect unavailable 2012–2026 history with today's snapshots. [I06–I07; V01]

### A06 — Critical mathematical gap: a scalar-price condition number is not identification

For p free assumptions and one price, the local Jacobian has shape 1×p and rank at most one. A wide matrix can have a benign ratio of its nonzero singular values while retaining a large nullspace. For the illustrative model V(g,m)=g+m, J=[1,1] has one nonzero singular value and an apparent nonzero-spectrum condition ratio of one, yet (g+a,m−a) has the same price for every a.

**Correction:** report parameter dimension, number of genuinely independent observations, rank, nullspace dimension, profiles under declared locks, boundary dependence, and prior sensitivity. Distinguish identification by observations from narrowing by constraints or priors. Repeated prices are not independent equations for fixed operating assumptions unless a defensible dynamic model says they are. This is a mathematical derivation, not an empirical finding.

### A07 — Critical mathematical gap: the illustrative “28% overlap” has no defined measure

The report's example gives a percentage overlap of a fundamental/analyst distribution and an admissible joint region without defining the joint distribution, coordinate measure, tolerance or integration rule. Uniform volume changes under parameterization. Marginal forecast ranges are not a joint physical distribution. For an exact price equality in a continuous high-dimensional model, an equality manifold can have zero ambient probability even when it is economically central.

**Correction:** initially show contours and scenario membership without percentages. A later compatibility mass must name the independent reference distribution F, valuation family M and tolerance ε: Pr under F that |V_M(θ)−P|≤ε. Print sensitivity to ε, reference distribution and model. This is not the probability a stock is mispriced. Never use the price-conditioned posterior itself as the supposedly independent evidence distribution.

### A08 — Major: requirement stretch needs a set-valued definition and zero-scale safeguards

The report writes a z-like quantity using a “required region” as though it were a point. A projected growth interval can intersect the reference interval even when no jointly feasible growth/margin/reinvestment configuration overlaps. Zero or tiny variance makes a z-score unstable.

**Correction:** show driver projection intervals, reference intervals and jointly feasible scenario witnesses. Any standardized distance specifies its projection, scale and zero-scale policy. Joint distance remains a research statistic, not a fused opportunity score. No summing driver z-scores into hidden ranking.

### A09 — Major: price requirements need an accounting-consistent forward owner

PAE cannot mix enterprise value, equity value and per-share price. FCFF/WACC and FCFE/cost-of-equity are different families. Debt, cash, leases, noncontrolling interests, nonoperating assets and share identity must follow the selected family. Growth, reinvestment and return on capital cannot be independently selected without checking economic consistency. [E03]

The current `engine/valuation_scenario.py` is a fixed-family earnings-multiple scenario calculator, not a DCF or a physical distribution. It explicitly uses reported outstanding shares rather than a nonexistent diluted-share count and forbids consensus/probability claims. Its existence is a reuse boundary and a warning against invented field mappings, not evidence that it already solves PAE. [I10]

**Correction:** use an owner-approved forward-model adapter. Preserve existing formula semantics; add a cash-flow family only through that owner's explicit extension. Separate financial institutions, REITs, pre-revenue and other incompatible species. Never relabel diluted EPS as diluted shares.

### A10 — Major: “risk-neutral” labeling alone does not qualify an options surface

The report is right about Q versus P. Additional gates are necessary: European versus American exercise treatment, actual maturity/settlement, dividends/rates, quote synchronization, bid/ask quality, finite-strike coverage, no-arbitrage fit and tail extrapolation. A naive second-strike derivative of raw American single-stock call quotes is not automatically the desired density. [E01–E02; E10]

**Correction:** consume certified owner surfaces. Partial options coverage must not become a synthetic full density. Price-return uncertainty, EPS dispersion and multi-year operating-growth scenarios are different random variables and horizons; keep them side by side unless an explicit conditional mapping is independently validated.

### A11 — Major: event-implied variance is not necessarily a variance risk premium

An event component extracted from option total variance is priced event variance under the selected options model. Calling it a premium requires a corresponding physical expected-variance reference. Use total variance IV²×T with consistent day-count/maturity conventions; subtracting IV levels has the wrong object. Background variance, multiple events and event-time uncertainty must be explicit.

**Correction:** initially label `IMPLIED_EVENT_VARIANCE` or an equivalent owner type. Reserve “variance risk premium” for a matched Q-minus-physical expectation estimand. Negative residual fits or insufficient term coverage are diagnostic failures, not values to floor silently.

### A12 — Major: some cited signals need unavailable data

Pan and Poteshman's cited predictor uses **buyer-initiated opening** option volume from a distinctive dataset. ThetaData's inspected trade/quote endpoint exposes trades and contemporaneous NBBO fields; that does not by itself establish opening/closing position identity. Quote-side classification is an estimate, not the original feature. [E04; E11]

**Correction:** reproduce feature semantics before invoking a paper as evidence for a local signal. A differently observed put/call ratio is a new hypothesis with its own registration, not a replication.

### A13 — Major: the exact frozen evaluator must survive the redesign

The report explicitly calls its q≤0.05 threshold a recommendation, so this is not a fabricated claim about v1. However, its executable sequence omits several binding v1 details: BY q=0.10, 64 total trials, fixed eras, a 63-session purge/embargo, 100 overall issuer episodes, 25 per claimed subgroup, 60% coverage/case gates, and ≥5% primary-loss improvement in both validation and locked holdout for research advancement. EVAL-0 never grants product or Prophet authority. [I07]

**Correction:** preserve v1 exactly. Register PAE/options/new coupling estimands prospectively under an explicit amended/new owner-approved record before tuning or inspecting their outcomes. A stricter q alone cannot rescue an undefined family, invalid p-values or uncounted search. Historical holdout dates cannot be changed merely because current free data do not cover them. [E05]

### A14 — Major: scientific evidence was mostly supportive, not a balanced replication adjudication

The classic earnings, revision, disagreement and attention claims are broadly supported by the retrieved primary abstracts. They do not establish current local implementability or incremental alpha after costs. Absolute drift and relative drift are also different estimands: Clement et al.'s later paper reports a smaller analyst-based **drift ratio**, qualifying a simplistic “analyst surprises always incorporate more slowly” reading. [E12–E16]

The replication literature is not one-sided. Hou, Xue and Zhang report substantial failure under their standardized implementation choices; Chen and Zimmermann reproduce most clearly significant original predictors when matching original constructions. McLean and Pontiff document out-of-sample and post-publication decay. These findings can coexist: original replication, robustness under altered portfolio construction, and deployable modern net performance are different questions. [E06–E08]

**Correction:** require both faithful feature replication and realistic robustness; neither assume every candidate will fail nor treat historical significance as local alpha. The original report's title “many attractive signals should fail” is a useful warning, not a measured probability.

### A15 — Major: descriptive usefulness and predictive success need separate completion laws

A sourced expectations trajectory and explicit scenario contour can be useful without return alpha. Conversely, clean UI and source lineage do not prove a forecasting edge.

**Correction:** release the qualified descriptive product on source, semantic, UX and operational proof. Maintain predictive hypotheses in shadow with terminal PASS/FAIL/INSUFFICIENT_DATA decisions. Do not call the whole program done while core consumer paths are missing, and do not keep the descriptive product permanently blocked because an optional alpha hypothesis fails.

## 5. Retain, revise, defer, reject

**Retain:** K3E/K3-E naming; owner-native raw truth; six non-equivalent information objects; explicit uncertainty and abstention; no hidden composite; separate research and financial authority; frozen preregistration; no forced cadence or synthetic historical proof.

**Revise:** current-state census; recovery route; consumer selection; source-use qualification; cadence-aware revision estimands; PAE identification and overlap definitions; forward-model ownership; options extraction contracts; evaluation activation timing; concrete delivery and rollback tests.

**Defer until evidence exists:** individual analyst skill/age-weighted contributor consensus, physical fundamental distributions, Q-to-P transformations, causal incorporation, positioning-amplified prediction, calibrated phase probabilities and regional extrapolation.

**Reject:** universal market belief inferred from one price; fused K3E rank; fabricated analyst-level history; current-data backfill; percent overlap without a measure; a risk-neutral density presented as a physical operating forecast; an unqualified options flow paper imported as a local signal; automatic Prophet influence.

## 6. Research quality and remaining uncertainty

This is a decision to **harden and proceed**, not a rejection of expectations-based analysis. The report is strongest on ontology, non-identifiability in principle, ownership and abstention. It is weakest on current orchestration recovery, populated-data readiness, exact mathematical statistics and actionable integration dependencies.

This audit verified load-bearing primary claims and selected contrary evidence rather than repeating every professional-product survey in the original brief. It did not replicate academic results, obtain vendor sample rows or contracts, prove all adjacent runtimes, inspect held-out financial outcomes, or certify a calibrated fundamental distribution. Bloomberg, AlphaSense, ORATS, Trade Alert, SpotGamma and other named targets are not newly qualified merely by appearing in the original commission. Their omitted comparison is an explicit evidence gap, not permission to invent capabilities or delay the already-designed VEND-0 sample with another generic survey.

No numerical overall quality score is assigned: the defects have different consequences. The actionable acceptance standard is whether the corrected masterplan closes A01–A15 through explicit contracts and tests. The accompanying plan does so without erasing the original architecture or the active incumbent work.
