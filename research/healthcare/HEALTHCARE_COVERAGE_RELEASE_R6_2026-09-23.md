# Healthcare R6 — coverage, missing mechanisms and the first useful workflow

Research date: 2026-09-23. Operation: `gmi-healthcare-deep-research-20260923-sol-001`. Existing carrier: Macro draft/HOLD PR #7788. Principal-led original research and design proposal. No foundational research delegated. This is not an accepted implementation design, complete company universe, clinical recommendation, stock ranking or Fable commission.

## 1. The result and the decision still to be made

R1–R5 established a substantial selected corpus: linked Healthcare dimensions; therapeutic rights; supplier and capital economics; devices and actual use; and payment/risk transfer. The next problem is not finding an unlimited number of additional companies. It is deciding whether those observations can support a coherent user task, exposing the remaining coverage honestly, and closing the gaps that would otherwise make the first implementation misleading.

**Proposed first user task:** starting from an existing Healthcare theme, explain a material development, identify the businesses with evidenced economic participation, distinguish what actually changed from what is only inferred, inspect the strongest counterevidence, and identify the next observation that would change the thesis. Keep market participation and entry context available from their existing owners without converting this explanation into security selection.

**Candidate first deep slice:** the existing `glp1_obesity` theme, supported by the R2 therapeutic and R3 supplier research. The reason is not that it is the best investment. It is that the existing identity and research contain a useful, difficult cross-participant test: treatment demand, formulation, net realization, licensors, qualified manufacturing, capacity relief and uncertainty about retained economics. It can demonstrate the full workflow without pretending to solve every disease market first.

**Full-sector ambition remains:** a discoverable coverage map spanning the original 12 overlapping research families, progressively deepened through the same contracts and user journey. A map entry means navigation and stated coverage, not a complete dossier, accepted basket, investable security or live signal.

This is a proposal for review, not a direction to dispatch Fable or modify the shared template. A descriptive current-evidence workflow may become useful before a predictive model is validated. That does not permit historical backtests, probabilities, target prices or trading claims without their separate evidence and acceptance.

## 4. Targeted missing mechanisms

### 4.1 Portfolio replacement and the explanation of a forecast revision

AbbVie's Q2 release reports immunology sales of $8.786 billion: Skyrizi $5.505 billion, Rinvoq $2.525 billion and Humira $0.756 billion. The first two grew while Humira declined. Its annual adjusted-EPS guidance midpoint fell by $0.04 because a $0.10 operating improvement was more than offset by $0.14 of dilution attributed to a proposed acquisition. These are release-date disclosures, not proof that the transaction subsequently closed. [R6-S01]

**Research implication:** explain portfolio-level growth using both replacement and erosion. Product sales alone do not establish patient switching, competitive share, net-price changes or comparable margins. A forecast revision needs a component bridge: operating assumptions, acquisition costs, financing, share count and other adjustments. Do not interpret a lower total EPS forecast as proof that the existing business weakened.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** obtain comparable product volume/net-realization and contribution evidence, current rights/competition and the relevant financing/closing record before extrapolating. The current case supports a distinction, not a fully valued immunology basket.

### 4.2 Contractual repayment can change retained economics without changing demand

Regeneron's Q2 announcement says its Sanofi Development Balance was fully repaid at quarter end and would cease reducing collaboration revenue in Q3. It separately identifies Dupixent sales recorded by Sanofi. Its table reports $2,032.6 million of antibody-commercialization profit participation and $141.7 million of manufacturing reimbursement, totaling $2,174.3 million of Sanofi collaboration revenue. The relevant issuer text was indexed; a full-page read failed. [R6-S02]

**Research implication:** an economic right has a time-dependent payment sequence, not only a percentage. Preserve recovery of earlier development funding, cost sharing, milestones, manufacturing reimbursement and profit participation. Neither total collaboration revenue divided by partner drug sales nor the change in that ratio establishes a universal royalty rate. The described Q3 effect is a release-date expectation until a later report supplies realization.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** the complete agreement/accounting bridge, subsequent period, cost obligations and product scope. Do not assume all assets with the partner inherit the same arrangement.

### 4.3 Global access is a set of jurisdiction-specific decisions

EMA's Leqembi page scopes EU use to the specified early-Alzheimer's population, confirmed amyloid pathology and ApoE4 non-carriers or heterozygotes. It describes controlled access. The NICE project page, by contrast, displays an appraisal in progress with expected publication TBC. An earlier NICE consultation explicitly says it is not current guidance. Those records have different roles and times. [R6-S03, R6-S04, R6-S05]

**Research implication:** do not collapse regulator authorization, health-technology appraisal, funding, local service readiness and treated patients into one global approved/not-approved field. A historical negative draft cannot be silently treated as the current final decision; an in-progress project is not affirmative reimbursement. An EU record does not establish England's or another country's treatment access.

A July issuer announcement concerning a US subcutaneous initiation option supplies a separate formulation/application event and a then-planned launch date. It does not remove all diagnostic or monitoring dependencies or prove subsequent launch and adoption. [R6-S06]

**Falsifier/next evidence:** current jurisdiction-specific final guidance, exact product/application, treatment-center access and realized use. Source-status uncertainty should qualify the country claim, not erase the whole theme.

### 4.4 Revenue can be paid for availability rather than maximum volume

NHS England's published antimicrobial subscription framework describes eligible payments delinked from volumes and obligations concerning availability. The acquired guidance is historical framework evidence, not a current company award, complete contract or proof of payment. Full-page retrieval returned unusable short content; the relevant official indexed text was available. [R6-S07]

**Research implication:** a utilization model must permit availability-based and other contractual mechanisms. Lower use is not automatically a weaker supplier business when an eligible contract deliberately pays on another basis. Conversely, the existence of the framework is not a revenue entitlement for every antibiotic producer. Assign the actual agreement, counterparty, eligibility, term, obligations and evidence of performance before estimating retained income.

This is a descriptive economic distinction, not an assessment of whether a public policy is desirable or successful. The missing current award and contract remain explicit.

### 4.5 Product duration, commercial rights, regulatory review and launch are separate

Organon's September 14 announcement describes completed global licensing of MIUDELLA, a current three-year US indication, acceptance for review of a requested six-year duration, and anticipated availability later in 2026. Review acceptance is not the requested approval; an anticipated launch is not recognized revenue. [R6-S08]

**Research implication:** duration changes may alter replacement-unit demand, patient-time served, training, net price and provider visits differently. No linear revenue reduction or increase follows from the duration alone. The commercial rights holder need not be the original applicant or inventor. This case complements, rather than duplicates, R2 one-time/chronic-therapy and R4 long-wear examples.

Organon separately announced that results would appear through its regular filings while earnings calls were suspended during a pending transaction. A missing call must not be mistaken for absent financial reporting or for a verified transaction closing. [R6-S09]

**Falsifier/next evidence:** current label/launch, license terms, demand and net economics; official filings and closing receipts rather than old investor-event habits.

### 4.6 Behavioral care: capacity requires a denominator and outcomes

LifeStance's Q2 disclosure reports $435.4 million revenue, approximately 2.6 million visits and 8,542 clinicians at period end. Visits grew 19% while ending clinician count grew 11%. Its center-margin and adjusted-EBITDA measures have different costs and definitions. [R6-S10]

**Research implication:** dividing quarterly visits by ending headcount does not produce a measured change in same-clinician productivity. Average full-time-equivalent capacity, start dates, credentialing, working time, no-shows, modality mix and payer terms matter. Growth caused by capacity additions, better scheduling, patient demand or coding/payment changes is not interchangeable. Operational efficiency must be considered alongside access and quality, not as a recommendation to maximize encounter counts.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** comparable clinician cohorts and available hours; paid visits and contractual realizations; labor/administrative costs; independently appropriate quality and access measures. Public headcount is not enough to establish a workforce bottleneck or a scalable software margin.

### 4.7 Rehabilitation: admissions, patient intensity, beds and cash can diverge

Encompass's Q2 release reports total discharge growth of 5.6% and same-store growth of 2.8%, alongside higher revenue per discharge. It also reports adjusted-EBITDA growth while adjusted free cash flow declined. Its capacity discussion distinguishes facilities opened from planned additions. [R6-S11]

**Research implication:** distinguish a licensed bed, physical bed, staffed bed, occupied bed-day and completed episode. Discharge volume, length of stay, case intensity and net payment can affect the same business differently. An added facility can improve future access while consuming capital and ramp expenses today. These selected rehabilitation cases do not complete hospice, skilled-nursing or long-term-care research.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** comparable capacity, staffing, utilization, patient mix, contractual rates, quality and capital/cash requirements. Do not infer margin or patient benefit solely from discharges.

### 4.8 Animal health is not a renamed human Healthcare market

Zoetis reports divergent US companion-animal and livestock results and attributes them to different customer and market conditions. Its release defines a product-level blockbuster threshold as at least $100 million in annual revenue. That issuer-specific label must not inherit a different conventional threshold. [R6-S12]

IDEXX separately reports growth in recurring companion-animal diagnostics and instrument placements. These are company-specific observations, not proof that diagnostics are immune to veterinary-visit changes or that all installed instruments generate the same recurring income. [R6-S13]

**Research implication:** record species, purchaser, country, use and economic mechanism. Household affordability, veterinary workflows, livestock-producer economics and procurement may be relevant research questions, but none establishes the other by analogy. A human therapeutic approval does not establish veterinary authorization. A private veterinary business remains a useful dependency without an invented public security.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** comparable visits/tests, net realization, active instrument usage, production-population context, cost and jurisdiction-specific product scope. Do not create a causal hedge or company ranking by comparing two issuers' headlines.

### 4.9 Research services and instruments are not a proxy for trial success

Medpace reports $795.7 million of net new business awards and $707.3 million revenue, supporting a rounded net book-to-bill ratio of 1.13. Awards, beginning backlog, ending backlog and recognized revenue are different denominators. The period also includes reimbursed out-of-pocket activity. [R6-S14]

Illumina's reported revenue growth and rest-of-world organic growth exclude different items and geography. Its organic comparison is not a measure of all worldwide sequencing demand. [R6-S15]

**Research implication:** trial count, clinical success, sponsor funding, contracted services and vendor profitability require distinct evidence. End backlog is not automatically the denominator for a reported beginning-backlog conversion rate. Orders and placements do not establish paid consumable use. A geographic or acquisition-adjusted metric cannot silently become a total-market fact.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** cancellations, funding, conversion timing, actual service mix, installed utilization, consumable intensity, net realization and customer concentration. This begins the missing CRO/tools bridge; it does not complete all research-instrument and academic-spending exposure.

### 4.10 Vaccine and pipeline economics need seasonality and negative evidence

Moderna's Q2 release describes revenue affected by country timing and long-term partnership activity. Its cost-of-sales discussion includes inventory write-downs and idle-capacity costs. Quarterly sales therefore do not directly measure annual vaccination demand or unconstrained profitable capacity. [R6-S16]

GSK reports stronger selected operating measures while also identifying a camlipixant impairment and a decision not to progress the program following trial results. Its reported and core operating profit differ by more than that named impairment alone. [R6-S17]

**Research implication:** use both positive and adverse program evidence, preserving drug-specific scope. Inventory, delivery schedules, standby obligations and impairments are not uniform current demand signals. A failed program does not invalidate every therapeutic platform, while an adjusted measure does not make the economic cost of a failed investment disappear. No general clinical efficacy conclusion is inferred here from financial reporting.

**Falsifier/next evidence:** contract/delivery schedules, appropriate trial results, actual demand and recurrence, inventory/capacity utilization and the complete financial reconciliation.

## 5. What these additions change in the common research model

R1's multiple dimensions remain preferable to a single deep tree. R6 adds no new canonical entities. It refines the proposed questions asked of each existing evidence-backed relationship:

1. **What is the relevant unit?** Product, formulation, indication, patient segment, test, procedure, instrument, service episode or availability obligation.
2. **Where and when does the statement apply?** Jurisdiction, contract territory, observation period, publication time, effective interval and supersession.
3. **Who has which role?** Applicant, licensor, commercializer, manufacturer, provider, payer, administrator, supplier or owner. Different roles may be held by one group without eliminating internal transfers.
4. **How does value transfer?** Product sales, gross/net profit participation, reimbursement, fees, reserved capacity, availability payment, milestone or other evidenced basis.
5. **What changes the amount retained?** Earlier cost recovery, shared obligations, mix, net realization, operating expense, financing and dilution.
6. **What evidence is absent?** Missing terms, denominators, access, adoption, ownership resolution or historical records are not zero and are not proof of no relationship.

These questions should lead to synthesis, not a form that users must complete manually. The page should summarize the consequence and let the user inspect the details supporting it. Extraction may suggest assertions; accepted identity and evidence owners govern what becomes a published fact. No new autonomous writer or policy engine is proposed.

## 6. First-slice alternatives and recommendation

### A. Publish the complete taxonomy first

This creates visible breadth quickly and can help navigation. It does not prove the important job: explaining a development and its economic significance. A taxonomy can also create false confidence when empty families look equivalent to researched ones. Use the coverage map as part of the product, not as the whole first release.

### B. One deep evidence-to-economic-thesis workflow, with visible full-sector coverage

This is the recommended candidate. Reuse `glp1_obesity` and the existing Theme Tracker route. Connect selected therapeutic and supplier evidence to company roles, material uncertainty and counterevidence. Include the coverage map, but make the active deep journey and the unfinished families unmistakable. The same design should be tested against at least one counter-case outside metabolic therapies before it is generalized.

Benefits: builds from existing identity and research; tests economic rights and opposing participant effects; creates a useful investor task; and supplies a repeatable extension path. Costs: source access, identity joins, accepted display contracts and visible missing-state behavior still need proof. These are not waived by selecting the candidate.

### C. A complete real-time predictive Healthcare graph and security-ranking product

The ambition includes continuous broad coverage and useful validated forecasting where justified. Building all of that before proving the descriptive journey would multiply unverified data, identity and evaluation dependencies. This is a later expansion, not rejected ambition. It requires genuinely point-in-time evidence, observable forecasts, historical/out-of-sample evaluation, appropriate data rights and approval from existing decision owners. The R6 proposal does not authorize it.

Recommendation B is a product-research choice, not approval to implement. It does not rename canonical themes or remove existing market/timing dimensions. No new price basket or company ranking is required to show a company's evidenced participation.

## 7. Candidate first-slice walkthrough

### Entry and context

The user opens `state_of_themes.html` and enters the existing `glp1_obesity` detail context. The existing theme identity, market context and navigation remain. The new research section clearly states its evidence horizon and whether it is current, stale, partial or unavailable. The full Healthcare coverage map remains accessible without pretending its children are accepted canonical themes.

### Development and meaning

The selected demonstration is not a synthetic positive headline. It uses the R2/R3 tension: treatment demand can grow while net realization, manufacturing utilization or the economics of an outside supplier weaken. Existing dated research is a prototype input, not a live refresh receipt. Before acceptance, the same story must be built from newly acquired, allowed source records through the intended production reader.

A useful explanation has five components: the observed change; the business mechanism; evidenced participants; the strongest contrary evidence; and a discriminating next observation. A conclusion may be conditional or explicitly unavailable. Avoid a single bullish/bearish Healthcare score.

### Participants and roles

Start with the evidenced Lilly/Novo commercial roles and the Chugai licensor example from R2. Their identifiers, rights and source versions must be resolved through existing owners before publication. Supplier capability can be shown as a research lead, but it cannot be upgraded into a named customer contract or measured materiality without evidence. A business can be discoverable here without becoming an approved basket constituent.

For each participating business the user should see the specific role, supporting relationship, period/jurisdiction and unknown company-wide materiality. Do not add commercializer sales and licensor participation as independent estimates of the total drug market. Do not double count different securities or parent/subsidiary exposures.

### Contradiction and revision

The explanation should distinguish true contradiction from different time, geography, product, or financial scope. A capacity-expansion plan and another supplier's spare qualified capacity can both be true. An issuer's statement remains attributed and can be outweighed by more directly relevant evidence without being silently deleted.

When a source is corrected, the current view should change and the evidence history should remain inspectable under the incumbent correction/PIT contract. A new generation date cannot silently relabel an old observation as fresh. Previously available records and subsequently discovered facts must remain distinguishable.

### Next action for the user

The user finishes with a defensible statement of what warrants further investigation and what would change the interpretation. Existing technical entry/selection owners may provide their separately labeled context. This research section must not originate a trade, alter eligibility or supply a position size. No parallel watchlist or notification service is needed; integration with an existing permitted research workflow can be designed later.

## 8. Release gates and the distinction between blockers and later depth

### Blockers for the candidate descriptive slice

- **Actual source acquisition:** the selected source bytes or permitted structured response must be acquired, attributable and recoverable according to current source-owner policy. A search excerpt alone is not the production ingestion proof.
- **Rights and serving boundary:** establish permitted storage, display and retention for each source. Public accessibility is not by itself a redistribution license. No restricted trial corpus or patient data in the public artifact path.
- **Identity and scope:** resolve the existing theme, issuer/business, instrument where relevant, product, territory and relationship through the current owners. No invented CIK, ticker, supplier contract or canonical subtheme.
- **Temporal and correction behavior:** separate event/observation/publication/ingestion clocks; preserve corrections and applicability. Show incomplete historical coverage rather than backfill hindsight as if known earlier.
- **Economic synthesis:** demonstrate a real evidence-to-economic explanation, including counterevidence and important unknowns. Sources without useful interpretation do not pass the user task.
- **Current shared contract and custody:** refresh relevant shared-template/builder/evidence interfaces and active owners before a proposed code change. The historical architecture document is not sufficient release approval.
- **Visible degraded states:** selected, stale, unavailable and genuinely contradictory evidence must remain understandable. A missing required section must not create an apparently healthy empty page.
- **Real-path proof:** an allowed real input must reach the intended page through the incumbent producer, be visible in a browser, and preserve owner dimensions. An isolated fixture or green artifact checker is insufficient.
- **Independent review and authority:** evidence/semantic review and implementation acceptance remain separate. No Fable handoff until the reviewed design and bounded plan state the missing gates and their owners.

### Depth that need not block a truthfully scoped descriptive first slice

A complete worldwide payer model; every disease and private supplier; all licensed commercial datasets; calibrated probabilities; current-consensus disagreement; target prices; and historical investment-performance claims can remain unavailable while a narrow current-evidence workflow is useful. This is not permission to claim those capabilities. Their absence must block conclusions that depend on them, and the full-sector roadmap must keep them visible.

Conversely, a missing contract or identity in the very relationship used to explain who benefits cannot be excused as later depth. The distinction is dependence: does the chosen user conclusion require the missing evidence? If yes, resolve it or abstain from that conclusion.

## 9. Acceptance questions for the eventual implementation

These are proposed application acceptance specifications, not executed tests:

- Can a user state what changed without confusing a release date with the service or observation period?
- Can the user distinguish commercial revenue, royalty, profit share, reimbursement and an unverified supplier association?
- Does a missing current-consensus baseline prevent a claim that the market is underestimating the event, without blocking the factual operating explanation?
- Does the page retain a decline or disconfirming observation rather than summarize only favorable developments?
- Can a corrected source revise the conclusion without rewriting what was known in an earlier view?
- Does a country-specific draft or in-progress appraisal remain different from final funding and actual treatment access?
- Can the reader see where full Healthcare coverage is absent rather than assume that every family has equal depth?
- Are a named development program and a current security linked only through the accepted identity/rights path?
- Can a source become stale or unavailable without the page presenting a silent all-clear?
- Do the existing market leadership and entry dimensions remain intact, separately owned and incapable of being overridden by the new narrative?

Proposed evaluation includes factual assertion review, unit/period checks, source-versus-inference distinction, completion of the intended user task, and errors under correction/missingness/cross-scope cases. Do not manufacture a universal confidence number. Use the existing evaluation owner to test a frozen acceptance set; the research examples are seeds, not proven behavior.

## 11. Scope-aware analytical models to preserve

These are original modeling requirements rather than empirical estimates:

- **Portfolio replacement:** separate product-level growth/erosion and contribution before evaluating the company. A net segment total can conceal opposite underlying movements.
- **Payment sequence:** compute retained income only after the applicable cost recovery, sharing, reimbursement and effective-date rules. An expired repayment obligation can matter without a new market share assumption.
- **Patient-time versus units:** duration changes can change unit turnover without the same change in patients served. Do not equate shipped devices, prescriptions, eligible patients and paid patient-time.
- **Capacity:** use comparable available clinician hours or staffed capacity when evaluating throughput; endpoint headcount and nominal beds are incomplete denominators.
- **Availability contract:** qualify the specific contract before linking revenue to either use or readiness. No framework-wide company entitlement.
- **Expectations:** record operating facts, a revised business assumption and a price/consensus comparison as separate layers. When the last is absent, do not invent undervaluation. Formula illustrations are not stock forecasts.

An intentionally hypothetical paid-use example starts with 100 patient-months at 100 currency units of net revenue and a 60% contribution margin. Growing patient-months 20%, reducing net realization 15%, and reducing contribution margin to 55% produces revenue of 10,200 versus 10,000, but contribution of 5,610 versus 6,000. It demonstrates that volume growth, revenue growth and retained contribution can move differently. It is not a measured price, patient cohort, company estimate or treatment recommendation.

A separate capacity example with quarterly visits and period-end clinicians should abstain from calculating a same-clinician productivity change when average FTEs and comparable hours are unavailable. An unknown result can be the correct analytical output rather than a failure to produce a number.
