Once those controls and the other admission gates pass, the first real source can be admitted by its authorized owner and the exact-object positive/negative checks repeated. Public evidence contains no credentials, protected body or sensitive object locator. This sequence avoids demanding that sensitive data be uploaded before the storage privacy gate can be evaluated.
