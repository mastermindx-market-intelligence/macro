## 1. What became concrete

The first Healthcare workflow has a viable *design path*, not a proved production path. The existing `glp1_obesity` theme and `obesity_glp1` price basket are resolved in the crosswalk. Source-backed financial and licensing examples can support a useful explanation. Existing owner contracts identify how that explanation must be admitted, retained, referenced and served. They also identify what is missing.

A material surprise emerged: the incumbent FDA shortage feed is not safe to reuse uncritically. Eleven semantic/acquisition/history behaviors were reproduced offline against exact, hash-matched production source files, alongside five controls. Those are observed behaviors of the pinned functions with synthetic inputs. They are not an observed live incident or proof that all production outputs are wrong. No repair was made.

The chosen user job remains: **from a Healthcare development, explain the economic change, identify evidenced participants, inspect counterevidence, and know what observation could change the thesis**. Keep the incumbent market, group, entry and security-selection dimensions separate. The explanatory product should not require the user to reconstruct an argument from a source drawer, nor should it manufacture a target price when current expectations are unavailable.

## 4. Minimal real evidence set and a useful explanation

The example intentionally uses a small number of discriminating sources rather than another company census.

**Lilly Q2 results.** The August 5 release reports $22,974 million of quarterly revenue and company-wide volume and realized-price effects of +60% and -13%. Reported gross-margin percentage nevertheless increased to 85.8%, with the company attributing the improvement to production cost and mix, partly offset by price. Those are reported company-level measures, not Foundayo-specific price elasticity, a supplier-margin estimate, or a security valuation. [R7-S03]

**Licensing participation.** The June 30-period filing identifies Lilly's worldwide orforglipron/Foundayo development and commercialization rights from Chugai, with tiered royalties described from mid-single digits to low teens and recognized by Lilly in cost of sales. Exact tiers and security materiality are not supplied here. The filing index establishes publication on August 5 and displays acceptance at 14:40:40; the retrieved page does not label that timestamp's time zone, so the example stores the display literally rather than inventing UTC. [R7-S01, R7-S02]

**Supply semantics.** FDA distinguishes national shortage determination from manufacturer-reported presentation availability. Resolution says demand is no longer exceeding supply; it is not a measurement of excess capacity or shareholder pricing power. [R7-S04]

### Proposed visible explanation

Treatment-related demand and a company's retained economics need not move proportionally. The reported company-level volume increase coexists with lower realized pricing; production cost and mix can offset that price pressure. A documented licensing participant has a different claim on product economics from the commercializer. A supply-status observation, when eligible and current, should be shown separately rather than used to infer a sector-wide glut.

The next discriminating observations are comparable product and geographic net realization, production cost/mix, source-specific qualified capacity and actual royalty reporting. No current consensus baseline, product-level margin or supplier contract was acquired for this example. The view must therefore abstain from an underpricing claim, a royalty-rate midpoint and a named bottleneck winner.

That paragraph is an original analytical interpretation, not a claim that the cited companies validate the proposed model. The financial release and filing are the same issuer's disclosures; they are not automatically two independent confirmations of one commercial thesis.

### Clocks to retain

For the quarter, preserve April 1–June 30 as the measurement interval, August 5 as source publication, the accession's literal acceptance timestamp and unknown timezone, the research observation date, and a later native admission/retention date only when actually received. For an undated definition page, upstream publication stays unknown. An immutable house-curation receipt would record its own publication separately. A newly written assertion cannot move the source observation or system knowledge into the past.

Current source access was selected HTML reading. A URL is not an immutable retained-source receipt, and the locally hashed original research file is not the hash of the upstream filing. Those missing native retention and admission receipts remain visible.

Five controls also reproduced: Current plus limited availability stays active; Resolved is recognized; empty frame produces unavailable; complete one-page acquisition writes its row; initial connection failure does not publish. A fix must preserve these valid behaviors rather than remove the feed indiscriminately.

The local receipt reports eleven findings and five controls, zero network calls, zero production writes, `product_repaired=false` and `live_incident_observed=false`. Several findings are related symptoms, not eleven independent root causes. The main causes are status/availability conflation, status-based coverage inference, unjustified economic wording, lack of complete-generation admission, and use of a merged latest cache without historical/absence semantics.

Do not count package rows as independent shortages, affected patients or supplier share. Do not automatically interpret disappearance as resolution: the FDA webpage has finite retention, and its retention behavior is not by itself proof of identical API behavior. A new complete snapshot can establish absence from that snapshot, not an unreported clinical or commercial event.

## 7. Written first-vertical design for review

### 7.1 Shared representation

Adopt the Robotics proposal's optional native `curation_assertion` extension rather than create a Healthcare database or overload a GMI node. Products and contract counterparties remain source-scoped descriptions until their actual identity owner supplies stronger bindings. Preserve original labels and unknown revisions. The native evidence schema, column persistence, admission, read validation and K1 subtype must evolve together under their incumbent owners.

Existing proposed financial and operating assertion types can cover the corresponding reports. A contractual royalty right is not an operating metric, and a regulatory supply status is not a clinical approval. Propose two narrowly typed additions to the *same* shared assertion vocabulary: a reported economic-right assertion and a reported supply-status assertion. Their final enum names and field contracts require shared-owner review. Do not deploy an arbitrary predicate string or silently reinterpret Robotics' development-agreement type.

An economic-right statement must retain source-native parties, asset, territory, contractual basis, costs borne where disclosed, exact/range/qualitative amount, applicability, evidence and materiality unknowns. A supply statement must retain source jurisdiction, molecule/form/presentation or exact record scope, regulator status, manufacturer availability, source-generation completeness, observation time, and any missingness. Neither automatically creates a graph edge, eligible holding or beneficiary rank.

### 7.2 Evidence and identity composition

K1 references the owner-native assertion object and preserves all admitted source, observation, curation and business-valid clocks. It does not store the statement body. Native evidence-subject blocks may be displayed as thematic research after the subtype is accepted. A CIK-to-security or theme-node-to-security declaration is not a bridge receipt. Unsupported joint security-subject recipes refuse; optional stock navigation visibly degrades. The investment narrative cannot use a refused join to import a current stock valuation.

Keep the business observation, analyst interpretation, forward hypothesis and market-price comparison as separate classes. A model may draft the interpretation with source-backed constraints; it cannot self-ratify the underlying assertion or determine economic materiality by rhetorical confidence.

### 7.3 First view and transport

Use the existing Theme Tracker to existing `basket/obesity_glp1.html` route. Follow the shared Robotics client-side paid-hydration direction, preserving the existing page builder and analysis sections. The actual endpoint/module must be the accepted common theme-research read surface, not a second Healthcare server or a copied Robotics API. Its implementation is not already proved by this document.

The detail module should show, in order: one clear economic explanation; dated supporting observations; participants and their distinct rights; the strongest counterevidence; unavailable important measures; and the next observation. A source drawer supports this argument but is not its replacement. Whole-sector family navigation remains visible with explicit research-depth labels; facets do not become new canonical themes or price baskets.

Reuse the existing signed-in/site-full access pattern and private/no-store handling. Anonymous/expired-session/rights-revoked behavior must be tested through the real path, without copying current bodies into static HTML, preloads, local persistent browser stores or public mirrors. Never place full BioCatalyst trial facts in this module's public shell. Trial-detail links must respect BioCatalyst's own entitlement and fact-only scope.

### 7.4 Missingness and correction behavior

Required missing evidence blocks its dependent conclusion; it does not require deleting unrelated valid observations. For example, missing canonical Chugai instrument resolution can suppress a stock link while retaining the attributed licensing relationship. Missing royalty tiers suppress a point earnings estimate. Missing source rights suppress the governed body. Failed FDA refresh cannot look like no shortages, and an optional unqualified supply feed must not silently corroborate the economic narrative.

If legacy shortage output appears on the same user path, owner repair or an explicit accepted supersession is needed before presenting a coherent current story. Adding a correct paragraph next to a misleading “glut” chip is not acceptance. Historical examples retain their original source scope and do not fabricate a present source state.

A source correction appends an immutable reviewed assertion revision and recompiles dependent views through the existing owner. A changed body at the same URL and publication date needs a distinct revision/locator, not accidental reuse of a dated URL identity. Upstream-source publication and house-curation publication remain separate. This design does not claim the current basic evidence schema alone implements the richer revision contract.
