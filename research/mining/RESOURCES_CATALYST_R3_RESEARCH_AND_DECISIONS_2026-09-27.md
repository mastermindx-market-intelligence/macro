## 4. Freeze the probability question before fitting it

An operating project and a dated investor claim can have different endings. We select two parallel targets:

**Project target:** first attainment of the explicitly defined physical/commercial milestone, with restart and later phases available in a richer multi-state model.

**Original-security target:** attainment while the original economic claim remains entitled to the modeled payoff. Extinguishment of that claim before delivery is a competing event. A sale settling that claim in cash or successor shares is a separate terminal settlement state; its payoff can be favorable or unfavorable. Distress, a financing delay, an exchange suspension or a mere legal filing is not automatically extinction. Label adjudication must inspect the actual rights and recovery.

The baseline is a common-entry, exact-time **Aalen–Johansen first-transition cumulative incidence**. At event time t, with prior event-free survival S, n at risk and d_j events of cause j:

`CIF_j(t) = CIF_j(t−) + S(t−) × d_j(t)/n(t)`

`S(t) = S(t−) × [1 − sum_j d_j(t)/n(t)]`.

Remove administrative censors after tied events under the stated discrete observation convention. The incidence of a specific event is not the same as its cause-specific hazard or one-minus-Kaplan–Meier with competing events treated as ordinary censoring. Austin/Lee/Fine's primary methodological paper supports this distinction; no clinical probabilities or coefficients are imported into mining.[S8]

**Invented counterexample:** four claims enter at day0. Two are extinguished at day1. The other two reach delivery at day2. Correct first-event delivery incidence is50%, with50% claim extinction. Deleting the first two records or treating those competing outcomes as innocuous loss-to-follow-up produces an apparent100% delivery result among those remaining. This is a test of semantics, not an empirical mining estimate or sufficient sample for calibration.

The pure reference refuses unknown causes, interval-valued dates, delayed-entry records, duplicate identities and invalid times. It withholds the requested-horizon value when follow-up has ended with nonzero unresolved survival. It does not assert independent censoring merely because a record says `censored`. It does not fit covariates, estimate uncertainty or authorize ranking.

## 6. Model comparison and promotion experiments

**Stage A: source and label qualification.** Independent, outcome-aware label adjudication is separated from outcome-blind feature reconstruction. Quantify enrollment coverage, duplicate episodes, delayed discoveries, unresolved legal claims and source availability. An actor who already knows the outcome cannot claim a blinded historical prediction. For LLM-based retrospective features, use source-constrained extraction with date-evidence checks and explicitly report contamination risk; only a prospective shadow run can test real-time research without that historical knowledge problem.

**Stage B: simple benchmarks.** Compare the issuer's original window, empirical stratified first-transition incidence and simple cause-specific/discrete-time baseline with the proposed history-aware hierarchical model. Do not assume a complex model wins. Financing-availability, minimum interim cash, recovery/metallurgy, schedule, retained rights and dilution features are entered as separate families with predeclared ablations. Literature gives method options, not accepted likelihoods for these mines.

**Stage C: held-out diagnosis.** Split by forward time and hold out related sponsor/project families, with overlapping event/commodity/counterparty groups kept together as appropriate. Report calibration at each horizon, calibration uncertainty, Brier/log-loss with a justified censoring treatment, event timing error, coverage/abstention and failure detection. Bootstrap dependence clusters, not individual repeated releases. Group validation and confidence interval procedures must be qualified before use; they are not implemented by the four-row reference.

**Stage D: equity and trading-independent shadow evaluation.** Through existing financial/expectations/evaluation owners, bind actual historical prices, currency, corporate actions, cash distributions, dilution, liquidity and costs. Compare same-horizon net total return to declared benchmark and commodity-factor baselines. Fit commodity exposures from pre-event data, not the post-event rally. Report event reaction and sustained rerating separately; event overlap or structural breaks can make attribution unavailable. An absolute winner during a metal rally need not be event-specific alpha.

**Options ablations:** measure separately whether qualified synchronized flow/IV/skew/term structure adds information about (a) the milestone outcome, (b) market expectations, (c) immediate response, or (d) entry/crowding. Use the existing #7298 flow truth/package/OI lineage. No reconstructed dealer position without explicit signed-inventory and shock assumptions. Raw off-exchange prints are not informed-buyer evidence. Selection/coverage effects must be reported so rich options coverage does not win by excluding small companies.

**Promotion criterion:** set sample/cluster adequacy from the actual observed dependence, calibration precision and detection-power requirement, not a universal minimum count. Freeze numerical acceptance tolerances before looking at the prospective outcomes. Candidate models must outperform appropriate baselines on the chosen metrics without unacceptable tail, coverage or subgroup degradation; the existing evaluation/recommendation decision owners approve the exact thresholds and live scope. Design/reference readiness, forecast qualification and production acceptance remain separate decisions.
