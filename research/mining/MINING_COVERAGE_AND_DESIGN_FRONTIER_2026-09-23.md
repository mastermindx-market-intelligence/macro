## 1. The decision this matrix is intended to support

The next useful step is no longer an unrestricted search for more mining company names. The corpus contains representative tests of physical quantities, economic rights, reporting bases, capital requirements and expectations. The remaining task is to identify where a materially different business mechanism is missing, where an individual claim must remain unknown, and where a true design or release dependency still exists.

The ambition remains an investor moving from a theme into an asset, product, contract and company and understanding why a development could change cash generation per share. The corresponding machine job is to preserve identity, period, specification, ownership, uncertainty and correction history well enough to support that explanation. Neither a source count nor a long taxonomy proves that the job is achievable.

This is a qualitative coverage assessment, not a numerical completeness score. A case study can establish a discriminating test without establishing global coverage. A domain with several researched issuers can still lack the input most important to its valuation. Conversely, one unresolved issuer reconciliation need not prevent a useful, accurately limited dossier.

## 4. Three kinds of unfinished work must remain distinct

### G1 — Claim-specific unknowns

Examples include an actual stream threshold balance, a minor-metal recovery rate, a customer's accepted quantity, or a residual between simplified and reported production. These gaps stop the affected calculation or strength of conclusion. They do not automatically stop a descriptive, correctly dated dossier.

The required behavior is to keep the source facts, state the precise missing input, and explain what would resolve it. Conditional arithmetic must remain conditional. A narrative should not imply a numerical conclusion that its calculator correctly refuses to produce. Equally, an unknown number should not erase a well-supported positive commercial milestone.

### G2 — Missing economic regimes

P07 and P08 supply representative aluminium/energy and nickel/cobalt/anode contrasts. They test enough different economic mechanisms to prepare a bounded first-dossier design without pretending the entire Mining universe is mapped. Further unrestricted issuer collection is not the next dependency.

Primary PGM operating/demand detail, selected manganese/ferroalloy and steel-route substitution, thermal/industrial-coal economics and long-tail specialties remain thinner. For the first vertical, mark them explicitly outside its admitted claim set rather than silently importing a neighboring commodity's assumptions. Keep their future research obligation in the expanded taxonomy; this scoping does not cancel the full-sector ambition.

Add another domain case before design only when it could invalidate the selected user job, native representation or a claim the first slice actually promises. Geographic breadth, global cost curves and full proprietary contract coverage are expansion work unless required by that claim. Unknown local values continue under G1; native release requirements remain G3.

### G3 — True design and release dependencies

Research completeness does not confer source rights, accepted native storage, shared-path custody, or production proof. Before implementation, inspect current GMI identity and assertion/correction contracts, the shared Themes template, and F04's actual authenticated/public publication boundaries. Determine how each source may be stored, derived and displayed. A public webpage does not by itself authorize bulk redistribution or a paid-data mirror.

The written design must identify the existing consumer, data flow, correction behavior and failure states. The implementation plan must resolve actual source collisions and define independent review and browser proof. These are integration dependencies, not reasons to invent a new graph, identity master, event log, scheduler, publication system or investment authority.

## 5. Research sufficiency for a useful first vertical

A bounded first vertical does not need a complete world cost curve, every issuer or a numerical fair value for every asset. It does need enough evidence and behavior to answer its declared investor question. The proposed sufficiency test has six parts:

**A concrete job.** The user can ask what changed for a selected theme/company/asset, how it could affect economics, and what would invalidate the interpretation. A browsable taxonomy alone does not satisfy the job.

**A representative positive and negative case.** The model should recognize supported new capability or improved economics, while rejecting a misleading aggregation, ownership adjustment or incompatible comparison. It should not be designed only to refuse calculations.

**A complete explanation at the permitted evidence strength.** Identity, product, period, reporting basis and rights must be adequate for the claim actually made. Missing data should narrow the claim visibly rather than be filled with guessed precision.

**An update and correction path.** The reader can distinguish what was known or published earlier, what changed later, and what was only reconstructed now. A changed event must update the relevant program or asset, not automatically every business of the issuer.

**A lawful real data path.** Original evidence or a permitted derived representation reaches the existing product consumer through the approved owner and publication path. Attractive documentation is not proof that this path works.

**A demonstrated user result.** Browser proof shows the user completing the job on real inputs, including an unknown-input case and a changed-source case. Application behavior and investment validation remain distinct.

The proposed first slice remains a small economic dossier within the existing Themes journey, not a new mining portal or a large autonomous valuation engine. Copper/asset-rights examples remain a plausible starting point because the prior research already supplies contrasting reporting and entitlement cases. This is a proposal; current shared-owner interfaces and the remaining representative research must inform the written design before acceptance.

## 6. What should be frozen before Fable receives the implementation package

The package should contain a principal-reviewed taxonomy with explicit coverage, a limited set of representative asset/company dossiers, and a source dictionary that includes observation periods, units, reporting boundaries and reuse constraints. It should specify the investor journey and the corresponding machine transformations, with counterexamples linked to the behavior they test.

A requirements matrix should consolidate overlapping MN/MA/MB/fuel/MG/MS/ME/MC proposals into a smaller coherent set of behaviors. Repeated requirements are not independent features. For example, several cases express one rule: compare observations only when period, material, accounting scope and basis are compatible. Preserve each case as a test of the rule without creating separate control systems for it.

The package must also identify what remains unknown or deliberately out of the first slice. Fable should not be asked to manufacture contracts, customer quantities or cost curves during implementation. Bounded source validation can be assigned later, but foundational domain decisions stay with the principal until resolved or explicitly scoped out.

Acceptance should include meaningful visible synthesis, not only a populated graph or clean CI. Security weights, rankings, forecasts and trading decisions remain separate authorities; descriptive theme membership or model sentiment cannot quietly become permission to originate or size trades.
