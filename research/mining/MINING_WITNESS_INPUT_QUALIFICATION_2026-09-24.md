**Counter-thesis:** shipment timing, excluded charges and changing by-product conditions can explain an apparently favorable result without proving a sustainable improvement in attributable cash. The next evidence is a compatible later operating/cash observation, not an automatically generated trade.

M1 must not interpret their simple difference as verified physical inventory depletion. **Proposed useful answer:** processing/commercial progress must be evaluated together with external revenue, separately defined contractual income and the distinction between positive adjusted EBITDA and a GAAP loss. None of those observations alone establishes adequate invested-capital returns.

For product display, prefer the issuer's reported total and its source-linked components; any newly computed result requires a native financial derivation. Preserve negative signs through the Company/financial route, not the nonnegative physical-quantity assertion slot.

The financial packet adapter accepts explicit canonical-to-source identity and existing governed metric/query inputs. Its demonstration fixture loader is not a real-company importer.
