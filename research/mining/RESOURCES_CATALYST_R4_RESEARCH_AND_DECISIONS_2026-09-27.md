## 1. Decisions reached

The first study must have separate financing-attempt and construction-decision populations. Requiring a company to have completed financing or begun building would remove some of the very funding failures the engine must anticipate. Formal approval, conditional approval, early works, refinancing and continuation are different observations. One project may supply several observations but never becomes several independent investments merely because its documents have different dates.

First attainment is not durable operation. A commercial-production declaration may be a correctly achieved target even when the mine later suspends. Conversely it is not proof of positive cash generation, shareholder profit or sustained nameplate output. Preserve the original target result and model subsequent operating and financing transitions separately.

Original-security settlement is not ticker disappearance. A completed acquisition can exchange the original common share for cash or another company's shares. That path needs the actual consideration and subsequent wealth history. Court protection, operating suspension and an extinguished equity claim are not synonyms.

The current audit contains **15 disclosure observations, 12 project families and 29 source records**. These are purposefully selected historical development and boundary cases, already exposed to outcomes. They are **not a complete exchange population, a random sample, 12 independent prospective tests, or an estimated mining-success rate**. The code keeps that restriction explicit. It tests outcome-free *enrollment inputs*, not an assertion that this researcher was blind to outcomes.

## 4. Four real mechanism checks

### PureGold: a target can be met before the investment thesis fails

The construction decision was disclosed on 7 August 2019. Commercial production was declared on 3 August 2021, effective 1 August—inside a 24-calendar-month window. The October 2022 update subsequently reported suspension, no consistent positive site-level cash flow and no proceeds from the expected warrant exercises. These are different events. The later adverse state does not erase the earlier attained target; the earlier target does not establish investment success. [PG_BUILD; PG_COMMERCIAL; PG_SUSPEND]

The reference returns ACHIEVED for that issuer-commercial target using public sources through the 24-month anniversary. It returns UNKNOWN for historical system possession because the supplied capture date is in 2026. Court protection has its own record; legal extinguishment and recovery remain null. [PG_CCAA]

**Consequence:** use first-attainment likelihood for the declared milestone and a separate post-attainment operating/cash trajectory. A single absorbing successful-mine state is insufficient for shareholder research. No actual PureGold total return has been reconstructed.

### Sabina: the claim changes before the mine produces

Sabina's September 2022 decision targeted first production in Q1 2025. B2Gold reported completion of the acquisition on 19 April 2023, exchanging each Sabina common share for 0.3867 B2Gold shares. B2Gold later reported Goose's first gold on 30 June 2025. Preserve the original window and the corporate-action path; the source's first-production wording is not automatically identical to every first-gold or commercial-production target. [GOOSE_BUILD; SABINA_EXCHANGE; GOOSE_FIRST]

With an expressly **invented CAD10 successor-share price**, the gross value is CAD3.867 per old share. This is only exchange-ratio arithmetic. Historical prices, distributions, fees, fractional treatment, native action identity and the original entry price remain unbound. The ongoing exposure after settlement is to B2Gold's security, not an isolated Goose project.

**Consequence:** original-claim settlement is distinct from extinction and from operational delivery. Preserve original-horizon wealth via cash/successor shares; do not substitute zero because the old ticker stops trading or sum project-level standalone upside into the successor company.

### Premier: mill readiness is not mine readiness

Ascot's first gold was poured on 20 April 2024 and disclosed two days later. In September it reported strong mill progress but insufficient developed stoping areas and ore feed, and suspended operations to pursue mine development and funding. Its available cash was not a demonstrated fully financed restart budget. [PREMIER_FIRST; PREMIER_SUSPEND]

The identical first-gold event is outside 24 months of the December 2020 finance landmark but inside 24 months of the December 2022 refinancing landmark. Both results are mechanically correct for their respective questions. The later landmark must not reset the old deadline or supply another independent successful project.

**Consequence:** separate ore access, developed feed, metallurgical performance, plant throughput, working capital and executable funding. An isolated throughput improvement cannot stand in for all of them. Suspension is not automatically commercial attainment, permanent closure or original-equity extinction.

## 6. Methods and discriminating experiments

**Baseline and model sequence.** Start with announced-window performance and a simple stage-specific historical first-transition baseline. R3's cumulative-incidence reference remains a narrow arithmetic baseline, not a trained mining prior. For a richer candidate, use discrete monthly multi-state transition probabilities with a no-transition state, stage/episode-type effects and justified partial pooling. Interval-valued attainment contributes probability over its admissible months, not an arbitrary midpoint. Fit only after the source/identity frame is qualified. Evaluate the incremental complexity against the simpler baseline rather than assuming it wins.

A separate post-attainment transition component tracks operation, temporary suspension, restart and permanent closure where labels exist; claim settlement/extinction remains a linked but distinct process. Observable production is not automatically positive cash flow. The financial owner must propagate rights, claims, funding terms and scenario-linked dilution into conditional security value. R4 does not implement or fit this model.

**Feature experiments.** Compare nested families on the same eligible population: (A) stage/cohort plus issuer timetable; (B) deterministic critical-path evidence and financing access; (C) evidence-bound specialist LLM findings; (D) independently sourced expectations; (E) optional qualified options/positioning. Preserve source vintages and explicit unavailable flags. Useful candidate features include qualified spend versus conditional draw requirements, minimum forecast cash before milestone, mine-feed readiness versus plant readiness, schedule revision history and retained economics. Historical LLM model weights can contain future outcome knowledge even when retrieval is cut off; these cases cannot establish a clean historical LLM edge.

**Splits and uncertainty.** Group project episodes, issuers, original claims and transaction/source-origin families as required by the intended generalization claim; do not randomly split rows about the same mine. Use chronological training/validation and a later prospectively registered untouched evaluation period. Report cluster-aware intervals, calibration, log/Brier losses, timing error and coverage by stage and source availability. An exact-date subset is a sensitivity analysis, not a silently substituted full cohort.

**Sample adequacy.** There is no accepted universal project count. Before collecting the untouched test, the existing evaluation owner should simulate power under the pilot's cluster/censoring/coverage structure over absolute Brier improvements of 0.01, 0.02 and 0.05 and report the effective number of independent clusters needed at 80% and 90% power. These are planning sensitivities, not promotion thresholds. Freeze the economically meaningful tolerance and calibration/utility guardrails before exposing that test; do not choose them after the result. If coverage or dependence makes the proposed comparison uninformative, keep the model unqualified and collect shadow evidence rather than fit a confident prior to the audit.
