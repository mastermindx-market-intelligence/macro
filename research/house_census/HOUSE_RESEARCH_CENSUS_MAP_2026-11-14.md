# House research census map — read, not run

## Plain summary

The house built completed-bar entry signals, group-turn and cycle displays, risk warnings, exit rules, options measurements, and paper-evaluation tools.
It also built the identity, timestamp, replay and evidence machinery needed to ask whether those tools work.
This census is a reading of the house research record, not a new backtest or a runtime inspection.
Nothing from 2024 onward is shown as a market result; results with unknown endpoints are withheld too.
No eligible rotation buy or avoid forecasting edge was found in the house record reviewed here.
That does not mean nothing was built, that every family failed, or that an unrun test returned zero.
The historical entry line to beat is house T1 on sector ETFs, 2013-01-02 through 2023-05-31.
Its lateness-matched fake-out rate is **21.2%**, **8.7 percentage points fewer than random dates [−12.8, −4.2]**.
That entry comparison is distinct from the missing rotation and exact stock-entry × group-confirmation comparisons.
The house avoid line is **no warning**: its 34.3% fake-breakdown rate is worse than 31.7% for random dates and 29.2% for a volatility rule.
An avoid successor must beat the volatility rule at the same share of time active, not merely look better on selected examples.
Six useful questions remain open:
1. Can a group turn, dated when it first became knowable, beat ordinary relative strength and price at the same delay, false-start rate and cost?
2. Does confirming a stock entry with its group's tier improve that same entry under the same fills and stops?
3. Can a warning identify a particular kind of damage better than its strongest input, without blocking the recovery that follows?
4. Can conditional reclaim work on untouched dates with the original holding rule unchanged?
5. Are original-vintage inputs, markers, coverage and repaired Radar histories consistent enough to support probabilities?
6. Can a new holding or exit rule reduce downside without cutting off the winners on a fresh, matched cohort?

Three older Radar deaths—incumbent top, incumbent bottom and three-leg PRE—are **pending a repair check**, not reaffirmed failures.
A replay repair changed 152 of 8,216 state days and invalidated four studies; its order relative to those older studies is unknown.
Built displays, historical associations, rejected constructions and missing evidence remain separate throughout this document.

Census locators: S12_A §1.1 P129; S12_B §1.3 P40; S12_B §1.7/C3; S10_B reconciliation1; S13 Card2–4; S14 Card2; W4 §4. The entry and avoid comparison is the supplied recorded-run synthesis, receipt `RESULTS v2 896432ec`; it was not rerun for this document.

## 1. Scope, evidence and reading rules

The map folds **173 supplied family cards into 35 organizing families**. A family is not one strategy, one experiment or one independent sample. Report/script/register copies and repeated cohorts are not replications. The six assigned synthesis documents were read for this adaptation; the underlying source code, raw captures, datasets and strategies were not executed or independently audited.

- **Built and live** below means source-reported display or integration, not observed present runtime. A surviving display does not validate a forecast or authorize action.
- **Built, untested** means the stated efficacy question remains unqualified, even where components or related constructions were tested.
- **Tested null or dead** is scoped to the named construction and comparator. **Abandoned** can mean a method or reference was retired, not that it lost money.
- Evidence grades: reported n ≥ 20 is **tested**, n 8–19 **limited history**, n < 8 **case-based**. Unknown n is ungraded, not zero. Overlapping days, trades and independent episodes are not interchangeable; a nominal grade is not scientific acceptance.
- Coverage ≥ 0.90 is **usable**; 0.75–<0.90 is **survivor-biased**; below 0.75 is **not shown** for cohort outcomes. Unknown coverage stays ungraded. These grades do not establish point-in-time membership or causal identification.
- Market results must have an explicitly eligible endpoint before 2024. Neither a historical sub-result nor an engineering count validates a complete current rule.
- Citations such as S12_A §1.1 P129, S13 Card4 and S12F1-36 are census locators inherited from the supplied synthesis, not claims of fresh underlying-source reads. W4 denotes the supplied recorded-run synthesis. F01–F35 and D001–D221 are organizing identifiers, not new experiments.

## 5. The line to beat

### Entry

The supplied recorded-run synthesis reports **house T1 raw-cross take**, not the live board's validated set, on **sector ETFs, 2013-01-02..2023-05-31**:

- n393; lateness-matched fake-outs **21.2%** (pooled **21.3%**).
- **8.7 percentage points fewer fake-outs than random dates [−12.8, −4.2]**.
- **9.3 percentage points fewer** than circular shift; **7.2 percentage points fewer** than plain strength.
- FF12 comparison borderline: **−7.7 percentage points [−15.8, 0.2]**; do not call it established.
- `rel21` **+0.1%**; this source metric is not generalized into an executable return claim.

Nominal grade: tested (n ≥ 20). The supplied synthesis records a desk run and a separate reproduction, receipt `RESULTS v2 896432ec`; neither was repeated here. This is an entry comparator, not evidence that rotation discovery or exact stock × group confirmation has passed. (W4 §4; supplied census benchmark receipt `RESULTS v2 896432ec`.)

### Avoid

The house not-topped veto provides **no warning advantage** on the reported fake-breakdown endpoint: **34.3%**, versus **31.7% random** and **29.2% volatility rule**. Member drawdown was not tested. A successor must beat the volatility rule at matched active-time share; it cannot substitute a different drawdown endpoint or a favorable selected period. House T2 is thin (ETF counts 29/31); no efficacy claim is made. (Supplied census benchmark receipt `RESULTS v2 896432ec`; W4 §4.)

### Things that must not be counted as additional empirical deaths

- **Scoped nulls:** selected rotation ICs, modern calendars, stop means, mean-return versus risk-control comparisons, weak ownership drift, selected warning descriptions without comparators, and options associations that do not replicate uniformly. A null in one endpoint is not a whole-family rejection. (S08 death3/8/10–12; S09 D38–40/D47–58; S10_B K210/227; S12_B §1.3; S14-06/28/42/47.)
- **Restrictions, not tests:** rotation × cycle-position DON'T-TEST; insider × T2; no population merging, forced top-K, duplicate scorecards/stores, inferred signed positioning from unsigned OI, multiplied relationship coefficients or generated direction as authority. Preserve restrictions; do not turn them into experiment counts. (S10_A K011–19; S10_B K095–96/K121; S11 register47–50; W4 §4.)
- **Invalidated methods, not reliable losses:** option-input estimators; provisional-bar and outcome-conditioned scorecards; incorrect bar anchors; adjusted-price legal-limit studies; leaked folds; corrupted plan clocks; wrong-centred controls; double differencing; payout/share basis; future-run-confirmed onset; end-edge exits; immature regression labels. Both favorable and adverse old scores are invalidated. (S09 D59–74; S10_A K004/K020; S10_B K090–91; S12_A S-B1/P51/P142/S-H8; S12_B C5–7; S14-43–44.)
- **Retirements, not trading results:** incompatible append destinations, obsolete books/shortlists, duplicate stores, abandoned source-format compiler and policy/architecture closures. (S10_A K001–03/D-AM35; S10_B K049/K078/K098; S11 AF-21; S14 retirements.)
- **Unavailable or unrun:** exact stock × group confirmation, G-T2X group/dispersion arms, missing E-E peer store, prospective grader failures, qualified option fills, data-limited option hypotheses, unavailable point-in-time membership/minute clocks, immature ledgers and unbuilt graders/variables. Preserve NOT-RUN, UNAVAILABLE, ACCRUE and insufficient-power meanings. (W4 §4; S10_B K034/36/46/102/138/170; S12F1-01–03/18/25/42; S13 U01/02/04/07/17.)
