## 6. What remains unverified and why it matters

Actual installed news activation and source entitlements; complete real-time coverage; point-in-time intraday historical data; current availability of every economic/financial input; true historical Mastermind observation/delivery; independent real-user production acceptance; end-to-end operating cost; and incremental prospective predictive value remain unverified.

These are not reasons to abandon the design or defer every useful feature. The work packages specify how to resolve each one, which independent paths can advance, and which claims remain unavailable. No research artifact, code count or confident narrative substitutes for that evidence.
