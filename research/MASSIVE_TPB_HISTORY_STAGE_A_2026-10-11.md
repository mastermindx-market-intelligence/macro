## Exact owner-supplied input and clocks

`measure_source_snapshot(snapshot)` admits **one bounded, owner-reconciled current vintage** at a declared as-of time:

- Strict `equity.tp_b.source_snapshot/v0`; one ticker and explicit `YYYY-MM-DD:RTH`. Both source snapshots and derived historical rank candidates require `start_ns` to equal 09:30 America/New_York on that stated date, translated to integer UTC nanoseconds with DST (13:30 UTC in EDT, 14:30 UTC in EST), with the original 390-minute maximum. This **cross-checks date/clock identity only**: a well-formed timestamp, holiday or fictional early-close duration is NOT proof that a market session existed. The original source/calendar owner must externally qualify real open days and ends. Explicit bounded `end_ns`, `asof_ns`, `watermark_complete_ns` and original `watermark_available_ns` remain required.
- Separate immutable SHA256s for source manifest, source generation, previous/superseded generation, market calendar, volume policy, exchange reference and corporate-action basis vintage; explicit `split_basis_id` and `split_segment`. These are **supplied receipts**; a digest is not vendor authentication.
- At most 20,000 current native print identities per source snapshot. Each current row explicitly identifies latest revision/ACTIVE or CANCELLED action, source receipt SHA, source condition/volume eligibility, venue/TRF pipe, exact decimal price/shares, SIP clock, optional participant and TRF-report clocks, and original availability. No correction chain is independently replayed: revised/cancelled rows require a previous-generation ref, and an external correction owner must retain the immutable predecessor.
- A TRF observation needs an exchange-4 reporting code, recognized 201/202/203 TRF pipe and `volume_eligible is True`. Non-TRF, ineligible and unknown-policy rows are not silently imputed as qualified off-exchange volume. A TRF pipe is **not** a named ATS.
- No more than 390 explicitly sourced cumulative-minute records. Each minute has an exact minute index, source receipt, source availability no earlier than the minute end, an explicit prefix-coverage assertion and cumulative OE/consolidated shares from one admitted **RTH, same-share-basis** source contract. Missing/invalid/zero denominator produces a null ratio, not 0% participation. Observed minute source and historical source rights/authenticity remain externally unverified.
- A partial market day is allowed and cannot produce **daily record ranks**. Coverage after the qualified source watermark, late original print receipts, impossible native report/participant clocks, duplicate current identities or non-monotone cumulative rows are refused, not guessed.

This is a **source-owner-prepared normalized read** rather than a second trade signer. No live connection, Massive call, REST/flat-file crawl, credentials, device permission, writer/lock, scheduler, container, public API, metadata registries or raw-payload export.

## Outputs and semantics

The source-free `equity.tp_b.historical_ruler/v0` result has distinct observed measurements:

1. **Largest individual qualifying TRF print** by exact USD notional.
2. **Largest same-TRF-pipe + exact-price cluster** of at least two qualifying prints in the maximum-notional **rolling 60-second SIP-time window**. Prints at other prices can interleave; they do not fracture a matching level. The separate `n_clusters` field counts nonoverlapping, first-print-anchored observed bursts, and is not the count of every overlapping candidate window. *SIP-time grouping is a report-time proxy, not same-order execution or actor identity.* The native participant and TRF-report clock presence and deltas remain separate from the receipt clock.
3. **Daily measured TRF total** notional and shares, not the same as one print or cluster; source coverage is unverified and cannot be described as total market volume.
4. Absolute observed block tiers at $100k/$500k/$1m: counts, rates and notional fractions. These source measurements **do not yet include historical block-tier distributions**. The attempted separate tier-history profile edit was explicitly refused before tool dispatch by the platform, `EFFECT_NONE`. Do not retry the denied effect through a different carrier/account/rephrasing.

`calibrate_history(target, previous, minute_index, evaluation_ns, min_history=20)` then uses only the caller's **existing** source-observation candidates. It never performs acquisition or mutation. Its three independently scoped daily objects get competition rank with a stated tie count, strict new-record indicator only when strictly greater than all prior qualified values, explicit prior N and historical coverage start. **"Rank 1 in observed comparable sample" does not mean "largest in history."**

Daily object ranks require **identical fully covered RTH session durations**: a 210-minute early close cannot be compared with a 390-minute regular day. The intraday part matches **exact RTH minute index** and compares only historical *cumulative* OE share at that minute, never 10:00 cumulative to prior full-day participation. The calibration reader checks each derived minute ratio against its exact cumulative numerator/denominator and refuses contradictory or post-snapshot minute receipts; a source sample with **no policy-qualified TRF prints** returns null observational off-exchange totals rather than invented numeric market zeros. Source decimal inputs retain the 128-character fixed-width ceiling, while mathematically derived cumulative ratios have a separately bounded 280-character ceiling; otherwise a valid tiny ratio generated by the source would be rejected by its own historical reader. Neither rule grants market capture completeness. Earlier qualified prefixes can remain comparable across different full-day lengths; daily duration-matched N and excluded-duration count are reported separately. The target and future revisions are excluded from training; historical correction/source rows first knowable **after the target's frozen `asof_ns`** are excluded even if a researcher runs the evaluation later. Exact split-basis/vintage/segment mismatches are counted and quarantined. Historical volume-condition, venue-reference and session-calendar SHA vintages must also match the frozen target; differently hashed histories are excluded from daily ranks **and** minute baselines, with separate policy/calendar exclusion reasons. A raw response hash difference need not prove different rule semantics, but this source-free Stage A has **no owner-attested equivalence crosswalk** and must abstain rather than silently pool them. Malformed source/provenance hashes are refused for the target and excluded from historical cohorts. Each derived record is also checked for impossible source/qualifying-print counts, largest print/cluster notional above the recorded total, and inconsistent or non-nested observed absolute block tier counts, rates and fractions. These are **internal derived-record invariants only**, not a new publisher, cryptographic signature or certification of the missing original trade-source custody. Median, MAD, midrank percentile and robust z are observational with source N. Missing minute / thin history / zero MAD yield typed null, not fabricated normality. Unfinished sessions allow a minute-conditioned read but not day ranks. No future-return/outcome joins, score threshold optimization or alpha audition.

Every produced record keeps `public_delivery_allowed=False`, `ranking_trading_alert_authority=False`, `source_authenticated=False` or equivalent unqualified status, `signal=None`. No named ATS/institutional owner or quote-rule accuracy is inferred. The original source raw trade IDs never enter the derived result. Accepted source artifacts remain confidential to authorized private readers.
