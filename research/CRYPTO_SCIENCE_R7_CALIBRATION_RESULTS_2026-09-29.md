### Calibration comes only from prior out-of-training forecasts

At each calendar quarter, calibration uses earlier R6 predictions that were themselves out of that base learner's fit. Require issue<quarter, full end+24h embargo<quarter, finite p1/p2, mature binary outcome and existing valid prediction status. Calibration never fits to a base learner's in-sample predictions or the current quarter's outcomes. Minimum80observations,15of each class and weighted effective sample size40 remain fixed.

Recency weights have a365-day half-life. The recent-rate forecast uses those same weights and observations, with one positive/one negative pseudo-observation. CAL_PRICE and CAL_VOLUME fit only a log-odds intercept; slope stays1, penalty0.01, with a bracketed scalar derivative root. No half-life, regularization, feature or threshold search occurred. A within-quarter monotone transformation cannot add ranking information, although aggregate cross-quarter AUC can change when different quarters receive different offsets.

Seven forecasts are retained: original R6 expanding rate, raw price model, raw price-plus-volume model, same-cohort unweighted historical rate, recent-weighted rate, recalibrated price and recalibrated price-plus-volume. Methods are scored on the SAME eligible future episodes, not different coverage sets.

### An explicit, separately frozen protection objective

The event account follows the incumbent until the six-hour landmark. The alternative stays cash after that action until the original24h endpoint, then restores the incumbent target. Costs are0/10/25basis points one way; primary10bp. Both paths share their pre-action history, so earlier losses are not credited to forecasting.

Let R be net event-account return and L positive maximum marked drawdown. The primary research utility is:

`U = R - max(L - 0.05, 0)`.

Penalties0and2 on the excess-drawdown term are reported as sensitivities. This five-percent allowance is an experimental preference, not a user risk mandate, a stop order or a promise losses cannot exceed5%. The utility uses the established hourly-open marked path, not intrahour worst executable loss. Fees already enter R and account marks; turnover is not charged a second time.

Using prior eligible outcomes only, estimate class-conditional cash-minus-incumbent return and utility, shrunk by10pseudo-observations toward the weighted overall mean. Choose cash only if expected utility improvement>0 and expected return sacrifice is no worse than20basis points of initial episode equity. This is a hypothesis linking a binary barrier probability to economic outcomes, not a guaranteed mapping. The current episode's future label is NOT consulted when selecting an action.

A frequency-matched diagnostic compares actual selections with expected uniform random selections at the same observed quarterly cash frequency. It is retrospective, not an executable alternative, not exact notional-exposure matching and not an equal-risk portfolio. It asks whether selecting particular episodes adds value beyond how often the policy avoids exposure.

## 6. Scientific interpretation and next discriminating question

The informative advance is separating three things that earlier dashboards often collapse: adjusting average event frequency; discriminating individual episodes; and choosing an action with favorable downside/missed-rebound economics. The calibration comparison improves probability level, yet recent proper-score performance is matched by a simple recent-rate estimate. A small positive protective-utility cell does not survive enough adverse checks to grant action authority.

The experiment does not establish that risk-averse protection is worthless or that volume can never help. It establishes that these fixed forecasts, base-rate assumptions and two-class payoff mapping do not yet deliver dependable utility at this post-break landmark. A5%-before3% binary outcome omits the loss severity, time profile, failed recovery and incumbent exposure that determine protection value. Merely recalibrating a probability cannot repair missing information about those quantities.

The next scientific unit should distinguish a limitation of the sampled decision problem from a limitation of the learner. First obtain independent review of the temporal/account/likelihood evidence before any promotion. Then freeze an action-aligned severity/avoided-loss versus missed-rebound comparison, including whether useful risk remains at this six-hour landmark and an independently defined earlier observable decision cohort. That is a new question and protocol, not permission to move the present landmark or target after seeing its results. Explicitly quantify how much loss already occurred and how much incumbent exposure is already reduced before crediting a fast protective signal.

Do not respond by searching many calibrator half-lives, probability thresholds, payoff penalties or ridge coefficients on these same rare events. A direct conditional-loss or hazard model must earn incremental held-out calibration/economic value over transparent prevalence and incumbent baselines and then fresh issued-forward evidence. Resting-liquidity absorption still cannot be inferred from unsigned OHLCV; signed flow stays excluded until its instrument/time/units are qualified. Recovery fit minimums remain intact. No live forecast ledger, provider collection or new sizing plane is created by this plan.
