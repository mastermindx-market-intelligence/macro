## 5. Interface behavior

- A compact What changed strip stays below the leading investment case. It is scoped by the current candidate filter and does not introduce a competing top-level product.
- The paired review shows the old/new stance and payoff first. Each card opens the existing company case's What changed tab.
- The case explains the changed input, the unchanged facts, and the resulting probability/valuation delta. Original source wording and clocks are disclosed on demand.
- Exact analytical version IDs remain in an expandable inspection block, not the glance layer.
- Read the updated valuation, read specialist evidence, and open the original call are functional local actions. Returning restores the originating case, filter, scroll and focus.
- Source-pending and completed-review states are visually and semantically distinct. No spinner or timeout fabricates an assessment completion.
- Recommendation revision is not realized performance. The UI explicitly says no observed investment win/loss is provided.

Both themes preserve the same semantics. Dark uses graphite/luminance; light uses white material on the shared cool canvas. Red/green rails represent adverse/favorable decision changes, not decorative sector identities. On mobile, the replay-control explanation is demoted so the first decision and its action appear within the initial viewport; the synthetic label, stance and return remain visible.
