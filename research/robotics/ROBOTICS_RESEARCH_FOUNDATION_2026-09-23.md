### BOM accounting rules

Bind quantities and costs to platform, generation, variant, application, purchased-assembly level, unit, cost basis, currency and observation/evidence dates. Unknown values stay null.

Expand an actuator into its motor/reducer/encoder for dependency analysis, but never add those component costs again if the purchased actuator price includes them. Apply the same rule to camera modules and complete warehouse installations. Keep list price, manufacturing cost, teardown estimate, selling price and fully installed project cost separate. Off-robot training/simulation, integration and ongoing support are not automatically on-robot BOM.

DoF is not universally equal to motor count or purchased component count. Optional configurations and coupled tendon actuation require explicit treatment. [S22, S23] FANUC's integrated force-sensing functions also prevent assuming every cobot must purchase an external six-axis sensor. [S34]

## Bottleneck intelligence

Separate structural concentration, dated shortage and supplier profit capture. The first product should show named observations and uncertainty, not a universal numerical score.

IEA's 2024 process-specific shares—approximately 60% mining, 91% refining and 94% sintered permanent magnet production in China—establish a historical structural concentration. They do not establish a current robot-only shortage. [S33] Reducer role/product evidence is stronger than this packet's evidence of current reducer lead times. Linear actuation likewise has product evidence but no verified global shortage series.

For each component/grade/location: record qualified suppliers, capacity announced/commissioned/qualified, actual output, utilization, yield, delivery lead time, order backlog/cancellations, inventories, prices, warranty burden, substitutions and evidence horizon. Headquarters do not imply manufacturing location; nameplate capacity does not equal qualified output. Do not manufacture a time series from unsourced narrative adjectives.

Research hypothesis: the binding constraint can migrate between precision motion, hands, sensing, compute/thermal, integration and reliable autonomous operation. Test that migration against operating data instead of assuming more robots automatically mean more pricing power for today's fashionable component.

For deployment, distinguish productive autonomous tasks/hours, on-task accuracy, uptime, intervention labor, aggregate fleet history, customer acceptance and repeat orders. Digit's existing-generation history, conditional successor orders and planned successor availability are separate data. [S24,S25]

A transparent scenario may calculate `robot units × relevant components/unit × ASP × outsourced fraction × supplier share`. Every uncertain term must be labeled as an assumption; outputs are scenarios, not reported revenue or trade authority. Insourcing, alternative architectures and falling ASPs can offset unit growth.

An all-in economic view should include financing/depreciation, integration, software, energy, maintenance, replacement parts, on-site/remote assistance and downtime per successful autonomous task. Hardware BOM alone does not measure economic usefulness.
