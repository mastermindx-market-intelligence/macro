# N-STORE lifecycle coverage — 2026-10-12

Coverage of the ticker-news reducer and store for correction, withdrawal, duplicate delivery, stale revision, change cursors, read-only open, and field redaction. Display-tier behavior only. No schema change.

Empty teaser and url on a read are `""`. A fully withdrawn story is `story()` returning `None`. `CursorConflict` is the commit compare-and-swap error. `changes(after_sequence=...)` does not raise it.
