Market Regime / 市场状态

A plain-word read of the market's current posture — risk-on, mixed, or risk-off — drawn from the dashboard's composite indicators, shown alongside the day's briefing.

Gives a fast, one-word orientation before reading any detail, so you know whether the backdrop currently favours or discourages taking risk.

Categorical label (risk-on / mixed / risk-off)

Risk-on — supportive backdrop; trend, breadth, and cross-asset signals broadly line up.

Risk-off — stressed backdrop; tape under pressure and trend pointing lower.

Mixed — signals disagree or the regime is in transition; treat with extra caution.

A confirmation-style read of current conditions, not a forecast of what happens next.

Can change quickly when underlying signals flip; check the update time before acting.

对市场当前态势的简明判断——风险偏好、混合或避险——来自看板综合指标，与当日简报一并展示。

在查看细节前先给出一个词的方向感，帮助判断当前环境是偏向承担风险还是偏向谨慎。

分类标签（风险偏好／混合／避险）

风险偏好——环境偏支持性；趋势、广度与跨资产信号总体一致。

避险——环境承压；盘面承压，趋势向下。

混合——信号不一致或市场状态处于转换期，应格外谨慎。

这是对当前状态的确认性判断，不是对未来走势的预测。

底层信号变化时该判断可能迅速更新，行动前请核对更新时间。

Market State Score / 市场状态分

A 0-100 dial summarising current US equity market posture, blending trend, cross-asset risk appetite, volatility, breadth, liquidity, and stress into one composite read.

Turns six separate market inputs into one glanceable number so you can track whether conditions are improving or deteriorating day to day.

0-100 composite score

Lower score — tape under stress, trend pointing lower, signals aligned negative.

Mid-range score — components disagree or the picture is transitional.

Higher score — trend, breadth, and cross-asset signals broadly align supportive.

A blended confirmation measure of current conditions, not a predictive score.

Individual component legs can pull the score in different directions; check the breakdown before drawing conclusions from the headline number alone.

0-100综合仪表盘，反映美股当前市场态势，综合趋势、跨资产风险偏好、波动率、市场广度、流动性与压力六项因子。

将六项市场输入合并为一个直观数字，方便逐日跟踪环境是在改善还是在恶化。

0-100综合评分

评分较低——盘面承压，趋势向下，信号呈负面一致。

评分处于中间区间——各分项不一致，或整体格局处于过渡阶段。

评分较高——趋势、广度与跨资产信号总体呈支持性一致。

这是对当前状态的综合确认性衡量，不是预测性评分。

各分项因子可能方向不一致，仅看总分容易误判，建议查看分项拆解。

Regime Quadrant / 宏观象限

A four-way classification of the macro backdrop by the direction of growth and inflation: e.g. growth rising with inflation falling, or growth falling with inflation rising.

Different quadrants historically favour different sectors and factors, so it helps frame which parts of the market the backdrop tends to support.

Categorical quadrant label with a stability qualifier

Growth up / inflation down — historically the most supportive quadrant for equities.

Growth and inflation both rising or both falling — a mixed backdrop for sector tilts.

Growth down / inflation up — historically the most defensive quadrant.

This is a coincident, market-derived read of the cycle, not an independent forecast of the real economy — it tends to move with price rather than ahead of it.

The label can lag the sign-rule read of today's data during a confirmation window.

按增长与通胀的方向变化，将宏观环境划分为四种象限：例如增长上行、通胀下行，或增长下行、通胀上行等组合。

不同象限历史上通常利好不同板块与风格因子，有助于判断当前环境更支持哪类资产。

象限分类标签，附带稳定性说明

增长上行／通胀下行——历史上对股票最为有利的象限。

增长与通胀同向上行或同向下行——对板块配置而言属于混合环境。

增长下行／通胀上行——历史上最需防御的象限。

该判断主要由与市场同步的指标推导，属于同期指标而非独立的经济预测，不具备领先性。

在确认期内，该标签可能滞后于当日数据本身的方向。

Transition State / 转换状态

Shows how settled the current regime quadrant is — stable, shifting, weakening, or newly changed.

Tells you how much to trust the current regime read: a stable label can be leaned on, a shifting one calls for lighter positioning.

Categorical label (stable / shifting / weakening / new regime)

No directional up/down reading — this describes confidence in the current regime label, not a market direction.

A stable label describes confirmation, not certainty — regimes can and do turn.

A "new regime" label reflects a just-confirmed change and may itself still be tested.

显示当前宏观象限的稳定程度——稳定、转换中、走弱，或刚刚切换。

帮助判断当前象限判断的可信度：稳定状态可作为依据，转换中则应更谨慎地控制仓位。

分类标签（稳定／转换中／走弱／刚切换）

无上行／下行方向性判断——该指标描述的是对当前象限标签的可信度，而非市场方向。

稳定标签代表确认结果，并非绝对保证——宏观象限仍可能发生转变。

「刚切换」标签代表刚确认的变化，其本身仍可能被后续数据检验。

Risk Radar / 回撤风险雷达

A leading advisory score for pullback risk across seven scare types — credit stress, rate shocks, bubble unwind, growth scares, volatility events, and breadth breakdowns.

Flags rising pullback risk earlier than a lagging drawdown measure, so you can size or hedge before stress becomes visible in price.

Categorical band (calm / watch / caution / elevated / risk-off)

Calm or watch — no unusual pullback pressure detected among the tracked scare types.

Caution — some scare types are stirring; a reason to tighten risk controls, not to override a supportive market read.

Elevated or risk-off — historically higher-than-normal odds of a near-term pullback; a de-risking sizing consideration, not an exit signal.

This is a probability-of-pullback advisory, not a certainty or a timing call.

Elevated/caution bands describe sizing context; they do not by themselves override the broader market read.

针对七类「惊吓」情景（信用压力、利率冲击、泡沫回落、增长担忧、波动率事件、市场广度恶化等）给出的前瞻性回撤风险提示评分。

相比滞后性的回撤指标能更早提示风险上升，帮助在压力体现到价格之前调整仓位或对冲。

分类区间（平静／观察／谨慎／偏高／避险）

平静或观察——所跟踪的各类「惊吓」情景中未检测到异常回撤压力。

谨慎——部分「惊吓」情景出现苗头，提示应收紧风险控制，但不足以推翻整体偏支持性的市场判断。

偏高或避险——历史上意味着近期回撤概率高于平常，属于降低仓位风险的参考，而非清仓信号。

这是回撤概率的提示性评分，不是确定性判断或择时信号。

高位/谨慎区间提供的是仓位管理参考，不会单独凌驾于整体市场判断之上。

Liquidity State / 流动性状态

A four-way classification of financial-system liquidity conditions: stress expansion, clean expansion, contraction, or neutral.

Liquidity tends to lift or drag most risk assets together, so knowing the current state helps explain broad market tailwinds or headwinds.

Categorical label (stress expansion / clean expansion / contraction / neutral)

Clean expansion — liquidity growing without unusual stress signatures; generally supportive for risk assets.

Contraction — liquidity shrinking; historically a headwind for risk assets.

Stress expansion or neutral — liquidity is growing but alongside stress signals, or showing no clear direction; read with the accompanying context.

Liquidity conditions shift gradually and lag some faster market-moving events.

"Expansion" describes system liquidity, not a guarantee that every asset benefits equally or immediately.

对金融体系流动性状况的四分类判断：压力型扩张、健康型扩张、收缩，或中性。

流动性变化往往同步影响多数风险资产，了解当前状态有助于理解市场整体的顺风或逆风环境。

分类标签（压力型扩张／健康型扩张／收缩／中性）

健康型扩张——流动性增长且未伴随异常压力特征，总体对风险资产有利。

收缩——流动性收缩，历史上对风险资产构成逆风。

压力型扩张或中性——流动性在增长但伴随压力信号，或方向不明朗，建议结合具体背景解读。

流动性状况变化通常较为缓慢，可能滞后于部分反应更快的市场事件。

「扩张」描述的是系统整体流动性，不代表所有资产都会同等或立即受益。

https://fred.stlouisfed.org

https://www.federalreserve.gov

Trend Factor / 趋势因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: the multi-timeframe price trend of major US equity indexes, from daily through monthly.

Confirms whether the tape's own price action supports the broader market read, weighted toward longer timeframes for durability.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — trend broadly aligned bullish across timeframes.

Low score — trend broadly aligned bearish across timeframes.

Mid score — short- and longer-term trends disagree.

Weighted toward weekly and monthly timeframes; a strong daily move alone will not move this factor much.

A trend confirmation measure, not a prediction of the trend's continuation.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：主要美股指数在日线至月线多个周期上的价格趋势。

用于确认盘面自身走势是否支持整体市场判断，权重更偏向较长周期以体现持续性。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——各周期趋势总体呈看涨一致。

评分较低——各周期趋势总体呈看跌一致。

评分居中——短期与长期趋势不一致。

权重偏向周线与月线，单日大幅波动对该因子影响有限。

这是对趋势的确认性衡量，不是对趋势能否延续的预测。

Risk Appetite Factor / 风险偏好因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: cross-asset risk appetite, comparing how risk-seeking versus risk-averse assets are performing relative to each other.

Confirms whether appetite for risk is broad-based across asset classes, not just concentrated in equities.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — risk-seeking assets outperforming defensive ones broadly.

Low score — defensive assets outperforming; a risk-off signature.

Mid score — cross-asset signals mixed or offsetting.

Reflects relative performance across assets, not any single asset's absolute level.

Can diverge from equity-only trend factors during narrow, index-led moves.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：跨资产风险偏好，比较偏风险型资产与偏防御型资产的相对表现。

用于确认风险偏好是否在各类资产间广泛存在，而不仅局限于股票市场。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——偏风险型资产整体跑赢防御型资产。

评分较低——防御型资产跑赢，呈现避险特征。

评分居中——跨资产信号不一致或相互抵消。

反映的是各类资产之间的相对表现，而非单一资产的绝对水平。

在个别指数领涨的窄幅行情中，可能与仅基于股票的趋势因子出现分化。

Volatility Factor / 波动率因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: the measured volatility regime, including the term structure of implied volatility.

Calm, orderly volatility historically supports risk-taking; spiking or inverted volatility term structure is a caution signal.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — volatility low and orderly; supportive backdrop.

Low score — volatility elevated or term structure stressed.

Mid score — volatility signals transitional or mixed.

A measured, current-state reading of volatility, not a forecast of a future spike.

Volatility can shift abruptly around scheduled events; check the calendar context.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：以隐含波动率期限结构等指标衡量的波动率环境。

平稳有序的波动率环境历史上有利于承担风险；波动率飙升或期限结构倒挂通常是警示信号。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——波动率处于低位且运行有序，环境偏支持性。

评分较低——波动率偏高，或期限结构承压。

评分居中——波动率信号处于过渡期或不一致。

这是对当前波动率状态的衡量，不是对未来波动率飙升的预测。

波动率可能在重要事件公布前后出现骤变，建议结合日历事件一并查看。

https://www.cboe.com

Breadth Factor / 广度因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: how broadly individual stocks are participating in the current market move, not just headline indexes.

A strong index move with weak breadth is a classic warning sign that the rally is narrow and more fragile than it looks.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — participation broad across stocks; a healthier move.

Low score — participation narrow; a warning on move durability.

Mid score — breadth improving or deteriorating, not yet decisive.

A snapshot of current participation, not a guarantee that narrow breadth will resolve into a reversal.

Best read alongside the trend factor rather than in isolation.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：衡量个股参与本轮行情的广泛程度，而非仅看指数表现。

指数上涨但广度偏弱是典型的警示信号，说明涨势较为集中，比表面看起来更脆弱。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——个股参与广泛，行情较为健康。

评分较低——参与度狭窄，对行情持续性发出警示。

评分居中——广度正在改善或恶化，方向尚未明确。

该指标反映当前参与度快照，广度偏弱不代表必然出现反转。

建议与趋势因子结合查看，避免单独解读。

Liquidity Factor / 流动性因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: broad money and credit conditions, sometimes called the "money tide."

Liquidity conditions historically influence how much fuel is available for risk assets to keep rising, independent of price trend alone.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — liquidity and credit conditions supportive.

Low score — liquidity and credit conditions tightening.

Mid score — liquidity signals mixed or transitional.

Liquidity conditions move gradually; large day-to-day swings in this factor are uncommon.

Reflects system-level conditions, not any single fund flow or institution.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：广义货币与信用状况，有时也称为「资金潮汐」。

资金面状况历史上会影响风险资产持续上涨的「燃料」是否充足，独立于价格趋势本身。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——流动性与信用状况偏支持性。

评分较低——流动性与信用状况趋紧。

评分居中——流动性信号不一致或处于过渡期。

流动性状况变化通常较为缓慢，该因子日间大幅波动的情况并不常见。

反映的是系统层面的整体状况，而非某一资金流或机构的情况。

https://fred.stlouisfed.org

Stress Factor / 压力因子

One of six factors feeding the Market State Score: a drawdown-risk guard measuring acute systemic stress signatures.

Designed to catch acute stress conditions quickly, since this factor can cap the overall market read even when other factors look supportive.

0-100 sub-score (0 = fully risk-off, 100 = fully risk-on)

High score — no acute stress signatures detected.

Low score — acute stress signatures present; a defensive signal.

Mid score — stress signals building but not yet acute.

Designed to react quickly to acute stress; can move faster than the other five factors.

A current-condition guard, not a forecast of whether stress will worsen.

构成市场状态分的六项因子之一：作为回撤风险防护，衡量急性系统性压力特征。

旨在快速捕捉急性压力状况，因此即便其他因子表现良好，该因子也可能拉低整体市场判断上限。

0-100分项评分（0为完全避险，100为完全风险偏好）

评分较高——未检测到急性压力特征。

评分较低——出现急性压力特征，属于防御性信号。

评分居中——压力信号正在累积，但尚未达到急性程度。

该因子对急性压力反应较快，波动速度可能快于其余五项因子。

这是对当前状况的防护性衡量，不是对压力是否会加剧的预测。

Signal Divergence / 信号分歧

When two or more market indicators point in different directions at the same time, shown as a "note" (minor) or "tension" (more significant) severity.

Divergence is a flag to slow down: acting on one signal while ignoring a conflicting one raises the odds of being wrong-footed.

Categorical severity (note / tension)

No single up/down reading — divergence describes disagreement between signals, and its resolution can go either direction.

Divergence does not by itself say which signal will prove correct.

A "tension" flag calls for more caution, not automatically a change in position.

指两个或以上市场指标在同一时间给出不同方向的判断，按严重程度分为「提示」（较轻微）与「张力」（较显著）两级。

信号分歧提示应更谨慎：只依据其中一个信号而忽略相互矛盾的另一个，容易导致判断失误。

分类严重程度（提示／张力）

无单一上行／下行判断——信号分歧描述的是信号之间的不一致，其最终解决方向可能是任一方向。

信号分歧本身并不能说明最终哪个信号会被验证正确。

「张力」级别的分歧提示应更加谨慎，但并不意味着必须立即改变仓位。

Confidence Tier / 置信度

A high / medium / low rating showing how firmly a briefing's read holds, based on how well the underlying signals agree.

Tells you how much weight to put on a given read — a low-confidence read calls for more independent verification before acting.

Categorical tier (high / medium / low)

No directional reading — this rates certainty of a read, not market direction.

A high-confidence tier reflects signal agreement, not a guarantee of a correct call.

Confidence can change quickly as underlying signals move.

高／中／低三档评级，反映简报解读的把握程度，取决于底层信号之间的一致性。

提示应赋予该判断多大的权重——置信度较低时，行动前更需要独立核实。

分类等级（高／中／低）

无方向性判断——该指标衡量的是判断的确定性，而非市场方向。

高置信度反映的是信号间的一致性，并不保证判断结果一定正确。

随底层信号变化，置信度可能迅速调整。

Stance Ladder / 行动等级

The briefing's four-word action vocabulary, from calmest to most urgent: ignore, stand-aside, protect, act — describing posture, never a specific trade.

Gives a consistent, plain-word scale for how seriously to treat a given read, so urgency is comparable across different briefings and days.

Categorical ladder (ignore / stand-aside / protect / act)

No up/down reading — this is a vocabulary of posture, not a market direction.

Describes posture and urgency only; it is never a specific instruction to buy or sell any security.

"Act" signals urgency to review, not a predetermined action.

简报使用的四级应对用语，由平静到紧急依次为：忽略、观望、保护、行动——描述的是姿态，而非具体交易指令。

提供统一、直白的分级标准，帮助判断某一判断的紧迫程度，使不同简报、不同日期之间具备可比性。

分类等级（忽略／观望／保护／行动）

无上行／下行判断——这是一套姿态用语，而非市场方向指标。

仅描述姿态与紧迫程度，绝非针对具体证券的买卖指令。

「行动」级别提示应尽快复核，而非预先设定的具体操作。

Surprise Skew / 数据意外偏向

Whether an economic release is likely to land cooler, in line, or hotter than the consensus forecast, based on available leading indicators.

Helps set expectations ahead of a release, so the eventual market reaction is easier to interpret in context.

Categorical skew (cooler / inline / hotter)

Hotter skew — leading indicators point above consensus; may pressure rate-sensitive assets if realized.

Cooler skew — leading indicators point below consensus; may support rate-sensitive assets if realized.

Inline skew — leading indicators broadly agree with consensus.

A pre-release lean based on available indicators, not the actual release result.

Markets can still react unexpectedly even when the release matches the skew.

基于可获得的领先指标，判断某项经济数据公布结果相对市场一致预期偏冷、符合预期，还是偏热。

有助于在数据公布前设定预期，便于结合背景理解公布后的市场反应。

分类偏向（偏冷／符合预期／偏热）

偏热——领先指标指向高于市场一致预期，若兑现可能对利率敏感资产构成压力。

偏冷——领先指标指向低于市场一致预期，若兑现可能对利率敏感资产形成支撑。

符合预期——领先指标与市场一致预期总体相符。

这是公布前基于现有指标的倾向判断，并非实际公布结果。

即便公布结果符合该偏向，市场反应仍可能超出预期。

https://www.bls.gov

https://www.bea.gov

Rebalance Flow / 再平衡资金流

Mechanical buying and selling by index funds and large asset allocators around quarter-end, driven by target weights rather than views on individual securities.

Can temporarily distort price and volume patterns near quarter-end, unrelated to fundamentals or sentiment.

Calendar window (typically final trading days of a quarter)

No directional reading — rebalance flow is a mechanical event, not a market signal.

Flow direction depends on relative performance since the last rebalance, and is not published in advance with certainty.

Effects are typically short-lived and can reverse quickly after the window closes.

指数基金及大型资产配置机构在季末前后，为达到目标权重而进行的机械性买卖，其驱动因素是权重目标而非对个别证券的判断。

可能在季末前后暂时扭曲价格与成交量模式，与基本面或市场情绪无关。

日历窗口（通常为季度最后几个交易日）

无方向性判断——再平衡资金流是机械性事件，而非市场信号。

资金流方向取决于上次再平衡以来的相对表现，事前难以完全确定。

相关影响通常较为短暂，窗口结束后可能迅速逆转。

High-Impact Event / 高影响力事件

A scheduled economic release, central bank decision, or other calendar event judged likely to move markets, shown with its exact time and source.

Lets you plan around known catalysts in advance, rather than being surprised by volatility around a scheduled release.

Scheduled calendar entry with exact time and source

No directional reading — this marks a scheduled event, not a market call.

Being on the calendar means the event is judged likely to matter; it does not predict the direction of any market reaction.

Times and scope can change; always confirm against the original source close to the event.

被判断为可能对市场产生较大影响的经济数据公布、央行决议或其他日历事件，附带具体时间与来源。

附带具体时间与来源的日历事项

Yield Curve (and Inversion) / 收益率曲线（及倒挂）

A chart of government bond yields across maturities; "inverted" means shorter-dated yields sit above longer-dated ones, the reverse of the usual upward-sloping shape.

An inverted curve has historically preceded many past US recessions, though with variable and sometimes long lead times.

Percent yield by maturity; spread in basis points (e.g. 2s10s)

Steepening / normalizing — curve reverting to its usual upward slope.

Inverting further — short-dated yields rising above long-dated yields.

Flat curve — little difference between short- and long-dated yields.

An inversion is a historical statistical association with recessions, not a fixed rule, and the lead time before any downturn has varied widely.

Curve shape can be affected by policy actions and technical factors, not only growth or inflation expectations.

不同期限政府债券收益率的走势图；「倒挂」指短期收益率高于长期收益率，与通常向上倾斜的正常形态相反。

收益率曲线倒挂历史上曾出现在多次美国经济衰退之前，但领先时间长短不一，有时较长。

按期限计的收益率百分比；利差以基点计（如2年10年利差）

陡峭化／正常化——曲线正回归通常的向上倾斜形态。

倒挂加深——短期收益率进一步升至长期收益率之上。

曲线走平——短期与长期收益率差距很小。

曲线倒挂与经济衰退之间是历史上的统计关联，并非确定性规律，且领先时间差异很大。

曲线形态也可能受政策操作及技术性因素影响，并非只反映增长或通胀预期。

Curve Steepening / Flattening / 曲线陡峭化／平坦化

Describes how the gap between short- and long-dated bond yields is changing — widening (steepening) or narrowing (flattening) — and whether it is driven mainly by short or long rates moving.

The combination of steepening/flattening with rising/falling rates carries different economic implications, from growth optimism to recession fears.

Change in spread between two maturities, in basis points

Steepening — the spread between long and short yields is widening.

Flattening — the spread between long and short yields is narrowing.

Little change in the spread over the period observed.

The same steepening or flattening move can carry opposite implications depending on whether short or long rates are driving it.

Short-term noise in bond markets can create moves that reverse quickly.

描述短期与长期债券收益率之差如何变化——扩大（陡峭化）或缩小（平坦化）——以及主要是由短端还是长端利率变动驱动。

陡峭化／平坦化与利率上行／下行的不同组合，通常对应不同的经济含义，从对增长乐观到对衰退的担忧不等。

两个期限之间利差的变化，以基点计

陡峭化——长短端收益率利差正在扩大。

平坦化——长短端收益率利差正在收窄。

观察期内利差变化不大。

相同的陡峭化或平坦化走势，其经济含义可能因驱动力来自短端还是长端而截然不同。

债券市场的短期波动可能导致该走势迅速逆转。

Real Rates / 实际利率

A bond yield after subtracting expected inflation — the actual purchasing-power return an investor expects to earn.

Rising real rates raise the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding or long-duration assets, and have historically pressured segments like gold and long-duration growth stocks.

Percent (nominal yield minus breakeven inflation)

Rising real rates — higher opportunity cost for non-yielding/long-duration assets.

Falling real rates — lower opportunity cost; historically supportive for gold and long-duration assets.

Real rates roughly flat over the period observed.

Derived from market-based inflation expectations, which can themselves be noisy or distorted by liquidity conditions.

Relationships with other assets are historical tendencies, not fixed rules.

扣除预期通胀后的债券收益率——投资者实际预期获得的购买力回报。

实际利率上升会提高持有无息或长久期资产的机会成本，历史上通常对黄金及长久期成长股等板块构成压力。

百分比（名义收益率减去盈亏平衡通胀率）

实际利率上升——无息资产及长久期资产的机会成本上升。

实际利率下降——机会成本降低，历史上对黄金及长久期资产构成支撑。

观察期内实际利率大致持平。

该指标基于市场隐含的通胀预期计算，而通胀预期本身可能受流动性等因素扰动。

与其他资产的关系属于历史经验规律，并非固定法则。

Breakeven Inflation / 盈亏平衡通胀率

The market-implied average inflation rate over a given period, derived from the yield gap between a nominal bond and its inflation-protected equivalent.

Gives a real-time, market-based read on inflation expectations, distinct from surveys or economist forecasts.

Percent (implied annual inflation rate)

Rising breakevens — market pricing in higher future inflation.

Falling breakevens — market pricing in lower future inflation.

Breakevens roughly stable over the period observed.

Reflects market pricing, which can be distorted by liquidity or technical factors in the underlying bond markets, not only genuine inflation views.

A market expectation, not a guarantee of realized future inflation.

市场隐含的某一期限内平均通胀预期，通过普通债券与对应通胀保值债券之间的收益率差计算得出。

提供基于市场定价的实时通胀预期读数，区别于调查或经济学家预测。

百分比（隐含年化通胀率）

盈亏平衡通胀率上升——市场正在计入更高的未来通胀预期。

盈亏平衡通胀率下降——市场正在计入更低的未来通胀预期。

观察期内盈亏平衡通胀率大致稳定。

反映的是市场定价，可能受相关债券市场流动性或技术性因素影响，并非纯粹的通胀判断。

这是市场预期，不保证未来实际通胀会与之一致。

Term Premium / 期限溢价

The extra yield investors demand for holding a longer-maturity bond instead of rolling over a series of shorter-maturity ones, compensating for extra risk and uncertainty over time.

A rising term premium can push long-end yields higher even if growth and inflation expectations are unchanged, a distinct driver worth separating out.

Percent (estimated component of long-dated yields)

Rising term premium — investors demanding more compensation to hold long-dated debt.

Falling term premium — investors demanding less compensation for holding long-dated debt.

Term premium roughly stable over the period observed.

An estimated, model-derived quantity rather than a directly observable market price; different models can disagree.

Should be read alongside growth and inflation expectations, not in isolation.

投资者持有长期债券而非滚动持有一系列短期债券所要求的额外收益，用以补偿更长时间内承担的额外风险与不确定性。

期限溢价上升可能在增长与通胀预期不变的情况下单独推高长端收益率，是值得区分开来看的独立驱动因素。

百分比（长端收益率中的估算组成部分）

期限溢价上升——投资者要求更高补偿以持有长期债券。

期限溢价下降——投资者要求的持有长期债券补偿降低。

观察期内期限溢价大致稳定。

该指标属于模型估算值，而非可直接观察的市场价格，不同模型的估算结果可能存在差异。

建议结合增长与通胀预期一并解读，不宜单独使用。

https://www.federalreserve.gov

Credit Spread / 信用利差

The extra yield a corporate bond pays over a comparable-maturity government bond, compensating investors for default and liquidity risk.

Widening spreads signal rising perceived credit risk and tend to coincide with broader risk-off conditions across markets.

Basis points over a comparable government bond yield

Widening spread — rising perceived credit risk; historically a risk-off signal.

Narrowing spread — falling perceived credit risk; historically a risk-on signal.

Spread roughly stable over the period observed.

Spreads reflect perceived risk at a point in time and can move ahead of, or lag, actual default outcomes.

Liquidity conditions in the bond market itself can widen spreads independent of credit quality.

企业债相对于同期限政府债券支付的额外收益，用于补偿投资者所承担的违约风险与流动性风险。

利差走阔通常意味着市场感知的信用风险上升，往往与更广泛的市场避险情绪同时出现。

相对同期限政府债券收益率的基点利差

利差走阔——市场感知的信用风险上升，历史上属于避险信号。

利差收窄——市场感知的信用风险下降，历史上属于风险偏好信号。

观察期内利差大致稳定。

利差反映的是某一时点市场感知的风险，可能领先或滞后于实际违约情况。

债券市场自身的流动性状况也可能推高利差，未必与信用质量变化相关。

https://fred.stlouisfed.org

High-Yield Spread / 高收益债利差

The credit spread specifically on below-investment-grade ("high-yield" or "junk") corporate bonds — a widely watched gauge of stress in riskier credit markets.

High-yield borrowers are typically the first to feel funding stress, so this spread often widens earlier and more sharply than investment-grade spreads.

Basis points over a comparable government bond yield

Widening spread — rising stress among lower-rated borrowers; a risk-off signal.

Narrowing spread — improving conditions for lower-rated borrowers; a risk-on signal.

Spread roughly stable over the period observed.

Can be more volatile than investment-grade spreads and swing on liquidity as well as credit fundamentals.

A market-based stress gauge, not a direct measure of actual default rates.

专指投资级以下（「高收益」或「垃圾」级）企业债的信用利差——是衡量较高风险信用市场压力的常用指标。

高收益债发行人通常最先感受到融资压力，因此该利差往往比投资级利差更早、更大幅度地走阔。

相对同期限政府债券收益率的基点利差

利差走阔——低评级发行人压力上升，属于避险信号。

利差收窄——低评级发行人状况改善，属于风险偏好信号。

观察期内利差大致稳定。

波动性通常高于投资级利差，除信用基本面外也会受流动性因素影响。

这是基于市场定价的压力指标，并非实际违约率的直接度量。

Dollar Index / 美元指数

A measure of the US dollar's value against a basket of major foreign currencies, widely used as a shorthand for overall dollar strength or weakness.

A stronger dollar tends to tighten global financial conditions and can pressure commodities and emerging markets; a weaker dollar tends to ease them.

Index level (relative, no fixed unit)

Rising — dollar strengthening; historically a headwind for commodities and EM assets.

Falling — dollar weakening; historically supportive for commodities and EM assets.

Index roughly flat over the period observed.

The basket is weighted toward a handful of major currencies and does not capture every bilateral dollar relationship.

Relationships with other assets are historical tendencies, not fixed rules.

衡量美元相对一篮子主要外币价值的指标，常被用作衡量美元整体强弱的简便参考。

美元走强通常会收紧全球金融条件，并对大宗商品及新兴市场构成压力；美元走弱则通常起到缓解作用。

指数点位（相对值，无固定单位）

上升——美元走强，历史上对大宗商品及新兴市场资产构成逆风。

下降——美元走弱，历史上对大宗商品及新兴市场资产构成支撑。

观察期内指数大致持平。

该指数篮子权重集中于少数主要货币，无法反映美元对所有货币的双边情况。

与其他资产的关系属于历史经验规律，并非固定法则。

VIX / VIX恐慌指数

An index measuring the market's expectation of S&P 500 volatility over the next 30 days, derived from options prices; often called the "fear gauge."

Sharp VIX spikes typically coincide with sudden risk-off moves, while sustained low levels typically coincide with calmer, trending markets.

Index level (annualized implied volatility percentage)

Rising VIX — rising expected volatility; often coincides with market stress.

Falling VIX — falling expected volatility; often coincides with calmer markets.

VIX roughly stable over the period observed.

Measures expected future volatility implied by options, not volatility that has already occurred.

Extended low readings do not by themselves predict when volatility will rise.

基于期权价格计算的标普500未来30天预期波动率指数，常被称为「恐慌指数」。

VIX急剧飙升通常与突发性避险行情同时出现，而长期维持低位则通常对应较为平稳、趋势明确的市场。

指数点位（年化隐含波动率百分比）

VIX上升——预期波动率上升，通常与市场压力同时出现。

VIX下降——预期波动率下降，通常与市场趋于平稳同时出现。

观察期内VIX大致稳定。

衡量的是期权隐含的未来预期波动率，而非已经实现的历史波动率。

Volatility Regime / 波动率环境

A broader read of the current volatility environment, including whether implied volatility for near-term options sits above or below longer-dated options.

An inverted term structure (near-term volatility above longer-term) often signals acute near-term stress rather than a durable volatility regime shift.

Categorical / relative comparison across option expirations

Elevated / inverted — near-term stress dominating; historically a caution signal.

Low / normal upward-sloping — calmer, more typical volatility conditions.

Volatility term structure roughly flat or transitional.

Term-structure inversions can resolve quickly once the triggering event passes.

Describes options-market pricing, not a forecast of realized future volatility.

对当前波动率环境的更全面判断，包括近月期权隐含波动率是否高于或低于远月期权。

波动率期限结构倒挂（近月高于远月）通常意味着短期急性压力，而非波动率环境的持久性转变。

分类／不同到期期权之间的相对比较

偏高／倒挂——近期压力主导，历史上属于谨慎信号。

偏低／正常向上倾斜——波动率环境更为平稳、典型。

波动率期限结构大致走平或处于过渡期。

期限结构倒挂在触发事件消退后往往会较快恢复正常。

反映的是期权市场定价，不是对未来实际波动率的预测。

Risk-On / Risk-Off / 风险偏好／避险

A shorthand for whether investors are broadly favouring riskier assets (risk-on) or broadly seeking safety (risk-off) across markets at the same time.

Recognizing the prevailing risk sentiment helps explain why many unrelated assets can move together on the same day.

Categorical (risk-on / risk-off / mixed)

Risk-on — riskier assets broadly outperforming safer ones.

Risk-off — safer assets broadly outperforming riskier ones.

Mixed — no clear broad-based sentiment across asset classes.

A descriptive label for observed cross-asset behaviour, not a predictive signal.

Individual assets can still diverge from the broad label for idiosyncratic reasons.

简明描述投资者在同一时期是普遍偏向风险资产（风险偏好）还是普遍寻求避险资产（避险）的用语。

识别当前主导的风险情绪，有助于理解为何看似无关的多类资产会在同一天同向变动。

分类（风险偏好／避险／混合）

风险偏好——风险资产整体跑赢避险资产。

避险——避险资产整体跑赢风险资产。

混合——各类资产之间未呈现明确的整体情绪方向。

这是对已观察到的跨资产行为的描述性标签，并非预测性信号。

个别资产仍可能因自身特殊原因而偏离整体标签所描述的方向。

Flight to Quality / 避险资金流

A shift of investor capital into perceived safer assets — such as government bonds, the dollar, or gold — typically during periods of market stress.

Recognizing a flight-to-quality move helps explain simultaneous declines in risk assets alongside rallies in traditional safe havens.

Descriptive / observed cross-asset pattern

No single up/down reading — this describes a pattern of capital movement, most relevant during risk-off episodes.

Which assets act as "safe havens" can shift over time and across episodes.

A descriptive pattern, not a rule that safe havens always rise during stress.

投资者资金转向被认为更安全的资产（如政府债券、美元或黄金），通常发生在市场承压时期。

识别避险资金流有助于理解为何风险资产下跌的同时，传统避险资产往往同步上涨。

描述性／观察到的跨资产模式

无单一上行／下行判断——该指标描述的是资金流动模式，在避险时期最具参考意义。

被视为「避险资产」的具体品种可能随时间及具体事件而变化。

这是描述性模式，并不意味着避险资产在每次压力事件中都必然上涨。

Drawdown / 回撤

The percentage decline from a recent peak value to a subsequent trough, a standard way to measure the depth of a market or portfolio decline.

Drawdown size is a plain, comparable way to gauge how severe a given pullback has been relative to history.

Percent decline from peak

No single up/down reading — drawdown is a backward-looking measure of decline already observed, reported as a magnitude.

Measures a decline that has already happened; it does not predict whether the decline will deepen or recover.

Comparisons across periods should account for differences in overall volatility.

从近期高点回落至后续低点的百分比跌幅，是衡量市场或组合下跌深度的常用方式。

回撤幅度是衡量本轮下跌相对历史严重程度的直观、可比较的方式。

相对高点的百分比跌幅

无单一上行／下行判断——回撤是对已发生跌幅的回顾性衡量，以幅度形式呈现。

衡量的是已经发生的跌幅，不能预测跌幅是会进一步加深还是随后回升。

不同时期的回撤比较应考虑整体波动率水平的差异。

Correlation Regime / 相关性环境

How closely different assets or stocks are moving together, and whether that relationship is currently tighter (higher correlation) or looser (lower correlation) than usual.

Rising correlations reduce the benefit of diversification, since more assets tend to move together during periods of stress.

Correlation coefficient or relative descriptor (high / normal / low)

Rising correlation — assets moving more together; diversification benefit reduced.

Falling correlation — assets moving more independently; diversification benefit improved.

Correlation roughly typical for the period observed.

Correlation is measured over a historical window and can shift quickly around major market events.

High correlation does not itself indicate direction — only that assets are moving together.

不同资产或个股之间同向变动的紧密程度，以及当前该相关性相较平常是偏高还是偏低。

相关性上升会削弱分散配置的效果，因为压力时期更多资产往往同向变动。

相关系数或相对描述（高／正常／低）

相关性上升——资产同向变动增多，分散配置效果减弱。

相关性下降——资产变动更趋独立，分散配置效果提升。

观察期内相关性大致处于常态水平。

相关性基于历史窗口计算，在重大市场事件前后可能迅速变化。

相关性偏高本身并不能说明方向，只说明资产之间同向变动的程度。

Market Breadth / 市场广度

A measure of how many individual stocks are participating in a market move, as opposed to the move being driven by a small number of large stocks.

Narrow breadth behind a rising index is a classic warning sign that a rally may be more fragile than the headline number suggests.

Descriptive / ratio-based (e.g. advancing vs declining issues)

Broadening — more stocks participating in the move; historically a healthier signal.

Narrowing — fewer stocks participating; historically a warning sign.

Breadth roughly stable over the period observed.

Narrow breadth does not by itself predict when or whether a reversal will occur.

Different breadth measures (advance-decline, new highs/lows, cap-weighted vs equal-weighted) can occasionally disagree.

衡量有多少只个股参与本轮市场行情，而非行情主要由少数大盘股驱动。

指数上涨但广度狭窄是典型警示信号，说明涨势可能比指数表面表现更脆弱。

描述性／基于比率（如上涨与下跌家数对比）

广度扩大——更多个股参与本轮行情，历史上属于更健康的信号。
