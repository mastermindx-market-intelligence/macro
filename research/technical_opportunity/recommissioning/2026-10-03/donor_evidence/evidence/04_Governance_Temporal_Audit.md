### 6.1 Measurement principles to carry into W3

**Separate three clocks and two kinds of N.** A signal bar's time, its information-availability time and its first feasible execution time are distinct. Trial N counts configurations/search decisions; effective sample N reflects dependent episodes/dates/months. One must never substitute for the other. TrialLedger defaults to literal distinct configuration count, has no automatic correlation discount, and declared budget is a floor; repeated aliases must be normalized before defining the family, not used afterward to erase attempted trials. [TRIAL]

**Use the ruler that matches the claim.** Setup Species's entry constitution measures stopped, dead-money, cushioned and clean-liftoff states with one declared primary class. It requires all-fire denominators, recall, per-name prevalence, time/name robustness, next-bar fills and explicit wait premiums. It forbids interpreting round-trip equity-curve metrics or return precision alone as proof of better entry quality. The old numerical thresholds and 21/126-day barriers are historical species law; TOI's additional activation/remaining-path heads need their own preregistered estimands, not a claim that those old numbers validate every objective. [SSPLAN] [BOTTOM]

**Confirmation is a paired intervention.** Preserve the raw trigger, eventual confirmation status/time and both feasible fills for the same episode. Report failures and never-confirmed cases as well as survivors, plus the price/lead/path cost of waiting. Historical bottom work measured an oracle selection advantage that largely disappeared after actual confirmation wait; the August-6 known-date repair preserved that conclusion but changed absolute rates. Do not copy earlier absolute numbers without their vintage. [BOTTOM]

**Do not turn labels into features.** Durable-bottom troughs and forward clean-hold patterns are hindsight evaluation labels. They may define test outcomes, never causal admission, trigger or remaining-opportunity inputs. Local onset-fingerprint kills show why a label containing “hold” makes hold-feature success tautological. [BOTTOM] [DNR]

**Conditioning must earn both estimability and increment.** The existing meter requires ≥20% stamped coverage, ≥2 observed states and ≥12 distinct months in the thinnest state for its axis-level estimability verdict. Setup Species learned-cell law additionally freezes ≤2 axes/≤6 cells and needs ≥20 distinct regime episodes, ≥5 in each time half, with the stated purge separation; live-stamped rows own learned authority. An axis present on all names at a date cannot itself rank names that date. A calibrated main effect is not evidence for the interaction. [REGIMECOV] [SSPLAN]

**Calibration helpers are already built, but defaults need honest use.** `reliability_curve()`, `brier_decomposition()`, `era_split_stability()` and `eb_shrink()` exist. Wilson intervals on repeated overlapping cross-sectional rows understate uncertainty; use whole-date/block inference. `eb_shrink()` defaults to fitting its prior on the same cells and explicitly labels this in-sample: train-only or frozen priors are required for honest untouched claims. Partial pooling reduces variance; it does not create previously unobserved regimes. [STATS]

**Avoid a source sign trap.** The Track-E PTT description defines `tdt` as trough offset, negative meaning the trough came before the signal. Its later sentence calling −2 “before trough” is inconsistent with that definition. The DNR F2/F4 rows correctly treat −9 as a later post-trough confirmer than −2. TOI should define the signed distance once in the actual evaluation contract and include a deterministic semantic example, rather than inherit inconsistent prose. [TRACK] [DNR]

**No generic promotion shortcut.** Research Factory `promote_eligible` means eligible to propose a new program ruling; challenger output is advisory and cannot select a terminal/promotion branch. Signal Foundry retrospectives are screening evidence and only its forward-after-registration evidence may support promotion. TOI's statistical reports are zero-authority until the relevant existing product owner admits a narrowly scoped consumer. [RF] [SF]
