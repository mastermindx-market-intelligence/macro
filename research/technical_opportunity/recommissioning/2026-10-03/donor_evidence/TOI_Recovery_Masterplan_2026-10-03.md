# B. Revised technical opportunity thesis

**Technical Opportunity Intelligence estimates the observable state and possible future paths of a specified market process, at a specified information time, for a specified decision.**

An opportunity is not an indicator count. It is a conditional case: an identifiable instrument and mechanism, a currently observable phase, boundaries or conditions that would activate or invalidate the case, a decision horizon, and an explicit statement of uncertainty about its future path.

The user-facing questions are:

1. What process is observable now?
2. What is its phase, and how fresh is that observation?
3. What must happen next, and what would falsify the case?
4. What would waiting for additional confirmation cost?
5. From the present decision point, what favorable and adverse paths remain plausible?
6. Which action, if any, has actually earned authority in this product?

“Causal” in this paper primarily means **available from the permitted observed prefix**. It does not assert that a sensor has been identified as an economic cause of returns. Compression, trend, volume and peer behavior can reflect a common process; different transformations of the same path may aid a restricted predictor without constituting independent causal evidence.

Three scientific claims must stay separate:

| Claim | Example | Necessary comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Descriptive validity | A range has narrowed relative to its own past, and price is near a previously fixed boundary | Exact implementation and prefix/time invariance |
| Predictive increment | The state improves activation or failure forecasts | Strong simpler predictors, chronological held-out scores and dependence-aware uncertainty |
| Decision value | Waiting, admitting, monitoring or warning improves the actual job | Original eligible cohort, feasible timing, missed opportunities, costs and consumer-specific utility |

This separation permits useful nulls. A compression descriptor may improve preparation while adding no directional information. A failure warning may improve an existing holder's response while never justifying a short. A complex pattern may be retired as a predictor while its geometric explanation remains helpful.

## B1. Why compression remains first

Compression has a clear observation-to-event structure: quiet state, persistent formation, a fixed reference range, a directional break, optional confirmation, extension, invalidation and failure. Existing code and S16 provide a meaningful incumbent. Those qualities make it an efficient falsification and integration test.

The literature gives no warrant to call a quiet market “stored energy about to explode.” Volatility persistence explicitly includes quiet periods remaining quiet. The first hypothesis must therefore compare future range/volatility to a persistence baseline. Direction after a break and the economics of waiting are separate tests. [L11]

Retain the W0 complete vertical: upward and downward compression release across completed W/D/4H. Downward states initially mean warning, avoidance or deterioration context; they do not create short authority. Completed Monthly is structural context. Tactical 5m events remain owned by Live Entry Radar. A Daily-only preliminary study could be proposed as a separate phase amendment, but it must not silently satisfy the original complete-vertical acceptance gate.

They do not estimate effective independent tests or empirical sample size. Most formula documentation proves how something is calculated, not whether it predicts returns. [W1DATA]

Use five relationship labels:

- **Alias:** same construction under the same inputs, parameters, units and clock.
- **Exact algebraic equivalence:** equality proved under explicit assumptions.
- **Overlapping sensor:** related aspects of a path without equality.
- **Shared input:** common price, volume, membership or context observations.
- **Shared job:** different methods proposed for the same decision.

For example, with identical centers, BB-inside-KC reduces to a comparison of standard deviation and ATR. That identity does not make ATR, narrow-range bars, width percentile and inside-bar nesting identical. Nor do they become independent bullish votes. A restricted model may benefit from a ratio or threshold representation; an incremental-information claim must still compare against its component measurements.

# G. Opportunity lifecycle model

## G1. Two lifecycles and one observation history

The **scientific lifecycle** remains the existing species/experiment progression—PHASE0, ACCRUING, VALIDATED, FALSIFIED or RETIRED. The **opportunity lifecycle** describes a particular occurrence. CONFIRMED as an occurrence never means scientifically validated.

A strict one-dimensional chain is insufficient. A gap can trigger an already extended case; an occurrence can meet a geometric target and later reverse; a confirmed observation can later fail. The proposed W0 clarification is to retain the requested labels as a deterministic presentation over three owned fields:

- **Stage:** FORMING, ARMED, TRIGGERED, CONFIRMED.
- **Maturity observations:** extension/target/age measures and, when admitted, a calibrated exhaustion estimate.
- **Disposition:** OPEN, INVALIDATED, FAILED, EXPIRED, SUPERSEDED or DATA_UNRESOLVED.

This is a proposed semantic extension for owner review, not a new persisted lifecycle database. Existing occurrence/forward-episode artifacts carry the facts. # H. Confirmation-cost framework

## H1. Separate information gain from waiting policy value

There are two legitimate questions:

1. **At the same information time**, does the added observation improve a forecast beyond the already known state?
2. **From the original opportunity cohort**, does a policy that waits improve the decision after delay, price paid and missed cases?

A confirmed subset can have a higher success rate because it excludes early failures, is closer to target or survives longer. That conditional probability can be useful, but it is not the value of waiting. For a hypothetical 100 triggers with 50 eventual successes, suppose 40 confirm and 30 of those succeed. The confirmed hit rate is 75%, yet the filter retains only 60% of the original successful cases. No economic conclusion follows without entry prices, adverse paths, costs and the 60 unconfirmed cases.

S16's existing evidence requires special care. Its registry describes direction-and-volume-confirmed release, while the detector and both enumerators include all FIRED_UP onsets and keep volume as a separate diagnostic. Only 46.7% of the deep sample and 50.1% of the basket sample are volume confirmed in the historical report. The registered 12-trial history remains. Reconcile the verbal phenotype and actual population before inheriting any evidence; do not retroactively filter historical results. [S16], [SSQ]

## H2. Frozen policies

The first empirical envelope uses only **post-trigger policies**: feasible next opportunity after the completed trigger; trigger-time participation filter with the same timing; and one additional completed-bar hold, with feasible subsequent execution. No FORMING/ARMED entry policy is introduced. Retest and longer waits are later, separately counted amendments if justified; they are not free variants.

For occurrence i, direction d in {+1,−1}, trigger reference time τ₀ and policy observation τπ, record:

\[
\Delta p_{i,\pi}=d\frac{P^{fill}_{i,\pi}-P^{fill}_{i,0}}{ATR_{i,\tau_0}},
\qquad
\Delta t_{i,\pi}=\tau^{fill}_{i,\pi}-\tau^{fill}_{i,0}.
\]

ATR is frozen at the original trigger for this comparison. Report elapsed clock time, trading minutes and construction-bar count separately. A changed normalization must not hide the price paid.

# I. Remaining opportunity and chase

## I1. Define the future from the current decision point

At an admissible landmark t, let Pₜ be current reference price, Aₜ a strictly past-known positive scale, d the case direction, and T the fixed terminal horizon. Define nonnegative remaining excursions, including the current point:

\[
R^+_{t,T}=\max_{u\in[t,T]}\left[d(P_u-P_t)/A_t\right],\qquad
R^-_{t,T}=\max_{u\in[t,T]}\left[-d(P_u-P_t)/A_t\right].
\]

For executable policy evaluation, replace reference prices with the admitted fill/mark convention and cost treatment. Daily OHLC labels describe extrema within their resolution; intrabar order may be unknown. For live prediction, R⁺ and R⁻ are unknown future labels. The model estimates distributions or quantiles from only information available at t.

Past excursion is a separate observation:

\[
M^+_{\tau_0,t}=\max_{u\in[\tau_0,t]}d(P_u-P_{\tau_0})/A_{\tau_0}.
\]

It is not valid to subtract past MFE from predicted total MFE and call the remainder a fixed pool of opportunity. Future paths can retrace, consolidate or make a new expansion; the present price and conditional state matter. A “percent consumed” ratio can exceed 100%, have a near-zero denominator or become unstable. It must not be presented as a probability.

## I2. Recommended presentation and estimation

Initially show observed distance from origin, trigger and invalidation; age; past favorable/adverse excursion; current liquidity; and remaining fixed horizon. After validation, add median and interval/quantile estimates for remaining favorable and adverse paths, plus target-before-invalidation and time-to-event probabilities.

Separate three different intervals:

- A **predictive interval** describes dispersion of future paths.
- An **uncertainty interval** describes uncertainty about an estimated quantity such as a mean probability.
- A **geometric range/target** follows a construction rule and may have no empirical calibration.

The first estimator should be transparent: a train-only conditional distribution or simple regularized model using frozen state, age, extension, volatility and location. Matched analogs must include failed, expired and stagnant paths from the appropriate risk set. Matching on eventual success, final pattern shape, future regime or the completed path is prohibited.

Compare remaining-path forecasts against lifecycle/age-only and simple current-state baselines. Display the unconditional-at-clock distribution as a nonselectable descriptive reference; promoting it to a third fitted competitor expands the manifest. Use proper scores for probabilities and distributions, quantile loss, calibration by horizon and actual consumer utility. Gneiting–Raftery's proper-scoring framework supports truthful probabilistic evaluation; it does not establish a trading edge. [SCORING]

Adaptive conformal methods may later help monitor coverage under drift, but long-run coverage is not guaranteed conditional accuracy in a rare regime or each recent window. Updates must wait until labels mature. Do not use future outcomes to calibrate today's interval. [CONFORMAL]

## I3. Chase and exhaustion are conditional decision states

“Chase” should describe a case where current feasible reward/risk, uncertainty or remaining time has deteriorated relative to the intended decision, not merely a high RSI or strong past return. A large extension can accompany continued momentum; an apparently cheap pullback can invalidate the case.

A future exhaustion claim needs an explicit decision threshold and evidence—for example, the estimated chance of further useful favorable excursion before invalidation is too low for a specific monitoring policy. Until that consumer is admitted, display facts such as “extended,” “target reached,” “little horizon remains” or “estimate unavailable.” Avoid a falsely precise universal opportunity-consumed percentage.

“Dead money” needs a frozen economic or species-native definition before outcomes. It is not whatever threshold later creates a favorable comparison. Administrative censoring, trading suspension, data loss, expiry and a genuine no-progress event remain distinguishable.

## J2. First modeling choice

Use discrete-time **competing-risk hazards** as the initial architecture for activation and first terminal events. A small multinomial or cause-specific construction with coherent cumulative-incidence assembly is easier to audit than separate binary heads that can imply impossible totals. Age can enter explicitly, giving a semi-Markov interpretation where transition probability depends on time already spent in the state.

If hₖ(j|x) is the hazard for first event k in interval j and S(j−1|x) the probability of remaining at risk:

\[
F_k(H|x)=\sum_{j=1}^{H}S(j-1|x)h_k(j|x),\qquad
S(j|x)=S(j-1|x)\left[1-\sum_k h_k(j|x)\right].
\]

Require nonnegative hazards with sum no greater than one. This gives coherent first-event probabilities. Marginal excursion quantiles are separate targets and do not by themselves define a full joint path distribution. State that limitation rather than manufacturing a simulated joint process.

Landmarking uses the current eligible risk set and updated covariates without pretending a later snapshot existed at origin. This is a transfer of established statistical methodology, not published validation in this market. [MULTISTATE], [LANDMARK]

## J3. More complex models and honest abstention

Online change-point and filtered state-space models can be later challengers for regime/state uncertainty.
