The original report recommends explicit occurrence geometry and separately qualified forecasts, not a larger weighted confluence score. The additional report preserves the same two discovery queues and a retained history. That is the controlling product direction for this first vertical. Do not let a catalog, a calibration grid, a CI repair or a social-card fix replace that user job. ## 3. First useful release: one complete descriptive journey

### A. Keep the first slice narrow in breadth, complete in behavior

Use the existing compression/release focus and a single explicitly qualified Daily construction first. Completed Weekly context appears only when its own use is qualified. This does not choose an unapproved detector formula or silently replace the proposed research population. Intraday, standalone Weekly, alternate sensors and additional species remain held rather than becoming first-release requirements.

The descriptive pilot and the historical research population are separate scopes. A bounded current population may be considered only with explicit per-use admission and a visible coverage label. It cannot substitute for a historical whole-US denominator or retrospectively cure survivorship. The data owner still must specify listing/security/venue eligibility, exclusions, missingness and the population to which every claim applies.

### B. Two queues, not one confidence leaderboard

**Preparing — FORMING / ARMED:** show the process, original trigger condition, distance to it, age, next required observation and invalidation. Near a trigger does not mean ready to enter.

**Active — TRIGGERED / CONFIRMED:** show when the original transition became known, what confirmation actually occurred, movement and time since that trigger, observed extension, strongest contradiction and the status of any remaining-path estimate.

Retain invalidated, expired, corrected and data-unresolved cases in history/monitoring. Do not make the past look cleaner by deleting failures. Default to stable neutral ordering and explicit user filters; do not hide a new ranking inside presentation order.

### C. First glance should answer six questions

| User question | First-glance content | Deeper evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Which instrument? | Ticker, company, listing and clock | Security/listing lineage and eligible universe scope |
| What process and phase? | Named construction and phase, with maturity and validity separately stated | Formula/source version and observed state history |
| Why now? | The latest material observation or transition and its first-known time | Original event snapshot and any later correction |
| What needs to happen next? | One explicit state-changing observation | Its exact threshold, finality and confirmation rule |
| What invalidates this case? | The original invalidation condition | Frozen geometry and distinct failure/expiry/censor rules |
| How current is this, and what remains? | Freshness, observed extension and a labeled estimate or unavailable reason | Source vintage, forecast horizon, uncertainty, support and qualification |

These are information-hierarchy choices, not permission to introduce a new canonical object store. The read model joins the existing occurrence, species, source, identity and evidence owners.

### D. The detail view must preserve the same case

Selection opens the exact listing and construction that the queue described. The chart shows original trigger/invalidation geometry and first-known timestamps separately from the latest chart rendering. A later breakout must not reset an already-triggered occurrence's age or erase its prior failure. A price correction must disclose what changed.

Keep phase, maturity and validity separate: a confirmed setup can be extended; an early setup can be invalid; an unavailable source is not a bearish outcome. Keep cross-clock contradictions as parallel facts, such as Weekly trend intact while the Daily occurrence has failed. Do not convert disagreements into correlated votes.

The Terminal handoff preserves occurrence/listing identity and the exact recipe. Switching to incompatible extended-hours or coarse fallback bars must produce an explicit incompatibility or obtain the matching object; it cannot quietly retain an RTH forecast over a different chart. Returning to discovery should preserve the user's selection and filter context. Use existing navigation, components, design tokens, chart and Alert Center owners.

### E. Forecasts must be earned separately

Before qualification, show observed geometry, age, distance/extension and the declared confirmation price/time cost. Show **not yet calibrated** for activation or remaining-path forecasts. Distance to a level is not probability, a barrier-hit rate is not structural-fakeout risk, and extension is not a percentage of the future opportunity already consumed.

After qualification, each predictive head identifies its estimand, horizon, source/recipe, calibration vintage, support and uncertainty. Activation, conditional path, adverse excursion and structural failure need not qualify together. A null or inconclusive study can leave a useful descriptive product intact; it must not trigger an unregistered search for a more attractive answer.

### Native accounting constraints now tested

**One family can preserve the intended count without a new ledger.** On the unchanged native API, 22 itemized synthetic configs plus a declared floor of 22 yields 22, not 44. Identical reruns remain idempotent after reopening the ledger. The separate synthetic historical family retains 12 without being added to the new family. Context-manager and decorator registration write the budget before the body when passed the same explicit ledger. These are API observations from this continuation, not new real registrations.

**Search-bearing identity must be inside the hashed config.** Native identity hashes the family and canonical config. The separate `source` and `info_cutoff` arguments are metadata. The test demonstrated that changing only those metadata fields with an identical config is deduplicated. Accordingly, the adopted configuration/manifest binding must include all material choices that distinguish a searched construction: native formula/code version, selected training/data cuts and eligible population, basis/clock, policy terminal/fill/cost convention, features, model settings and any searched endpoint variants. Otherwise a new search could be mislabeled a rerun.

This does not mean every new observation under a fixed prospective protocol is a new searched configuration. Scheduled accrual and prespecified evaluation looks belong to the accepted update/look policy. Outcome-driven tuning or a changed searched recipe must not hide there. Use the existing Evaluation and TrialLedger owners to make that distinction; do not create a second counter or charge all calendar updates as experiments.

**A positive effective count is not admission.** An empty native ledger reports effective N=1 while literal count and declared budget are zero and no file exists. Never use `effective_n()>0` as proof of preregistration. Actual accepted identities, source/holdout receipts and the admitted manifest remain the gates. The native floor is intentional numerical behavior, not a proposed ledger bug to rewrite.

## 6. Acceptance scenarios for the first vertical

The release review should exercise a developing case that never triggers; a newly triggered case; a confirmed but extended case; an invalidated original occurrence followed by another break; an expired case; missing/stale/partial data; a source correction or split; and conflicting/incompatible clocks. The point is a complete decision journey, not eight attractive cards.

For each, verify that a user can correctly identify phase, next condition, original invalidation, information clock and whether any number is observed or predicted. Check return navigation and monitoring retention, keyboard access, mobile layout, both themes and EN/ZH. Use the existing native evidence capture/receipt plane. Measure time to understand a case, duplicate/stale alerts and invalidation visibility against the incumbent workflow, but do not claim an improvement before measuring it. Forecast quality and usability are separate acceptance questions.

No new Paper/Figma design or real TOI screen was produced in this continuation. The information hierarchy above is a steering specification grounded in the supplied reports, not screenshot evidence or a completed usability study.
