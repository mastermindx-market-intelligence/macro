## 4. W3 release decision

The next research owner should be able to answer, before reading outcomes:

**What exactly is the first question?** Whether causally observed compression improves activation/path knowledge beyond simple geometry, and whether confirmation or qualified higher-horizon context improves the usable opportunity frontier.

**What is fixed?** Population, source/basis/clock, scientific identity, original geometry, lookbacks, event/label definitions, model specifications, confirmation policies, comparator set, folds, holdout and complete search history.

**What can stop the run?** Source/custody ambiguity, identity or rights failure, incorrect finality/basis, future-input leakage, unresolved DNR/S16 collision, incomplete registration, and insufficient data support.

**What is a successful return?** A scientifically readable result—positive, null, or inconclusive—with enough causal and operating evidence to decide the next bounded build. It is not a forced positive technical signal.

**Adopt:** the causal opportunity-process thesis; separate estimands; original-occurrence geometry; confirmation-cost and remaining-distribution frameworks; independent horizon states; failure retention; existing-owner reuse; Daily-first conditional sequencing.
