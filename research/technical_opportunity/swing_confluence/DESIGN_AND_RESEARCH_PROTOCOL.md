**Operation:** swing-confluence-lab-20261007-c1-001. **Parent:** existing Technical Opportunity Intelligence, with Temporal Grain and Live Entry Radar as sibling owners. **State:** proposed research consumer; no data admission, no registered outcome read, no fitted rule, no production authority.

## Product decision

Reuse Strategy Lab (`engine/lab.py`) and its canonical indicator functions. Put saved recipes, experiment comparison and chart-replay navigation in the existing lab/product surfaces; do not create another laboratory service, collector, calendar, registry, evaluator, chart renderer, lifecycle or signal store. Signal Lab's presentation is not evidence that this complete multi-timeframe experiment path is already built.

The first executable slice is deliberately a **plan compiler/preflight**, not a backtester. A recipe change must reproducibly change the candidate matrix and planned-look accounting. References in a recipe are evidence pointers, never authentication or an owner admission. The executable exposes no outcome-run command and writes no TrialLedger or market data.

## Two independent questions

**Leader pullback:** Among names that were RS/continuation leaders at the decision time, does completed higher-timeframe pullback stabilization plus a lower-timeframe reclaim improve a short swing entry versus the same setup without that timing condition?

**Range reversal:** Among names classified as range-like using prior observations only, does a range-low rejection plus a lower-timeframe reclaim improve a short swing entry versus simple range proximity and matched entry times?

A company in an economically cyclical industry is not automatically a stock in a stationary oscillating range. Do not pool the two populations, use current leaders to select historical examples, select a per-name winning timeframe from historical returns, or rename killed Personality/Trend Persistence timers.

## Saved recipe requirements

The included recipe is an outcome-blind draft search plan. It proposes six trigger/setup pairs, two kernel-memory controls, three mechanism bundles, five holding rulers, two exits, three one-way cost stresses and four baselines. The compiler's counts are candidate counts, **not backtests**. Exact scientific species, causal feature definitions/parameters, dataset dates, folds, power/maturity targets, fill contract and canonical trial registration are prerequisites, not silently filled assumptions.

Initial pairs: 15/60, 30/120, 30/180, 30/130, 30/195 and 60/240 minutes. The 30/120 and 30/180 pairs directly represent the Chairman's question; none is privileged as a winner. Equal-session partitions are artifact controls. Regular-session duration is supplied as an arithmetic example (390 minutes), not computed by a new calendar. Early closes, DST, vendor bar boundaries and actual publication delays require existing owner receipts.

The two memory controls must ultimately use the Temporal Grain owner's definitions: fixed bar count versus a fixed elapsed-time kernel. They are not interchangeable indicator settings. For example, 14 thirty-minute bars and 14 three-hour bars represent different observed spans; elapsed-memory matching must account for shortened session-end bars and distinguish trading time from wall-clock time. The planner does not implement these kernels.

Mechanism bundles: structure alone; structure plus one momentum family; structure plus participation. Momentum variants are correlated alternatives, not independent votes. More indicators are admitted only as separately counted refinements under the existing trial family.

## Evaluation contract to freeze before outcome access

- Universe and leader/range classification use point-in-time membership, identity and trailing-only information; delistings, ticker reuse, unavailable names and historical liquidity denominators stay visible.
- Every feature joins by completed bar end and actual available-at time. No current daily/high-timeframe close, centered smoother, hindsight ZigZag, future-confirmed pivot or revised economic value may become a past feature. Pivot extrema may be evaluation labels only, with their later resolution time retained.
- Execution uses the next eligible observable price after confirmation and source latency. Model spread/slippage, gap-through stops, ambiguous intrabar stop/target ordering, overnight exposure and earnings; no same-close fill assumption without executable evidence.
- Holding rulers: 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10 trading sessions, each registered. Compare fixed holding with causal invalidation plus the same time cap. Select only on training/validation; do not issue off-horizon verdicts.
- Define the opportunity episode independently of the candidate clock through the existing episode owner. Keep the eligible denominator fixed when measuring entry improvement. Keep setup-only, trigger-only, matched random and fixed-multiscale/no-selector controls. Compare entry improvement within the same eligible opportunity, including the cost of waiting and missed moves.
- Primary economics: net paired expectancy and incremental utility. Also report maximum adverse/favorable excursion, false starts, missed opportunities, confirmation delay, turnover, drawdown and concentration. Hindsight proximity to a local low is diagnostic, not the optimization target.
- Training/validation/untouched test periods and issuer-disjoint confirmation are fixed before reveal. Purge overlapping holding labels and embargo boundaries; cluster/block by calendar time and account for common sector/event shocks. Do not treat many stocks on one FOMC date as independent events.
- All looks, failed variants, chart-guided revisions and held-out peeks are accounted through TrialLedger/Evaluation OS. A new recipe hash never resets the family budget. Conservative planned comparison counts are not a substitute for the owner's realized-look accounting or multiplicity adjustment.
- A null, insufficient sample, lack of transfer to new names, or defeat by a simple baseline is a valid finding. Promotion requires independent review, cost robustness, untouched evidence, prospective zero-authority shadow and separate product acceptance.

## Rates and event conditioning

Study calendar exposure and realized surprises separately. A known FOMC/CPI/payroll/auction date may be used before release; its surprise, auction tail or post-event rate response may only be used after actual availability. Auction announcement, bidding/result time and settlement are distinct. Without consensus or when-issued evidence, label the feature calendar-only, not a measured surprise.

Test rates as a conditional effect modifier, not an automatic entry gate: prior 2y/10y yield changes, curve shape and available real-yield/risk measures; training-only stock-rate sensitivity controlling market and sector; pre/post-release behavior; and overlapping earnings or macro events. Ask whether rates/events explain incremental entry utility beyond the technical baseline, not whether two hindsight charts look similar. Reuse existing rate/release/auction owners and vintage records. No new macro-calendar store.
