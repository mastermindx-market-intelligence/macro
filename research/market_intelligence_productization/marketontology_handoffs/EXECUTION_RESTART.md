## Outcome and moat

A researcher should start at an ordinary Mastermind entry point, understand a real
market change, follow an evidence-backed transmission to relevant themes and
securities, inspect alternatives and uncertainty, save a thesis, and later see
what changed in the thesis or monitored holdings. The long-term advantage is a
revision-aware evidence/intelligence/research loop with observable learning, not
an impressive graph, another dashboard directory or a pile of research reports.

Parity remains every admitted lawful capability with explicit disposition. The
130-row F00C scope and the separate retained historical-source admission remain
separate. Priorities sequence the full scope; they do not discard inconvenient
rows. New competitor findings go through existing admission, not denominator drift.

## Throughput and review policy

Two product delivery trains are an initial scheduling target, not a global runtime
capacity setting. Within each train, parallelize only path/contract-disjoint units
whose returned work can actually be integrated. Three prepared research packets
can proceed independently; don't make a builder wait for already-settled questions.

One decision-bearing review per material candidate is preferable to serial
re-litigation. Reuse accepted review when the exact semantics/dependencies remain
valid. Preserve required independent security/authority reviews and trusted CI.
No unavailable autonomous reviewer should create a new bureaucracy gate beyond
current source law. Ordinary mechanical checks need no principal research turn.

CI failures are classified once into product regression, inherited base failure,
runner/network problem, conflicting source, or stale proof binding. Existing CI
owners repair shared faults. Never add another merge/runner controller, bypass a
required check, or rerun an unchanged product failure until it happens to pass.
