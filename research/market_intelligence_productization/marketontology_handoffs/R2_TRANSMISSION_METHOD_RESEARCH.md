## Research questions

1. Represent observed association, economic mechanism hypothesis, estimated response,
   and intervention/causal evidence separately. What may the interface actually claim?
2. Specify event time, publication time, knowledge/belief time, effective interval,
   vintage/revision and correction behavior without another temporal store.
3. How should direction, sign changes, lag windows, regime dependence, cross-asset
   exposure and conflicting paths be represented without false precision?
4. When is 'no observed transmission' data absence versus genuine negative evidence?
5. How can new evidence update or invalidate a saved explanation and notify a researcher
   through the existing thesis/monitoring owner?
6. Which one or two experiments discriminate useful extra intelligence from a static
   graph, a simple event-window baseline and the current deterministic system?

## Required experimental design

For each proposed empirical claim define:
- exact lawful data source, fields, units, universe and sampling clock;
- treatment/event construction and pre-registered outcomes;
- point-in-time availability, publication lag and revised-versus-as-observed treatment;
- market-session alignment, missingness, survivorship and entity-resolution rules;
- baseline, holdout or rolling evaluation, leakage checks and negative controls;
- simultaneous-event/confounding handling and alternative explanation tests;
- effective sample sizes, overlap, uncertainty and multiple-comparison handling;
- calibration or stability checks appropriate to the output;
- failure threshold, how it changes the product claim, and an ablation.

A methodology proposal is not a tested result. Execute a small study only when inputs
and approved tools/budget are genuinely available. Record real command/code/input
hash/output and failures; otherwise return a runnable study design with the exact
missing data. Do not replace absent observations with synthetic evidence of alpha.
A toy example may explain a contract but must be marked synthetic and prove no
real-world performance.
