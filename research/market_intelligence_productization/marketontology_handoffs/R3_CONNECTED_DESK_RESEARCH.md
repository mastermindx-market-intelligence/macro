## Required state and interaction specification

For each screen or transition name:
- user question and one useful action;
- real owning entity, route and contract;
- context passed as bounded references versus context that must be re-resolved;
- read/write/auth/entitlement boundary;
- loading, genuine empty, stale, corrected, unavailable, denied and conflict states;
- persisted versus one-turn context and back/reopen behavior;
- desktop/mobile layout, keyboard/focus behavior and bilingual copy;
- evidence/source visibility without burying the main conclusion;
- what is explicitly NOT inferred from missing data.

Do not put opaque internal state codes, raw UUIDs, fabricated scores or excessive
provenance detail in the main reading tier. Do not hide uncertainty in tooltips alone.
No dead menu entries or pseudo-live scheduling controls. Personal and team data must
remain distinct; a shared UI does not authorize sharing private research.
