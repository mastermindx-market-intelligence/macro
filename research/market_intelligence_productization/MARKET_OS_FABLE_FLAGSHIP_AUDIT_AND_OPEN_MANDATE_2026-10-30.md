# 1. Executive thesis and Chairman outcome

Mastermind has invested in many important market, company, portfolio, event, research and trading-context engines. The product gap is that a person still cannot reliably traverse one clear, powerful, attractive, source-truthful investor workflow in which these capabilities feel like one operating system. The job is **product convergence and completion**, not another large framework or relaunch of already-accepted technical work.

The flagship promise: **institutional depth, retail compression, one canonical intelligence substrate**. A newcomer should understand the market and one company within moments; a serious investor should drill into evidence, relative leadership, chart context, catalysts, contradictions, scenario risk, personal exposure and the next observable; a returning user should know what actually changed since the last visit. The experience should work across the existing Macro and Terminal products, rather than require users to understand internal repositories or systems.

Fable is the accountable creative/product integration principal, not a subordinate implementation clerk. Fable should have the freedom to challenge navigation, information architecture, screen hierarchy, page relationships, interaction design, release sequence, problem decomposition, use of native views versus compatible legacy pages, experiment hypotheses and delivery-team organization. Fable is encouraged to propose a substantially better solution than the author imagines. The outcome and truth constraints, not the suggested UI, control success.

**Done when:** an independent reviewer and actual users can complete representative discover -> understand -> evaluate -> save -> revisit -> monitor journeys on the admitted deployed product, including negative/degraded states, with true data, authenticated state, trustworthy source/rights lineage, coherent browser behavior, correct saved-user identity and verified Macro/Terminal continuity. Product-level completion remains separate from all 130 MarketOntology parity obligations and from speculative predictive/trading performance.

# 6. Personas and candidate end-to-end jobs

Fable may replace or reorder these examples after credible product tests; none is a literal UI specification.

### J-A. Discover and understand
An anonymous or entitled user opens the market, understands regime/breadth/theme and real changes, selects a qualified security, sees a concise state/change/risk/opportunity explanation, opens evidence and chart context, and returns to the same point without losing history or focus. Ambiguous listings, stale data, unqualified catalysts and rights-denied details must fail informatively.

### J-B. My portfolio matters to me
An authenticated investor imports or reviews holdings, sees the correct canonical positions independently of attention Watchlists, identifies concentration/defensive exposure or relevant company/market developments, explores source-backed scenario implications, saves a personal review, and sees the same authority truth across Macro and Terminal. A failed/unknown write is not "Saved"; user identity switches cannot leak state.

### J-C. Theme to leader to setup context
A user starts from a sector, theme, subtheme or economic event, sees participant breadth and leadership evidence, compares companies, reviews an event/catalyst hypothesis and structured technical-entry context, and can monitor/watch without having a model manufacture a buy signal. Unvalidated early leader detection remains descriptive or shadow; users can inspect contradictory and missing evidence.

### J-D. Research case and revisitation
A researcher follows an evidence trail from event -> company -> assumptions -> scenario -> exact revision. They save a thesis or investigation using existing native storage, reopen the *same* revision, see source correction history, and optionally share only with a genuinely eligible recipient. A denied or withdrawn share cannot downgrade into a public workaround.

### J-E. Returning user and interruption recovery
On a later session a user sees what materially changed, what is unchanged, what became stale/unknown and what affects their selected holdings or monitored names; they can resume an in-progress investigation. Cross-tab, browser history, sign-out, back/forward, locale/theme, error recovery and 320px/390px layouts are parts of the product, not polish deferred to the end.

### J-F. Professional deeper read
An analyst can expand the same case into original observations, source/rights, PIT, competing explanations, caveats, scenario inputs, model/evaluation receipts, revisions and appropriate export/sharing. The desk view should be a deeper lens over the same truth, not a separate ungoverned intelligence engine.

# 10. Acceptance framework: what would disprove success

A flagship or wave is not accepted merely because a draft PR exists, CI is green, a screenshot is pretty, a static quote is current, or a child said DONE.

**Product proof:** For each chosen persona, demonstrate a coherent entry/action/return from a real user-accessible release. Prove the actual route, user context, source owner, correction and trust; show the public and authenticated variants where relevant. Test more than one real issuer/listing and one genuine meaningful source-change event before claiming generality.

**Negative truth:** Demonstrate missing source, stale/withdrawn/contradictory data, rights-denied, expired auth, wrong account, failed save, unknown save effect, delayed load, mismatched issuer identity, null scenario inputs, empty portfolio and unentitled features. No false "Saved", false zero, false rank, false "live" or quiet replacement by a latest revision.

**Cross-system identity:** Owned Portfolio and Watchlist populations remain separate; authoritative cloud readback remains authoritative; cross-tab/account and Macro/Terminal values match after real saves; source correction and replay are legible; no private holdings or grants leak.

**Real UX:** Desktop, 820px/tablet, 390px/320px mobile, 200% text, light/dark, EN/ZH, keyboard focus/recovery, assistive labels, back/forward, direct/deep links, search, chart/legacy compatibility and failure recovery. No giant unscannable dashboards or dead-end drawers accepted as the flagship.

**Engineering:** Exact changed-head CI, applicable security/rights evaluation, independent non-author review, deterministic read/schema and permission tests, approved release and rollback, installed SHA/source parity, service health and natural producer/consumer receipts. If runtime proof is unavailable, report BUILT_NOT_PROVEN.

**Business utility:** A user can correctly state what changed, why it matters, how trustworthy it is, which exposure is affected, what would invalidate the view and what to revisit. Fable should propose measurable success targets after measuring the existing product, not invent retrospective conversion baselines.

**Forecast/rank authority:** Research, model-generated summaries and descriptive confluence never independently trigger trading, sizing, auto-exit, forecast probabilities or validated-leader labels. Promote only through the accepted evaluation and prospective evidence gates.
