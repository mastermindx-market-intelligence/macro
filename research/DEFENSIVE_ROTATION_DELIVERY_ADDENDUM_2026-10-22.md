## 4. Measurements before judgments

Use existing rights-qualified, consistently adjusted canonical observations. Keep observation time, market session, availability/publication time, retrieval time and source revision distinct. Each displayed comparison names instrument/group identity, benchmark, horizon, return basis, source clock, coverage and vintage.

Primary display clocks are 5/10/20/60 completed trading sessions, not calendar days. Intraday 5/15/30/60-minute observations are separate only where the existing source contract is qualified. Weekly/two-week candles distinguish completed from developing bars and keep calendar origin explicit.

For comparable returns R_a and R_b, relative wealth = (1 + R_a)/(1 + R_b) - 1. Return subtraction may be shown only as a separately named percentage-point spread. Do not call price relative strength a measured capital flow. Do not mix price return and total return. Missing, stale, partial, revised and unavailable are states, not zeros.

Measure separately: XLP, XLU, XLV, their eligible members and equal-weight cohorts; QQQ, XLK, SOXX and SMH. None of these instruments is interchangeable. A technology index can conceal semiconductor weakness or a small group of mega-cap winners. A defensive ETF can conceal strength in selected members. Show membership and survivorship assumptions.

### Four economically different observations

| Absolute defensive leg | Relative versus risk leg | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | Positive | Defensive leadership with positive absolute performance |
| Non-positive | Positive | Relative shelter only; not evidence that a leveraged long is safe |
| Positive | Non-positive | Broad rally / defensive participation, not defensive leadership |
| Non-positive | Non-positive | No defensive shelter demonstrated |

A declining risk leg plus a rising defensive leg is especially legible divergence, but remains a measurement. It does not by itself prove a future crash, profitable short, or appropriate leverage.

## 5. State, evidence, uncertainty and invalidation

Reuse the existing rotation transition owner. The product vocabulary should distinguish WATCH (emerging measured change), OBSERVED/PERSISTENT (the stated measurement persisted), WEAKENING, INVALIDATED, and UNKNOWN/STALE. Final enum mapping must use the incumbent contract rather than adding an alternate lifecycle.

Every state contains the exact rule/version, supporting observations, strongest contrary evidence and disconfirmation condition. Persistence may reduce alert churn, but its delay must be measured. Do not hide the first weak observation until a slow confirmation arrives. An emerging watch and an observed persistent state should have different presentation, not different invented probabilities.

Keep three axes separate:
- **Data quality:** timeliness, basis, common-session alignment, coverage and revisions.
- **Observed setup:** direction, pace, persistence, breadth and agreement/disagreement across clocks, printed separately.
- **Predictive qualification:** unqualified / research-only / qualified for a named objective and horizon under a specific accepted protocol.

No additive vote count becomes crash confidence, a new fused health score, ranking authority or sizing. No LLM can originate measured values, forecast probabilities, confirmations, alerts or trades. AI may explain a versioned evidence packet and must be checked against it.

Invalidation examples to implement through the existing owner: relative leadership reverses; absolute defensive trend turns negative; prior tech weakness is repaired; a short leg rallies violently; participation narrows; source freshness or basis fails; or a corporate action revises the observation. A data failure withdraws confidence without pretending the market thesis was disproved.

It compares retaining growth, reducing beta, defensive long-only rotation, a risk-controlled relative-value hedge, and cash/short-duration alternatives. ### The decision product that must follow qualification

The useful destination is a policy output for a declared portfolio and objective, not an unexplained confidence badge. The existing portfolio/decision owner should consume a versioned qualified estimate containing its objective and horizon, model/protocol identity, probability and uncertainty interval for the specific relative/absolute outcome, expected magnitude after costs, adverse-excursion/stress estimates, source age and coverage. It then compares permitted actions against the user's recorded book and risk limits. The emitted recommendation must explain recommended exposure change, maximum permitted exposure, invalidation and what contrary evidence would reduce it. This is a proposed consumer contract, not an activated policy or accepted forecast schema.

Separate three questions in that qualification:
1. Will the defensive basket outperform the chosen risk basket?
2. Will the defensive basket itself have a positive return after costs?
3. Will the chosen expression improve the user's objective and downside exposure, including a short squeeze, rate shock, financing stress or option decay?

Success on (1) does not establish (2) or (3). A rising stock ratio can coexist with two losing legs. Positive expected relative return is not permission to maximize gross leverage. Gross/net/beta, concentration, borrowing/financing, gap losses and the portfolio-level stressed-loss budget constrain any accepted action, even when a forecast is strong.

Early observation, persistent evidence and later macro confirmation must be separately timestamped. Slow credit/macro confirmation may affect confidence or risk budget through its own accepted owner, but must not erase a legitimately earlier sector observation. Conversely, faster observations are not automatically more predictive. Evaluate latency, false positives, missed rotations and decision utility at each clock, including quiet markets and rebounds.

None of this promotes the old failed crash rule or combines the exploratory nested-cycle setup into a prohibited confluence score. The new scientific question is prospective decision utility from available leadership evidence under explicit costs and constraints. All October observations already inspected in this mission are development/retrospective evidence. New forward witness collection and an immutable accepted protocol are required before a future qualification claim.

### Risk-control guidance must not be held hostage to an alpha forecast

Separate ordinary portfolio risk controls from an alpha claim. With a current, permissioned holdings snapshot and a user-approved risk policy, the product can deterministically identify a technology/sector concentration breach, show gross/net/beta and scenario losses, compare reducing exposure with rotating or hedging, and calculate the exposure consistent with a stated loss budget. These are conditional measurements against explicit policy, not calibrated crash predictions or claims of optimal returns. They still need correct contracts, costs, holdings completeness and real-path tests, but do not require pretending that a new return forecast has been scientifically qualified.

By contrast, recommending an alpha-driven overweight, a semiconductor short, or additional leverage because a rotation is expected to continue needs the corresponding accepted decision-utility evidence and expression-specific constraints. The first useful decision surface should show both lanes: `Risk-budget comparison available` where the existing book/policy can support it, and `Alpha-driven sizing not qualified` where it cannot. Never collapse both into a blank 'no action' box or use risk-control arithmetic to smuggle in an unvalidated directional bet.

The historical October 8 artifact also shows why 'defensives' is not a single buy instruction: staples had a positive absolute two-session return while utilities were still negative but more resilient than technology. The product must distinguish sectors and instruments, and distinguish an early short-horizon turn against the old trend from a later short-horizon deterioration within a mature positive trend. Both are observable before a slow macro composite changes, but their predictive usefulness and appropriate expression must be measured separately.
