## Implemented design reference

The self-contained `preview.html` uses the 372 published rows from the October 29 capture. The board opens with SHW. Search covers the full tracked history through Find a stock. The map and stock ledger link to the same evidence panel. The advanced drawer retains the original share, trend, short-volume spike and own-history filters. CSV preserves the original metric fields, adds the observation scope and venue week, and withholds invalid derived numbers.

The normal and unusual scopes differ. Twenty-seven current names exceed the 1.5 robust-z threshold. Twenty-five also have a published price pattern and at least 40% participation. The map can show either group on the same robust-z axis. The horizontal axis uses up to 252 prior sessions. The ledger separately shows percentage points versus the preceding 60-session median. The vertical axis uses five closing-price sessions through the latest available price on or before the observation. The default current ordering follows the published desk ordering. Older records have an explicit observation date and alphabetical default order.

The 39.4% headline comes from 259 current names with positive off-exchange dollar volume and valid participation. It recomputes as 39.35545% from the captured rows. The 275 current observations include 16 names without a usable same-day dollar input. ATS venues have their own September 21 report week, 38 days before the daily observation. Historical rows do not acquire attribution from a later reporting week.
