How to read the desk
Look for a departure from the stock’s own history
The unusual group uses the desk’s settled-session observations, an own-history score of at least 1.5, at least 40% off-exchange share, and a price observation. The map places each name by the difference from its normal share in percentage points and its price change over the past week.
Know what the tape measures
Off-exchange activity includes registered pools and internalizers. The daily tape does not identify buyers, sellers or institutional intent. The headline share weights constituents by dollar volume. A stock’s individual share uses traded share volume.
