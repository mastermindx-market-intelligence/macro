## 0. Acceptance gates

The user must be able to understand the observed market state, the independently specified core posture, an overlay's eligibility and invalidation, the actual qualified paper holdings, and the part of the result that survives economic accounting. The machine must preserve the same facts, rules, versions and funded positions across research and product consumers.

Not done unless:

- Publisher claims, image estimates, hypothetical scenarios, original backtests, forward shadow results and admitted paper-account results remain distinct. A mixed-clock or known-date calculation cannot be labeled synchronized whole-history NAV.

- Qualified inputs, next-executable prices, costs, cash, corporate actions, benchmark and daily open-position marks support the claimed result. Missing data is not ordinary no-signal.

- Product proof covers dark/light, English/Chinese, desktop/mobile and stale, missing, corrected, failed and incompatible-version states. Studies stay off the render path.
