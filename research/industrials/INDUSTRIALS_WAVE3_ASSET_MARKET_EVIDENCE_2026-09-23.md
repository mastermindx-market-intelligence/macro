## W3-S28 - Sandhills August used-equipment report

Source: https://www.sandhills.com/news/article/250048193
Published: 4 September 2026. Period: August 2026. Locators: U.S. Used Aerial Lifts; market methodology/EVI description.

Aerial-lift inventory declined 3.75% year over year while the reported auction-value measure declined 6.36%. Sandhills describes EVI values as estimates. This is not a matched-machine transaction-price index or all rental fleets. It provides a counterexample to assuming lower inventory guarantees stronger residuals. Source population, equipment class and valuation method must accompany a later resale scenario.

## W3-S29 - Tractor Zoom August category comparison

Source: https://www.tractorzoompro.com/blog/august-2026-equipment-market-update-lots-clear-values-split
Published: 9 September 2026. Period: August 2026. Locators: 300-424 HP tractors; 425+ HP tractors; Definitions & data sources.

Reported auction averages rose 21% for the first category and fell 19% for the second. These are category averages excluding major outliers, not constant-quality prices. The glossary defines aged inventory as a share, and 60-day sales velocity as a count of qualifying sales, not a probability. Ambiguous introductory descriptions are not used. Cohort mix, reporting population and denominators need reconciliation before historical modeling.

## W3-S30 - Chicago Fed agricultural credit survey

Source: https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/agletter/2025-2029/august-2026
Publication: August 2026 issue; exact publication clock not retained in this register. Period: Q2 2026. Locators: Credit conditions; table 3; closing survey-population note.

The repayment diffusion index was 73 versus 63 in Q1. Zero percent of respondents reported faster repayment and 27% slower repayment year over year. This is neither a 73% repayment rate nor a borrower default rate. Results include Farm Credit System institutions from Q1 2026; earlier surveys included commercial banks only. The publication expects weaker machinery-loan volumes, an attributed expectation rather than an outcome.

## W3-S31 - Kansas City Fed multi-district credit synthesis

Source: https://www.kansascityfed.org/center-for-agriculture-and-the-economy/agricultural-finance/farm-finance-deterioration-eases-slightly/
Published: 26 August 2026. Period: Q2 2026 with historical comparisons. Locators: opening analysis; Farm Loan Repayment Rates; Collateral Requirements; index definition.

The authors report slower deterioration in farm finances alongside further collateral tightening. The diffusion measure is 100 plus the share of respondents reporting higher conditions minus the share reporting lower conditions. Direction, pace and level remain separate. This synthesis overlaps constituent regional surveys and is not an additional independent confirmation of each district's observations or a customer-level equipment-purchase forecast.

## Research interpretation and testable design implications

The useful concept is market clearing: what must change for the customer, dealer and owner to transact at a sustainable price? A shrinking inventory pool can reflect sales, slower replenishment, fewer listings or scope changes. A higher average sale price can reflect newer or more valuable units being sold. Neither observation alone establishes improving economics for the same machine or the same customer.

A proposed used-asset observation therefore retains category, model/generation, hours, age, condition, location, channel, asking versus estimated versus transacted value, sample count, exclusions, listing duration, reported change basis and source vintage. Unknown fields remain unknown. No inferred machine-level identity is created from a category average.

The investor question is not merely whether auction values rise. A decline may help a buyer of used equipment while weakening an existing owner's trade-in affordability, a dealer's inventory value or a lessor's disposal economics. The sign of the effect depends on the position held and the next transaction. Our replacement and rental sensitivities explain these conditional effects without claiming an empirically estimated elasticity.

Customer credit adds a separate decision constraint. Loan cost, down payment, collateral requirements, liquidity and lender approval may bind even where a replacement has positive economic value. A diffusion index becoming less negative can indicate slower deterioration, not healthy conditions or freely available credit. Compare the customer's actual decision with the appropriate regional/business population; do not attach a regional lender index directly to every company's sales forecast.

The new sources partially resolve the earlier gap in public used-market and finance observations. They do not supply licensed transaction histories, a representative fleet-age distribution, matched credit decisions, constant-quality residual-price indices or calibrated replacement probabilities. Those remain named data and validation obligations rather than reasons to invent a universal cycle score.
