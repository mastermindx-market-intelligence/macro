## 1. Outcome: an explanation the investor can use

The investor should be able to investigate a granular industrial theme, identify which businesses actually participate, understand the mechanism changing their earnings and cash, distinguish that change from what was expected, and see what evidence would overturn the interpretation. A user should not have to read eight research waves or reconstruct the chain from a collection of citations.

The machine's job is to connect evidence without erasing its business scope, denominator, timing, uncertainty or access restrictions. It must preserve the distinction between an eligible explanation and a validated forecast. The research moat is the accumulated mapping of business mechanisms, counterexamples and historical information sets, not simply a larger company list or another sentiment score.

Three questions have different prerequisites: what the business does; what its economics are doing; and whether a security's price misrepresents the future. Product composition should support the first two when evidence permits while showing exactly why the third is unavailable. A missing analyst panel must not suppress a valid explanation of a contract or profit bridge. A strong explanation must not fill the absent panel with a model-generated consensus.

This synthesis does not claim the entire sector is exhaustively mapped. It establishes a reusable set of completed research questions and identifies what a bounded first product must do. Global specialists, private-company linkages, true cohort retention, contract parameters, lifecycle capital and expectations validation remain selective rather than comprehensive. Those omissions are prioritized below by the decisions they prevent.

## 2. Selected approach and alternatives

**Static sector encyclopedia.** It would organize the research neatly, but the user would still have to determine what changed and why it matters. Reject it as the primary workflow; retain long-form research as inspectable support.

**One global attractiveness or bottleneck score.** It would make sorting easy but combine incompatible contract roles, recurring-revenue definitions, cycle stages and capital burdens. Reject this as a new authority. Existing accepted Theme Tracker analytical outputs remain unchanged; this research is not permission to replace them.

**Task-composed business dossiers inside the shared theme experience. Selected proposal.** Start from the investor's question, select the economic measurements that answer it, show a concise mechanism and counterargument, and connect to source and company workflows. Reuse shared composition, evidence and identity owners rather than create an Industrials database. The same business can legitimately appear in several questions, with different dates and exposure boundaries.

This selection preserves full-scale ambition while allowing a useful vertical slice: one real input can become an explanation, a qualified comparison and a visible limitation on the real authenticated path. A large framework with no admitted data does not satisfy the job; neither does one flattering example with no negative state.

## 3. Reconcile the ontology without flattening the businesses

Use independent dimensions rather than a single rigid tree. Conventional industry describes the issuer or business; economic archetype describes how it earns and reinvests; demand theme describes the customer's spending or use; product/capability describes what is supplied; research slice groups a question; region must specify sales, customer use or production; period and ownership qualify each relationship. Tradable basket membership is a separate accepted owner output.

For example, AI physical infrastructure can connect electrical equipment, cooling, specialist contracting, rental and maintenance. Those businesses share a customer-spending theme but not one margin model. An automation group may contain device sales, project engineering and paid software. An aircraft-engine program may create both installation investment and a maintenance obligation. A conventional sector label cannot resolve these distinctions.

A company-product label establishes neither revenue purity nor a named supply relationship. Revenue share, profit share, contribution to profit growth, capital exposure and security-return sensitivity are distinct. They need their own evidence and cannot be inferred from one another. Source-scoped descriptions can remain useful without inventing a global product identifier or guessing a listing from a similar name.

Overlaps require a declared aggregation boundary. A component contained in a purchased system is not added again to end-customer spending; a joint venture's physical production is counted once even when both parents discuss it. Several business roles may link to one issuer without becoming several independent equity positions. Where a non-overlapping financial allocation is unavailable, show a map and unknown allocation rather than fabricate a weighted exposure.

The catalog below reconciles repeated research into 24 questions, not 24 mandatory new baskets. It deliberately allows existing theme families to share a task. A schema with a field for every sentence in the prior waves would be unwieldy and would confuse editorial research with native authority.

A large table without that synthesis does not complete the task. Falsifiers should name an observable contradiction, not merely warn that markets are uncertain.

## 5. Eight reusable modules, not eight new systems

The shared dossier can compose **business exposure; demand and commercial commitment; operational conversion; contracts and risk; profit and cash; cohorts and customer economics; expectations and scenarios; and evidence/corrections**. These are presentation and analysis groupings, not new services or stores. A staffing question can omit physical supply mapping; an engine question needs contract roles; a project question needs recognition and cash milestones.

### 5.1 Native integration responsibilities

The Robotics specification was rechecked at #7773 head `f10211657c6c31df3c9af73cd4b9484e2dd7690a`, still open/draft. Its proposed optional curation assertion supplies a useful representation direction. It does not prove schema enrollment, private persistence, clock-preserving K1 compilation or source admission. Industrials must consume the accepted outcome of that shared work, not independently create a similarly named extension.

The existing GMI evidence owner is the destination for reviewed source-scoped financial, operating, product and ownership assertions when its contract permits them. Data OS and the incumbent identity-resolution path bind issuer/security/listing relationships; source labels remain visible but unbound until validated. K1 references native evidence and preserves source/business/observation clocks; it is not a second fact warehouse. Existing company-intelligence and earnings owners retain fiscal and actual/estimate semantics. Existing forecast/evaluation owners retain hypothesis, prediction and outcome authority. Price and corporate-action owners retain market data.

F04/shared page composition should consume qualified outputs inside existing authenticated transport. No live full-fidelity research body is added to public Git, generated static payloads, source maps or browser persistent storage. Proposed public research examples and a public shell are different from current paid intelligence. Rights and retention remain prerequisites for admitting real claims to the product.

A browser never recalculates an incumbent theme score or invents a security join. Deterministic arithmetic should consume accepted values and return definitions and limitations with its result. A displayed scenario must expose assumptions and method revision; it must not silently become the house forecast or an order instruction. No research label here changes basket membership, rank, timing, sizing or execution.

### 5.2 Comparison and failure behavior

An unavailable layer should explain its exact cause and preserve other qualified layers. Missing disclosure is not zero exposure. A held identity is not a worthless source. An unread actual cannot be reconstructed from rounded revenue and margin just to fill a card. A stale estimate can remain historically visible but cannot masquerade as latest consensus. A withdrawn assertion remains visible in history but is not reused as current evidence.

The display must distinguish upstream publication, business period/effective date, first observation, retained revision and page build. Day-grain evidence cannot establish same-day ordering. A file-level freshness timestamp cannot certify every value inside it. Evidence that an issuer made a forecast is stronger than evidence that the forecast will occur; the interface should communicate both scopes rather than assign one ambiguous confidence percentage.

Cross-business comparisons require aligned bases or an explicit reconciliation. Currency translation, acquisitions, severance, pass-through revenue, corporate costs, client funds and estimated future obligations cannot disappear between the evidence drawer and headline. Where data do not reconcile, expose the residual and refrain from assigning a cause without support.

## 6. Six complete reference dossiers

These are proposed paper-level user acceptance cases, not rendered application proof or current investment recommendations. Their purpose is to make the final implementation package unambiguous about what a useful user result looks like.

### W9-D01 - Expert-service growth with a held consensus verdict

The reader enters from an existing thematic detail, selects the documented expert-service business and asks whether reported growth improved its economics. Present the applicable business and fiscal period, revenue before reimbursements alongside total revenue, and the correct margin denominator. Link the dated management guidance separately from the actual result. That supports an operational explanation without requiring a stock verdict.

The historical-data audit adds a real negative state: EXPO's retained 0.55 value has a June19 row clock and unspecified basis. Exponent's July30 report states diluted EPS of0.60, but the product must not certify a beat by merely comparing the two. Explain which basis/vintage fields are missing. A separately sourced planned after-close session may enrich timing without pretending the 4:30pm conference call is the exact release time. Preserve a return link to the existing company workflow only through a validated listing.

Completion is a reader who understands the business result and why the consensus verdict is unavailable. A blank whole page fails; a green beat badge fails. Source examples and exact limits are in the companion W9 historical-data audit and W7.

### W9-D02 - A preliminary warning changes the information set

The reader asks what was new at Pentair's final result. Show the sequence of management guidance, July14 preliminary disclosure and subsequent final-result source from W6, each with its own publication and basis. Show the stored June19 external-associated value only as an unqualified historical row. Do not substitute the preliminary management estimate into an external-consensus field.

The user should be able to change the historical cutoff and see which sources were available by that date, without a later source leaking backward. Where exact intraday order is unknown, provide a day-level view with a limitation rather than synthetic midnight. A price-reaction study stays held until the event interval, listing, adjusted price data and intervening events are qualified.

This case tests information novelty, not whether the stock was attractive. It is a development case selected after the result, never a claimed out-of-sample success. The successful new direct read of the July14 release closes one access gap, not all source admission and market-data prerequisites.

### W9-D03 - Machinery recovery without retail growth

The reader compares end-customer purchases, dealer stock and manufacturer shipments over matched periods using W3's existing research. Separate the observed company disclosures from the hypothetical inventory identity. Let the scenario demonstrate that ending underproduction can lift shipments and fixed-cost absorption while retail remains flat. Label unavailable dealer geography or stock targets explicitly.

The explanation should state the positive mechanism and its strongest adverse case: weaker retail or a lower desired stock target can overwhelm normalization. It should not imply that a shipment increase proves an economic boom. Existing price/timing outputs remain unchanged. The user can inspect the customer-return and financing evidence that would discriminate between healthy replacement and temporary channel rebuilding.

Completion means the investor can explain how earnings might change and which next data would overturn that explanation. It does not require a fitted demand forecast, fabricated channel stock or an automatic stock recommendation.

### W9-D04 - Aerospace durability has opposite contractual effects

Starting from an engine/aftermarket slice, show the provider's payment and obligation roles before displaying the operating change. W4 establishes why better time-on-wing can reduce an hourly contract owner's fulfillment costs while reducing event-paid repair opportunities. Show demanded work, completed work, accounting recognition and cash as separate measurements.

A hypothetical sensitivity may compare the roles with fixed assumptions, but active fleet ages, grounded hours, compensation and shop capacity remain required for an actual forecast. The source drawer identifies whether evidence is a general service proposition or a signed contract. JV production is counted once; issuer economic participation is a separate relation.

The user should understand the sign of the mechanism without being told that every aerospace supplier benefits identically. A generic positive aftermarket-growth badge fails this case. Timing delays and capital requirements remain part of the lifetime explanation.

### W9-D05 - Retained services versus apparent recurrence

The reader asks whether a building-service or certification relationship deserves a longer earning horizon. Compose the recurring-demand evidence with cohort retention, realized pricing, support obligations, capacity and reinvestment. W6-W7 establish that a service label or ongoing revenue percentage does not identify churn or profit persistence.

When retention is undisclosed, show a range of explicit scenario assumptions rather than back-solving a precise rate from aggregate revenue. Explain that the same ending revenue can result from high retention with few additions or poor retention with expensive replacement business. For a retrofit, also show the payer, benefit recipient and justified baseline; for local services, show route/resource limits instead of universal density benefits.

Completion is a conditional durability thesis and specific missing cohort evidence, not a subscription valuation multiple assigned to all repeated sales. The investor should see what repeat activity is documented and what future economics remain assumed.

### W9-D06 - Payroll quality without treating client money as equity cash

The reader investigates service retention and earnings resilience. Show the service-fee business and the client-funds investment/financing contribution separately. W8's research distinguishes client principal from corporate excess cash and shows why maturity vintages can dominate immediate spot-rate direction.

A scenario may vary the portfolio and service assumptions, but it must retain repayment obligations, liquidity and financing costs. PEO benefits pass-through and risk-bearing insurance contribution are separate from payroll administration. Acquisitions, corporate costs and shareholder financing remain visible before a per-share valuation bridge.

The useful result is an explanation of which earnings are recurring-service economics and which depend on balances, yields or risk. The product must not add client assets to a cash-rich equity screen or attribute all interest growth to software quality. No new financial trading authority follows from the dossier.

## 8. Research readiness and the evidence that changes it

Treat readiness by conclusion rather than a percentage-complete score. Source-supported business explanation is one outcome; calibrated financial prediction is another; market mispricing and executable trading are further outcomes with additional evidence and separate authority. A substantial sector research program can responsibly release explanatory functionality before it proves alpha, provided that boundary is visible and accepted.

The two main classes of remaining work are native source qualification for complete dossiers and empirical validation of any stronger forecast. Avoid indefinite broad source sweeps. Each unresolved item should identify which conclusion it blocks, the smallest source that would resolve it, and whether it affects the whole dossier or one optional panel.

| Priority | Decision prevented | Smallest useful next evidence / treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Admit real research to the shared paid detail | Accepted native assertion/clock/identity contract, approved private retention and publication binding; otherwise keep live admission held |
| Critical | Label a historical consensus beat/miss | Standard-reader confirmation of the exact recovered file, then basis-matched pre-event estimate revisions and original clocks; stale rows remain historical only |
| Critical | Attribute a price move or test a forecast | Canonical listing, corporate actions, benchmark/horizon, event interval, intervening disclosures and retained forecast before outcome |
| High | Claim narrow thematic purity or profit relevance | Source-disclosed business/product denominator or transparently bounded allocation; do not substitute catalog mentions |
| High | Claim durable service economics | Defined active cohort, renewal/attachment and contribution after service/replacement costs; unknown retention remains unknown |
| High | Claim capacity growth creates value | Qualified utilization/throughput, full lifecycle and acquisition/license capital, cost/price risk and adverse cohorts |
| Targeted | Resolve earlier source contradictions | Sunbelt denominator, Rheinmetall arithmetic, TP normalization, AGCO geography and ODFL residual only where selected case depends on them |
| Targeted | Improve global/specialist breadth | Business cases selected for missing mechanisms, not another indiscriminate list of tickers |

The earlier waves contain source-local corrections and boundaries that remain controlling for their statements. The catalog does not make them accepted facts by repeating them. There is no need to resolve every possible industry detail before a bounded explanatory vertical, but the final packet must declare what is covered, why those cases are representative, and what remains held.

## 9. Validation protocol: no relabeling retrospective examples

SGS, Randstad, Exponent and Pentair examples were selected after their results. They are development cases. They can test extraction, comparison definitions, temporal restrictions and whether a user understands the dossier. They cannot be counted as held-out forecast wins merely because the record has been put into a repository.

A proposed subsequent evaluation starts with a frozen issuer candidate set drawn from already researched mechanisms: Rockwell, Eaton, Deere, United Rentals, GE Aerospace, Otis, Exponent and ADP. These are issuer labels for evaluation design, not resolved constituents or recommendations. Observe the first eligible earnings event per candidate after the existing evaluation owner accepts the protocol; events before that acceptance are excluded. If the owner accepts the protocol after a company's next event, use the following event. The set and rule are defined here before inspecting those later outcomes; no future-result search or watcher was initiated.

The eight-company set is a feasibility sample, not enough for an alpha claim. Inclusion is based on mechanism coverage, not a prediction that results will be positive. Both favorable and adverse outcomes stay in the denominator. Missing-data events also remain reported; the evaluation must not silently retain only convenient positive/negative pairs. Do not specify a quota of winning and losing realized outcomes, which would condition selection on the answer.

Before the event, an accepted prediction must name its target measure, business/period, expected direction or numerical interval, mechanism, competing explanation, source version and falsifier. External consensus must have separately qualified provenance and basis. No forecast is committed by this document because those inputs are not yet qualified. Predictions and outcomes belong to the existing forecast/evaluation owner, not a new JSON registry under this catalog.

Evaluate distinct tasks separately. First, test whether cited values and comparison boundaries are correct and whether the explanation survives the next evidence. Second, where actual numerical forecasts exist, measure error and interval coverage against predeclared simple baselines such as the unchanged prior forecast and a documented consensus. Third, only with qualified market data, examine the incremental relationship to security returns after market/industry effects, event clustering and corporate actions. Costs and capacity matter for any later tradable rule, but this research does not originate one.

Do not treat a residual multiple change as uniquely caused by recognition of our thesis. Different discount rates, financing, growth, capital intensity and other events can fit the same price. Reverse valuation should expose alternative coherent assumptions. The empirical result must report missing coverage and uncertainty even if its average looks favorable.
