## Research implications and unresolveds

The first acquisition pass resolves important measurement and mechanism questions: customer need is not financing capacity; channel destocking is not final demand; rental productivity is composite; fleet capital and disposal economics are material; consolidated acquisition growth is not pro-forma organic growth; gross margin can differ from cost-to-serve economics; freight revenue can rise without more physical freight; KPI denominators and historical populations can change.

Still absent: a representative company-level equipment-age distribution, actual replacement hazard estimates, matched customer loan and used-price cohorts, full lifecycle maintenance/capex attribution, a licensed point-in-time valuation/estimate panel and empirical predictive validation. Sunbelt S14 has an unresolved internal definition conflict. Fastenal S16 remains indexed-only. Komtrax revised comparisons must not contaminate a vintage backtest. Fable's final handoff remains withheld; no product, basket, forecast or trade authority changes.
