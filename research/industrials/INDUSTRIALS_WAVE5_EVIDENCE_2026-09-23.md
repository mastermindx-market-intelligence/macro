## W5-S20 - FAR firm-fixed-price description

Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/16.202-1
Publication: 2026-03-13. Period/scope: Base FAR page FAC 2026-01; contract-specific applicability required. Locator: Description.

The base FAR description allocates actual-cost risk and resulting profit or loss to the contractor for firm-fixed-price work. It does not imply that other incorporated clauses can never change scope or price. The research uses the description to distinguish economic sensitivities, not to determine a particular contract's legal outcome.

## W5-S21 - FAR cost-reimbursement description

Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/16.301-1
Publication: 2026-03-13. Period/scope: Base FAR page FAC 2026-01. Locator: Description and cost limit.

Cost reimbursement concerns allowable incurred costs within the contract's established limits and approval conditions. It is not an unconditional promise to reimburse any expenditure. Scope, cost allowability, ceilings, authorization and funding must be retained before modeling risk transfer.

## W5-S22 - FAR cost-plus-fixed-fee description

Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/16.306
Publication: 2026-03-13. Period/scope: Base FAR page FAC 2026-01. Locator: Paragraph (a) and scope-change distinction.

The fee is negotiated and fixed rather than varying with actual cost, but changes in the work can change the fee. More reimbursed cost can therefore change revenue and percentage margin without an equivalent increase in fee profit. The illustrative model assumes all modeled costs remain allowable and authorized.

## W5-S23 - FAR fixed-price incentive, firm target

Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/16.403-1
Publication: 2026-03-13. Period/scope: Base FAR page FAC 2026-01. Locator: Target cost, target profit, sharing formula and price ceiling.

Target cost, target profit, sharing and ceiling determine the contractor's economic exposure. The ceiling limits price, not losses: cost above it can create a loss. Other clauses may adjust the agreement. Numerical target/share/ceiling examples in the model are authored assumptions, not disclosed issuer contract terms.

## Coverage and unresolved source integrity

The 27 entries comprise 18 issuer-document records across 13 corporate groups, one GAO summary, and eight government contract/reference records. They are not 27 independent confirmations of a single thesis. Leonardo DRS does not substitute for researched parent-company accounts. Complete Leonardo-parent/Thales evidence, detailed small-company coverage, underlying contracts, acceptance/payment events, source-retention rights and point-in-time external estimates remain gaps.
