## 4. Concrete compatibility boundaries found in candidate code

### A. Signed financial amounts cannot be coerced into physical quantities

At the #7870 pin, `_check_observation` in `engine/theme_graph/curation_assertion.py` rejects negative numeric `value` and `value_high`. The file's blob is `23a25614782b8b1cb76ce7e3f292b64d35bfb4f6`.

That protects a physical-quantity use case but cannot directly carry every loss, cash outflow or negative adjustment required by Industrials. The selected response is to retain signed accounting facts and derivations in the financial owner and reference them from GMI. Do not take absolute values, clamp to zero, put the sign in a caption, or fill the graph with a duplicate financial dataset. A future typed signed observation, if genuinely needed, is a shared-owner version decision; it must not weaken non-negative physical quantities indiscriminately.

### B. Matching unknown definitions are not proof of comparability

The candidate guidance projector describes its inputs as already validated. Its optional equality function treats two missing values as equal. Its comparison checks basis, currency, perimeter and definition using that equality. Blob: `f07cdcb01cb3fc0d664ef465c97b30487578722d`.

This does not prove a production defect: a caller may already have qualified the missing fields. It does establish an Industrials input obligation. Unknown GAAP basis or unknown reporting perimeter cannot be promoted to known-compatible because both happen to be null. The native caller must establish required meaning or return a limitation. Explicitly inapplicable dimensions require an accepted owner representation, not a new ad hoc string invented by the consumer.

### C. A finalized preview is not next-period guidance

The same module rejects a `new_outlook` whose horizon matches the actual's fiscal period and marks the next-outlook comparison as a different-period relationship. That is appropriate for its declared job. Pentair's same-period preliminary/final sequence must use document-edition and event-history semantics, not a fake future horizon. No new outlook is invented merely to populate a mandatory-looking widget. Missing optional guidance leaves the qualified financial dossier usable.

### D. Domain selectors are not universal infrastructure

The accepted Semiconductor plan proposes a query with `hbm_packaging` and `sic_gan_specialty` slices and five industrial views. These are deliberate domain choices. An Industrials adapter cannot simply pass an arbitrary sector key through a closed Semiconductor response and call it supported.

Share only repeated semantics demonstrated by real consumers: source clocks, identity references, revisions, access, evidence drawers and section status. Keep qualified domain observations and economic calculations under their actual owners. Service retention, signed cash, insurance contribution and project editions do not need wafer diameter, a component graph or an artificial manufacturing stage.

### E. Fresh policy reading is implemented in candidate source, not yet proven end to end

`engine/theme_graph/rights.py`, blob `758937246e9a07ac150ba65123ebfa876bbc66c0`, now contains a fresh byte-read snapshot with a content-derived revision. That supersedes the premise that an Industrials implementation must independently solve the old path-cache problem.

The remaining obligation is consumption: the actual serving route must use the accepted snapshot at the required disclosure boundary, under current source-use and user-entitlement rules. Shared implementation evidence can be reused; an Industrials warm-revocation and dependent-prose regression is still needed. Old stored permissions cannot authorize new disclosure.
