## W6-S01 - Comfort Systems, June 2026 Form 10-Q
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035983/000110465926086258/fix-20260630x10q.htm
Period: quarter and half year ended June 30, 2026; publication timestamp not independently captured. Locators: revenue, remaining obligations, cash flow and liquidity.

Quarter growth was 50.3%, comprising 43.8 percentage points same-store and 6.5 acquisition. Short maintenance agreements are excluded from remaining obligations. Half-year operating cash was $1,528.254m versus $164.545m; billings/deferred-revenue cash contributions were $1,061.506m versus $376.780m. Building purchases of $188.9m supported modular growth. Those facts require separate demand, funding and investment interpretations; advances are not completed-profit receipts.
