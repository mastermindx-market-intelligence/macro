# Original-release guidance measurement pilot

## Status and scope

Completed **12 of 12 current-event packets**: Microsoft, Caterpillar and Duke Energy, each with all four quarterly earnings releases **published during calendar 2022**. The issuer list and event rule were fixed before extraction. This is a purposive source-format pilot, not a representative issuer sample or an investment test. January/February releases can report FY2021; the FY2022 year-end releases published in 2023 are outside this sample.

The companion `guidance_packets.json` contains 34 primary-source records, exact URLs, publication dates, source locations, availability qualifications, prior comparators, and provisional labels. No market series, returns, analyst expectations or charts were joined to the packets. No verbatim evidence excerpts are included.

**Blinding qualification:** initial searches using only site terms returned off-scope market-response/consensus/current-price snippets. A later issuer-report snippet also contained TSR. These were not opened for analysis or used in labels. The achieved design is therefore **outcome-unjoined provisional classification**, not pristine outcome blinding. Issuer identities were visible; later same-issuer releases were read for their own packets. This was not independent double annotation.

## What the pilot changes about measurement

**Disclosure stage must be recorded.** All four Microsoft releases explicitly point to the later call for guidance. The original official April, July and October call transcripts contain annual commentary, so it would also be wrong to conclude that Microsoft only provides quarter-ahead forecasts. Those calls are retained as separate context and cannot overwrite release-time unavailable labels.

**A maintained annual total does not establish a maintained remainder.** Duke's May and August releases reaffirm the annual range, but do not identify the old expectation for the newly completed period or supply a comparable remainder bridge. No EPS subtraction was used to manufacture one.

**Scope and metric basis precede direction.** Duke's November guidance excludes Commercial Renewables; its separate historical reported/adjusted earnings difference concerns the older International Disposal Group. Caterpillar's October change concerns an annual tax estimate, not an identified change in remaining-period operating demand or profit. The May Investor Day margin reaffirmation concerns a longer horizon.

**An interim update can change the comparator.** Microsoft's June 2 SEC exhibit provides a quarter-specific FX update before July. Its starred revenue/EPS totals are issuer-calculated, rather than previously explicit guidance, and its rounded figures should not be forced to reconcile exactly. The update does not establish a demand revision or an FY2023 annual revision.

**Even later-call labels require decomposition.** Microsoft's July FY2023 outlook includes a depreciation-life change. October's call identifies lower remaining-year Windows OEM expectations alongside completed-quarter effects and higher energy costs. Its conditional constant-currency margin language also changes exclusions. The packet preserves those components instead of assigning the entire annual movement to one mechanism.

## Temporal qualification and gaps

None of the 12 events has a verified **earliest public release timestamp** in this pilot. Caterpillar's January and October SEC acceptance records were retrieved; they remain separate from issuer dissemination time, with the raw timezone qualification preserved. Duke's intended 07:00 ET schedules are documented, but intended timing is not observed publication. Call start times do not establish the moment guidance was spoken or a transcript appeared.

Historical issuer pages retrieved today do not independently prove unchanged original contents. Microsoft's January availability notice expressly acknowledges a subsequent text/link update. Original SEC exhibits provide version identifiers for Caterpillar, Duke and Microsoft's June update, but an accession does not establish the earliest release timestamp.

The searches were bounded, not a complete census of interim calls, conference remarks or presentations. Microsoft's original FY22 Q1/Q2 call DOCX links were identified but content retrieval failed. Caterpillar call commentary was outside its release-body extraction lane. The Duke October presentation was text-inspected; complete image/transcript coverage was not established. These gaps remain explicit, rather than becoming unchanged labels.

## Disposition

The pilot supports a richer measurement schema: disclosure stage, horizon, business scope, accounting/currency basis, explicit versus calculated forecast, realized versus future contribution, and unresolved states. It does **not** qualify these fields as trading signals or qualify the existing earnings/transcript archives for historical use.

The next measurement gate is independent source-based label review. A later, separately timestamped call-stage exercise could test whether that source resolves the missing remainder information. Labels should be frozen before any future outcome join; no watcher, live feature or ingestion service was created.
