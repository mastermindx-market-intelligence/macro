## What the fresh census changes

### P1: recover an acceptance receipt, not another implementation campaign

The current source preserves three different truths. The ordinary visit path previously produced 145 net-new rows and an accepted dossier demonstration. Later malformed-key/crash-consistency work was implemented through #6269, but the source still marks the larger P1 lifecycle partial. The relevant frontier is the first healthy natural production run containing that amendment, with real source accounting and deployed consumer evidence—not a new synthetic malfunction injected into production. [I02,I03]

The fresh health artifact materially narrows the task. Its last attempt is 2026-10-02T16:38:52.752790+00:00 and is `upstream_degraded` because CNInfo SSE returned HTTP 504. The last successful run is 2026-10-01T16:56:21.687678+00:00. It accounts for 1,777 eligible records, 1,777 represented, zero typed exclusions, a readable exception ledger, and `boundary_persist_ok:true`. These are useful positive observations. They are **not** by themselves the required exact-checkout/natural-run/deployed-dossier acceptance receipt. [I04]

The new plan therefore asks the owner to recover already-existing natural-run evidence, verify the required commit and producer order, and issue an explicit ruling. A failed October 2 transport does not erase October 1's success; a historical success does not permit clean absence claims on the failed October 2 coverage interval. This is a proof-recovery task with a narrow possible transport follow-up, not permission to rebuild the collector.

### Prophet: actual treatment and outcome truth before alpha attribution

PR #6871's current body explicitly rejects affirmative `172/172`, zero-rank-delta, healthy-shadow and V3-fallback conclusions until the incumbent writer repairs the proof. Its unresolved issues include audited entry values being reused as their own reference, duplicate identities, mutable input revisions, a disconnected R4 payload, and differences between the public ledger fill owner and the explanatory audit fill owner. Historical Shenzhen price-vintage differences are also disclosed. [I08]

Consequently, neither “v4 works” nor “v4 caused the losses” is established by this audit. The proper benchmark is an exact, independently reconstructed serving policy with observed treatment exposure. If an episode was actually served by a fallback, label it as such only from validated observations. Separate the public arithmetic, original latch, earliest recoverable source vintage, and present-price reconstruction. Missing historical bytes are a limitation; replacing them with today's prices is not a repair.

This recovery constrains performance inference and live promotion. It need not stop source-rights review, bounded documentation, context UX design, or—once their separate P1 gate is lawfully released—owner-native evidence accrual and offline extraction-quality work.

### A federation contract is useful; a new federation service is not yet justified

The report is strongest when “federation” means consistent evidence semantics across existing systems. It is weakest when that word quietly commits the organization to a new persistent layer before validating any customer workflow. The accepted alternative is:

`owner stores -> versioned read adapters -> bounded company evidence packet -> existing consumers`

The packet may be published using the existing owner/publication path. It need not originate a universal evidence warehouse, event-ID service, ranker, alert queue or workflow engine. A pointer cache becomes permissible only after repeated cross-owner reads demonstrate a concrete cost/latency problem and the canonical owners approve its scope. It remains fully rebuildable and non-authoritative. [I02]

## Scientific reassessment

### Mechanism support is not demonstrated incremental edge

The institutional-visit return figure is recoverable from the primary JFQA abstract: a relative performance spread for abnormal visits among Shenzhen-listed firms. That is useful motivation for a bounded discovery experiment. It does not establish an all-China, after-cost, implementable result for Mastermind. Full sample-period and reproduction details were not recovered in this audit. [E01]

The inquiry-letter paper supports an information-production mechanism, not a universal bearish trade. The procurement evidence recovered here concerns operating and organizational outcomes, not proof of equity alpha. The old margin study is a warning against an informed-money shortcut, not proof that every future leverage feature is useless. These distinctions change labels, experiment objectives and promotion conditions. [E04–E07]

A contradictory site-visit study links visits with crash risk and analyst silence; that is a reason to stratify by incentives, coverage and information environment, not average incompatible findings into “visits bullish.” Its accessible evidence is abstract-level, and the register states that limitation. [E03]

### Old measurements need provenance, not numerical repetition

The older internal LHB and block numbers are preserved as archived research findings. No fresh return panel was loaded and no new backtest was run here. Their numerical precision should not be mistaken for contemporary confidence. CIE-03 must locate exact scripts, source manifests, definitions, pricing vintages, covered populations and later adjudications before using them as priors for a registered test. [I12]

A concrete lesson is the supply-absorption report: the original prose said a block tape did not exist, but later amendments show the real issue was a missing worktree link. It also confused ratio units with percentage-point units. A subsequent staged test closed under its frozen ruler. Reading only a search snippet would have reproduced the wrong “data unavailable” story. [I13]

### Absence, correlations and recognition require causal discipline

Repeated source coverage is not independent evidence. The Mastermind intake implementation counts distinct desk labels, takes their maximum score and adds 0.08 for each extra desk. This is confirmed code behavior, not proof of economically independent information. The current profile and actual book impact were not inspected at runtime. Do not remove or promote the bonus by fiat; preserve existing behavior while isolating the incremental effect through the consumer owner. [I10]

Recognition should describe how evidence has propagated, not assume that low attention means mispricing. Neglect may reflect illiquidity, poor coverage or low information quality. Ownership reports describe past reporting dates and may be obsolete by disclosure. Procurement award value is not automatically attributable revenue, cash or profit. These are separate intermediate hypotheses with their own falsifiers.

Flow Observatory is accepted-with-limitations, not a blank check to claim every live transition reverified today. [I02,I05,I06,I07]

**Sequence next:** policy implementation links, owner-routed CSG pilot, public-fund vintages and manager complexes, corporate projects/capacity, and filing-derived economic relationships. Each source family earns expansion by extraction quality, source reliability, cost and measurable user/decision value—not by completing a fashionable ontology.

**Do not build as default:** an all-China super-score, a new company master, a rival event spine, a new alert engine, source-count conviction, universal negative sentiment, copied terminal datasets, unlicensed intraday feeds, or new versions of closed Limit/supply-absorption experiments.

**Keep as admissible research baselines, not live rules:** raw visit counts, raw analyst counts and simple flow measures. “No raw count may ever be studied” would make falsification weaker. The restriction is on unearned authority and prohibited resurrection of an already closed identical construction.

**Remove unsupported claims from implementation authority:** numerical vendor superiority, uncited sample-level literature assertions, guaranteed moat language, calendar-based promotion promises, and any implication that the review has already solved historical outcome provenance.

## Final hardened thesis

The practical opportunity is a company-centered evidence workflow that is materially more reliable and useful than today's fragmented context surfaces. Its value might be earlier discovery, better prioritization, faster contradiction detection, better explanations, or reduced harmful decisions. Which of those benefits is real must be measured separately.

Build the smallest owner-native path that can show what changed, what supports it, what is unknown, what contradicts it, and when it became usable. Then test whether that path improves predefined decisions under the real market and source constraints. Scale successful families, retain useful context, and close failed hypotheses without relabeling failure as another feature. That is a defensible masterplan; the uploaded report's stronger claims are not yet defensible.
