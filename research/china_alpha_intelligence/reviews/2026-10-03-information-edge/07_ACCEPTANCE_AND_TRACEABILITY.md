This package completes a **standard-Pro adversarial review and commissioning plan**: it adjudicates material report claims, re-censuses the critical source/owner/consumer dependencies, restores portable references for relied-on findings, specifies an end-to-end program and supplies the Astra CEO launch prompt.

It does **not** complete implementation of China Information Edge, accept P1, repair #6871, verify every live endpoint/page, produce a new return backtest, establish all commercial-source rights, or demonstrate new alpha. Those differences are part of the acceptance design, not hidden footnotes.

The original report is **REQUEST_CHANGES** as a definitive, execution-authoritative research return. Its core product direction is retained with the corrections in document 01. The receiving CEO can start bounded proof recovery and then owner-authorized implementation without another open-ended research campaign.

## 3. Three completion axes

### ENGINEERING_COMPLETE

All accepted in-scope source/family/consumer capabilities work through their canonical owners, have actual required production proof, expose truthful unknown/degraded states, pass their tests/CI, and have operating/rollback owners. Every requested family is explicitly shipped at its earned authority, rejected, or parked under an accepted scope decision. A family cannot be called shipped merely because it is parked.

A narrower first slice may be **SLICE_ENGINEERING_COMPLETE** while the program still has unfinished packets. Do not use the first-slice milestone to declare the entire commission done.

### SCIENTIFICALLY_ADJUDICATED

Every in-scope registered construction has a valid frozen design, sufficient evidence for its stated decision and an accepted outcome: useful context, no demonstrated practical effect, rejected construction or narrowly supported treatment. A well-powered null is acceptable. A weak/uninformative result remains UNINFORMATIVE and may require accrual or closure on cost/feasibility grounds; it is not proof of no effect.

A registered prospective experiment lacking enough independent observations is **ACCRUAL_GATED**, with exact data, owner and review trigger. A durable accrual plan permits a responsible implementation handoff, not a false claim that the scientific question is settled.

### EDGE_PROVEN

At least one specified family/treatment has improved a preregistered decision metric over the relevant baseline, with credible uncertainty, costs/constraints, acceptable downside, prospective or otherwise properly held-out evidence, actual consumer behavior and explicit limited promotion. This is a capability claim earned by evidence, not a mandatory condition imposed on researchers to find a winner.

A completed engineering/research program can honestly conclude `NO_INCREMENTAL_EDGE_DEMONSTRATED` for its tested scope. It must not market that as EDGE_PROVEN. Conversely, a positive historical estimate with no viable operational source cannot satisfy engineering or live-promotion acceptance.

## 6. Research quality and epistemic closeout

The report is strategically stronger than its proof layer. The accepted corrections are actionable: source gate recovery, original outcome independence, actor/body prerequisites, owner-native interfaces, explicit clocks and separate evaluation questions. Those corrections have been carried into the work packets rather than merely listed as criticism.

The literature/source register does not pretend to satisfy unverified method details with polished prose. A source can justify a hypothesis without justifying an implementation threshold. For unverified vendor marketing, the correct operational result is no new dependency, not a fabricated comparison. For an unperformed backtest, the correct result is a reproducible future packet with a frozen estimand, not invented statistics.

## 7. Required final Astra return

Astra's final program return should include: exact code/package/source identities; a truthful feature/authority ledger; all changed/merged/published carriers; test commands and terminal CI evidence; source-rights and source-quality dispositions; natural production and user/machine-consumer receipts; actual scientific results including failures and denominator loss; narrow promotion/rollback records; residual accrual with named owner and review trigger; and closed worker/carrier/watch state.

The return must answer the commission's practical question in concrete terms: what was kept, finished, rejected, added, integrated, evaluated and promoted; what decision benefit was actually demonstrated; what remains context or uncertain; and who now maintains it. A live collector, merged PR, completed chart or impressive research narrative alone is not end-to-end completion.
