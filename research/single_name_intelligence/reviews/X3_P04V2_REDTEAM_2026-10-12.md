**Counts:** BLOCKER 3 · MAJOR 4 · MINOR 2 · NIT 1

**Does the v2 draft, as written, prevent the v1 TUNE leak?** Only partly.

Empty TUNE plus `never_refill` stop reuse of the retired TUNE members and stop
a v2 TUNE outcome table. The leak *mechanism* — a later adjustment vintage
rewriting bars already used at earlier decision dates — remains open. The
detector inspects `q_t` steps whose calendar date falls inside the split

still allows bars `<= D(s)` read from a vintage taken at or after those step
dates. That is SL §7 class 3 (INFO-LEAK) on TRAIN, with REG §1 still
BLOCKED-AS-EVIDENCE until a real pin exists. Draft tests stay green after the
