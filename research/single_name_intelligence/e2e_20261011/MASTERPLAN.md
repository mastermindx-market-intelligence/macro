## 1. Decision and product thesis

Build the deep, continuously maintained research workspace behind Mastermind's compact stock dossier. It must connect **what the business is doing, what this exact security is doing, what changed, what can be inferred, and what Mastermind previously believed**. Its advantage should be coherent, correction-safe evidence and accumulated evaluation history, not a larger collection of generated summaries.

The first reference companies remain Alibaba and Tencent. Alibaba's US ADS, HKD counter and RMB counter are related but not interchangeable identities; Tencent's HKD/RMB counters provide a second test of the relationship grammar. After this reference release, prove the same implementation on NVDA, MSFT and TSLA, then AAPL, AMZN, META and Alphabet. Adding a company must primarily require a coverage profile and source-backed KPI definitions, not bespoke application or trading code. The existing architecture defines this progression; it does not authorize an immediate all-market rollout. [S02–S10]

**Assessment:** The difficult part is not assembling thirteen tabs. It is preserving identity, financial basis, decision-time knowledge, source rights and uncertainty while producing useful synthesis across partially complete owners. The existing architecture is worth recovering, but its August capability and release statements cannot be treated as today's evidence. [S03–S25]

The plan therefore has two concurrent tracks. The **product track** delivers an excellent reference workspace using admitted observations. The **science track** tests the genuinely new residual, response and forecast hypotheses. A useful observation product must not wait for a sophisticated model to win; conversely, a beautiful product must not advertise forecasts before their evidence exists.

### Three completion boundaries

1. **Reference release:** Real Alibaba/Tencent journeys, required evidence coverage, traceable changes, historical versions, honest unavailable states, and production/browser proof. This is a milestone, not the end of SNI.
2. **Research and generalization closure:** Every registered first-generation research family receives an evidence-based disposition; the US stress set and remaining Mag 7 use the same compiler; forecasts and pending outcomes remain recoverable. A well-supported NO-GO can close a research experiment. A merely unobserved outcome cannot.
3. **SNI-8 closure:** Configuration-driven onboarding, coverage-tier economics, reliability and cross-name semantic generalization are demonstrated. Wider index rollout receives an explicit scope/rights/economics decision. Do not call a loop over 500 tickers broad product acceptance.

The entire mission remains incomplete while mandatory natural-time proofs, unresolved release gates, required coverage or admitted research dispositions are outstanding. Preserve these obligations through existing durable owners rather than keeping a model alive to poll.

## 5. Architecture to freeze before integration

### 5.1 Separate three objects

**Identity relationship bundle:** retain the existing SNI-1A pure contract. It proves source-backed relations without I/O or price math. The `snir_`/`snib_` identifiers identify derived records, not canonical securities. [S05, S10]

**Reference twin:** retain the proposed `mastermind.single_name.reference_twin/v1` envelope and its closed owner-key vocabulary, subject to explicit accepted amendments. A compiler consumes pinned owner views and a source/coverage profile. It does not query an LLM or network while rendering a user request. Keep `spec_hash`, immutable generation identity, `as_of`, selected counter, source clocks, rights, correction lineage, material changes, next known events, evidence health and the all-false authority block. [S06]

**Belief/forecast projection:** a separately versioned reader of accepted forecast/evaluation records. It distinguishes observations, deterministic calculations, analyst/model hypotheses, estimated distributions, and unavailable research. An attractive narrative cannot create a probability or valuation output.

### 5.2 Identity and cross-listing guard

Use owner-supplied canonical IDs only. Preserve the fact that a counter can be observed officially but absent from the master; refuse operations needing its missing canonical identity. Historical identities require the accepted historical bridge, not today's issuer grouping applied backward.

The later comparison service may evaluate the documented ADS conversion relationship, contemporaneous FX and market prices. It must bind the ratio's effective interval, quote timestamps, currency/units, venue calendars, corporate actions, price type and staleness thresholds. A BABA/9988 basis is a diagnostic, not guaranteed arbitrage. Tencent's KWEB overnight proxy is never a Tencent ADS quote. Do not pool HKD/RMB counters as extra independent issuers or forecasting examples. [S05, S13, S35, E03]

### 5.3 Time is part of every field

Distinguish economic period, event time, first public availability where evidenced, owner observation/ingest time, owner generation time, SNI publication time and user-visible time where required. A research row at cutoff T may consume only the version available under its declared clock at T. Downloading old facts today does not prove they were known historically.

Corrections append or select a new owner version; they do not overwrite the prior published belief. Current truth and then-known truth are distinct views. A clock mismatch, holiday, lunch break, halt, missing session or failed source must not be flattened into a current zero. A late historical backfill is labeled reconstruction and excluded from the prospective record.

### 5.4 Economics and narrative

Coverage profiles define source-backed metrics by issuer, period, currency, unit, scope, dimensions, accounting/non-GAAP basis, definition version and source occurrence. Revenue, ARR, orders, guidance and management targets are different objects. Derive margins only from matched denominators. Segment changes create version boundaries unless the issuer provides a valid recast. Preserve missing and discontinued metrics.

Each economic driver has an explicit status: **reported fact**, **accounting identity**, **management assertion**, **research hypothesis**, or **estimated relationship**. Do not put an unexplained numerical elasticity on every business or supplier edge. Scenario arithmetic must show assumptions and owner-backed denominators; causal or predictive coefficients require separate evaluation. The unmerged #8401 research is useful input, not accepted implementation authority. [S07, S30]

A language model may propose extraction candidates or explain admitted evidence. Deterministic/owner validation must decide identity, unit/basis, source spans and publication eligibility. Retrieved documents are data, not instructions. The application may not execute tools because a filing or research document tells it to.

### 5.5 Publication, authorization and degraded behavior

Use existing immutable-generation and marker/manifest publication facilities; no new SNI queue or publication control plane. A view is published only after every referenced component is verified, with correction/supersession visible. Reconcile ambiguous writes against the original target; never replay through another operator.

Keep licensed/private evidence behind existing authorization and entitlement checks. Validate permission before opening the provider or private object. Do not put private full text, forecasts or user thesis content in a public R2 artifact, static bundle, URL, browser log or shared cache. User-specific thesis annotations are not canonical company truth.

Return typed current, stale-last-good, unavailable, partial, rights-blocked, identity-unresolved and not-applicable states using the accepted contract. Last-good is permitted only when its age and coverage are visible. One slow optional owner must not block the primary viewport. Never use a demo fixture as an unannounced production fallback.

## 8. Scientific program: difficult questions with falsifiers

**S0 freezes before outcomes are examined:** target and prediction/observation timestamp, eligible cohort and exclusions, outcome horizon, data clocks, feature set, baselines, split rules, trial budget, proper score, uncertainty/power approach, non-inferiority or improvement criterion, and kill/continue rules. No per-name choice of whichever strategy or indicator won historically.

### Residual / normal-versus-abnormal

Start with a transparent rolling market/sector factor baseline and simple historical-volatility normalization. A contemporaneous factor decomposition answers how unusual a realized move was; it does not become a pre-move forecast. Distinguish factor returns known at the observation cutoff from quantities forecast at a decision cutoff.

Compare a bounded conditional-loading/IPCA-family challenger only if lawful point-in-time characteristics and sufficient cross-name coverage exist. A nonlinear model is a later challenger, not the default because it is more sophisticated. IPCA and machine-learning asset-pricing papers motivate candidates; their results do not establish performance for Alibaba/Tencent event responses or intraday forecasts. [E04, E05]

Primary outputs: observed return, baseline common component, residual, uncertainty/normalization, input coverage, model version and abstention. Evaluate out-of-time residual calibration/stability and incremental explanation against a simple baseline. A residual alone is not news impact, insider information or mispricing.

### Event-response surfaces

Begin with a small preregistered set of earnings/result, capital-action and regulatory/material-event families where the source clocks support the question. Do not combine all news into one event class. Define the response anchor, market benchmark, overlap window, concurrent events, comparison group and censoring before analysis.

Use a declared hierarchy: broad event prior -> economic/behavioral neighborhood -> issuer/instrument structure -> bounded own-name adjustment. Publish event count, distinct calendar clusters, effective N, pooling weight and uncertainty. Repeated reports, linked counters, overlapping horizons and same-day market shocks do not create independent samples. Sparse families remain descriptive or cohort-level. Set minimum evidence using a prospective precision/power design, not a universal arbitrary N.

### Expectations, scenarios and forecasting

Separate scenario arithmetic from estimated probabilities. A user assumption can drive a clearly labeled hypothetical valuation scenario without becoming a house forecast. Consensus is usable only with the correct licensed snapshot, currency, period and accounting basis. No paid estimate source means the expectation gap may be unavailable; do not invent consensus from a model's memory.

Start registered short-horizon return/range or event-reaction distributions with simple baselines where inputs qualify. Add KPI/valuation and longer horizons only through their target-specific evidence and economics gates. Do not use one model for every horizon. Candidate uncertainty methods may include adaptive conformal calibration; long-run coverage theory is not a guarantee of per-stock, per-regime or finite-window coverage in this product. Measure interval width and empirical coverage together. [E06]
