## 4. Mechanism-grounded research direction

The following primary research motivates hypotheses; none is a replication or current alpha acceptance:

- Daniel and Moskowitz, Momentum Crashes, NBER w20439 / JFE 2016: examines momentum drawdowns in panic/rebound environments and the behaviour of past losers. Long-short strategy findings do not establish that a long-only leader holder necessarily loses in the same circumstances. https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439
- Novy-Marx, Fundamentally, Momentum is Fundamental Momentum, NBER w20984 (2015): studies earnings momentum and its relation to price momentum. This motivates explicit price-versus-fundamental comparisons, not assuming the old sample's findings settle this product. https://www.nber.org/papers/w20984
- Moskowitz and Grinblatt, Do Industries Explain Momentum?, Journal of Finance (1999): documents an industry component in momentum. A 49-industry model cannot settle within-industry stock selection; a stock study should report industry and within-industry components separately. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00146
- Cohen and Frazzini, Economic Links and Predictable Returns, Journal of Finance (2008): studies economically linked customer/supplier firms. This motivates using the existing time-stamped economic graph, not generic narrative similarity or a new graph authority. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01379.x
- Jarocinski and Karadi, Deconstructing Monetary Policy Surprises, ECB WP2133 / AEJ Macro 2020: distinguishes policy and information shocks using announcement-window rate/equity comovement. The sign of a daily yield move is not a causal shock classification. https://cepr.org/publications/dp12765

Current working decomposition: observed price trajectory; observable business/fundamental trajectory; valuation/expectations evidence when actually available; macro and industry exposures; economic-network transmission; prospective transition forecasts only when separately validated. These are explanatory/input families, not six independent alpha votes. Correlated evidence must not be counted repeatedly.
