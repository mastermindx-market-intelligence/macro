## 3. Cohort freeze requirements

The executable preregistration must specify, with immutable hashes or exact repository identities:

1. point-in-time eligible universe and as-of cutoff;
2. security/issuer/future/contract identities and grouping rules;
3. source/vendor/feed/reference identity;
4. rights basis and redistribution/storage constraints;
5. session, timezone, DST/holiday/early-close/maintenance grammar;
6. price adjustment, correction, settlement and roll rules;
7. minimum history and finite-comparison requirements;
8. inclusion/exclusion rules and mechanically failed-case accounting;
9. stratification variables and random seed;
10. W1B discovery, W2 development and W3 confirmation assignment;
11. contamination/exposure ledger;
12. inactive/delisted support or explicit target-population limitation.

**Owner gate for U.S. equities:** `WS:TECHNICAL-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE` W2-0 is the canonical broad U.S.-equity Daily/Weekly/4H data, clock, correction, coverage, rights and Terminal-parity archaeology. It remains `todo` as of this recovery. A U.S.-equity V2 cohort may not self-declare a load-bearing source plane `ADMIT`, infer rights from successful API access, or mint a competing data/clock contract. Its executable preregistration must either consume an accepted W2-0 store/clock/rights receipt for every load-bearing plane or bind another already-canonical owner receipt that proves the exact required use. Unknown rights remain unknown; a non-null source-rights reference is required before any intended use is treated as allowed.

WMT 720-minute and motivating silver 480-minute cases may be described only as legacy discovery examples and are excluded from confirmation.

## 4. Allowed outcome-blind input families

Every field must name its canonical owner, native units, censoring and `known_at` semantics. Convenience copies do not become new truth.

Candidate families may include:

- signed-return autocorrelation diagnostics;
- variance-ratio diagnostics;
- stationary-residual mean-reversion half-life with explicit non-identification/cap semantics;
- trend/efficiency persistence;
- volatility persistence;
- swing-period distributions using only causally confirmed extrema;
- qualified activity/trade/volume/variance-duration distributions;
- session/overnight decomposition;
- owner-provided regime-duration/state-age features that are causally issued;
- event/catalyst cadence only from a point-in-time owner.

Response statistics or future-resolved episode fields may not route the structural clock. Stock Identity fields that are capped estimates or future-resolved response measurements must be flagged or excluded rather than silently interpreted as clean time constants.

## 5. Mechanical contract

V2-M extends, rather than rebuilds, PR #6803. Each admitted case must prove or abstain on:

- exact subject and data-plane identity;
- source/reference contract;
- bar open/close/availability consistency;
- actual vs nominal duration;
- session/timezone/holiday/DST/maintenance handling;
- chart construction and correction basis;
- observed-reference versus owner-control separation;
- reference kernel parity;
- sufficient warmup and finite comparable history;
- seed/prefix/truncation invariance appropriate to the kernel;
- fixed-grain versus memory-matched kernel interventions;
- G/A/K/D perturbations with separately attributed effects;
- causal thresholds/model states;
- provisional/correction/late-data refusal;
- typed `UNRESOLVED_DATA`/artifact behavior.

The existing #6803 RMA/EMA median-mapped controls are a starting diagnostic, not proof of exact continuous-memory equivalence for nonlinear RSI/RSI-MACD/StochRSI stacks. V2-M must state what memory quantity each intervention actually preserves.

## 7. Outcome rulers

The executable preregistration must choose one consumer task and one primary target before outcomes.

### 7.1 Localization

Freeze an independently defined event/reference, event-confirmation delay, one-event/one-attempt matching, tolerances, false-early/miss treatment and signed latency. A retrospective trough/peak may be an evaluation label, never a contemporaneous input.

### 7.2 Adverse excursion / risk

Measure from first eligible executable observation after decision availability on actual prices and a fixed interval. Any normalization must be ex ante. Future realized volatility/range is forbidden as a favorable normalizer.

### 7.3 False starts

Freeze direction, invalidation condition, time allowance and event matching. Report coverage, signal frequency and delay with the rate.

### 7.4 Economics

Economics is an independent later endpoint with fixed cost/risk assumptions. It cannot rescue failed localization/risk by changing the task.

## 8. Evaluation horizons

The first confirmatory study uses fixed registered horizon roles. A variable clock-derived evaluation horizon is a later experiment and cannot be used to move an unfavorable target after issuance.

`DNR:KILL-OFFHORIZON-VERDICTS` remains binding.

## 9. Dependence, multiplicity and power

The executable preregistration must bind:

- observation count **and** independent block/episode count;
- purge/embargo or equivalent overlap treatment;
- time-block or dependence-aware bootstrap/permutation design;
- era splits required by `DNR:LAW-ERA-SPLIT`;
- no ticker-only bootstrap where `DNR:LAW-TIME-CLUSTERED-CI` applies;
- full attempted model/threshold/grid family in TrialLedger;
- familywise decision rule and look count;
- practical superiority/noninferiority margins;
- joint power for the **complete acceptance rule**, not only one pairwise test.

TrialLedger `effective_n()` is a multiple-testing research-trial count. It is not the independent market-observation N used for statistical power.

## 10. Clock output semantics

Do not force a one-hot timeframe. The structural candidate must be able to return:

- `STABLE_BAND`
- `MULTISCALE`
- `UNSTABLE`
- `UNRESOLVED`

Transition is separate:

- `COMPRESSING`
- `EXPANDING`
- `UNCHANGED`
- `UNKNOWN`

The record must bind subject, signal/kernel version, estimator/protocol version, target, units, lower/central/upper band or supported modes, censoring, uncertainty/calibration scope, source/session/rights/vintage, measurement/publication/receipt/computation/decision-cutoff times, actual filter-state/retention reference, drivers, contrary evidence, falsifiers, abstention reason and a **separate fixed evaluation-target reference**.

## 12. Promotion/kill logic

The program narrows or stops if B4/B5 equal or beat the adaptive candidate with lower instability/complexity, if the edge disappears under A/K matching, if scale transfer fails, if point-in-time qualification removes the gain, or if adaptation requires outcome audition.
