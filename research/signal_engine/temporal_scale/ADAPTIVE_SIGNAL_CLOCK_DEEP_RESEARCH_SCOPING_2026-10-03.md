## Executive scientific conclusion

The promising hypothesis is **not** “find each ticker's best timeframe.” It is a **point-in-time, uncertainty-bearing estimate of temporal scales relevant to an instrument × signal family × regime**, plus a compatibility check against the signal's observation construction and effective memory.

Prefer a **band, distribution, plateau, hierarchy or MULTISCALE** output to a one-hot 4H/1D/3D/Weekly winner. Return **UNRESOLVED** for insufficient evidence and **UNSTABLE** for excessive scale turnover. The claim that such a clock transfers across instruments and improves signal interpretation is **unproven**.

The adversarial null is that, after exact data/session identity, matched effective filter memory, point-in-time controls and fixed multiscale/no-selector challengers, explicit clock selection provides **no stable incremental benefit**. A negative result is a successful scientific outcome.

W0 merged architecture does not prove usefulness. #6803's session/indicator/parity/G/A/K/D machinery does not prove its historical examples. No Adaptive Signal Clock OUT_OF_SAMPLE, PROSPECTIVE or PRODUCTION_PROVEN evidence is established.

## Temporal ontology

| Clock | Meaning | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Market/information | Pace of information, activity, order flow, volatility, liquidity and price discovery | Candidate structural inference target; may be multiscale |
| Observation/sampling | Wall/session/traded-time bars, tick/event, volume, dollar, variance, range, imbalance | Versioned measurement intervention |
| Filter/memory | EMA/RMA decay, nonlinear warmup, oscillator history, feature/state persistence | Measured separately from sampling grain |
| Decision | Thesis/candidate re-evaluation, staleness, expiry, shock monitoring | Downstream context, no authority at birth |
| Outcome/evaluation | Frozen prospective scientific grading horizon | TrialLedger/Evaluation OS owner; never chosen after outcomes |
| Regime/state duration | Causal state age, persistence and transition speed | Upstream structural context unless distinct identification is proven |

A fixed indicator period on different bar grains generally changes elapsed/traded-time memory. For nonlinear indicator stacks, matching one EMA parameter may not match the entire effective kernel. A bullish signal can exist while its memory is slower than the observed information-scale band; that is **compatibility context**, not a new directional trade.

## Preserve G/A/K/D

| Axis | Question | Confounder |
|---|---|---|
| G — grain | What observation aggregation/sampling changed? | Unequal actual bar durations or event-bar formation |
| A — anchor/session | What session/timezone/overnight grammar changed? | Extended hours, maintenance breaks, DST, gaps |
| K — kernel | What effective memory/warmup changed? | Same period count, different half-life |
| D — data/instrument plane | What feed/contract/roll/adjustment/rights identity changed? | Proxy feed or different economic instrument |

Mechanical smoothness, cross counts, indicator geometry and parity are **not** localization or risk-utility proof. Activity/information time, regime duration, decision cadence and evaluation horizon should remain distinct concepts; new acronym letters are not a substitute for identifiability.
