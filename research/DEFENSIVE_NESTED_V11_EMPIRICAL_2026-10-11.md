## 1. What changed

The corrected evidence supports the substance of the Chairman's observation: daily RSI-MACD bullish crosses accompanied by recent weekly AND two-week StochRSI washouts formed a stronger historical cohort in MCD/WMT/WM/COST/PG than crosses with neither washout. The earlier price-MACD and standalone RSI(2) studies did not test this method and cannot be used to dismiss it.

Three other findings remain separate: waiting for a fresh crossover after a watch armed often surrendered return; positive six-month outcomes could contain stop-outs or large adverse excursions; and reacting to new market stress reduced some damage but also surrendered recoveries. No universal optimum or guaranteed bottom/top detection is established.
