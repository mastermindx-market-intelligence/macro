## 2. What the native study actually covers

V1 selects H1 early detection, H2 future-only continuation/false-start durability, and H3 paired migration versus independent legs with common-factor/shared-constituent controls. Its population is seven fixed receiver/donor context pairs, split into defensive-sector and broadening-proxy groups that are never pooled. The pairs are XLP/XLK, XLU/XLK, XLV/XLK, XLP/SMH, DIA/QQQ, IWM/QQQ and RSP/QQQ. XLP/SMH is excluded from the reconstructed P2 partition because its instrument splice is unverified.

The native arms use price information available by the origin, with specific exceptions for source-limited breadth arms. A1 is a frozen ratio-momentum hysteresis construction; A2 waits five sessions for an A1 state that remains on; B3 is the receiver's benchmark-relative momentum comparator; B4 uses past-fitted beta adjustment. V1 explicitly excludes sector-cycle, positioning, flows, options, sentiment and LLM outputs from those arms.

Its primary evaluation horizon is 20 sessions and secondary descriptive horizon is 63, not this supplement's candidate 5/20/60 set. It already distinguishes next-session execution, common-origin comparisons and an initial-origin policy path. Therefore the presence of a fixed five-session delayed arm is **not by itself evidence that every native economic comparison is unfair**. The statistic and exact path must be inspected before asserting a defect.

The native retrospective zig-zag onset is an evaluation label, not a feature. Retrospective outcome labels can be legitimate. The required issue is whether origin selection, predictors, matching and denominators use information they should not. The supplement rejects the earlier advisory claim that all future-derived labels are inherently leakage.

V1 excludes dynamic theme-membership work Q04, unqualified second-region work Q10, and full economic simulation Q12. Breadth C3 is source-limited development/prospective material, not a V1 confirmatory feature. None of these exclusions is lifted just because this supplement discusses their eventual usefulness.
