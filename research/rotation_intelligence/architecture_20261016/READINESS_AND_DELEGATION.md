# Readiness and delegation: from research design to qualified implementation

Date: 2026-10-16. Status: pre-implementation work plan under PR #9507. This is a dependency map and a set of future bounded packets, not a new queue, worker assignment or runtime permission. Existing programme, source writers and release owners remain intact.

## 1. The next dependency, not the easiest new feature

The highest-value dependency is a statistically identifiable, executable research target with qualified inputs. More chart indicators or a new dashboard will not repair an unqualified estimand.

The active Sol responsibility is to move the existing scientific/data path forward, not wait indefinitely for a historical owner title. Before modifying canonical LR-W6/V3 or an active source branch, resolve its actual current writer and outstanding effects once through the existing owner. A historical role is not a veto; a live writer or unresolved modifying effect is a real fence. This supplement stays path-disjoint and supplies the proposed design without taking that writer's place.

### Dependency sequence

`source/identity contracts + target/accounting specification`

`→ native statistical size/power qualification and dated registration`

`→ admitted historical development and honest held-out/prospective evaluation`

`→ forecast/policy allowed-use qualification`

`→ bounded owner-adapter implementation`

`→ existing consumer integration, normal-source production/browser proof`

Metadata/source-clock audits and mechanism design can proceed in parallel with statistical design. A held market-data read does not prevent those independent tasks. Source implementation must not outrun the qualifications that its consumer would claim.

## 3. Industry mechanism research book — initial hypotheses

The following table defines research questions, not current trades, verified signals or already available fields. It frontloads economic structure so later feature work does not become a generic indicator pile. Each row must retain company/industry granularity, publication time, expected timing and an explicit competing explanation.

| Archetype | Proposed mechanism and observations | Required distinction | Falsifier and incumbent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semiconductor supply chain | Orders, customer inventories, pricing, utilization, installed capacity and future capex | Memory, equipment, foundry and fabless economics; demand growth versus capacity response | No incremental value beyond price/revisions, or apparent scarcity explained by inventory timing. Extend existing demand-chain/fingerprint sources and dated exposure graph |
| Software/cloud and AI monetization | Bookings/RPO conversion, retention where comparable, margins, sales efficiency and customer spending | Announced AI demand versus recognized revenue/cash; capex payer versus recipient; organic versus acquisition effects | Backlog fails to convert or revisions already contain all the information. Extend earnings/revisions and demand-chain owners, not another narrative score |
| Banks and lenders | Loan repricing, deposit/funding costs, credit loss, balance-sheet duration and capital constraints | Beneficial curve change versus worsening credit; rate level versus marginal change | Yield sensitivity disappears after credit/funding conditions; shared sector label masks opposite exposures. Use existing financial-statement and macro/risk owners |
| Energy/materials producers | Inventories, capacity discipline, project supply, realized prices, input costs and financing | Demand-led improvement versus temporary supply disruption; commodity beta versus company cash generation | Economics deteriorate despite rising commodity prices, or no forward increment after curve/price controls. Extend existing commodity/activity/exposure sources |
| Utilities/grid/power | Demand commitments, physical capacity constraints, financing, project economics and regulatory recovery | Rate-sensitive valuation versus recoverable investment opportunity; signed commitment versus narrative | Financing/dilution overwhelms growth or commitments fail to become usable cash flows. Extend power-scarcity/demand-chain and source-qualified filings |
| Healthcare industries | Reimbursement, procedure/utilization trends, labor/input costs, patent/product concentration and policy events | Providers, insurers, devices, pharma and biotech are not one economic bet | A broad healthcare read vanishes ex-largest members or is driven by an unrelated subgroup. Reuse member dispersion and existing company/catalyst evidence |

For every proposed observation, the researcher records: economic quantity; unit; exact source; release/capture/revision clocks; company/cohort mapping; contemporaneous versus leading interpretation; plausible alternatives; and the incremental test. Missing expected values do not permit fabricated surprise scores. Annual headcount is not a live hiring feed. Company guidance is not analyst consensus. A signed order is not necessarily recognized revenue.

A model may use a qualified economic interaction, but the mechanism book does not authorize the prohibited rotation×cycle gate, LLM detection or connect-flow gating. It does not declare a new sector-classification authority. Any exact proposal conflicting with DNR must be returned for construction-specific adjudication rather than renamed.

## 4. Research challenge ladder

Start with the smallest model capable of falsifying the hypothesis. The research worker should return the best supported conclusion, including a null, not be rewarded for making a more complex model win.

**R0: mathematical and data invariants.** Prove no identity, clock, basis, overlap or accounting artifact can produce the intended success by construction. Standalone witnesses in this PR cover twelve such examples and a known-covariance demonstration; they are not native qualification.

**R1: regularized receiver-only baseline.** Freeze the population, horizons, information and cost/bill convention. Compare with simple selectors and the identical unconditioned specification. No real outcome is loaded until the native read gate permits it.

**R2: independent information.** Add one admitted source family at a time. Explain the economic mechanism and its clock before fitting; preserve multiple-testing accounting. A new source can be a useful measurement improvement without earning forecasting authority.

**R3: advanced challengers.** Partial pooling, nonlinear interactions, dynamic exposures, causal change detection, selection-aware uncertainty and cost-aware adjustment each require a named failure of R1/R2 they are meant to address. Demonstrate that the actual assumptions, feedback delay and sample support fit. No new always-on model service is required to test a challenger.

**R4: policy and stock-expression evaluation.** Hold group decisions fixed when testing residual stock selection. Evaluate cash/no-action, concentration, look-through overlap and execution costs. Do not select the group retrospectively because the stocks subsequently won.

## 5. Later implementation packets — prepared, not dispatched

Each packet below must be bound to the actual accepted design/source revision and current writer before dispatch. They are examples of bounded execution units, not approval to edit those paths now.

### Packet A — As-known group/feature reader

**Objective:** a research caller can obtain the eligible historical group/input snapshot or an explicit unavailable result, without current-membership or later-vintage leakage.

**Incumbent surface:** existing Data OS and Theme Graph/membership adapters; existing leadership measurement input contract. No duplicate identity service or dataset mirror.

**Inputs:** exact admitted snapshot references, membership/weight versions, calendar/basis and cutoff. **Output:** typed measurement/coverage with immutable lineage. **Acceptance:** later-announced membership, a revised macro value, pre-inception ETF, missing delist treatment and incompatible currency/basis are rejected or explicitly separated by evidence mode. A true zero survives; missing is not zero. **Escalation:** absent source-use/availability evidence or a live overlapping writer.

### Packet B — Basket range qualification

**Objective:** distinguish synchronized basket extrema from weighted individual daily extrema while preserving the existing close-based analytical product.

**Incumbent surface:** `engine/basket_index.py` and its qualified consumers, only after actual custody resolution. **Non-goal:** changing T2 math, importing a new price feed or relabelling an approximate range as observed.

**Acceptance:** anti-correlated constituent witness; asynchronous/missing marks; fixed shares versus rebalanced weights; close/total-return basis; explicit synthetic-range metadata. Existing close outputs remain compatible where the same method is retained. **Escalation:** required synchronized source absent; do not fabricate intraday history.

### Packet C — Native estimand/size/power integration

**Objective:** implement the accepted dated LR registration exactly in the existing native evaluator, not a new research harness.

**Inputs:** accepted protocol and immutable source pins, null/alternative generator specification, margins/error budget, and deterministic examples. **Acceptance:** mechanical lead cannot pass economic superiority; null receiver-only drift and unequal-beta common-factor worlds do not qualify donor increment; all eligible origins retained; size and power records bind executed bytes. P1 remains refused until the native gate passes. **Escalation:** ambiguity changes the estimand, margin, verdict or look budget.

### Packet D — Forecast contract adapter

**Objective:** emit a qualified research estimate with complete target, population, horizon, model, uncertainty and source identity, or explicit unmeasured output.

**Incumbent surface:** owner-authorized extension of `ResearchEstimate` and existing publishers. **Acceptance:** incomplete identity, wrong horizon/cohort, unsupported population, degraded source, coverage-as-probability and unauthorized score promotion all fail. Existing GroupMeasurement and TransitionObservation meanings/permissions are unchanged. **Escalation:** no admitted model; the correct result is unavailable, not a placeholder probability.

### Packet E — Costed policy evaluator

**Objective:** compare early, delayed, no-action and group-selection policies on the same initial capital and terminal dates.

**Incumbent surface:** existing evaluation and portfolio-risk owners. **Acceptance:** bill accrual while waiting; actual next-executable fills; no-confirmation path; costs once per actual trade; no borrow in long-only baseline; overlapping episodes do not become an impossible capital allocation. Separate trade-independent horizon labels from continuous portfolio returns. **Escalation:** price, cash or cost assumptions cannot be qualified.

### Packet F — Existing product and grounded-chat consumer

**Objective:** the user sees what changed, what is observed versus predicted, why a candidate is supported/contested, and when no allocation edge is qualified.

**Incumbent surface:** existing Sector Intelligence/Rotation Command/Terminal/grounded-chat path. **Acceptance:** exact source/forecast receipts; horizon and benchmark clarity; explicit unavailable and stale states; rank uncertainty/ties; original observation time retained; no display-to-sizing permission jump. Include responsive signed-in browser proof and normal-source publication/rollback at the actual release stage. **Non-goal:** a new recommendation service or data store.

## 6. Acceptance matrix for adversarial review

The bounded implementation review must cover, at minimum:

| Category | Required adverse cases |
|---|---|
| Identity | Duplicate symbols; sector reclassification; theme overlap; unseen membership; missing weight map |
| Time | Future-known membership; revised macro input; partial session; UTC/display-epoch mismatch; stale benchmark |
| Basis | Price versus total return; mismatched FX; ETF before inception; stock split; unknown terminal loss |
| Measurement | Missing versus zero; changing breadth denominator; synthetic candle range; rounding-driven flow; no implied investor-level transfer |
| Statistics | Same-date factor exposure; unequal betas; overlapping targets; immature training labels; full-sample preprocessing; selection on future success |
| Policy | No future confirmation; waiting cash; common terminal date; cost double-counting; turnover/capacity; concentration through duplicate themes |
| Consumer | Wrong horizon/cohort; incomplete ResearchEstimate; marginal versus selected calibration; unqualified probability; stale source with new publication time |
| Operations | One writer; original carrier; exact source and result hashes; no replay of uncertain effects; no public-data permission inferred from a model claim |

Tests should distinguish the forbidden behavior, not pass because an unrelated import fails. Unit tests establish implementation behavior; empirical qualification and production proof remain separate.

## 7. Delegation economics and closure

Use the installed M2 shell Fabric adapter and current routing policy for independently checkable work. A mechanical source/contract census is C0; a frozen adapter/test change is usually C1; nontrivial statistical or cross-file review is C2. Unresolved architecture remains with the principal. Do not label a hard statistical design mechanical to force a cheaper provider; do not reserve Fable for routine extraction merely because the programme matters.

Choose the shallowest useful graph. A small number of independent leaves is better than a large hierarchy whose review costs dominate. Concurrency is bounded by live admission, source custody, budget and review capacity—not the planned number of packets or an assumed per-chat quota. Preserve original root/parent, stable IDs and one writer per path. No budget multiplication or unsupported native nesting claim.

Each real dispatch must contain exact source, one objective, non-goals, allowed effects, checkable output and return path. Retain the same-ID result and adjudicate actual evidence; successful process exit is not acceptance. Independent review requires provenance and actual review, not the word independent in a packet. No watcher or durable background promise is created by this plan.

## 8. Stop and continue rules for the project

Continue independent architecture/source-metadata work while a particular data or statistical use is blocked. Do not escalate historical-owner silence as a Chairman-only obstacle. Preserve an actual permission denial or unresolved effect; do not reroute it through another tool or child.

The architecture package is complete only when its linked documents, source references, synthetic outputs and review dispositions are published and verified. Product implementation starts at its separately ready dependency; it does not start because a plan or PR exists. The broader capability remains incomplete until qualified decisions work through the actual product under the required production proof.
