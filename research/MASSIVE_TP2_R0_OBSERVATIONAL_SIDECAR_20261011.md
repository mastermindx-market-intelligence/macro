## Semantics, disclosure and guards

The new separate candidate schema `darkpool.tp2_r0_observed_research/v0` carries the typed states `R0_CONTEXT_NOT_SUPPLIED`, `R0_CONTEXT_NOT_YET_KNOWABLE`, or `R0_OBSERVATIONAL_CONTEXT_HOLD`. A source with original R0 `decision_ns` after the requested time carries **no future ticker/session/manifest, receipt SHA or derived values**. Known observations require NYSE US-equity RTH opening alignment with daylight-saving time, a 1–5 completed-minute window, original source-mode/distribution/authority (all HOLD), original SHA policy/quote receipts, bounded counts/exact notional conservation and valid signed descriptive ratios. These are **internal presentation controls**, not a complete market holiday calendar, vendor authenticity, historical correction finality or a quote-rule accuracy study.

Permitted read-only displays are descriptive: buy/sell/unknown sampled **quote-location proxies**, classified-notional coverage, signed `pressure_balance` (not a validated bid/ask aggressor), same-completed-window midpoint movement (not a future-return prediction), and bid/ask displayed NBBO-size recovery **states** (not hidden resting order or true absorption). All original nanosecond clocks are decimal strings for JavaScript safety. The artifact explicitly maintains `join_to_tp1_or_tpb=false`, `source_authenticated=false`, `public_delivery_allowed=false`, `rank_trade_alert_authority=false`, `forward_label=null`, `absorption_signal=null`.

**No volume/notional summation with TP1's provisional raw TRF series or TP-B's condition-qualified historical total.** Their sources, venue definitions, correction vintages, clock cutoffs and denominators remain separately unqualified. The existing TP2 `v0` source and its canonical private serializer/readback are not modified by this sidecar; a later authenticated combined schema requires an explicit consumer version agreement and owner authorization.
