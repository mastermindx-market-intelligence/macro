Primary/source locators:

The aggregate differences are **-4.69% EBITDA** and **-5.66% EPS**. They are descriptive same-horizon differences between returned views, **not proven within-analyst revisions**. A generic counterexample explains why this matters. Estimates `[100, 100, 200]` have median 100. Remove one unchanged 100 and the remaining median becomes 150: a **50% aggregate increase with no analyst changing a forecast**. The product therefore needs sample-entry/exit and paired-contributor controls before labeling an aggregate move a revision.

### Hydro mechanism

Fortum attributed weaker Q2 results partly to achieved-power-price effects while describing hydrology, nuclear availability, hedges and optimization. A hydro portfolio's realized economics therefore depend on physical water availability, dispatch timing, location, technical constraints and contract vintages. Higher market prices can coexist with lower saleable generation or already-fixed receipts. Research should reconcile achieved price to the correct volume before inferring earnings sensitivity from spot power.

## 2. Neste — structural volume growth is already paired with expected margin normalization

The Neste/Vara source labelled **2026-09-11** shows a single forecast curve, not a revision history. Selected FY2026/FY2028 medians are:
- renewable-product sales volume: **4.095m tonnes -> 5.500m tonnes**;
- renewable-product comparable sales margin: **USD1,024/t -> USD743/t**;
- group comparable EBITDA: **EUR4,039m -> EUR3,466m**.

Sources:
- https://www.neste.com/investors/share-and-shareholders/consensus-estimates
- https://vara-services.com/neste/consensus-estimates/
- https://www.neste.com/en-us/news/nestes-half-year-financial-report-for-january-june-2026

Across that one snapshot, volume is approximately **+34.31%**, the margin measure **-27.44%**, and group comparable EBITDA **-14.19%**. This is a cross-horizon expectation curve, **not evidence that analysts cut a fixed-year forecast**. The cells have different sample counts and independent medians, so they must not be multiplied into a fabricated joint analyst model.

The stronger research question is therefore not whether renewable-fuel demand grows. It is whether actual conversion economics, feedstock flexibility, utilization, product realization, required capital and working capital become better or worse than the margin normalization already embedded in the forecast. New capacity creates shareholder value only if its future spread and cash conversion exceed its incremental capital and financing burden.

A future expectation layer should show fixed target years side by side and compare later observations against the **same target year**. Rolling from FY2026 to FY2027 because time passed is horizon rollover, not an earnings revision.

## 3. Northern Lights — carbon tonnes do not reveal who owns the economics

Northern Lights reports initial storage operation, a wastewater-derived CO2 pilot with receiving capacity **up to 7,000 tonnes/year**, and a separate expansion from **1.5m tonnes/year to at least 5m tonnes/year**. Its Exergi arrangement is described as up to **900,000 tonnes/year**. These are capacity/customer figures, not measured annual delivered tonnage, storage revenue, carbon-credit value, or partner earnings.

Sources:
- https://norlights.com/news/northern-lights-jv-has-successfully-stored-first-co%E2%82%82/
- https://norlights.com/news/northern-lights-has-injected-first-co%E2%82%82-from-wastewater/
- https://norlights.com/news/northern-lights-is-expanding-capacity-through-commercial-agreement/
- https://norlights.com/news/northern-lights-enters-into-cross-border-transport-and-storage-agreement-with-orsted/

The economically useful chain is: capture equipment/operation -> conditioning/transport -> terminal/pipeline -> injection/storage -> monitoring/liability -> any separate credit issuance and sale. One tonne moving through several links cannot be counted as several tonnes of end demand, and the storage operator does not automatically own the emitter's avoided compliance cost or a removal credit sold by another party.

Hypothetical allocation: an emitter avoids a charge of 100 per eligible tonne, pays 60 for capture and 25 for transport/storage. The residual is 15 before other costs; storage-provider revenue is **25**, not 100 or 125. This is invented arithmetic, not a current carbon price, tariff, credit rule or project return.

For re-rating research, the evidence that matters is contracted throughput on workable terms, actual operation, expansion capex/funding, cash rights, and liability allocation. First injection resolves a different uncertainty from funded expansion or a customer guarantee.

- FY2028 revenue **EUR23,897m** with the same displayed revenue panel scope;

Sources:

- https://www.vestas.com/en/investor/share-and-capital-structure/analysts
- https://www.vestas.com/en/investor/reports-and-presentations/financial

The ratio of aggregate FY2026 mean EBIT to aggregate mean revenue is approximately **8.47%**, within management's August 12 **7-9%** EBIT-margin range. This is not the mean of individual analyst margin forecasts. Management guidance is not consensus, and the post-Q2 panel cannot be used as pre-Q2 expectations.

The displayed EPS profile rises faster than revenue through FY2028. That means the research hurdle is more demanding than "wind installations grow": backlog conversion, equipment margins, service profitability, working capital, financing and share effects must be tested against profitability improvement that is already visible in the source-labelled forecast.

## 5. VERBUND — retrospective price, cash and P/E decomposition without fabricating a forward re-rating

VERBUND's H1 report shows achieved hydropower prices **EUR117.2/MWh -> EUR86.2/MWh** and hydro coefficient **0.76 -> 0.68**. Its year-end/half-year tables also permit a bounded retrospective market bridge.

Sources:
- https://www.verbund.com/media/5egl3i51/verbund-half-year-financial-report-2026-englisch-final.pdf
- https://www.verbund.com/en/group/investor-relations/latest-financial-results-and-5-year-comparison/latest-financial-results
- https://www.verbund.com/en/group/investor-relations/latest-financial-results-and-5-year-comparison/5-year-comparison
- https://www.verbund.com/de/konzern/news-presse/presseaussendungen/2026/7/30/corporate-news-ergebnis-quartale-1-22026

Year-end 2025 price **EUR62.0** and June 2026 price **EUR55.6** imply approximately **-10.32% price-only**. The H1 report confirms **EUR3.15/share** of cash dividends paid during the half, including **EUR1.15 special**. For a continuously held share, adding that gross cash gives approximately **-5.24%**, before taxes/fees and without reinvestment. This is an explicit cash-return illustration, not a replicated total-return index.

The balance-sheet period must also be aligned. Reported net debt **EUR2,818.1m at 2025-12-31 -> EUR3,817.4m at 2026-06-30** increases **EUR999.3m**. Market capitalization at the paired endpoints moves **EUR21,539.8m -> EUR19,316.3m**. Adding the two reported measures gives **EUR24,357.9m -> EUR23,133.7m**, but these sums are **not certified enterprise values** because all minority interests, non-operating assets and claim definitions are not reconciled.

The issuer's annual comparison reports year-end price **70 -> 62**, EPS **5.40 -> 4.29**, and published P/E **12.97x -> 14.46x** from 2024 to 2025. Price falls approximately **11.43%**, EPS falls approximately **20.56%**, while published P/E rises approximately **11.49%**. A higher observed multiple therefore does not itself prove the market awarded a quality premium: the earnings denominator can fall faster than price.

These are retrospective reported ratios. Later full-year earnings do not prove what investors forecast on the preceding year-end date. H1 earnings also must not be mechanically doubled into a forward valuation. The proper R5B study still requires estimates independently known before the event, fixed horizons, exact listing/corporate-action treatment and a declared return convention.
