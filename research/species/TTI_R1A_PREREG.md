## Feature clock and eligible information
Premarket decision is 09:25 ET, using only bars ending at or before 09:25 (last possible bar starts 09:20). Early price-reference entry is the exact 09:35 bar open, ten minutes after decision. Opening confirmation occurs 09:45 using the three completed 09:30/35/40 bars; late price-reference entry is exact 09:50 open. Neither decision may inspect its entry or later bar. Previous session means the calendar's immediate predecessor, never last available day. All day windows use the existing calendar and real UTC inversion, never machine-local timezone.

Require complete prior RTH observations plus 20 preceding complete daily true ranges for ATR and 40-60 paired prior stock/QQQ close-to-close returns for beta. Beta is covariance/variance on the preceding calendar-aligned returns, clipped to [0,3]; constant/insufficient benchmark variation is unknown. No current RTH result enters ATR/beta. Both AH and PM require at least six observed positive-volume bars, at least 120 minutes from first start through last end, and no interior observation gap greater than 60 minutes. The preceding AH last observed bar must end at or after 19:30; PM must end exactly at 09:25. Prior early-close AH is unqualified and excluded with a separate count. Zero-volume bars do not supply price evidence to this particular recipe, but remain visible in qualification. These participation rules are preregistered research comparability limits, not feed-completeness or execution-liquidity claims. All eligibility failures are counted.

For each AH/PM segment: return = last close / first open - 1; signed efficiency = (last close-first open)/(abs(first close-first open)+sum(abs(successive observed close differences))), zero for a zero path. Gaps remain observed jumps, never interpolated. Bar VWAP proxy is expanding sum(HLC3*volume)/sum(volume). The fraction of observed closes strictly above their expanding proxy is sample-weighted, not clock-time-weighted. The archive discarded the provider `vw` field, so these are NOT exact trade VWAPs or evidence of aggressor flow. Positive-volume span, maximum gap and path length remain printed with every candidate.

Prior weakness = previous regular close-to-close return <0 OR the three-session close change <= -0.5 times prior ATR20. A three-session change is price difference over exactly three scheduled sessions, not three observations. It does not prove manipulation, capitulation, fake breakdown or news foreknowledge.

## Frozen arms
ALL_EARLY: every comparable premarket row, entry 09:35. GAP_UP: same row with last PM close above previous RTH close and PM segment return >0. PERSISTENT: both AH and PM segment returns >0, efficiency >=0.25 and above-expanding-bar-VWAP fraction >=0.65 in each. WEAKNESS_PERSISTENT: PERSISTENT plus prior weakness. WEAKNESS_RECLAIM: prior weakness and last PM close above its final bar VWAP proxy; no persistence requirement. ALL_LATE: every comparable row, entry 09:50. PERSISTENT_OPEN_ACCEPT: PERSISTENT at 09:25 plus complete first 15 RTH minutes, 09:40 close above both PM last close and first-15 bar VWAP proxy, and first-15 low at or above PM low; entry 09:50. One observation per stock/date/arm. Long stock price direction only in this batch; top/short symmetry is NOT presumed and will need its own preregistration.
