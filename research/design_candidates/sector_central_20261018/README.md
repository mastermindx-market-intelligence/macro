# Sector Central design candidates

These three preserved documents are unreleased design proposals. Their original
text and identifiers are unchanged. They do not supply an approved migration
packet or authorize a consuming build.

Admission to `research/migration_packets/` still requires the existing approved
RIG reference and its `RIG-RECEIPT` under RIG section 8, L8. Once that qualification
exists, the Sector Central delivery owner can promote the applicable proposal.

## Classification and retained release boundary

These documents retain the original Sector programme's proposed decisions and
92-capability dispositions as review inputs only. Under the assigned principal's
October 19 documentation-classification ruling, this namespace correction
supersedes the prior no-relocation instruction solely to withdraw unapproved
migration admission. It does not approve the proposed design or authorize a build.

Any consuming Sector migration, build or registry-compliance claim still requires
Full RIG for the actual frozen October before/after artifacts and the complete
92-capability scope, including the required independent reviews and genuine
approval receipt. No historical or unrelated receipt substitutes for that approval.
The candidate directory cannot authorize a migration builder or earn conformance
credit. See `DEC:UNAPPROVED-SECTOR-DOCUMENTATION-DEADMISSION` and the explicit
classification reconciliation on PR #9648.
