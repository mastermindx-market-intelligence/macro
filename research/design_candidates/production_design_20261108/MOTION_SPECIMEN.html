How this reaches the product

Use lib/illus.py with the existing templates/illus.css and templates/illus.js. This file embeds their output from the pinned source; it does not introduce a second chart renderer.

In-page figures reveal once. Opening an evidence dialog may reveal its chart again after the dialog entrance settles. Closing it restores focus to the invoking control. Reduced motion skips the choreography and shows the same final data.

Read the producer’s availability and timestamps before rendering. An empty or insufficient series shows an honest null. Interactive candles, crosshairs and zoom remain on the existing Terminal chart stack.

This specimen uses an explicitly synthetic index to demonstrate motion. It is not a market series, forecast, score, performance result, or live product path.
