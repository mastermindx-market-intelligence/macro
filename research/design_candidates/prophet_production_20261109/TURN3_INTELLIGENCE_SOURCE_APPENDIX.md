## 2. Early Leadership: independent peer evidence with honest scope

### What is already implemented

`build_early_leadership_evidence` in S2 builds a content-addressed descriptive envelope from candidate, peer-excluding-candidate, ThemeState, economic exposure, setup observation and entry geometry. It does not discover candidates, rank, decide B4 availability, originate plans or trade. Its non-authority flags remain false.

The peer contract demands `candidate_excluded:true` **and** `issuer_aliases_excluded:true`. It checks member/priced counts and exact coverage reconciliation. `priced_peer_count<=1` requires null equal-weight return, median return and positive share, and emits `UNAVAILABLE_SINGLETON_OR_EMPTY`; partial pricing is `PARTIAL`. A singleton is not strong confirmation.

Candidate, peer and theme must share the same as-of; peer/theme membership vintage must agree; candidate/peer return window and return basis must agree; issuer/security/theme joins must match. Candidate/setup/geometry share `identity_epoch`, `episode_id`, `candidate_generation_id`, with missing lineage kept unavailable rather than allocated by this compiler. The geometry projection explicitly says `GEOMETRY_CONTEXT_ONLY_B4_REMAINS_OWNER`.

`_theme_owner_read` consumes `gmi.theme_state_read/v1` with owner status `UNAVAILABLE`, `DESCRIPTIVE` or `QUALIFIED`; it binds `generation_id`, `state_sha256`, generation/effective/known clocks, subject identity and qualification. A digest binds a generation; it does not authenticate a source or confer entitlement. Economic exposure distinguishes `CONFIRMED_DIRECT`, `CONFIRMED_INDIRECT`, `MEMBERSHIP_ONLY`, `UNAVAILABLE`; confirmed claims require evidence refs. Theme membership alone is not economic exposure.

### Hardening / implementation obligation

Keep the share of priced peers visible. Do not let the frontend rebuild peers from a current membership list.

**Falsifiers / acceptance:** changing a candidate's own return must not change its excluded-peer result; adding another listing of the same issuer must not leak it into peers; later membership, mixed price adjustment basis/window, future-known fields, mismatched identity/generation, inconsistent coverage and non-null singleton metrics must fail qualification. ## 3. Quality Earnings: factual comparability before prediction

### Existing numerical contracts and the unbound per-share gap

S3 `MetricBasis` carries issuer, metric, fiscal period/role/start/end, currency, unit, accounting basis and share basis. `reported_change` checks availability before the decision, comparable basis and nonoverlapping chronological periods. A same-period restatement is a correction, not growth. `surprise` requires a matching expectation basis and both forecast and availability **strictly before** actual public release. Missing expectation is `EXPECTATION_UNAVAILABLE`; a seasonal EPS comparison is not consensus surprise.

Surprise calibration excludes the same event, errors unavailable before release, differing expectation method and differing calibration scope. Insufficient history or zero/invalid variance yields null standardized surprise. The code deliberately avoids unstable percentage-beat denominators. `matched_revisions` freezes the fiscal period and uses the common contributor intersection; entered/exited contributors and changing-roster residual are separate. Duplicate/conflicting same-time revisions fail.

**Concrete source gap:** `per_share_bridge` is scalar arithmetic, not source-bound evidence. It decomposes EPS change exactly using income-first then share-count effects. Its caller `factual_dossier` checks per-share schema/kind/non-authority at lines915–918, but does **not** perform the issuer/event/time/basis matching imposed on reported-change and revision children. A correct arithmetic bridge can therefore be attached without the same provenance qualification. Close this within the existing Earnings/D5 adapter: qualify comparable income/share observations, issuer, fiscal periods, GAAP/adjusted basis, currency/unit, diluted/basic share basis, source revisions and availability before calculating or attaching the child; otherwise show unavailable with the exact missing fact. Do not call the helper a live source-admitted diluted-share capability.

### Original facts, later correction and three separate event clocks

S4 D5 selects a source revision only when **all three** `source_available_at`, `observed_at`, and `generated_at` are <= the candidate decision cut. It selects newest source time, then newest observed time; unresolved ties are conflicted, not a license to choose the attractive value. Missing clocks become unknown. `source_effective_at` is an intentional named-null for earnings results; `captured_at` is also not asserted because the revision receipt does not expose per-source recording. Do not invent either from a nearby timestamp.

The initial release, subsequent call/transcript, and later filing must remain distinct source events with their native public/owner-known clocks. Release-time research cannot consume later call guidance or a filing merely because all belong to the same fiscal quarter. Expand missing event/species support at the existing Earnings/Event Workspace/D5 owner; do not promise a generic filing timeline from the current fixed comparator. Future owner evidence must preserve which source first made each fact usable, which source later corrected it and which version a user actually saw.

D5 retains `correction.decision_version_ref_ids`, `later_correction_ref_ids`, `later_revision_receipts`, `later_revision_state`, `current_state`. A visible later revision with no projectable refs is `OBSERVED_UNPROJECTABLE` and current state unknown; it is not silently current. Dependence groups identify common information origin; a release and its derivative summaries cannot count as independent corroboration.

S5 `project_earnings_detail` consumes one admitted vector. The Q06 comparable-revenue loader is bound to one accepted record and fixed rights/source receipts; it is not a general SEC/consensus feed. **Falsifiers / acceptance:** GAAP versus adjusted mismatch, fiscal rollover, current-to-prior same-period corrections, changed analyst roster, post-release forecast, late capture/generation, selected attractive event, diluted/basic shares mixed, or already-earned release gap must not generate a better historical signal. ## 4. Cycle Capture: path survival and original shareholder capture

S6 is a **candidate** implementation on PR8308, not main or a deployed feed. It composes incumbent capital-need, cash-runway/share-count facts; it does not collect filings or infer financing availability. `build_funding_path`, `recovery_scenario`, `build_cycle_equity_case` and `cycle_equity_brief` provide a useful owner-preserving seam if the incumbent candidate clears its own checks and integration.

The path uses `minimum_operating_cash`, accessible versus restricted cash, dated funding events, debt, share count, original-cohort shares and contributions. Equity raises add gross proceeds less fees to cash while increasing shares; debt draw adds net cash and debt; conversion reduces debt and increases shares. Breaches include the starting decision cut before a later rescue financing. Completeness must cover the recovery date; missing cash/debt, incomplete debt ladder, unknown restricted cash or unestablished funding schedule stay limitations. State is `UNAVAILABLE`, `BREACHED_BEFORE_RECOVERY` or `FUNDED_TO_RECOVERY`; a mathematically funded scenario still does not establish real financing access.

Scenario events carry `observed` versus `scenario_assumption`. Display those distinctions at the row level. A Paper cash path of 100→80→−10 against an operating floor30 implies a40 shortfall at the final point; it is an illustrative path, not sourced issuer financing evidence. Show the timing of the breach, not just terminal cash after a rescue.

`recovery_scenario` explicitly preserves `security_state`, original purchase cost, additional original-cohort contributions, total contributed, original-cohort terminal value, scenario return on total contributed, and the EV needed to recover original purchase versus all contributed capital. Replacement shares cannot be silently spliced onto canceled original shares. Distribution to an original canceled security needs a qualified claim mapping; absent mapping remains unavailable. Financing cash must not be counted twice in enterprise-to-equity conversion. Enterprise value recovery alone does not show original shareholders benefit.

**Falsifiers / acceptance:** a company may survive while old shares receive no value; a later financing cannot erase an earlier funding breach; a cash raise cannot increase value without reflecting proceeds, fees and dilution consistently; a contribution-funded recovery cannot be divided only by initial investment. Include failed firms, delisted/canceled securities and reorganizations in the eventual research population. Do not select cycle troughs retrospectively or relabel a losing tactical plan as a long-term core. Q07 qualifies a proving domain, Q08 qualifies source-backed survival/original-equity economics, and Q17 separately tests milestone hold/core/add/exit. DNR washout-turn, rotation-cycle-confluence, regime-scorecard, fused-composite and Prophet-pop-merge constraints remain binding through S1. Lack of data/rights/history blocks a claim; it does not resurrect a killed mechanism.
