# Prophet × Sector Intelligence: beta-first integration amendment

**Version:** 5.0, 9 November 2026 America/Vancouver. **Authority:** new Chairman-directed scope after the accepted four-turn editorial baseline. **Nature:** product, source-owner, design, research and implementation contract; no production implementation or scientific promotion is asserted.

The original 38 design-to-owner contracts, 29 B build units, 24 Q research packets, 12 D decisions, 36 R requirements, seven P delivery packets and all eight sleeve families remain. This amendment adds twenty integration contracts SI01–SI20 and twenty-four discriminating proof cases SP01–SP24. It does not renumber or replace the existing programme, create a new runtime, revive killed mechanisms, or authorize protected-outcome access, capital allocation or trades.

## 18.1 Governing decision: market participation before selection claims

Prophet's default investigation must answer five connected questions: **What is the market doing? Where is participation strengthening? Which companies add independent evidence? Is there a qualified action under the selected policy? What did each layer contribute?** Sector Intelligence becomes a first-class input to discovery, explanation, saved context, monitoring and evaluation. A sector badge or a link hidden at the bottom of a stock page does not satisfy this contract.

The Chairman's diagnosis is that the existing product has failed to capture market beta consistently. This pass has not independently measured that performance. The new programme therefore makes the diagnosis testable and makes missed broad-market opportunity visible. Product ambition is benchmark participation followed by incremental sector allocation and within-sector selection value. Sector rotation itself is an active bet: concentrated sectors, cash, late rotation, wrong turns and costs can cause underperformance even when some chosen names win.

Use **market participation → sector/industry choice → company selection → qualified action → honest attribution** as the default research order. Keep direct security search, All research, independent emergence, early rotation, defensive and countertrend research available. Current leadership is not a universal admission gate across all sleeves. Preserve the original `KILL-ROTATION-CYCLE-CONFLUENCE` ruling and other killed-mechanism decisions: sharing facts, identities and models does not revive a rejected combined signal.

The three initial economic jobs remain Early Leadership, Earnings and Cycle. Each gains explicit market and group context, relevant benchmark evaluation and a support/challenge view. Neither the new order nor descriptive launch work closes the predictive obligations for remaining opportunity, pivots, entry, management or later markets.

A detail request must not silently reread a newer Sector generation halfway through the investigation. No Sector build runs during a Prophet read.

Sector and Prophet retain their own native generation IDs. Current quote, original assessment, publication, source availability and evaluation time remain distinct. A current quote cannot refresh the age of a sector assessment. Sources can be asynchronous when their actual availability supports the declared cutoff; the UI must not imply simultaneous closes.

## 18.4 Bidirectional evidence without self-confirmation

**Sector → Prophet:** measured market direction, absolute and relative group return, breadth, concentration, source-qualified economics, dated membership and native transitions guide discovery and explanation. A user can choose Healthcare, inspect the full eligible population, compare members and carry the exact context into a saved investigation. Automatic ranking, gating, sizing, entry, management or exit changes require separate Strategy/Evaluation/Portfolio acceptance; descriptive integration does not inherit those permissions.

**Constituent evidence → Sector:** return original dated issuer observations to existing Sector/member/earnings producers. Useful aggregates include earnings-improvement breadth, revision participation, economic confirmations and contradictions, concentration, early emergence and research coverage. Use the complete point-in-time declared population, source availability, reporting cohort, reported/usable/unknown counts, issuer deduplication, weighting, revisions and source roots. An absent report is unknown, not a deterioration or zero.

Prophet-selected winners, popularity, final ranks and confidence labels must not become upstream independent sector confirmation. A count of selected Prophet names is a selection/coverage statistic, not sector fundamentals or institutional flow. Learned feedback features require a frozen schedule, cross-fitting or equivalent leakage control where necessary, and ablation under the existing Evaluation owner.

For candidate X, independent group confirmation excludes **all same-issuer listings and aliases** in the relevant population. Keep the raw sector object for market monitoring and the leave-issuer-out object for candidate support; show excluded weight, member count and concentration. A singleton or concentrated near-singleton group may be unable to confirm independently. For the issuer-exclusion test, hold the captured benchmark, membership, model parameters and all nonissuer inputs fixed. Perturbing X's prices/news across all relevant listings must not change the leave-issuer-out constituents, weights or raw peer measures. A separately recomputed benchmark can itself contain X; identify any benchmark-mediated effect and do not present it as independent issuer confirmation.

Use the existing `dedupe_source_roots` semantics. Sector trend, overlapping theme trend, name-relative return and an explanation generated from those facts can share one price-source root; they are not four independent votes. Distinguish economic relations from taxonomy association. Post-decision outcomes return to the existing Evaluation/learning owner with original cohort/protocol identity, not to the pre-decision facts that justified those decisions.

## 18.5 Beta, returns, allocation and alpha: four distinct claims

1. **Observed relative performance:** compare name, sector and market over an identical declared basis. Arithmetic `name − market = (sector − market) + (name − sector)` is an exact comparison in percentage points, not causal attribution. Relative wealth `(1 + group return)/(1 + benchmark return) − 1` is a different percentage measure and cannot use the same label.
2. **Estimated factor exposure/residual:** use the existing owner with past-known estimation, market-orthogonal sector factor, lagged coefficients, shrinkage/method vintage, coverage and uncertainty. Raw market plus raw sector returns double-count common exposure. Residual is model-dependent; it is not automatically skill or alpha.
3. **Strategy/portfolio participation and attribution:** require actual positions or an explicitly simulated, qualified policy with capital, weights, cash, fills, FX and costs. Use one named accounting method and multiperiod linking rule. Unknown positions or missing weights yield unavailable attribution, not zero.
4. **Incremental selection value:** accepted out-of-sample economic evidence over market/sector baselines after risk, exposure and costs. A winning name or positive arithmetic residual cannot establish it.

The existing Record enum remains `MODEL_PLAN_RECORD | REGISTERED_OPPORTUNITY_COHORT` in its source-era contract. Preserve its denominator, plan IDs, date semantics, entry/outcome/integrity axes, legacy grading and correction history. Add **Strategy evaluation** as a separately qualified adapter/view over existing Evaluation results; do not quietly add a third Record population or derive a portfolio from plan returns. Actual account exposure belongs to Portfolio/Risk, with holdings and account rights explicitly verified.

The evaluation view must answer whether the strategy participated in a rising market, whether sector allocation helped, whether member selection added value, and what cash, turnover, costs and unavailable/no-fill states explain. A strategy can show positive selection contribution and still fail the benchmark-participation objective. This is an intended visible outcome, not an error to hide.

## 18.6 Direction, continuation, remaining opportunity and pivots

Observed direction, relative leadership, continuation forecasts and a turning-point forecast are separate capabilities. Show the compact answer and its counter-case, then allow inspection of breadth, concentration, dispersion, volatility, economic evidence and method. A sector can lead relatively while its absolute price falls. A market can rise while leadership narrows. These disagreements must remain visible.

Before predictive qualification, show realized movement, extension, phase age, known transitions, historical same-definition windows and the next observable strengthening/weakening condition. Phase age uses actual sessions and distinguishes exact, lower-bound and unknown age. A historical rhythm window stays anchored to its native confirmed pivot; the window does not slide forward merely because today advanced.

Separate a pivot's event date from its later confirmation/first-known date. Do not train on future-smoothed cycle labels and present them as online knowledge. Legacy history without confirmation availability cannot prove decision-time parity.

The remaining-opportunity programme must qualify separate heads for future net/benchmark-relative return distribution, adverse-tail risk, target-before-invalidation probability and time-to-transition/target. Each requires target definition, horizon, cutoff, model/feature/version, maturity, censoring/competing-event treatment, calibration, coverage and abstention. An accepted head does not qualify its siblings. No decorative percentage-complete gauge, LLM estimate or historical-average duration may fabricate remaining upside. Missing forecasts leave useful observed evidence visible while the predictive work remains an open commissioned obligation.

## 18.7 Quantitative programme and market-native acceptance

Historical research motivates this architecture but does not establish the proposed product's performance. Moskowitz and Grinblatt find an industry component in stock momentum; Hong, Torous and Valkanov report industry-to-market information with time-varying results; Daniel and Moskowitz document severe momentum reversal failures. The source review records read limits and exact references. No historical return figure is transplanted into a product promise.

The user's company-news timing mechanism becomes a **testable hypothesis**. A dedicated event study may ask whether favorable announcements cluster after an observable sector-attention state, controlling for scheduled reporting, fiscal seasonality, issuer effects, common fundamentals, coverage and source/availability times. Correlation does not establish deliberate scheduling. Price, volume and breadth are not direct proof of institutional or market-maker buying; a flow claim requires a qualified flow source with scope and lag.

Before outcomes, freeze objective, market/channel, universe, benchmark, currency, cadence, horizon/label, PIT source availability, code/config, costs/capacity, candidate-selection history, primary metric and meaningful acceptance/noninferiority margin under the existing Evaluation owner. This amendment neither registers a trial nor authorizes protected outcomes. Do not invent a universal Sharpe, win-rate or beta threshold in a design document.

| Required comparator | What it isolates |
|---|---|
| Passive investable market | Accessible benchmark participation, with index versus vehicle and tracking costs disclosed |
| Simple participation rule | Whether complicated timing adds value beyond transparent exposure management |
| Sector allocation only | Context-driven allocation with neutral/passive member choice |
| Member selection only | Name selection under fixed benchmark sector allocation or a prespecified sector-neutral method |
| Joint sector and name selection | Increment over each component and a simple additive alternative |
| Exact incumbent Prophet | Original source/policy/evidence era, without retrofitted improved context |
| Independent-emergence lane | Recall of early leaders and new rotations outside the current leading groups |

Match capital and risk/exposure as well as raw P&L. Where market timing is also changed, preregister the relevant factorial ablations rather than post-hoc searching every toggle. Include invested time, beta uncertainty, tracking error, upside/downside capture, near-zero denominator handling, concentration, cash yield, turnover, costs and missed-participation attribution. Preserve full eligible opportunities, never-confirmers, no-entry/no-fill, halts, delistings, failures and unavailable cases. A selected winners-only record cannot pass.

Use time-respecting rolling/expanding evaluation, PIT membership and revisions, transforms/residualization fitted only on permitted training history, purging of overlapping labels and justified dependence handling. Fit calibrators only on protocol-designated past training/calibration windows, never on the untouched test set. Evaluate calibration, selected-cohort performance and abstention on held-out observations. Dates, issuers and sectors create shared dependence; report effective sample sizes and uncertainty. Include rebound/crash, failed rotation, crowded leadership and concentration failures. Selected top-K, thin context cells and abstention coverage need separate examination.

US, HK and mainland China have native benchmark, currency, taxonomy, calendar, availability and investor-channel rules. A/H securities share issuer evidence but retain distinct price, venue, access and execution. Later US information cannot feed an earlier Asia decision just because the calendar date matches. HK curated/equal-weight baskets are not automatically official or investable sector indices; China concept boards are not automatically official industries; top-eight previews cannot supply the full selection denominator. Current taxonomy cannot retrospectively prove PIT classification.

Native execution constraints are versioned by date/instrument/channel. Include spreads, applicable commissions/taxes/fees, slippage, liquidity/participation, dividends, corporate actions, currency and cash. No locked-limit, halted or future-close fill is assumed. Current HKEX guidance is a source to the constraints owner, not permission to hard-code one universal China price-limit or calendar rule.

## 18.11 Twenty-four acceptance falsifiers
