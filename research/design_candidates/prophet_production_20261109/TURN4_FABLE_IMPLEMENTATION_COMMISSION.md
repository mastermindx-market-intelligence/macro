## 1. Final product promise

Prophet gives a user a short, trustworthy first read and a deep path through the same opportunity: discover it, understand the economic change, challenge the evidence, inspect currently qualified entry conditions, compare alternatives, retain a dated investigation, follow meaningful changes, and review what actually happened. Every deeper view preserves the original subject and source context. The first screen answers five questions: what changed, why it matters, what could be wrong, what can presently be assessed, and what to inspect next.

Today, Screener and Plans remain the main navigation. Setup is the central investigation surface. Compare, Sources, Context, saved investigations and Record complete the journey. The new Terminal `/prophet` route is the intended shared-shell entry; the legacy macro/options management view remains available until its own parity proof permits replacement. The UI uses the reviewed Paper direction, with concise primary explanations and deliberate drill-down into exact evidence, dates, assumptions and model limits.

The launch design includes three deep economic investigations: Early Leadership, Earnings and Cycle. The other five sleeve families, later instruments and CN/HK/CA architecture remain obligations with their original dependencies. A common visual component does not make their policies interchangeable.
