## 2. Selected direction and product judgment

Direction A is selected for Today: a calm research workspace with three substantial cards, a clear research question, a chart that explains the question, a challenge, a next check and an obvious Open setup action. Direction B is preserved as a broader instrument study. Its analytical structure informs Setup, where the user has chosen to spend attention.

The selection follows the intended progression: discover quickly, investigate deeply, compare deliberately and retain the original record. Today should expose enough reasoning to choose an investigation. Setup should expose the evidence needed to challenge it. This is a design judgment, not a measured usability or conversion result.

The shared shell keeps Today, Screener and Plans. Setup exposes Setup, Evidence, Context and History within the selected research object. Record lives within Plans. Developing observations remain accessible before a qualified setup or entry exists. These destinations preserve the six original R6 jobs without creating six competing top-level product modes.

The visual vocabulary uses the existing light/dark tokens, Inter, PingFang SC for Chinese, quiet neutral card boundaries, tabular numerical treatment, clear chart legends and restrained semantic colour. There is no new token family, brand, navigation service or coloured card-edge system. Native mobile status-bar structure was retained. F01 candidate.4 is contextual, candidate.5 remains proposed, and final joint v1 remains unreleased.

## 4. Three distinct intelligence investigations

All numbers below are deliberately illustrative fixtures. They are not current prices, reported-company facts, scientific results, forecasts, recommendations or trading authorization.

### Early Leadership

The central question is whether company strength is distinct from a stronger group. The sample uses a matched 20-session window, October 6 through November 2, with stock +5.8%, peers +8.5% and market +3.0%.

The decomposition is arithmetic: stock versus market +2.8 percentage points equals peers versus market +5.5 plus stock versus peers -2.7. It is not a causal attribution or incremental-alpha claim. Peer coverage is 12 of 14, equal weighted, issuer excluded and tied to a dated membership snapshot.

The deep chart includes a numerical return axis and explicit dates. The current quote is November 3 at 09:41 ET; the assessment is November 2 at 17:00 ET. Current entry terms and strategy horizon are unavailable. The last published waiting assessment remains visible without becoming a current qualified entry judgment. A plan relation is unavailable unless an owner supplies the exact occurrence relation; a matching ticker does not create it.

The supporting and challenging facts are both visible: the group is improving, while the company still trails its peers. The next check is an observable confirmation, not a fabricated readiness score.

### Quality Earnings

The central question is whether economic growth reaches each diluted share and whether comparable expectations support a further conclusion. The matched-quarter GAAP continuing-operations USD fixture is revenue 5,000 to 5,700, net income 1,000 to 1,060 and diluted shares 1,000 to 1,100 million. Revenue rises 14%, profit 6%, shares 10%, and EPS declines from 1.0000 to 0.9636, approximately 3.6%.

Release, call and filing are distinct sample events. Reported economics are separate from consensus, revisions and surprise. Where no compatible expectations snapshot exists, that field is unavailable; the previous reported figure is not substituted for consensus.

The correction specimen retains the original 1,020m shares and EPS 1.0392 alongside corrected 1,100m shares and EPS 0.9636. The original evidence and conclusion remain inspectable. Effective figures do not rewrite what was knowable at the earlier cutoff.

### Cycle Capture

The central question is whether an operating recovery can survive financing and benefit the original equity. The sample begins with reported cash of 120m less restricted cash of 20m, leaving 100m available. Assumed quarterly flows of -15m, -10m and +5m leave 80m before debt. Debt of 90m produces -10m after debt. An explicitly assumed 30m minimum cash floor produces a modeled funding gap of 40m.

Reported and projected points use distinct treatment; scenario assumptions are named. The copy says operations improve **in this scenario** and asks the user to review financing against the modeled gap. It does not assign a recovery probability, position size or entry permission.

A separate state specimen shows that an existing model-plan holding thesis can remain intact while a new entry waits for breakout confirmation. Each state requires its respective owner and dated assessment; neither is inferred from the other.

## 8. Responsive, typographic and accessibility review

The inspected layout widths are 1440 with 1168px main content, 768 with 728px content, 390 with 358px content and a true 320px browser content viewport with 288px usable content. The 320 specimen has a larger review frame around it; it is not an invented 320px operating-system phone mockup.

The 390 Today first useful card and its primary action fit within the illustrated initial 844px phone view. Deep content continues by scrolling. Mobile Compare brings subject-opening actions near the headers and preserves readable paired rows. The three-selection specimen uses 44px subject selectors rather than squeezing three numerical columns.

Typography was inspected through selected JSX and native screenshots. Explicit Inter and PingFang SC replaced unintended fallback text. Dark secondary evidence text was strengthened. Eighteen selection-control outlines and two cash-bar labels were corrected after contrast review. Developing card actions were aligned while preserving readable wrapped explanations.

Measured example contrast ratios include light body text on white 15.62:1, light muted text on white 5.43:1, positive body ink 6.30:1, warning ink 6.32:1, dark body text on its panel 12.51:1 and dark muted text 5.57:1. The 4.30:1 light semantic-green heading is 24px large text. Decorative separators were not treated as control indicators.

Standards references: [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), [target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html). Normal text requires 4.5:1 and qualifying large text 3:1; meaningful component/graphic contrast uses 3:1. The AA target-size criterion is 24 CSS pixels subject to its exceptions; 44px primary mobile actions are this design's stronger target, not a claim that 44px is the AA minimum.

Static inspection does not certify keyboard order, screen-reader semantics, chart data alternatives, focus return, 200% zoom, dynamic reflow, reduced motion, virtualized tables or accessible errors. Those remain executable browser gates. No WCAG conformance certification is claimed.
