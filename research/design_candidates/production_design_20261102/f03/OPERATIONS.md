### Action-state contract

`NOT_DISPATCHED` means no upstream effect was requested. `PREPARED` is possible only with a real admission record. `APPLIED_UNVERIFIED` requires a reported effect but has incomplete readback. `UNKNOWN` requires reconciliation before retry. `VERIFIED` requires exact final URLs, identity map and all applicable preservation evidence. These are organization effect labels, **not RIG statuses**. RIG retains its actual draft/in_review/approved/revise/rejected/superseded states and separate receipts.

Failure copy: “Organization was not applied. Original remains at [exact URL].” Unknown copy: “Outcome unconfirmed. Inspect the operation and both locations before retry.” Partial copy: “Destination exists; [specific preservation check] is unresolved. Original remains retained.” Verified copy may name the actual destination only after readback.
