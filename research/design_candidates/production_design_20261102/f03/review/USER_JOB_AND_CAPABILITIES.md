# F03 closed first-pass review input

## User and business job

Mastermind builders and designers need to find current design references, full source contracts, owners, approval state, predecessor/successor history, and changed targets without repeatedly exploring very large Paper canvases. Organization must preserve existing product capabilities and source identities.

## Required tasks

1. A new builder chooses a product family, then reaches its exact approved candidate or complete contract within two directory choices. If these have not been established, that absence and its owner must be discoverable in the same place.
2. A designer finds a retained predecessor, rejection/hold reason, and exact successor without opening a giant archive canvas. Missing evidence must remain visible.
3. A returning session identifies changed targets, known overlaps, and unowned route coverage without repeating the census.
4. Find a reference by product name, exact route, Paper file/page/artboard ID, or assignment. Preserve the ability to inspect canonical records separately from source deltas.
5. Distinguish product owner, integration owner, page custodian, delegated slice, and live writer. Preserve route access/payload and generated-family identity.
6. Keep originals, comments, annotations, assets, source links, redirects, rejection and hold evidence. Never infer approval or successful organization from a label.

Assess product regression, discoverability, language clarity, hierarchy, density, and likely visual/accessibility weaknesses. Distinguish observed behavior from source-based inference. Do not infer a rendered layout pass from HTML/CSS. Do not read any other files, rationale or constraints until your first-pass findings are frozen and returned.
