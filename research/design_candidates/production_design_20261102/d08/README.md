## The investor's job

An investor coming from a sector holding or a flow event needs to identify the exact instrument, understand what changed, examine the evidence available at that time, and decide what to investigate or monitor. The machine's job is to carry the selected object and its evidence through these transitions and disclose when the next workspace cannot substantiate it. The investor must be able to return to the same holding, filter or observation.

On entry, show the requested task and instrument, the strongest qualified observation, its measurement window and one next step. Example for a dated book: **Reported gamma** above the signed value, **at the 600 strike** beside the selected row, and **Compare sessions** beside the dates. On a sparse or withheld book use **Choose another session** or **Inspect available strikes**. Keep IDs, coverage decomposition, model, sources and corrections one disclosure away. Introduce the additional controls at the point where they answer a question. Research navigation must never offer a buy/sell execution button or represent an unqualified forecast as expected profit or win rate. Buy/sell labels inside a hypothetical leg editor describe assumed leg direction.

## Two complete journeys

1. **From a holding to a later review.** From D05 Sector Central, open Exposure for a particular listing. Preserve the existing sector origin, selected company and roster order. An overview can summarize that exact listing and offer **Open exposure**. A direct Exposure link bypasses the overview. At one strike and expiry, show the signed value, the spot and its clock, the measured population and an exact-row alternative. Compare two admitted book vintages and inspect matched cells, entrants to the *reported set* and qualification losses separately. A reader may inspect the selected archived date or open the chart with its validated hash. A plan begins only after selecting uniquely identified contract rows. Enter sides, quantities, premiums, thesis, horizon and invalidation. Run the supported hypothetical with the assumptions visible. With native save admission and owner readback, create a research revision, optionally confirm one monitor condition, later reopen that revision and compare its original evidence against a new observation. **Current stop:** the native Plan draft and bounded payoff exist. The prepared Investigation host is not mounted by Options. Show **Draft only** and the precise dependency. A future save, firing receipt or reviewed holding is not a current outcome.
2. **From a print to replay and back.** In a filtered Tape, open one observation. Identify source/time, root, expiry, strike, right, premium and the basis for any side estimate. Keep a reported trade distinct from an aggregate event. Open Volatility for the same root and expiry, or explicitly choose a supported tenor with interpolation disclosed. **Replay equity depth** opens the verified symbol at the admission gate. Futures requires an explicit ES/NQ pair and a supported mapping basis. Never translate an equity print into a futures fill. Record the originating filter, row, clock, scroll and focus on its existing history entry. Guest, paid-plan gate, service outage, invalid input, missing archive and accessible replay have different recoveries. On return restore the original observation, even when the live list has advanced. If it expired, announce **Original print is outside this window** and offer the original scan. No substitute print.

The source-backed candidate’s decision order is *observation → evidence → assumption → explicit action → receipt*. Existing Macro Brief, Flow, Scanner, Ticker and Leaders remain reachable alongside the native workspace. The proposed concise local headings are **Desk**, **Flow**, **Exposure**, **Structure**, **Volatility**, **Statistics** and **Prophet**. Futures / replay is a distinct destination. The existing default is Tape. A label change preserves initial selection. Plan / Compare / Review are contextual tasks, with native availability stated individually. Consumer recognition of these labels and their Chinese equivalents remains a visual/interaction test.

## Screen and interaction contract

1. **Header.** One global destination and one local view. Keep a separate listing selector, session label and state, each independently named. The first viewport answers “which object, as of when, what changed?” with one strong value and a visible route into detail. Use tabular numerals, aligned units, signed values and text/symbol in addition to color. An unknown denominator displays “408 names · universe unknown”. Keep supporting methodology behind **Source and coverage**.
2. **Selection.** Matrix axes, scale, legend, zero, spot line and selected strike stay visible together. Cells are operable by arrow keys, Home/End and Enter. Screen-reader names include expiry, strike, metric, unit and availability. Announce the selected cell once; live refresh does not steal focus. A tap or keyboard activation opens the same details as hover. In 3D, provide **Exact table** with the same coordinate set, selected row and compare operations; under reduced motion use a static view with explicit zoom/rotate or default to table.
3. **Details.** Wide layouts use one inspector; a new selection replaces its contents. Tablet/phone use a labeled sheet/full-height details view, with focus moved to its heading, Escape/back and a visible Close. A modal traps focus; a desktop side panel does not. Close restores the original row and scroll, or its containing list heading if the row disappeared. Avoid stacking an inspector, state dialog and a Plan editor. Preserve a return entry before crossing tasks.
4. **Updates.** Key pending requests to owner, view, root, source and generation. Discard late A results after selection B. Immediately label B while loading and clear A's metrics. An authorized last-known read for *B* can remain visibly dated. Do not re-rank a focused row mid-action. Offer **Show new prints** above a bounded buffer. No additional feed poller or subscription. End protected subscriptions on revocation and clear affected values.
5. **Reflow.** Use the current F01/F02 candidate geometry as a provisional input, then measure with the released shell. Dock a 320px inspector with a 24px gap only if at least 640px of primary analysis remains. Otherwise use a sheet/overlay. Keep 72px compact navigation user-selected. Below 680px of usable primary width, prioritize selected instrument/session, two readable values, one chart/table and the directly accessible exact row. At phone widths keep the active local view and a **Views** picker containing every local task. Scoped 2D overflow has visible edge affordances and keyboard access. Wrap or stack critical numbers and labels. The 680px threshold is D08's local hierarchy proposal, separate from F01's docking decision.
6. **Language and input.** Test EN/ZH in both themes, 200% text size and 400% reflow, VoiceOver/NVDA equivalent names, keyboard-only, touch and reduced motion. Important tap targets ≥44 CSS px. Normal text contrast ≥4.5:1, large text and meaningful controls/graphics ≥3:1. Use text and shape for polarity, side-estimate and selected cells. Provide a row-level list for dense plots and genuine headings, captions and table headers. Keep timestamp timezone and decimal meaning stable across locale. **查看来源**, **仅草稿**, **返回筛选结果**, **保存状态待确认** are proposed localized labels for copy review.
