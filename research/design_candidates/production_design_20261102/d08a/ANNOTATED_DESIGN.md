## Visual and hierarchy intent

The first view should make the selected population and one useful result clear. Use restrained surfaces, typographic hierarchy, aligned numerical columns and neutral separators. Source qualification is legible beside the value it qualifies. Avoid decorative score dashboards, repeated large disclaimers and competing hero cards. Keep powerful comparisons and filters accessible through deliberate disclosure.

F02 owns final type and color tokens. Domain hierarchy requests: a clear view title, compact source-session line, one action row, then the principal analytical object. Display figures use tabular numerals, not monospace merely to appear technical. Distinguish metric magnitude from estimated side; neither red/green nor language alone conveys qualification. No colored container borders/accent stripes.

The existing public Brief's dated modules, unavailable-brief explanation and retained earlier research are valuable. Its desktop Flow needs a shorter route into an actionable scan and clearer separation among premium, share-change estimates and Tide. Native Largest's phone card anatomy provides a stronger starting pattern than Tape's column hiding.

## Screen-specific decisions

**Desk:** preserve heuristic scores, tiers, breakdown, detections, elite/whale/0DTE/sweep filters, watchlist, active roots, gauge, radar, chain heat and full inspector. Default ordering remains source-supported. “Smart Money” terminology needs neutral explanatory copy; do not invent measured intent. Move nonessential tutorial onboarding out of the initial task path, with persistent Guide access. Heat unavailable leaves the primary list useful.

**Screener:** display Top premium; Largest premium deviations; Volume > prior OI; OI changes;0DTE share; Hot contracts. Stable machine keys unchanged. A subtitle explains each predicate in one sentence. For OI/hot unavailable source, retain the tab and a precise absence/error/access state; no auto-switch. Reuse source selectors and existing shortlist. Unknown references do not become numerical0. If the user chose a field unavailable in the next preset, show the actual active sort rather than preserving a misleading label.

**0DTE and Largest:** no rank badge implies an individual execution. Add full expiry year where needed to distinguish next January. A source flag/date conflict produces a visible qualification count and source explanation. Do not “correct” the publisher silently. Details expose available n_prints/aggregation, and missing constituents are explicit.

**Tide:** line chart with cumulative USD premium, call/put or signed basis labeled; retain submodes and rebase. Axis/tooltips never imply per-trade quantity. Mark data gaps; no unsupported interpolation or forward fill. A selected interval opens interval evidence, not a synthetic contract.

**Tickers:** root selection and current-list scope clear. Independently show flow,volatility/GEX/levels context only with actual source dates and owning seams. Changing to an uncovered region/listing yields unsupported coverage, not nearest US symbol.

**Statistics:** use its present strong separation of counts and qualified premium. Keep expanded venues, quality/exclusion counts and first/last windows. Visually compress repeated unavailable premium labels without removing the0%coverage meaning. No universal “live” badge.

**Prophet:** Macro Plans, Options Alpha and Issue Desk retain lane identity and source lineage. Model-close/forward-ledger facts stay separate from trade-time observations. Research review remains explicit operator work; no ordinary scan click issues a plan.

**Public:** keep Brief's selected completed session, per-module source freshness, dealer-level estimates and previous research. Flow's sector premium, estimated ETF creations/redemptions or share-change inference, theme grouping and Tide stay individually named. If themes unavailable, compact that state and keep entry rather than reserving a large empty module. Scanner and Ticker remain first-class public tasks; public landing/retry/open-original behavior must be proven before claiming the journey.
