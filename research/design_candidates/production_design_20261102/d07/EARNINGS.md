# Earnings, dossiers, and calendars

A reader must be able to answer three different questions. “When is the report?” uses a scheduled event. “What did the issuer report or say?” uses a fiscal-period record. “What happened to this listing's price?” uses dated bars. A date shared by all three is insufficient to join them.

### Contracts for the builder

**Scheduled and observed events.** The existing planner reads `<SYM>.fund.json → earnings.{next_date,next_period}` and the file's own `asof`. The producers emit a date-only day. Display “Nov 5 · Time not supplied” without shifting it into Nov 4 in another viewer zone. If a genuinely offset-qualified instant is supplied, format both exchange and viewer time from that instant. An offset-less date-time is unreadable. The planner's local `earnings:<SYMBOL>:<period|next>` identity permits a rescheduled date to replace the prior display row. It does not establish that results are published. Keep failed/past/unread/unsupported rows out of an all-clear. The separate Macro nightly calendar store supplies estimates and source-dependent time hints. Its newest row alone cannot establish freshness across the universe. [Planner and identity rules](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/mastermind-terminal/blob/3cc8da8432d8d2d4e4cb597f49c4eaa95f1980c9/terminal/lib/watchlistEventPlanner.ts) · [Macro collector](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/fc85b0428cf8b0ca4f2fbbc5a415b2871f15a1c9/collectors/equity_earnings.py) · [Coverage audit](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/fc85b0428cf8b0ca4f2fbbc5a415b2871f15a1c9/scripts/audit_earnings_freshness.py)

**Issuer, event, receipt.** Resolve listings/aliases against the existing event-ID adapter. An issuer result such as `evt_cik0000320193_2026q3_results` can have listing-keyed `cie_…`, `TICKER/YYYYQn` and public-wire slug aliases. Do not mint multiple issuer events for share classes or merge price histories. The event-workspace contract carries fiscal period, observation/availability times and revisions. Keep the event ID stable through a correction, retain the earlier revision for historical views, and bind a displayed claim to its document revision and locator. If the archival revision cannot be retrieved, label “Earlier version unavailable.” Event fiscal end, filing accepted/publication time, call date, estimate known-at and reader generation time must be independently attributable. [Event ID adapter](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/fc85b0428cf8b0ca4f2fbbc5a415b2871f15a1c9/engine/company_intelligence/event_id_adapter.py) · [Event workspace](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/fc85b0428cf8b0ca4f2fbbc5a415b2871f15a1c9/engine/company_intelligence/event_workspace.py)

**Evidence and access.** The Wire builder verifies the immutable packet generation, body length/hash and contract before rendering. The public article keys include `event.{ticker,transcript_id,period,date,slug}`, `packet.{generation_id,story_revision_id}`, and `source.{index_generated_at,body_sha256}`. A quote or numeric receipt binds source/segment/text hashes, segment index and byte bounds. Article identity follows the fiscal call slug, independently of a later index-generation timestamp. The static builder emits the redacted catalog, index pages (96 articles per page), individual articles and weekly reports where present. The article preview is limited to two facts. Member bytes come from the entitled path. Keep protected excerpts out of static HTML, metadata and shared caches. An input outage may preserve prior rendered bytes for at most 48 hours under the builder's existing policy. The UI names the age of the call and source separately. **Public dossier.** The teaser is public-cacheable (`max-age=300, stale-while-revalidate=900`). The event-workspace projected glance and its typed coverage absence use `60+240s`. The glance strips private receipts, hashes, URLs and internal locators. Guest-to-member navigation should preserve the intended call. Keep 404 coverage absence separate from 422 malformed listing, 429 rate limit and 503 ambiguous/verification/upstream failure. Market reaction comes only from `completeness.reaction.status`. Issuer guidance belongs to management. Consensus follows `completeness.consensus.status` and applicable display rights. Use “Consensus unavailable” or “Price reaction unavailable” per field while the readable evidence remains visible. [Public API and cache policy](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/macro/blob/fc85b0428cf8b0ca4f2fbbc5a415b2871f15a1c9/app/company_intelligence.py)

**Calendar return and outcomes.** In #814 a daily move is current daily close divided by prior trading-day close, minus one. A session move is its close divided by its open, minus one. Daily bar OHLC and eligible aggregate-bar session extrema have different meanings. Expanded detail selects 30-minute bars, overnight starts 20:00 ET on the previous calendar day, premarket starts 04:00, regular starts 09:30 and postmarket starts at the exchange-projected close. Unknown exchange hours, empty feed, or unentitled overnight data leave their own cells unavailable. Daily bars remain visible. Label each time using the listing's exchange. Separately, a **proposed** event-forward comparison requires a D12-qualified release instant, calendar, pre-event anchor, adjustment/currency basis, observed endpoint, complete horizon, and matching benchmark if displaying excess return. Printing a return before its endpoint is observable creates look-ahead. Until an owner supplies the join, use “Market reaction not linked.” Preserve the options/price/evidence owners and the protected #814/#1043 boundaries.
