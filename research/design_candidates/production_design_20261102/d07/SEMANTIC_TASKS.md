## Remaining source and build questions

1. **CN type/producer drift remains concrete.** `FundEstimates.eps_q` is typed as `EstimateSeries` and the comments describe two forward years, while the CN producer emits null quarters and up to three annual targets. `Fund.estimates` also retains a “null for CN” comment contradicted by the producer. The current contract must be reconciled by its existing owner. The shared `ForecastPage` serves CN as well as other regions. Its unchanged helper functions now reproduce nullable-quarter and three/two/single/sparse-target cases. The actual beta CN route displays all three annual targets and retains actual quarters when quarterly estimates are unavailable. Controlled component fixtures remain blocked by npm E403. The source-inspected early `ActualsTab` return when `estimates` is absent still needs an actual render fixture. [Forecast diagnostics](checks/diagnostics/forecast/README.md) · [Live subset](baseline/continuation-20261104/README.md)
2. **Guidance is narrower than generic numeric evidence.** The event composer currently admits only nonnegative percentage strings matching its precise regular expression. Its projection derives those strings from guidance low/high. Dollar guidance and negative percentage examples need typed source-owner treatment before production composition. Parsing a formatted string or broadening the regular expression cannot establish matching metric, currency, period, basis or pre-event time.
3. **Clock absence cannot be solved with a renamed shared timestamp.** Fund offers build and optional acquisition receipts, while its provider-observation time is absent. Macro's descriptive series offers collection time but omits raw issued/effective clocks and rights lineage. The owner must supply a qualified retained receipt for any new historical comparison. The present study must show the missing state.
4. **Legacy financial history remains a compatibility requirement.** The shared helper uses positional annual/four-period fallbacks when added identity metadata is entirely absent. Partial fiscal identity refuses positional fallback when duration or comparison-basis metadata is supplied. Fully qualified comparisons also reject incompatible supplied durations/bases and ambiguous current or prior fiscal identities. A future design must make the available source scope understandable without deleting supported older data or silently redefining those helpers.
5. **A visual thesis requires explicit authorship.** Evidence replay establishes the quoted source material. A producer interpretation needs its attribution, while “Your thesis” belongs to the existing owner-scoped record. A support or falsifier label alone cannot prove a private lifecycle command, causal repricing or forecast.
