# Using Company to challenge an earnings thesis

## The decision on first arrival

A portfolio analyst follows a company from a market desk or opens a report from the calendar. The first view must identify the listing, issuer, fiscal period and whether the user is reading the current company or a historical event. It should say what changed, show one primary comparison with its unit and basis, and offer the evidence that could overturn the reading. An investor may disagree with the editorial interpretation and still verify every figure.

Use the existing Company R3 example as the design control. Its dated NVDA brief puts the conclusion above reported revenue, Data Center revenue and GAAP gross margin. The chart separates reported fiscal quarters from next-quarter guidance with an outlined range and legend. Three invalidation checks sit beside it, each with a source or observable next event. The source line explicitly identifies the unavailable consensus snapshot. Preserve those relationships, text labels and chart/table equivalence. The example is a historical Paper case at Q2 FY2026, August 27, 2025. Never join its guidance to the current beta's Q3 FY2027 estimate.

## Four complete journeys

1. **Thesis to evidence.** Open the exact listing. Intelligence › Brief presents a dated company reading. Choose the reported revenue metric, open its filing or event receipt, inspect unit/basis and close back to the same metric. Open the earlier outlook and compare the actual to that guidance on the same basis. In the historical R3 example, reported 46.743B and midpoint 45.000B yield +3.873…%, displayed +3.9%. $44.10–45.90B is the underlying range. Label it “Above prior guidance.” A consensus surprise would require a separate admissible pre-report snapshot. Open Results and the exact earnings call. A saved personal thesis may cite the observation without rewriting the event or making a trade. On a deliberate watch, show the selected list, verify the owner readback and return to the original event.
2. **Calendar to report and back.** Choose a watchlist, read batch and scheduled row. Preserve the list/membership context, originating listing, scheduled day and precision, row, scroll and focus. Show the fund-file date separately from any owner-supplied schedule observation clock. An unmatched row offers “View company.” Once the existing owner resolves the event, open its exact report or public article. Reconcile requested call identity if Company has advanced. Return through the actual navigation entries. Restore the current row only for the same qualified event, announcing a changed date. If the row or list is unavailable, focus the return-status notice. Preserve date/filter controls when a later owner-qualified view introduces them. Reading keeps watch, thesis and alert state unchanged. [Return protocol](JOURNEY_RETURN.md)
3. **Expectations to comparison.** Pick EPS or revenue, then the explicit horizon and fiscal ending. Read analyst count and actual data collection time alongside the last comparable observation. Open the computation or data table. A period roll starts a new comparison. Missing consensus still leaves the actual, guidance, period and available documents accessible. On Expectation Surface, the next action opens the covered company's public page. No descriptive movement changes a Prophet, K3E, portfolio or recommendation score.
4. **Unsupported, stale or disputed evidence.** Keep the requested listing, venue and currency visible. A stale value retains its last verified date and source. A true zero remains a value. A stale comparison, different accounting scope or unknown issuing time disables only the affected comparison. Show the exact missing input and what is still usable. A new or ambiguous symbol must not inherit the previous issuer's quote, estimate or watch object. Reopening a now-restricted historical receipt shows its access state and offers the permitted current view only by explicit choice.

## Accessibility and visual acceptance

Semantic headings, row/column headers and programmatically named landmarks must survive every layout. Use text, line style and symbol in addition to color for actual/guidance/estimate and positive/negative/unknown. Expose a formula and raw comparable inputs without requiring hover. Keep keyboard order aligned with reading order. Give symbol-only controls a localized accessible name, selected state and visible focus ring. Observe the 44px mobile touch target. Target-size AA minimum and its spacing/exceptions are distinct. Verify normal text at 4.5:1, large text and essential non-text/focus indicators at 3:1 where applicable. Check light/dark, EN/ZH, large text, reduced motion, forced focus, 400% zoom, vertical reflow and data tables. Do not use a colored stripe to represent a card's state.

For both phone widths, test metric → benchmark → fiscal period → result → source → back using touch and keyboard. At narrow width, prefer a labeled disclosure to clipped vital text. Drawers must dismiss without losing the query, active row or measured scroll anchor. Respect reduced motion on changes of family and chart focus. Desktop may add detail but cannot be the only way to inspect computation, source or invalidation.
