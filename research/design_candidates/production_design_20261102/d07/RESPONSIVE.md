### Control boxes and text fit

Important phone controls have at least 44px target height and width where independently actionable. A full-width selector or primary source action uses a 48px nominal height and grows for wrapped content. Table rows use at least 48px with a 44px evidence target. House targets remain distinct from WCAG target-size criteria.

At 288px inner width, two equal controls with an 8px gap each receive 140px. At 358px they receive 175px. These budgets suit short, verified labels such as “Annual” and “Quarterly.” A long fiscal target, a localized compound label or a third item must wrap onto another row or use the full-width selector. Never compress every control to fit one row. A 48px period selector remains available beside or directly after the cadence choice. The actual selected period remains visible when its popup closes.

Use the F02 candidate’s 14px body, 13px table figures and 12px source receipts as starting roles, with tabular financial digits.
