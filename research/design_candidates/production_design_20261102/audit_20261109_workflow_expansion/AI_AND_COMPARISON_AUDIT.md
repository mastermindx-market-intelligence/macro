## Why the AI product screens were retained

The start screen already gives the composer priority, keeps empty Send disabled and offers three useful starting questions. Add context and starter selection do not silently send; Send needs text.

Saved cases are distinct from chat history. The history screen identifies the saved version and evidence snapshots, while reopening does not refresh sources. The reopened case keeps its saved version visible.

Save unconfirmed is distinct from expiry before dispatch. The former preserves the original request and offers status reconciliation without another save. The latter returns from sign-in to review without automatically saving.

Chat recovery keeps the current and selected conversations distinct. Failure retains the current conversation and draft; loading does not commit the switch; Stay / Discard exposes the unsent text before leaving. The owner contract already addresses successful-empty versus failed history, exact targets, superseded reads, account changes and original request custody.

No additional product screen was justified by the inspected material. The builder wording correction removes the demonstrated inconsistency without duplicating those states.
