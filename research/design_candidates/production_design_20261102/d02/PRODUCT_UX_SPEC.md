## 1. Product, people and decisions

Macro helps an investor understand the current market and economic setting, inspect the evidence and its limits, and continue into a specific asset or company investigation. The primary user is an investor returning to an existing research process, often on a phone between market sessions. A second user is an analyst checking a claim, its definition, timing and historical support. Both need an answer and an efficient route to the underlying series.

The opening question is **“What regime are we in, what changed, and what would change this assessment?”** Follow-on questions are “Is participation confirming the move?”, “Which stress is increasing?”, “What is already priced?”, and “Which asset or company should I inspect next?” D02 supplies research context. It does not gain sizing, execution, signal-origination or model-promotion authority through a more confident visual treatment.

The assignment contains 26 canonical rows: 25 document paths plus a historical nested-family row. Current source resolves that family to eleven Macro Command fragments, not eleven standalone indicator documents. Keep all assigned identities accounted for, but do not create routes from the old placeholder. All fourteen economic workspaces currently declare US support. Regional selection must never relabel these snapshots as China, Hong Kong, Canada or International.

Success means the user can read the state, identify the strongest support and counter-evidence, understand the relevant clocks, inspect the exact definition, identify an invalidating condition and reach an exact research destination. Source provenance and model limitations must remain attached to a claim throughout that journey.

## 3. Overview: the normal-data experience

### First useful view

The proposed reading order is:

1. **Regime answer and observed date.** A sentence supported by the actual canonical state, a compact score with the correct unit, and one material limitation if present.
2. **What changed and the recent path.** Retain the last up-to-60-session market-state chart. Show the date range, current point, change definition and a readable value on focus/tap.
3. **Support and counter-evidence.** The strongest supporting and opposing indicators share a visual field with the answer. Each names its own scale and observation date.
4. **What would change the view.** Show the producer's nearest boundary, pending confirmation and broader conditions. A component crossing is not itself a promised label flip.
5. **Affected markets and next research step.** Preserve index/sector/asset observations and exact company/chart destinations. The user should see why the destination matters before leaving.

Retain the score as context; reduce its screen area where it displaces the time-series chart. Keep no more than two supporting objects competing with the primary analytical object in the first fold. This is a proposed hierarchy, not a fabricated approved composition.

Normal source fixture for qualification: the inspected November 2 regime is Q2/Reflation, with growth +0.20 and inflation +0.40, aggregate heuristic confidence 0.22, and a separate market-state composite 46/Mixed. These are different objects; do not title all of them “regime score.” The source fixture is frozen evidence and must not be described as a current live reading when reused.

### Progressive detail

The primary action is “Inspect the evidence / 查看证据”. It opens the relevant local inspector with the current state, selected horizon and source context intact. A row expands to its definition, raw input, transform, historical context and provenance. “Dates & sources / 日期与来源” contains the full observation, release, first-known, collection, build and revision record; the claim-local date and consequential freshness state stay visible outside it.

Keep named entrances to Evidence, Markets, Risk, Events, Policy/Fed, Sentiment, News/Brief and Deep context. The exact current dialog census is in the capability ledger. A new shell must not silently drop content merely because it is currently in a hidden dialog or a legacy offscreen carrier.

The historical replay and the current point need explicit treatment. A recomputed current market-state value can differ from the first-write-wins forward log. Show their identities and do not rewrite the historical issue receipt to make the path look continuous.

### End-to-end journey: regime change to company evidence

Open /macro.html at its existing route. Read the answer and recent path; inspect a changed component; examine its history and definition; open the affected sector or asset through its existing route; select the exact company/listing; open its chart/options evidence using the destination owner's supported URL; return to the originating Macro state, scroll and focus. Preserve region, instrument identity, query bytes where meaningful, selected horizon, locale and source cutoff. An absent destination contract remains a named seam; do not invent a generic /company/:ticker endpoint.

## 4. Risk, pullback and breadth

### Separate trend, stress and positioning

Retain Risk Radar's dominant answer, driver ladder, counterread, trend/resilience, stress inputs, positioning/sentiment and leadership context. Scores on different scales are not interchangeable or averaged into a decorative composite. A retained risk-on trend is not an all-clear when current stress coverage is incomplete.

Current source can coherently say caution while its ungated state is risk-off: the broad-tape confirmation gate is false. Proposed copy: **“Several stress inputs are high; broad market confirmation is absent.”** Keep the gate and its underlying evidence inspectable. Do not recompute it in the UI.

The model estimate belongs beside its target and evidence class. Current inspected RiskRadar h21 is 22%, baseline 17.8%, for a loss of at least 5% from issue close within the next 21 native SPY closing observations. The matching reconstructed cell has n=66, 16 events and a 90% observed-rate interval of 4.99%–45.45%; precision_grade is false. The state-only caution rate is 16%; the conjunction-adjusted estimate is a different quantity. The prospective exact-model cohort has four issued and zero graded observations. The historical Paper 13% remains a September specimen.

Use **“Model estimate · limited historical evidence”** with adjacent event/horizon and an accessible evidence action. Do not turn the wide reconstructed observed-rate interval into a predictive band around today's estimate. Withhold a precision-qualified or “validated” badge until the incumbent model owner releases the required evidence. Preserve useful qualified numbers where the source permits them.

### Pullback underway

Reuse the existing separation of observed decline, estimated further decline from today, and implied total depth from the same episode peak. Keep “worst observed” distinct from current distance from peak and rebound. A range of future lows is not a price path, a bottom date or a loss limit.

Where a qualified forecast is absent, show the measured decline and its history, state **“Further downside estimate unavailable”**, and retain the route to missing evidence. Do not substitute a US model for China. The existing China Paper variant is a strong refusal pattern, but its illustrative readings are not a current China data contract. D19 and the incumbent model owner qualify that regional behavior.

### Breadth

Reuse the Paper hierarchy: participation answer, advance/decline tape, price-versus-breadth history, moving-average participation, new highs/lows, sector/index comparisons and coverage funnel. Every chart must name its universe and denominator. NYSE operating companies and S&P 1500 are not the same population; constituent history, corporate actions, duplicates, eligibility and available histories belong in the evidence layer.

Correct the illustrative arithmetic before a future freeze: 128 highs and 67 lows imply 65.64 under the stated 100×highs/(highs+lows) formula, not 57.1. If 57.1 uses another window or smoothing, the producer/fixture must say so. Keep current and historical universes distinguishable. “All gates pass” on a mockup is not evidence that the producer passed those gates.

## 5. Macro Command and economic workspaces

Command's overview should remain a compact cross-section of the economy, not fourteen equally prominent cards. Retain one line per section, useful movement, what the owner is watching and the exact deep-workspace destination. New content must fit the existing local-navigation slot F01 provides.

Current, Drivers and History remain separate views:

- **Current:** domain answer, axes where valid, consequential change, one contradiction or coverage limit, and the next useful inspection.
- **Drivers:** contributions with raw values, transforms, weights, coverage and direction semantics. A negative contribution is not automatically “bad”; the axis definition decides.
- **History:** series, prior-publication comparison and revisions. A method change breaks numerical comparability unless the source explicitly supports it.
- **Evidence inspector:** source record, definition, unit/basis, relevant clocks, rights and available history.

Liquidity Regime's current decision-first variant is an existing implementation starting point. Extending it to the other thirteen workspaces is a proposal to qualify against their actual data. Several current workspaces deliberately have no headline quadrant; do not manufacture a state to make the layout symmetric.

The fourteen-source census includes 321 metric records, 101 source entries and ten series records. [The machine-readable extract](evidence/workspace-contract-census.json) preserves every metric's unit, basis, owner, transformation, timestamps, freshness, null reason, source references and authority ceiling. Typed empty states are first-class content. Current growth has a nowcast-versus-hard-data contradiction; national debt has issuance/auction stress versus a calm bond-desk reading. Show both meanings instead of averaging them into a single reassurance.

Scenario and alert descriptors exist, but the current closed schema fixes execution_available=false and service_available=false. Allow inspection of available assumptions/conditions; do not display runnable simulation, saved condition or active alert success without the owning service contract.

## 7. Phone, language and visual requirements

At 320/390 CSS pixels, preserve the same semantic order as desktop: answer, observed change/path, support/counter-evidence, invalidation, next research step. Compress the score and metadata before shrinking the analytical chart. Put essential qualifications beside the value; hover is never their only carrier. Local section access remains visible through F01's approved pattern. Do not invent a domain bottom bar.

Use F02's eventual type, spacing, number, table/chart and state tokens. Until released, the F02 width table is only a stress fixture. Neutral containers, clear typographic hierarchy and restrained semantic marks support dense analysis. Current doctrine disallows colored container borders/accent stripes; visible keyboard focus outlines remain required. The observed amber stripes on Risk Radar variants need revision at the composition phase.

Provide light/dark and EN/ZH pairs for each materially different archetype. Long Chinese labels must wrap without losing units, selected state or buttons. Locale-sensitive red/green conventions cannot carry meaning alone: signs, text, markers and legends remain explicit. Dates must retain timezone and precision; a month-only source does not become a day-level timestamp.

## 8. Measurement and proposed additions

Future usability tasks: identify the regime and counter-evidence; find the clock behind a stale input; explain why a comparison is withheld; recover the exact previous view; distinguish observed pullback from an unqualified forecast. Measure task completion, wrong-interpretation rate, time to evidence and return success separately on phone and desktop. No user study has been conducted in this phase.

Reuse existing first-party instrumentation and workspace learning.event_names. Any additional events are proposals requiring the existing analytics/privacy owner; suggested event meanings are evidence opened, source inspected, invalidation inspected and return completed. Do not log private thesis text or create a parallel analytics service.

Proposals requiring qualification: shared consumer reading envelope for legacy Macro fields; decision-first hierarchy beyond Liquidity Regime; date-correct unified rate/Fed comparison; D23 condition-save handoff; accessible evidence successor for the denied Macro Signals link. None is represented as an existing API, released contract or accepted visual reference.
