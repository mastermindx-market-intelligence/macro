Do not treat the nine compositions as evidence that the complete task is preserved.** A supplies more useful entry points than B while allocating the same width to the demonstrated Macro table. ## Concrete findings and falsifiable closures

### P01 — High: the Company financial surface loses necessary working controls

**Evidence:** C image; `C.jsx` nodes `70DK-0` and `70RM-0` contain the workspace and seven major tabs. The next control group, `70FN-0`, contains only Annual and Quarterly; `70FQ-0` says “USD billions · Earlier periods ←.” The content proceeds directly to Revenue history and Reported periods. There are no Financials subview/statement-type controls in the supplied composition. The served `Company-overview.png` separately shows Saved research and Your theses.

**Consequence:** A reader can recognize a Revenue summary but cannot determine how to reach statements, statistics, dividends, comparison, a different statement type, available transcripts, or saved research. A global search is not an adequate visible substitute for those local routes. The parent Theses tab may cover Your theses, but that mapping has not been shown.

**Closure:** Show the full Company local hierarchy, including Financials subviews and statement type, with the evidence dock open. Demonstrate earlier history and the return to later periods, entry to an available transcript, and entry to Saved research and the personal theses destination. Preserve the exact company/listing through each step. This can be satisfied without expanding every view into the first fold.

### P02 — High: the Options object is shorter than the retained capability and its export contract is unspecified

**Evidence:** D image and `D.jsx`: `71EP-0` contains the seven main names but no actual subviews. `70ET-0` is “Root: All · Filter · Export.” Table header `70EU-0` contains Time ET, Root, Contract, Expiry, Premium, Side, Evidence, and More columns. `70GJ-0` says “Scroll the tape horizontally for all 12 fields. Inspect opens the complete row.” The table container `70EQ-0` uses `overflow: 'clip'`; the supplied composition contains no further data columns. The pane adds price/contracts and bid/ask but still omits Sector, DTE, Mny, and Flags. C/P is embedded in Contract, and Size is available only in the example pane. Saved/share view, workflow guide, and local Futures entry are absent. F does provide a global Futures destination.

**Consequence:** Investors lose the ability to scan and compare the retained attributes across prints. An Inspect pane cannot replace several missing comparison columns. “12 fields” also risks conflating the display schema with the separate 30-column all-filtered/sorted-record export. Filter and Export labels establish intended actions, not their full retained capabilities.

**Closure:** Present the actual 11-column Tape schema from the brief, with a useful overflow/frozen-identity design and complete row access. Explicitly preserve all filtered/sorted records and all 30 export columns independently of displayed columns. Show the actual subview paths, filters, row drill, saved/share view, guide, and Futures route. Remove the unexplained 12-field claim or reconcile it with an explicit schema.

### P03 — High: exact-period evidence has no corresponding visible selection

**Evidence:** C `70EA-0` identifies NVIDIA · Revenue · FY2024 and `70EB-0` shows USD 61.0bn. The row has four period values (`70GO-0`–`70GR-0`) and a single trailing information control (`70GS-0`). FY2024’s `70GQ-0` has no selected treatment. The chart `70FW-0` visually emphasizes the FY2025 bar rather than identifying the inspected FY2024 value. The close instruction names the Revenue evidence control, not an exact period trigger.

**Consequence:** The user cannot tell from the table which period launched the pane, how to request FY2023 instead, or which exact selection receives focus after closing. A readable panel title does not resolve an ambiguous trigger.

**Closure:** Bind evidence access to a field-and-period cell or an explicit period chooser. Show the selected cell and matching chart state. Open FY2024, replace it once with FY2023, close, and return to the FY2023 trigger with the same table/history position. Do not create a second independent pane during replacement.

### P04 — High: D prevents the required comparison while reading evidence

**Evidence:** In D, `70JO-0` places a dark scrim across all 1024px, while `70JQ-0` positions a 400px pane at x=624. The remaining undimmed reading surface is the pane; the underlying Options table, local controls, and later columns are covered or heavily dimmed. This is directly visible in the image. C, by contrast, retains its table beside the dock.

**Consequence:** The analyst must close the print to recover the table, then remember its contents while choosing another print. The required compare-while-reading path and direct one-step replacement are not supported by the pictured state. Modal behavior at this breakpoint could be a valid choice for another task, but it does not by itself satisfy this one.

**Closure:** At 1024px, show an arrangement or explicit in-pane comparison/replacement control that preserves the intended comparison without losing identity. Demonstrate replacement once and close back to the original row/filter/horizontal-scroll context. Establish a width rule for dock versus modal rather than relying on the desktop dock example alone.

### P05 — High: compact mode is not visibly reversible and mobile drops local destinations

**Evidence:** H’s 72px rail (`70Z9-0`) has no depicted expand control. Its destination labels, including `710Q-0`, `710I-0`, and `7100-0`, have zero width/height and `visibility: 'hidden'`; the selected Market overview is a bookmark-like icon. E retains exact table values, source links, View all sectors, and a combined History and sources link (`70RX-0`, `70RY-0`), but has no Overview/Participation/Regime/History/Sources selector equivalent to A. G’s drawer contains global destinations, not those local views.

**Consequence:** A new user cannot confidently choose or reverse compact mode. Phone users cannot discover all local research destinations from the represented controls. The advancing-sectors summary from A is also not represented in E.

**Closure:** Show a discoverable reversible compact control, usable destination names on keyboard focus and touch, and an accessible naming design. At 320/390 and tablet widths, make every local view reachable. Complete one important research path through a local view, evidence, and return, preserving exact values/history rather than reducing the object to a few cards.

### P06 — Medium: evidence distinguishes uncertainty honestly but does not yet establish source or event precision

**Evidence:** C explicitly states no filing citation is supplied (`70EF-0`) and delegates filing link, basis, units, and retrieval time to the field owner (`70EG-0`). D says Source ID: Not supplied (`70LM-0`, `70LN-0`). Its “Time · exchange” row displays only 15:58:12 ET, without an exchange name or explicit event date. Expiry is present, but expiry is not observation time. A and D separate illustrative/source-age cues from market/session or transport status, which is a strength.

**Consequence:** The user can read the illustrative number but cannot audit the intended filing or distinguish an exact print from another similar event. This is a missing design contract, not a claim that the illustrative values are fabricated live data.

**Closure:** Supply an example with concrete source identity, exact field/event identity, reporting basis, units, observation/publication/retrieval times where applicable, and freshness. Also show the unavailable-source state without implying that a connected transport or open market validates freshness.

### P07 — High evidence gap: the complete access/data/write state set is not represented

**Evidence:** I contains eight rows (`715X-0` through `71AF-0`). It includes good owner distinctions and prohibits save replay, silent guest conversion, and client-side unlocking. It does not separately represent empty-with-reason, stale/partial, or pending/saved/failed/uncertain writes. Read failed with retained old content and expired-session reconciliation cover parts of those cases. The retained Expired-uncertain-save screen explicitly tells the user the last save was unconfirmed and to check the watchlist before trying again.

**Consequence:** There is no complete reviewable answer for when a write has an uncertain outcome, how reconciliation establishes its status, when retry becomes safe, or how empty differs from loading/denial/failure. No unsafe write execution is established by this packet.

**Closure:** Add concrete user-facing state specimens for the missing cases and connect them to owner-confirmed inputs. Show an uncertain save becoming confirmed or remaining uncertain through reauthentication, with no automatic duplicate save. Preserve the requested route/query/hash and draft through the recoveries. Keep the useful retained public-browsing alternatives.

### P08 — Medium evidence gap: layer, focus, and phone-target contracts need specific states

**Evidence:** C `70EJ-0` promises Escape and page-order Tab; F `70S5-0` promises query/category restoration. D/G show scrims without a demonstrated focus/return sequence. F’s close control is explicitly 44×44 and E’s menu/search controls are 44×44. E’s language label `70M5-0`, market action `70NN-0`, and source actions such as `70P0-0` lack a declared 44px action height; the source row’s 52px height does not establish that the link owns the entire row. F’s Clear `70OO-0` likewise lacks its own 44px target.

**Consequence:** The packet cannot establish keyboard/touch parity, safe modal stacking, assistant coexistence, or the house target for all important phone actions. Static JSX cannot settle these runtime questions.

**Closure:** Specify action hit areas independently of text size. Show focus entry and return for search, account, drawer, evidence, and assistant; define whether each is modal or nonmodal and what opening another layer does. Verify zoom and touch completion on the represented narrow widths.

### P09 — Medium: B gives up useful entries without improving the demonstrated data density

**Evidence:** A’s table container `7038-0` and B’s `7061-0` are both 1168px wide with matching five-row content and column widths. B’s centered content uses the same 1216px outer width as A’s content area. B’s header contains the six main destinations, company search, All tools, EN, Dark, and Account, but no visible chart action, assistant, or quick destinations. A retains Open chart, Ask Mastermind, Market overview, Prophet, and Sectors & themes.

**Consequence:** B costs direct access to useful tasks without a demonstrated increase in the analytical object. The six primary destinations remain visible in both. A is therefore the stronger product starting point on this evidence, even though B may be a plausible visual preference.

**Closure:** If B remains a candidate, restore those actions in an equally discoverable way and demonstrate a complete task advantage at the same width. Do not justify the switch only by the absence of a sidebar.

### P10 — Medium: the Macro table does not explain its incomplete sector set

**Evidence:** A/B/H show 7 of 11 advancing sectors but only five sector rows, with no depicted “5 of 11” qualifier or View all sectors control. Their data is explicitly illustrative and coverage is marked partial because a session is missing. E does provide View all sectors.

**Consequence:** The displayed five rows cannot account for the seven advancing sectors. The user is not told whether this is a deliberate subset, ranking, or incomplete sector coverage; missing-session coverage is a different issue.

**Closure:** Provide all eleven sectors or identify the subset and its selection rule, with a visible route to the complete table. Keep observation-window missingness separate from sector-count coverage.

## Bounded direction and next review evidence

Retain A’s labeled global navigation, C’s exact-listing header and nonmodal evidence-dock direction, F’s four-route distinction, and I’s owner-specific access distinctions. Preserve the retained focused Terminal with its own chart controls and assistant; make the exact-company handoff and named return explicit. Treat H as an optional reversible mode, not the default discoverability solution.

The next useful revision is a complete capability-preserving set of task paths: Company Financials with history and exact-period replacement; Options Tape with the original comparison/export contracts; one narrow research task with all local destinations; and one uncertain-write recovery. A visual polish pass alone would not close these findings. Regional identity, account/preference ownership, public/internal boundaries, and query/hash restoration remain deliberately UNKNOWN until evidence covers them.
