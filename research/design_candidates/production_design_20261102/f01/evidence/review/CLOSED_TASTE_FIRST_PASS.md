## Findings, consequences, and falsifiable closure

Severity: **S1 — high** materially obstructs a required research operation or omits a substantial analytical object; **S2 — medium** weakens recognition, legibility, or represented coverage; **S3 — low** is local polish. An **evidence gap** describes a missing demonstration, not a claim of broken behavior.

### V01 — S1: Company depth is compressed into an underspecified Financials summary

**Evidence:** C.jpg; C.jsx `70RM-0` through `70RT-0` show the seven primary Company tabs. `70FM-0` through `70FQ-0` contain Annual, Quarterly, and Earlier periods. `70G7-0` is a five-row, four-period summary. No Statements/Statistics/Revenue/Dividends/Compare or statement-type controls appear in the admitted C composition. The served `Company-overview.png` visibly exposes Saved research and Your theses; C has neither explicit entry.

**Consequence:** The composition looks tidy partly because the controls that let an investor choose the analytical object and recover saved work are missing. Users cannot tell whether they are seeing income statements, a hand-selected summary, or the Revenue subview. Putting Theses in the top product tabs does not by itself establish the retained Your theses entry and its ownership/context.

**Closure:** Supply a same-width C revision that visibly locates all required Financials destinations and statement type, retains saved-work entry points, and demonstrates earlier-period navigation followed by return to later periods with the evidence pane open. Available transcripts may live in Earnings, but their reachable location must be shown. The resulting table must retain useful historical columns rather than merely shrinking text below the current 13px data text.

### V02 — S1: The Options object is incomplete, and its completeness copy overstates the artifact

**Evidence:** D.jsx `70EU-0` through `70F2-0` contain Time ET, Root, Contract, Expiry, Premium, Side, Evidence, and a More columns hint. The actual rows contain six data fields and Inspect. `70GJ-0` says “all 12 fields” and “complete row,” but the admitted export contains no hidden full column set. The brief specifies eleven current Tape columns and a separate thirty-column export containing all filtered/sorted records. The pane supplies price/contracts and bid/ask, but does not recover Sector, DTE, Mny, or Flags. `70ET-0` compresses Root, Filter, and Export into one text run; save/share, actual subviews, and guide are absent.

**Consequence:** An investor could not assess the promised Tape object from this composition. The small example looks complete while material screening and contract context are unavailable. More columns is a promise, not a represented overflow state, and twelve-field wording is not reconciled with either the eleven-column Tape or thirty-column export.

**Closure:** Show the actual eleven-column Tape structure and at least one useful horizontal-overflow or exact-value state. Its row inspection must identify every required field, including missingness. Show the real subview navigation, guide, save/share affordances, and export control with an unambiguous thirty-column/all-filtered-and-sorted-record scope. Remove or correct the twelve-field claim.

### V03 — S1: Company evidence has no unmistakable selected object

**Evidence:** C.jsx `70EA-0`/`70EB-0` identify NVIDIA, Revenue, FY2024, USD 61.0bn. The corresponding table cell `70GQ-0` has the same visual treatment as adjacent cells. `70GS-0` is a single Revenue-row information glyph, not a period-specific selection cue. In chart `70FW-0`, FY2025 alone uses the darker fill, while FY2024 shares the other bars' fill. C.jpg makes this conflict visible.

**Consequence:** The pane's text and the strongest chart emphasis point to different years. A user comparing periods must repeatedly reread the pane to know which value is under inspection. The row-wide glyph leaves period choice unexplained.

**Closure:** The evidence-open state must distinctly mark Revenue × FY2024 in both the table and chart, and identify the originating control. If the dark FY2025 bar denotes “latest,” label that meaning and use a separate selected-state treatment. Show one subsequent selection of a different field/period replacing the same pane, with no duplicate layer and an equally clear new selection.

### V04 — S1: Options evidence visually prevents comparison and replacement

**Consequence:** The user cannot comfortably compare the current print with another while reading evidence. The interface encourages a close–find–reopen loop despite the task explicitly requiring comparison and replacement while inspecting.

**Closure:** Show a 1024px composition in which the selected row and meaningful comparison values remain readable while evidence is open, or provide an equivalent explicit comparison/record-switching control inside the evidence surface. Demonstrate selecting one other print, then closing to that exact row and horizontal position.
