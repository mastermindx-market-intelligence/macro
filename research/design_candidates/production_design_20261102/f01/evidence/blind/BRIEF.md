# F01 closed first-pass artifact brief

## User and business job

An investor needs one legible way to move between market workspaces, find an exact company or tool, retain the analytical view and its history, inspect evidence, enter a focused chart and return to the intended research context. The shell must distinguish global location, market destination, entity identity and local view. Navigation must not silently save work or alter holdings/watchlists. Evaluate the complete task rather than the appearance of an isolated first fold.
