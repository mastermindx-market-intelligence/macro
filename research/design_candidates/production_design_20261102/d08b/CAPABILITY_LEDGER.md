# Capability retention ledger

This is a source-to-candidate contract, not a claim that new runtime paths have been implemented. Baselines are at the source pins in README; Paper references are in Terminal `01M3NSZGE8JWN2EZCSF0DT05RM`. Formal reference status remains RIG draft. The independent source audit supports the map; bounded product/taste findings and their limits are in REVIEW.

**Disposition counts:** BLOCKED_DATA 5, IMPROVE 25, RELOCATE 3, REMOVE 0, RETAIN 27 — 60 capabilities. No product capability is silently removed.

`BLOCKED_DATA` retains a useful visible source/unsupported state and a named owner. `RELOCATE` has an explicit reachable successor; it does not authorize a new owner or store. Advanced controls under menus remain acceptance obligations. Draft specifications of unsupported interpretations are not production removal decisions.

## Boundary and review notes

D08A scan/tape, C-owned Plan/payoff/saved scenarios, D-owned replay admission/cursor/distribution, other Options categories and global shell/registry ownership are not writable D08B scope. The ledger retains their transitions without duplicating their screens or authorities.

Phone qualification, complete menu destinations and exact-value targets passed the independent static remediation review. Source audit and later full product/taste review remain advisory; they do not make every ledger row formally approved. Source-level retention must be verified again in the future implementation, especially closed-menu destinations, marginal graphics, source-specific calculations and keyboard/return behavior.
