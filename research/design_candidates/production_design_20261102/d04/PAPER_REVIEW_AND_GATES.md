### Art direction

Use neutral planes, deliberate whitespace and a compact rhythm that puts analytical marks ahead of decoration. Dark should feel precise and quiet; light should offer clear paper-like separation without merely inverting dark values. Apply F02 semantic roles for surface, secondary surface, primary/muted ink, divider, action, focus, positive/negative/unknown and provenance. Names here are specification vocabulary, not new CSS tokens or exported components. Do not copy same-spelled aliases across Macro and Terminal without the token map.

Use one strong page title, a short descriptive line, restrained section labels, tabular data numerals, and readable evidence text. Critical sample sizes, clocks and caveats must meet the body/supporting type floor; do not squeeze them into near-10px footnotes. Long recipes and Chinese company names increase row height rather than clipping. Keep chart area/scale meanings visible next to the object. Contrast comes from type/plane hierarchy and data marks; colored left edges and colored container outlines are not permitted as decoration. Focus outlines and semantic chart/selection marks remain legitimate.
