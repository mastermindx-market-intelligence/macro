## Required execution evidence

For each later build run, record source/build/deployment identity, fixture identity and cutoff, account/entitlement class without credentials, action, observed result, evidence link, failure owner and disposition. Do not change PLANNED to PASS based on this design packet or source inspection.

The real production audit already observed a scope/population mismatch, working Energy selection, correct history-mismatch suppression, Retail HTTP524 and a Canadian navigation timeout. Those observations are preserved in AUDIT_OBSERVATIONS.md. They are not automatically the results of these future exact-build cases.

The three final principal-review falsifiers are included: D05-DATA-08 (price histories versus positive-volume coverage), D05-DATA-11/12 (scored census and absent committed fields), and D05-STATE-06 (missing thesis/summary must not become successful no-invalidation evidence).
