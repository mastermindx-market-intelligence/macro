# Frozen first-pass critique packet

## User/business job

A researcher starts at a particular Options/chart selection, opens futures or equity replay, selects a historical observation, inspects its evidence, and returns to the original research state.

## Capability and task manifest

- Exact instrument, session and time; historical versus current distinction.
- Futures pair, interval, row grouping, expiry lens and mapping; depth, footprint, CVD, GEX, source key levels and observed POC; captured volume and unknown-side accounting; separate weekly positioning.
- Equity symbol and observed-sample selection; depth and executed-volume views; near/all price window; book totals, gap/partial/source and correction qualifications.
- Source evidence; guest, plan-required, unsupported, missing, loading and failure outcomes.
- Mobile and narrow-screen selection/evidence access; named exact return.
