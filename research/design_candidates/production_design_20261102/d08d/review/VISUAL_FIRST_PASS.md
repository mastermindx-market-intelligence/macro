## Additional ordering and copy findings

Mobile evidence's ordering is useful: selected time/sample, capture limitation, clocks/source identity and quantities. The 1024 drawer should prioritize quantity/scope/omission before machine identifiers and retain the fuller acquisition limitation. “Archive acquired later” alone is weaker than saying that the archive cannot establish what was known at market time.

The state atlas contains API/review language such as `access_required`, `feed_not_published`, “Verified account feature result” and “Fallback destination explicitly named.” If annotation-only, that is not a product-copy defect; if rendered in the application, replace it with concrete user-facing copy. The boundary between annotations and proposed UI must be clear.

## Strengths and evidence limits

The visible hierarchy, instrument/source subtitles, historical/partial/retention qualifications and readable mobile evidence are useful. The reviewer saw no obvious horizontal clipping in the320 main view and found the drawn primary mobile controls deliberately sized. Actual hit areas remain unverified.

Static images do not establish selection correctness, exact return, advanced control contents, keyboard/focus/screen-reader/touch behavior, reduced motion, source/accounting correctness, Chinese localization or full width/theme/state coverage. The frozen recommendation was to amend the three P1 findings, improve mobile chart prominence and clarify evidence/return before visual acceptance. Rationale cannot replace visible repairs.
