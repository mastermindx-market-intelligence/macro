## 4. Implementation rules

Store integer counts and explicit availability in the synthetic fixture. A valid denominator counts securities that have both observations needed for the rate; eligible-population coverage is a separate count pair. These are fixture semantics, not field names to introduce into a product API without its owner's contract.

Calculate before rounding:

```text
rate_percent = 100 × above_count / valid_count
delta_pp = 100 × (current_above/current_valid − prior_above/prior_valid)
delta_bps = 10000 × (current_above/current_valid − prior_above/prior_valid)
relative_percent = 100 × (current_rate/prior_rate − 1)
```

Use decimal half-up formatting at the display boundary. Do not subtract rounded percentages, compute basis points from rounded percentage points, or infer a numerator from a rounded rate. Unknown denominators/observations produce an unavailable rate; a zero or unknown baseline makes relative percent change undefined. A genuine same-value comparison produces an explicit zero delta.

Keep read availability, source age, coverage and mutation outcome separate. The missing-clock fixture may reuse the valid universe counts while its source time remains unknown. The stale/partial saved-state composition retains the same universe observation and seven-of-eight history coverage after an illustrative save confirmation. An unavailable-detail panel retains only its already available Communication summary and dated evidence; it does not establish a new method endpoint or real producer response.
