The 600px content-gutter boundary is the existing F02 specimen boundary. It is distinct from the 860px shell-navigation boundary. The inequalities above deliberately cover fractional effective CSS widths such as 600.5, 860.5 and 1199.5px. F01 accepted this continuous clarification in the candidate geometry: the historical source uses literal `min-width:861px` / `max-width:1199px` for its middle class, so its behavior in fractional gaps is not asserted to equal this continuous rule. Header internal padding remains F01's composition responsibility; a content gutter is not automatically a header token. In the desktop shell, an explicitly selected compact rail is 72px. Neither F02 nor a consumer may automatically change the user's navigation preference to make a dock fit.

Let `C` be the **measured usable content width after the actual shell, local reserved chrome and both content gutters**. Dock only when `C − 320 − 24 ≥ 640`; equivalently `C ≥ 984`. Additional reserved local chrome reduces `C`. The width of an enclosing Paper artboard or a device label is not a substitute for this measurement.
