## Archive preconditions

Archiving is permitted only after all of the following are established:

- Exact source identity, content classification and domain custodian.
- Named successor and verified retention ledger, or explicit reason to preserve an unimplemented/rejected experiment as historical evidence.
- Comments, annotations, source links, assets and rejection/approval conditions preserved or linked from the successor.
- No unresolved overlap with an active admitted writer on the affected targets.
- Source/destination integrity checked after the supported move/copy, with bookmarks/backlinks and redirect notice updated.
- Archive index states superseded/rejected/source-only/held separately, and states whether any accepted reference still depends on it.

Age, name, folder, attractive appearance and “copy” suffix are never sufficient alone. Do not permanently delete work as part of this programme. Preserve rejected work with its rejection reason; do not quietly revive it under a new label. No bulk moves while the write schema is unqualified.
