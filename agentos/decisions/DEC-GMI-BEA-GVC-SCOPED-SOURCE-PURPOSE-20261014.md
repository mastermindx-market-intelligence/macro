## Decision boundaries

statistical input values can be retained, computed and displayed as
current-vintage descriptive information, including to paying subscribers.
Cite the original BEA release and give attribution. Never imply BEA endorsement,
reproduce BEA's logo as a product endorsement, or silently include third-party
licensed content. BEA's public-domain default is the grounding for this scoped
decision, not a privately issued license or proof of every embedded artifact.

**Accountability.** The existing `macro-dashboard` Data OS organization/function
is appointed accountable for this exact BEA source family. The active
Chairman-delegated CEO makes the source-purpose ruling and owns completion
sequencing. This appointment is not a new worker lease, an impersonated
human sign-off, or authority to displace an incumbent active source writer.
The Data OS registry, temporal rules, original evidence, and GMI product owners
remain the only implementation/read/publication planes.

known-at reconstruction, company-level supplier-buyer edges, forecast, trading
rank, trade size, model-training provenance, or automated execution. Estimated
industry-level import allocation must remain typed as an imputation. `5241X`
and `5241XX` are not interchangeable exact identifiers: the level-aware
2012-classification relation needs the exact 2026 concordance before an alias
may be adopted.

as `PROPOSED` only. Their provisional producer names and storage locations

retained original government responses and 138 research views are
DO_NOT_REACQUIRE; existing view-hash receipts do not establish native L0
