## Operating consequences

1. **One office, two modes.** `CHAIRMAN_COGNITION` and `CEO_EXECUTION` are responsibilities of the
   existing logical Meta-CEO office, not new identity, lifecycle or database owners.
2. **Three separate questions.** Strategic value, organizational authority and current
   serviceability remain independent. High value never manufactures authority; an envelope never
   cures stale source truth or `EFFECT_UNKNOWN`.
3. **Partial order.** Candidate transformations remain on an explicit Pareto frontier when they are
   genuinely incomparable; no hidden global weighted score silently decides company strategy.
4. **First-class strategic actions.** Hold, start, pause, resume, retire, combine, split, resource
   reallocation and organizational restructure are explicit choices rather than generic tasks.
5. **Delegated decision space.** Chris delegates bounded action classes, scope, carriers, budget,
   child count, expiry and reversibility—not one approval for every low-level move.
6. **Current initial mode.** Modifying autonomy begins only through `SUPERVISED_LIVE_CANARY` while
   unbounded autonomous strategic modification remains prohibited. Every modifying canary requires
   stop, rollback and falsifier controls.
7. **Constitutional boundary.** Charter/terminal-objective changes, unbounded authority, irreversible
   or unknown-reversibility actions, major budget/admin changes, autonomous production deployment and
   live-capital execution remain Chairman decisions under current law.
8. **No direct effect.** Chairman Cognition packets are deterministic evidence/projections and never
   reusable authority tokens. Existing effect owners re-read current source and enforce their
   mutation contracts.
9. **Learning loop.** Record prediction, selected option, evidence, actual capability, time/cost,
   rework, Chairman intervention and next envelope ruling in existing Agent OS/GitHub homes.
10. **Completion.** The program is complete only when Chris no longer carries routine portfolio
    reconstruction, session/account allocation, message shuttling, watcher repair, carrier archaeology
    or initiative babysitting—and real company capabilities still reach proof.
