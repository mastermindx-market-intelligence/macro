## Binding rulings

- **R-IND-00 — base ownership.** Industrials mints no shell, evidence, rights,
  route or mount vocabulary; T01–T06 extend the incumbent Company Intelligence,
  Fundamental Forensics and Earnings owners, while T07–T09 wait for
  Semiconductor B's shared route family and mount aggregator on main.
- **R-IND-01 — task-PR carrier.** One PR per task starts from fresh
  `origin/main`, merges in dependency order, coordinates on #7789, and relies on
  the on-main checkpoint for continuity; no single long-lived DRAFT/HOLD
  implementation carrier is permitted.
- **R-IND-02 — one gate:code job.** T01 creates the disabled
  `industrials-result-cash` job from the proven recipe with explicit paths and
  exclusive scope; every later task adds its suite to both the job paths and its
  run line, with a clean-venv install line proven for that task.
- **R-IND-03 — synthetic corpus.** The 17-fixture corpus uses invented issuers,
  wholly synthetic amounts, `synthetic: true`, `example.invalid` sources and
  real fiscal endpoints; it never copies live Exponent or Pentair figures and
  never marks a real company RESOLVED.
- **R-IND-04 — external-lane labor.** External fabric lanes execute packets
  with commit-and-push-per-step discipline; the seat and Opus READ_ONLY audit
  red-team every task PR before its dependents branch, while Opus and Fable act
  only as sub-orchestrators or auditors, never labor.
- **R-IND-05 — dependency order.** Run T01 → (T02 ∥ T04) → (T03 ∥ T05) → T06 →
  wait for Semiconductor B → T07 → T08 → T09; only path-disjoint pairs may run
  in parallel, and the issuer-profile and financial-dossier seams remain
  serialized by merge order.
- **R-IND-06 — route family only.** The shared POST
  `/api/themes/v1/research/{query,evidence}` family is the only external route;
  until it exists on main, `publication_harness().client()` refuses with a typed
  `route_unbound` result rather than offering a stub route or the old GET.
- **R-IND-07 — financial composition.** Qualified operating facts and
  result-to-cash derivations, same-period source-edition history, and optional
  management history remain independent; missing management history never
  suppresses the other layers, and a limitation-tagged comparison is never a
  certified beat/miss, badge, growth estimate or headline.

## Reversibility

Any ruling may be revised in its task PR before that task merges. After the
task merges, revision requires a superseding `DEC:*` record supported by new
evidence.
