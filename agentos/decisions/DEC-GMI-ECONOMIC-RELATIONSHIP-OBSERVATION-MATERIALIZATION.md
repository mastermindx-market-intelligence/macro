## Applied decision and ownership

**BUILD the bounded private observation capability.** This records a concrete
implementation choice under the existing execution commission. It is not an
Executive Job, runtime lease, source admission, product acceptance or a change
to the incumbent company authority map. Root owns integration and release;
the assigned builder owns only the two new application/test files in its brief.

Company Intelligence owns the adjacent inspection record. The original candidate
and its actual inspection remain the economic assertion; the new module owns
neither a second semantic inspector nor canonical relationship truth. Data OS
retains identity, temporal and source-policy law. Native vertical owners retain
their admitted facts, GMI its semantics and ThemeState, K3-D/Alpha its propagation
composition, F04 its product composition, and incumbent scoring/action owners
their decisions. Existing held work is preserved.

The [storage adjudication](../../research/theme_graph/economic_network_execution_20261009/continuation_pro_003/NATIVE_OBSERVATION_STORAGE_ADJUDICATION.md)

## Exact v1 scope

The writer accepts exactly seven role-distinct byte inputs: original candidate,
complete original UTF-8 source, source-record JSON, frozen pilot-adjudications
JSONL, frozen independent web-review JSON, retained-byte first-reader judgment,
and retained-byte principal judgment. Both retained-byte judgments are required.
The separately computed complete inspection is also sealed. This supersedes the
adjudication's earlier single optional addendum sketch; it does not create an
arbitrary attachment interface.

The writer computes `inspect_candidate` itself for the current inspection request.
It accepts no precomputed success or producer-authentication assertion. Candidate
and metadata parsing rejects duplicate JSON keys and nonfinite numbers, uses
explicit structural bounds, and validates bindings before any storage write.
Original file identity and canonical candidate-payload identity stay distinct.

The complete serialized evidence package, including JSON escaping, is at most
1,048,576 bytes. Each chunk is at most 16,384 bytes, at most 64 chunks are allowed,
and the closed manifest is at most 16,384 bytes. The candidate cap is 256 KiB;
each metadata/review input is at most 64 KiB. These ceilings never override the
stricter total package bound. The interface is intentionally sufficient for the
retained C01 evidence, not an unlimited document service.

Use the injected incumbent store and only its strict create-only and exact-length
bounded read APIs. Derive keys in
`company_intelligence/relationship_observations/v1/` from verified content. The
returned reference binds the manifest digest and its exact byte count. No
environment-selected factory, credential fallback, acquisition callback, list
operation, latest pointer, automatic registry entry, new bucket or shared-store
modification belongs in this increment.

Validate all chunks by readback before publishing the manifest. Repeating the
same inputs returns the same reference only after validating the complete
committed closure. Existing corrupt bytes are never repaired or overwritten.
An incomplete precommit leaves uncommitted components. An ambiguous final write
must expose its expected reference with an effect-unknown outcome until exact
readback resolves it. Correction/review links preserve old records and remain
unresolved declarations; they do not select a winner automatically.

## Economic and temporal meaning

C01 concerns Micron's announcement dated 2024-02-26: planned integration of its
**24GB 8H HBM3E into NVIDIA H200**, with Q2-2024 shipping planned. The two retained
reads independently identified that H200 is explicit in the source/span but is
not a separate field in the original candidate's `product_scope`. Preserve that
target restriction in the checked scope summary and exact attached judgments.
The separate statement that memory production began cannot become evidence of
delivered H200 systems or continuing 2026 bilateral supply.

Publication precision remains date-only. Canonical legal-party IDs, shipments,
volumes, revenue, share, exclusivity and present commercial state remain unknown.
The six purposively chosen source cases are not a 120-issuer acceptance sample or
a precision/recall estimate. Their different dispositions are retained in full.

The original semantic reviews read publisher web representations and retain null
raw-byte hashes for those web reads. The new addendum binds actual retained source,
candidate, span and inspected context. It does not rewrite the scope of the prior
reviews. The readers knew the prior case; this was not a blinded sample. Artifact
hashes and reviewer labels do not authenticate authorship, independence, source
custody or semantic truth.

Every read recomputes the real incumbent inspector. An unchanged request must
compare the complete fresh result with the sealed inspection. Request changes
and replay drift are separately visible. When the fresh inspector refuses or
excludes a historical case, return its complete actual outcome and withhold
saved positive labels, support and review semantics. For the retained C01 input,
the natural historical request lacks the required native dataset/history binding;
private retention cannot backdate availability.

Every record remains private and `NOT_ADMITTED`; Graph 1 projection and economic
magnitudes remain null and every downstream authority flag false. No publisher
production, redistribution, training or predictive permission is inferred.
