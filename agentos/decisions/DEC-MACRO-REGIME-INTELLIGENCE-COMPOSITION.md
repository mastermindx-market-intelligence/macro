# Decision and limits

The full design baseline is `research/macro_regime_intelligence/PROGRAM_ARCHITECTURE_2026-09-12.md`.
It is **SPEC_ONLY / pending independent architecture review**, not a shipped engine,
accepted numerical model, worker assignment, runtime admission or capital-authority grant.

## First useful capability

Compare orderly disinflation, growth deterioration, renewed inflation pressure,
long-end premium pressure and a technical pause on the existing transmission
surface, with source-dated supporting, contradicting and unknown evidence.
These overlapping research paths are not a probability simplex and must not be
normalized into numerical odds from condition counts.

The machine and user must consume the same derived RIC projection. Probability
models remain separately owned, targeted and evaluated. Historical similarity is
not causality; an economic-regime forecast is not automatically an asset-return
forecast. Magnitude, momentum, breadth, persistence and confidence remain separate.

## What this does not supersede

No new workstream/program parent, queue, database, RAG service, forecast ledger,
shock classifier, score, rank, sizing rule, alert escalation or deployment plane.
No changed DNR row. No overwrite of the RIC workstream record owned by PR6593.
No transfer of the existing 7015/7024/6860/6788/7040 source reservations or the
Market Ontology surface split. Current domain-owner formulas and accepted review
receipts remain intact.

## Reversal criteria

Revise this composition decision if a fresh owner census proves that the same
complete user journey is already production-proven, or an independent architecture
review identifies an unavoidable dependency cycle or a duplicate truth owner.
Change modeling choices when preregistered out-of-sample evidence supports a better
construction; do not reinterpret a failed candidate as a permanent ban on research.
