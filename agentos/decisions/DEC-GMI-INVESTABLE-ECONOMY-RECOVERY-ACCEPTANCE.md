## Scope and acceptance

This decision supplements DEC:GMI-THEME-GRAPH-END-TO-END-COMPLETION-OWNERSHIP-SEQUENCING.
Its original D2/W3 development boundary and rejection of duplicate GMI W4/W5/W6
remain valid. The current commission also requires the expanded descriptive
economic knowledge and coverage obligations; the old development boundary is not
a completion certificate for that broader commission.

The detailed capability map, research verdict, retained/revised contracts, explicit
weight meanings, supported-universe limits, measurable thresholds, cases and
dependency order live in the linked research record. The proposed activity-admission
and current-state contract extensions still require source implementation and real
native evidence. Activity admission also requires an admitted source-owned fact species,
applicable source-use rights and exact issuer/group scope before a probation profile
can qualify it. The current C01 planned-integration species cannot supply those by
analogy. The old TI-R2 deferral is narrowed only for individually evidenced, reviewed
activity/role admission; the ban on deriving company-to-theme facts merely from basket
membership and EXPRESSES remains. This decision grants no source rights, ratifies no candidate,
publishes no generation and confers no predictive/trading authority.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The numeric thresholds are prospective acceptance criteria, not measured passes.
A source-specific permission or immutable C01 publication does not grant unrelated
capture, graph-evidence or display purposes. Preserve the specific refused #8793
and Terminal #868 release effects and the unresolved F04 API524 effects. Historical
session names alone do not prove a live writer.

Reconsider a contract choice when an incumbent owner already supplies the missing
capability, a primary source changes its semantics, an adversarial case invalidates
the proposed rule, or consumer evaluation demonstrates that the chosen representation
misleads. Record narrow supersession and preserve historical evidence.
