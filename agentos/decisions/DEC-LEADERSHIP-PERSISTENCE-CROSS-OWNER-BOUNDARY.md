## Boundary detail

Published-output persistence can later inform explanatory context on an existing theme surface, but
it cannot rank themes, overwrite ThemeState, choose a technical timeframe, waive an entry blocker or
originate a trade. The first product consumer must preserve all five dimensions—level, memory,
residency, score pressure and evidence quality—and must display disagreement rather than fuse it away.
