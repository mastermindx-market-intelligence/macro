## Operational distinctions

- Relative strength is observable; ownership transfer is not established by
  unsigned off-exchange volume. A smaller decline is relative resilience.
- Index proxies cannot establish prior-loser constituent breadth.
- A recorded Sep 29 to Sep 30 Risk-on-to-Mixed transition is a prior backdrop
  for the Oct 5–8 motivating episode, not a new event-week confirmation.
- Source observation, first availability, publication and build time are
  different clocks. Missing first availability remains unknown.
- The early-context date can advance while the legacy last-confirmed-event
  date does not. Readers qualify each on its native clock.
- A held live display label does not authorize borrowing settled numerical
  causes for a live score. A recent wrapper build cannot freshen old evidence.
- Portfolio receives descriptive context through its existing Neural Web
  reader and Market View raw record; decision arithmetic and existing flags
  retain their prior behavior.
