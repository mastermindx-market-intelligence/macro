## Product acceptance

A user must be able to move from market/regime context to the strengthening group,
to an appropriate member, to a usable entry or explicit wait, to a maintained thesis
and its invalidation, then to an honestly measured outcome. Use the existing V4
candidate episodes, GMI membership/state, Radar events, Fusion machinery, entry
semantics, plan lifecycle, and Evaluation owners. Do not infer one plane from another.

Keep broad-market risk, theme leadership, security selection and entry permission
separate. A risk-off backdrop must be considered alongside genuine relative strength,
not silently erase its observation; strength must not override stale prices, broken
structure or unsafe entry geometry. Do not invent an unvalidated macro-risk waiver.

Discovery, research priority and licensed entry remain different. Show a detected
candidate's actual source session and real decision-path refusal, not an unrelated
proxy or a reassuring label. Preserve the complete candidate universe; a featured
card cap must not become deletion from discovery, explanation or evaluation.

Distinguish first reversal, liquidity-sweep/reclaim, leader pullback, continuation and
catalyst-driven setups where existing accepted detectors support them. Unsupported
pattern claims stay hypotheses. A missed first entry should remain an episode with
lawful next-opportunity conditions, not disappear merely because its first cross aged.

Thesis support must explain evidence, counterevidence, expected path, uncertainty and
observable conditions to maintain, reduce, exit or reconsider. It must not manufacture
confidence, narrate away losses, or tell users to hold solely because a theme is strong.
Rates, real yields and oil are context with causal hypotheses, not universal sell flags.

Keep premarket, regular-session, after-hours and overnight observations distinct.
An overnight print is not a completed NYSE daily bar. Reconstructed historical inputs
are not proof of a recommendation or notification delivered at that historical time.
Identity and exchange/session differences, including BABA/9988, remain owner-resolved.

The Chairman's semis, memory, optical infrastructure, crypto-linked equities, miners
and BABA are motivating diagnostic cases, not a hardcoded preferred universe. Include
failed bounces, non-leaders, later reversals and point-in-time universe controls.
