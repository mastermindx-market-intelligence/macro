sP, sA, z=(B/sB,(B-P)/sA) and w=(B/sB,P/sP). Equivalent lag coefficients alpha
and acceleration coefficients beta obey:

```text
beta_now = alpha_now + (sB/sP)*alpha_prev
beta_accel = -(sA/sP)*alpha_prev
||beta||^2 = (alpha_now+(sB/sP)*alpha_prev)^2 + (sA/sP)^2*alpha_prev^2
```

scales require a frozen explicit convention; they are outside this positive-scale
