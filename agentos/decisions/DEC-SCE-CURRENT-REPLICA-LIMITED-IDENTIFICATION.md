## Supersession boundary

This decision supersedes only the open-ended current-version identification instruction in the
SCE V9/V11 continuation records. It does not erase earlier historical source/access evidence,
V8's favorable restricted drawdown-feasibility result, or the August-family
`NO_EXIT_RULE_VALIDATED` finding. Those retain their scoped meaning.

The September current-version architecture remains partially disclosed: universe and engine-level
composition are known, exact engine rules are not. A later genuinely new current-version method
disclosure may reopen identification without revisiting this decision as an error.

## What reopens the replica

Reopen only on version-matched evidence that materially resolves one of the missing current rules,
such as the actual Section 3, a deterministic Trend Base transition with threshold/state semantics,
a complete Dislocation Overlay entry/exit disclosure, or a same-date options-context example that
proves deterministic portfolio authority. New evidence must be dated and attributable to the current
method generation.

A new chart fit, a better proxy, a June/July rule, a plausible options structure, or a performance
match is not a reopening event.
