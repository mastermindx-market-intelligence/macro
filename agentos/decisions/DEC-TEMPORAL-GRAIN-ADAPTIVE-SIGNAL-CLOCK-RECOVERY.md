# Scope of the amendment

This decision **amends but does not supersede** `DEC:TEMPORAL-GRAIN-OWNERSHIP-AND-ZERO-AUTHORITY`.
Its ownership, G/A/K/D separation, W1A-vs-W1B evidence distinction, TrialLedger/Evaluation OS
ownership and zero-authority constraints remain controlling.

The only prerequisite replaced is the requirement that a future general empirical continuation
must first obtain the motivating WMT/silver historical recipe result. That requirement still governs
the **historical reproduction question itself**.

# Release gate

Acceptance of this decision is necessary but not sufficient to open W1B outcomes. A complete
executable preregistration, qualified rights/data/custody, current-main-qualified incumbent harness,
accepted V2-M receipts and a fresh action-scoped Sol continuation are additionally required.
