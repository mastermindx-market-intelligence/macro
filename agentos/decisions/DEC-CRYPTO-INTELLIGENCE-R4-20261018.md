# Crypto Intelligence R4

This decision supersedes the earlier proposed front-door design and candidate release
plan. It carries forward the earlier decision's canonical model, chart, snapshot
and alert-owner boundaries. It does not reopen accepted P0A or alter the admitted H5
crypto envelope. The Chairman's new commission extends beyond the historical
October 13 polish frontier; those completed waves remain completed.

## The product contract

Overview answers the current market question and next conditional test. Bitcoin
provides structure and its six evidence families. Markets supports leadership and
relative-strength investigation for assets with actual coverage. Scenarios compares
dated conditional cases, expiry, invalidation and frozen snapshots. Historical review
and model tools are contextual study surfaces, not additional top-level navigation.

The implementation uses the existing SignalInk owner for a server-rendered daily
chart. It does not replace the existing qualified replay owner with a new chart
engine. The canonical btc.decision/v1 receipt remains the model source; exploratory
scenarios have no allocation authority.

The candidate's 7D and 30D price conditions are descriptive prior-close ranges
(excluding the issue-day close), not validated support/resistance targets or price
probabilities. Missing demand remains missing confirmation. Macro hypotheses are
research mechanisms, not an estimated causal response model.

https://crypto-intelligence-r4-review-20261018.mastermindx3.chatgpt.site .
