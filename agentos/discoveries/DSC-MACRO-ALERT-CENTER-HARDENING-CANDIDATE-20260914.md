## Important repair to the earlier brainstorming

The first Market Changes Desk sketch risked drifting into Archetype A by using a permanent 2/3 + 1/3
command layout and treating What Matters Now as a separate dashboard hero. The binding design system
says `alerts.html` is Archetype G (`monitor`), whose identity device is the change-log timeline. The
hardened candidate corrects this: What Matters Now is the 0-3 lead group *inside* the timeline, context
is compact/supporting, and the page does not become a second `start.html` or `macro.html`.

## Exact deterministic lead rule

Lead items are one-to-one with canonical observations in v1; no synthetic development identity and no
LLM grouping. Unknown-time, future, context-tier and nonfresh observations cannot enter. Reuse the
canonical fresh threshold (`age_days <= 2`). Fresh `act` may enter; fresh `watch` enters only with
post-governance major/critical severity. Take at most three in existing attention order, never pad the
layout or force source diversity. A fresh re-fire says re-fired/observed again, never persistent unless
the source has continuity proof. Blocking coverage withdraws whole-market synthesis while retaining
usable source-specific evidence.

This rule intentionally handles the measured live score compression (55/60 rows at 48) without a new
score. Numeric priority becomes an inspector receipt rather than the dominant visual hierarchy.

## Experience and authority boundaries

Now is a board-day timeline; group by event day and keep existing attention order within day. Explore
holds the complete accessible population and filters. History is actual firings/corrections, not
interpolated state. Broad clusters are facets. #7022 same-source subject bundles are only Related
observations and explicitly not independent confirmation. `Situation` remains reserved for a future
grounded cross-domain capability.

The fast path works without a model. A model may later explain selected evidence but cannot select,
promote, rank, mint identity, infer persistence, create causality or trigger another firing because its
wording changed. News requires accepted event/identity/clock/rights/correction semantics. Prophet is
not currently a customer source in shared alert_triage and is not drawn into the v1 mockup as if it is;
a future adapter consumes owner-issued Prophet state only.

Private personal monitoring remains F08/Terminal/account-owned. Macro shared HTML does not gain
holdings, private watchlist, read/archive, quiet-hour, email-preference or delivery state.

## Required visual states before design acceptance

Complete-with-lead, quiet-lead, true-empty, blocking partial coverage, nonblocking unavailable source,
unknown event time, first vs re-fire, Explore no-results/reset, bounded History, historical-vs-current
evidence, missing deep-link after correction/window roll, JS-evidence failure, long EN/ZH, both themes,
390px mobile, keyboard/focus/reduced-motion. No state may invent currentness or hide missing evidence.
