## Final design direction

The current live page's alert/source/time/coverage truth is useful; the dominant visual hierarchy is
not. The redesigned page keeps Alert Center's Monitor identity as a board-day change log. `What Matters
Now` is a display-only 0-3 lead group, not another hero/dashboard and not a new durable event type.

The lead rule stays deterministic: known event time, canonical fresh window (`age_days <= 2`), fresh
`act`, or fresh post-governance major/critical `watch`; no `context`, no future event, no model
promotion, maximum three, no padding. Re-fires say observed again/re-fired, not persistent unless the
source proves continuity. Blocking coverage withdraws whole-market synthesis without hiding usable
source-specific evidence.

The accepted primary tasks are `Now / Explore / History`. Clusters are Explore facets. #7022's
same-source exact-subject grouping may appear as `Related observations` with an explicit not-independent-
confirmation caveat; `Situation` stays reserved for future grounded cross-domain intelligence.

The raw 0-100 priority score, giant pressure hero/gauge, four-cell scoreboard, storyline cards,
repeated recurrence/validation chips and methodology forest are demoted from Tier 1, not deleted from
evidence. The inspector owns provenance, ranking components, validation receipts and actual firing
history. Current `alert_triage`, alert IDs, source tier/severity, clocks, coverage, validation and push
authority remain unchanged.
