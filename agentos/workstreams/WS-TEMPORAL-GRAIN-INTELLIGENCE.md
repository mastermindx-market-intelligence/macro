## Scientific prior

The broader hypothesis remains unproven: an instrument/signal/regime may have one supported scale,
several concurrent scales or no stable useful scale. The system should prefer bands, multiscale
states and typed abstention over a brittle one-hot timeframe unless untouched evidence says otherwise.

The original WMT/silver cases provide no mechanical-survival evidence. They remain historical design
motivation and negative evidence about recoverability, not confirmation instruments.

## Ownership boundary

- Market Ontology/data/instrument owners retain instrument, vendor, venue, contract, roll, session,
  timezone, adjustment, correction and rights truth.
- Signal Engine/Temporal Grain owns only the deterministic kernel/memory and causal temporal research.
- Stock Identity supplies structural measurements read-only unless separately versioned.
- Granular Regime owns regime-state/duration methods; Temporal Grain may consume causal outputs.
- Technical Opportunity owns broad data/clock/rights qualification and Terminal parity.
- Entry Radar owns tactical intraday events.
- TrialLedger/Evaluation OS owns attempted-look accounting and outcomes.
- Agent OS owns organizational continuity; GitHub owns source/evidence; Executive OS owns runtime.
