## Architecture direction

Use one accountable delivery owner per programme. Economical qualified operators
own bounded execution, review, repair and integration; frontier Astra/Fable
reasoning is used for genuinely ambiguous cross-system decisions, not routine
relay or polling. Sol, Grok and Opus may lead delivery when the task/harness
combination has passed real outcome qualification. No global prestige ranking
substitutes for task-specific success and total accepted-work cost.

Keep role, task complexity, risk, harness capability and provider/account supply
separate. Preserve the first lawful model suitability tier before scarcity or
reset-date preferences. Reserve the remaining review/repair and interactive
obligations in the existing shared account budget. Treat possible promotional
resets as uncertain upside, never as guaranteed refill or permission to clear a
lease/refusal/unknown effect. Recompute future placement only from fresh owner
observations; keep started work sticky.

Routine peer status belongs in existing durable records, not unsolicited model
interrupts. Use existing correlated one-peer consultation and exact original
parent results. Add project-assignment checks to the existing authorization owner
where missing, rather than inventing another ACL database or message scheduler.

Match harness comparisons on task, tool grants, context, effort, review budget
and acceptance criteria. Claude-style project coordination is the target
workflow: scope, delegate into isolated workspaces, inspect output, repair,
integrate and prove the consumer result. A larger model or another manager does
not independently produce that workflow.
