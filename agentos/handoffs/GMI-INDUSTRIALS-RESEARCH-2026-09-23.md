## Decisions, falsifiers and unresolveds

Ending dealer underproduction can lift manufacturer earnings without retail growth; that thesis fails if retail deterioration or excess rebuilding dominates. Rental growth must cover lifetime capital and residual risk, not only EBITDA. Distribution requires contribution after service and working capital. Freight invoices, physical activity and broker spreads need different denominators.
