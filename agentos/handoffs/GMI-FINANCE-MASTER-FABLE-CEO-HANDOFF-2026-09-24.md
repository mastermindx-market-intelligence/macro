financial-system workflow
→ business role/economic model
→ operating / earnings / book / FCF / capital transmission
→ expectations/revisions
→ valuation anchor/required return
→ price recognition

The four planes never collapse:
1. operating economics;
2. expectations;
3. valuation;
4. price recognition.

Three system maps remain separate:
1. contractual money/risk flow;
2. regulated infrastructure/access;
3. public-equity economics.
