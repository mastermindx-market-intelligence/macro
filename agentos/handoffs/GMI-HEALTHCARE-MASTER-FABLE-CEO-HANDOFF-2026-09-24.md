## 7. Economic and evidence semantics that must survive engineering

Treat an asset's indication, population, geography, stage, rights, payment base, applicable period and observation date as separate dimensions. Companies can participate in multiple subthemes without duplicate accounting or invented basket membership.

An economic right identifies what is received, the denominator, costs borne, conditions and loss participation. Net sales is a payment base, not a gross/net flag. Not disclosed is missing information, not estimation provenance. Qualitative facts are useful without a numeric value. Unknown rates, tiers and deductions remain unknown; no zero, midpoint or implied earnings substitute. Preserve signed financial values while rejecting invalid negative physical counts. Percentage changes, percentage points, amounts, flows and stocks remain distinguishable.

Keep clinical result, formal trial conclusion, authorization, reimbursement and actual paid use separate. An updated analysis is not an independent study. Manufacturer availability, regulator shortage status and an economic capacity thesis are different observations. Qualified production, contracted activity, utilization, margin and cash realization are different links. Revenue growth may reflect price, mix, acquisition, timing or measurement boundaries rather than greater use. Preserve contrary evidence and unresolved materiality.

The synthesis must depend on explicit source assertions. A withdrawn, corrected, stale or ineligible required assertion cannot leave its dependent conclusion displayed as current with the citation merely removed. Distinguish a source correction from an analyst reinterpretation and an actual contractual change. Unknown historical availability or timezone remains unknown; do not backdate admission or fabricate point-in-time knowledge.
