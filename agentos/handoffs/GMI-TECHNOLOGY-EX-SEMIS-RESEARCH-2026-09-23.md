## Material conclusions and falsifiers

Keep classification, business/product, workload, monetization, theme, financial materiality and security identity separate. For software, distinguish useful activity, contractual billing and customer outcome; do not translate adoption into earnings without price, resource-intensity, cost and displacement evidence. Separate operating changes from metric definition, timing and business-perimeter changes.

Strong R2 cases include MongoDB 30/90-day consumption-derived customer ARR; Datadog monthly annualization; UiPath Annualized Renewal Run-rate; Dassault Annual Run Rate; Alphabet short-contract backlog reporting change; Guidewire current versus ramped ARR; Elastic overlapping reporting partitions; security acquisitions; PTC divestments; paid seats versus user populations; and gross-profit dollars versus margins. These are date/source-specific findings, not universal sector verdicts.

Guidance bridge: for comparable selected total-revenue vintages, implied remaining-year revision = FY midpoint increase minus completed-quarter revenue above its old guided midpoint, adjusted for any earlier-period revisions. Decimal-verified examples in USD millions: Datadog 93.546; MongoDB 29.727; UiPath 0.244. The earlier actuals reconcile to the new first-half figures. Do not apply the additive formula to EPS, margins or ending ARR without native reconstruction. Residuals are not consensus or pure organic demand and do not rank stocks.

Billing cases: standard action versus completed case; warehouse provisioning minimum versus job count; log ingestion versus indexed subset; per-person promotional credit allowance versus shared pool; product-day versus application opens.
